CHAPTER XIX

On Hariścandra's Kingdom Taken

व्यास उवाच

इति तस्य वचः श्रुवा भूपतेः कौशिको मुनि: ।

प्रहस्य प्रत्युवाचेदं हरिश्छ्रं तदा नृप: ॥ १ ॥

राजसैतीधीमिदं पुण्यं पावनं पापनाशनम् ।

स्नानं कुक महाभाग चितृणां तपाँगं तथा ॥ २ ॥

Vyāsa said: O King! Hearing thus the words of the King Hariścandra, the Maharṣi Kauśika smilingly said: "O King! This Tiratha is very sacred; if one bathes here, one is cleansed of one's sins and virtue springs up. So, highly fortunate One! Bathe in this and do peace-offerings (tarpaṇam) to your fathers.

कालः शुभातमोदसीते तीर्थे सन्ताल विशोधते ।

दानं दद्व्य सफलाउन पुण्यतीर्थे तिपावाने ॥ 3 ॥

प्राय तीर्थे महापुण्यमसन्ताल यस्तु गछविति ।

स भवेदात्माव भूय इति सत्यंभुवोज्विति ॥ 4 ॥

O King! This time is very auspicious and highly meritorious; so take a bath in this sacred Punya Tirtha and make charities as far as it lies in your power. Svāyambhava Manu says: “He, who arriving at a Tirtha capable to give high merits (Punya), does not bathe and make charities, deceives himself; so he is the slayer of his soul, no doubt.

तस्मातीर्वेते राजकुन कु पुचं स्वशक्ततः ।

दर्शरिय्यामि मार्गे ते नतासि नगर ततः ॥ 5 ॥

आगाधिष्यार्ह मार्देशुनार्थ ततवान्य ।

त्वया सहासुधकाकुतस्थ तव दानेन तोपितः ॥ 6 ॥

So, O King! Do meritorious acts as best as you can in this excellent tīrtha. Then I will shew you the way and you will go to Ayodhyā. O Kakuttsha! Today I will be pleased with your gifts and I will accompany you to shew you the way; this I have decided."

तच्छुत्वा वचनं राजा मुनेः कपटपिडतम् ।

वासांस्युतायं विधित्वलनातुमध्याययो नतीम् ॥ 7 ॥

Hearing the deceitful words of the Maharṣi, the

King took off his upper garments and tying the horse on to a tree, went towards the river to bathe according to due rites.

बन्यस्तिवहा हयं युवे सुनिवाक्येन मोहितः ।

अवर्यंभावियोगेन तड्गास्तु तद्धपवत् ॥ 8 ॥

राजा स्नानविधि कृत्वा सन्तयं पितृदेवता: ।

विश्वामिज्ञपावेचे स्वामिन्दानं द्वादि मे ॥ 9 ॥

O King! The accidental combination, that was to have been so (sure to come), so enchanted the King by the Muni's words, that he got himself entirely under the control of the Muni. He duly completed his bath and offered peace offerings to the Devas and the Pitts and then spoke to Visvāmitra. "O Lord! I am now making gifts to you.

यदिच्छसि महाभाग तते दास्यामि साम्यान् ।

गायो भूमिरिंग्चं च गजाध्यथावहान् ॥ १ ० ॥

O Fortunate One! Cows, lands, jewels, elephants, horses, chariots or horses, etc., anything that you like I will give you just now. There is nothing that I cannot give.

नादेवं मे किमयास्ति कुममेतद्रतं पुरा ।

गजसूये मखश्रेष्ठ मुनीनां सन्धिप्राविण ॥  1 1 ॥

तस्पात्विह सम्यापतस्नीर्ध़स्म्यम्यरे मुने ।

यतेजसि वांषितं बृहि ददामि तव वांषितम् ॥ 1 2 ॥

When I performed previously the Rājasūya sacrifice, I took, then, before all the Munis, this vow. So, O Muni! You are also present at this principal Tirtha (place of pilgrimage); so express what you desire; I will give you your desired object."

विश्वासिज्ञ जवाच

मया पूर्वे श्रुता राजकन्नितिते विपुला भुचि ।

वसिद्धेन च सम्योक्ता दाता नासि महीतले ॥ 13 ॥

हरिश्छाने नृपश्रेष्ठः सूर्यवेगे महीपति: ।

तादृषी नृपतिर्द्राता न पृती न भविष्यति ॥ 14 ॥

पृथिव्यां परमोदारक्षिज्ञकृतनयो यथा ।

Book VII Chapter XIX

Vísvāmitra said: “O King! Your glory is spread far and wide in this world; especially I have already heard that there is no second man charitable like you. The Muni Vasisṭha has said: “The King of the solar dynasty, the Trīśanku’s son, Hariścandra is foremost and first amongst the kings in this world and there is no one so liberal-minded as he is; such a king there never was nor ever there will be.”

अतस्तां प्रार्थ्यायस्य विवाहो मेर्सित पार्थिव ॥ 15 ॥

पुरस्य च महाभागा तदर्थे दिहे मे धनम् ।

So, O King! Now the marriage time of my son has arrived; so I pray before you to-day, that you give me wealth to celebrate this marriage."

राजोवाच

विवाहं कुरु विज्ञे-त्र द्वादिं प्रार्थितं तव ॥ १६ ॥

यदिष्ठसि धनं कामं दत्ता तस्यामिन क्रिशुम् ।

The King said: “O Brāhmīn! Yes! Celebrate the marriage ceremony; I will give you your desired wealth. What more can be said than this that whatever wealth you would want, I will give that abundantly. There is no doubt in this.”

व्यास उचाच

इत्युक्तः कीशिकस्तेन वंचनात्तयो मुनि: ॥ 17 ॥

उद्वालय मायां गान्धर्वीं पार्थिवायायदर्शयेत् ।

Vyāsa said: O King! Hearing these words of the King, the Muni Kausika became ready to deceive him and originating the Gāndharvi Māyā, created a beautiful youth and one daughter aged ten years and showing them to the King, said “The marriage of these two is to be celebrated today.

कुमार: सुकुमारघ्छ कन्य च एशावर्षिकी ॥ १ ८ ॥

एतयोः कार्यमध्यच कर्तेल्य नृपसत्तम ।

O King! To marry the boys and the girls in the household is to earn more merits than the Rājasūya sacrifice.

राजसूयाधिकं पुणयं गृहस्थस्य विवाहितः ॥ १९ ॥

भविष्यति तत्वाठीव विगुपुरविवाहुतः ।

So today you will get that desired fruit if you make charities for the marriage of this Brāhmīn Youth."

तच्छूला वचनं राजा मायया तस्य मोहितः ॥ 20 ॥

तथेति च प्रतिज्ञाय नोवाचालयं वचस्तथा ।

The King was much enchanted by his Māyā; so no sooner he heard those words, he immediately promised: "That will be done" he did not raise any objection whatsoever.

तेन दर्शितमार्णोंसी नगर प्रति जीमवान् ॥ 21 ॥

विश्वामीजीपि राजानं वंचवितासे उसमं ययो ।

Vísvāmitra then showed the way and the King went to his city. Vísvāmitra, too, thus deceiving the King, went back to his Āśrama.

कृतोज्ञात्विधित्साविद्धामित्रोउवबीजुपम् ॥ 22 ॥

वेदीमध्ये नृपाढ्च त्वं देहि द्वानं यथेवित्यन्त ।

When the King was staying in Agnisālā (cook room), Vīsāvimitra Muni went to him and said: "O King! The marriage rites have been finished; so today give me what I desire in this sacrifice hall."

राजोवाच

किं तेउभीष्ट हिंज बृहि द्वादीय वास्छितं किल ॥ 23 ॥

अदेवमपि संसारे ययः कामोत्रिस्य संग्रतम् ।

व्यर्थ हिं जीवितं तस्य विषयं प्राप्य येन वै ॥ 24 ॥

नोपार्जितं यश: शुद्धं परलोकसुखप्रदम् ।

The King said: “O Brāhmīn! Speak out what you want; now I like to get fame. So if there be anything in the world, that is not to be given by me, if you want, I will give that even to you, no doubt. The mortal, possessing all wealth, if he do not earn good name and fame capable to give happiness to him in his next world, passes his life in vain.”

विश्वाधित्र ज्ञान

राज्यं देहि महाराज वराय सप्तिच्छदूम ॥ 25 ॥

गजाश्रधरलाळं बेदीमाधेउर्जिषायने ।

Víśvāmitra said: “O King! Give to this bridegroom, while within this sacred sacrificial alter, you entire kingdom with the royal umbrella and Cāmara for fanning the king and elephants, horses, chariots, infantry and all the gems and jewels.”

At this time, his soldiers who lost their roads in quest of their king, came to him. They were very glad to see him; but, seeing him anxious, they began to praise him in great haste.

व्यास उवाच

बुत्वा तेनां वचो राजा नोकवा किंविदच्छूभाणुम् ।

चित्नयन्स्वकृतं कर्म ययावन्तःपुरे ततः ॥ 34 ॥

Vyāsa said: O King! Hearing their words, the King did not say anything, good or bad; but thinking on his own doing, entered into the zenānā.

किं मया स्वीकृतं दानं सर्वेयं वत्समर्पितम् ।

वढ्धितोई जिंजेनात्र यने पाटच्छररित ॥ 35 ॥

Oh! What have I promised to give? I have made a gift of all that I have; I am cheated in this matter by the Muni like one robbed by a thief in a wilderness.

राज्यं सोपवक्ता तसे मया सर्व प्रतिश्रुतम् ।

भात्रयं सुवर्णस्य साधं च दक्षिणा पुनः ॥ ३६ ॥

My dominion including my dress I have promised to give to him. Moreover I will have to pay besides two and a half loads of gold.

कि कंपोरि मतिभंधीन न ज्ञातं कवटं मुनेः ।

प्रतितिरोई सहसा ब्राह्मणेन तपत्थिता ॥ 37 ॥

न जाने देहवाक्याँ वै हा देह किं पविष्यति ।

इति चिन्तापरो राजा गृहं प्रापोजीतिवहून: ॥ 38 ॥

My brain seems to have been completely destroyed. What to do now? I did not know the cunningness of the Muni. Therefore I am cheated by this deceitful Brāhmīn. It is next to impossible to understand the work of Daiva. Oh! My Fate! What will happen to me now?' Very much bewildered, the King entered in the interior of the palace.

पर्ति चिन्तापरं दृष्टवा राजी पप्रष्छ कारणम् ।

कि प्रभो विमान भाति का चिन्ता बूहिं समाप्तम् ॥ 39 ।

वन्तापुत्र: समायातो राजसूय: कृत: पुरा ।

कसमच्छोचित राजेज्ञ शोकस्य कारणं चद ॥ 40 ॥

The queen seeing her husband immersed in cares, enquired into the cause, thus: "O Lord! Why have you become so absent-minded? Kindly say what you are thinking now? O King! The son has

come back from the forest before you completed your Rajasüya sacrifice; why then are you in grief now? Kindly speak out the cause of your sorrow.

नारातिथिंघते क्रापि बतलावनुबैलो3पि वा ।

वरणोपरि सूमंतुः: कृतकृत्योपि भृतले ।। 4 1 ।।

चिन्ताव क्षीयते देहो नासि चिन्तासमा भृति: ।

त्यन्तां तंपगशर्तै स्वयो भव विश्वेषण ।। 4 2 ।।

तिशगय चिंशावावयं प्रीतिपूर्वं नाराधितः ।

प्रोवार किंचिचित्नाता: कारणं च रुषाणुभम् ॥ 4 3 ॥

Nowhere is your enemy, strong or weak; only Varuna was angry with you; now he is also very satisfied. So there is nothing further for you to do or to think. O King! Owing to cares, this body gets weaker and weaker, day by day. So nothing is like cares to lead one to death." When his dear wife said so, the King expressed to her somewhat the cause of his cares, good or bad.

भोजनं न चकारासी चिन्ताविष्टस्या नृप: ।

सुत्वारिज शयने रुष्रे तेषे निद्धि न भूमिपः ॥ 4 4 ॥

प्रातरस्थाय चिन्तातों वासलेन्यादिका: क्रिया: ।

But the King was much absorbed with his cares so that he could not eat nor sleep though his bedding was perfectly white and clear. Early in the next morning, when, getting up from his bed, he was doing anxiously his morning duties, Viśvāmitra came up there.

करीति नृपतित्साविद्धधार्मि: समाप्ततः ॥ 4 5 ॥

क्षता निविदितो राते भृति: सर्वस्वहारकः ।

आगत्योबाच राजानं प्रणामन्ते पुनः पुनः ॥ 46 ॥

When the sentinel informed the King of the arrival of Vísvāmitra, he gave order for him to enter. Vísvāmitra, the Looter of his all and everything, came before him and told the King who repeatedly bowed down to him:

विश्वासि उवाच

राजस्पत्न सर्वसाथे मे देहि वाचा प्रतिश्रुतम् ।

सुवर्णी सूश राजेत्र सत्यवाभ्यव सामप्रम्न।। ४ ७ ॥

“O King! Now leave your kingdom and give me the gold that you promised as Dakṣinā and prove that you are truthful.”

हरिश्रान्त्र जवाच

स्वामिजार्जं तद्वेदे मे मया दर्श किलाप्राना ।

त्यक्ताप्रान्य ग्रविष्यामि मा चित्ता कुर्य कीकीरक ॥48॥

Harīścandra said: “O Lord! I have given you this vast dominion of mine; so my Kingdom has now become yours; I am leaving this Kingdom and going to somewhere else.

सर्वस्वं मम ते ब्रह्म-गृहीतं विधिविष्ठो ।

सुवर्णदक्षिणं दातुभगतकां हुधाना हिज ॥ 49 ॥

दानं ददायि ते ततवधान्ये स्याद्धानागमः ।

O Kauśika! You need not think a bit for this. O Brāhmaṇa! You have taken my all according to the technical rule; so now I am unable to give you Dakṣinā.

पुनःश्रुकात्योगेन तदा दास्यामि दक्षिणाम् ॥ 50 ॥

इन्युक्तना नृपति: प्राङ्ग पुरं भाष्यां च माध्यांम् ।

राज्यमसी प्रदर्ते वे मया वेधां सुविस्तम् ॥ 51 ॥

If, in time, wealth comes to me, I will at once give you your Daksinā." Saying him thus, the King told his wife Śaivyā and his son Rohita "In this Agnihotra room I say that I have given my vast dominion to the Muni Viśvāmitra."

हस्यश्रधार्यसुवर्ते तनहिंमसपित्वम् ।

त्वक्वा श्रीण शरीराणि सर्वे चास्ये समर्पितम् ॥ 52 ॥

त्वक्वाप्राधोगं याभियाधिम कुत्रधिद्नगढ़े ।

गृह्ञ्तिवं दुनिः सम्यप्राज्यं सर्वेसगृद्धिमत् ॥ 53 ॥

Elephants, horses, chariots, gold and jewels all I have given to him along with my kingdom. What more than this that save us three, everything else I have given to him. "O Maharṣi! Take fully this prosperous dominion; we are going somewhere else to a forest or a mountain cave."

इत्याभाष्य सुतं भार्यां हरिश्रान्नः स्वमंदिता ।

विनिर्भतः सुधामतिमा मानसर्वत द्विज्ञातमम् ॥ 54 ॥

The exceedingly virtus Hairiscandra spoke thus to his wife and son, and, paying respects to the Muni, went out from his house.

ब्रजतं भूपतीं वीक्ष्य भारामुग्रावुभावपि ।

चिन्तातुरी सुतीरामयौ जामनु: श्रुतस्तदा ॥ 55 ॥

Seeing the King going thus away, his wife and son, afflicted with cares, followed him with their sad faces.

हाहाकारो महानासीत्रगारे वीक्ष्य तांतस्था ।

घुळ्गु: ग्राणिनः सर्वे साकेतपुरावासिनः ॥ 56 ॥

Seeing thus, all the inhabitants of Ayodhyā cried aloud, and great consternation and uproar arose in the city.

हा राजन्कि कृतं कर्म कुतः कलेशः: सभागतः ।

बिड्जोरिस महाराज विधिनासपिड्जनेन ॥  57 ॥

O King! What is this act that you have done? How has this suffering come to you! O King! The great Fate, without any consideration, has certainly deceived you.

सर्वे बणीस्तादा दुः:ख्यामानुयुक्तं महीपतिम् ।

विलोक्य भार्यया साधु पुंशेण च महान्यान ॥ ५ ८ ॥

The Brāhmanas, Kṣattriyas, Vaísyas and Śūdras, all the four Varṇas gave vent to their sorrows, when they saw the King going away with his wife and son.

निर्विदुर्बाहणं तं तु दुराचारं भृतीकसः ।

धूर्तीर्यमिति भार्योत दुःखाती बाहाणादयः ॥ ५९ ॥

निराल्य नगरातस्मादिष्यामित्रः क्षितीक्षान् ।

गच्छन्तं तमुवाचेदं समेत्य निरुक्तं वचः ॥ 60 ॥

The Brāhmins and the other inhabitants of the city, all were afflicted with sorrows and began to

abuse the vicious Brāhmaṇa saying that “He is a cheat, etc.”

दक्षिणायाः सुवर्णं मे दत्ता गच्छ नाराधिप ।

नाहं दास्यामि वा बृहि मया तर्चकं सुवर्णकम् ॥ 6 1 ॥

O King! Give the gold for Dakṣinā and then go; or say that you will not be able to give and I will then not take the Dakṣinā.

राज्यं गृहाण वा सर्वे लोपश्छीद्ध विवर्ते ।

दर्तं चेन्मन्यसे राजन्देहि यद्यतप्रतिश्रुतम् ॥ 62 ॥

Or if you entertain within yourself any greed, then take back all your Kingdom. O King! If you think that you have really made this gifts, then give what you have promised."

एवं बुद्रतं गाधेयं हरिश्रान्ने महीपाति: ।

प्राणियन्त सुर्जीन्तात्मा कृतांजलिसुपुटोत्रबीत् ॥ 63 ॥

श्रिति श्रीमदेवीभागवते महापुराणो सतमस्कन्ने

एकोनिरिशोरध्यात: 11 19 11

The son of Gādhī was saying so, when the King Harisācandra very humbly bowed down to him with folded palms and said to him.

Here ends the Nineteenth Chapter of the Seventh Book on the taking away of Harisācandra's Kingdom

in the Mahā Purānam Śrimaddevibhāgavatam of

18,000 verses, by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

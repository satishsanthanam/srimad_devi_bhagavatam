# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 7, CHAPTER 8

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ७.८ अष्टमोऽध्यायः । इक्ष्वाकुवंशवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> जनमेजय उवाच -
> संशयोऽयं महान् ब्रह्मन् वर्तते मम मानसे ।
> ब्रह्मलोकं गतो राजा रेवतीसंयुतः स्वयम् ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Janamejaya said :-- “O Brāhmaṇā! The King was a Kṣattriya; how could he go himself with his daughter Revatī to the Brahmāloka (the abode of Brahmā)? I entertain a great doubt on this point.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 2-3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मया पूर्वं श्रुतं कृत्स्नं ब्राह्मणेभ्यः कथान्तरे ।
> ब्राह्मणो ब्रह्मविच्छान्तो ब्रह्मलोकमवाप्नुयात् ॥ २ ॥
> 
> राजा कथं गतस्तत्र रेवतीसंयुतः स्वयम् ।
> सत्यलोकेऽतिदुष्प्रापे भूर्लोकादिति संशयः ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I heard of yore while conversing about matters connected with the Brāhmins, that the Brāhmin only who was self-restrained and the knower of Brahmāṇ could alone go to the Brahmāloka. The Satyaloka is very hard for the worldly people to go; so I doubt how the king could have gone with Revatī from the Bhūrloka to the Satyaloka.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मृतः स्वर्गमवाप्नोति सर्वशास्त्रेषु निर्णयः ।
> (मानुषेण तु देहेन ब्रह्मलोके गतिः कथं । )
> स्वर्गात्पुनः कथं लोके मानुषे जायते गतिः ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Man, when he discards his body, can go to the Heavens. So is recited in all the Śāstras. How then, people, while, in their human bodies can go to the Brahmāloka.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एतन्मे संस्ययं विद्वंश्छेत्तुमर्हसि साम्प्रतम् ।
> यथा राजा गतस्तत्र प्रष्टुकामः प्रजापतिम् ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

So cut asunder my doubts how the King Revata could go to the Brahmāloka to ask the Prajāpati on certain matters.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 6-7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच -
> मेरोस्तु शिखरे राजन् सर्वे लोकाः प्रतिष्ठिताः ।
> इन्द्रलोको वह्निलोको या च संयमिनी पुरी ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> तथैव सत्यलोकश्च कैलासश्च तथा पुनः ।
> वैकुण्ठश्च पुनस्तत्र वैष्णवं पदमुच्यते ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- “O King! On the top of the mountain Sumeru, are located the Indra’s heavens called Amarāvati (the abode of the Immortals) the Samyamanī city of Yama, the Satyaloka, the Vahniloka, the Kailāsa, Vaikunṭha the abode of Viṣṇu, and others.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 8-9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यथार्जुनः शक्रलोके गतः पार्थो धनुर्धरः ।
> पञ्चवर्षाणि कौन्तेयः स्थितस्तत्र सुरालये ॥ ८ ॥
> 
> मानुषेणैव देहेन वासवस्य च सन्निधौ ।
> तथैवान्येऽपि भूपालाः ककुत्स्थप्रमुखाः किल ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The great archer Arjuṇa, the son of Prithā, went to the Indraloka and spent five years there. In ancient times, the Kings Kakutstha and others went to Indraloka, in their human bodies.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 10-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्वर्लोकगतयः पश्चाद्दैत्याश्चापि महाबलाः ।
> जित्वेन्द्रसदनं प्राप्य संस्थितास्तत्र कामतः ॥ १० ॥
> 
> महाभिषः पुरा राजा ब्रह्मलोकं गतः स्वराट् ।
> आगच्छन्तीं नृपो गङ्गामपश्यच्चातिसुन्दरीम् ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Even the powerful Daityas used to conquer the Indraloka and resided there at their will and pleasure. In ancient times, when the sovereign of the earth, the King Mahābhiṣa went to the Brahmāloka, the most beautiful Gaṅgā also was coming  to the Brahmaloka.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 12-13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वायुनाम्बरमस्यास्तु दैवादपहृतं नृप ।
> किञ्चिन्नग्ना नृपेणाथ दृष्टा सा सुन्दरी तथा ॥ १२ ॥
> 
> स्मितं चकार कामार्तः सा च किञ्चिज्जहास वै ।
> ब्रह्मणा तौ तदा दृष्टौ शप्तौ जातौ वसुन्धराम् ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On the way the King saw Her. O King! Accidentally at that time her clothings were cast aside by the wind; the King saw her partly in her naked state, and, overpowered with lust, smiled; Gaṅgā also smiled. Seeing the states of them, Brahmā instantly cursed them; and they had to come in this world and take their births.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 14-15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वैकुण्ठेऽपि सुराः सर्वे पीडिता दैत्यदानवैः ।
> गत्वा हरिं जगन्नाथमस्तुवन्कमलापतिम् ॥ १४ ॥
> 
> सन्देहो नात्र कर्तव्यः सर्वथा नृपसत्तम ।
> गम्याः सर्वेऽपि लोकाः स्युर्मानवानां नराधिप ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

All the Devas, when oppressed by the Dānavas, went to Vaikunṭha and sang hymns to Hari, the Lord of Kamalā. O King! Men can go to all the Lokas; in fact those high-souled men that perform Yajñas or severe asceticism and thus have acquired great merits, those performers of Sacrifices and ascetics surely go to the Heavens.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अवश्यं कृतपुण्यानां तापसानां नराधिप ।
> पुण्यसद्भाव एवात्र गमने कारणं नृप ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! It is only the abundance of good merits that is the only cause of going to Heavens. So you ought not to entertain any doubts on this.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 17-18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तथैव यजमानानां यज्ञेन भावितात्मनाम् ।
> जनमेजय उवाच -
> रेवतो रेवतीं कन्यां गृहीत्वा चारुलोचनाम् ॥ १७ ॥
> 
> ब्रह्मलोकं गतः पश्चात्किं कृतं तेने भूभुजा ।
> ब्रह्मणा किं समादिष्टं कस्मै दत्ता सुता पुनः ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Janamejaya said :-- “O Muni! The King Revata went with his daughter Revatī to the Brahmāloka; but what did he do when he went there? What did Brahmā order him? And to whom did the King betroth his daughter, when ordered by Brahmā? O Brāhmaṇa! Speak out all these in details to me now.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 19-20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तत्सर्वं विस्तराद्बह्मन् कथय त्वं ममाधुना ।
> व्यास उवाच -
> निशामय महीपाल राजा रेवतकः किल ॥ १९ ॥
> 
> पुत्र्या वरं परिप्रष्टुं ब्रह्मलोकं गतो यदा ।
> आवर्तमाने गान्धर्वे स्थितो लब्धक्षणः क्षणम् ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- “O King! Hear. When the King went to Brahmāloka to ask about the proper bridegroom of his daughter, there was going on singing and music; so he waited a while to find an opportunity when the assembly would have a leisure; but he was so very pleased with music that he could not desist from hearing it till the end.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शृण्वन्नतृप्यद्धृष्टात्मा सभायां तु सकन्यकः ।
> समाप्ते तत्र गान्धर्वे प्रणम्य परमेश्वरम् ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When the music was finished, the King bowed down to Brahmā and shewed him his daughter and informed Him of his intention.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 22-23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दर्शयित्वा सुतां तस्मै स्वाभिप्रायं न्यवेदयत् ।
> राजोवाच -
> वरं कथय देवेश कन्येयं मम पुत्रिका ॥ २२ ॥
> 
> कस्मिंश्चिन्मे मनः कामं नोपतिष्ठति चञ्चलम् ।
> तस्मात्त्वां देवदेवेश प्रष्टुमत्रागतोऽस्म्यहम् ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The King said :-- “O Deva! This good daughter is mine; now kindly say who will be her bridegroom. O Brahmā! To whom shall I betroth this daughter? I have come to you to ask on this point I have searched for many princes and seen also a good many of them and none of them is to my liking and so my mind is not at rest.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 24-25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तदाज्ञापय सर्वज्ञ योग्यं राजसुतं वरम् ।
> कुलीनं बलवन्तं च सर्वलक्षणसंयुतम् ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Lord of the Devas! Therefore I have come to you. Kindly select one bridegroom for her. He is to be a Kulīn (of good family), powerful, religious, liberal, and a prince endowed with all auspicious qualities. This is my prayer.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दातारं धर्मशीलं च राजपुत्रं समादिश ।
> व्यास उवाच -
> तदाकर्ण्य जगत्कर्ता वचनं नृपतेस्तदा ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! Brahmā, the lotus-born, the Creator of the world, hearing these words, laughed, thinking that a very long interval had passed away. He then said :--

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तमुवाच हसन्वाक्यं दृष्ट्वा कालस्य पर्ययम् ।
> ब्रह्मोवाच -
> राजपुत्रास्त्वया राजन् वरा ये हृदये कृताः ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! The princes that you thought would become the bridegroom of your daughter, all died; their sons and grandsons and their friends even have all passed away.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 28-29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ग्रस्ताः कालेन ते सर्वे सपितृपौत्रबान्धवाः ।
> सप्तविंशतिमोऽद्यैव द्वापरस्तु प्रवर्तते ॥ २८ ॥
> 
> वंशजास्ते मृताः सर्वे पुरी दैत्यैर्विलुण्ठिता ।
> सोमवंशोद्भवस्तत्र राजा राज्यं प्रशास्ति हि ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The twenty-seventh Manvantara of the Dvāpara Yuga is now going on; so none of the princes of your family are now existing. The Daityas sacked your city. Now Ugrasena, the King of Mathurā, is reigning in that place.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 30-33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उग्रसेन इति ख्यातो मथुराधिपतिः किल ।
> ययातिवंशसम्भूतो राजा माथुरमण्डले ॥ ३० ॥
> 
> उग्रसेनात्मजः कंसः सुरद्वेषी महाबलः ।
> दैत्यांशः पितरं सोऽपि कारागारं न्यवेशयत् ॥ ३१ ॥
> 
> स्वयं राज्यं चकारासौ नृपाणां मदगर्वितः ।
> मेदिनी चातिभारार्ता ब्रह्माणं शरणं गता ॥ ३२ ॥
> 
> दुष्टराजन्यसैन्यायां भारेणातिसमाकुला ।
> अंशावतरणं तत्र गदितं सुरसत्तमैः ॥ ३३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He belongs to the illustrious lunar family of Yayāti. His son, the powerful Kaṇsa, born of a Dānava, began to do injuries always to the Devas; he threw his own father to the prison. Becoming very haughty, he began to govern himself the countries of other kings and began to tyrranise over the subjects. O King. The Earth became so much troubled by the armies of the wicked Demon Kings, that She became quite unable to bear further loads. So She went to seek refuge to Brahmā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 34-35</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वासुदेवः समुत्पन्नः कृष्णः कमललोचनः ।
> देवक्यां देवरूपिण्यां योऽसौ नारायणो मुनिः ॥ ३४ ॥
> 
> तपश्चचार दुःसाध्यं धर्मपुत्रः सनातनः ।

>
**English Translation:**

Brahmā and the other Devas then began to say :-- “O Earth! To remove your burden the lotus-eyed Nārāyaṇā will incarnate Himself as part incarnation in the form of Śrī Kṛṣṇa. He who is Nārāyaṇā practised in ancient times, as the son of Dharma, a very severe asceticism, in company with his brother Nara in the sacred hermitage of Badari.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सोऽवतीर्णो यदुकुले वासुदेवोऽपि विश्रुतः ।
> तेनासौ निहतः पापः कंसः कृष्णेन सत्तम ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Now this very Deva is born in the great family of Yadu in the womb of Devakī by Vasudeva and is now celebrated by the name of Vāsudeva.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 37</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उग्रसेनाय राज्यं वै दत्तं हत्वा खलं सुतम् ।
> कंसस्य श्वशुरः पापो जरासन्धो महाबलः ॥ ३७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! He has slain that vicious wicked Kanṣa and has installed Ugrasena in his place. The very powerful Jarāsandha, the vicious King of Magadha, is the father-in-law of Kanṣa. On hearing the death of his son-in-law, he became infuriated with rage, came to Mathurā, and raged a terrible war.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 38-39</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> आगत्य मथुरां क्रोधाच्चकार सङ्गरं मुदा ।
> कृष्णेनासौ जितः सङ्ख्ये जरासन्धो महाबलः ॥ ३८ ॥
> 
> प्रेषयामास युद्धाय सबलं यवनं ततः ।
> श्रुत्वाऽऽयान्तं महाशूरं ससैन्यं यवनाधिपम् ॥ ३९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vāsudeva defeated in a battle that Jarāsandha, proud of his mighty valour. Though defeated, Jarāsandha sent Kālayavana with his host of army to fight again with Kṛṣṇa. Bhāgavan Vāsudeva, when he heard that the King of Yavanas arrived, sent away all the members of his family and the Yādavas to Dvārkā and began to wait with his brother Balarāma for the Yavana King. Then he went alone to the camp of Yavana and led him away to a mountain cave where was sleeping the King Muchu Kunda and had then the Yavana King slain by Muchukunda.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> [कृष्णस्तु मथुरां त्यक्त्वा पुरीं द्वारावतीमगात् ।
> प्रभग्नां तां पुरीं कृष्णः शिल्पिभिः सह सङ्गतैः ॥
> 
> कारयामास दुर्गाढ्यां हट्टशालाविमण्डिताम् ।
> जीर्णोद्धारं पुरः कृत्वा वासुदेवः प्रतापवान् ।

> वासुदेवस्तु तत्राद्य वर्तते बान्धवैः सह ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Kṛṣṇa then went to Dvārkā. The city of Dvārkā was then a dilapidated condition. Kṛṣṇa brought together the artists and got built exquisitely the beautiful palaces, forts, and markets and stalls, etc., and so added to the beauty of the place. That Vāsudeva, of mighty prowess, thus improving the city, made Ugrasena the King of that place; and Kṛṣṇa is now waiting there with his friends.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 41</b></summary>

His elder brother Baladeva, the carrier of the plough, is celebrated. Thus he with Musala in his hands is a great warrior and the part incarnation of Ananta Deva.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 43</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दत्त्वा पुत्रीं नृपश्रेष्ठ गच्छ त्वं बदरिकाश्रमम् ।
> तपस्तप्तुं सुरारामं पावनं कामदं नृणाम् ॥ ४३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

After giving your daughter marriage, go to the hermitage of Badari and practise tapasyā. That sacred retreat is the (park) recreation ground of the Devas, holy and yielding to human beings the objects of their desires.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 44-45</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच -
> इति राजा समादिष्टो ब्रह्मणा पद्मयोनिना ।
> जगाम तरसा राजन् द्वारकां कन्ययान्वितः ॥ ४४ ॥
> 
> ददौ तां बलदेवाय कन्यां वै शुभलक्षणाम् ।
> ततस्तप्त्वा तपस्तीव्रं नृपतिः कालपर्यये ॥ ४५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- “O King! Thus ordered by the lotus-born Brahmā, the King went to Dvārkā with his daughter. Reaching there he gave over his all auspicious daughter in marriage to Bala Deva duly according to the rules and regulations. At last, according to Brahmā’s injunction, he became engaged in severe austerities in the Badarikāśram and, when the time of death arrived, left off his mortal coil on the banks of the river and went to the world of Gods.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 46-48</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जगाम त्रिदशावासं त्यक्त्वा देहं सरित्तटे ।
> राजोवाच -
> भगवन्महदाश्चर्यं भवता समुदाहृतम् ॥ ४६ ॥
> 
> रेवतस्तु स्थितस्तत्र ब्रह्मलोके सुतार्थतः ।
> युगानां तु गतं तत्र शतमष्टोत्तरं किल ॥ ४७ ॥
> 
> कन्या वृद्धा न सञ्जाता राजा वातितरां नु किम् ।
> एतावन्तं तथा कालमायुः पूर्णं तयोः कथम् ॥ ४८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Janamejaya said :-- “O Bhagavān! You have uttered one wonderful thing. One hundred and eight Yugas passed away when the King of Revata with his daughter was deeply absorbed in hearing music in the Brahmāloka yet neither the good King nor the daughter did get sufficiently old. How could this be! How could they have lived so long! Were their longevities ordained to be such a long period!”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 49</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच -
> न जरा क्षुपित्पासा वा न मृत्युर्न भयं पुनः ।
> न तु ग्लानिः प्रभवति ब्रह्मलोके सदानघ ॥ ४९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- “O King! The Brahmāloka is not touched by any vice nor sin; old age, hunger, thirst or fear of death nothing exists there; nor is there any other cause by which weariness comes. So what doubt there can be that the people there will be long-lived, free from old age and death!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 50-51</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मेरुं गतस्य शर्यातेः सन्तती राक्षसैर्हता ।
> गताः कुशस्थलीं त्यक्त्वा भयभीता इतस्ततः ॥ ५० ॥

When the King Śaryāti went up to the Heavens, his sons were all destroyed by the Rāksasas; those that remained, they, terrified left Kuśasthalī and fled on all sides. Vaivasvata Manu sneezed; owing to that, came out of his nose one powerful son; his name was Iksāku.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 52-53</b></summary>

He spread the Solar dynasty and became celebrated. Getting excellent initiation from the Maharṣi Nārada, he began to meditate the Devī constantly and practised severe tapasyā for the spread of his race. O King! Iksāku had one hundred sons;

</details>

<details>

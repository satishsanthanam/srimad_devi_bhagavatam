Scriptures

Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam

BOOK 7 • CHAPTER 12
On the description of Vaśiṣṭha’s curse on Triśaṅku
← Previous Chapter ☰ Index Next Chapter →
Ch 12 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
७.१२ द्वादशोऽध्यायः । त्रिशङ्कूपाख्यानवर्णनम् । एवं प्रबोधितः पित्रा त्रिशङ्कुः प्रणतो नृपः । तथेति पितरं प्राह प्रेमगद्गदया गिरा ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Thus giving the advice to his son, the King Triśaṅku was excited with feelings of love and, in a choked voice, said to his father that he would fulfil what he had been ordered.
Ch 12 Verse 2 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विप्रानाहूय मन्त्रज्ञान्वेदशास्त्रविशारदान् । अभिषेकाय सम्भारान् कारयामास सत्वरम् ॥ २ ॥
English Translation
The King then called the Brāhmins, versed in the Vedas and Mantrams, and had all the materials for installation collected quickly.
Ch 12 Verse 3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सलिलं सर्वतीर्थानां समानाय्य विशाम्पतिः । प्रकृतीश्च समाहूय सामन्तान्भूपतींस्तथा ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
He brought the waters from all the sacred places of prigrimages; he then called together with great respect all the kings.
Ch 12 Verse 4 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पुण्येऽह्नि विधिवत्तस्मै ददावासनमुत्तमम् । अभिषिच्य सुतं राज्ये त्रिशङ्कुं विधिवत्पिता ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation
On a sacred day, the father installed his son on the throne and gave him, in accordance with due rites and ceremonies, the royal throne.
Ch 12 Verse 5 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तृतीयमाश्रमं पुण्यं जग्राह भार्यया युतः । वने त्रिपथगाकूले चचार दुश्चरं तपः ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation
The King then adopted with his wife the third Vānaprastha stage of life and practised a severe tapasyā on the banks of the Ganges.
Ch 12 Verse 6 Verse
Sanskrit Text
काले प्राप्ते ययौ स्वर्गं पूजितस्त्रिदशैरपि । इन्द्रासनसमीपस्थो रराज रविवत्सदा ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation
Then in due course of time the King went to the Heavens. There he began to shine like a second Sun by the side of Indra, respected by all the gods.
Ch 12 Verse 7-8 Verse
Sanskrit Text
राजोवाच । पूर्वं भगवता प्रोक्तं कथायोगेन साम्प्रतम् । सत्यव्रतो वसिष्ठेन शप्तो दोग्ध्रीवधात्किल ॥ ७ ॥ कुपितेने पिशाचत्वं प्रापितो गुरुणा ततः । कथं मुक्तः पिशाचत्वादित्येतत्संशयः प्रभो ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
Janamejaya said :-- “O Bhagavān! You spoke before in course of conversation that Satyavrata was cursed by Vaśiṣṭha on the killing of his cow to become a Piśāca; how then he got himself freed of this curse. There is a doubt on this point. Kindly clear it and oblige. Satyavrata was cursed; hence pronounced unfit to succeed to the throne.
Ch 12 Verse 9-10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न सिंहासनयोग्यो हि भवेच्छापसमन्वितः । मुनिना मोचितः शापात्केनान्येन च कर्मणा ॥ ९ ॥ एतन्मे ब्रूहि विप्रर्षे शापमोक्षणकारणम् । आनीतस्तु कथं पित्रा स्वगृहे तादृशाकृतिः ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
How was the Muni, by what actions, was he freed of the curse? How could the father bring back to his home his son of the form of a Piśāca? O Viprarṣi! Kindly narrate to me how the Muni was freed of his curse.”
Ch 12 Verse 11-12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच - वसिष्ठेन च शप्तोऽसौ सद्यः पैशाचतां गतः । दुर्वेषश्चातिदुर्धर्षः सर्वलोकभयङ्करः ॥ ११ ॥ यदैवोपासिता देवी भक्त्या सत्यव्रतेन ह । तया प्रसन्नया राजन् दिव्यदेहः कृतः क्षणात् ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- Cursed by Vaśiṣṭha, Satyavrata became then and there transformed into a Piśāca, very ugly, violent and terrible to all; but when he worshipped the Devī with devotion, immediately the Devī gave him a beautiful divine body.
Ch 12 Verse 13-14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पिशाचत्वं गतं तस्य पापं चैव क्षयं गतम् । विपाप्मा चातितेजस्वी सम्भूतस्तत्कृपामृतात् ॥ १३ ॥ वसिष्ठोऽपि प्रसन्नात्मा जातः शक्तिप्रसादतः । पितापि च बभूवास्य प्रेमयुक्तस्त्वनुग्रहात् ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
By the grace of the Devī, his sins were all washed away and his Piśāca form vanished. Satyavrata, then, freed from his sins became very much vigorous and energetic. Vaśiṣṭha also became pleased with him, blessed thus by the Supreme Force and so was his father, too.
Ch 12 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
राज्यं शशास धर्मात्मा मृते पितरि पार्थिवः । ईजे च विविधैर्यज्ञैर्देवदेवीं सनातनीम् ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
When his father died, the virtuous Satyavrata became King, governed his subjects and performed various sacrifices and worshipped, too, the Eternal Mother of the Gods.
Ch 12 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तस्य पुत्रो बभूवाथ हरिश्चन्द्रः सुशोभनः । लक्षणैः शास्त्रनिर्दिष्टैः संयुतश्चातिसुन्दरः ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
O King! Triśaṅku had a very beautiful son born to him, named Hariścandra, endowed in all his limbs with auspicious signs.
Ch 12 Verse 17 Verse
Sanskrit Text
युवराजं सुतं कृत्वा त्रिशङ्कुः पृथिवीपतिः । मानुषेण शरीरेण स्वर्गं भोक्तुं मनो दधे ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation
The King Triśaṅku wanted to make his son Yuvarāja (the Crown prince) and then in his that very body while living, enjoy the Heavens.
Ch 12 Verse 18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वसिष्ठस्याश्रमं गत्वा प्रणम्य विधिवन्नृपः । उवाच वचनं प्रीतः कृताञ्जलिपुटस्तदा ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
The King went to the Āśrama of Vaśiṣṭha and gladly asked him, with folded palms, bowing down before him duly.
Ch 12 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
राजोवाच - ब्रह्मपुत्र महाभाग सर्वमन्त्रविशारद । विज्ञप्तिं मे सुमनसा श्रोतुमर्हसि तापस ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
O Ascetic! You are the son of Brahmā , versed in all the Vaidik Mantrams; so you are exceedingly fortunate; now I beg to inform you one thing; hear it gladly.
Ch 12 Verse 20 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इच्छा मेऽद्य समुत्पन्ना स्वर्गलोकसुखाय च । अनेनैव शरीरेण भोगान्भोक्तुममानुषान् ॥ २० ॥
English Translation
I now desire to enjoy the happiness of the Heavens and all the enjoyments of the Devas, while I am in this body.
Ch 12 Verse 21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अप्सरोभिश्च संवासः क्रीडितुं नन्दने वने । देवगन्धर्वगानं च श्रोतव्यं मधुरं किल ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
To enjoy in the Nandana Garden, to live with the Apsarās and to hear the sweet music of the Devas and the Gandharbas, these ideas now have taken a strong hold of my heart.
Ch 12 Verse 22 Verse
Sanskrit Text
याजय त्वं मखेनाशु तादृशेन महामुने । यथानेन शरीरेण वसे लोकं त्रिविष्टपम् ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation
Therefore, O Great Muni! Engage me in such a sacrifice as will enable me, in this very body to live in the Svarloka.
Ch 12 Verse 23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
समर्थोऽसि मुनिश्रेष्ठ कुरु कार्यं ममाधुना । प्रापयाशु मखं कृत्वा देवलोकं दुरासदम् ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
O Muni! You are fully competent to do this; therefore be ready for this. Have the sacrifice done and let me have quickly the Devaloka, so difficult to be obtained!
Ch 12 Verse 24-25 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वसिष्ठ उवाच - राजन् मानुषदेहेन स्वर्गे वासः सुदुर्लभः । मृतस्य हि ध्रुवं स्वर्गः कथितः पुण्यकर्मणा ॥ २४ ॥ तस्माद् बिभेमि सर्वज्ञ दुर्लभाच्च मनोरथात् । अप्सरोभिश्च संवासो जीवमानस्य दुर्लभः ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation
Vaśiṣṭha said :-- “O King! It is exceedingly hard to live in the Heavens while in this mortal body. The departed only live in the Heavens by their merits, this is a known fact. Therefore, O Omniscient One! Your desire is hard to be attained. I am afraid of this. O King! The living men can hardly enjoy the Apsarās. Therefore, O Blessed One! Do the sacrifice first. Then, when you leave this body, you will go to the Heavens.”
Ch 12 Verse 26-27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कुरु यज्ञान्महाभाग मृतः स्वर्गमवाप्स्यसि । व्यास उवाच - इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य राजा परमदुर्मनाः ॥ २६ ॥ उवाच वचनं भूयो वसिष्ठं पूर्वरोषितम् । न त्वं याजयसे ब्रह्मन् गर्वावेशाच्च मां यदि ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! The Maharṣi Vaśiṣṭha was already angry with the King; therefore when he spoke these words, the King heard and became absent-minded. He again spoke to the Maharṣi :-- O Brāhmaṇa! If you do not allow me to do the sacrifice, on account of your haughtiness, I will have the sacrifice performed now by another priest.
Ch 12 Verse 28-30 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अन्यं पुरोहितं कृत्वा यक्ष्येऽहं किल साम्प्रतम् । तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्य वसिष्ठः कोपसंयुतः ॥ २८ ॥ शशाप भूपतिं चेति चाण्डालो भव दुर्मते । अनेन त्वं शरीरेण श्वपचो भव सत्वरम् ॥ २९ ॥ स्वर्गकृन्तन पापिष्ठ सुरभीवधदूषित । ब्रह्मपत्नीहरोच्छिन्न धर्ममार्गविदूषक ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation
Vaśiṣṭha became very angry at the words of the King and cursed him :-- “O evilminded One! Be as soon as possible a Cāṇḍāla in this body. You have committed acts by which your path to the Heaven is obstructed. You have stolen a Brāhmini’s wife, and defiled the path of religion; you have killed the Surabhi Cow and you are a libertine. Therefore, O Sinner! Never you will go to the Heavens, even after your death.”
Ch 12 Verse 31-33 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न ते स्वर्गगतिः पाप मृतस्यापि कथञ्चन । व्यास उवाच - इत्युक्तो गुरुणा राजंस्त्रिशङ्कुस्तत्क्षणादपि ॥ ३१ ॥ तत्र तेन शरीरेण बभूव श्वपचाकृतिः । कुण्डलेऽश्ममये वापि जाते तस्य च तत्क्षणात् ॥ ३२ ॥ देहे चन्दनगन्धश्च विगन्धो ह्यभवत्तदा । नीलवर्णेऽथ सञ्जाते दिव्ये पीताम्बरे तनौ ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing these harsh words from the Guru, Triśaṅku became immediately Cāṇḍāla in that very body. His golden earrings became turned into iron; the sweet sandal smell over his body smelled like faeces; his beautiful yellow clothings became blue, the colour of his body became like that of an elephant, due to his curse.
Ch 12 Verse 34 Verse
Sanskrit Text
राजवर्णोऽभवद्देहः शापात्तस्य महात्मनः । शक्त्युपासकरोषेण फलमेतदभून्नृप ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation
O King! Those who are the worshippers of the Supreme Force can produce such things when they are angry; there is not the slightest doubt in this. Therefore one ought never to insult any devotee of the Supreme Force.
Ch 12 Verse 35-36 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तस्माच्छ्रीशक्तिभक्तो हि नावमान्यः कदाचन । गायत्रीजपनिष्ठो हि वसिष्ठो मुनिसत्तमः ॥ ३५ ॥ दृष्ट्वा निन्द्यं निजं देहं राजा दुःखमवाप्तवान् । न जगाम गृहे दीनो वनमेवाभितो ययौ ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation
The Muni Vaśiṣṭha is always engaged in repeating silently the Gāyatrī of the Devī. So what wonder is there that the body of the King will be reduced to such a wretched state by his rage.
Ch 12 Verse 37 Verse
Sanskrit Text
चिन्तयामास दुःखार्तास्त्रिशङ्कुः शोकविह्वलः । किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि देहो मेऽतीव निन्दितः ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation
The King Triśaṅku became very sorry to see his ugly body; he did not go home; rather he remained in the forest in that form and poor dress.
Ch 12 Verse 38 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कर्तव्यं नैव पश्यामि येन मे दुःखसङ्क्षयः । गृहे गच्छामि चेत्पुत्रः पीडितोऽद्य भविष्यति ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation
He began to think, distressed with sorrow and over-powered with misery :-- “My body is now blameable to the extreme, so what to do and where to go in this wretched state! I find no remedy to exhaust all my sufferings.
Ch 12 Verse 39-40 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भार्यापि श्वपचं दृष्ट्वा नाङ्गीकारं करिष्यति । सचिवा नादरिष्यन्ति वीक्ष्य मामीदृशं पुनः ॥ ३९ ॥ ज्ञातयो बन्धुवर्गश्च सङ्गतो न भजिष्यति । सर्वैस्तक्तस्य मे नूनं जीवितान्मरणं वरम् ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation
If I go home, my son will be, no doubt, very much pained with sorrow. My wife, when she will see my Cāṇḍāla appearance, she won’t accept me; my ministers will not regard me as they used to do before.
Ch 12 Verse 41 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विषं वा भक्षयित्वाद्य पतित्वा वा जलाशये । कृत्वा वा कण्ठपाशं च देहत्यागं करोम्यहम् ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation
My friends and relations, when they will come to me, will not serve me with the former care. So it is far better to die than to live, thus despised.
Ch 12 Verse 42 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अग्नौ वा ज्वलिते देहं जुहोमि विधिवद् बलात् । कृत्वा वानशनं प्राणांस्त्यजामि दूषितान्भृशम् ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation
I will drink poison or drown myself in waters or hang myself. Or I will burn myself in the funeral pyre duly or I will quit this blameable life by starvation.
Ch 12 Verse 43-44 Verse
Sanskrit Text
आत्महत्या भवेन्नूनं पुनर्जन्मनि जन्मनि । श्वपचत्वं च शापश्च हत्यादोषाद्भवेदपि ॥ ४३ ॥ पुनर्विचार्य भूपालश्चेतसा समचिन्तयत् । आत्महत्या न कर्तव्या सर्वथैव मयाधुना ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation
But, Alas! I will be guilty of suicide; so again due to this sin I will be born a Cāṇḍāla and I will be again cursed.” Thus thinking, the King again thought that at present he ought not to commit suicide by any means.
Ch 12 Verse 45 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भोक्तव्यं स्वकृतं कर्म देहेनानेन कानने । भोगेनास्य विपाकस्य भविता सर्वथा क्षयः ॥ ४५ ॥
English Translation
“I will have to suffer for my Karma; and, after due suffering, this Karma will be exhausted. So I will suffer in this forest for my Karma in this my body.
Ch 12 Verse 46 Verse
Sanskrit Text
प्रारब्धकर्मणां भोगादन्यथा न क्षयो भवेत् । तस्मान्मयात्र भोक्तव्यं कृतं कर्म शुभाशुभम् ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation
Without the enjoyment of the fruits, the past actions can never die out; therefore all actions done by me, auspicious or inauspicious, I will enjoy or suffer in this place.
Ch 12 Verse 47 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कुर्वन्पुण्याश्रमाभ्याशे तीर्थानां सेवनं तथा । स्मरणं चाम्बिकायास्तु साधूनां सेवनं तथा ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation
Always to remain close to a holy Āśrama, to wander in holy places of pilgrimage, to remember the Devī Ambikā, and to serve the saints will now be my duties.
Ch 12 Verse 48 Verse
Sanskrit Text
एवं कर्मक्षयं नूनं करिष्यामि वने वसन् । भाग्ययोगात्कदाचित्तु भवेत्साधुसमागमः ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation
Thus I will no doubt exhaust all my actions, residing in this forest; then, if chance permits, and if I meet with a saintly person, all my intentions will be crowned with success.”
Ch 12 Verse 49-50 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इति सञ्चिन्त्य मनसा त्यक्त्वा स्वनगरं नृपः । गङ्गातीरे गतः कामं शोचंस्तत्रैव संस्थितः ॥ ४९ ॥ हरिश्चन्द्रस्तदा ज्ञात्वा पितुः शापस्य कारणम् । दुःखितः सचिवांस्तत्र प्रेषयामास पार्थिवः ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation
Thus thinking, the King quitting his city went to the banks of the Ganges and repenting very much, remained there on the Ganges. The King Hariścandra came to know the cause of his father’s curse and with a sorrowful heart sent ministers to him.
Ch 12 Verse 51-52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सचिवास्तत्र गत्वाऽऽशु तमूचुः प्रश्रयान्विताः । प्रणम्य श्वपचाकारं निःश्वसन्तं मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ५१ ॥ राजन् पुत्रेण ते नूनं प्रेषितान्समुपागतान् । अवेहि सचिवांस्त्वं नो हरिश्चन्द्राज्ञया स्थितान् ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation
Like a Cāṇḍāla, the King was respiring frequently; at this time the ministers went to him and bowing humbly, said : -- O King! Your son has ordered us to come here; we have come at his command; we are the ministers of the King Hariścandra.
Ch 12 Verse 53 Verse
Sanskrit Text
युवराजसुतः प्राह यत्तच्छृणु नराधिप । आनयध्वं नृपं यूयं सम्मान्य पितरं मम ॥ ५३ ॥
English Translation
Know this verily, O King! Kindly hear what the Crown Prince has said :-- “Go and bring my Father here without any delay.”
Ch 12 Verse 54 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तस्माद्राजन् समागच्छ राज्यं प्रति गतव्यथः । सेवां सर्वे करिष्यन्ति सचिवाश्च प्रजास्तथा ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation
Therefore, O King! Cast aside your mental agonies and come to the city. The ministers, the subjects all will be always at your service.
Ch 12 Verse 55 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गुरुं प्रसादयिष्यामः स यथा तु दयेत वै । प्रसन्नोऽसौ महातेजा दुःखस्यान्तं करिष्यति ॥ ५५ ॥
English Translation
We will all try our best to please Vaśiṣṭha, so that he may favour you. And that greatly illustrious Muni being pleased will certainly remove your sorrows quickly.
Ch 12 Verse 56 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इति पुत्रेण ते राजन् कथितं बहुधा किल । तस्माद् गमनमेवाशु रोचतां निजसद्मनि ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation
O King! Thus your son has spoken to us many words; so now be pleased to go to your own abode.
Ch 12 Verse 57 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच - इति तेषां नृपः श्रुत्वा भाषितं श्वपचाकृतिः । स्वगृहं गमनायासौ न मतिं कृतवानतः ॥ ५७ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! That Cāndāla-like King, hearing even their words thus, did not consent to go back to his house.
Ch 12 Verse 58 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तानुवाच तदा वाक्यं व्रजन्तु सचिवाः पुरम् । गत्वा पुरं महाभागा ब्रुवन्तु वचनाच्च मे ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation
Rather he told them :-- “Ministers, go back, all of you to the city; and at my word, tell my son that I won’t go back to my house. Better leaving off all idleness, you better govern the Kingdom carefully.
Ch 12 Verse 59-60 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नागमिष्याम्यहं पुत्र कुरु राज्यमतन्द्रितः । मानयन्ब्राह्मणान्देवान्यजन्यज्ञैरनेकशः ॥ ५९ ॥ नाहं श्वपचवेषेण गर्हितेन महात्मभिः । आगमिष्याम्ययोध्यायां सर्वे गच्छन्तु मा चिरम् ॥ ६० ॥
English Translation
Shew your respect specially to the Brāhmins and perform various sacrifices and worship the Devas. I do not like in this blameable Cāṇḍāla form to go to the city of Ayodhyā with the high-souled ones; so you all go back to Ayodhyā without any further delay.
Ch 12 Verse 61 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पुत्रं सिंहासने स्थाप्य हरिश्चन्द्रं महाबलम् । कुर्वन्तु राज्यकर्माणि यूयं तत्र ममाज्ञया ॥ ६१ ॥
English Translation
Install, at my order, my powerful son Hariścandra on the throne and do all these stately duties.”
Ch 12 Verse 62 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इत्यादिष्टास्ततस्ते तु रुरुदुश्चातुरा भृशम् । सचिवा निर्ययुस्तूर्णं नत्वा तं च वनाश्रमात् ॥ ६२ ॥
English Translation
When the ministers heard thus the King ordering them, they began to cry very much, and, bowing down, they went away early out of the hermitage.
Ch 12 Verse 63 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अयोध्यायामुपागत्य पुण्येऽह्नि विधिपूर्वकम् । अभिषेकं तदा चक्रुर्हरिश्चन्द्रस्य मूर्ध्नि ते ॥ ६३ ॥
English Translation
On coming back to Ayodhyā they regularly installed on a sacred day the King Hariścandra with Abhiṣeka water, purified with Mantrams.
Ch 12 Verse 64-65 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अभिषिक्तस्तु तेजस्वी सचिवैश्च नृपाज्ञया । राज्यं चकार धर्मिष्ठः पितरं चिन्तयन्भृशम् ॥ ६४ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां सप्तमस्कन्धे त्रिशङ्कूपाख्यानवर्णनं नाम द्वादशोऽध्यायः ॥ ७.१२॥
English Translation
Thus the powerful virtuous Hariścandra, on being installed on the royal throne by the command of the King, remembered always his father and began to govern his Kingdom with his ministers according to the dictates of Dharma. Here ends the Twelfth Chapter of the Seventh Book on the description of Vaśiṣṭha’s curse on Triśaṅku in the Mahā Purāṇam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

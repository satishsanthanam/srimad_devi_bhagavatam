# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 4, CHAPTER 18

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ४.१८ अष्टादशोऽध्यायः । ब्रह्माणं प्रति विष्णुवाक्यम् ।
> 
> 
> व्यास उवाच ।
> शृणु राजन्प्रवक्ष्यामि कृष्णस्य चरितं महत् ।
> अवतारकारणं चैव देव्याश्चरितमद्भुतम् ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O king! Hear in detail the complete life and the deeds in the incarnation of Śrī Kṛṣṇa and also the various wonderful achievements by the Goddess of this universe.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> धरैकदा भराक्रान्ता रुदती चातिकर्शिता ।
> गोरूपधारिणी दीना भीतागच्छत्त्रिविष्टपम् ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Once on a time, the Earth was very much overburdened by the load of wicked kings and She was therefore very much afraid. 3. She then assumed the appearance of a cow and went to the Devaloka crying and very much dejected.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पृष्टा शक्रेण किं तेऽद्य वर्तते भयमित्यथ ।
> केन वै पीडितासि त्वं किं ते दुःखं वसुन्धरे ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Indra, the lord of the Devas, asked her, O Vasundhare! What is the cause of your fear now? Who has troubled you? What afflictions are you merged in? Please tell me all these.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तच्छ्रुत्वेला तदोवाच शृणु देवेश मेऽखिलम् ।
> दुःखं पृच्छसि यत्त्वं मे भाराक्रान्तोऽस्मि मानद ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On hearing Indra’s words, the Earth exclaimed :-- O Respect giver! When You have asked me, I am explaining to you the cause of all my afflictions and sorrows; at present I am overburdened with too much load.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 5-8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जरासन्धो महापापी मागधेषु पतिर्मम ।
> शिशुपालस्तथा चैद्यः काशिराजः प्रतापवान् ॥ ५ ॥
> 
> रुक्मी च बलवान्कंसो नरकश्च महाबलः ।
> शाल्वः सौभपतिः क्रूरः केशी धेनुकवत्सकौ ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> सर्वे धर्मविहीनाश्च परस्परविरोधिनः ।
> पापाचारा मदोन्मत्ताः कालरूपाश्च पार्थिवाः ॥ ७ ॥
> 
> तैरहं पीडिता शक्र भाराक्रान्ताक्षमा विभो ।
> किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि चिन्ता मे महती स्थिता ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Now is reigning in the earth Jārāsandha, the king of Magadha, a very very vicious person. Thus the other Śiśupāl, the lord of the Chedis, the uncontrollable Kāśirāj, Rukmī, the powerful Kaṃsa, the strong Naraka, the Sauvapati Śālva, the wicked Keśī, Dhenuka, and Batsaka all these are now in royal positions. O Lord of the Devas! These kings are all devoid of the least trace of virtue, quarrelsome against each other, infatuated with vanity, and addicted to vicious deeds. These have become kings as if they were personified Yamas, the Lords of Death, and are constantly troubling me. I am now unable to carry their loads; where shall I go now? This great thought is constantly ailing me.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 9-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पीडिताहं वराहेण विष्णुना प्रभविष्णुना ।
> शक्र जानीहि हरिणा दुःखाद्दुःखतरं गता ॥ ९ ॥
> 
> यतोऽहं दुष्टदैत्येन कश्यपस्यात्मजेन वै ।
> हृताहं हिरण्याक्षेण मग्ना तस्मिन्महार्णवे ॥ १० ॥
> 
> तदा सूकररूपेण विष्णुना निहतोऽप्यसौ ।
> उद्धृताहं वराहेण स्थापिता हि स्थिरा कृता ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Vasava! What to tell! The Bhagavān in His Boar Incarnation is the cause of all these my afflictions; O Indra! These present troubles I am fallen into only through Him; for when the cruel Daitya Hirānyākṣa; the son of Ka’syapa stole me away and drowned me in the great ocean, then it was Viṣṇu in his Boar incarnation that killed him and rescued me from the ocean and then kept me in this my stable position.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नोचेद्रसातले स्वस्था स्थिता स्यां सुखशायिनी ।
> न शक्तास्म्यद्य देवेश भारं वोढुं दुरात्मनाम् ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Had he not then lifted me up, I would have rested safe in the depths of Rasātala; O Lord of the Devas! Now I am quite unable to bear the load of these vicious persons.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अग्रे दुष्टः समायाति ह्यष्टाविंशस्तथा कलिः ।
> तदाहं पीडिता शक्र गन्तास्म्याशु रसातलम् ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Surendra! The vicious twenty eight Kali is coming quickly in front. Thinking of His influence, it seems to me that I will be very troubled then and will have to go down to Rasātala.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्मात्त्वं देवदेवेश दुःखरूपार्णवस्य च ।
> पारदो भव भारं मे हर पादौ नमामि ते ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Therefore, O Lord of the Devas! I am bowing down before Your feet, kindly relieve me of my burden and save me from these endless troubles.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इन्द्र उवाच ।
> इले किं ते करोम्यद्य ब्रह्माणं शरणं व्रज ।
> अहं तत्रागमिष्यामि स ते दुःखं हरिष्यति ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Indra said :-- O Earth! I cannot do anything for you. You better go and take refuge of Brahmā. I am also going to Him. He will remove all your troubles.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तच्छ्रुत्वा त्वरिता पृथ्वी ब्रह्मलोकं गता तदा ।
> शक्रोऽपि पृष्ठतः प्राप्तः सर्वदेवपुरःसरः ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing Indra’s words the Earth hurriedly went to the realm of Brahmā and Indra and all the other Devas followed Her; and all reached the Brahmaloka.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 17-18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सुरभीमागतां तत्र दृष्ट्वोवाच प्रजापतिः ।
> महीं ज्ञात्वा महाराज ध्यानेन समुपस्थिताम् ॥ १७ ॥
> 
> कस्माद्रुदसि कल्याणि किं ते दुःखं वदाधुना ।
> पीडितासि च केन त्वं पापाचारेण भूर्वद ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! The Grand Father Brahmā saw the Earth coming to him and through the power of meditation, found out the cause of Her coming and said :-- O Auspicious One! why are You crying? What troubles You have now? What wicked person has given You troubles.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> धरोवाच ।
> कलिरायाति दुष्टोऽयं बिभेमि तद्भयादहम् ।
> पापाचाराः प्रजास्तत्र भविष्यन्ति जगत्पते ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Earth said :-- O Lord of the Earth! The vicious Kali is coming before; under Its influence the subjects will be horribly vicious; therefore I am very much afraid of this Kali.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> राजानश्च दुराचाराः परस्परविरोधिनः ।
> चौरकर्मरताः सर्वे राक्षसाः पूर्णवैरिणः ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

In the beginning of this Kali Yuga, the ancient enemies, the Asuras have now incarnated on this earth as kings. They will be extremely wicked, quarreling against each other, and will be clever in stealing otherś things. There is no doubt in these.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

>
**English Translation:**

O Grand Father! Now kill these vicious kings and relieve my burden. O Lord! I am very much troubled by the armies of these kings.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रह्योवाच ।
> नाहं शक्तस्तथा देवि भारावतरणे तव ।

>
**English Translation:**

Brahmā said :-- O Goddess! I, too, am unable like Indra to remove your load. Let us go to that Viṣṇu, the Holder of the disc.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

That Janārdana will remove your burden. I thought of this well before and settled what to do.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 24-25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तत्र गच्छ सुरश्रेष्ठ यत्र देवो जनार्दनः ।
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इत्युक्त्वा वेदकर्तासौ पुरस्कृत्य सुरांश्च गाम् ॥ २४ ॥
> 
> जगाम विष्णुसदनं हंसारूढश्चतुर्मुखः ।

>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- Thus saying, the four faced Brahmā, the Author of the Vedas, rode on His Hamsa Vehicle taking in front the Goddess Earth; and the Devas went to Viṣṇu and began to praise Viṣṇu Janārdana, the Deva of the Devas, with the words of the Vedas with full devotion.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रह्मोवाच ।
> सहस्रशीर्षास्त्वमसि सहस्राक्षः सहस्रपात् ।

>
**English Translation:**

Brahmā said :-- Thou hast thousand heads, thousand faces, thousand feet. Thou art the Puruṣa of the Vedas, Thou art the Deva of the Devas, and Thou art Eternal.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 27</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

O Omnipresent! Thou art the Past, Present, and Future! O Lord of Lakṣmī! Thou hast awarded immortality unto us.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

>
**English Translation:**

Thou art the Creator of universe, the Preserver and the Destroyer; Thou art the One and the Only goal and thou art the God. Everybody knows that all these glories belong to Thee.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 29</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O king! When Brahmā praised thus, Viṣṇu whose sign was Garuḍa, was highly pleased and appeared before Brahmā and the other Devas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 30</b></summary>

**English Translation:**

The Bhagavān asked them about their welfare and enquired in detail into the cause of their arrival there.

</details>

<details>

>
**English Translation:**

Brahmā them bowed down to Him and, remembering the cause of the sorrows of the Goddess Earth, said :-- O Lord! Thou art now to relieve the burden of the Earth.

</details>

<details>

**English Translation:**

O Thou, Ocean of mercy! When the end of the Dvāpara yuga will come Thou art to incarnate in the world and kill the wicked kings and thus to relieve the burden of the Earth.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 33-34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विष्णुरुवाच ।
> नाहं स्वतन्त्र एवात्र न ब्रह्मा न शिवस्तथा ।
> नेन्द्रोऽग्निर्न यमस्त्वष्टा न सूर्यो वरुणस्तथा ॥ ३३ ॥
> 
> योगमायावशे सर्वमिदं स्थावरजङ्गमम् ।
> ब्रह्मादिस्तम्बपर्यन्तं ग्रथितं गुणसूत्रतः ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Viṣṇu said :-- I am not independent in these matters; why I? Brahmā Maheśa, Indra, Agni, Yama, Visvakarmā, the Sun and Varuṇa and other Devas, nobody is independent. This whole universe, moving and unmoving is existing under the control of the Yoga Māyā; and from Brahmā up to the clot of grass, all are strung into the thread of Her Three qualities.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 35</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यथा सा स्वेच्छया पूर्वं कर्तुमिच्छति सुव्रत ।
> तथा करोति सुहिता वयं सर्वेऽपि तद्वशाः ॥ ३५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O One of good vows! Whatever that Yoga Māyā, the Supreme Goddess, Who is All will, Whose mouth is inward, Who does good at all times, what She wills She does that at any time. You should all know that we are entirely under Her control.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 36-37</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यद्यहं स्यां स्वतन्त्रो वै चिन्तयन्तु धिया किल ।
> कुतोऽभवं मत्स्यवपुः कच्छपो वा महार्णवे ॥ ३६ ॥
> 
> तिर्यग्योनिषु को भोगः का कीर्तिः किं सुखं पुनः ।

>
**English Translation:**

You better think that were I independent, what for would I have stayed in the great ocean, incarnating in the Fish and Tortoise Bodies! O Brāhmin! What name or pleasure is there in enjoyment in the body of lower animals! What holy merit or any other reward may I expect from being born in the wombs  of lower animals?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कोलो वाथ नृसिंहो वा वामनो वाभवं कुतः ।
> जमदग्निसुतः कस्मात्सम्भवेयं पितामह ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

What is the reason that I assume the body of a Boar? or of a Man-Lion? or of a Dwarf? Why was I born as the son of Jamadagni. Especially why did I, being born of that highsouled Brāhmaṇ Jamadagni, and being the best of the Brāhmins, do the most atrocious act like that of a heartless brute and fill up the lakes with their blood.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 39-40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नृशंसं वा कथं कर्म कृतवानस्मि भूतले ।
> क्षतजैस्तु ह्रदान्सर्वान्पूरयेयं कथं पुनः ॥ ३९ ॥
> 
> तत्कथं जमदग्नेश्च पुत्रो भूत्वा द्विजोत्तमः ।

>
**English Translation:**

Alas! I killed the Kṣatriyas mercilessly; to say nothing more than that I killed the sons that were then in the wombs. Were I independent, what for I would have done these horrible and cruel deeds! O Lord of the Devas! See again.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रामो भूत्वाथ देवेन्द्र प्राविशद्दण्डकं वनम् ।
> पदातिश्चीरवासाश्च जटावल्कलवान्पुनः ॥ ४१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

In my Rāma incarnation I roamed on foot, helpless and without any provision, in the fearful Daṇḍaka forest unfrequented by anybody, wearing clotted hair, bark, rags, like a man who feels no shame, and behaved like a hunter and killed many animals.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 42-44</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> असहायो ह्यपाथेयो भीषणे निर्जने वने ।
> कुर्वन्नाखेटकं तत्र व्यचरं विगतत्रपः ॥ ४२ ॥
> 
> न ज्ञातवान्मृगं हैमं मायया पिहितस्तदा ।
> उटजे जानकीं त्यक्त्वा निर्गतस्तत्पदानुगः ॥ ४३ ॥
> 
> लक्ष्मणोऽपि च तां त्यक्त्वा निर्गतो मत्पदानुगः ।
> वारितोऽपि मयात्यर्थं मोहितः प्राकृतैर्गुणैः ॥ ४४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Being under the delusion of Māyā, I could not make out the real nature of the golden deer; consequently leaving Janakī in the thatched cottage, I went out pursuing the deer. Though repeatedly warned by me not to leave the place, Lakṣmaṇa was moved by the qualities of Prakriti, forsook her and went out on my search.

</details>

Then the hypocrite Rāvaṇa, the king of the Rākṣasas, under the garb of a beggar; stole away by force the daughter of Janaka, who had become very lean on account of sorrows.

I was very much distressed owing to the separation from my dear wife and roamed about weeping sorely in forest and formed friendship with Sugrīva, under the influence of the circumstances.

It was an act of gross injustice on my part to kill Bālī, the king of the monkeys. I freed him from his curse; afterwards, aided by the monkeys, I had to go to Laṅkā.

**English Translation:**

When my younger Lakṣmaṇa and myself were both enchained under the chain of the serpents, Nāgapāśa, and were senseless, the monkeys all were astonished.

**English Translation:**

Then Garuḍa came and freed us the two brothers, from those Nāgapāśas! I considered then what adverse inauspicious circumstances Fate sometimes ordains on our lot.

**English Translation:**

I lost my kingdom, lived in the forest, my father died, Janakī was stolen and I had to suffer extreme troubles in very deadly battles; I could not know what worse fate still awaited for us?

<details>

**English Translation:**

O Suras! What more calamity can you expect to befall any person than that I was from the very first deprived of my kingdoms and wealth, and had to go to the forest with the princess Sītā dwelling in and taking shelter in a dense forest!

</details>

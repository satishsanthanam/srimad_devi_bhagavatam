On chanting the hymns to the Devī

Ch 19 Verse 1-3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
४.१९ एकोनविंशोऽध्यायः । देवान् प्रति देवीवाक्यवर्णनम् । व्यास उवाच । इत्युक्त्वा भगवान्विष्णुः पुनराह प्रजापतिम् । यन्मायामोहितः सर्वस्तत्त्वं जानाति नो जनः ॥ १ ॥ वयं मायावृताः कामं न स्मरामो जगद्गुरुम् । परमं पुरुषं शान्तं सच्चिदानन्दमव्ययम् ॥ २ ॥ अहं विष्णुरहं ब्रह्मा शिवोऽहमिति मोहिताः । न जानीमो वयं धातः परं वस्तु सनातनम् ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
O Brahmā! I am Viṣṇu, I am Brahmā, I am Rudra, thus our I-ness ahamkāra has blinded our eyesight; and we are made unable to recognise That Eternal Highest Self.
Ch 19 Verse 4 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यन्मायामोहितश्चाहं सदा वर्ते परात्मनः । परवान्दारुपाञ्चाली मायिकस्य यथा वशे ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation
As the wooden dolls dance according to the will of the player, the magician, I also am similarly fascinated by the Māyā and am thus incessantly rolling about like a dependent man.
Ch 19 Verse 5-6 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भवतापि तथा दृष्टा विभूतिस्तस्य चाद्भुता । कल्पादौ भवयुक्तेन मयापि च सुधार्णवे ॥ ५ ॥ मणिद्वीपेऽथ मन्दारविटपे रासमण्डले । समाजे तत्र सा दृष्टा श्रुता न वचसापि च ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation
O Brahmā :-- In the beginning of the Kalpa, Maheśvara, You and I saw the wonderful unspeakable form and glory of that Highest Self at the time of Rāsamandala in the Māni Dvīpa where there was the Mandāra Tree and the Devas assembled. Then I also saw that wonderful thing a second time in the Sudhārnava ocean of nectar, and the most wonderful of it is this, that until we were able to See that Form, we did not hear anything of Her before!
Ch 19 Verse 7 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तस्मात्तां परमां शक्तिं स्मरन्त्वद्य सुराः शिवाम् । सर्वकामप्रदां मायामाद्यां शक्तिं परात्मनः ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
Therefore, O Devas! Today do you all remember that Prime Force, the Highest Self, that all beneficent Force that yields all good and auspicious things; That Very Force will fulfill now all your desires.
Ch 19 Verse 8 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । इत्युक्ता हरिणा देवा ब्रह्माद्या भुवनेश्वरीम् । सस्मरुर्मनसा देवीं योगमायां सनातनीम् ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O king! No sooner the Bhagavān Hari addressed thus, Brahmā and the other Devas at once mentally began to meditate on that Eternal Yoga Māyā, the Goddess of the Universe.
Ch 19 Verse 9 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्मृतमात्रा तदा देवी प्रत्यक्षं दर्शनं ददौ । पाशाङ्कुशवराभीतिधरा देवी जपारुणा । दृष्ट्वा प्रमुदिता देवास्तुष्टुवुस्तां सुदर्शनाम् ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation
Being thus meditated, at once appeared before their eyesight the Devī, the Goddess of the Universe, whose colours were like the blood red Javā flower, holding in two of Her hands noose, hook, or goad, while the third hand indicated favour and the fourth hand bade all discard every sort of fear.
Ch 19 Verse 10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवा ऊचुः । ऊर्णनाभाद्यथा तन्तुर्विस्फुलिङ्गा विभावसोः । तथा जगद्यदेतस्या निर्गतं तां नता वयम् ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
As the web comes out of a spider and sparks come out of fire, so this whole Universe comes out of that Goddess. We all bow down before Her with our humble hearts, full of devotion.
Ch 19 Verse 11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यन्मायाशक्तिसङ्कॢप्तं जगत्सर्वं चराचरम् । तां चितं भुवनाधीशां स्मरामः करुणार्णवाम् ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
We all salute to that Goddess of the Universe, Bhuvaneśvarī, under Whose Māyic force this whole Universe, moving and unmoving, is created; Who is All-Intelligence and the Ocean of Mercy.
Ch 19 Verse 12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यदज्ञानाद्भवोत्पत्तिर्यज्ज्ञानाद्भवनाशनम् । संविद्रूपां च तां देवीं स्मरामः सा प्रचोदयात् ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
This world appears real to him who is unable to realise Her Real Essence; and the world drops off as unreal no sooner he realises Her Presence. We all meditate on that All Intelligent Goddess and we all pray to Her that She may grant unto us more strength to meditate Her and Her alone so.
Ch 19 Verse 13 Verse
Sanskrit Text
महालक्ष्यै च विद्महे सर्वशक्त्यै च धीमहि । तन्नो देवी प्रचोदयात् ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation
We all want to know Mahā Lakṣmī, we all meditate on the Omnipotent; May the Goddess grant unto us strength to meditate on Her so.
Ch 19 Verse 14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मातर्नताः स्म भुवनार्तिहरे प्रसीद शन्तो विधेहि कुरु कार्यमिदं दयार्द्रे । भारं हरस्व विनिहत्य सुरारिवर्गं मह्या महेश्वरि सतां कुरु शं भवानि ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
O Thou, the Remover of the world’s afflictions! Best Thou pleased unto us; O Thou, kind hearted! Do this work for us and promote our good. O Thou, Lord of the Earth! Dost Thou relieve the burden of the earth by killing these Asuras and bring on our welfare.
Ch 19 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यद्यम्बुजाक्षि दयसे न सुरान्कदाचित् किं ते क्षमा रणमुखेऽसिशरैः प्रहर्तुम् । एतत्त्वयैव गदितं ननु यक्षरूपं धृत्वा तृणं दह हुताश पदाभिलाषैः ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
O Thou, Lotus-Eyed! If Thou dost not show Thy mercy towards the gods, they will never be able to strike their enemies with their weapons in the battlefield. O Goddess! Thou didst verify the truth of this before, when Thou didst assume the appearance of a Yakṣa and utter the following sentences “O Fire! You burn this blade of grass,” etc. (in the Kena Talavakāra Upanīṣada.)
Ch 19 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कंसः कुजोऽथ यवनेन्द्रसुतश्च केशी बार्हद्रथो बकबकीखरशाल्वमुख्याः । येऽन्ये तथा नृपतयो भुवि सन्ति तांस्त्वं हत्वा हरस्व जगतो भरमाशु मातः ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! Kaṃsa, Bhauma, Kālayavana, Keśī, Jarāsandha, the son of Brihadratha, Vaka, Pūtanā, Khara, Śālva and other vicious kings respectively are dwelling on the earth. Dost Thou better kill them and relieve the burden of the Earth.
Ch 19 Verse 17 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ये विष्णुना न निहताः किल शङ्करेण ये वा विगृह्य जलजाक्षि पुरन्दरेण । ते ते मुखं सुखकरं सुसमीक्षमाणाः सङ्ख्ये शरैर्विनिहता निजलीलया ते ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation
O Lotus-Eyed! Thou hadst killed those Asuras without any effort that were not slain by Viṣṇu or Maheśvara and they ended their lives, while they were beholding Thy pleasing countenance.
Ch 19 Verse 18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शक्तिं विना हरिहरप्रमुखाः सुराश्च ॥ नैवेश्वरा विचलितुं तव देवदेवि । किं धारणाविरहितः प्रभुरप्यनन्तो धर्तुं धराञ्च रजनीशकलावतंसे ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
O Goddess! Holding Moon on Her forehead! These Hari, Hara, Brahmā and the other gods are unable to move a step without the help of Force; nothing to speak any further, the thousand headed King of Serpents is unable to hold this earth even for a moment, were he not provided with the Śakti of upholding this world.
Ch 19 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इन्द्र उवाच । वाचा विना विधिरलं भवतीह विश्वं कर्तुं हरिः किमु रमारहितोऽथ पातुम् । संहर्तुमीश उमयोज्झित ईश्वरः किं ते ताभिरेव सहिताः प्रभवः प्रजेशाः ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
Indra said :-- O Bhagavatī! Would Brahmā be ever able to create this world without the aid of Sarasvatī! Would Viṣṇu, the Deva of the Devas, be ever able to preserve it without the aid of Lakṣmī or would Maheśvara be ever able, to destroy this world without the help of His Umā; no, never! These Devas, the Trinity, are able to take their respective parts in the keeping up of the world simply because they are united with the forces, incarnate in Sarasvatī, etc., who are again parts of Thee.
Ch 19 Verse 20 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विष्णुरुवाच । कर्तुं प्रभुर्न द्रुहिणो न कदाचनाहं नापीश्वरस्तव कलारहितस्त्रिलोक्याः । कर्तुं प्रभुत्वमनघेऽत्र तथा विहर्तुं त्वं वै समस्तविभवेश्वरि भासि नूनम् ॥ २० ॥
English Translation
Viṣṇu said :-- O Vimale! Were we deprived of Thy Force, the Creator would never have been able to create the world, I would never have been able to preserve it and Maheśvara would never have been able to destroy. Therefore O Devī! Thou alone art reigning as the Supreme Goddess amidst all the glories in the creation, preservation, and destruction of this Universe.
Ch 19 Verse 21-22 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । एवं स्तुता तदा देवी तानाह विबुधेश्वरान् । किं तत्कार्यं वदन्त्वद्य करोमि विगतज्वराः ॥ २१ ॥ असाध्यमपि लोकेऽस्मिंस्तत्करोमि सुरेप्सितम् । शंसन्तु भवतां दुःखं धरायाश्च सुरोत्तमाः ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O king! Thus praised by the Gods, the Goddess then addressed them :-- O Devas! Let all anxieties leave you all; I will do what the Devas have desired, even if it be very difficult to do in this world; now explain what is troubling the Goddess Earth.
Ch 19 Verse 23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवा ऊचुः । वसुधेयं भराक्रान्ता सम्प्राप्ता विबुधान्प्रति । रुदती वेपमाना च पीडिता दुष्टभूभुजैः ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
The Devas replied :-- The wicked kings are troubling very much this Earth. She cannot any longer bear their burden; therefore She came crying unto us.
Ch 19 Verse 24 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भारापहरणं चास्याः कर्तव्यं भुवनेश्वरि । देवानामीष्मित कार्यमेतदेवाधुना शिवे ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation
O Bhuvaneśvarī! Now it is Your duty to remove this load of earth. O Auspicious One! Know this, the desire of the gods.
Ch 19 Verse 25-27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
घातितस्तु पुरा मातस्त्वया महिषरूपभृत् । दानवोऽतिबलाक्रान्तस्तत्सहायाश्च कोटिशः ॥ २५ ॥ तथा शुम्भो निशुम्भश्च रक्तबीजस्तथापरः । चण्डमुण्डौ महावीर्यौ तथैव धूम्रलोचनः ॥ २६ ॥ दुर्मुखो दुःसहश्चैव करालश्चाति वीर्यवान् । अन्ये च बहवः क्रूरास्त्वयैव च निपातिताः ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! In days of yore, You have killed the most powerful Dānava named Mahiṣa, attended by lakhs and lakhs of Dānavas and Daityas; What to speak! You have slain Śumbha, Niśumbha, Raktavīja, the very powerful Caṇḍa Munḍa, Dhumralocana, Durmukha, Duhsana, the strong Karāla and many other cruel Dānavas.
Ch 19 Verse 28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तथैव च सुरारींश्च जहि सर्वान्महीश्वरान् । (भारं हर धरायाश्च दुर्धरं दुष्टभूभुजां ।)॥ व्यास उवाच । इत्युक्ता सा तदा देवी देवानाहाम्बिका शिवा ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
Now, similarly kill the wicked kings, the enemies of the Gods and save the Earth from their heavy loads.
Ch 19 Verse 28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तथैव च सुरारींश्च जहि सर्वान्महीश्वरान् । (भारं हर धरायाश्च दुर्धरं दुष्टभूभुजां ।)॥ व्यास उवाच । इत्युक्ता सा तदा देवी देवानाहाम्बिका शिवा ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- Thus addressed by the Devas, the Most Auspicious One, the Devī, whose colour was black and who looked askance, said smiling in a tone, grave and deep, like that of the rumbling of a raincloud.
Ch 19 Verse 29 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सम्प्रहस्यासितापाङ्गी मेघगम्भीरया गिरा । श्रीदेव्युवाच । मयेदं चिन्तितं पूर्वमंशावतरणं सुराः ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation
O Suras! I have already thought over how the burden of those wicked kings, the parts incarnate of Asuras, can be removed.
Ch 19 Verse 30 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भारावतरणं चैव यथा स्याद्दुष्टभूभुजाम् । मया सर्वे निहन्तव्या दैत्येशा ये महीभुजः ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation
I will, by My own power, rob them of their powers, of that highly prosperous and affluent Jārasandha, the king of Magadha, and others, the parts incarnate of the wicked Asuras, that are now shining on the Earth.
Ch 19 Verse 31 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मागधाद्या महाभागाः स्वशक्त्या मन्दतेजसः । भवद्भिरपि स्वैरंशैरवतीर्य धरातले ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation
O Devas! You would all better go down and incarnate yourselves as part incarnations, impregnated with My power, and help thus in the removal of the load of this earth.
Ch 19 Verse 32 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मच्छक्तियुक्तैः कर्तव्यं भारावतरणं सुराः । कश्यपो भार्यया सार्धं दिविजानां प्रजापतिः ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation
The Deva Prajāpati Maharṣi Kaśyapa would first of all go with his his wife and incarnate as Ānaka Dundubhi Vasudeva, in the race of Yadu.
Ch 19 Verse 33 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यादवानां कुले पूर्वं भविताऽऽनकदुन्दुभिः । तथैव भृगुशापाद्वै भगवान्विष्णुरव्ययः ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation
The Imperishable Bhagavān Viṣṇu will also incarnate as part, owing to the curse of Bhṛgu, as the son of Vasudeva.
Ch 19 Verse 34 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अंशेन भविता तत्र वसुदेवसुतो हरिः । तदाहं प्रभविष्यामि यशोदायां च गोकुले ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation
O Devas! I will be born, then, in the womb of Yaśodā, in Gokula and will accomplish all the ends of the Gods.
Ch 19 Verse 35 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कार्यं सर्वं करिष्यामि सुराणां सुरसत्तमाः । कारागारे गतं विष्णुं प्रापयिष्यामि गोकुले ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation
I will send Viṣṇu from the prison to the Gokula; also I will transfer Anāntā Deva from the womb of Devakī to the womb of Rohin?
Ch 19 Verse 36 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शेषं च देवकीगर्भात्प्रापयिष्यामि रोहिणीम् । मच्छक्त्योपचितौ तौ च कर्तारौ दुष्टसङ्क्षयम् ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation
They two will grow, day by day, by My Force and at the end of the Dvāpara Yuga, will no doubt kill the wicked kings.
Ch 19 Verse 37-38 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दुष्टानां भूभुजां कामं द्वापरान्ते सुनिश्चितम् । इन्द्रांशोऽप्यर्जुनः साक्षात्करिष्यति बलक्षयम् ॥ ३७ ॥ धर्मांशोऽपि महाराजो भविष्यति युधिष्ठिरः । वाय्वंशो भीमसेनश्चाश्विन्यंशौ च यमावपि ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation
Arjuṇa too, the part incarnate of Indra, will destroy the armies of those kings. Yudhiṣṭhira, the part incarnate of Dharma, Bhīmsena, that of of Vāyu, Nakula and Sahadeva the incarnate of the two A’svins, and Bhīsma, the incarnate of Vasu as the son of the Gaṅgā, will take their respective births and exhaust their armies.
Ch 19 Verse 39 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वसोरंशोऽथ गाङ्गेयः करिष्यति बलक्षयम् । व्रजन्तु च भवन्तोऽद्य धरा भवतु सुस्थिरा ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation
O Devas! Now be calm and quiet and go. Let the Earth be also calm and quiet; be confident that I will certainly remove the load of this earth.
Ch 19 Verse 40 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भारावतरणं नूनं करिष्यामि सुरोत्तमाः । कृत्वा निमित्तमात्रांस्तान्स्वशक्त्याहं न संशयः ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation
I will make the above mentioned Gods as my instruments merely and I will destroy, by My own force, the Kṣatriyas in the battlefield of Kurukṣettra.
Ch 19 Verse 41 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कुरुक्षेत्रे करिष्यामि क्षत्त्रियाणां च सङ्क्षयम् । असूयेर्ष्या मतिस्तृष्णा ममताभिमता स्पृहा ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation
Malice, jealousy, foul intention, desire, attachment, vanity, covetousness, desire for victory, lust, and fascination are the vices that will destroy the Yādavas.
Ch 19 Verse 42 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जिगीषा मदनो मोहो दोषैर्नक्ष्यन्ति यादवाः । ब्राह्मणस्य च शापेन वंशनाशो भविष्यति ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation
The race of Yadu will end owing to the curse of a Brāhmaṇa. The Bhagavān also will leave His mortal coil due to a curse.
Ch 19 Verse 43 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भगवानपि शापेन त्यक्ष्यत्येतत्कलेवरम् । भवन्तोऽपि निजाङ्गैश्च सहायाः शार्ङ्गधन्वनः ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation
Now go and incarnate in your respective parts, with your wives, in Gokula and Mathurā, and be helpers in the work of the Lord.
Ch 19 Verse 44-45 Verse
Sanskrit Text
प्रभवन्तु सनारीका मथुरायां च गोकुले । व्यास उवाच । इत्युक्त्वान्तर्दधे देवी योगमाया परात्मनः ॥ ४४ ॥ सधरा वै सुराः सर्वे जग्मुः स्वान्यालयानि च । धरापि सुस्थिरा जाता तस्या वाक्येन तोषिता ॥ ४५ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- Saying thus, the Devī the Goddess of the Universe, the Māyā Incarnate of the Highest Self disappeared. The Devas and the Earth went to their respective places. O king Janamejaya! the Goddess Earth was pleased with the Devī’s words, and being quiet, remained surrounded with various medicinal plants and creepers.
Ch 19 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इन्द्र उवाच । वाचा विना विधिरलं भवतीह विश्वं कर्तुं हरिः किमु रमारहितोऽथ पातुम् । संहर्तुमीश उमयोज्झित ईश्वरः किं ते ताभिरेव सहिताः प्रभवः प्रजेशाः ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
Then the subjects felt peace and pleasure, the Brāhmaṇs also grew stronger in happiness and prosperity, and the Munis became glad and began to perform their religious acts with fresh zeal and alacrity. Here ends the Nineteenth Chapter in the Fourth Book of Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses, the Mahā Purāṇam, on chanting the hymns to the Devī by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

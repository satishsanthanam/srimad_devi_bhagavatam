# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 4, CHAPTER 25

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ४.२५ पञ्चविंशोऽध्यायः । पराशक्तेः सर्वज्ञत्वकथनम् ।
> 
> 
> राजोवाच ।
> सन्देहो मे मुनिश्रेष्ठ जायते वचनात्तव ।
> वैष्णवांशे भगवति दुःखोत्पत्तिं विलोक्य च ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The King said :-- O Best of Munis! Hearing these sorrows of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the part incarnate of Viṣṇu Bhagavān, I am in doubt on your utterances.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नारायणांशसम्भूतो वासुदेवः प्रतापवान् ।
> कथं स सूतिकागाराद्धृतो बालो हरेरपि ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Behold! Bhagavān Vāsudeva is the part incarnate of Nārāyaṇa; how could the Asura Śambara steal away His son from the lying-in-chamber!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सुगुप्तनगरे रम्ये गुप्तेऽथ सूतिकागृहे ।
> प्रविश्य तेन दैत्येन गृहीतोऽसौ कथं शिशुः ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The beautiful Dvārakā city is specially well-guarded; the lying-in-chamber is again within the centre of that; under these circumstances, how was it that the Daitya could enter there and steal away the child!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> न ज्ञातो वासुदेवेन चित्रमेतन्ममाद्भुतम् ।
> जायते महदाश्चर्यं चित्ते सत्यवतीसुत ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Son of Satyavatī! How was it that Vāsudeva could not know that! This appears very strange to me!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रूहि तत्कारणं ब्रह्मन्न ज्ञातं केशवेन यत् ।
> हरणं तत्रसंस्थेन शिशोर्वा सूतिकागृहात् ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Brāhmaṇa! Please explain to me why was that child stolen away from the lying-in-chamber, though Śrī Kṛṣṇa was staying there at the city; and how was it that he was not able to know this beforehand!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> माया बलवती राजन्नराणां बुद्धिमोहिनी ।
> शाम्भवी विश्रुता लोके को वा मोहं न गच्छति ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! The Māyā called Śāmbhavī (Pārvatī’s) is the cause; it fascinates and deludes the minds of human beings. Thus it is known to us. Who is there in this world that is not deluded by this Māyā?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मानुषं जन्म सम्प्राप्य गुणाः सर्वेऽपि मानुषाः ।
> भवन्ति देहजा कामं न देवा नासुरास्तदा ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Jīvas, no sooner they are born as human beings, are immediately overcome with human qualities; the Deva or Asura qualities or their natures do not then visibly exist.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 8-9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> क्षुत्तृण्निद्रा भयं तन्द्रा व्यामोहः शोकसंशयः ।
> हर्षश्चैवाभिमानश्च जरामरणमेव च ॥ ८ ॥
> 
> अज्ञानं ग्लानिरप्रीतिरीर्ष्यासूया मदः श्रमः ।
> एते देहभवा भावाः प्रभवन्ति नराधिप ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Hunger, thirst, sleep, fear, lassitude, delusion, sorrow, doubt, pleasure, egoism, old age, disease, death, non-knowledge, knowledge, displeasure, envy, jealousy, pride and weariness; all these human qualities are seen to exist in human embodiments.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 10-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यथा हेममृगं रामो न बुबोध पुरोगतम् ।
> जानक्या हरणञ्चैव जटायुमरणं तथा ॥ १० ॥
> 
> अभिषेकदिने रामो वनवासं न वेद च ।
> तथा न ज्ञातवान् रामः स्वशोकान्मरणं पितुः ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Behold! The night wanderer Rākṣasa Mārica assumed, by his Māyā, the form of a golden deer and came before Śrī Rāmcandra; and Rāmcandra was not the least aware of it. Then the stealing away of Sītā, the death of Jaṭāyu, Rāma’s going to the forest on the very day of his installation to the throne of Ayodhyā; the death of his father due to his bereavement, all these Śrī Rāmcandra did not know a bit beforehand.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अज्ञवद्विचचारासौ पश्यमानो वने वने ।
> जानकीं न विवेदाथ रावणेन हृतां बलात् ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When Rāvaṇa stole away Jānakī and carried her by force Rāma did not know this before or after that event had happened. He wandered from forest to forest in search of Her, like a quite ignorant man.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सहायान् वानरान्कृत्वा हत्वा शक्रसुतं बलात् ।
> सागरे सेतुबन्धञ्च कृत्वोत्तीर्य सरित्पतिम् ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Afterwards He killed Bāli, the son of Indra and with the help of the monkeys, erected a bridge across the ocean, and, crossing it, went to Laṅkā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> प्रेषयामास सर्वासु दिक्षु तान्कपिकुञ्जरान् ।
> सङ्ग्रामं कृतवान्घोरं दुःखं प्राप रणाजिरे ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He sent the chief monkeys to all the quarters in search of Sītā and had to undergo all the troubles of deadly battles in the great battlefield.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> बन्धनं नागपाशेन प्राप रामो महाबलः ।
> गरुडान्मोक्षणं पश्चादन्वभूद्रघुनन्दनः ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The most powerful Raghunandana was tied down by Nāgapāśa (snakes) and was afterwards freed from it by Garuḍa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अहनद्रावणं सङ्ख्ये कुम्भकर्णं महाबलम् ।
> मेघनादं निकुम्भञ्च कुपितो रघुनन्दनः ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then, being furiously enraged, the great Rāghava slew Kumbhakarṇa, Nikumbha, Megha Nāda and Rāvaṇa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अदूष्यत्वञ्च जानक्या न विवेद जनार्दनः ।
> दिव्यञ्च कारयामास ज्वलितेऽग्नौ प्रवेशनम् ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Janārdan Rāmcandra was not aware of the innocence of Sītā; and therefore He made her take an oath about the purity of Her character and even made Her undergo an ordeal of fire.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> लोकापवादाच्च परं ततस्तत्याज तां प्रियाम् ।
> अदूष्यां दूषितां मत्वा सीतां दशरथात्मजः ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Afterwards Rāmacandra, the son of Daśaratha, had to banish his dear blameless Sītā on the mere ground of bad name, imputed to her by some ignorant person and that he would be thus blamed by the public.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> न ज्ञातौ स्वसुतौ तेन रामेण च कुशीलवौ ।
> मुनिना कथितौ तौ तु तस्य पुत्रौ महाबलौ ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He did not know that Kuśī and Lava were His two sons, born in the forest. Afterwards when the Muni Vālmikī told him, He came to know of them.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पातालगमनं चैव जानक्या ज्ञातवान्न च ।
> राघवः कोपसंयुक्तो भ्रातरं हन्तुमुद्यतः ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Behold also Rāmacandra could not know about the departure of Sītā to Pātāla; getting angry once He was about to kill his brother Lakṣmaṇa even.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कालस्यागमनञ्चैव न विवेद खरान्तकः ।
> मानुषं देहमाश्रित्य चक्रे मानुषचेष्टितम् ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Rāma, the slayer of the Rākṣ’asa Khara did not know that Kāla Puruṣa was coming to him. He, incarnating in the human body, did acts all becoming to a man. Similarly Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the descendant of Yadu, taking human birth did acts all like a man. What more discussion can there be in this?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तथैव मानुषान्भावान्नात्र कार्या विचारणा ।
> पूर्वं कंसभयात्प्राप्तो गोकुले यदुनन्दनः ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Lo! From the very outset He fled, out of fear of Kaṃsa, to Gokula; afterwards he fled out of fear of Jarāsandha to the Dvārkā city.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जरासन्धभयात्पश्चाद् द्वारवत्यां गतो हरिः ।
> अधर्मं कृतवान्कृष्णो रुक्मिण्या हरणञ्च यत् ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Knowing all the rites and ceremonies of the Sanātan Dharma (the Eternal Religion) He stole away Rukmiṇī who was chosen as bride elect by Śiśupāla. This act was very unreligious of Him.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शिशुपालवृतायाश्च जानन्धर्मं सनातनम् ।
> शुशोच बालकं कृष्णः शम्बरेण हृतं बलात् ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Śambara Daitya stole away his newly born child and Kṛṣṇa lamented for this. Afterwards on coming to know of the real state of things from the Goddess Bhagavatī, He was very glad. Therefore it can be easily seen from all these circumstances that He had to yield to pleasures and to undergo remorse like ordinary human beings.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मुमोद जानन्पुत्रं तं हर्षशोकयुतस्ततः ।
> सत्यभामाज्ञया यत्तु युयुधे स्वर्गतः किल ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Again, under the orders of his wife Satyabhāmā, He had to go to Heaven to bring the Pārijāta tree and He had to fight with Indra. This shows clearly that He was under the subjection of His wife.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इन्द्रेण पादपार्थं तु स्त्रीजितत्वं प्रकाशयन् ।
> जहार कल्पवृक्षं यः पराभूय शतक्रतुम् ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

In that battle Hari with disc in hand defeated Indra; the Lord of the Devas, took away the Kalpa tree and retained the prestige of His respected wife (whom He had offended).

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मानिनीमानरक्षार्थं हरिश्चित्रधरः प्रभुः ।
> बद्ध्वा वृक्षे हरिं सत्या नारदाय ददौ पतिम् ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Again Satyabhāmā tied down Hari against a tree and presented Him as a gift to Nārada; afterwards she, the passionate woman, freed Kṛṣṇa on paying an equivalent of gold coins.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 28-29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दत्त्वाथ कानकं कृष्णं मोचयामास भामिनी ।
> दृष्ट्वा पुत्रान्पुरुगुणान्प्रद्युम्नप्रमुखानथ ॥ २८ ॥
> 
> कृष्णं जाम्बवती दीना ययाचे सन्ततिं शुभाम् ।
> स ययौ पर्वतं कृष्णस्तपस्याकृतनिश्चयः ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On seeing Rukmiṇī’s many sons, Pradyūmna and others, all qualified with diverse qualifications, His wife Jāmbavatī prayed to Śrī Kṛṣṇa with humility, so that she may have also many beautiful sons. For her sake, Kṛṣṇa firmly resolved to practise tapasyā and went to the place where the great devotee of Śiva, Upamanyu, was staying.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उपमन्युर्मुनिर्यत्र शिवभक्तः परन्तपः ।
> उपमन्युं गुरु कृत्वा दीक्षां पाशुपतो हरिः ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hari desiring to have sons engaged Upamanyu as His spiritual guide and obtained from him the Mantram called Pāśupata Mantra and became a Dundee (holder of a staff) and shaved His head.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 31-32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जग्राह पुत्रकामस्तु मुण्डी दण्डी बभूव ह ।
> उग्रं तत्र तपस्तेपे मासमेकं फलाशनः ॥ ३१ ॥
> 
> जजाप शिवमन्त्रं तु शिवध्यानपरो हरिः ।
> द्वितीये तु जलाहारस्तिष्ठन्नेकपदा हरिः ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

In the first month He subsisted on fruits only and meditated on Śiva and repeated silently the Śiva mantra. Thus He practised very severe austerities. In the second month He subsisted on water only and stood on only one leg. In the third month he lived on air only and stood on the end of His great toe.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 33-36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तृतीये वायुभक्षस्तु पादाङ्गुष्ठाग्रसंस्थितः ।
> षष्ठे तु भगवान् रुद्रः प्रसन्नो भक्तिभावतः ॥ ३३ ॥
> 
> दर्शनञ्च ददौ तत्र सोमः सोमकलाधरः ।
> आजगाम वृषारूढः सुरैरिन्द्रादिभिर्भूतः ॥ ३४ ॥
> 
> ब्रह्मविष्णुयुतः साक्षाद्यक्षगन्धर्वसेवितः ।
> सम्बोधयन्वासुदेवं शङ्करस्तमुवाच ह ॥ ३५ ॥
> 
> तुष्टोऽस्मि कृष्ण तपसा तवोग्रेण महामते ।
> ददामि वाच्छितान्कामान्ब्रूहि यादवनन्दन ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus time passed away. In the sixth month the God Rudra, holding Moon on His forehead, was pleased with His asceticism and devotion and appeared before Him on that spot. The God Mahā Deva came on a bull; He was attended by Brahmā and Viṣṇu, Indra and the other Devas, Yakshas and Gandarbhas and addressed thus :-- “O high minded Kṛṣṇa of Yadu’s descent; I am pleased with Your severe asceticism; now ask Your desired boon; I will grant it just now. I fulfil all the desires of all my devotees; what desire, then, there can be that is not fulfilled, when I am seen by the devotees!”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 37-38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मयि दृष्टे कामपूरे कामशेषो न सम्भवेत् ।
> व्यास उवाच ।
> तं दृष्ट्वा शङ्करं तुष्टं भगवान्देवकीसुतः ॥ ३७ ॥
> 
> पपात पादयोस्तस्य दण्डवत्प्रेमसंयुतः ।
> स्तुतिं चकार देवेशो मेघगम्भीरया गिरा ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- The son of Devakī was very glad to see the God Śamkara and fell prostrate at His feet. Then that eternal supreme God of the Devas began to recite hymns in praise of Him in a tone as deep as the rumbling of a cloud.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 39</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्थितस्तु पुरतः शम्भोर्वासुदेवः सनातनः ।
> श्रीकृष्ण उवाच ।
> देवदेव जगन्ताथ सर्वभूतार्तिनाशन ॥ ३९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Kṛṣṇa said :-- O Deva of the Devas! O Lord of the world! You alone destroy the misfortunes and sorrows of all the beings. O Destroyer of Asuras! You are the Cause and Creator of this universe. I salute Thee.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विश्वयोने सुरारिघ्न नमस्त्रैलोक्यकारक ।
> नीलकण्ठ नमस्तुभ्यं शूलिने ते नमो नमः ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O One having a blue throat! I bow down to Thee! O Holder of trident! I again and again salute Thee! O Lord of Pārvatī! You destroyed Dakṣa’s sacrifice. I salute Thee.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शैलजावल्लभायाश्च यज्ञघ्नाय नमोऽस्तु ते ।
> धन्योऽहं कृतकृत्योऽहं दर्शनात्तव सुव्रत ॥ ४१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I am blessed by Thy sight and think myself as having discharged all my duties and satisfied. O Virtuous One! My human birth is crowned with success by saluting Thy feet.

</details>

<details>

> इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां
> चतुर्थस्कन्धे पराशक्तेः सर्वज्ञत्वकथनं नाम पञ्चविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ४.२५॥
>
**English Translation:**

Sūta said :-- “O Ṛṣis! This Srī Mad Bhāgavatam, called otherwise the Fifth Purāṇam was recited, in detail, in days of yore by Vyāsa. Whatsoever I heard from him, I have now told exactly the same to you.” Here ends the 25th Chapter in the Fourth Skandha of Srī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam, the Mahā Purāṇam, of 18,000 verses, by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa on the Devī’s Highest Supremacy. Note :-- The best mantra is the whole hearted devotion to one’s Guru, and devotion and surrender of one’s Self to the Supreme Mother, doing works without attachment to the fruits thereof. This will lead to dispassion and Renunciation. To one who is faithful in this, all the other mantras will be duly revealed and all his desires will be found to be true and fulfilled.

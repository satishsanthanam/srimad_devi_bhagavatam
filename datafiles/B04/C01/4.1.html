Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam

BOOK 4 • CHAPTER 1
On the questions put by Janamejaya regarding Kṛṣṇa’s incarnation
← Previous Chapter ☰ Index Next Chapter →
Ch 1 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
४.१ प्रथमोऽध्यायः । जनमेजयप्रश्नाः । जनमेजय उवाच । वासवेय मुनिश्रेष्ठ सर्वज्ञाननिधेऽनघ । प्रष्टुमिच्छाम्यहं स्वामिन्नस्माकं कुलवर्धन ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Janamejaya said :-- O Vāsaveya! the Chief amongst the Munis, the Ocean of all knowledge, O the Sinless One! the Lord and Well Wisher of our families, I come to you with a mind to ask you certain questions. * Vāsavī is the name of the mother of Vyāsa.
Ch 1 Verse 2-3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शूरसेनसुतः श्रीमान्वसुदेवः प्रतापवान् । श्रुतं मया हरिर्यस्य पुत्रभावमवाप्तवान् ॥ २ ॥ देवानामपि पूज्योऽभून्नाम्ना चानकदुन्दुभिः । कारागारे कथं बद्धः कंसस्य धर्मतत्परः ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
I heard of yore, but I do not know why was that illustrious son of Sūrasena, that powerful Ānakadundubhi (1), the pious and illustrious Vasudeva, the father of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the incarnation of the God Hari Himself, and who was worshipped even by the Gods, thrown into prison by Kaṃsa? Note :-- (1) Ānakadundubhi is the epithet of Vasudeva, father of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, since at Vasudeva’s birth, drums called Ānakas and Dundubhis were resounded in the sky.
Ch 1 Verse 4-5 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवक्या भार्यया सार्धं किमागः कृतवानसौ । देवक्या बालषट्कस्य विनाशश्च कृतः पुनः ॥ ४ ॥ तेन कंसेन कस्माद्वै ययातिकुलजेन च । कारागारे कथं जन्म वासुदेवस्य वै हरेः ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation
What faults had he and his wife Devakī committed? Why that Kaṃsa the descendant of Yayāti, killed the six infant sons of Devakī? And for what reason did the God Śrī Hari incarnate Himself as the son of Vasudeva in the prison house of Kaṃsa?
Ch 1 Verse 6 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गोकुले च कथं नीतो भगवान्सात्वतां पतिः । गतो जन्मान्तरं कस्मात्पितरौ निगडे स्थितौ ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation
How was that Lord of the Universe, Śrī Bhagavān, and the Ruler of the Yādava clan, taken to Gokula? Why was He, born of a Kṣattriya family, generally recognised as of the Gopāla (cow-herd) clan?
Ch 1 Verse 7-8 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवकीवसुदेवौ च कृष्णस्यामिततेजसः । कथं न मोचितौ वृद्धौ पितरौ हरिणामुना ॥ ७ ॥ जगत्कर्तुं समर्थेन स्थितेन जनकोदरे । प्राक्तनं किं तयोः कर्म दुर्विज्ञेयं महात्मभिः ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
Why were His father Vasudeva and mother Devakī thrown into prison? And why did not Śrī Kṛṣṇa, of indomitable prowess, and capable to create and preserve the worlds, could release instantly, father and mother from their imprisoned state?
Ch 1 Verse 9-10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जन्म वै वासुदेवस्य यत्रासीत्परमात्मनः । के ते पुत्राश्च का बाला या कंसेन विपोथिता ॥ ९ ॥ शिलायां निर्गता व्योम्नि जाता त्वष्टभुजा पुनः । गार्हस्थ्यञ्च हरेर्ब्रूहि बहुभार्यस्य चानघ ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
I cannot conceive that the so-called Fate could have any influence on such high souled persons, the father and mother of the Supreme Being, Śrī Kṛṣṇa; who were those sons of Vasudeva that were killed by Kaṃsa? And who was that girl child who when struck by Kaṃsa on a slab of stone instantly rose up above the sky, assuming the form of Aṣṭabhujā, the eight armed Goddess? O Sinless One! Kindly explain unto me how did Śrī Hari manage to perform the house-holder’s duties, when he had married the several wives? and what were those glorious deeds that he did in His this incarnation and how did he finally pass away from his mortal physical coil?
Ch 1 Verse 11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कार्याणि तत्र तान्येव देहत्यागं च तस्य वै । किंवदन्त्या श्रुतं यत्तन्मनो मोहयतीव मे ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
My mind sinks into an ocean of confusion, when I hear of the several things done by Śrī Hari; some times I find the deeds, not capable of being done by any other than the Supreme Being Himself and sometimes I hear of deeds that can be done by an ordinary man. And, therefore I cannot decide whether Vasudeva was the Incarnation of God or an ordinary being. Be pleased to remove these doubts from my mind and describe the life of Vāsudeva in its true light.
Ch 1 Verse 12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
चरितं वासुदेवस्य त्वमाख्याहि यथातथम् । नरनारायणौ देवौ पुराणावृषिसत्तमौ ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
In days of yore, the two sons of Dharma were the two best amongst the Ṛṣis, and were the Devas Nara and Nārāyaṇa.
Ch 1 Verse 13-14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
धर्मपुत्रौ महात्मानौ तपश्चेरतुरुत्तमम् । यौ मुनी बहुवर्षाणि पुण्ये बदरिकाश्रमे ॥ १३ ॥ निराहारौ जितात्मानौ निःस्पृहौ जितषड्गुणौ । विष्णोरंशौ जगत्स्थेम्ने तपश्चेरतुरुत्तमम् ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
They were very high souled persons and they performed severe austerities for long extending years. They were born as part incarnations of Viṣṇu; and, for the good of the world, did they, in the Vadarikā’srama, perform penances, controlling their six passions, and free from desires.
Ch 1 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तयोरंशावतारौ हि जिष्णुकृष्णौ महाबलौ । प्रसिद्धौ मुनिभिः प्रोक्तौ सर्वज्ञैर्नारदादिभिः ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
The all-knowing sages Nārada and others say that the well known Arjuna and Śrī Kṛṣṇa of indomitable prowess were the two part incarnations of those two ancient Munis Nara and Nārāyaṇa.
Ch 1 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विद्यमानशरीरौ तौ कथं देहान्तरं गतौ । नरनारायणौ देवौ पुनः कृष्णार्जुनौ कथम् ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
How came those two Devas Nara and Nārāyaṇa to be born in the two bodies of Kṛṣṇa and Arjuṇa, though they did not relinquish their previous bodies!
Ch 1 Verse 17 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यौ चक्रतुस्तपश्चोग्रं मुक्त्यर्थं मुनिसत्तमौ । तौ कथं प्रापतुर्देहौ प्राप्तयोगौ महातपौ ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation
And also when those two Munis had attained liberation, their goal in their Yogas, by performing severe penances, how could they again be born in other bodies!
Ch 1 Verse 18-19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शूद्रः स्वधर्मनिष्ठस्तु देहान्ते क्षत्रियस्तु सः । शुभाचारो मृतो यो वै स शूद्रो ब्राह्मणो भवेत् ॥ १८ ॥ ब्राह्मणो निःस्पृहः शान्तो भवरोगाद्विमुच्यते । विपरीतमिदं भाति नरनारायणौ च तौ ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
If any Śūdra dies performing his own religion, he takes up a Vaiśya body in his next incarnation; if any Vaiśya died so, he takes up a Kṣattriya body and a Kṣattriya when adhering to his own rites and ceremonies, dying takes up a Brāhmaṇ body in his next incarnation. And if a Brāhmin be free from desires and resorts to the path of peace, when he dies, he becomes free from incarnations and is saved from this disease of getting into the world.
Ch 1 Verse 20-21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तपसा शोषितात्मानौ क्षत्रियौ तौ बभूवतुः । केन तौ कर्मणा शान्तौ जातौ शापेन वा पुनः ॥ २० ॥ ब्राह्मणौ क्षत्रियौ जातौ कारणं तन्मुने वद । यादवानां विनाशश्च ब्रह्मशापादिति श्रुतः ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
Now the reverse seems to take place in the case of Nara and Nārāyaṇa. In spite of withering up their bodies by hard penances, they took up Kṣattriya bodies. Under what influence of Karma, did they take up up birth when they were Yogis? Or might they, the Brāhmins, become Kṣattriyas owing to some curse? Whatever it may be, kindly remove my doubts, explaining to me their causes.
Ch 1 Verse 22 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कृष्णस्यापि हि गान्धार्याः शापेनैव कुलक्षयः । प्रद्युम्नहरणं चैव शम्बरेण कथं कृतम् ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation
It is heard that the Yādava clan suffered destruction through the curse of a Brāhmaṇ and in spite of Śrī Kṛṣṇa being the incarnation of the Supreme Being, his family died of the effect of curse from Gāndhārī.
Ch 1 Verse 23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वर्तमाने वासुदेवे देवदेवे जनार्दने । पुत्रस्य सूतिकागेहाद्धरणं चातिदुर्घटम् ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
How was it that Pradyumna was stolen away by Śambara, the lord of the Asuras; and for what purpose when Vasudeva, the Deva of the Devas was present, how was it that his son was stolen away from the lying-in room? This seems impossible.
Ch 1 Verse 24 Verse
Sanskrit Text
द्वारकादुर्गमध्याद्वै हरिवेश्माद्दुरत्ययात् । न ज्ञातं वासुदेवेन तत्कथं दिव्यचक्षुषा ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation
Why did not Vasudeva see, with His inner vision, the stealing away of his son from the impregnable walls of His fortress-like mansion in Dwārkā? (And could thus have prevented this!)
Ch 1 Verse 25-26 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सन्देहोऽयं महान्ब्रह्मन्निसन्देहं कुरु प्रभो । यत्पत्न्यो वासुदेवस्य दस्युभिर्लुण्ठिता हृताः ॥ २५ ॥ स्वर्गते देवदेवे तु तत्कथं मुनिसत्तम । संशयो जायते ब्रह्मंश्चित्तान्दोलनकारकः ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation
O Muni! After Vāsudeva had gone to Heaven, his wives were plundered by the dacoits on the way. I am very much in doubt on this point. Also I cannot understand why did this event take place just after His translation to Heaven?
Ch 1 Verse 27-28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विष्णोरंशः समुद्भूतः शौरिर्भूभारहारकृत् । स कथं मथुराराज्यं भयात्त्यक्त्वा जनार्दनः ॥ २७ ॥ द्वारवत्यां गतः साधो ससैन्यः ससुहृद्गणः । अवतारो हरेः प्रोक्तो भूभारहरणाय वै ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
Again how was Śrī Kṛṣṇa, who was Viṣṇu’s Full incarnation, born into this world to take off the vicious load of this earth, and to destroy the evil-doers, terrified and He fled through the fear of Jarāsandha, evacuated His kingdom of Mathurā and went with His armies and friends to Dwārkā?
Ch 1 Verse 29 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पापात्मनां विनाशाय धर्मसंस्थापनाय च । तत्कथं वासुदेवेन चौरास्ते न निपातिताः ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation
Vāsudeva came here to destroy the evil miscreants and to conserve the religion; how then did He not, as He was all knowing, previously kill those dacoits, who afterward stole and plundered His wives?
Ch 1 Verse 30-31 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यैर्हृता वासुदेवस्य पत्न्यः संलुण्ठिताश्च ताः । स्तेनास्ते किं न विज्ञाताः सर्वज्ञेन सता पुनः ॥ ३० ॥ भीष्मद्रोणवधः कामं भूभारहरणे मतः । अर्चिताश्च महात्मानः पाण्डवा धर्मतत्पराः ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation
Was it, that He, being Omniscient did not know those dacoits? He protected the Pāṇḍavas, no doubt, who were high souled, righteous and virtuous; but I cannot understand how did He consider the high souled virtuous persons like Bhīṣma, Droṇa and others as loads of earth and slay them.
Ch 1 Verse 32-34 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कृष्णभक्ताः सदाचारा युधिष्ठिरपुरोगमाः । ते कृत्वा राजसूयञ्च यज्ञराजं विधानतः ॥ ३२ ॥ दक्षिणा विविधा दत्त्वा ब्राह्मणेभ्योऽतिभावतः । पाण्डुपुत्रास्तु देवांशा वासुदेवाश्रिता मुने ॥ ३३ ॥ घोरं दुःखं कथं प्राप्ताः क्व गतं सुकृतञ्च तत् । किं तत्पापं महारौद्रं येन ते पीडिताः सदा ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation
The devotees of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, observing good customs, conducts and practices, Yudhiṣṭhira and his brothers performed the Rājasuya sacrifice, according to rules, giving various offerings to the Brāhmins and depended entirely on Vāsudeva; still, O Muni! they suffered terrible hardships; whither were their virtuous deeds by this time? what horrible sins did they commit that they had to suffer pains and troubles in the assembly.
Ch 1 Verse 35 Verse
Sanskrit Text
द्रौपदी च महाभागा वेदीमध्यात्समुत्थिता । रमांशजा च साध्वी च कृष्णभक्तियुता तथा ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation
The highly merited Draupadī arose from the midst of sacrificial fire and is born of the part of Lakṣmī, pure and devoted to Śrī Kṛṣṇa.
Ch 1 Verse 36-37 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सा कथं दुःखमतुलं प्राप घोरं पुनः पुनः । दुःशासनेन सा केशे गृहीता पीडिता भृशम् ॥ ३६ ॥ रजस्वला सभायां तु नीता भीतैकवाससा । विराटनगरे दासी जाता मत्स्यस्य सा पुनः ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation
How did such a glorious woman meet with incomparable terrible pains often and often; how could she be caught hold of by her hair on her head by Duh’sāsan; and carried to the royal assembly, when she was in her menses and extremely terrified and harassed?
Ch 1 Verse 38-39 Verse
Sanskrit Text
धर्षिता कीचकेनाथ रुदती कुररी यथा । हृता जयद्रथेनाथ क्रन्दमानातिदुःखिता ॥ ३८ ॥ मोचिता पाण्डवैः पश्चाद्बलवद्भिर्महात्मभिः । पूर्वजन्मकृतं पापं किं तद्येन च पीडिताः ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation
How did she come to be a slave of Matsya Rāj in his kingdom Virāṭ, and though actually crying aloud like a female osprey (eagle) she was highly insulted by Kīcaka! Alas! how could Draupadī be stolen away by Jayadratha, though latterly released by the Pāṇḍavas? What evil deeds did the Pāṇḍavas commit in their previous births, that they had to befall under so many telling difficulties?
Ch 1 Verse 40 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दुःखान्यनेकान्याप्तास्ते कथयाद्य महामते । राजसूयं क्रतुवरं कृत्वा ते मम पूर्वजाः ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation
O high minded Muni! My ancestors performed the Rājasūya sacrifice and still they fell under so many serious difficulties. Kindly explain the causes of these to me.
Ch 1 Verse 41 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दुःखं महत्तरं प्राप्ताः पूर्वजन्मकृतेन वै । देवांशानां कथं तेषां संशयोऽयं महान्हि मे ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation
If it be urged, that they suffered so many serious calamities, due to their actions in their former lives, that seems impossible. Because they are born of the Devas; thus arises my doubt; be pleased to explain to me.
Ch 1 Verse 42 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सदाचारैस्तु कौन्तेयैर्भीष्मद्रोणादयो हताः । छलेन धनलाभार्थं जानानैर्नश्वरं जगत् ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation
Again how the Pāṇḍavas, the sons of Kunti, of good conduct, and knowing the illusory nature of the world, why did they, out of pretence kill Bhīṣma, Drona and others?
Ch 1 Verse 43 Verse
Sanskrit Text
प्रेरिता वासुदेवेन पापे घोरे महात्मना । कुलं क्षयितवन्तस्ते हरिणा परमात्मना ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation
This appears a riddle to me that these Pāṇḍavas were led to the extermination of their race, being inspired by Hari Vāsudeva, to this horrible act.
Ch 1 Verse 44 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वरं भिक्षाटनं साधोर्नीवारैर्जीवनं वरम् । योधान्न हत्वा लोभेन शिल्पेन जीवनं वरम् ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation
Rather to live on begging alms and to live on rice, growing wild or without cultivation, or to live as an artisan or artist than to kill the valiant warriors unlawfully in a battle, simply out of voluptuous greed.
Ch 1 Verse 45 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
O Best of the Munis! You have preserved this extirpated race by producing the Goloka sons (i. e. sons born by other persons of women after their husbands are dead) of indomitable prowess.
Ch 1 Verse 46 Verse

English Translation
And why did my honoured father, born of Uttara in this respected family , encircle a snake round the neck of a Brāhmin ascetic?
Ch 1 Verse 47-48 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
No body, born of a Kṣattriya family, shews signs of hatred and jealousy towards a Brāhmin. Is it that my father shewed such feeling to that ascetic, who took the vow of silence!

Sanskrit Text
इति शीमद्वैभागवो चतुर्वैकवन्ये जनोजवप्रसनो नाम प्रथमोउधाय: ॥ 49 ॥
English Translation
O Best of the Munis -- These things and lots of others are troubling my mind with many grave doubts. O merciful saint! You know every thing; be kind enough to quell this the disturbed state of my mind. Thus ends the First Adhyāya in the Fourth Book of Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavata Purāṇa of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam

BOOK 4 • CHAPTER 24
On the stealing away of Pradyūmna
← Previous Chapter ☰ Index Next Chapter →
Ch 24 Verse 1-2 Verse
Sanskrit Text
४.२४ चतुर्विंशोऽध्यायः । देव्या कृष्णशोकायनोदनम् । व्यास उवाच । प्रातर्नन्दगृहे जातः पुत्रजन्ममहोत्सवः । किंवदन्त्यथ कंसेन श्रुता चारमुखादपि ॥ १ ॥ जानाति वसुदेवस्य दारास्तत्र वसन्ति हि । पशवो दासवर्गश्च सर्वे ते नन्दगोकुले ॥ २ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- On the other hand, there, at the house of Nanda, early in the next morning, commenced the grand birth day festivity. Kaṃsa came to know, afterwards, by his spies as will as by general rumour, that at Gokula, in the house of Nanda, a very joyous festival on a grand scale was being performed; he also knew before that the other wives of Vāsudeva, his animals and his servants were all staying at Nanda’s residence in Gokula.
Ch 24 Verse 3-4 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तेन शङ्कासमाविष्टो गोकुलं प्रति भारत । नारदेनापि तत्सर्वं कथितं कारणं पुरा ॥ ३ ॥ गोकुले ये च नन्दाद्यास्तत्पत्न्यश्च सुरांशजाः । देवकीवसुदेवाद्याः सर्वे ते शत्रवः किल ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation
O Bhārata! Thus all these gave reasons to Kaṃsa to suspect the place Gokula. Especially Nārada told him before that the residents, the cowherds at Gokula, Nanda and others, their wives, Devakī and Vāsudeva were all Devas incarnates; and consequently they were his enemies.
Ch 24 Verse 5-7 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इति नारदवाक्येन बोधितोऽसौ कुलाधमः । जातः कोपमना राजन् कंसः परमपापकृत् ॥ ५ ॥ पूतना निहता तत्र कृष्णेनामिततेजसा । बको वत्सासुरश्चापि धेनुकश्च महाबलः ॥ ६ ॥ प्रलम्बो निहतस्तेन तथा गोवर्धनो धृतः । श्रुत्वैतत्कर्म कंसस्तु मेने मरणमात्मनः ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
Thus being brought to more confidence by Nārada’s words, that vicious Kaṃsa, the disgrace to his family, was very angry and sent there his demons Pūtanā, Baka, Vatsa, the great Asura, the powerful Dhenuka, Pralamba. They were all killed by Kṛṣṇa, of unsurpassable prowess. Śrī Kṛṣṇa held aloft also the hillock Gobardhana (to protect the cow herds and cows, etc.) Hearing all these, Kaṃsa became certain also of his own death.
Ch 24 Verse 8 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तथा विनिहतः केशी ज्ञात्वा कंसोऽतिदुर्मनाः । धनुर्यागमिषेणाशु तावानेतुं प्रचक्रमे ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
Lastly when the evil minded Kaṃsa heard that the Daitya Keśī was also slain, then he made arrangements for a sacrifice, known as Dhanuryajña; and under this pretext wanted to bring over there at Mathurā the two brothers Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma.
Ch 24 Verse 9 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अक्रूरं प्रेषयामास क्रूरः पापमतिस्तदा । आनेतुं रामकृष्णौ च वधायामितविक्रमौ ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation
The evil minded Kaṃsa to effect the death of those two, Rāma and Kṛṣṇa, of unsurpassable prowess, sent Akrūra to Gokula to bring them over to Mathurā.
Ch 24 Verse 10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
रथमारोप्य गोपालौ गोकुलाद् गान्दिनीसुतः । आगतो मथुरायां तु कंसादेशे स्थितः किल ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
Akrūra, the son of Gandinī, under the orders of Kaṃsa, went to Gokula and brought the two boys on a chariot to Mathurā.
Ch 24 Verse 11-12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तावागत्य तदा तत्र धनुर्भङ्गञ्च चक्रतुः । हत्वाथ रजकं कामं गजं चाणूरमुष्टिकम् ॥ ११ ॥ शलं च तोशलं चैव निजघान हरिस्तदा । जघान कंसं देवेशः केशेष्वाकृष्य लीलया ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
On arriving at Mathurā, Rāma and Kṛṣṇa first broke the bow; killed Rājaka, the elephant Kubalaya, Cāṇūr, Muṣṭika, Śala, Tośala and other athletes and warriors. Last of all, Hari, the Lord of the Devas, holding Kaṃsa by his hair, killed him with utmost ease.
Ch 24 Verse 13 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पितरौ मोचयित्वाथ गतदुःखौ चकार ह । उग्रसेनाय राज्यं तद्ददावरिनिषूदनः ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation
The enemy-destroyer Kṛṣṇa removed the sorrows of his father and mother and released them from their prisons and gave over the kingdom of Mathurā to Ugrasena (the father of Kaṃsa).
Ch 24 Verse 14-15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वसुदेवस्तयोस्तत्र मौञ्जीबन्धनपूर्वकम् । कारयामास विधिवद् व्रतबन्धं महामनाः ॥ १४ ॥ उपनीतौ तदा तौ तु गतौ सान्दीपनालयम् । विद्याः सर्वाः समभ्यस्य मथुरामागतौ पुनः ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
The high minded Vāsudeva, then, with the triple girdle made of Muñja grass, performed the Upanayana ceremonies (wearing the sacred thread round the body) of Rāma and Kṛṣṇa and made them accept the vow of Brahmacarya. They then departed to the hermitage of the holy Muni Śandīpana to acquire knowledge. Learning all the vidyās (knowledges) there, they returned quickly to Mathurā.
Ch 24 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जातौ द्वादशवर्षीयौ कृतविद्यौ महाबलौ । मथुरायां स्थितौ वीरौ सुतावानकदुन्दुभेः ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
The two sons of Ānakadundubhi stayed there and on attaining their twelfth year, became proficient in all the branches of learning and became very powerful.
Ch 24 Verse 17 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मागधस्तु जरासन्धो जामातृवधदुःखितः । कृत्वा सैन्यसमाजं स मथुरामागतः पुरीम् ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation
That time Jarāsandha, being grieved at the killing of his son-in-law Kaṃsa, collected a strong and numerous army and marched to Mathurā.
Ch 24 Verse 18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स सप्तदशवारं तु कृष्णेन कृतबुद्धिना । जितः सङ्ग्राममासाद्य मधुपुर्यां निवासिना ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
Seventeen times Jarāsandha, the king of Magadha, attacked Mathurā and seventeen times he was defeated by the ingenuity of that highly intelligent Śrī Kṛṣṇa, who was of firm resolve and was then residing in Mathurā.
Ch 24 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पश्चाच्च प्रेरितस्तेन स कालयवनाभिधः । सर्वम्लेच्छाधिपः शूरो यादवानां भयङ्करः ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
Lastly, Jarāsandha sent the Kālayavana (Black Yavana) to invade Mathurā. These Yavanas were brave and the lords of all Mleccās (untouchables) and extremely terrific to the Yādavas. Note: Kālayavana - A king of Yavanas and enemy of Kṛṣṇa and an invincible foe of the Yādavas. Kṛṣṇa finding it impossible to vanquish him in the field of battle, cunningly decoyed him to the cave where Muchukunda was sleeping who burnt him down.
Ch 24 Verse 20-21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
श्रुत्वा यवनमायान्तं कृष्णः सर्वान् यदूत्तमान् । आनाय्य च तथा राममुवाच मधुसूदनः ॥ २० ॥ भयं नोऽत्र समुत्पन्नं जरासन्धान्महाबलात् । किं कर्तव्यं महाभाग यवनः समुपैति वै ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
Yavana means a Greek, an Ionian; then any foreigner, or barbarian (the word is applied at present to a Mahomedan or a European also). 20-21. Hearing that Kāla Yavana was coming to attack the Yādavas, Kṛṣṇa the destroyer of Mādhu, called all the Yādavas and Baladeva and addressed them thus :-- “O blessed ones! Now a cause of great terror has appeared amongst us; Kāla Yavana is being sent by our powerful enemy Jarāsandha to attack Mathurā. Now what to do? It is better to save one’s life by leaving aside all our homes, wealth and army.
Ch 24 Verse 22 Verse
Sanskrit Text
प्राणत्राणं प्रकर्तव्यं त्यक्त्वा गेहं बलं धनम् । सुखेन स्थीयते यत्र स देशः खलु पैतृकः ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation
You should all know that is the place of our fathers and forefathers where we can safely and happily dwell; where there is a constant source of anxiety and uneasiness that, though the place of our fathers and forefathers, ought to be avoided; never ought anyone to dwell there.
Ch 24 Verse 23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सदोद्वेगकरः कामं किं कर्तव्यः कुलोचितः । शैलसागरसान्निध्ये स्थातव्यं सुखमिच्छता ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
If you want to dwell at ease and comfort, you ought to dwell in that country or place which is adjacent to a sea or a mountain; where there is no fear from an enemy, the sages will always remain there.
Ch 24 Verse 24 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यत्र वैरिभयं न स्यात्स्थातव्यं तत्र पण्डितैः । शेषशय्यां समाश्रित्य हरिः स्वपिति सागरे ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation
See! The Bhagavān Hari, being afraid, as it were, of his enemy has taken refuge on the body of the thousand headed Śeṣa serpent as his sleeping place and is sleeping at ease and comfort on the ocean. It seems likely that the enemy of Trīpurā, the great Śiva is also dwelling on the Kailāśa mountain.
Ch 24 Verse 25 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तथैव च भयाद्भीतः कैलासे त्रिपुरार्दनः । तस्मान्नात्रैव स्थातव्यमस्माभिः शत्रुतापितैः ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation
We, too, are being constantly worried by our enemies here; therefore we ought not to live here any longer. We should all go to Dwārkā city with our friends, relatives and wealth.
Ch 24 Verse 26 Verse
Sanskrit Text
द्वारवत्यां गमिष्यामः सहिताः सर्व एव वै । कथिता गरुडेनाद्य रम्या द्वारवती पुरी ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation
Garuḍa, the king of the birds, has given us the detailed information of the city Dwārkā. That beautiful city is situated on the sea shore in the vicinity of the Raivataka mountain.”
Ch 24 Verse 27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
रैवताचलसानिध्ये सिन्धुकूले मनोहरा । व्यास उवाच । तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तथ्यं सर्वे यादवपुङ्गवाः ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- The Yādava chiefs, hearing Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s utterances fraught with their welfare, were ready to depart to that place Dwārkā, attended by their friends, relatives, and appurtenances.
Ch 24 Verse 28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गमनाय मतिं चक्रुः सकुटुम्बाः सवाहनाः । शकटानि तथोष्ट्राश्च वाम्यश्च महिषास्तथा ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
They then collected their camels, mares, and buffaloes and filled their conveyances with wealth, gems and precious stones and marched out of their place.
Ch 24 Verse 29 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Rāma and Kṛṣṇa went in front; the Yādavas and other subjects then marched in groups (several parties).
Ch 24 Verse 30 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Marching some days, they all reached Dvārāvatī. Then the portions of the city that were dilapidated or destroyed, Śrī Kṛṣṇa had them repaired by engineers, artisans and craftsmen.
Ch 24 Verse 31 Verse

English Translation
Placing the Yādavas there, Keśava and Baladeva quickly returned to Mathurā and began to stay in that desolated city.
Ch 24 Verse 32 Verse

English Translation
The extremely powerful king of the Yavanas arrived then at Mathurā. Kṛṣṇa knowing that the Yavana chief had come there, went out of the city.
Ch 24 Verse 33 Verse

English Translation
The Bhagavān Madhusūdana, the destroyer of the boastings of Asuras and other people, dressed in yellow robes, appeared on foot before the Kālayavan with smile on his lips.
Ch 24 Verse 34 Verse

English Translation
Seeing the lotus-eyed Kṛṣṇa before him, the treacherous Lord of the Yavanas, pursued him on foot to catch hold of him.
Ch 24 Verse 35 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Where the powerful Rājarṣi Muchukunda was sleeping soundly, the Bhagavān Hari led Kālayavana there.
Ch 24 Verse 36 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
There Śrī Kṛṣṇa, saw Muchukunda and vanished away at once; the king of the Yavanas on arriving there found the Rājarsi (the royal sage) there in deep sleep.
Ch 24 Verse 37 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तत्रैव यवनः प्राप्तः सुप्तभूतमपश्यत । मत्वा तं वासुदेवं स पादेनाताडयन्नृपम् ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation
The wicked Yavana mistaking Muchukunda for Śrī Kṛṣṇa, gave him a good kick. The powerful king Muchukunda got up and was very angry; his eyes became red and reduced that vicious Yavana instantly into ashes.
Ch 24 Verse 38 Verse
Sanskrit Text
प्रबुद्धः क्रोधरक्ताक्षस्तं ददाह महाबलः । तं दग्ध्वा मुचुकुन्दोऽथ ददर्श कमलेक्षणम् ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation
When Muchukunda burnt the Yavana, he saw the lotus-eyed Kṛṣṇa; he bowed down to that Supreme Deva, Vāsudeva, and went to forest.
Ch 24 Verse 39 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Śrī Kṛṣṇa then went back to the city Dwārkā with Rāma and made Ugrasena there the king and began to enjoy at his will.
Ch 24 Verse 40 Verse
Sanskrit Text
उग्रसेनं नृपं कृत्वा विजहार यथारुचि । अहरद्रुक्मिणी कामं शिशुपालस्वयंवरात् ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation
At the marriage ceremony of Śiśupāla, at the palace of the king of Vidarbha, Janārdan Viṣṇu carried away by force Rukminī, the bride elect from the Svayambara assembly (where the husband is self elected by the bride herself) and afterwards married her according to the rule called Rākhṣasa Vidhi (one of the eight forms of marriage in Hindu Law in which a girl is forcibly seized and carried away after the defeat or destruction of her relatives in battle).
Ch 24 Verse 41-42 Verse
Sanskrit Text
राक्षसेन विवाहेन चक्रे दारविधिं हरिः । ततो जाम्बवतीं सत्यां मित्रविन्दाञ्च भामिनीम् ॥ ४१ ॥ कालिन्दीं लक्षणां भद्रां तथा नाग्नजितीं शुभाम् । पृथक्पृथक्समानीयाप्युपयेमे जनार्दनः ॥ ४२ ॥

Afterwards He brought also Jāmbavatī, Satyabhāmā, Mitravindā, Kālindī, Lakṣmanā, Bhadrā, and auspicious Nāgnajitī (the daughter of the king Nagnajit) on various occasions and married them. O Lord of the earth! These eight women were the best and most beautiful of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s wives.
Ch 24 Verse 43 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Rukminī first gave birth to the beautiful child Pradyūmna and Śrī Kṛṣṇa performed the religious ceremony at the birth of his child.
Ch 24 Verse 44 Verse

Then the powerful Dānava named Śamvara stole away the little baby from the lying-in-chamber and carried him to his own city and made him over under the charge of Māyāvatī.
Ch 24 Verse 45 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Coming to know that His son had been stolen away, Śrī Kṛṣṇa became very much overpowered with sorrow and took the shelter of the Supreme Goddess, the Devī, with a heart full of devotion.
Ch 24 Verse 46-47 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Śrī Kṛṣṇa then began, to chant, in sweet auspicious tone, hymns in alphabets, conveying the highest meanings, in adoration of the Yoga Māyā, Who slew Vritrāsura and other Daityas with ease and alacrity.
Ch 24 Verse 48 Verse
Sanskrit Text
श्रीकृष्ण उवाच । मातर्मयातितपसा परितोषिता त्वं प्राग्जन्मनि प्रसुमनादिभिरर्चितासि । धर्मात्मजेन बदरीवनखण्डमध्ये किं विस्मृतो जननि ते त्वयि भक्तिभावः ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! I, in my former birth as the son of Dharma, appeased You by my ascetic practices in the hermitage of Badari and worshipped You with various offerings; O Mother! Have you now forgotten all my devotion to You?
Ch 24 Verse 49 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सूतीगृहादपहृतः किमु बालको मे केनापि दुष्टमनसाप्यथ कौतुकाद्वा । मानापहारकरणाय ममाद्य नूनं लज्जा तवाम्ब खलु भक्तजनस्य युक्ता ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! Has any evil minded enemy stolen away my son from the lying-in chamber? Or have You Yourself done this to make a fun and see the amusement? It seems that some one of my enemies has done so to insult me; however, You, O Mother! ought not to put your devotee under this shameful condition.
Ch 24 Verse 50 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दुर्गो महानतितरां नगरी सुगुप्ता तत्रापि मेऽस्ति सदनं किल मध्यभागे । अन्तःपुरे च पिहितं ननु सूतिगेहं बालो हृतः खलु तथापि ममैव दोषात् ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation
O Mother! This Dwārakā city is well guarded; a very strong fort is built in its middle and my place is in the midst of that again; and the lying in-chamber is again in the middle; I therefore must say that it is due to my bad luck that the child is stolen away!
Ch 24 Verse 51 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नाहं गतः परपुरं न च यादवाश्च रक्षावतीव नगरी किल वीरवर्यैः । माया तवैव जननि प्रकटप्रभावा मे बालकः परिहृतः कुहकेन केन ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! I did not go to the house of my enemy; the Yādavas also did not go there; this city is guarded by valiant soldiers; then how is it, under what charm, the baby has been stolen? O Mother! Now I come to know that it is due to Your Māyā; such things are common due to Your Māyā in the three worlds.
Ch 24 Verse 52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नो वेद्म्यहं जननि ते चरितं सुगुप्तं को वेद मन्दमतिरल्पविदेव देही । क्वासौ गतो मम भटैर्न च वीक्षितो वा हर्ताम्बिके जवनिका तव कल्पितेयम् ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! When I am ignorant of your deepest mysteries, how can there exist anyone among the little minded Jīvas that can know your doings? My watchmen could not see anything, where my child was taken away and who has stolen it. O Mother! I come to the conclusion that it is hidden behind the screen of Your Māyā.
Ch 24 Verse 53 Verse
Sanskrit Text
चित्रं न तेऽत्र पुरतो मम मातृगर्भो नीतस्त्वयार्धसमये किल माययासौ । यं रोहिणी हलधरं सुषुवे प्रसिद्धं दूरे स्थिता पतिपरा मिथुनं विनापि ॥ ५३ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! It is not strange with You; to the chaste woman, Rohiṇī Devī, though situated at a great distance and not connected with any male persons, You, in the fifth month, moved away the son to my knowledge from the womb of my mother; and thus Baladeva was born to Rohinī. This is now known to all.
Ch 24 Verse 54 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सृष्टिं करोषि जगतामनुपालनं च नाशं तथैव पुनरप्यनिशं गुणैस्त्वम् । को वेद तेऽम्ब चरितं दुरितान्तकारि प्रायेण सर्वमखिलं विहितं त्वयैतत् ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation
Mother! You are incessantly creating, preserving, and destroying this whole universe by the mixture of the three qualities. Who can know Your sin-destroying doings? Mother! There is no need of dwelling at length. Suffice it to say that You, no doubt, are doing all that are being done in this whole universe.
Ch 24 Verse 55 Verse
Sanskrit Text
उत्पाद्य पुत्रजननप्रभवं प्रमोदं दत्त्वा पुनर्विरहजं किल दुःखभारम् । त्वं क्रीडसे सुललितैः खलु तैर्विहारै- र्नो चेत्कथं मम सुताप्तिरतिर्वृथा स्यात् ॥ ५५ ॥
English Translation
You first create the joy at the birth of a child; again You load us with heavy burdens of sorrows due to the separation from that child; thus you are always sporting; otherwise how my joy at the birth of my child would thus be rendered quite useless?
Ch 24 Verse 56 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मातास्य रोदिति भृशं कुररीव बाला दुःखं तनोति मम सन्निधिगा सदैव । कष्टं न वेत्सि ललितेऽप्रमितप्रभावे मातस्त्वमेव शरणं भवपीडितानाम् ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation
The mother of that child is always weeping like an ewe, straying from a flock; she is giving vent to her sorrows always to me; O Kind-hearted! Being thus endowed with illimitable prowess and understanding, do You not know my troubles! O Mother! You are the only source of consolation to one, suffering from the sorrows of this world. There is no doubt in this.
Ch 24 Verse 57 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सीमा सुखस्य सुतजन्म तदीयनाशो दुःखस्य देवि भवने विबुधा वदन्ति । तत्किं करोमि जननि प्रथमे प्रनष्टे पुत्रे ममाद्य हृदयं स्फुटतीव मातः ॥ ५७ ॥
English Translation
O Goddess! The wise seers say that the birth of a child in any house is the highest bliss there, and the death of a child is the greatest sorrow that can befall to any house. Therefore, O Mother! What shall I do in this? What shall I say more than that my heart is going to burst, due to the disappearance of my child.
Ch 24 Verse 58 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यज्ञं करोमि तव तुष्टिकरं व्रतं वा दैवं च पूजनमथाखिलदुःखहा त्वम् । मातः सुतोऽत्र यदि जीवति दर्शयाशु त्वं वै क्षमा सकलशोकविनाशनाय ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! I will perform all the necessary sacrifices, take up vows, perform all sorts of worship to the entire satisfaction of the Great Fate (Ordainer of things); You be pleased to remove my sorrow. O Mother! If my son be alive, kindly shew him once to me. Mother! There is no other than You Who is fully capable to destroy this my pain and sorrow, raging in my heart.
Ch 24 Verse 59 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । एवं स्तुता तदा देवी कृष्णेनाक्लिष्टकर्मणा । प्रत्यक्षदर्शना भूत्वा तमुवाच जगद्गुरुम् ॥ ५९ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- He who brings into practice, things that are considered impracticable for the Devas and removes the load of the Goddess Earth with ease and alacrity, the same Saviour of world, Śrī Kṛṣṇa thus chanted hymns in adoration of the Great Goddess. The Devī then became visible to him and said.
Ch 24 Verse 60 Verse
Sanskrit Text
श्रीदेव्युवाच । शोकं मा कुरु देवेश शापोऽयं ते पुरातनः । तस्य योगेन पुत्रस्ते शम्बरेण हृतो बलात् ॥ ६० ॥
English Translation
O Lord of the Devas! Do not any longer be sorrowful and miserable; there had been a curse on you before; and, for that reason, the Daitya Śambara has stolen away your son by his demonic magic.
Ch 24 Verse 61 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अतस्ते षोडशे वर्षे हत्वा तं शम्बरं बलात् । आगमिष्यति पुत्रस्ते मत्प्रसादान्न संशयः ॥ ६१ ॥
English Translation
Therefore, when your son will grow sixteen years old, then he will, by My Grace, kill the Daitya perforce and will return to you. There is no doubt in this.
Ch 24 Verse 62-63 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । इत्युक्त्वान्तर्दधे देवी चण्डिका चण्डविक्रमा । भगवानपि पुत्रस्य शोकं त्यक्त्वाभवत्सुखी ॥ ६२ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां चतुर्थस्कन्धे देव्या कृष्णशोकापनोदनं नाम चतुर्विंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ४.२४॥
English Translation
O king! Thus saying these words full of hope and confidence, the Great Goddess Candikā, of formidable prowess, disappeared. Kṛṣṇa too, quitted his sorrows, due to the bereavement of his child, and began to spend his time in happiness and peace. Here ends the Twenty-fourth Chapter of the Fourth Book of Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam, the Mahā Purāṇam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa on the stealing away of Pradyūmna.

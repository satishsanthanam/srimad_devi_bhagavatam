# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 4, CHAPTER 10

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 1-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ४.१० दशमोऽध्यायः । भृगुशापकारणवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> जनमेजय उवाच ।
> सन्देहोऽयं महानत्र पाराशर्य कथानके ।
> नरनारायणौ शान्तौ वैष्णवांशौ तपोधनौ ॥ १ ॥
> 
> तीर्थाश्रयौ सत्त्वयुक्तौ वन्याशनपरौ सदा ।
> धर्मपुत्रौ महात्मानौ तापसौ सत्यसंस्थितौ ॥ २ ॥
> 
> कथं रागसमायुक्तौ जातौ युद्धे परस्परम् ।
> सङ्ग्रामं चक्रतुः कस्मात्त्यक्त्वा तपिमनुत्तमाम् ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> प्रह्लादेन समं पूर्णं दिव्यवर्षशतं किल ।
> हित्वा शान्तिसुखं युद्धं कृतवन्तौ कथं मुनी ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Janamejaya said :-- O Son of Parāśara! There has arisen a great doubt in my mind on hearing just now your words. These Nara Nārāyaṇa are the two sons of Dharma; they are ascetics, calm and quiet, the Aṃśas of Viṣṇu; they reside in a holy place of pilgrimage! They are filled with the Sattvic qualities, subsisting always on roots and fruits of the forest, the highsouled hermits and truthful. How were they addicted to such warfare? Why had they left their invaluable asceticism? And with what object were they fighting for full one thousand Deva years with Prahlāda.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कथं तौ चक्रतुर्युद्धं प्रह्लादेन समं मुनी ।
> कथयस्व महाभाग कारणं विग्रहस्य वै ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

What was the end, O Muni, of their fight with Prahlāda? Kindly explain to me in detail the cause of this warfare.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> (कामिनी कनकं कार्यं कारणं विग्रहस्य वै)॥
> 
> युद्धबुद्धिः कथं जाता तयोश्च तद्विरक्तयोः ।
> तथाविधं तपस्तप्तं ताभ्याञ्च केन हेतुना ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Women, wealth or any other worldly object can be the cause of any quarrel or fight amongst any persons; but, in this case, the two ascetics had none of these; how then this idea of fight sprung within their minds.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मोहार्थं सुखभोगार्थं स्वर्गार्थं वा परन्तप ।
> कृतमत्युत्कटं ताभ्यां तपः सर्वफलप्रदम् ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

And why did they practise such severe austerities? Was it that they had to overpower others, or enjoy pleasures themselves or to reach Heaven that they practised tapasyā? What fruits did they eventually obtain from such penances?

</details>

<details>

They became very lean and thin through their asceticism; still how could they fight full one thousand Deva years without getting fatigued.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 10</b></summary>

**English Translation:**

They were not entangled in this fight for kingdom, or wealth or for women or for any other worldly object; then why did they fight with the high souled Prahlāda?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 11</b></summary>
  <br>

Having no attachment for any worldly object nor any desire to gain any thing therefrom, why did they engage themselves so thoroughly, in such pains giving battle?

</details>

Intelligent persons always do works leading to bliss; they never do painful works; this is the long standing rule of the world.

**English Translation:**

The two sons of Dharma were the Aṃśas of Hari, all knowing and adorned with all qualities; why did they fight, subversive of religion?

</details>

<details>

>
**English Translation:**

O Mahārṣi! Even the dull and stupid persons in the world won’t go to these deadly battles leaving asceticism and samādhi, leading to the purification of all desires.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अहङ्कारभवात्पापात्पातितः पृथिवीतले ।

>
**English Translation:**

I have heard that Yayāti, the Lord of the earth was dropped from Heaven to this world, owing to his Ahamkāra, though he was a virtuous king devoted to charities and sacrifices.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शब्दोच्चारणमात्रेण पातितो वज्रपाणिना ।
> अहङ्कारमृते युद्धं न भवत्येव निश्चयः ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

No sooner Yayāti, said the king :-- did Aśvamedha sacrifice, etc., with Ahamkāra, egoism, he was dropped by Indra with thunderbolt in his hands. So one can see that, without Ahamkāra no fight can occur. The ascetics had no bodily strength; therefore if they had to fight, it is through the waste of their Tapasyā that they could do so.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> किं फलं तस्य युद्धस्य मुनेः पुण्यविनाशनम् ।
> व्यास उवाच ।
> राजन् संसारमूलं हि त्रिविधः परिकीर्तितः ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O king! The all knowing sages that have realised the truth or Dharma declare the threefold Ahamkāra arising out the Sattvic, Rajasic and Tamasic qualities respectively to be the causes of this world.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 18</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

How, then, can these two Munis being embodied forego their Ahamkāras? Without any cause, no actions follow; this is quite certain.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 19</b></summary>

>
**English Translation:**

Tapas, charities, sacrifices all originate from the Sattvic qualities. And quarrels arise from the Rajasic or Tamasic qualities.

</details>

<details>

All arise from Ahamkāra, whether good or bad; this is quite certain.

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 21</b></summary>

**English Translation:**

There is no other thing that enchains a soul than this Ahamkāra. It is out of Ahamkāra that this Universe is created: how can it be then free from it?

O King! Brahma, Viṣṇu, Maheśha, even these are with Ahamkāras. Then how can you expect other ordinary Munis to be free from it?

</details>

**English Translation:**

O king! Indra, of unequalled prowess, with the aid of Viṣṇu, deprived the Daityas of their kingdom. The defeated Daityas took refuge of their family spiritual guide Śukrācārya and addressed him thus, O Brāhmana! You are endowed with your fiery strength of Tapasyā and you are now powerful; why are you not lending your helping hands to your Daitya followers. O foremost of the councillors. If you do not help us and save us, we will not be able to stay in this earth and will soon have to go down to Pātāla.

</details>

> 
> मा भैष्ट धारयिष्यामि तेजसा स्वेन भोऽसुराः ।
> मन्त्रैस्तथौषधीभिश्च साहाय्यं वः सदैव हि ॥ ४३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- Thus addressed by the Daityas, the very kind hearted Śukrācārya said, O Daityas! Do not be afraid; I will protect you by my fire of strength and vigour; and help you with sound counsels and medicines. Be brave and energetic and cast aside your mental agony and sorrow.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 44-46</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> करिष्यामि कृतोत्साहा भवन्तु विगतज्वराः ।
> व्यास उवाच ।
> ततस्ते निर्भया जाता दैत्याः काव्यस्य संश्रयात् ॥ ४४ ॥
> 
> देवैः श्रुतस्तु वृत्तान्तः सर्वैश्चारमुखात्किल ।
> तत्र सम्मन्त्र्य ते देवाः शक्रेण च परस्परम् ॥ ४५ ॥
> 
> मन्त्रं चक्रुः सुसंविग्नाः काव्यमन्त्रप्रभावतः ।
> योद्धुं गच्छामहे तूर्णं यावन्न च्यावयन्ति वै ॥ ४६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O king! The Daityas became fearless under the patronage of Śukrācārya. The Devas had their spies and knew all about these. They held councils with Indra and settled that before the Daityas had time to dislodge us from our Heaven with the mantra of Śukrācārya, we will speedily go and attack them. Thus attacked all on a sudden, they will all be slain by us and we will drive them down to the Pātāla.

</details>

<details>

> **Sanskrit:**
> प्रसह्य हत्वा शिष्टांस्तु पातालं प्रापयामहे ।
> दैत्याञ्जग्मुस्ततो देवाः संरुष्टाः शस्त्रपाणयः ॥ ४७ ॥

**English Translation:**

Thus forming their resolves, with fully equipped arms and weapons, they went out of rage to fight with the Daityas and orderd by Indra and aided by Viṣṇu, they began to kill the Demons.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 48</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जग्मुस्तान्विष्णुसहिता दानवान् हरिणोदिताः ।
> वध्यमानास्तु ते दैत्याः सन्त्रस्ता भयपीडिताः ॥ ४८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When the Devas were thus slaying the Demons, they got very much terrified and exclaimed “O Lord! Protect us! Protect us!” and took the refuge of Sukra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 10 Verse 10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> किमर्थं तु कृतं युद्धं ताभ्यां तेन महात्मना ।
> निरीहः पुरुषः कस्मात्प्रकुर्याद्युद्धमीदृशम् ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Śukrācārya, seeing the Daityas very much perplexed and distracted, at once cried aloud out of the influence of his Mantra “No fear, no fear,” Then the Devas on seeing Śukrācārya left the Daityas and fled away to their own places. Here ends the Tenth Chapter of the Fourth Book of the Mahā Purāṇam, Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam, of 18000 verses by Maharṣī Veda Vyāsa on the curse on Viṣṇu by Bhrigu.

</details>

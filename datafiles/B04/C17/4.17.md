# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 4, CHAPTER 17

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ४.१७ सप्तदशोऽध्यायः । नारायणवरदानम् ।
> 
> 
> जनमेजय उवाच ।
> वाराङ्गनास्त्वयाख्याता नरनारायणाश्रमे ।
> एकं नारायणं शान्तं कामयाना स्मरातुराः ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Janamejaya said :-- O Muni! You told before that the heavenly prostitutes sent by Indra in the hermitage of Nara Nārāyaṇa became lustful and desired to live with Nārāyaṇa only, whose heart was calm and quiet.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शप्तुकामस्तदा जातो मुनिर्नारायणश्च ताः ।
> निवारितो नरेणाथ भ्रात्रा धर्मविदा नृप ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

At that moment when Nārāyaṇa was about to curse them, his brother Nara desisted him from taking that step.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 3-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> किं कृतं मुनिना तेन व्यसने समुपस्थिते ।
> ताभिः सङ्कल्पितेनाथ कामार्थाभिर्भृशं मुने ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> शक्रेणोत्पादिताभिश्च बहुप्रार्थनया पुनः ।
> याचितेन विवाहार्थं किं कृतं तेन जिष्णुना ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Now I ask you what did that triumphant Nārāyaṇa Muni do, in the critical juncture, when he was repeatedly asked by those prostitutes, sent by lndra, to satisfy their lust?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इत्येतच्छ्रोतुमिच्छामि चरितं तस्य मोक्षदम् ।
> नारायणस्य मे ब्रूहि विस्तरेण पितामह ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Grand Sire! I am very eager to know the deeds of Nārāyaṇa, leading to one’s freedom. Kindly describe in detail and fulfill my wishes.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> शृणु राजन्प्रवक्ष्यामि यथा तस्य महात्मनः ।
> धर्मपुत्रस्य धर्मज्ञ विस्तरेण वदामि ते ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- Hear, O king! I am describing to you in detail, what that high souled son of Dharma did.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शप्तुकामस्तु सन्दृष्टो नरेणाथ यदा हरिः ।
> वारितोऽसौ समाश्वास्य मुनिर्नारायणस्तदा ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When Nārāyaṇa Hari was ready to curse them, the Ṛṣi Nara, seeing this, consoled him and desisted him.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शान्तकोपस्तदोवाच तास्तपस्वी महामुनिः ।
> स्मितपूर्वमिदं वाक्यं मधुरं धर्मनन्दनः ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then the great sage, the ascetic son of Dharma, Nārāyaṇa, leaving aside his anger, began to address them in sweet words with countenance smiling.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 9-10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अस्मिञ्जन्मनि चार्वङ्ग्यः कृतसङ्कल्पवानहम् ।
> आवाभ्याञ्च न कर्तव्यः सर्वथा दारसङ्ग्रहः ॥ ९ ॥
> 
> तस्माद् गच्छन्तु त्रिदिवं कृपां कृत्वा ममोपरि ।
> धर्मज्ञा न प्रकुर्वन्ति व्रतभङ्गं परस्य वै ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Fair women! We have determined to practise asceticism in this life; it does not therefore behove us to accept any wife; therefore shew your kindness unto us and go back to your Heaven. You would better think that those who know what is religion, they never desire to break the vow of another.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शृङ्गारेऽस्मिन् रसे नूनं स्थायीभावो रतिः स्मृतः ।
> कथं करोमि सम्बन्धं तदभावे सुलोचनाः ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O beautiful eyed ones! In the sexual pleasure, it is the delightful feeling of passionate joy that is requisite; and we are wanting in those feelings; then how can we effect that union?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कारणेन विना कार्यं न भवेदिति निश्चयः ।
> कविभिः कथितं शास्त्रे स्थायीभावो रसः किल ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

No action can come out of no cause; this is all clear. The poets say that the sexual power and pleasure, is the feeling, the mental attitude that corresponds; and that is the only thing, that lasts. And we have no desire for that.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> धन्यः सुचारुसर्वाङ्गः सभाग्योऽहं धरातले ।
> प्रीतिपात्रं यतो जातो भवतीनामकृत्रिमम् ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

However my limbs are all very graceful, I am very fortunate and blessed in this world, otherwise how can I be the object of your sincere love towards me.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भवतीभिः कृपां कृत्वा रक्षणीयं व्रतं मम ।
> भविष्यामि महाभागाः पतिरप्यन्यजन्मनि ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

You all are very fortunate; therefore do now show this mercy unto me “do not break my vow.” I pray now that, in a subsequent birth, I may become your husband.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 15-16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अष्टाविंशे विशालाक्ष्यो द्वापरेऽस्मिन्धरातले ।
> देवानां कार्यसिद्ध्यर्थं प्रभविष्यामि सर्वथा ॥ १५ ॥
> 
> तदा भवत्यो मद्दाराः प्राप्य जन्म पृथक्पृथक् ।
> भूपतीनां सुता भूत्वा पलीभावं गमिष्यथ ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O large eyed fair women! In the twenty-eighth Dvāpara Yuga, I will certainly incarnate on earth to effect the purpose of the Devas; then you all also would respectively incarnate as the daughters of kings and would also become my wives.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इत्याश्वास्य हरिस्तास्तु प्रतिश्रुत्य परिग्रहम् ।
> व्यसर्जयत्स भगवाञ्जग्मुश्च विगतज्वराः ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārāyaṇa thus consented to marry them in some other next birth; and consoling them, made them go back to their Heavens. They also abandoned their mental disquietude and, on reaching back to Heavens, they explained everything to Indra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 18-19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एवं विसर्जितास्तेन गताः स्वर्गं तदाङ्गनाः ।
> शक्राय कथयामासुः कारणं सकलं पुनः ॥ १८ ॥
> 
> आश्रुत्य मघवांस्ताभ्यो वृत्तान्तं तस्य विस्तरात् ।
> तुष्टाव तं महात्मानं नारीर्दृष्ट्वा तथोर्वशीः ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Indra heard (from these heavenly women) what the two two Ṛṣis did and saw before him Urvaśī and other women created by Nārāyaṇa from his thighs, etc., and began to extol the merits of the high souled Nārāyaṇa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इन्द्र उवाच ।
> अहो धैर्यं मुनेः कामं तथैव च तपोबलम् ।
> येनोर्वश्यः स्वतपसा तादृग्रूपाः प्रकल्पिताः ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Indra said :-- O! How wonderful is the patience of the Muni? What is the wonderful influence of his Tapas! Oh! He has created, by the sheer force of his Tapas, Urvaśī and these fair women, unrivalled for their beauties, from his thighs.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इति स्तुत्वा प्रसन्नात्मा बभूव सुरराट् ततः ।
> नारायणोऽपि धर्मात्मा तपस्यभिरतोऽभवत् ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Lord of the Devas thus extolled his merits and became freed from his anxieties. The virtuous Nārāyaṇa, too, devoted himself to the practice of his Tapasyā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

O king! Thus I have described to you, in detail, all the wonderful accounts regarding Nara Nārāyaṇa.

</details>

<details>

>
**English Translation:**

O Superior in the descendants of Bharata! These two Nara and Nārāyaṇa afterwards incarnated themselves, due to Bhṛgu’s curse as the two great heroes Arjuṇa and Kṛṣṇa, to relieve the burden of the earth.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

> कृष्णावतारचरितं विस्तरेण वदस्व मे ।
> सन्देहो मम चित्तेऽस्ति तं निवारय मानद ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The king said :-- O respect giving Muni! Now describe in detail the life of the Avatar Kṛṣṇa and dispel my mental doubt.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 25-26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ययोः पुत्रत्वमापन्नौ हर्यनन्तौ महाबलौ ।
> देवकीवसुदेवौ तौ दुःखभाजौ कथं मुने ॥ २५ ॥
> 
> कंसेन निगडे बद्धौ पीडितौ बहुवत्सरान् ।
> ययोः पुत्रो हरिः साक्षात्तपसा तोषितोऽभवत् ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O best of the Munis! Why were Vāsudeva and Devakī, who were chosen by the very powerful Hari and Ananta as their parents, doomed to so many miseries and afflictions. Why had these parents to remain for good many years in the prison of Kaṃsa, who pleased directly by their Tapasyā that Bhagavān Janārdana.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जातोऽसौ मथुरायां तु गोकुले स कथं गतः ।
> कंसं हत्वा द्वारवत्यां निवासं कृतवान्कथम् ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Why did Kṛṣṇa taking his birth at Mathurā, go to Gokula? Also what was his object to go to Dvārkā, situated in the ocean, when he killed the enemy Kaṃsa?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 28</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

Also why did his father, mother and relatives, leave their old holy places of residences and go abroad to live in a wretched old country?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 29-31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कुलञ्च द्विजशापेन कथमुत्सादितं हरेः ।
> भारावतारणं कृत्वा वासुदेवः सनातनः ॥ २९ ॥
> 
> देहं मुमोच तरसा जगाम च दिवं हरिः ।
> पापिष्ठानाञ्च भारेण व्याकुलाभूच्च मेदिनी ॥ ३० ॥
> 
> ते हता वासुदेवेन पार्थेनामितकर्मणा ।
> लुण्ठिता यैर्हरेः पत्न्यस्ते कथं न निपातिताः ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Why was the Yadu race destroyed by the curse from a Brāhmaṇa! How did Śrī Kṛṣṇa Vāsudeva leave finally His body after He had relieved the burden of the earth and was about to enter into His Heaven? The evildoers of the earth were slain by Kṛṣṇa and Arjuṇa, of unequalled prowess; but how was it, that those who plundered the wives of Śrī Hari, were not at all punished by Him?

</details>

<details>

  <br>

The great personages Bhīṣma, Droṇa, Karṇa, the king Vālhīka, Virāṭa, Vikarṇa, Dhṛṣṭadyumna, the king Somadatta were destroyed for relieving the burden of the earth; and the plunderers were acquitted! Kindly explain the cause of this.

</details>

<details>

How was it that those chaste and virtuous wives of Śrī Kṛṣṇa go into troubles at the latter end of their lives? There has arisen a doubt in my mind on this point.

</details>

Why was it that Her five sons residing in Her house were killed by Aśvaththāmā? What was the cause that the son of Subhadrā had to die in the battlefield?

</details>

<details>

Why did the king Kaṃsa kill the six sons of Devakī; and why was it that Śrī Hari who was capable of averting the Fate did not at all prevent that?

</details>

<details>

What a wonder is this that in the matters of Brāhmaṇa’s curse toward the Jādavas, their being killed in the Prabhāsa, the total extermination the Jadu race and the plundering of His wives, why did He allow Fate to do these great momentous things?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 17 Verse 43</b></summary>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विष्णुना चेश्वरेणापि साक्षान्नारायणेन च ।
> उग्रसेनस्य सेवा वै दासवत्सततं कृता ॥ ४३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

If He was the all-powerful God and He Himself Nārāyaṇa, that why did He incessantly act like a slave towards Ugrasena. N.B. -- Ugrasena was the king of Mathurā and father of Kaṃsa. He was deposed by his son; but Kṛṣṇa after having slain Kaṃsa restored him to the throne.

</details>

All these bring doubt in our minds regarding Nārāyaṇa Muni that His deeds are always like those of ordinary persons; why did his pleasures and pains resemble those of ordinary human beings? Were he God, why his actions were not Godly? (i.e., superhuman)

Therefore dost Thou describe in detail all the Divine Leelas (playful sports) done by Hari of superhuman powers in this world.

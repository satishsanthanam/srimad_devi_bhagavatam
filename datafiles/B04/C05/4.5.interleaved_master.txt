[Ch 5 Verse 1]
४.५ पञ्चमोऽध्यायः । नरनारायणकथावर्णनम् ।


व्यास उवाच ।
अथ किं बहुनोक्तेन संसारेऽस्मिन्नृपोत्तम ।
धर्मात्माद्रोहबुद्धिस्तु कश्चिद्भवति कर्हिचित् ॥ १ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O best of kings! There is no need of dwelling at length on this point; suffice to say, that in this world, are found persons very rare that are religious, and free from egoism, jealousy, anger, etc.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 2]
रागद्वेषावृतं विश्वं सर्वं स्थावरजङ्गमम् ।
आद्ये युगेऽपि राजेन्द्र किमद्य कलिदूषिते ॥ २ ॥
English Translation:
O king of kings! Even in the Satya Yuga, the Golden age, this world, moving and unmoving, was covered with feelings of jealousy and anger. What to say in this Kali Yuga (Dark Age)! (There is no wonder that this world would be full of these vicious things.)
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 3]
देवाः सेर्ष्याश्च सद्रोहाश्छलकर्मरताः सदा ।
मानुषाणां तिरश्चां च का वार्ता नृप गण्यते ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation:
O best of kings! When the Devas are deceitful, jealous, and filled with feelings of anger, what is to be said with human beings and other lower creations!
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 4]
द्रोहपरे द्रोहपरो भवेदिति समानता ।
अद्रोहिणि तथा शान्ते विद्वेषः खलता स्मृता ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation:
O Lord of the Earth! It is natural, that injury be inflicted on those persons that commit injury; but when peaceful persons, void of any enmity, are injured, that is certainly an act wicked and mischievous.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 5]
यः कश्चित्तापसः शान्तो जपध्यानपरायणः ।
भवेत्तस्य जपे विघ्नकर्ता वै मघवा परम् ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation:
Whenever, any devout ascetic, calm and quiet, is engaged in prayer and meditation, and silent muttering of one’s mantrams, the king of the Immortals throws hindrance in his asceticism. (This is certainly a mischievous act.)
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 6]
सतां सत्ययुगं साक्षात्सर्वदैवासतां कलिः ।
मध्यमो मध्यमानां तु क्रियायोगौ युगे स्मृतौ ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation:
(Holy, unholy and mixed persons exist in all the yugas). To those that are holy, all the yugas are the Satya yuga; to the unholy ones always it is the Kali yuga (Dark age); and to the mixed ones, always it is Tretā and Dvāpara.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 7]
कश्चित्कदाचिद्भवति सत्यधर्मानुवर्तकः ।
अन्यथान्ययुगानां वै सर्वे धर्मपरायणाः ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation:
You will very seldom find a few persons, following really the True Religion; otherwise, you would have found all the persons in the different yugas religious, appropriate to those yugas.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 8]
वासना कारणं राजन् सर्वत्र धर्मसंस्थितौ ।
तस्यां वै मलिनायां तु धर्मोऽपि मलिनो भवेत् ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation:
O king! In all cases where the conservation of religions and religious affairs are concerned, know that the original wish and desire is the cause. If this desire be impure and sullied, religion becomes also sullied for, verily, this impurity in one’s desire is one’s cause of ruin in every respect. (Therefore the impure desires are never to be cherished and indulged.)
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 9]
मलिना वासना सत्यं विनाशायेति सर्वथा ।
ब्रह्मणो हृदयाज्जातः पुत्रो धर्म इति स्मृतः ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation:
A son, named Dharma, was born of the heart of Brahmā; he was devoted to Brahmājñāna (the knowledge of Brahmā), truthful, and always engaged in rites and ceremonies and in accordance with the Vedic religion.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 10]
ब्राह्मणः सत्यसम्पन्नो वेदधर्मरतः सदा ।
दक्षस्य दुहितारो हि वृता दश महात्मना ॥ १० ॥
English Translation:
This high souled Muni Dharma was a householder and married duly, according to the proper procedure, to the ten daughters of Dakṣa Prajāpati.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 11]
विवाहविधिना सम्यङ्मुनिना गृहधर्मिणा ।
तास्वजीजनयत्पुत्रान्धर्मः सत्यवतां वरः ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation:
This Dharma, the foremost amongst the followers of truth, impregnated them and had four sons, named respectively Hari, Kṛṣṇa, Nara, and Nārāyaṇa.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 12]
हरिं कृष्णं नरं चैव तथा नारायणं नृप ।
योगाभ्यासरतो नित्यं हरिः कृष्णो बभूव ह ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation:
Hari and Kṛṣṇa, amongst the four, used to remain always in the practising of the yoga.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 13]
नरनारायणौ चैव चेरतुस्तप उत्तमम् ।
प्रालेयाद्रिं समागत्य तीर्थे बदरिकाश्रमे ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation:
Nara and Nārāyaṇa came over to the Himālayān mountains and, in the hermitage of Badarikā, commenced the difficult religious asceticism and penance.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 14]
तपस्विषु धुरीणौ तौ पुराणौ मुनिसत्तमौ ।
गृणन्तौ तत्परं ब्रह्म गङ्गाया विपुले तटे ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation:
The foremost of the ascetics, those two ancient Munis, began to recite that highest mantra of Para Brahmā, the Gāyatrī, on the wide spacious bank of the Ganges.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 15]
हरेरंशौ स्थितौ तत्र नरनारायणावृषी ।
पूर्णं वर्षसहस्रं तु चक्राते तप उत्तमम् ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation:
The two Risis named Nara and Nārāyaṇa, born of Hari’s Aṃśa, practised excellent tapasyā for full one thousand years.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 16]
तापितं च जगत्सर्वं तपसा सचराचरम् ।
नरनारायणाभ्यां च शक्रः क्षोभं तदा ययौ ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation:
The whole Universe, moving and unmoving, became hot through the Fire of their Tapas. Indra became also perplexed.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 17-18]
चिन्ताविष्टः सहस्राक्षो मनसा समकल्पयत् ।
किं कर्तव्यं धर्मपुत्रौ तापसौ ध्यानसंयुतौ ॥ १७ ॥

सिद्धार्थां सुभृशं श्रेष्ठमासनं मे ग्रहीष्यतः ।
विघ्नः कथं प्रकर्तव्यस्तपो येन भवेन्न हि ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation:
The thousand-eyed Indra became anxious, thought and within himself thus :-- What is to be done now? These two sons of Dharma are practising Tapas and are in meditation. If they succeed, they can occupy my excellent seat in Heaven; how can I break their Tapasyā and what steps shall I take to hinder them.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 19-20]
उत्पाद्य कामं क्रोधञ्च लोभं वाप्यतिदारुणम् ।
इत्युद्दिश्य सहस्राक्षः समारुह्य गजोत्तमम् ॥ १९ ॥

विघ्नकामस्तु तरसा जगाम गन्धमादनम् ।
गत्वा तत्राश्रमे पुण्ये तावपश्यच्छतक्रतुः ॥ २० ॥
English Translation:
Lust, anger, and insurmountable avarice Indra brought into existence and, intent on hindering their tapasyā, mounted on the elephant Airāvata, went quickly to the hill Gandhamādan, and approaching the holy hermitage, saw the two ancient Ṛṣis.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 21]
तपसा दीप्तदेहौ तु भास्कराविव चोदितौ ।
ब्रह्मविष्णू किमेतौ वै प्रकटौ वा विभावसू ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation:
Their bodies were incandescent by Tapasyā, as if they were the two rising Suns. Were they Brahmā, Viṣṇu manifested there or were they the two shining sources of light? These two Ṛṣis were the sons of Dharma. What would they do with their Tapasyā?
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 22-23]
धर्मपुत्रावृषी एतौ तपसा किं करिष्यतः ।
इति सञ्चिन्त्य तौ दृष्ट्वा तदोवाच शचीपतिः ॥ २२ ॥

किं वा कार्यं महाभागौ ब्रूतं धर्मसुतौ किल ।
ददामि वां वरं श्रेष्ठं दातुं यातोऽस्म्यहमृषी ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation:
Thinking thus, the lord of Śacī seeing them addressed thus :-- O highly fortunate ones! O two Ṛṣis the sons of Dharma! Please tell me what are your objects? I have come here to give thee excellent boons; I am very pleased with your Tapasyā; therefore ask boons from me; and even if they be not worth giving, I will give them to you.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 24-25]
अदेयमपि दास्यामि तुष्टोऽस्मि तपसा किल ।
व्यास उवाच ।
एवं पुनः पुनः शक्रस्तावुवाच पुरः स्थितः ॥ २४ ॥

नोचतुस्तावृषी ध्यानसंस्थितौ दृढचेतसौ ।
ततो वै मोहिनीं मायां चकार भयदां वृषः ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- The Ṛṣis were deeply immersed in meditation and seemed very firm and resolute; they, therefore, did not reply anything, though Indra, standing before them, repeatedly urged them to ask boons from him. Seeing this, the king of the Immortals began to terrify them with his supernatural enchanting fearful māyic powers.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 26]
वृकान्सिंहांश्च व्याघ्रांश्च समुत्पाद्याबिभीषयत् ।
वर्षं वातं तथा वह्निं समुत्पाद्य पुनः पुनः ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation:
He created lions, tigers, wolves and other murderous animals and began to terrify the two Ṛṣis with them; Indra also produced rains, hurricanes and fires very frequently so that they might yield.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 27]
भीषयामास तौ शक्रो मायां कृत्वा विमोहिनीम् ।
भयतोऽपि वशं नीतौ न तौ धर्मसुतौ मुनी ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation:
In spite of Indra’s attempt to terrify them by his wonderful Māyā, the two Munis, Nara Nārāyaṇa, the two sons of Dharma, could not be brought under his control. And Indra returned to his own place.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 28-31]
नरनारायणौ दृष्ट्वा शक्रः स्वभवनं गतः ।
वरदाने प्रलुब्धौ न न भीतौ वह्निवायुतः ॥ २८ ॥

व्याघ्रसिंहादिभिः कान्तौ चलितौ नाश्रमात्स्वकात् ।
न तयोर्ध्यानभङ्गं वै कर्तुं कोऽपि क्षमोऽभवत् ॥ २९ ॥

इन्द्रोऽपि सदनं गत्वा चिन्तयामास दुःखितः ।
चलितौ भयलोभाभ्यां नेमौ मुनिवरोत्तमौ ॥ ३० ॥

चिन्तयन्तौ महाविद्यामादिशक्तिं सनातनीम् ।
ईश्वरीं सर्वलोकानां परां प्रकृतिमद्भुताम् ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation:
And he became very sorry and thought thus :-- These two Munis could not be tempted away with boons, nor did they fly away from their place of worship, though terrified with fire, wind, wolves, tigers and lions. No one, I think, would be able to break their meditation. When fear and temptations have not distracted their meditation, they are certainly meditating on the Eternal Mahā Vidyā Śrī Bhuvaneśwarī, the Prime Force of Nature, the Source of all Māyās, and the Goddess the Creatrix of all the worlds, the wonderful highest Prakriti; what other expert in emitting Māyā there can be? Who can break their meditation!
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 32]
ध्यायतां कः क्षमो लोके बहुमायाविदप्युत ।
यन्मूलाः सकला माया देवासुरकृताः किल ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation:
Indeed! how can this whole host of Māyās that are created by Gods and Asuras overpower those purged of all their sins, who are meditating their Creator, the Supreme Māyā, that Illusion by which one considers the unreal Universe as really existent and as distinct from the Supreme Spirit, whence the Gods and Asuras have derived all their supernatural powers.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 33]
ते कथं बाधितुं शक्ता ध्यायन्ति गतकल्मषाः ।
वाग्बीजं कामबीजञ्च मायाबीजं तथैव च ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation:
He in whose heart reign the seed mantras of Vāk, Kāma and Māyā, called Vāgvījam, Kāmavijam, Māyāvījam, no one is able to stand against and overpower him.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 34-35]
चित्ते यस्य भवेत्तं तु बाधितुं कोऽपि न क्षमः ।
मायया मोहितः शक्रो भूयस्तस्य प्रतिक्रियाम् ॥ ३४ ॥

कर्तुं कामवसन्तौ तु समाहूयाब्रवीद्वचः ।
मनोभव वसन्तेन रत्या युक्तो व्रजाधुना ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation:
O king! Indra, enchanted by Māyā, did not desist from tempting the two Ṛṣis, but he went on thinking other means by which their asceticism could be baffled and asked Kāma and Vasanta (the god of Lust and the season spring) to come before him and addressed them, thus :-- O Kāma! You now be united with your wife Rati and Vasanta (the God of spring) and go to the hill Gandhamādan, accompanied by all the Apsarās (celestial damsels) and with all the Rasas (love sentiments). NOTE :-- The Gandhamādan is the mountain like unsurpassable intoxicating happiness of the senses.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 36]
अप्सरोभिः समायुक्तस्तरसा गन्धमादनम् ।
नरनारायणौ तत्र पुराणावृषिसत्तमौ ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation:
There you will find the two ancient excellent Ṛṣis Nara and Nārāyaṇa practising asceticism in solitude, in the hermitage of Badarikā. O Manamatha! You better go before them, and with the influence of your arrows, do now my work and make their hearts extremely lustful.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 37-38]
कुरुतस्तप एकान्ते स्थितौ बदरिकाश्रमे ।
गत्वा तत्र समीपे तु तयोर्मन्मथ मार्गणैः ॥ ३७ ॥

चित्तं कामातुरं कार्यं कुरु कार्यं ममाधुना ।
मोहयित्वोच्चाटयित्वा विशिखैस्ताडयाशु च ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation:
O Fortunate One! Charm over them by means of your arrows, make them leave their asceticism by magical spells.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 39]
वशीकुरु महाभाग मुनी धर्मसुतावपि ।
को ह्यस्मिन् सर्वसंसारे देवो दैत्योऽथ मानवः ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation:
Who is there in this world of Devas, Daityas, human beings, that, being whipped by your arrows, do not come under your control?
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 40]
यस्ते बाणवशं प्राप्तो न याति भृशताडितः ।
ब्रह्माहं गिरिजानाथश्चन्द्रो वह्निर्विमोहितः ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation:
When Brahmā, I, Mahādeva, Moon and Fire are all fascinated by your arrows, then is there any doubt that these two Ṛṣis would not be fascinated by them!
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 41]
गणना कानयोः काम त्वद्बाणानां पराक्रमे ।
वाराङ्गनागणोऽयं ते सहायार्थं मयेरितः ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation:
I am sending these public women as your assistants. Rambhā and other beautiful celestial nymphs would all follow you.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 42]
आगमिष्यति तत्रैव रम्भादीनां मनोरमः ।
एका तिलोत्तमा रम्भा कार्यं साधयितुं क्षमा ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation:
You alone, or Rambhā or Tilottamā alone can do this work. Will there be any doubt if you all unite in this?
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 43]
त्वमेवैकः क्षमः कामं मिलितैः कस्तु संशयः ।
कुरु कार्यं महाभाग ददामि तव वाञ्छितम् ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation:
O Good One! Do this work for me; I will confer on you your desired objects.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 44]
प्रलोभितौ मयात्यर्थं वरदानैस्तपस्विनौ ।
स्थानान्न चलितौ शान्तौ वृथायं मे गतः श्रमः ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation:
O Manmatha! I tempted them with boons but these two ascetics, of controlled minds, could not be displaced from their seats. My efforts were rendered useless.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 45]
तथा वै मायया कृत्वा भीषितौ तापसौ भृशम् ।
तथापि नोत्थितौ स्थानाद्देहरक्षापरौ न तौ ॥ ४५ ॥
English Translation:
I frightened them very much with all the Māyic powers; yet they could not be dislocated from their deep thoughts. It seems that they are quite heedless in the preservation of their bodies.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 46]
व्यास उवाच ।
इति तस्य वचः श्रुत्वा शक्रं प्राह मनोभवः ।
वासवाद्य करिष्यामि कार्यं ते मनसेप्सितम् ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- Kāmadeva, on hearing the king of the Devas, addressed him thus :-- O Indra! Today I will fulfill all your desires.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 47]
यदि विष्णुं महेशं वा ब्रह्माणं वा दिवाकरम् ।
ध्यायन्तौ तौ तदास्माकं भवितारौ वशौ मुनी ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation:
But there is one word. If these two ascetics be meditating Viṣṇu, Śiva or Brahmā or the Sun, then I will be able to bring them under my control.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 48-49]
देवीभक्तं वशीकर्तुं नाहं शक्तः कथञ्चन ।
कामराज महाबीजं चिन्तयन्तं मनस्यलम् ॥ ४८ ॥

तां देवीं चेन्महाशक्तिं संश्रितौ भक्तिभावतः ।
न तदा मम बाणानां गोचरौ तापसौ किल ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation:
If these two ascetics have devotedly taken refuge of the Great Power Mahā Devī, then they will not come under the sight of my arrows.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 5 Verse 50]
इन्द्र उवाच ।
गच्छ त्वं च महाभाग सर्वैस्तत्र समुद्यतैः ।
कार्यं ममातिदुःसाध्यं कर्ता हितमनुत्तमम् ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation:
Indra said :-- O Blessed One! Go now with your assistants, ready to do your work. No body but you, I find, that can fulfill my this beneficial, though very difficult work.

[Ch 5 Verse 51-52]
व्यास उवाच ।
इति तेन समादिष्टा ययुः सर्वे समुद्यताः ।
यत्र तौ धर्मपुत्रौ द्वौ तेपाते दुष्करं तपः ॥ ५१ ॥

इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां
चतुर्थस्कन्धे नरनारायणकथावर्णनं नाम पञ्चमोऽध्यायः ॥ ४.५॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- Thus ordered by Indra, they all departed to where the Dharma’s sons Nara, and Nārāyaṇa were performing their hard Tapasyās. Here ends the Fifth Chapter in the Fourth Book of Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam, the Mahā Purāṇam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

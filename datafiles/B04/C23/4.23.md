# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 4, CHAPTER 23

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 1-2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ४.२३ त्रयोविंशोऽध्यायः । कंसं प्रति योगमायावाक्यम् ।
> 
> 
> व्यास उवाच ।
> हतेषु षट्सु पुत्रेषु देवक्या औग्रसेनिना ।
> सप्तमे पतिते गर्भे वचनान्नारदस्य च ॥ १ ॥
> 
> अष्टमस्य च गर्भस्य रक्षणार्थमतन्द्रितः ।
> प्रयत्नमकरोद्राजा मरणं स्वं विचिन्तयन् ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- The six sons of Devakī being thus killed by Kaṃsa and the seventh womb being miscarried, Kaṃsa began, when Devakī was pregnant for the eighth time, to think of the cause of his own death as advised by Nārada and watched carefully, day and night, so that this time there would be born a son and there be no miscarriage.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> समये देवकीगर्भे प्रवेशमकरोद्धरिः ।
> अंशेन वसुदेवे तु समागत्य यथाक्रमम् ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On the other hand the Bhagavān Hari came down and rested on the body of Vāsudeva as part incarnate and then entered into the womb of Devakī, in due order.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तदेयं योगमाया च यशोदायां यथेच्छया ।
> प्रवेशमकरोद्देवी देवकार्यार्थसिद्धये ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

That very time the Goddess Yogamāyā, of Her own accord, enters into the womb of Yasodā in order to help the workings of the gods.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रोहिण्यास्तनयो रामो गोकुले समजायत ।
> यतः कंसभयोद्विग्ना संस्थिता सा च कामिनी ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Rohinī, the wife of Vāsudeva was anxiously dwelling at that time through the fear of Kaṃsa in the Nanda Gokula; Balarāma, the part incarnate of Ananta, took his birth there as Her son.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कारागारे ततः कंसो देवकीं देवसंस्तुताम् ।
> स्थापयामास रक्षार्थं सेवकान्समकल्पयत् ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Kaṃsa then imprisoned Devakī, who was being worshipped by gods, and engaged servants to take care of her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वसुदेवस्तु कामिन्याः प्रेमतन्तुनियन्त्रितः ।
> पुत्रोत्पत्तिं च सञ्चिन्त्य प्रविष्टः सह भार्यया ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vāsudeva, too, entered into the prison with his wife Devakī, thinking of his issue and also being attracted by his attachment towards his wife.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देवकीगर्भगो विष्णुर्देवकार्यार्थसिद्धये ।
> संस्तुतोऽमरसङ्घैश्च व्यवर्धत यथाक्रमम् ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Viṣṇu, here, the Deva of the Devas, to accomplish the purpose of the gods began to grow regularly in the womb of Devakī, being incessantly adored and praised by the gods.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 9-10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सञ्जाते दशमे तत्र मासेऽथ श्रावणे शुभे ।
> प्राजापत्यर्क्षसंयुक्ते कृष्णपक्षेऽष्टमीदिने ॥ ९ ॥
> 
> कंसस्तु दानवान्सर्वानुवाच भयविह्वलः ।
> रक्षणीया भवद्भिश्च देवकी गर्भमन्दिरे ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When the tenth month was complete, on the eighth day of the dark fortnight, under the star Rohinī, Kaṃsa became much bewildered with fear and called on his Dānava followers and said “You must all carefully protect Devakī in the prison.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 11-12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अष्टमो देवकीगर्भः शत्रुर्मे प्रभविष्यति ।
> रक्षणीयः प्रयत्नेन मृत्युरूपः स बालकः ॥ ११ ॥
> 
> हत्वैनं बालकं दैत्याः सुखं स्वप्स्यामि मन्दिरे ।
> निवृत्तिवर्जिते दुःखे नाशिते चाष्टमे सुते ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The son born from this eighth womb of Devakī will turn out my dire enemy; therefore do you all very carefully protect that child, my death incarnate (so that the child may not be transferred anywhere else). O Daityas! I will be able to sleep without any anxiety then, when I will be successful. Kill this eighth son of Devakī who has become the source of my constant worries and endless troubles.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> खड्गप्रासधराः सर्वे तिष्ठन्तु धृतकार्मुकाः ।
> निद्रातन्द्राविहीनाश्च सर्वत्र निहितेक्षणाः ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Equipped with swords, shafts and bow and darts do you all incessantly watch with eyes open on all sides, leaving off your sleep and drowsiness.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इत्यादिश्यासुरगणान् कृशोऽतिभयविह्वलः ।
> मन्दिरं स्वं जगामाशु न लेभे दानवः सुखम् ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- Thus ordering the Demons, the king Kaṃsa who had become worn out with this anxiety and was bewildered with fear, went quickly to his own palace room; but he could not find any trace of happiness.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 15-18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> निशीथे देवकी तत्र वसुदेवमुवाच ह ।
> किं करोमि महाराज प्रसवावसरो मम ॥ १५ ॥
> 
> बहवो रक्षपालाश्च तिष्ठन्त्यत्र भयानकाः ।
> नन्दपत्न्या मया सार्धं कृतोऽस्ति समयः पुरा ॥ १६ ॥
> 
> प्रेषितव्यस्त्वया पुत्रो मन्दिरे मम मानिनि ।
> पालयिष्याम्यहं तत्र तवातिमनसा किल ॥ १७ ॥
> 
> अपत्यं ते प्रदास्यामि कंसस्य प्रत्ययाय वै ।
> किं कर्तव्यं प्रभो चात्र विषमे समुपस्थिते ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On the other hand, Devakī, at dead of night, in that prison, told Vāsudeva “O king! My birth pain has come; I see here many guards, terrific, who are watching and protecting us; what shall I do now? Yasodā, the wife of Nanda, promised me before this. O respected one! Your heart is almost burnt up with the fire of grief; therefore do you send to my house your son; I will protect it with utmost care; and especially to iñcur Kaṃsa’s faith and belief, I will give you another son. O Lord! This is now a very hard moment; what are we to do now?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यत्ययः सन्ततेः शौरे कथं कर्तुं क्षमो भवेः ।
> दूरे तिष्ठस्व कान्ताद्य लज्जा मेऽतिदुरत्यया ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

And again how are you to exchange the two sons? However let what may come, come to pass; now the moment of child birth has come; I am possessed by a feeling of shame not liable to be overcome.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

You better turn away your face; there is no other remedy here.” 20. Thus saying to that blessed Vāsudeva, worshipped by the gods, Devakī, at dead of night, delivered a wonderful child.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 21</b></summary>

>
**English Translation:**

The blessed Devakī looking on that exceedingly beautiful child, was struck with wonder and told her husband, her whole body being filled with joy.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पतिं प्राह महाभागा हर्षोत्कुल्लकलेवरा ।

>
**English Translation:**

O Lord! Look at the face of your new born child, a very rare thing to see such a face as this. Alas! The son of my paternal uncle, Kaṃsa, will kill my this new born child.

</details>

<details>

>
**English Translation:**

“Kaṃsa will do so.” Saying this, Vāsudeva took up that child in his arms and began to look amorously at the face of that child of wonderful deeds.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 24</b></summary>

>
**English Translation:**

Thus looking, Vāsudeva thought “What can I do now so as to relieve me from my sorrows owing to the future destruction of this child.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 25-27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> किं करोमि कथं न स्याद्दुःखमस्य कृते मम ।
> एवं चिन्तातुरे तस्मिन्वागुवाचाशरीरिणी ॥ २५ ॥
> 
> वसुदेवं समाभाष्य गगने विशदाक्षरा ।
> वसुदेव गृहीत्वैनं गोकुलं नय सत्वरः ॥ २६ ॥
> 
> रक्षपालास्तथा सर्वे मया निद्राविमोहिताः ।
> विवृतानि कृतान्यष्ट कपाटानि च शृङ्खलाः ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

While Vāsudeva was thus anxiously pondering over, the Heaven Voice clearly said thus :-- “O Vāsudeva! Quickly go to Gokula with the child. The keepers and guards I have made dead asleep by My Māyā. The eight very strong doors are now lying wide open. You better free yourself from your chains and take this child, keep it in the house of Nanda and bring Yoga Māyā from there and come back here.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 28-29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मुक्त्वैनं नन्दगेहे त्वं योगमायां समानय ।
> श्रुत्वैवं वसुदेवस्तु तस्मिन्कारागृहे गतः ॥ २८ ॥
> 
> विवृतं द्वारमालोक्य बभूव तरसा नृप ।
> तमादाय ययावाशु द्वारपालैरलक्षितः ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus hearing the Heavenly Voice, he cast his glance at the doors and found them all wide open. O King! Very quickly then he took the child and stepped out of the prison, quite unnoticed by the guards and watchmen. Going to the banks of the Jumnā he found there the daughter of Kalindi, flowing with great force and became anxious.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 30-31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कालिन्दीतटमासाद्य पूरं दृष्ट्वा सुनिश्चितम् ।
> तदैव कटिदघ्नी सा बभूवाशु सरिद्वरा ॥ ३० ॥

> योगमायाप्रभावेण ततारानकदुन्दुभिः ।

>
**English Translation:**

But the river Jumnā instantaneously turned out to be easily fordable just knee deep; then Vāsudeva, guided by Yoga Māyā, crossed the Jumnā and betaking to an unfrequented road arrived at Gokul at dead of night. There, at the doorway of the house of Nanda, he began to see the cows, buffaloes, the wealth and property of Nanda.

</details>

<details>

**English Translation:**

At that very moment, there was born of Yośodā, the Mahā Devī, the part of the Divine Yoga Māyā, the Incarnate of the three qualities.

</details>

Then the Mahādevī Yoga Māyā, assuming the appearance of a female artisan, taking that divine female child in her arms came there and handed her over to Vāsudeva.

</details>

Vāsudeva, too, delivered his child over to the lotus like hands of the Devī and, taking the female child instead, quickly returned with his heart full of joy.

</details>

<details>

</details>

</details>

> सा करान्निःसृता बाला ययावाकाशमण्डलम् ॥ ४५ ॥
> 
> दिव्यरूपा तदा भूत्वा तमुवाच मृदुस्वना ।
> किं मया हतया पाप जातस्ते बलवान् रिपुः ॥ ४६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thinking thus, the cruel King Kaṃsa caught hold of that female child by her legs and, raising her high up in the air, was going to strike her against a stone, when the female child slipped out of his hands and flying into the air assumed a divine appearance and gently spoke to Kaṃsa, thus :-- “What will you get by killing me? Your powerful enemy is already born on the earth. O vilest of men! Disgrace to your family! He, the Excellent Human being Who is very difficult to be worshiped will certainly kill you.”

</details>

Saying thus, the auspicious female child, able to go anywhere according to her will, disappeared. Go all of you to serve my purpose. Kill anywhere the child whom you see just born.

The cruel king Kaṃsa, thus ordering the Demons, went away to his own palace, and, thinking on this matter over and over again became, very much afflicted with fear and his mind got immensely depressed.

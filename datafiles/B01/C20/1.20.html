On Vyāsa doing his duties

Ch 20 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
१.२० विंशोऽध्यायः । धृतराष्ट्रादीनामुत्पत्तिवर्णनम् । ऋषय ऊचुः । शुकस्तु परमां सिद्धिमाप्तवान्देवसत्तमः । किं चकार ततो व्यासस्तन्नो ब्रूहि सविस्तरम् ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
The Ṛṣis said :-- “O Sūta! What did Veda Vyāsa do, when the highest Yogi Śūka, Deva-like, acquired all the excellent supernatural powers? Kindly describe all these in detail.”
Ch 20 Verse 2-3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सूत उवाच । शिष्या व्यासस्य येऽप्यासन्वेदाभ्यासपरायणाः । आज्ञामादाय ते सर्वे गताः पूर्वं महीतले ॥ २ ॥ असितो देवलश्चैव वैशम्पायन एव च । जैमिनिश्च सुमन्तुश्च गताः सर्वे तपोधनाः ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
Hearing this question, Sūta spoke :-- O Rishis! Vyāsa already had with him many disciples Asita, Devala, Vaiśampāyana, Jaimini, Sumantu and others, all engaged in the study of the Vedas.
Ch 20 Verse 4-6 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तानेतान्वीक्ष्य पुत्रं च लोकान्तरितमप्युत । व्यासः शोकसमाक्रान्तो गमनायाकरोन्मतिम् ॥ ४ ॥ सस्मार मनसा व्यासस्तां निषादसुतां शुभाम् । मातरं जाह्नवीतीरे मुक्तां शोकसमन्विताम् ॥ ५ ॥ स्मृत्वा सत्यवतीं व्यासस्त्यक्त्वा तं पर्वतोत्तमम् । आजगाम महातेजा जन्मस्थानं स्वकं मुनिः ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation
After their studies were over, they all went out to propagate Dharma on the earth. Then Vyāsa , seeing that the disciples went to the earth and his son Śūka Deva had got to the next world, became very much distressed with sorrow and wanted to go to some other place. He then decided to go to his birth place and went to the banks of the Ganges and there remembered his auspicious mother Satyavatī, forsaken by him before, very sorrowful, and the daughter of a fisherman. He then quitted that heaven-like mountain, the source of all happiness and came to his own birth place.
Ch 20 Verse 7 Verse
Sanskrit Text
द्वीपं प्राप्याथ पप्रच्छ क्व गता सा वरानना । निषादास्तं समाचख्युर्दत्ता राज्ञे तु कन्यका ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
Reaching the island where he was born, he enquired the whereabouts of the beautiful faced, the fisherman’s daughter as well the wife of a king. The fishermen replied that their king had given her in marriage to the king Śantanu.

English Translation
the king of fishermen, seeing Vyāsa there, gladly worshipped him and gave him a cordial welcome and spoke with folded palms, thus :--
Ch 20 Verse 9 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दाशराज उवाच । अद्य मे सफलं जन्म पावितं नः कुलं मुने । देवानामपि दुर्दर्शं यज्जातं तव दर्शनम् ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation
O Muni! When I have become so fortunate as to see you, rare even to the Devas, then my birth has been sanctified today and you have purified my family.
Ch 20 Verse 10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यदर्थमागतोऽसि त्वं तद्ब्रूहि द्विजसत्तम । अपि दारा धनं पुत्रास्त्वदायत्तमिदं विभो ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
O Brāhmin! Kindly say what for have you come? My wife, son and all my riches and every other thing that I have are at your disposal.
Ch 20 Verse 11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सरस्वत्यास्तटे रम्ये चकाराश्रममण्डलम् । व्यासस्तपःसमायुक्तस्तत्रैवास समाहितः ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
Thus hearing the history of his mother Satyavatī, Vyāsa erected an Āśrama on the beautiful banks of the river Sarasvatī and remained there in tapasyā with an enlightened mind.
Ch 20 Verse 12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सत्यवत्याः सुतौ जातौ शन्तनोरमितद्युतेः । मत्वा तौ भ्रातरौ व्यासः सुखमाप वने स्थितः ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
Some time elapsed when the highly energetic Śantanu got through his wife Satyavatī two sons. Vyāsa Deva considered them as his two brothers and became very glad, though he himself used to live in the forest.
Ch 20 Verse 13-15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
चित्राङ्गदः प्रथमजो रूपवाञ्छत्रुतापनः । बभूव नृपतेः पुत्रः सर्वलक्षणसंयुतः ॥ १३ ॥ विचित्रवीर्यनामासौ द्वितीयः समजायत । सोऽपि सर्वगुणोपेतः शन्तनोः सुखवर्धनः ॥ १४ ॥ गाङ्गेयः प्रथमस्तस्य महावीरो बलाधिकः । तथैव तौ सुतौ जातौ सत्यवत्यां महाबलौ ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
The first son of the king Śantanu was Citrāṅgada, endowed with all auspicious qualities, exceedingly beautiful, and tormenting his foes; the second son was Vicitra-vīrya; he was endowed with all qualities. The king Śantanu became very happy to get these children. Śantanu had one son before through his wife Gaṅgā; he was a great hero and very powerful; and the two sons of Satyavatī were equally powerful.
Ch 20 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शन्तनुस्तान्सुतान्वीक्ष्य सर्वलक्षणसंयुतान् । अमंस्ताजय्यमात्मानं देवादीनां महामनाः ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
The high souled Śantanu now seeing the three sons, all endowed with all auspicious qualities, began to think that the Devas were incapable to defeat him.
Ch 20 Verse 17 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अथ कालेन कियता शन्तनुः कालपर्ययात् । तत्याज देहं धर्मात्मा देही जीर्णमिवाम्बरम् ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation
After some time, the religious Śantanu quitted his worn-out body as a man quits his clothes worn out in due time.
Ch 20 Verse 18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कालधर्मगते राज्ञि भीष्मश्चक्रे विधानतः । प्रेतकार्याणि सर्वाणि दानानि विविधानि च ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
After the king Śantanu had ascended the Heavens, the energetic Bhīśma performed duly his funeral obsequies and gave various things in charity to the Brāhmaṇas.
Ch 20 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
चित्राङ्गदं ततो राज्ये स्थापयामास वीर्यवान् । स्वयं न कृतवान् राज्यं तस्माद्देवव्रतोऽभवत् ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
He did not accept the kingdom himself; but placed Citrāṅgada on the throne and became known by the name of Devavrata (truthful in vow like the Devas).
Ch 20 Verse 20 Verse
Sanskrit Text
चित्राङ्गदस्तु वीर्येण प्रमत्तः परदुःखदः । बभूव बलवान्वीरः सत्यवत्यात्मजः शुचिः ॥ २० ॥
English Translation
The pure souled Citrāṅgada, born of Satyavatī became so much powerful by sheer force of his arms, and became so great a hero that the enemies felt endless troubles.
Ch 20 Verse 21-22 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अथैकदा महाबाहुः सैन्येन महतावृतः । प्रचचार वनोद्देशात्पश्यन्वध्यान्मृगान् रुरून् ॥ २१ ॥ चित्राङ्गदस्तु गन्धर्वो दृष्ट्वा तं मार्गगं नृपम् । उत्ततारान्तिकं भूमेर्विमानवरमास्थितः ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation
Now once on an occasion, the greatly powerful Citrāṅgada, surrounded by a great army, went on an hunting excursion to the forest in quest of rurū deer, etc., when the Gandarbha Citrāṅgada, seeing the king on the way, alighted from his chariot.
Ch 20 Verse 23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तत्राभूच्च महद्युद्धं तयोः सदृशवीर्ययोः । कुरुक्षेत्रे महास्थाने त्रीणि वर्षाणि तापसाः ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
O ascetics! A fierce battle then ensued for three years on that sacred and wide expanse Kurukṣettra between the two heroes, both equally powerful.
Ch 20 Verse 24-25 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इन्द्रलोकमवापाशु गन्धर्वेण हतो रणे । भीष्मः श्रुत्वा चकाराशु तस्यौर्ध्वदैहिकं तदा ॥ २४ ॥ गाङ्गेयः कृतशोकस्तु मन्त्रिभिः परिवारितः । विचित्रवीर्यनामानं राज्येशं च चकार ह ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation
In the battle, the king Citrāṅgada, the son of Śantanu was slain by the Gandarbha Citrāṅgada and went up to Heavens. Bhīśma, born of the womb of Gaṅgā, hearing the above news, expressed his sorrows and, being surrounded by the ministers completed all the funeral obsequies and installed Vicitravīrya on the throne.
Ch 20 Verse 26-27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मन्त्रिभिर्बोधिता पश्चाद्गुरुभिश्च महात्मभिः । स्वपुत्रं राज्यगं दृष्ट्वा पुत्रशोकहतापि च ॥ २६ ॥ सत्यवत्यतिसन्तुष्टा बभूव वरवर्णिनी । व्यासोऽपि भ्रातरं श्रुत्वा राजानं मुदितोऽभवत् ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
beautiful Satyavatī became very much agitated by the death of her son; but when the ministers and the highsouled spiritual teachers consoled her, she became glad when she saw that her youngest son became king. Vyāsa Deva, too, felt himself glad to hear that his youngest brother had been made king.
Ch 20 Verse 28 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
After some time when the all auspicious, Satyavatī’s son Vicītravīrya attained his youth, Bhīśma began to think of his marriage.
Ch 20 Verse 29 Verse

English Translation
At this time the king of Kāsī (Kāśīrāj) called an assembly Svayamvara (where the kings are invited and the bride selects the bridegroom) for the marriage of her three daughters, endowed with all auspicious qualities, at one and the same time.

English Translation
Thousands and thousands of kings and princes from various countries were invited there in the assembly; and, worshipped duly, they went and decorated the hall.

English Translation
At that time the highly energetic fiery Bhīśma alone, mounting on his chariot, attacked the infantry and cavalry, and defeated all the kings assembled there, and perforce carried away the three daughters of Kāśīrāj and took them to Hastināpur.

Ch 20 Verse 37 Verse
Sanskrit Text

Ch 20 Verse 38-39 Verse

Ch 20 Verse 48 Verse
Sanskrit Text

Ch 20 Verse 49 Verse
Sanskrit Text

Hearing her words Bhīśma said :-- O beautiful one! How can I accept you, when your mind has become attached towards another. So, O fair one! You better go back soon to your own father with a calm, clear mind.

Now the other two daughters of Kāsīrāj, beautiful and all auspicious Ambālikā and Ambikā became the wives of the king Vicītravīrya.
Ch 20 Verse 52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
राजा विचित्रवीर्योऽसौ ताभ्यां सह महाबलः । रेमे नानाविहारैश्च गृहे चोपवने तथा ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation
Thus the powerful king Vicītravīrya began to enjoy various pleasures in the palace and in the gardens and thus passed his time.
Ch 20 Verse 53 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वर्षाणि नव राजेन्द्रः कुर्वन् क्रीडा मनोरमाम् । प्रापासौ मरणं भूयो गृहीतो राजयक्ष्मणा ॥ ५३ ॥
English Translation
For full nine years the king Vicītravīrya enjoyed the sexual pleasures and became attacked with consumption and fell into the jaws of death.
Ch 20 Verse 54 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मृते पुत्रेऽतिदुःखार्ता जाता सत्यवती तदा । कारयामास पुत्रस्य प्रेतकार्याणि मन्त्रिभिः ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation
Hearing the death news of her son Vicītravīrya, Satyavatī became very sorry and surrounded by her ministers, performed his funeral obsequies.
Ch 20 Verse 55-56 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भीष्ममाह तदैकान्ते वचनं चातिदुःखिता । राज्यं कुरु महाभाग पितुस्ते शन्तनोः सुत ॥ ५५ ॥ भ्रातुर्भार्यां गृहाण त्वं वंशञ्च परिरक्षय । यथा न नाशमायाति ययातेर्वंश इत्युत ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation
Then she spoke privately to Bhīśma with a grievous heart :-- “O highly fortunately son! now you better govern your father’s kingdom and see that the family of Yayāti does not become extinct. So better take your brother’s wife and try your best to continue your family line.
Ch 20 Verse 57 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भीष्म उवाच । प्रतिज्ञा मे श्रुता मातः पित्रर्थे या मया कृता । नाहं राज्यं करिष्यामि न चाहं दारसङ्ग्रहम् ॥ ५७ ॥
English Translation
Bhīśma then said :-- "O Mother! Did you not hear of the promise that I already made before my father? So I cannot ever marry and govern the kingdom.” Hearing these words of Bhīśma, Satyavatī became anxious.
Ch 20 Verse 58 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सूत उवाच । तदा चिन्तातुरा जाता कथं वंशो भवेदिति । नालसाद्धि सुखं मह्यं समुत्पन्ने ह्यराजके ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation
She began to think as follows :-- “How now the continuity of the family be kept! And it is not advisable to remain idle when the kingdom has become kingless; no happiness can be derived in this state.”
Ch 20 Verse 59 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गाङ्गेयस्तामुवाचेदं मा चिन्तां कुरु भामिनि । पुत्रं विचित्रवीर्यस्य क्षेत्रजं चोपपादय ॥ ५९ ॥
English Translation
Thus thinking, she became exceedingly distressed; then the Gaṅgā’s son, Bhīśma spoke to her :-- “O respected one! Do not worry your mind with cares; now take steps so as to secure a son from Vicītravīrya’s wife.
Ch 20 Verse 60 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कुलीनं द्विजमाहूय वध्वा सह नियोजय । नात्र दोषोऽस्ति वेदेऽपि कुलरक्षाविधौ किल ॥ ६० ॥
English Translation
Call some best Brāhmin, born of a good family and unite him with Vicītravīrya’s wife.
Ch 20 Verse 61-62 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पौत्रं चैवं समुत्पाद्य राज्यं देहि शुचिस्मिते । अहं च पालयिष्यामि तस्य शासनमेव हि ॥ ६१ ॥ तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्य कानीनं स्वसुतं मुनिम् । जगाम मनसा व्यासं द्वैपायनमकल्मषम् ॥ ६२ ॥
English Translation
There is no fault, as far as I know, in doing thus to keep up the family line. O sweet smiling one! Thus having begotten the grandson, give him this kingdom; I will also obey his commands.”
Ch 20 Verse 63 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्मृतमात्रस्ततो व्यास आजगाम स तापसः । कृत्वा प्रणामं मात्रेऽथ संस्थितो दीप्तिमान्मुनिः ॥ ६३ ॥
English Translation
Hearing these reasonable words of Bhīśma, Satyavatī remem bered her own son, the sinless Vyāsa Deva, who was born to her during her virginity. As soon as Vyāsa was remembered, he, the great ascetic and effulgent like the sun, came there and bowed down to his mother.
Ch 20 Verse 64 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भीष्मेण पूजितः कामं सत्यवत्या च मानितः । तस्थौ तत्र महातेजा विधूमोऽग्निरिवापरः ॥ ६४ ॥
English Translation
The highly energetic Vyāsa was then worshipped duly by Bhīśma and welcome by Satyavatī and began to rest there like a smokeless fire.
Ch 20 Verse 65 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तमुवाच मुनिं माता पुत्रमुत्पादयाधुना । क्षेत्रे विचित्रवीर्यस्य सुन्दरं तव वीर्यजम् ॥ ६५ ॥
English Translation
The mother Satyavatī then spoke to the chief Muni :-- “O son! Now procreate a beautiful son from your sperm and the ovum of Vicītravīrya’s wife.”
Ch 20 Verse 66 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यासः श्रुत्वा वचो मातुराप्तवाक्यममन्यत । ओमित्युक्त्वा स्थितस्तत्र ऋतुकालमचिन्तयत् ॥ ६६ ॥
English Translation
Hearing the mother’s words, Vyāsa considered them as Veda’s injunction and thought they must be obeyed and promised before her that he must obey and and fulfil her orders. He remained there, waiting for the menstruation period.
Ch 20 Verse 67-68 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अम्बिका च यदा स्नाता नारी ऋतुमती तदा । सङ्गं प्राप्य मुनेः पुत्रमसूतान्धं महाबलम् ॥ ६७ ॥ जन्मान्धं च सुतं वीक्ष्य दुःखिता सत्यवत्यपि । द्वितीयां च वधूमाह पुत्रमुत्पादयाशु वै ॥ ६८ ॥
English Translation
When the due period of menstruation arrived, Ambikā bathed and had a sexual intercourse with Vyāsa and begot a very powerful son, but a blind one (since she closed her eyes at the sight of Vyāsa during her intercourse). Seeing the son born blind Satyavatī became exceedingly sorry; she, then, asked her other son’s wife :-- “Go soon and get a son born of you in the aforesaid manner.”
Ch 20 Verse 69-71 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ऋतुकालेऽथ सम्प्राप्ते व्यासेन सह सङ्गता । तथा चाम्बालिका रात्रौ गर्भं नारी दधार सा ॥ ६९ ॥ सोऽपि पाण्डुः सुतो जातो राज्ययोग्यो न सम्मतः । पुत्रार्थे प्रेरयामास वर्षान्ते च पुनर्वधूम् ॥ ७० ॥ आहूय च ततो व्यासं सम्प्रार्थ्य मुनिसत्तमम् । प्रेषयामास रात्रौ सा शयनागारमुत्तमम् ॥ ७१ ॥
English Translation
When the menstruation period arrived, Ambālikā during the night time went to Vyāsa and mixed and became pregnant. In due time a son was born; that child became of a very pale colour; so Satyavatī thought the new child, too, unfit for the kingdom; therefore at the end of the year again asked her son’s wife Ambālikā to go to Vyāsa . She asked Vyāsa also for the same purpose and sent Ambālikā to his bed room. But Ambālikā became afraid, and could not go herself but sent her maid servant for the purpose.
Ch 20 Verse 72 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न गता च वधूस्तत्र प्रेष्या सम्प्रेषिता तया । तस्यां च विदुरो जातो दास्यां धर्मांशतः शुभः ॥ ७२ ॥
English Translation
Thus from the womb of the maid servant the high souled Vidura was born, having Dharma’s parts and the most auspicious towards all.
Ch 20 Verse 73-77 Verse
Sanskrit Text
एवं व्यासेन ते पुत्रा धृतराष्ट्रादयस्त्रयः । उत्पादिता महावीरा वंशरक्षणहेतवे ॥ ७३ ॥ एतद्वः सर्वमाख्यातं तस्य वंशसमुद्भवम् । व्यासेन रक्षितो वंशो भ्रातृधर्मविदानघाः ॥ ७४ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां प्रथमस्कन्धे धृतराष्ट्रादीनामुत्पत्तिवर्णनं नाम विंशोऽध्यायः ॥ १.२०॥
English Translation
Thus Vyāsa begot three very powerful sons Dhritarāṣṭra, Panḍu and Vidura for the continuity of the family line. O sinless Maharṣis! Thus I have described to you how my Guru Vyāsa Deva, who knows well all the Dharmas, kept up the continuity of his family and how he begot sons in the womb of his brother Vicītravīrya’s wives, according to the laws of Dharma, to keep up a family. Thus ends the twentieth chapter of the 1st Skandha as well as the first Skandha on Vyāsa doing his duties in the Mahāpurāṇam Śrī Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa .

# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 1, CHAPTER 5

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> १.५ पञ्चमोऽध्यायः । हयग्रीवावतारकथनम् ।
> 
> 
> ऋषय ऊचुः ।
> सूतास्माकं मनः कामं मग्नं संशयसागरे ।
> यथोक्तं महदाश्चर्यं जगद्विस्मयकारकम् ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Ṛṣis said :-- “O Sūta! Our minds are merged in the sea of doubt, hearing your this most wonderful saying, surprising to the whole world.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यन्मूर्धा माधवस्यापि गतो देहात्पुनः परम् ।
> हयग्रीवस्ततो जातः सर्वकर्ता जनार्दनः ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The head of Janārdan Mādhava, the Lord of all, was severed out of His body! And He was afterwards known as Hayagrīva, the horse-faced! Oh! what more wonder can there be than this?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 3-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वेदोऽपि स्तौति यं देवं देवाः सर्वे यदाश्रयाः ।
> आदिदेवो जगन्नाथः सर्वकारणकारणः ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> तस्यापि वदनं छिन्नं दैवयोगात्कथं तदा ।
> तत्सर्वं कथयाशु त्वं विस्तरेण महामते ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Whom the Vedas even praise, all the Devas rest on Whom, Who is the Cause of all causes, the Ādi Deva Jagannath (the Lord of the universe), Oh! how is it that His head came to be severed! O highly intelligent one! Describe all this to me in detail”.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 5-6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सूत उवाच ।
> श‍ृण्वन्तु मुनयः सर्वे सावधानाः समन्ततः ।
> चरितं देवदेवस्य विष्णोः परमतेजसः ॥ ५ ॥
> 
> कदाचिद्दारुणं युद्धं कृत्वा देवः सनातनः ।
> दशवर्षसहस्राणि परिश्रान्तो जनार्दनः ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Sūta said :-- O Munis! Hear all attentively the glorious deeds of the supremely energetic Viṣṇu, the Deva of the Devas. Once on a time the eternal Deva Janārdana became tired after the terrible continuous battle for ten thousand years.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 7-9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> समे देशे शुभे स्थाने कृत्वा पद्मासनं विभुः ।
> अवलम्ब्य धनुः सज्यं कण्ठदेशे धरास्थितम् ॥ ७ ॥
> 
> दत्त्वा भारं धनुष्कोट्यां निद्रामाप रमापतिः ।
> श्रान्तत्वाद्दैवयोगाच्च जातस्तत्रातिनिद्रितः ॥ ८ ॥
> 
> तदा कालेन कियता देवाः सर्वे सवासवाः ।
> ब्रह्मेशसहिताः सर्वे यज्ञं कर्तुं समुद्यताः ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

After this the Lord Nārāyana seated Himself on Padmāsan (a kind of posture) in some lovely place on a level plot of ground and placing his head on the front of his bow with the bow strung and placed erect on the ground fell fast asleep. Viṣṇu, the Lord of Ramā, was exceedingly tired and thus he fell soon into deep sleep. At this time Indra and the other Devas, with Brahmā and Maheṣā began a sacrifice.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 10-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> गताः सर्वेऽथ वैकुण्ठं द्रष्टुं देवं जनार्दनम् ।
> देवकार्यार्थसिद्ध्यर्थं मखानामधिपं प्रभुम् ॥ १० ॥
> 
> अदृष्ट्वा तं तदा तत्र ज्ञानदृष्ट्या विलोक्य ते ।
> यत्रास्ते भगवान् विष्णुर्जग्मुस्तत्र तदा सुराः ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then they, for the sake of success in Deva’s well, went to the region of Vaikuṇṭha to meet with the Deva Janārdana, the Lord of sacrifices. There the Devas, not finding Viṣṇu, came to know by their Dhyān (meditation) where Bhagavān Viṣṇu was staying and thither they went.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ददृशुस्ते तदेशानं योगनिद्रावशं गतम् ।
> विचेतनं विभुं विष्णुं तत्रासाञ्चक्रिरे सुराः ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

They saw that the Lord Viṣṇu, the Deva of the Devas was lying unconscious, being under the arms of Yoganidrā (the yogic sleep).

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्थितेषु सर्वदेवेषु निद्रासुप्ते जगत्पतौ ।
> चिन्तामापुः सुराः सर्वे ब्रह्मरुद्रपुरोगमाः ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Therefore they took their seats there. Seeing the Lord of the universe asleep, Brahmā, Rudra and the other Devas became anxious.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तानुवाच ततः शक्रः किं कर्तव्यं सुरोत्तमाः ।
> निद्राभङ्गः कथं कार्यश्चिन्तयन्तु सुरोत्तमाः ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Indra then addressed the Devas :--“O best of the Suras! Now what is to be done! How shall we rouse Bhagavān from His sleep? Now think of the means by which this can be effected”.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तमुवाच तदा शम्भुर्निद्राभङ्गेऽस्ति दूषणम् ।
> कार्यं चैव प्रकर्तव्यं यज्ञस्य सुरसत्तमाः ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing Indra’s words Śambhu said :-- “O good Devas! Now we must finish our sacrificial work. But if the sleep of Bhagavān be disturbed, He would get angry.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 16-17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उत्पादिता तदा वम्री ब्रह्मणा परमेष्ठिना ।
> तया भक्षयितुं तत्र धनुषोऽग्रं धरास्थितम् ॥ १६ ॥
> 
> भक्षितेऽग्रे तदा निम्नं गमिष्यति शरासनम् ।
> तदा निद्राविमुक्तोऽसौ देवदेवो भविष्यति ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing Śaṅkara’s words, Parameṣṭhī Brahmā created Vamrī insects (a sort of white ants) so that they might eat up the forepart of the bow that was lying on the ground causing the other end rise up and thus break His sleep.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देवकार्यं तदा सर्वं भविष्यति न संशयः ।
> स वम्रीं सन्दिदेशाथ देवदेवः सनातनः ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus the Deva’s purpose will, no doubt, be fulfilled. Thus settling his mind, the eternal Deva Brahmā ordered the white ants Vamrīs to cut the bow string.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तमुवाच तदा वम्री देवदेवस्य मापतेः ।
> निद्राभङ्गः कथं कार्यो देवस्य जगतां गुरोः ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing this order of Brahmā, Vamrī spoke to Brahmā, thus :-- “O Brahmān! How can I disturb the sleep of the Devadeva, Lord of Lakṣmī, the World Guru?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> निद्राभङ्गः कथाच्छेदो दम्पत्योः प्रीतिभेदनम् ।
> शिशुमातृविभेदश्च ब्रह्महत्यासमं स्मृतम् ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

To rouse one from one’s deep sleep, to interrupt one in one’s speech, to sever the love between a couple husband and wife, to separate a child from one’s mother, all these are equivalent to Brahmāhatyā (murdering a Brahmān).

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 21-22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तत्कथं देवदेवस्य करोमि सुखनाशनम् ।
> किं फलं भक्षणाद्देव येन पापं करोम्यहम् ॥ २१ ॥
> 
> सर्वः स्वार्थवशो लोकः कुरुते पातकं किल ।
> तस्मादहं करिष्यामि स्वार्थमेव प्रभक्षणम् ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Therefore, O Deva! how can I interrupt the happiness of sleep of the Devadeva? And what benefit shall I derive by eating the bowstring, so that I may iñcur this vicious act? But a man can commit a sin if there be any interest of his; I am ready to eat this, if I get a personal interest”.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रह्मोवाच ।
> तव भागं करिष्यामो मखमध्ये यथा श‍ृणु ।
> तेन त्वं कुरु कार्यं नो विष्णुं बोधय माचिरम् ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Brahmā said :-- We will give you, too, share in this our Yajña (sacrifice); so hear me; do our work and rouse Viṣṇu from His sleep.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

> तत्ते भागं विजानीहि कुरु कार्यं त्वरान्विता ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

During the time of performing Homa whatever ghee will fall outside the Homa-Kund (the sacrificial pit) will fall to your share; so be quick and do this.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सूत उवाच ।
> इत्युक्ता ब्रह्मणा वम्री धनुषोऽग्रं त्वरान्विता ।

>
**English Translation:**

Sūta said :-- Thus ordered by Brahmā, the Vamrī insect soon ate away the fore end of the bow that rested on the ground.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 26-28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> प्रत्यञ्जायां विमुक्तायां मुक्ता कोटिस्तथोत्तरा ।
> शब्दः समभवद्घोरस्तेन त्रस्ताः सुरास्तदा ॥ २६ ॥
> 
> ब्रह्माण्डं क्षुभितं सर्वं वसुधा कम्पिता तदा ।
> समुद्राश्च समुद्विग्नास्त्रेसुश्च जलजन्तवः ॥ २७ ॥
> 
> ववुर्वातास्तथा चोग्राः पर्वताश्च चकम्पिरे ।
> उल्कापाता महोत्पाता बभूवुर्दुःखशंसिनः ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Immediately the string gave way and the bow went up; the other end became free and a terrible sound took place. The Devas bcame afraid; the whole universe got agitated; the earth trembled. The sea became swollen; the aquatic animals became startled; violent wind blew; the mountains shook; ominous meteors fell.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 29-30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दिशो घोरतराश्चासन्सूर्योऽप्यस्तङ्गतोऽभवत् ।
> चिन्तामापुः सुराः सर्वे किं भविष्यति दुर्दिने ॥ २९ ॥
> 
> एवं चिन्तयतां तेषां मूर्धा विष्णोः सकुण्डलः ।
> गतः समुकुटः क्वापि देवदेवस्य तापसाः ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O ascetics! while the Devas were thus cogitating, the head with crown on it of the Devadeva Viṣṇu vanished away ; no body knew where it fell.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अन्धकारे तदा घोरे शान्ते ब्रह्महरौ तदा ।
> शिरोहीनं शरीरं तु ददृशाते विलक्षणम् ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When the awful darkness disappeared, Brahmā and Mahādeva saw the disfigured body of Viṣṇu with its head off.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 32-34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दृष्ट्वा कबन्धं विष्णोस्ते विस्मिताः सुरसत्तमाः ।
> चिन्तासागरमग्नाश्च रुरुदुः शोककर्शिताः ॥ ३२ ॥
> 
> हा नाथ किं प्रभो जातमत्यद्भुतममानुषम् ।
> वैशसं सर्वदेवानां देवदेव सनातन ॥ ३३ ॥
> 
> मायेयं कस्य देवस्य यया तेऽद्य शिरो हृतम् ।
> अच्छेद्यस्त्वमभेद्योऽसि अप्रदाह्योऽसि सर्वदा ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Seeing that headless figure of Viṣṇu they were greatly surprised; they were drowned in the ocean of cares and, overwhelmed with grief, began to weep aloud. O Lord! O Master! O Devadeva! O Eternal one! what unforeseen extraordinary mishap occurred to us to-day! O Deva! Thou canst not be pierced nor cut asunder, nor capable of being burnt; how is it then that Thy head has been taken away! Is this the Māyā (majic) of some. Deva? O all pervading one!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 35-36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एवं गते त्वयि विभो मरिष्यन्ति च देवताः ।
> कीदृशस्त्वयि नः स्नेहः स्वार्थेनैव रुदामहे ॥ ३५ ॥
> 
> नायं विघ्नः कृतो दैत्यैर्न यक्षैर्न च राक्षसैः ।

>
**English Translation:**

The Devas cannot live when Thy condition is thus; we do not know what affection dost Thou have towards us. We are crying because of our selfish ends; perhaps this therefore has occurred. The Daityas, Yakṣas, or Rākhṣasas have not done this; O Lord of Laksmī! Whose fault will we ascribe this to? The Devas themselves have committed this loss to themselves?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 37-41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

> शरणं नैव देवेश सुराणां मूढचेतसाम् ॥ ३७ ॥
> 
> न चैषा सात्त्विकी माया राजसी न च तामसी ।
> यया छिन्नं शिरस्तेऽद्य मायेशस्य जगद्गुरोः ॥ ३८ ॥
> 
> क्रन्दमानांस्तदा दृष्ट्वा देवाञ्छिवपुरोगमान् ।
> बृहस्पतिस्तदोवाच शमयन्वेदवित्तमः ॥ ३९ ॥
> 
> रुदितेन महाभागाः क्रन्दितेन तथापि किम् ।

> 
> दैवं पुरुषकारश्च देवेश सदृशावुभौ ।
> उपायश्च विधातव्यो दैवात्फलति सर्वथा ॥ ४१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Lord of the Devas! The Devas are. now dependent! They are under Thee. Now where are we to go? What are we to do? There is none to save the dull stupid Devas! At this juncture, seeing Śiva and the other Devas crying, Brihaspati, supremely versed in the Vedas, consoled them thus :-- “O highly fortunate one! what use there will be in thus crying and repenting? it ought you now to consider the means that you should adopt to redress your calamities. O Lord of the Devas! Fate and one’s own exertion and intelligence are equal; if the success comes not through Fate (Luck or chance) one is certainly to show one’s prowess and merit”.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 42</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

> दैवमेव परं मन्ये धिक्पौरुषमनर्थकम् ।

**English Translation:**

Indra said :-- Fie to your exertion when, before our eyes, the head of Bhagavān Viṣṇu Himself has been carried off! Fie, Fie to your prowess and intelligence! Fate is in my opinion, the supreme.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 43</b></summary>

Brahmā said :-- Whatever, auspicious or inauspicious, is ordained Daiva (Fate), every one must bear that; no one can go beyond the Daiva.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 44</b></summary>

When one has taken up a body, one must experience pleasure and pain; there is no manner of doubt in this. See, in long-past days, by the irony of Fate, Śambhu severed my head;

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 5 Verse 45</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

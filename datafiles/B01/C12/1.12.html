BOOK 1 • CHAPTER 12
On the birth of Pururavā

Ch 12 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
१.१२ द्वादशोऽध्यायः । सुद्युम्नस्तुतिः । सूत उवाच । ततः पुरूरवा जज्ञे इलायां कथयामि वः । बुधपुत्रोऽतिधर्मात्मा यज्ञकृद्दानतत्परः ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Sūta said :-- O Mahārṣis! The son of the above mentioned Budha is the greatly religious Pururavā, of a very charitable disposition, and always ready to perform sacrificial acts; he was born of a kshattriya woman named Ilā; and I will now describe how this Pururavā was born of Ilā and Budha, kindly listen.
Ch 12 Verse 2 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सुद्युम्नो नाम भूपालः सत्यवादी जितेन्द्रियः । सैन्धवं हयमारुह्य चचार मृगयां वने ॥ २ ॥
English Translation
In days of yore there was a king named Sudyumna, very truthful and highly capable in keeping his senses under control.
Ch 12 Verse 3-5 Verse
Sanskrit Text
युतः कतिपयामात्यैर्दर्शितश्चारुकुण्डलः । धनुराजगवं बद्ध्वा बाणसङ्घं तथाद्भुतम् ॥ ३ ॥ स भ्रमंस्तद्वनोद्देशे हन्यमानो रुरून्मृगान् । शशांश्च सूकरांश्चैव खड्गांश्च गवयांस्तथा ॥ ४ ॥ शरभान्महिषांश्चैव साम्भरान्वनकुक्कुटान् । निघ्नन्मेध्यान् पशून् राजा कुमारवनमाविशत् ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation
Once on a time, wearing beautiful ear-rings, with extraordinary bow named Ājagab and holding the arrow-case full of arrows on his back, he went out on hunt to a forest, riding on a horse, born of the country Sindhu, surrounded by a few of his ministers. Going about in the regions of forest, the king got for his shikār, buck, hare, boar, rhinoceros, bison, buffalo, young elephant, Srimar deer, wild fowl and various other animals fit for sacrificial purposes; thus he went on deep into the interior of the forest.
Ch 12 Verse 6-8 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मेरोरधस्तले दिव्यं मन्दारद्रुमराजितम् । अशोकलतिकाकीर्णं बकुलैरधिवासितम् ॥ ६ ॥ सालैस्तालैस्तमालैश्च चम्पकैः पनसैस्तथा । आम्रैर्नीपैर्मधूकैश्च माधवीमण्डपावृतम् ॥ ७ ॥ दाडिमैर्नारिकेलैश्च कदलीखण्डमण्डितम् । यूथिकामालतीकुन्दपुष्पवल्लीसमावृतम् ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
This divine forest was adorned with rows of Mandāra trees and situated at the bottom of the mount Sumeru. Various trees and flowers were spreading the beauty of the forest all around; at places were Asoka creepers, Vakula, Sāl, Tāl, Tamāl, Champak, Panasa, mangoe, Nīp, Madhūka, pomegranate, cocoanut, Yūthikā, plantain, kunda creeper, and various other trees and flowers; at some other places the bowers formed of Mādhavi creepers enhanced the beauty and shed the lustre all around.
Ch 12 Verse 9 Verse
Sanskrit Text
हंसकारण्डवाकीर्णं कीचकध्वनिनादितम् । भ्रमरालिरुतारामं वनं सर्वसुखावहम् ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation
There were tanks and reservoirs of water in the forest filled with swans, kāraṇḍavas, and other aquatic birds. The bamboo trees on their banks becoming filled with air were emitting sweet musical sounds; and at many places of that all blissgiving forest, there were bees humming sweetly and delighting the minds of people there.
Ch 12 Verse 10-11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दृष्ट्वा प्रमुदितो राजा सुद्युम्नः सेवकैर्वृतः । वृक्षान्सुपुष्पितान्वीक्ष्य कोकिलारावमण्डितान् ॥ १० ॥ प्रविष्टस्तत्र राजर्षिः स्त्रीत्वमाप क्षणात्ततः । अश्वोऽपि वडवा जातश्चिन्ताविष्टः स भूपतिः ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
Now the Rājarṣī Pradyumna became highly gladdened in his heart to see this interior of the forest, resonated with the cooing of the cuckoos and beautified by various lovely flowers; and no sooner he entered there than he was turned into a female and his horse, also, turned into a mare; the king, then, became seriously anxious.

Sanskrit Text
किमेतदिति चिन्तार्तश्चिन्त्यमानः पुनः पुनः । दुःखं बहुतरं प्राप्तः सुद्युम्नो लज्जयान्वितः ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
He began to think over and over again “What is this? How has this come to pass?” and became very ashamed and sorry and pondered over thus :--“What am I to do now?
Ch 12 Verse 13 Verse

किं करोमि कथं यामि गृहं स्त्रीभावसंयुतः । कथं राज्यं करिष्यामि केन वा वञ्चितो ह्यहम् ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation
How can I in this woman condition go back to my house and how shall I govern my kingdom? Alas! Who has deceived me thus!”
Ch 12 Verse 14 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Hearing these astounding words of Sūta, the Ṛṣis said :-- “O Sūta! You have mentioned that the king Sudyumna equal to god has been turned into a woman; this is very strange indeed!
Ch 12 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
किं तत्कारणमाचक्ष्व वने तत्र मनोहरे । किं कृतं तेन राज्ञा च विस्तरं वद सुव्रत ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
Therefore, O Suvrata! What is the reason of his being turned into a mare? Kindly describe fully what that beautiful king did in the forest?”
Ch 12 Verse 16-20 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सूत उवाच । एकदा गिरिशं द्रष्टुमृषयः सनकादयः । दिशो वितिमिराभासाः कुर्वन्तः समुपागमन् ॥ १६ ॥ तस्मिंश्च समये तत्र शङ्करः प्रमदायुतः । क्रीडासक्तो महादेवो विवस्त्रा कामिनी शिवा ॥ १७ ॥ उत्सङ्गे संस्थिता भर्तू रममाणा मनोरमा । तान्विलोक्याम्बिका देवी विवस्त्रा व्रीडिता भृशम् ॥ १८ ॥ भर्तुरङ्कात्समुत्थाय वस्त्रमादाय पर्यधात् । लज्जाविष्टा स्थिता तत्र वेपमानातिमानिनी ॥ १९ ॥ ऋषयोऽपि तयोर्वीक्ष्य प्रसङ्गं रममाणयोः । परिवृत्य ययुस्तूर्णं नरनारायणाश्रमम् ॥ २० ॥
English Translation
Sūta, said :-- Once on a time, Sanaka and other Ṛṣis went to this forest to pay a visit to Śaṅkara, illuminating the ten quarters by their holy aura. But then Bhagavān Śaṅkara was in amorous dealings with Śaṅkarī. The beautiful Devī Ambikā was then naked and sitting on the lap of Śaṅkara and therefore became very much ashamed at their sight; She got up hurriedly, and putting on her cloth, remained there shuddering, with great shame and sensitiveness. The Ṛṣis, also, seeing them engaged in amorous dealings, went away quickly to the hermitage of Nara Nārāyaṇa.
Ch 12 Verse 21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ह्रीयुतां कामिनीं वीक्ष्य प्रोवाच भगवान्हरः । कथं लज्जातुरासि त्वं सुखं ते प्रकरोम्यहम् ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
Then Bhagavān Śaṅkara, seeing Śaṅkari too much sensitive, said :-- “Why are you so much bashful and shy?
Ch 12 Verse 22 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अद्यप्रभृति यो मोहात्पुमान्कोऽपि वरानने । वनं च प्रविशेदेतत्स वै योषिद्भविष्यति ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation
I am doing just now what will give you pleasure. O Beautiful one! Whoever male will enter from to-day and hereafter, within the precincts of this forest, will be instantly converted into a woman.”
Ch 12 Verse 23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इति शप्तं वनं तेन ये जानन्ति जनाः क्वचित् । वर्जयन्तीह ते कामं वनं दोषसमृद्धिमत् ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
O Munis! Though the forest gave all delights to all, yet, having this defect, all the persons that know of this curse, carefully avoid the forest.
Ch 12 Verse 24 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सुद्युम्नस्तु तदज्ञानात्प्रविष्टः सचिवैः सह । तथैव स्त्रीत्वमापन्नस्तैः सहेति न संशयः ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation
No sooner did the King Sudyumna enter into the forest, out of ignorance, than he, as well as his attendants, were instantly turned into women; there could be no doubt in this.
Ch 12 Verse 25 Verse
Sanskrit Text
चिन्ताविष्टः स राजर्षिर्न जगाम गृहं ह्रिया । विचचार बहिस्तस्माद्वनदेशादितस्ततः ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation
The king became overpowered with great care and anxiety and did not go back, out of shame, to his palace; but he began to wander to and fro on the outer skirts of that forest.
Ch 12 Verse 26-28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इलेति नाम सम्प्राप्तं स्त्रीत्वे तेन महात्मना । विचरंस्तत्र सम्प्राप्तो बुधः सोमसुतो युवा ॥ २६ ॥ स्त्रीभिः परिवृतां तां तु दृष्ट्वा कान्तां मनोरमाम् । हावभावकलायुक्तां चकमे भगवाम्बुधः ॥ २७ ॥ सापि तं चकमे कान्तं बुधं सोमसुतं पतिम् । संयोगस्तत्र सञ्जातस्तयोः प्रेम्णा परस्परम् ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
He became known afterwards as the woman Ilā. Now, once on an occasion, Budh, while he was wandering at his will, came up there and seeing the beautiful Ilā with gestures and pastures and surrounded by women, became passionately attached towards her; and Ilā, too, seeing the beautiful Budh, the son of Candra deva, became desirous to have him as her husband. They became so very much tied in love towards each other, that intercourse took place there.
Ch 12 Verse 29 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स तस्यां जनयामास पुरूरवसमात्मजम् । इलायां सोमपुत्रस्तु चक्रवर्तिनमुत्तमम् ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation
Thus Bhagavān Budh generated, in the womb of Ilā, Pururavā; and Ilā gave birth, in due time, the son Pururavā in that forest.
Ch 12 Verse 30 Verse

English Translation
She then, with an anxious heart, recollected, while in the forest, her (rather his), family priest Vaṣiṣṭha Deva.
Ch 12 Verse 31 Verse

English Translation
Now then Vaṣiṣṭha Deva, seeing the distressed condition of the king Sudyumna became affected with pity and pleased Mahādeva, Śaṅkara, the most auspicious Deva of all, by hymns and praises.
Ch 12 Verse 32 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
When Bhagavān Śaṅkara wanted to grant him the boon that he desired, Vaṣiṣṭha Deva wanted that the king would be turned again into man as before.
Ch 12 Verse 33 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
At this Bhagavān Śaṅkara said, in recognition of His promise, that the king Sudyumna would be alternately one month a man and the second month a woman and so on.
Ch 12 Verse 34-35 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इत्थं प्राप्य वरं राजा जगाम स्वगृहं पुनः । चक्रे राज्यं स धर्मात्मा वसिष्ठस्याप्यनुग्रहात् ॥ ३४ ॥ स्त्रीत्वे तिष्ठति हर्म्येषु पुंस्त्वे राज्यं प्रशास्ति च । प्रजास्तस्मिन्समुद्विग्ना नाभ्यनन्दन्महीपतिम् ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation
Thus, by the favour of Vaṣiṣṭha Deva, the king Sudyumna got this boon and returned to his kingdom and began to govern it. When he used to be turned into a woman, he used to remain in the interior, and when he used to become a man, he governed his kingdom. At this the subjects became very anxious and did not welcome the king as they used to do before. Some days passed away in this way when the prince Pururavā grew up into manhood.

He went to a beautiful forest, variegated with all sorts of trees, and got from the Devarṣi Nārada the excellent mantra of the Bhagavatī Devī, consisting of nine letters.

Ch 12 Verse 41-42 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इलोवाच । दिव्यं च ते भगवति प्रथितं स्वरूपं दृष्टं मया सकललोकहितानुरूपम् । वन्दे त्वदङ्घ्रिकमलं सुरसङ्घसेव्यं कामप्रदं जननि चापि विमुक्तिदं च ॥ ४१ ॥ को वेत्ति तेऽम्ब भुवि मर्त्यतनुर्निकामं मुह्यन्ति यत्र मुनयश्च सुराश्च सर्वे । ऐश्वर्यमेतदखिलं कृपणे दयां च दृष्ट्वैव देवि सकलं किल विस्मयो मे ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation
“O Bhagavatī! What a fortunate being I am! That I have seen today the extraordinary world renowned benignant form of Thine granting grace and benefit to all the Lokas, I, therefore, bow down to Thy lotus-feet, granting desires and liberation, and served by the whole host of the Devas. O Mother! What mortal is there on this earth, who can fully comprehend Thy glories when all the Devas and Munis get bewildered in trying to know of them.
Ch 12 Verse 43 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शम्भुर्हरिः कमलजो मघवा रविश्च वित्तेशवह्निवरुणाः पवनश्च सोमः । जानन्ति नैव वसवोऽपि हि ते प्रभावं बुध्येत्कथं तव गुणानगुणो मनुष्यः ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation
O Devī! I am thoroughly astonished to see Thy glories and Thy compassion towards the distressed and poor and helpless people. How can a human being, who is devoid of attributes comprehend Thy attributes when Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśvara, Indra, Candra (moon), Pavana (wind), Sūrya, Kuvera, and the eight Vasus know not Thy powers.
Ch 12 Verse 44 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जानाति विष्णुरमितद्युतिरम्ब साक्षा- त्त्वां सात्त्विकीमुदधिजां सकलार्थदां च । को राजसीं हर उमां किल तामसीं त्वां वेदाम्बिके न तु पुनः खलु निर्गुणां त्वाम् ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! Bhagavān Viṣṇu, of unrivalled brilliancy, knows Thee as a part of Thine only, as Kamalā of Sattva Guṇas and giving one all one’s desires; Bhagavān Brahmā knows Thy part only as the form made of Rajo guṇa and Bhagavān Śaṅkara knows Thee as Umā only made of Tamo Guṇa; but, O Mother! none of them knows Thy turīya form, transcending all the Guṇas.
Ch 12 Verse 45 Verse
Sanskrit Text
क्वाहं सुमन्दमतिरप्रतिमप्रभावः क्वायं तवातिनिपुणो मयि सुप्रसादः । जाने भवानि चरितं करुणासमेतं यत्सेवकांश्च दयसे त्वयि भावयुक्तान् ॥ ४५ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! where is my humble self, that is of very dull intellect and powerless, and where is Thy extremely propitious serenity and graciousness! Indeed such a gracious favour on me is certainly beyond expectation. Therefore, O Bhavāni! I have come to realise, in particular, that Thy heart is full of unbounded mercy; for Thou dost certainly feel compassion for these Bhaktas that are full of Bhakti towards Thee.
Ch 12 Verse 46 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
O Mother! what more shall I say than this, that Bhagavān Madhusūdan Viṣṇu, though married to Kamalā, born from only a part of Thine, considers Himself unfit of Her and is therefore not happy; then the fact that He, the Ādi Puruṣa gets his feet shampooed by Kamalā merely corroborates the fact that He wants His feet to become pure and all auspicious to the world by the holy touch of Kamalā’s hands.
Ch 12 Verse 47 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वाञ्छत्यहो हरिरशोक इवातिकामं पादाहतिं प्रमुदितः पुरुषः पुराणः । तां त्वं करोषि रुषिता प्रणतं च पादे दृष्ट्वा पतिं सकलदेवनुतं स्मरार्तम् ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! It seems to me that the ancient Puruṣa Bhagavān Viṣṇu wants gladly to be kicked by Thee like Aśoka tree, for his own improvement and pleasure; and therefore it is that Thou dost want, as if Thou hast become angry to kick (beat with one’s legs) Thy husband, stricken by Smara (cupid, love) and worshipped by all the Devas, who lies prostrate below Thy feet.
Ch 12 Verse 48 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वक्षःस्थले वससि देवि सदैव तस्य पर्यङ्कवत्सुचरिते विपुलेऽतिशान्ते । सौदामिनीव सुघने सुविभूषिते च किं ते न वाहनमसौ जगदीश्वरोऽपि ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation
O Devī; when Thou always residest on the calm broad chest, as if on a great cot, adorned beautifully of Bhagavān Viṣṇu, as lightning in deep dense blue clouds, then it is without doubt that He, becoming the Lord of the Universe, has surely become Thy vāhan (vehicle) (on account of carrying Thee on His breast),
Ch 12 Verse 49 Verse
Sanskrit Text
त्वं चेज्जहासि मधुसूदनमम्ब कोपा- न्नैवार्चितोऽपि स भवेत्किल शक्तिहीनः । प्रत्यक्षमेव पुरुषं स्वजनास्त्वजन्ति शान्तं श्रियोज्झितमतीव गुणैर्वियुक्तम् ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! If Thou forsakest Madhusūdana, out of wrath, He becomes at once powerless and is not worshipped by any body; for it is seen everywhere that persons, though calm and serene, if devoid of Śrī (wealth and power) are forsaken by their relatives as reduced to a state having no qualities.
Ch 12 Verse 50 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ब्रह्मादयः सुरगणा न तु किं युवत्यो ये त्वत्पदाम्बुजमहर्निशमाश्रयन्ति । मन्ये त्वयैव विहिताः खलु ते पुमांसः किं वर्णयामि तव शक्तिमनन्तवीर्ये ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation
O Mother! I am not to be ignored by Thee, on account of my being a woman, for was it not the fact, that Brahmā and the other Devas who always take shelter of Thy lotus feet, had not all to assume once youthful feminine forms, while in Manidvīpa, and I know this surely that Thou again didst make them of male forms. Therefore, O Thou of unbounded power! What shall I describe about Thy power? Indeed, there is great doubt in my mind whether Thou art masculine or feminine?
Ch 12 Verse 51 Verse
Sanskrit Text
त्वं नापुमान्न च पुमानिति मे विकल्पो याकासि देवि सगुणा ननु निर्गुणा वा । तां त्वां नमामि सततं किल भावयुक्तो वाञ्छामि भक्तिमचलां त्वयि मातरं तु ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation
O Devī! Whoever Thou mayst be, whether with attributes on transcending the attributes, whether male or female, I always bow down to Thee, with heart full of devotion towards Thee. O Mother! I want that I may have one unflinching devotion, towards Thee in my final state.
Ch 12 Verse 52-54 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सूत उवाच । इति स्तुत्वा महीपालो जगाम शरणं तदा । परितुष्टा ददौ देवी तत्र सायुज्यमात्मनि ॥ ५२ ॥ सुद्युम्नस्तु ततः प्राप पदं परमकं स्थिरम् । तस्या देव्याः प्रसादेन मुनीनामपि दुर्लभम् ॥ ५३ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां प्रथमस्कन्धे सुद्युम्नस्तुतिर्नाम द्वादशोऽध्यायः ॥ १.१२॥
English Translation
Sūta said :-- Thus praising the Devī, the king Sudyumna, in the form of the feminine Ilā, took refuge of the World Mother; and the Devī, becoming greatly pleased, gave to the king, then and there, union with Her own Self. Thus the king got the highest steady place, so very rare even to the Munis, by the grace of the Prime Force, the Devī Brahmāmayī. Thus ends the Twelfth Chapter of the first Skandha on the birth of Pururavā, in the Mahāpurāṇam Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyās.

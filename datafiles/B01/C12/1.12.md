# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 1, CHAPTER 12

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 12 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> १.१२ द्वादशोऽध्यायः । सुद्युम्नस्तुतिः ।
> 
> 
> सूत उवाच ।
> ततः पुरूरवा जज्ञे इलायां कथयामि वः ।
> बुधपुत्रोऽतिधर्मात्मा यज्ञकृद्दानतत्परः ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Sūta said :-- O Mahārṣis! The son of the above mentioned Budha is the greatly religious Pururavā, of a very charitable disposition, and always ready to perform sacrificial acts; he was born of a kshattriya woman named Ilā; and I will now describe how this Pururavā was born of Ilā and Budha, kindly listen.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 12 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सुद्युम्नो नाम भूपालः सत्यवादी जितेन्द्रियः ।
> सैन्धवं हयमारुह्य चचार मृगयां वने ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

In days of yore there was a king named Sudyumna, very truthful and highly capable in keeping his senses under control.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 12 Verse 3-5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> युतः कतिपयामात्यैर्दर्शितश्चारुकुण्डलः ।
> धनुराजगवं बद्ध्वा बाणसङ्घं तथाद्भुतम् ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> स भ्रमंस्तद्वनोद्देशे हन्यमानो रुरून्मृगान् ।
> शशांश्च सूकरांश्चैव खड्गांश्च गवयांस्तथा ॥ ४ ॥
> 
> शरभान्महिषांश्चैव साम्भरान्वनकुक्कुटान् ।
> निघ्नन्मेध्यान् पशून् राजा कुमारवनमाविशत् ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Once on a time, wearing beautiful ear-rings, with extraordinary bow named Ājagab and holding the arrow-case full of arrows on his back, he went out on hunt to a forest, riding on a horse, born of the country Sindhu, surrounded by a few of his ministers. Going about in the regions of forest, the king got for his shikār, buck, hare, boar, rhinoceros, bison, buffalo, young elephant, Srimar deer, wild fowl and various other animals fit for sacrificial purposes; thus he went on deep into the interior of the forest.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 12 Verse 6-8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मेरोरधस्तले दिव्यं मन्दारद्रुमराजितम् ।
> अशोकलतिकाकीर्णं बकुलैरधिवासितम् ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> सालैस्तालैस्तमालैश्च चम्पकैः पनसैस्तथा ।
> आम्रैर्नीपैर्मधूकैश्च माधवीमण्डपावृतम् ॥ ७ ॥
> 
> दाडिमैर्नारिकेलैश्च कदलीखण्डमण्डितम् ।
> यूथिकामालतीकुन्दपुष्पवल्लीसमावृतम् ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

This divine forest was adorned with rows of Mandāra trees and situated at the bottom of the mount Sumeru. Various trees and flowers were spreading the beauty of the forest all around; at places were Asoka creepers, Vakula, Sāl, Tāl, Tamāl, Champak, Panasa, mangoe, Nīp, Madhūka, pomegranate, cocoanut, Yūthikā, plantain, kunda creeper, and various other trees and flowers; at some other places the bowers formed of Mādhavi creepers enhanced the beauty and shed the lustre all around.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 12 Verse 9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> हंसकारण्डवाकीर्णं कीचकध्वनिनादितम् ।
> भ्रमरालिरुतारामं वनं सर्वसुखावहम् ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

There were tanks and reservoirs of water in the forest filled with swans, kāraṇḍavas, and other aquatic birds. The bamboo trees on their banks becoming filled with air were emitting sweet musical sounds; and at many places of that all blissgiving forest, there were bees humming sweetly and delighting the minds of people there.

</details>

<details>

  <br>

> वृक्षान्सुपुष्पितान्वीक्ष्य कोकिलारावमण्डितान् ॥ १० ॥
>

**English Translation:**

Now the Rājarṣī Pradyumna became highly gladdened in his heart to see this interior of the forest, resonated with the cooing of the cuckoos and beautified by various lovely flowers; and no sooner he entered there than he was turned into a female and his horse, also, turned into a mare; the king, then, became seriously anxious.

</details>

**English Translation:**

He began to think over and over again “What is this? How has this come to pass?” and became very ashamed and sorry and pondered over thus :--“What am I to do now?

</details>

**English Translation:**

Hearing these astounding words of Sūta, the Ṛṣis said :-- “O Sūta! You have mentioned that the king Sudyumna equal to god has been turned into a woman; this is very strange indeed!

</details>

<details>

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 12 Verse 16-20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सूत उवाच ।
> एकदा गिरिशं द्रष्टुमृषयः सनकादयः ।
> दिशो वितिमिराभासाः कुर्वन्तः समुपागमन् ॥ १६ ॥
> 
> तस्मिंश्च समये तत्र शङ्करः प्रमदायुतः ।
> क्रीडासक्तो महादेवो विवस्त्रा कामिनी शिवा ॥ १७ ॥
> 
> उत्सङ्गे संस्थिता भर्तू रममाणा मनोरमा ।
> तान्विलोक्याम्बिका देवी विवस्त्रा व्रीडिता भृशम् ॥ १८ ॥
> 
> भर्तुरङ्कात्समुत्थाय वस्त्रमादाय पर्यधात् ।
> लज्जाविष्टा स्थिता तत्र वेपमानातिमानिनी ॥ १९ ॥
> 
> ऋषयोऽपि तयोर्वीक्ष्य प्रसङ्गं रममाणयोः ।
> परिवृत्य ययुस्तूर्णं नरनारायणाश्रमम् ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Sūta, said :-- Once on a time, Sanaka and other Ṛṣis went to this forest to pay a visit to Śaṅkara, illuminating the ten quarters by their holy aura. But then Bhagavān Śaṅkara was in amorous dealings with Śaṅkarī. The beautiful Devī Ambikā was then naked and sitting on the lap of Śaṅkara and therefore became very much ashamed at their sight; She got up hurriedly, and putting on her cloth, remained there shuddering, with great shame and sensitiveness. The Ṛṣis, also, seeing them engaged in amorous dealings, went away quickly to the hermitage of Nara Nārāyaṇa.

</details>

He became known afterwards as the woman Ilā. Now, once on an occasion, Budh, while he was wandering at his will, came up there and seeing the beautiful Ilā with gestures and pastures and surrounded by women, became passionately attached towards her; and Ilā, too, seeing the beautiful Budh, the son of Candra deva, became desirous to have him as her husband. They became so very much tied in love towards each other, that intercourse took place there.

</details>

At this the subjects became very anxious and did not welcome the king as they used to do before. Some days passed away in this way when the prince Pururavā grew up into manhood.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 12 Verse 43</b></summary>

> शम्भुर्हरिः कमलजो मघवा रविश्च
> वित्तेशवह्निवरुणाः पवनश्च सोमः ।
> जानन्ति नैव वसवोऽपि हि ते प्रभावं

**English Translation:**

O Devī! I am thoroughly astonished to see Thy glories and Thy compassion towards the distressed and poor and helpless people. How can a human being, who is devoid of attributes comprehend Thy attributes when Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśvara, Indra, Candra (moon), Pavana (wind), Sūrya, Kuvera, and the eight Vasus know not Thy powers.

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 12 Verse 52-54</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सूत उवाच ।
> इति स्तुत्वा महीपालो जगाम शरणं तदा ।
> परितुष्टा ददौ देवी तत्र सायुज्यमात्मनि ॥ ५२ ॥
> 
> सुद्युम्नस्तु ततः प्राप पदं परमकं स्थिरम् ।
> तस्या देव्याः प्रसादेन मुनीनामपि दुर्लभम् ॥ ५३ ॥
>
> *Colophon:*
> इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां
> प्रथमस्कन्धे सुद्युम्नस्तुतिर्नाम द्वादशोऽध्यायः ॥ १.१२॥
>
**English Translation:**

Sūta said :-- Thus praising the Devī, the king Sudyumna, in the form of the feminine Ilā, took refuge of the World Mother; and the Devī, becoming greatly pleased, gave to the king, then and there, union with Her own Self. Thus the king got the highest steady place, so very rare even to the Munis, by the grace of the Prime Force, the Devī Brahmāmayī. Thus ends the Twelfth Chapter of the first Skandha on the birth of Pururavā, in the Mahāpurāṇam Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyās.

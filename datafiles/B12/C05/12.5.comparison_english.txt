[Ch 5 Verse 2-4]
OLD OCR ENG ──► Nārāyaṇa said: O World-Mother! O Thou, favouring Thy devotees! O Thou, the Prime Force, O Omnipresent! Infinite! Śrī Sandhye! I bow down to Thee. O Bhagavatī! Thou art always meditated by the Munis as young in the morning, full of youth in the mid-day, and aged in the evening. I bow down to Thee.

[Ch 5 Verse 6-7]
OLD OCR ENG ──► Thou art seen within by the ascetics as manifesting the Rgveda (in the form of Śāvitrī) in this world, as manifesting Yayurveda in the middle space (antariksam) and as manifesting Sāmaveda everywhere in the Rudra loka, thus roaming in the three worlds. I bow down to Thee. O Devi! Thou art Rudrānī in the Rudra loka, Vaiṣṇavī in the Viṣṇu loka, and Brahmānī in the Brahmā loka; thus Thou shewest Thy favour to the Immortals.

[Ch 5 Verse 22-23]
OLD OCR ENG ──► Thou art the Kundalini in the Muldādhāra (sacral plexus), the Vyāpinī extending upto the roots of the hairs; Thou art Madhyāsanā on the crown of the head, and Thou art Manonmanī in the Brahmarandhra.

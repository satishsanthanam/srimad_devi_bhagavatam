Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam

BOOK 3 • CHAPTER 20
On the Svayamvara hall and the kings conversation there
← Previous Chapter ☰ Index Next Chapter →
Ch 20 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
३.२० विंशोऽध्यायः । स्वपितरं प्रति शशिकलावाक्यम् । व्यास उवाच । इतिवादिनि भूपाले केरलाधिपतौ तदा । प्रत्युवाच महाभाग युधाजिदपि पार्थिवः ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O Noble minded one! The king Yudhājit replied to the address of the king of Keral, thus :--
Ch 20 Verse 2-3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नीतिरेषा महीपाल यद्ब्रवीति भवानिह । समाजे पार्थिवानां वै सत्यवाग्विजितेन्द्रियः ॥ २ ॥ योग्येषु वर्तमानेषु कन्यारत्नं कुलोद्वह । अयोग्योऽर्हति भूपाल न्यायोऽयं तव रोचते ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
O King! You are truthful and have restrained your passions. What you have told just now in this assembly of kings is all correct and approved by morality. O best of the kings! You are born of a high family; you better say how can this take place that when so many fit persons are present here to become the bridegroom, can an unworthy person take away the offer?
Ch 20 Verse 4 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भागं सिंहस्य गोमायुर्भोक्तुमर्हति वा कथम् । तथा सुदर्शनोऽयं वै कन्यारत्नं किमर्हति ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation
As a jackal never becomes fit to enjoy what are the dues of a lion, so this Sudarśana is also unfit to acquire this bride elect.
Ch 20 Verse 5 Verse
Sanskrit Text
बलं वेदो हि विप्राणां भूभुजां चापजं बलम् । किमन्याय्यं महाराज ब्रवीम्यहमिहाधुना ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation
The Brāhmaṇas have the Vedas as their strength; the Kṣattriya kings take bows and arrows to be their source of strength; this is ordained everywhere. Therefore O King! What wrong have I done in my statement. Kindly explain.
Ch 20 Verse 6-7 Verse
Sanskrit Text
बलं शुल्कं यथा राज्ञां विवाहे परिकीर्तितम् । बलवानेव गृह्णातु नाबलस्तु कदाचन ॥ ६ ॥ तस्मात्कन्यापणं कृत्वा नीतिरत्र विधीयताम् । अन्यथा कलहः कामं भविष्यति महीभुजाम् ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
The power of the kings is the befitting money given to the parents of a bride; according to this, the strongest man is to acquire the bride, a jewel. The Kṣattriyas that are weak can never acquire that. Make this the rule in this marriage. This earth is fit to be enjoyed by the heroes only and not by the cowards and intriguing persons. Otherwise quarrels are sure to ensue amongst the kings.
Ch 20 Verse 8 Verse
Sanskrit Text
एवं विवादे संवृत्ते राज्ञां तत्र परस्परम् । आहूतस्तु सभामध्ये सुबाहुर्नृपसत्तमः ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
The dispute thus arose in that Svayamvara hall; and the king Subāhu was called in.
Ch 20 Verse 9 Verse
Sanskrit Text
समाहूय नृपाः सर्वे तमूचुस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः । राजन्नीतिस्त्वया कार्या विवाहेऽत्र समाहिता ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation
The kings that could see the reality of things then addressed the king Subāhu. “O king! You are requested to establish a golden rule in this marriage ceremony.
Ch 20 Verse 10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
किं ते चिकीर्षितं राजंस्तद्वदस्व समाहितः । पुत्र्याः प्रदानं कस्मै ते रोचते नृप चेतसि ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
What is your object in calling this Svayamvara. Better give it out after a mature consideration. Please be explicit whom have you intended to give over your daughter in this marriage?”
Ch 20 Verse 11-12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सुबाहुरुवाच । पुत्र्या मे मनसा कामं वृतः किल सुदर्शनः । मया निवारितोऽत्यर्थं न सा प्रत्येति मे वचः ॥ ११ ॥ किं करोमि सुताया मे न वशे वर्तते मनः । सुदर्शनस्तथैकाकी सम्प्राप्तोऽस्ति निराकुलः ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
Subāhu said :-- “My daughter has mentally selected Sudarśana; I prevented her repeatedly from doing this; but she did not accept my word. What shall I do now? The mind of my daughter now is not at her will. Sudarśana, too, though uninvited, has come here singly and is residing calmly, without any signs of disturbance in his mind.”
Ch 20 Verse 13-14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । सम्पन्ना भूभुजः सर्वे समाहूय सुदर्शनम् । ऊचुः समागतं शान्तमेकाकिनमतन्द्रिताः ॥ १३ ॥ राजपुत्र महाभाग केनाहूतोऽसि सुव्रत । एकाकी यः समायातः समाजे भूभृतामिह ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- Then the chief kings all invited Sudarśana there; Sudarśana, too, came there quietly, and the princes, seeing his quiet nature, asked him, “O one, engaged in practising vows! Who has invited you here? Why have you come here singly, in this assembly of kings?
Ch 20 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न वै सैन्यं न सचिवा न कोशो न बृहद्बलम् । किमर्थञ्च समायातस्तत्त्वं ब्रूहि महामते ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
You have no force, no ministers, no help, no wealth, and no army. O intelligent! Then explain why have you come here alone?
Ch 20 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
युद्धकामा नृपतयो वर्तन्तेऽत्र समागमे । कन्यार्थं सैन्यसम्पन्नाः किं त्वं कर्तुमिहेच्छसि ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
In this assembly of kings you see that the powerful monarchs are ready to fight with each other for the sake of this princess. What do you intend to do under those circumstances?
Ch 20 Verse 17 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भ्राता ते सुबलः शूरः सम्प्राप्तोऽस्ति जिघृक्षया । युधाजिच्च महाबाहुः साहाय्यं कर्तुमागतः ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation
Your brother, too, is come here to have the princess; he has got his army and is also marked with his strength and valor. The powerful Yudhājit has come here also to help him.
Ch 20 Verse 18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गच्छ वा तिष्ठ राजेन्द्र याथातथ्यमुदाहृतम् । त्वयि सैन्यविहीने च यथेष्टं कुरु सुव्रत ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
O observer of good vows! Seeing you without any army, we have narrated to you all the facts. Now think and do accordingly. If you please, remain here or go anywhere else.”
Ch 20 Verse 19-20 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सुदर्शन उवाच । न बलं न सहायो मे न कोशो दुर्गसंश्रयः । न मित्राणि न सौहार्दी न नृपा रक्षका मम ॥ १९ ॥ अत्र स्वयंवरं श्रुत्वा द्रष्टुकाम इहागतः । स्वप्ने देव्या प्रेरितोऽस्मि भगवत्या न संशयः ॥ २० ॥
English Translation
Sudarśana replied :-- “True, I have no army, no help, no wealth, no forts, no friends or no kings to protect me. Hearing that Svayamvara will be held here, I have come here to witness it. But there is one particularity here; it is this :-- The Devī Bhagavatī has ordered me in my dream to come here. Under Her command I have come here; there is no doubt in this.
Ch 20 Verse 21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नान्यच्चिकीर्षितं मेऽद्य मामाह जगदीश्वरी । तया यद्विहितं तच्च भविताऽद्य न संशयः ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
I have got no other object in view; I have obeyed what the Bhagavatī Bhuvaneśvarī has ordered me to do. Today will happen, no doubt, what She has ordained.
Ch 20 Verse 22-23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न शत्रुरस्ति संसारे कोऽप्यत्र जगतीश्वराः । सर्वत्र पश्यतो मेऽद्य भवानीं जगदम्बिकाम् ॥ २२ ॥ यः करिष्यति शत्रुत्वं मया सह नृपात्मजाः । शास्ता तस्य महाविद्या नाहं जानामि शत्रुताम् ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
O kings! I am seeing everywhere the Supreme Goddess Bhagavatī Bhavānī. Therefore there is no enemy of mine in this world; but he who will turn out an enemy of mine, will be duly punished by the Mahā Vidyā Mahā Māyā. I do not know what is enmity?
Ch 20 Verse 24 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यद्भावि तद्वै भविता नान्यथा नृपसत्तमाः । का चिन्ता ह्यत्र कर्तव्या दैवाधीनोऽस्मि सर्वथा ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation
O kings! What is inevitable will surely come to pass. There will be nothing otherwise. I am always depending on Fate, Destiny. What is the use, then, in thinking what will be the result?
Ch 20 Verse 25 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवभूतमनुष्येषु सर्वभूतेषु सर्वदा । सर्वेषां तत्कृता भक्तिर्नान्यथा नृपसत्तमाः ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation
Everywhere in the Devas, in the spirits, in men, in all the beings, the Devī’s power is existent; it cannot be otherwise.
Ch 20 Verse 26 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सा यं चिकीर्षते भूपं तं करोति नृपाधिपाः । निर्धनं वा नरं कामं का चिन्ता वै तदा मम ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation
O kings! Whenever She wishes, She makes kings, masters of wealth or devoid of wealth. What is, then, the use of bothering my head in this?
Ch 20 Verse 27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तामृते परमां शक्तिं ब्रह्मविष्णुहरादयः । न शक्ताः स्पन्दितुं देवाः का चिन्ता मे तदा नृपाः ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
When even the Gods Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Maheśa without Her presence, become powerless enough to move their hands or feet, then why shall I be anxious for the result?
Ch 20 Verse 28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अशक्तो वा सशक्तो वा यादृशस्तादृशस्त्वहम् । तदाज्ञया नृपाद्यैव सम्प्राप्तोऽस्मि स्वयंवरे ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
O kings! Whether I am unable or able or an ordinary person, you have nothing to consider; I have come here in this assembly hall under the command of the Supreme Bhagavatī.
Ch 20 Verse 29-30 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सा यदिच्छति तत्कुर्यान्मम किं चिन्तनेन वै । नात्र शङ्का प्रकर्तव्या सत्यमेतद्ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ २९ ॥ जये पराजये लज्जा न मेऽत्राण्वपि पार्थिवाः । भगवत्यास्तु लज्जास्ति तदधीनोऽस्मि सर्वथा ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation
What She has willed, She will do that. I am not to care for that. O high minded ones! You need not be afraid at all in this. I have told you all truth. Victory or defeat, I feel no shame in either of them. For I am always under the control of Bhagavatī; therefore if there be any shame here, it is all Hers.”
Ch 20 Verse 31-33 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । इति तस्य तदाकर्ण्य वचनं राजसत्तमाः । ऊचुः परस्परं प्रेक्ष्य निश्चयज्ञा नराधिपाः ॥ ३१ ॥ सत्यमुक्तं त्वया साधो न मिथ्या कर्हिचिद्भवेत् । तथाप्युज्जयनीनाथस्त्वां हन्तुं परिकाङ्क्षति ॥ ३२ ॥ त्वत्कृते न दयादिष्टा त्वां ब्रवीमो महामते । यद्युक्तं तत्त्वया कार्यं विचार्य मनसाऽनघ ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- Hearing thus his words, and seeing that his mind is firmly devoted to Bhagavatī, the kings saw each other and said thus :-- “O Sudarśana! What you have said is quite true; it is never otherwise; still Yudhājit, the king of Ujjain is intent on killing you. O intelligent! O sinless! we have all come to know that there is no trace of evil in you. We were all overcome with pity for you; hence we have informed you; now think and do the needful?”
Ch 20 Verse 34 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सुदर्शन उवाच । सत्यमुक्तं भवद्भिश्च कृपावद्भिः सुहृज्जनैः । किं ब्रवीमि पुनर्वाक्यमुक्त्वा नृपतिसत्तमाः ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation
Sudarśana said, “You are all kind and large hearted; what you all have said is quite true. What shall I tell you, being a minor as yet!
Ch 20 Verse 35 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न मृत्युः केनचिद्भाव्यः कस्यचिद्वा कदाचन । दैवाधीनमिदं सर्वं जगत्स्थावरजङ्गमम् ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation
O kings! No one can cause the death of another. All this world, moving and unmoving, is under the control of Fate.
Ch 20 Verse 36-38 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्ववशोऽयं न जीवोऽस्ति स्वकर्मवशगः सदा । तत्कर्म त्रिविधं प्रोक्तं विद्वद्भिस्तत्त्वदर्शिभिः ॥ ३६ ॥ सञ्चितं वर्तमानञ्च प्रारब्धञ्च तृतीयकम् । कालकर्मस्वभावैश्च ततं सर्वमिदं जगत् ॥ ३७ ॥ न देवो मानुषं हन्तुं शक्तः कालागमं विना । हतं निमित्तमात्रेण हन्ति कालः सनातनः ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation
No soul is independent; every one is under the effects of one’s own Karma. The Pundits that have realised the Truth, say that Karma is of three kinds, Accumulated, Present, and Prārabdha? This whole world is due to Kāla (Time), Karma (action) and Svabhāva (Nature); unless the proper time comes, even the Devas cannot kill men. The men are killed on account of some cause, immediate; but the Great Time is the real Destroyer.
Ch 20 Verse 39 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यथा पिता मे निहतः सिंहेनामित्रकर्षणः । तथा मातामहोऽप्येवं युद्धे युधाजिता हतः ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation
My father, though a destroyer of many others, was himself killed by a lion and my mother’s father was slain by Yudhājit in the battle.
Ch 20 Verse 40 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यत्नकोटिं प्रकुर्वाणो हन्यते दैवयोगतः । जीवेद्वर्षसहस्राणि रक्षणेन विना नरः ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation
The Jīvas, though caring hard to preserve their lives, are killed by Fate in spite of all their cares; and they live thousand years though there is none to protect them.
Ch 20 Verse 41 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नाहं बिभेमि धर्मिष्ठाः कदाचिच्च युधाजितः । दैवमेव परं मत्वा सुस्थितोऽस्मि सदा नृपाः ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation
O religious kings! I do not fear a bit from Yudhājit. I consider Fate as the Supreme and I therefore remain always undisturbed, calm and quiet.
Ch 20 Verse 42 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्मरणं सततं नित्यं भगवत्याः करोम्यहम् । विश्वस्य जननी देवी कल्याणं सा करिष्यति ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation
Daily and constantly I remember Bhagavatī, Who is the Mother of all this Universe. She will look after my welfare.
Ch 20 Verse 43 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पूर्वार्जितं हि भोक्तव्यं शुभं वाप्यशुभं तथा । स्वकृतस्य च भोगेन कीदृक्शोको विजानताम् ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation
Behold! One will have certainly to bear the burden of one’s past Karma, whether it be good or it be bad; one’s own actions must bear their fruits. Then why shall he be sorry, who has come to know this?
Ch 20 Verse 44 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्वकर्मफलयोगेन प्राप्य दुःखमचेतनः । निमित्तकारणे वैरं करोत्यल्पमतिः किल ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation
The less intelligent deluded persons, on getting pain from their own actions, turn out enemies on very trifling matters.
Ch 20 Verse 45 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न तथाऽहं विजानामि वैरं शोकं भयं तथा । निःशङ्कमिह सम्प्राप्तः समाजे भूभृतामिह ॥ ४५ ॥
English Translation
I do not grieve nor do I fear on account of such enemies. I am staying here in this assembly of kings, cool-minded.
Ch 20 Verse 46 Verse
Sanskrit Text
एकाकी द्रष्टुकामोऽहं स्वयंवरमनुत्तमम् । भविष्यति च यद्भाव्यं प्राप्तोऽस्मि चण्डिकाज्ञया ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation
Under the order of Candikā, I have come here to see this Svayamvara; whatever is inevitable will surely come to pass.
Ch 20 Verse 47 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भगवत्याः प्रमाणं मे नान्यं जानामि संयतः । तत्कृतञ्च सुखं दुःखं भविष्यति च नान्यथा ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation
The words of the Bhagavatī are the best proof; I do not know any other. My mind is entirely given up to Her. There will be nothing otherwise than what She has ordained; whether it is good or whether it is bad.
Ch 20 Verse 48 Verse
Sanskrit Text
युधाजित्सुखमाप्नोतु न मे वैरं नृपोत्तमाः । यः करिष्यति मे वैरं स प्राप्स्यति फलं तथा ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation
O kings! Let Yudhājit remain in peace. I have no enmity with him. He, who will deal inimically with me, will certainly reap his reward. There is not the least doubt in this.”
Ch 20 Verse 49 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । इत्युक्तास्ते तथा तेन सन्तुष्टा भूभुजः स्थिताः । सोऽपि स्वमाश्रमं प्राप्य सुस्थितः सम्बभूव ह ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O king! When Sudarśana addressed them thus, all the kings became very glad and they all remained there for the Svayamvara. Sudarśana, too, went to his camp and remained also calm and quiet.
Ch 20 Verse 50 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अपरेऽह्नि शुभे काले नृपाः सम्मन्त्रिताः किल । सुबाहुना नृपेणाथ रुचिरे वै स्वमण्डपे ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation
Next day the king Subāhu invited all the kings present in his city to their respective seats in the Svayamvara hall.
Ch 20 Verse 51 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दिव्यास्तरणयुक्तेषु मञ्चेषु रचितेषु च । उपविष्टाश्च राजानः शुभालङ्करणैर्युताः ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation
The princes and kings, decorated with best ornaments, came and took their seats on their respective platforms, covered with valuable carpets of best workmanship.
Ch 20 Verse 52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दिव्यवेषधराः कामं विमानेष्वमरा इव । दीप्यमानाः स्थितास्तत्र स्वयंवरदिदृक्षया ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation
The kings then looked like the celestial Devas, wearing divine ornaments and apparels, blazing with the lustrous light of gems, and remained to see the Svayamvara affair.
Ch 20 Verse 53 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इति चिन्तापराः सर्वे कदा साप्यागमिष्यति । भाग्यवन्तं नृपश्रेष्ठं श्रुतपुण्यं वरिष्यति ॥ ५३ ॥
English Translation
Every one there had this foremost thought in his mind when will the princess, the bride elect, would come there; and who will be the man so fortunate as to be blessed with garlands offered by her (as a token of selection of the bridegroom)!
Ch 20 Verse 54 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यदा सुदर्शनं दैवात्स्रजा सम्भूषयेदिह । विवादो वै नृपाणां च भविता नात्र संशयः ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation
If, accidentally, she offers the garland to Sudarśana in this Svayamvara assembly, then will ensue, no doubt, desperate struggles amongst the kings.
Ch 20 Verse 55-58 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इत्येवं चिन्त्यमानास्ते भूपा मञ्चेषु संस्थिताः । वादित्रघोषः सुमहानुत्थितो नृपमण्डपे ॥ ५५ ॥ अथ काशीपतिः प्राह सुतां स्नातां स्वलङ्कृताम् । मधूकमालासंयुक्तां क्षौमवासोविभूषिताम् ॥ ५६ ॥ विवाहोपस्करैर्युक्तां दिव्यां सिन्धुसुतोपमाम् । चिन्तापरां सुवसनां स्मितपूर्वमिदं वचः ॥ ५७ ॥ उत्तिष्ठ पुत्रि सुनसे करे धृत्वा शुभां स्रजम् । व्रज मण्डपमध्येऽद्य समाजं पश्य भूभुजाम् ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation
While they were thus meditating, sounds of drums were loudly sounded. 56-58. Then Subāhu, the king of Benares, went to her daughter and found that Śaśikalā had just taken her bath and put on her silken clothes, and adorned herself with various ornaments and sweet garlands. Thus, dressed in complete marriage dress, she began to shine like another Goddess Lakṣmī, the Goddess of wealth. The king, on seeing his daughter dressed in silken cloth, afflicted with anxious thoughts, just smiled and said, “Child! Rise and take the beautiful garlands by your hands and go to the the Svayamvara hall and just look at the assembly of kings.
Ch 20 Verse 59 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गुणवान् रूपसम्पन्नः कुलीनश्च नृपोत्तमः । तव चित्ते वसेद्यस्तु तं वृणुष्व सुमध्यमे ॥ ५९ ॥
English Translation
O lean bodied one! Whoever, well-qualified, beautiful, and of noble birth, amongst the kings is reigning in your mind, better select him.
Ch 20 Verse 60 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देशदेशाधिपाः सर्वे मञ्चेषु रचितेषु च । संविष्टाः पश्य तन्वङ्गि वरयस्व यथारुचि ॥ ६० ॥
English Translation
O graceful! The kings from various quarters are adorning their respective seats; better go and see and select whomever you like.”
Ch 20 Verse 61 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । तं तथा भाषमाणं वै पितरं मितभाषिणी । उवाच वचनं बाला ललितं धर्मसंयुतम् ॥ ६१ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- When Subāhu had spoken thus, Śaśikalā, who generally talked little, replied with sweet sonorous words, impregnated with religious truth.
Ch 20 Verse 62 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शशिकलोवाच । नाहं दृष्टिपथे राज्ञां गमिष्यामि पितः किल । कामुकानां नरेशानां गच्छन्त्यन्याश्च योषितः ॥ ६२ ॥
English Translation
“Father! I won’t go before the kings who are inspired by lust; women like me never go there; it is those that are dissolute that attend those places.
Ch 20 Verse 63 Verse
Sanskrit Text
धर्मशास्त्रे श्रुतं तात मयेदं वचनं किल । एक एव वरो नार्या निरीक्ष्यः स्यान्न चापरः ॥ ६३ ॥
English Translation
Father! I have heard from the religious texts that women should cast their glances on their husbands only and not on any other.
Ch 20 Verse 64 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सतीत्वं निर्गतं तस्या या प्रयाति बहूनथ । सङ्कल्पयन्ति ते सर्वे दृष्ट्वा मे भवतात्त्विति ॥ ६४ ॥
English Translation
The woman that goes to many persons is mentally claimed by all; each of them contemplates strongly “Let this woman be mine.” Thus her chastity is destroyed.
Ch 20 Verse 65-66 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्वयंवरे स्रजं धृत्वा यदागच्छति मण्डपे । सामान्या सा तदा जाता कुलटेवापरा वधूः ॥ ६५ ॥ वारस्त्री विपणे गत्वा यथा वीक्ष्य नरांस्थितान् । गुणागुणपरिज्ञानं करोति निजमानसे ॥ ६६ ॥
English Translation
Desirous of selecting her husband, when the woman holding in her hands, the garland for her would-be-husband, goes to the Svayamvara hall, then she turns out like an ordinary unchaste woman. As a prostitute going to a public shop looks on many persons and judges of their merits and demerits according to her own power of judgment, the maid that goes in the Svayamvara hall does exactly the same.
Ch 20 Verse 67 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नैकभावा यथा वेश्या वृथा पश्यति कामुकम् । तथाऽहं मण्डपे गत्वा कुर्वे वारस्त्रिया कृतम् ॥ ६७ ॥
English Translation
How can I behave myself in the hall of the assembly of kings like a prostitute, who does not attach her feelings firmly on a single individual but glances constantly at many lustful persons.
Ch 20 Verse 68 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वृद्धैरेतैः कृतं धर्मं न करिष्यामि साम्प्रतम् । पत्नीव्रतं तथा कामं चरिष्येऽहं धृतव्रता ॥ ६८ ॥
English Translation
Though this system of Svayamvara is approved by the elderly persons, I am not going to follow that now. I will take the vow of a chaste woman and act up to that doctrine as perfectly.
Ch 20 Verse 69 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सामान्या प्रथमं गत्वा कृत्वा सङ्कल्पितं बहु । वृणोति चैकं तद्वद्वै वृणोमि कथमद्य वै ॥ ६९ ॥
English Translation
I will never be able to act like an ordinary woman going in the Svayamvara hall, mentally determining many and finally selecting one.
Ch 20 Verse 70 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सुदर्शनो मया पूर्वं वृतः सर्वात्मना पितः । तमृते नान्यथा कर्तुमिच्छामि नृपसत्तम ॥ ७० ॥
English Translation
Father! From the very beginning I have given myself up to Sudarśana in mind, word and deed. I have not the least inclination to leave him and select another in his stead.
Ch 20 Verse 71 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विवाहविधिना देहि कन्यादानं शुभे दिने । सुदर्शनाय नृपते यदीच्छसि शुभं मम ॥ ७१ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां तृतीयस्कन्धे स्वपितरं प्रति शशिकलावाक्यं नामविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ३.२०॥
English Translation
O King! If you want to have my welfare, then give your daughter on an auspicious day and in an auspicious lagna to Sudarśana, according to the prescribed rites.” Thus ends the 20th Chapter on the Svayamvara hall and the kings conversation there in Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Mahārṣi Veda Vyāsa.

# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 3, CHAPTER 25

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 1-2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ३.२५ पञ्चविंशोऽध्यायः । देवीस्थापनवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> व्यास उवाच ।
> गत्वाऽयोध्यां नृपश्रेष्ठो गृहं राज्ञः सुहृद्वृतः ।
> शत्रुजिन्मातरं प्राह प्रणम्य शोकसङ्कुलाम् ॥ १ ॥
> 
> मातर्न ते मया पुत्रः सङ्ग्रामे निहतः किल ।
> न पिता ते युधाजिच्च शपे ते चरणौ तथा ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

1-4. Vyāsa said :--

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 3-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दुर्गया तौ हतौ सङ्ख्ये नापराधो ममात्र वै ।
> अवश्यम्भाविभावेषु प्रतीकारो न विद्यते ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> न शोकोऽत्र त्वया कार्यो मृतपुत्रस्य मानिनि ।
> स्वकर्मवशगो जीवो भुङ्क्ते भोगान्सुखासुखान् ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The king Sudarśana, surrounded by his friends, on coming to the palace at Ayodhyā, bowed down to Līlāvatī, the mother of Śatrujit, and said :-- “O mother! I swear by touching your feet, that I have not killed in battle your son Śatrujit nor your father Yudhājit; it is the Devī Durgā that has killed them; I am not to be blamed a bit in this. O mother! You need not be sensitive in this; there is no remedy for what will inevitably come to pass; therefore you do not be sorry for the death of your son; you must know that the Jīvas enjoy pleasure and pain as the results of their own Karmas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दासोऽस्मि तव भो मातर्यथा मम मनोरमा ।
> तथा त्वमपि धर्मज्ञे न भेदोऽस्ति मनागपि ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O mother! I am your servant; you are entitled to the same respect and worship as Manoramā, my own mother; there is no difference whatsoever between her and you.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अवश्यमेव भोक्तव्यं कृतं कर्म शुभाशुभम् ।
> तस्मान्न शोचितव्यं ते सुखे दुःखे कदाचन ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O mother! One must bear the effects of one’s Karma, good or bad; therefore when pleasure or pain arises, you should not be glad or otherwise.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दुःखे दुःखाधिकान्पश्येत्सुखे पश्येत्सुखाधिकम् ।
> आत्मानं शोकहर्षाभ्यां शत्रुभ्यामिव नार्पयेत् ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When pain arises, more pain is said to be conceived and when pleasure arises, more pleasure is seen. But the learned say that man ought not to subject himself to excessive pleasure or pain.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दैवाधीनमिदं सर्वं नात्माधीनं कदाचन ।
> न शोकेन तदाऽऽत्मानं शोषयेन्मतिमान्नरः ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O mother! This whole world is under Fate, Destiny; nothing of it is yours. Therefore the intelligent persons ought not to grieve their hearts at any time with sorrow.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यथा दारुमयी योषा नटादीनां प्रचेष्टते ।
> तथा स्वकर्मवशगो देही सर्वत्र वर्तते ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

As the wooden dolls dance in a stage as danced by the actor, so the individual souls here work as the results of their past Karmas; there is no doubt in this.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अहं वनगतो मातर्नाभवं दुःखमानसः ।
> चिन्तयन्स्वकृतं कर्म भोक्तव्यमिति वेद्मि च ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O mother! I know that the effect of one’s own Karma, must have to be borne; it is, on that account, that I never felt sorrow in my exile in the forest.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मृतो मातामहोऽत्रैव विधुरा जननी मम ।
> भयातुरा गृहीत्वा मां निर्ययौ गहनं वनम् ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

You are quite aware that my mother’s father was killed here, and my mother, becoming very much afraid and sorrowful, took me and escaped to the forest.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 12-13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> लुण्ठिता तस्करैर्मार्गे वस्त्रहीना तथा कृता ।
> पाथेयञ्च हृतं सर्वं बालपुत्रा निराश्रया ॥ १२ ॥
> 
> माता गृहीत्वा मां प्राप्ता भारद्वाजाश्रमं प्रति ।
> विदल्लोऽयं समायातस्तथा धात्रेयिकाऽबला ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The robbers robbed us of everything save our clothes on our bodies; I was then very young; my mother was without any shelter; she carried me with this minister Vidalla and my helpless nurse to the hermitage of Bhāradvāja.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मुनिभिर्मुनिपत्नीभिर्दयायुक्तैः समन्ततः ।
> पोषिताः फलनीवारैर्वयं तत्र स्थितास्त्रयः ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

There the kind hermit and his wife and the other wives of the hermits protected our lives in that forest, with the roots and fruits, that can be obtained there in that forest. Thus our time passed.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दुःखं नमे तदा ह्यासीत्सुखं नाद्य धनागमे ।
> न वैरं न च मात्सर्यं मम चित्ते तु कर्हिचित् ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Mother! I had felt no pain then; nor do I feel any pleasure at present, when wealth is flowing unto me. What more to say, I have no feeling of jealousy or envy whatsoever in my mind.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नीवारभक्षणं श्रेष्ठं राजभोगात्परन्तपे ।
> तदाशी नरकं याति न नीवाराशनः क्वचित् ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O mother! Rather it is better, in my eyes, to subsist on roots and fruits than to enjoy kingdoms; for the kings go to hell; but the ascetics living on roots and fruits never meet with that result.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> धर्मस्याचरणं कार्यं पुरुषेण विजानता ।
> सञ्जित्येन्द्रियवर्गं वै यथा न नरकं व्रजेत् ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The wise should undoubtedly practice Dharma and control their passions and thus save themselves from being led into hells.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 18-19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मानुष्यं दुर्लभं मातः खण्डेऽस्मिन्भारते शुभे ।
> आहारादि सुखं नूनं भवेत्सर्वासु योनिषु ॥ १८ ॥
> 
> प्राप्य तं मानुषं देहं कर्तव्यं धर्मसाधनम् ।
> स्वर्गमोक्षप्रदं नॄणां दुर्लभं चान्ययोनिषु ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O mother! The human birth in this auspicious Bhāratvarṣa is seldom obtained. The enjoyments in eating and drinking are possible in every womb, but it is highly iñcumbent on us when we have got the privilege of this human birth, to earn Dharma, leading to the Heavens and salvation which can be very rarely attained in being born in other wombs.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 20-21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इत्युक्ता सा तदा तेन लीलावत्यतिलज्जिता ।
> पुत्रशोकं परित्यज्य तमाहाश्रुविलोचना ॥ २० ॥
> 
> सापराधाऽस्मि पुत्राहं कृता पित्रा युधाजिता ।
> हत्या मातामहं तेऽत्र हृतं राज्यं तु येन वै ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- When Sudarśana had told thus, Līlāvatī became very abashed; she cast aside the sorrow for the death of her son, told him with tears in her eyes :-- “O my son Sudarśana! I am very much guilty on account of my father Yudhājit killing your mother’s father and taking hold of the sovereignty of this kingdom.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> न तं वारयितुं शक्ता तदाऽहं न सुतं मम ।
> यत्कृतं कर्म तेनैव नापराधोऽस्ति मे सुत ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I could not then hinder my father and son; whatever unlawful evil and cruel deeds were then committed, all were done by my father Yudhājit. Therefore, my child, I am not to be made guilty in any way in these doings.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तौ मृतौ स्वकृतेनैव कारणं त्वं तयोर्न च ।
> नाहं शोचामि तं पुत्रं सदा शोचामि तत्कृतम् ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Both my father and son were killed out of the wickedness of their own actions; how can you account for those wicked things? Child! I am not expressing sorrow at the death of my son; I have been pained by his doings.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 24-25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पुत्र त्वमसि कल्याण भगिनी मे मनोरमा ।
> न क्रोधो न च शोको मे त्वयि पुत्र मनागपि ॥ २४ ॥
> 
> कुरु राज्यं महाभाग प्रजाः पालय सुव्रत ।
> भगवत्याः प्रसादेन प्राप्तमेतदकण्टकम् ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O noble souled one; You are my son; Manoramā is my sister; Child! I am not at all offended with you nor am I the least sorry for your obtaining the kingdom; Child! you are very fortunate; therefore you have obtained, by the grace of Bhagavatī, this kingdom without any enemies; now rule your subjects according to the prescribed rules of Dharma.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 26-28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तदाकर्ण्य वचो मातुर्नत्वा तां नृपनन्दनः ।
> जगाम भवनं रम्यं यत्र पूर्वं मनोरमा ॥ २६ ॥
> 
> न्यवसत्तत्र गत्वा तु सर्वानाहूय मन्त्रिणः ।
> दैवज्ञानथ पप्रच्छ मुहूर्तं दिवसं शुभम् ॥ २७ ॥
> 
> सिंहासनं तथा हैमं कारयित्वा मनोहरम् ।
> सिंहासने स्थितां देवीं पूजयिष्ये सदाऽप्यहम् ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O king! The king Sudarśana heard Līlāvatī and bowed down at her feet. Then he went to the beautiful palace where Manoramā had previously gone and began to live there. Inviting the ministers and the astrologers, he asked them what was the auspicious day and the auspicious moment, that he can establish Durgā Devī on a beautiful golden throne and he would worship Her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्थापयित्वाऽऽसने देवीं धर्मार्थकाममोक्षदाम् ।
> राज्यं पश्चात्करिष्यामि यथा रामादिभिः कृतम् ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

“O ministers! First I will install on the throne the Devī, the Awarder of the four main objects of human pursuits (viz. virtue, wealth, enjoyment and final beatitude) and then I will govern my kingdom like the kings Śrī Rāma Candra and others.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पूजनीया सदा देवी सर्वैर्नागरिकैर्जनैः ।
> माननीया शिवा शक्तिः सर्वकामार्थसिद्धिदा ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

All the people of this city of Ayodhyā ought also to worship this Auspicious Śakti, the Highest Energy, the Giver of all desires and Siddhis, and that is respected and adored by all.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इत्युक्ता मन्त्रिणस्ते तु चक्रुर्वै राजशासनम् ।
> प्रासादं कारयामासुः शिल्पिभिः सुमनोरमम् ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The ministers, on hearing his words, had a beautiful palace built by the engineers, artists and workmen and proclaimed in the city the king’s proclamation.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 32</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

Then the king Sudarśana had an image of the Devī nicely built and got that installed with the help of the Pundits, versed in the Vedas, on an auspicious day and at an auspicious moment.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

>
**English Translation:**

The intelligent king performed the worship and Homa ceremony, according to the prescribed rules, and thus finally settled the ceremony of invocation of the Deity into the new image and established it as an idol in the temple.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 34</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

O Janamejaya! There the soundings of the various drums and other musical instruments, the chanting of the Veda mantrams by the Brāhmaṇas, and sweet music were heard; and various sorts of festivities and rejoicings were celebrated.

</details>

<details>

**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- Thus completing the installation ceremony of the Durgā Devī by the Brāhmaṇas, versed in the Vedas, the king Sudarśana duly worshipped the image in various ways, etc.

</details>

<details>

Thus gaining his father’s kingdom and worshipping the Devī, he and the Devī became celebrated throughout the kingdom.

</details>

<details>

**English Translation:**

The religious largehearted Sudarśana, on gaining his kingdom, brought all the other feudatory princes under his control by the sheer force of his religious character.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 38</b></summary>
  <br>

The subjects became happy and got honor in the reign of Sudarśana, as they got before in the reigns of Dilīp, Raghu and Rāmacandra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 39</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

The virtue of all the citizens under Varṇāśrama shone complete with all its four pādas; and there remained none in the world irreligious.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 40</b></summary>

**English Translation:**

In villages after villages, the chief townsmen began to build temples, worship the Goddess there with all their jolliness. Thus everywhere in the Kosala kingdom spread the Devī worship.

</details>

<details>

On the other hand, the king Subāhu established the Idol in Benares, had temples built and worshipped there the Devī.

</details>

<details>

**English Translation:**

The inhabitants of Kāśī became then filled with devotion and intense love towards the Devī and duly worshipped Her, as they used to do to Śiva in the temple of Viśvanātha.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 43</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विख्याता सा बभूवाथ दुर्गादेवी धरातले ।
> देशे देशे महाराज तस्या भक्तिर्व्यवर्धत ॥ ४३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus the Durgā Devī became very widely celebrated in this world. O king! Thus in different countries, the devotion began to increase towards the Goddess.

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 25 Verse 46</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नवरात्रेषु सर्वेषु चक्रुः सर्वे विधानतः ।
> अर्चनं हवनं यागं देव्या भक्तिपरा जनाः ॥ ४६ ॥
>
> *Colophon:*
> इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां
> तृतीयस्कन्धे देवीस्थापनवर्णनं नाम पञ्चविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ३.२५॥
>
**English Translation:**

O king! From that time all the people used to worship, perform Homa ceremony and sacrifice duly in honour of the Devī in every Navarātri (for the first nine days of the bright half in the months of Āśvin and Caitra). Here ends the 25th Chapter on the installation of the Devī in Ayodhyā and Benares in the Mahā Purāṇam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

</details>

[Ch 30 Verse 1-2]
३.३० त्रिंशोऽध्यायः । रामाय देवीवरदानम् ।


व्यास उवाच ।
एवं तौ संविदं कृत्वा यावत्तूष्णीं बभूवतुः ।
आजगाम तदाऽऽकाशान्नारदो भगवानृषिः ॥ १ ॥

रणयन्महतीं वीणां स्वरग्रामविभूषिताम् ।
गायन्बृहद्रथं साम तदा तमुपतस्थिवान् ॥ २ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O king! Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, discussing thus, remained silent; when the Ṛṣi Nārada appeared there from the sky above, singing the Rathāntara Sāma Veda hymns in tune and musical gamut with his renowned lute.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 3-4]
दृष्ट्वा तं राम उत्थाय ददावथ वृषं शुभम् ।
आसनं चार्घ्यपाद्यञ्च कृतवानमितद्युतिः ॥ ३ ॥

पूजां परमिकां कृत्वा कृताञ्जलिरुपस्थितः ।
उपविष्टः समीपे तु कृताज्ञो मुनिना हरिः ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation:
Rāmacandra, of indomitable prowess, on beholding him rose up from his seat and gave him quickly an excellent seat and offerings of water for washing his feet. Then he worshipped the Muni and stood with folded hands. When the Muni ordered him, he took his seat close by Nārada.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 5-8]
उपविष्टं तदा रामं सानुजं दुःखमानसम् ।
पप्रच्छ नारदः प्रीत्या कुशलं मुनिसत्तमः ॥ ५ ॥

कथं राघव शोकार्तो यथा वै प्राकृतो नरः ।
हृतां सीतां च जानामि रावणेन दुरात्मना ॥ ६ ॥

सुरसद्मगतश्चाहं श्रुतवाञ्जनकात्मजाम् ।
पौलस्त्येन हृतां मोहान्मरणं स्वमजानता ॥ ७ ॥

तव जन्म च काकुत्स्थ पौलस्त्यनिधनाय वै ।
मैथिलीहरणं जातमेतदर्थं नराधिप ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation:
On Rāmacandra taking his seat there with Lakṣmaṇa with a grievous heart, Nārada asked him in a sweet tone “O Descendant of Raghu! Why are you being afflicted with sorrows like an ordinary mortal? I know that the evil minded Rāvaṇa has stolen Sītā Devī. I heard while in the heavens that Rāvaṇa, the descendant of Pulastya, stole away Jānakī, out of fascination, could not know that would be the cause of his death. O Descendant in the family of Kākutstha! It is for the killing of Rāvaṇa that your birth has taken place; and for that purpose Jānakī has been stolen now. Note :-- The real Jānakī was not stolen; Her shadow form was stolen.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 9-12]
पूर्वजन्मनि वैदेही मुनिपुत्री तपस्विनी ।
रावणेन वने दृष्टा तपस्यन्ती शुचिस्मिता ॥ ९ ॥

प्रार्थिता रावणेनासौ भव भार्येति राघव ।
तिरस्कृतस्तयाऽसौ वै जग्राह कबरं बलात् ॥ १० ॥

शशाप तत्क्षणं राम रावणं तापसी भृशम् ।
कुपिता त्यक्तुमिच्छन्ती देहं संस्पर्शदूषितम् ॥ ११ ॥

दुरात्मंस्तव नाशार्थं भविष्यामि धरातले ।
अयोनिजा वरा नारी त्यक्त्वा देहं जहावपि ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation:
O Rāghava! The Devī Jānakī, in her previous birth, was the daughter of a Muni and practised asceticism. While engaged in her austerities, in her holy hermitage, Rāvaṇa came and looking at her, prayed that beautiful woman to become his wife. Hearing this, she gave a good reproach to Rāvaṇa, when he perforce caught hold of her hairs. That ascetic woman got very angry, and, considering her body polluted by the devil’s contact, resolved to put an end to her life and cursed Rāvaṇa, thus :-- “O Villain! I will be born on the surface of the earth, not from any womb but simply for your destruction and ruin.” Thus saying, she parted with her life.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 13]
सेयं रमांशसम्भूता गृहीता तेन रक्षसा ।
विनाशार्थं कुलस्यैव व्याली स्रगिव सम्भ्रमात् ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation:
O Tormentor of the foes! Rāvaṇa, the king of the Rākṣasas, mistook a garland for the extremely poisonous serpent and has stolen away Sītā Devī, the part incarnation of Lakṣmī, in order to root out his race.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 14]
तव जन्म च काकुत्स्थ तस्य नाशाय चामरैः ।
प्रार्थितस्य हरेरंशादजवंशेऽप्यजन्मनः ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation:
O Kākutstha! When the Devas prayed for the destruction of that wicked insolent Rāvaṇa, difficult to be subdued, you are born on this earth, in the family of Aja, as a part incarnate of Hari, beyond birth, old age and death.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 15]
कुरु धैर्यं महाबाहो तत्र सा वर्ततेऽवशा ।
सती धर्मरता सीता त्वां ध्यायन्ती दिवानिशम् ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation:
O mighty-armed! Have patience; Sītā Devī is meditating you, day and night.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 16-17]
कामधेनुपयः पात्रे कृत्वा मघवता स्वयम् ।
पानार्थं प्रेषितं तस्याः पीतं चैवामृतं यथा ॥ १६ ॥

सुरभीदुग्धपानात्सा क्षुत्तुड्दुःखविवर्जिता ।
जाता कमलपत्राक्षी वर्तते वीक्षिता मया ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation:
Indra himself, the king of the Devas, sends the nectar and the Heavenly Cow’s Milk in a pot to Her daily; and She subsists on that, alone. O Lord! On drinking the Heavenly Cow’s Milk, the lotus eyed Sītā Devī is living without any hunger or thirst! I use to see Her daily.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 18]
उपायं कथयाम्यद्य तस्य नाशाय राघव ।
व्रतं कुरुष्व श्रद्धावानाश्विने मासि साम्प्रतम् ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation:
O Descendant of Raghu! I am now telling how that Rāvaṇa can be killed. Perform, in this very month of Āśvin, the vow with devotion.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 19]
नवरात्रोपवासञ्च भगवत्याः प्रपूजनम् ।
सर्वसिद्धिकरं राम जपहोमविधानतः ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation:
Fasting for nine nights, the worship of the Bhagavatī, and repeating the Mantram silently and performing the Homa ceremony, observing all the rules, will certainly fulfill one’s all the desires.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 20-21]
मेघ्यैश्च पशुभिर्देव्या बलिं दत्त्वा विशंसितैः ।
दशांशं हवनं कृत्वा सशक्तस्त्वं भविष्यसि ॥ २० ॥

विष्णुना चरितं पूर्वं महादेवेन ब्रह्मणा ।
तथा मघवता चीर्णं स्वर्गमध्यस्थितेन वै ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation:
In days of yore, Viṣṇu, Śiva and Brahmā and the Devas in the Heavens all performed this worship of the Goddess.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 22]
सुखिना राम कर्तव्यं नवरात्रव्रतं शुभम् ।
विशेषेण च कर्तव्यं पुंसा कष्टगतेन वै ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation:
Therefore, O Rāghava! Every person desiring happiness, specially those that have fallen under great difficulties, ought to do this auspicious ceremony, without the least hesitation.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 23-24]
विश्वामित्रेण काकुत्स्थ कृतमेतन्न संशयः ।
भृगुणाऽथ वसिष्ठेन कश्यपेन तथैव च ॥ २३ ॥

गुरुणा हृतदारेण कृतमेतन्महाव्रतम् ।
तस्मात्त्वं कुरु राजेन्द्र रावणस्य वधाय च ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation:
O Kākutstha! Viśvamitra, Bhṛgu, Vaśiṣṭha and Kaśyapa all of them did this worship before. When some stole away the wife of Bṛhaspati, the Guru of the Devas, he, too, by the force of this worship, got his wife back. Therefore O king! dost thou also celebrate the Pūjā for the destruction of Rāvaṇa.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 25-26]
इन्द्रेण वृत्रनाशाय कृतं व्रतमनुत्तमम् ।
त्रिपुरस्य विनाशाय शिवेनापि पुरा कृतम् ॥ २५ ॥

हरिणा मधुनाशाय कृतं मेरौ महामते ।
विधिवत्कुरु काकुत्स्थ व्रतमेतदतन्द्रितः ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation:
O high minded one! This vow was practised before by Indra for the destruction of Vritra, by Śiva for killing the demon Tripurā, by Nārāyaṇa for the killing of the demons Madhu and Kaiṭava; so you should also firmly resolve to perform duly this vow with your whole heart.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 27]
श्रीराम उवाच ।
का देवी किं प्रभावा सा कुतो जाता किमाह्वया ।
व्रतं किं विधिवद्ब्रूहि सर्वज्ञोऽसि दयानिधे ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation:
Rāma replied :-- “O Ocean of Knowledge! Who is that Devī? What is Her influence; whence has She sprung? What is Her Name? And how is that vow to be duly observed? Kindly describe all these to me in detail.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 28]
नारद उवाच ।
शृणु राम सदा नित्या शक्तिराद्या सनातनी ।
सर्वकामप्रदा देवी पूजिता दुःखनाशिनी ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation:
Nārada answered :-- “Listen, O Rāghava! That Goddess is Eternal and Ever Constant Primordial Force. If you worship Her, all your difficulties will be removed and all your desires will be fulfilled.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 29]
कारणं सर्वजन्तूनां ब्रह्मादीनां रघूद्वह ।
तस्याः शक्तिं विना कोऽपि स्पन्दितुं न क्षमो भवेत् ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation:
She is the source of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and others and of all these living beings. Without Her force, no body would be able even to move their limbs.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 30]
विष्णोः पालनशक्तिः सा कर्तृशक्तिः पितुर्मम ।
रुद्रस्य नाशशक्तिः सा त्वन्याशक्तिः परा शिवा ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation:
That Supreme Auspicious Goddess is the preserving energy of Viṣṇu, is the creative power of Brahmā, and is the destroying force of Śiva.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 31]
यच्च किञ्चित्क्वचिद्वस्तु सदसद्भुवनत्रये ।
तस्य सर्वस्य या शक्तिस्तदुत्पत्तिः कुतो भवेत् ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation:
Whatever there exists in this infinite Universe, whether Temporal or Eternal, She is the Underlying Force of all; how, then, can She have an origin!
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 32-33]
न ब्रह्मा न यदा विष्णुर्न रुद्रो न दिवाकरः ।
न चेन्द्राद्याः सुराः सर्वे न धरा न धराधराः ॥ ३२ ॥

तदा सा प्रकृतिः पूर्णा पुरुषेण परेण वै ।
संयुता विहरत्येव युगादौ निर्गुणा शिवा ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation:
Her origin is not Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśa, Sun, Indra, or the other Devas, not this Earth nor this Upholder of the Earth; She is devoid of any qualities, the Giver of Salvation of all, the Full Prakriti. In the time of the final dissolution of this Universe, She lives with the Supreme Puruṣa.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 34]
सा भूत्वा सगुणा पश्चात्करोति भुवनत्रयम् ।
पूर्वं संसृज्य ब्रह्मादीन्दत्त्वा शक्तीश्च सर्वशः ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation:
She is also Saguṇa, full of qualities, and is the Creatrix of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśa, and has empowered them, in every way, to create the three Lokas.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 35]
तां ज्ञात्वा मुच्यते जन्तुर्जन्मसंसारबन्धनात् ।
सा विद्या परमा ज्ञेया वेदाद्या वेदकारिणी ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation:
She is the Supreme Knowledge, existing before the Vedas, and the Originator of the Vedas. The individual souls, knowing Her Nature, become able to free themselves from the bondages of the world.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 36]
असङ्ख्यातानि नामानि तस्या ब्रह्मादिभिः किल ।
गुणकर्मविधानैस्तु कल्पितानि च किं ब्रुवे ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation:
She is known by endless names. The Brahmā, and the other Devas might choose to call Her according to their actions and qualities. I am unable to describe those names.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 37]
अकारादिक्षकारान्तैः स्वरैर्वर्णैस्तु योजितैः ।
असङ्ख्येयानि नामानि भवन्ति रघुनन्दन ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation:
O descendant in the race of Raghu! Her endless names are formed by the various combinations of the various vowels and consonants from the letter ‘A’ to the letter ‘Kṣa.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 38]
राम उवाच ।
विधिं मे ब्रूहि विप्रर्षे व्रतस्यास्य समासतः ।
करोम्यद्यैव श्रद्धावाञ्छ्रीदेव्याः पूजनं तथा ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation:
Rāma said :-- “O best of the Munis! Describe briefly all the rules and regulations as to how that vow and worship are to be performed. With my heart, full of devotion and faith, I will worship the Goddess today.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 39]
नारद उवाच ।
पीठं कृत्वा समे स्थाने संस्थाप्य जगदम्बिकाम् ।
उपवासान्नवैव त्वं कुरु राम विधानतः ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation:
Nārada said :-- “O Rāghava! On a level plot of ground, prepare an altar. Place the Goddess there and fast for nine days.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 40]
आचार्योऽहं भविष्यामि कर्मण्यस्मिन्महीपते ।
देवकार्यविधानार्थमुत्साहं प्रकरोम्यहम् ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation:
O king! I will be your priest and I will, with great energy, carry out this yajña to fullfil the work of the Gods.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 41-42]
व्यास उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं सत्यं मत्वा रामः प्रतापवान् ।
कारयित्वा शुभं पीठं स्थापयित्वाम्बिकां शिवाम् ॥ ४१ ॥

विधिवत्पूजनं तस्याश्चकार व्रतवान् हरिः ।
सम्प्राप्ते चाश्विने मासि तस्मिन्गिरिवरे तदा ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- Then the powerful Bhagavān Hari, hearing all from the Muni, believed them to be true; and, on the approach of the month of Āśvin, prepared the altar on the top of a hill and placed the Auspicious Goddess, the World Mother and, observing all the rules, performed the vow and worshipped the Goddess.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 43-46]
उपवासपरो रामः कृतवान्व्रतमुत्तमम् ।
होमञ्च विधिवत्तत्र बलिदानञ्च पूजनम् ॥ ४३ ॥

भ्रातरौ चक्रतुः प्रेम्णा व्रतं नारदसम्मतम् ।
अष्टम्यां मध्यरात्रे तु देवी भगवती हि सा ॥ ४४ ॥

सिंहारूढा ददौ तत्र दर्शनं प्रतिपूजिता ।
गिरिशृङ्गे स्थितोवाच राघवं सानुजं गिरा ॥ ४५ ॥

मेघगम्भीरया चेदं भक्तिभावेन तोषिता ।
देव्युवाच ।
राम राम महाबाहो तुष्टाऽस्म्यद्म व्रतेन ते ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation:
When, on the grand night of the Eighth lunar day, the two brothers completed the vow as told by Nārada, the Supreme Bhagavatī was pleased with the worship and appeared before them, mounted on a lion, and remaining there on the mountain top, addressed Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, in a sweet grave tone, like the rumbling of a rain cloud, thus :-- “Rāma, I am satisfied with your worship; ask from me what you desire.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 47]
प्रार्थयस्व वरं कामं यत्ते मनसि वर्तते ।
नारायणांशसम्भूतस्त्वं वंशे मानवेऽनघे ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation:
Rāma! You are sent by the gods for the destruction of Rāvaṇa and are born as a part incarnate of Nārāyaṇa, in the pure and stainless family of Manu.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 48]
रावणस्य वधायैव प्रार्थितस्त्वमरैरसि ।
पुरा मत्स्यतनुं कृत्वा हत्वा घोरञ्च राक्षसम् ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation:
It is You that, in ancient times, incarnated as a fish for serving the purpose of the Devas and preserved the Vedas by killing the terrible Rākṣasas for the welfare of the Universe.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 49]
त्वया वै रक्षिता वेदाः सुराणां हितमिच्छता ।
भूत्वा कच्छपरूपस्तु धृतवान्मन्दरं गिरिम् ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation:
It is You that incarnated as a tortoise and held aloft the Mandara mountain, churned the ocean and nourished the Devas.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 50-51]
अकूपारं प्रमन्थानं कृत्वा देवानपोषयः ।
कोलरूपं परं कृत्वा दशनाग्रेण मेदिनीम् ॥ ५० ॥

धृतवानसि यद्राम हिरण्याक्षं जघान च ।
नारसिंहीं तनुं कृत्वा हिरण्यकशिपुं पुरा ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation:
O Rāma! It is You that incarnated, in days of yore, as a boar and held aloft on your teeth this earth. It is You that assumed the form of a Man-Lion and preserved Prahlāda, by tearing asunder the body of Hiraṇya Kaśipu, by Your sharp nails.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 52]
प्रह्लादं राम रक्षित्वा हतवानसि राघव ।
वामनं वपुरास्थाय पुरा छलितवान्बलिम् ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation:
O Descent of Raghu! It is You that assumed, in ancient times, the form of a dwarf and served the purpose of the Devas, by deceitfully cheating Balī, the younger of Indra.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 53]
भूत्वेन्द्रस्यानुजः कामं देवकार्यप्रसाधकः ।
जमदग्निसुतस्त्वं मे विष्णोरंशेन सङ्गतः ॥ ५३ ॥
English Translation:
O son of Kauśalya! You incarnated as the son of Jamadagni in the Brāhmin family, extirpated the line of Kṣattrīya kings and gave over this whole earth to Bhagavān Kaśyapa Ṛṣi.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 54]
कृत्वान्तं क्षत्रियाणां तु दानं भूमेरदाद्द्विजे ।
तथेदानीं तु काकुत्स्थ जातो दशरथात्मज ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation:
So You are now born as the son of Daśaratha, in the stainless race of Kākutstha, at the request of the Devas, harassed by Rāvaṇa.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 55-56]
प्रार्थितस्तु सुरैः सर्वै रावणेनातिपीडितैः ।
कपयस्ते सहाया वै देवांशा बलवत्तराः ॥ ५५ ॥

भविष्यन्ति नरव्याघ्र मच्छक्तिसंयुता ह्यमी ।
शेषांशोऽप्यनुजस्तेऽयं रावणात्मजनाशकः ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation:
These powerful monkeys, born as Deva incarnates, all endowed with great power by Me, will help you. Your younger Lakṣmaṇa is the incarnate of Śeṣa serpent; this indomitable man will kill undoubtedly Indragit, the son of Rāvaṇa.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 57]
भविष्यति न सन्देहः कर्तव्योऽत्र त्वयाऽनघ ।
वसन्ते सेवनं कार्यं त्वया तत्रातिश्रद्धया ॥ ५७ ॥
English Translation:
You will kill Rāvaṇa; then you would worship Me, with great devotion, in the vernal season and then enjoy your kingdom according to your liking.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 58]
हत्वाऽथ रावणं पापं कुरु राज्यं यथासुखम् ।
एकादश सहस्राणि वर्षाणि पृथिवीतले ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation:
O best of the Raghus! For full eleven thousand years you will reign on this earth; and after that reenter your heavenly abode.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 59-60]
कृत्वा राज्यं रघुश्रेष्ठ गन्ताऽसि त्रिदिवं पुनः ।
व्यास उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वान्तर्दधे देवी रामस्तु प्रीतमानसः ॥ ५९ ॥

समाप्य तद्व्रतं चक्रे प्रयाणं दशमीदिने ।
विजयापूजनं कृत्वा दत्त्वा दानान्यनेकशः ॥ ६० ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O king! Thus saying, the Devī disappeared. Rāma Candra became very glad and, completing that most auspicious ceremony, performed the Bejoyā Pūjā on the tenth day and gave lots of presents to Nārada and made him go towards the ocean.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 61]
कपिपतिबलयुक्तः सानुजः श्रीपतिश्च
प्रकटपरमशक्त्या प्रेरितः पूर्णकामः ।
उदधितटगतोऽसौ सेतुबन्धं विधाया-
प्यहनदमरशत्रुं रावणं गीतकीर्तिः ॥ ६१ ॥
English Translation:
O king! Thus stimulated by the Supreme Energy, the Highest Goddess brought front to front, Rāmacandra, the husband of Kamalā, went to the shores of the ocean, accompanied by Lakṣmaṇa and the monkeys. Then he erected the bridge across the ocean and killed Rāvaṇa, the enemy of the gods. His unparalleled fame spread everywhere throughout the three Lokas.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 62]
यः शृणोति नरो भक्त्या देव्याश्चरितमुत्तमम् ।
स भुक्त्वा विपुलान्भोगान्प्राप्नोति परमं पदम् ॥ ६२ ॥
English Translation:
He who hears with devotion this excellent account of the Devī, will get the greatest happiness in this world, and, in the end, will get the final beatitude. There is no doubt in this.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 30 Verse 63-64]
सन्त्यन्यानि पुराणानि विस्तराणि बहूनि च ।
श्रीमद्भागवतस्यास्य न तुल्यानीति मे मतिः ॥ ६३ ॥

इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां
तृतीयस्कन्धे रामाय देवीवरदानं नाम त्रिंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ३.३०॥
English Translation:
O king! There are extant many other Purāṇas, but none is equal to this Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam. Know, this is my firm belief. Here ends the thirtieth chapter on the narration of the Navarātra ceremony by Nārada and the performance of that by Rāma Candra in the 3rd Adhyāya in Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa. Here ends the Third Book. ***

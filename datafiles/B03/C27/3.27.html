Scriptures

Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam

BOOK 3 • CHAPTER 27
On the virgins fit to be worshipped and the Glory of the Devī
← Previous Chapter ☰ Index Next Chapter →
Ch 27 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
३.२७ सप्तविंशोऽध्यायः । देवीपूजामहत्त्ववर्णनम् । व्यास उवाच । हीनाङ्गीं वर्जयेत्कन्यां कुष्ठयुक्तां व्रणाङ्किताम् । गन्धस्फुरितहीनाङ्गीं विशालकुलसम्भवाम् ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O king! Those Kumārīs, who are defective in limbs, who are lepers, who are filled with sores and ulcers over their bodies, whose bodies emit offensive smell or whose bodies are polluted, or those who are of a bad family are never to be accepted for worship in the Navarātra ceremony festival.
Ch 27 Verse 2-3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जात्यन्धां केकरां काणां कुरूपां बहुरोमशाम् । सन्त्यजेद्रोगिणीं कन्यां रक्तपुष्पादिनाङ्किताम् ॥ २ ॥ क्षामां गर्भसमुद्भूतां गोलकां कन्यकोद्भवाम् । वर्जनीयाः सदा चैताः सर्वपूजादिकर्मसु ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
Those who are born blind, who are squint-eyed, who are blind of one eye, of disgraceful appearance, whose bodies are overgrown with hairs, or who are diseased or who are in their menstruation or in any other signs, indicating thus their passionate youthful tendencies, or those who are very lean and thin, or born of widows, or of women unmarried are always to be avoided in this Pūjā.
Ch 27 Verse 4 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अरोगिणीं सुरूपाङ्गीं सुन्दरीं व्रणवर्जिताम् । एकवंशसमुद्भूतां कन्यां सम्यक्प्रपूजयेत् ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation
O king! It is only the healthy, graceful, beautiful, without any ulcers, and who are not bastards, those virgins are to be selected for the Kumārī Pūjā.
Ch 27 Verse 5 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ब्राह्मणी सर्वकार्येषु जयार्थे नृपवंशजा । लाभार्थे वैश्यवंशोत्था मता वा शूद्रवंशजा ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation
In all the cases, the Kumārīs, born of the Brāhmin families, can be taken; when victory is desired, the Kumārīs of the Kṣattriya families are preferred; when profit is wanted, the Vaiśya Kumārīs and, when general welfare is wanted, the Śūdra Kumārīs are to be taken.
Ch 27 Verse 6-7 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ब्राह्मणैर्ब्रह्मजाः पूज्या राजन्यैर्ब्रह्मवंशजाः । वैश्यैस्त्रिवर्गजाः पूज्याश्चतस्रः पादसम्भवैः ॥ ६ ॥ कारुभिश्चैव वंशोत्था यथायोग्यं प्रपूजयेत् । नवरात्रविधानेन भक्तिपूर्वं सदैव हि ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
O king! In the Navarātri Pūjā, the Brāhmins should select for worship the Brāhmin Kumārīs; Kṣattriyas, Brāhmin or Kṣattriya; the Vaiśya worshippers can select for worship Brāhmin, Kṣattriya, or Vaiśya Kumārīs. And the Śūdra worshippers can select, for worship, any of the four classes. But artists and artisans should select for worship the Kumārīs from their own families and tribes respectively.
Ch 27 Verse 8 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अशक्तो नियतं पूजां कर्तुञ्चेन्नवरात्रके । अष्टम्यां च विशेषेण कर्तव्यं पूजनं सदा ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
If persons become unable to worship on all the days, then it is advised that they should perform the special worship on the eighth day (Astamī tithi).
Ch 27 Verse 9-10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पुराऽष्टम्यां भद्रकाली दक्षयज्ञविनाशिनी । प्रादुर्भूता महाघोरा योगिनी कोटिभिः सह ॥ ९ ॥ अतोऽष्टम्यां विशेषेण कर्तव्यं पूजनं सदा । नानाविधोपहारैश्च गन्धमाल्यानुलेपनैः ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
In ancient times, on the eighth day, Bhadra Kāli Goddess, the destroyer of the sacrifice, started by Dakṣa, appeared on that day in hideous forms, surrounded by hundreds and lakhs of Yoginīs (one of a class of sixty goddesses or female attendants on Kāli). Therefore one should worship in particular on the eighth day with scents, garlands, and pastes and various offerings.
Ch 27 Verse 11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पायसैरामिषैर्होमैर्ब्राह्मणानां च भोजनैः । फलपुष्पोपहारैश्च तोषयेज्जगदम्बिकाम् ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
On this day, Pāyasa (a food prepared of rice, milk and sugar), and fresh fish are to be specially offered to the Deity. The Homa ceremonies, feasting of the Brāhmaṇs, and the worship of the Mother Goddess are done with various offerings, the fruits and flowers, and in good quantities.
Ch 27 Verse 12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
उपवासे ह्यशक्तानां नवरात्रव्रते पुनः । उपोषणत्रयं प्रोक्तं यथोक्तफलदं नृप ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
O king! Those who are unable to observe the fasting in this Navarātra Pūjā, will reap the same fruits, if they observe fasting for the three days only the Saptamī, the Aṣṭamī, and the Navamī tithis.
Ch 27 Verse 13 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सप्तम्यां च तथाऽष्टम्यां नवम्यां भक्तिभावतः । त्रिरात्रकरणात्सर्वं फलं भवति पूजनात् ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation
On the seventh, eighth, and ninth days, in these three tithis (lunar days) if one worships with devotion, one will acquire all the merits.
Ch 27 Verse 14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पूजाभिश्चैव होमैश्च कुमारिपूजनैस्तथा । सम्पूर्णं तद्व्रतं प्रोक्तं विप्राणां चैव भोजनैः ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
When the Devī’s worship, Homa, Kumārī worship and the feasting of the Brāhmaṇas, all these are done, know that the Navarātri Pūjā is completed.
Ch 27 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्रतानि यानि चान्यानि दानानि विविधानि च । नवरात्रव्रतस्यास्य नैव तुल्यानि भूतले ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
O Janamejaya! No worship or vow or charitable gifts extant in this world, can be compared, as regards their meritorious effects, with this Navarātra Pūjā.
Ch 27 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
धनधान्यप्रदं नित्यं सुखसन्तानवृद्धिदम् । आयुरारोग्यदं चैव स्वर्गदं मोक्षदं तथा ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
On observing this Navarātram Vrata, one gets riches, crops, sons and grandsons, prosperity and happiness, longevity, health and heaven and even the final beatitude.
Ch 27 Verse 17 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विद्यार्थी वा धनार्थी वा पुत्रार्थी वा भवेन्नरः । तेनेदं विधिवत्कार्यं व्रतं सौभाग्यदं शिवम् ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation
Those who are desirous of learning, riches, or sons will get them all if they perform this most auspicious Navarātra ceremony, able to confer fortunes on the devotees.
Ch 27 Verse 18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विद्यार्थी सर्वविद्यां वै प्राप्नोति व्रतसाधनात् । राजभ्रष्टो नृपो राज्यं समवाप्नोति सर्वदा ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
On the performance of this sacrifice, those who want learning get all the learning; and he, who is deprived of his kingdom will get back all his kingdoms.
Ch 27 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पूर्वजन्मनि यैर्नूनं न कृतं व्रतमुत्तमम् । ते व्याधिनो दरिद्राश्च भवन्ति पुत्रवर्जिताः ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
Those who did not, in their previous births, perform this meritorious vow, they become diseased, poor and devoid of sons in their present births.
Ch 27 Verse 20 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वन्ध्या च या भवेन्नारी विधवा धनवर्जिता । अनुमा तत्र कर्तव्या नेयं कृतवती व्रतम् ॥ २० ॥
English Translation
Those women that are barren, or widows or devoid of sons, infer that they never, in their previous births, performed this sacrifice.
Ch 27 Verse 21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नवरात्रव्रतं प्रोक्तं न कृतं येन भूतले । स कथं विभवं प्राप्य मोदते च तथा दिवि ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
Those who have not performed the Navarātra ceremony, how can they acquire riches in this world and acquire happiness and peace in the next?
Ch 27 Verse 22 Verse
Sanskrit Text
रक्तचन्दनसम्मिश्रैः कोमलैर्बिल्वपत्रकैः । भवानी पूजिता येन स भवेन्नृपतिः क्षितौ ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation
He who has worshipped the Goddess Bhagavatī Bhavānī Devī with young leaves of the Bel tree, besmeared with red sandal paste, it is he that will undoubtedly become the king in this world.
Ch 27 Verse 23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नाराधिता येन शिवा सनातनी दुःखार्तिहा सिद्धिकरी जगद्वरा । दुःखावृतः शत्रुयुतश्च भूतले नूनं दरिद्रो भवतीह मानवः ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
That man who has failed to worship the Goddess of the whole universe, Who fructifies all the pursuits of human life, Who destroys all the troubles, pains and miseries, Who is all suspicious Bhagavatī Bhavānī, that fellow is sure to pass his days in this world, wretched, impoverished, and surrounded by his enemies on all sides.
Ch 27 Verse 24 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यां विष्णुरिन्द्रो हरपद्मजौ तथा वह्निः कुबेरो वरुणो दिवाकरः । ध्यायन्ति सर्वार्थसमाप्तिनन्दिता- स्तां किं मनुष्या न भजन्ति चण्डिकाम् ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation
When Hari, Hara, Brahmā, Indra, Fire, Varuṇa, Kuvera, and the Sun when all these possessing all the wealth and powers and filled with the highest felicities, when they meditate constantly the Goddess of the universe, Who is All Existence Intelligence, and Bliss, then what to speak of the human beings! How is it that persons do not worship that Candikā Devī, the One that leads all human pursuits to success!
Ch 27 Verse 25-26 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्वाहा स्वधा नाम मनुप्रभावै- स्तृप्यन्ति देवाः पितरस्तथैव । यज्ञेषु सर्वेषु मुदा हरन्ति यन्नाम युग्मश्रुतिभिर्मुनीन्द्राः ॥ २५ ॥ यस्येच्छया सृजति विश्वमिदं प्रजेशो नानावतारकलनं कुरुते हरिश्च । नूनं करोति जगतः किल भस्म शम्भु- स्तां शर्मदां न भजते नु कथं मनुष्यः ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation
Why should not the people worship the Goddess Bhavānī, the bestower of all happiness, whose other names are Svahā and Svadhā, the mantrams under whose intrinsic energies the Devas and the Pitris always get satisfied, and which are recited by all the Munis when they chant in every sacrifice the Vedic mantrams? Under Whose Will power Brahmā the Creator, creates all this Universe? Under Whose energy, the Viṣṇu Janāradan, the Deva of the Devas, incarnates in this earth in various forms and preserves this world, and under Whose power, Śaṅkara destroys this whole Universe?
Ch 27 Verse 27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नैकोऽस्ति सर्वभुवनेषु तया विहीनो देवो नरोऽथ विहगः किल पन्नगो वा । गन्धर्वराक्षसपिशाचनगेषु नूनं यः स्पन्दितुं भवति शक्तियुतो यथेच्छम् ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
No body, in this whole universe, can have his existence without having recourse to that Prakriti Devī, the Śakti incarnate; be he a Devī, a human being or a bird, or a serpent, Gandharva, Rāksasa, without the help of this Force.
Ch 27 Verse 28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तां न सेवेत कश्चण्डीं सर्वकामार्थदां शिवाम् । व्रतं तस्या न कः कुर्याद्वाञ्छन्नर्थचतुष्टयम् ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
Therefore, why should not anybody worship that Candikā Devī, the Awarder of all desires and wealth? And how is it, that a man desiring one of the 4 objects of human pursuits, Dharma, wealth, desires, and the final beatitude, observes not the vow regarding that Deity.
Ch 27 Verse 29 Verse
Sanskrit Text
महापातकसंयुक्तो नवरात्रव्रतं चरेत् । मुच्यते सर्वपापेभ्यो नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation
So much so, that even a man who has committed a heinous offence, five such are enumerated, viz. (1) killing a Brāhmaṇ, (2) drinking liquor, (3) stealing gold, (4) adultery with the wife of a spiritual guide (5) associating with any such person, if he performs the Navarātra vow, he will be absolved entirely from all such sins; there is no doubt in this.
Ch 27 Verse 30 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पुरा कश्चिद्वणिग्दीनो धनहीनः सुदुःखितः । कुटुम्बी चाभवत्कश्चित् कोसले नृपसत्तम ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation
O king! Once on a time there lived in the country of Kosala, a trader, poor and miserable, having under him many relations and dependants in his family, whose provisions he had to provide.
Ch 27 Verse 31 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अपत्यानि बहून्यस्याभवन्क्षुत्पीडितानि च । भक्ष्यं किञ्चित्तु सायाह्ने प्रापुस्तस्य च बालकाः ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation
He had many sons and daughters; when they were very hungry and distressed, then they used to get a little food and that in the evening, only once in twenty-four hours.
Ch 27 Verse 32 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भुङ्क्ते स्म कार्यकर्ताऽसौ परस्याथ बुभुक्षितः । कुटुम्बभरणं तत्र चकारातिनिराकुलः ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation
That trader, too, worked under another, the whole day; and when it was evening, he used also to take his meals. Thus, being very much anxious and distressed, he maintained somehow or other his family members (that are to be maintained).
Ch 27 Verse 33-34 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सदा धर्मरतः शान्तः सदाचारश्च सत्यवाक् । अक्रोधनश्च धृतिमान्निर्मदश्चानसूयकः ॥ ३३ ॥ सम्पूज्य देवता नित्यं पितॄनप्यतिथींस्तथा । भुञ्जाने पोष्यवर्गेऽथ कृतवान्भोजनं वणिक् ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation
This trader was of a quiet temper, of a good conduct, truthful, always ready to act religiously, devoid of anger, steady and contented, void of vanity and jealousy; daily he used to worship the Devas, Pitris, and the guests and used to take his meals after all his family members had taken their meals.
Ch 27 Verse 35-36 Verse
Sanskrit Text
एवं गच्छति काले वै सुशिलो नामतो गुणैः । दारिद्र्यार्तो द्विजं शान्तं पप्रच्छातिबुभुक्षितः ॥ ३५ ॥ सुशील उवाच । भो भूदेव कृपां कृत्वा वदस्वाद्य महामते । कथं दारिद्र्यनाशः स्यादिति मे निश्चयेन वै ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation
Thus many days passed away when that good trader, named Suśīla, being very much perplexed with poverty and hunger, asked a quiet tempered Brāhmin “O Bhūdeva! (deva incarnate on the earth) kindly tell me positively how this state of poverty can be got rid off!
Ch 27 Verse 37 Verse
Sanskrit Text
धनैषणा मे नैवास्ति धनी स्यामिति मानद । कुटुम्बभरणार्थं वै पृच्छामि त्वां द्विजोत्तम ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation
O holy minded! Kindly advise me such as preserves my honour; I do not want wealth, nor do I like to be a rich man; O Brāhmin! I want just enough to meet with the expenses, iñcurred in maintaining my family; please advise so that I may be able to earn this much only.
Ch 27 Verse 38 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पुत्री सुतस्तु मे बालो भक्षार्थी रोदते भृशम् । तावन्मात्रं गृहे नान्नं मुष्टिमेकां ददाम्यहम् ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation
I have many sons; I have not got any food, sufficient enough to give them even a handful of rice.
Ch 27 Verse 39 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विसर्जितो यतो गेहाद्गतो बालो रुदन्मया । अतो मे दह्यतेऽत्यर्थं किं करोमि धनं विना ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation
Alas! My youngest son was crying today for food; I have driven him out of the house by chastising him. O Brāhmin! What am I do? I have got no wealth; my heart is burning with grief and sorrow; my baby has gone out of the house, weeping and hungry.
Ch 27 Verse 40 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विवाहोऽस्ति सुताया मे नास्ति वित्तं करोमि किम् । दशवर्षाधिकायास्तु दानकालोऽपि यात्यलम् ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation
My daughter has come to a marriageable age; I have no money. Her age has exceeded ten years; the marriageable age limit has been exceeded. Alas! What am I to do?
Ch 27 Verse 41-42 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तेन शोचामि विप्रेन्द्र सर्वज्ञोऽसि दयानिधे । तपो दानं व्रतं किञ्चिद्ब्रूहि मन्त्रजपं तथा ॥ ४१ ॥ येनाहं पोष्यवर्गस्य करोमि द्विज पोषणम् । तावन्मे स्याद्धनप्राप्तिर्नाधिकं प्रार्थये किल ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation
O Brāhmin! I am expressing my sorrow for all that. You are merciful, and all-knowing; tell me any means, be it asceticism, gifts, vow, or the reciting of any mantrams by which I can maintain my family; I want wealth just sufficient for that purpose and nothing more.
Ch 27 Verse 43 Verse
Sanskrit Text
त्वत्प्रसादात्कुटुम्बं मे सुखितं प्रभवेदिह । तत्कुरुष्व महाभाग ज्ञानेन परिचिन्त्य च ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation
O high minded one! Kindly devise and tell me some means by which my family members become happy in this world.”
Ch 27 Verse 44-46 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । इति पृष्टस्तथा तेन ब्राह्मणः संशितव्रतः । उवाच परमप्रीतस्तं वैश्यं नृपसत्तम ॥ ४४ ॥ वैश्यवर्य कुरुष्वाद्य नवरात्रव्रतं शुभम् । पूजनं भगवत्याश्च हवनं भोजनं तथा ॥ ४५ ॥ वेदपारायणं शक्तिजपहोमादिकं तथा । कुरुष्वाद्य यथाशक्ति तव कार्यं भविष्यति ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- The Brāhmin that used to practice vows when thus asked by the trader told him gladly “O trader! Do now the Navarātri vow, the most auspicious, and worship the Bhagavatī, perform Homa, and feast the Brāhmins. Have the Vedas and Purāṇas recited and recite then slowly the Śakti mantram and try, as much as you can, to do other concomitant ceremonies; and your desires will thus be undoubtedly fulfilled.

Sanskrit Text
एतस्मादपरं किञ्चिद्व्रतं नास्ति धरातले । नवरात्राभिधं वैश्य पावनं सुखदं तथा ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation
There is no other vow superior to this in this world; this vow is very holy and will bring unto you happiness.

English Translation
This vow leads to wisdom and liberation; destroys enemies and increases posterity and prosperity.
Ch 27 Verse 49 Verse
Sanskrit Text
राज्यभ्रष्टेन रामेण सीताविरहितेन च । किष्किन्धायां व्रतं चैतत्कृतं दुःखातुरेण वै ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation
In former days, Śrī Rāma Candra suffered very much owing to his being deprived of his kingdom; and, then on account of his wife being stolen away. Subsequently he performed this Navarātra vow in Kiskindhyā, his heart being heavily laden with grief.
Ch 27 Verse 50 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Though troubled very much, on account of the bereavement of Sītā, still Rāma Candra observed the Vow of Navarātra and worshipped the Goddess according to the prescribed rules and rites.
Ch 27 Verse 51-52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तेन प्राप्ताऽथ वैदेही कृत्वा सेतुं महार्णवे । हत्वा मन्दोदरीनाथं कुम्भकर्णं महाबलम् ॥ ५१ ॥ मेघनादं सुतं हत्वा कृत्वा भूपं बिभीषणम् । पश्चादयोध्यामागत्य प्राप्तं राज्यमकण्टकम् ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation
As a fruit of this worship he was able to bridge the great ocean and kill the giant Kumbha Karṇa, Meghanāda, the Rāvaṇa’s son, and Rāvaṇa, the king of Lanka; and subsequently he was able to recover his Sītā. He installed Vibhīṣaṇa on the throne of Lanka (Ceylon) and at last returned to Ayodhyā and reigned there without any enemies.

English Translation
O best of the Vaiśyas! Rāma Candra, of incomparable prowess, was able to obtain happiness in this world on account of the influence of this Navarātra ceremony.

Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । इति विप्रवचः श्रुत्वा स वैश्यस्तं द्विजं गुरुम् । कृत्वा जग्राह सन्मन्त्रं मायाबीजाभिधं नृप ॥ ५४ ॥ जजाप परया भक्त्या नवरात्रमतन्द्रितः । नानाविधोपहारैश्च पूजयामास सादरम् ॥ ५५ ॥ नवसंवत्सरं चैव मायाबीजपरायणः । नवमे वत्सरान्ते तु महाष्टम्यां महेश्वरी ॥ ५६ ॥ अर्धरात्रे तु सञ्जाते प्रत्यक्षं दर्शनं ददौ । नानावरप्रदानैश्च कृतकृत्यञ्चकार तम् ॥ ५७ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां तृतीयस्कन्धे देवीपूजामहत्त्ववर्णनं नाम सप्तविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ३.२७॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O king! That Vaiśya, hearing thus the Brāhmin’s words, made him his Guru, was initiated by him in the seed mantra of Māyā and ceaselessly, without any laziness, recited slowly the mantram for nine nights and worshipped the Devī, with great caution and with various offerings. Thus for nine consecutive years he devoted himself to the Japam (reciting slowly) of the seed mantra of Māyā till, at last, when the ninth year was completed, the Great Goddess appeared distinctly before his eyes on the night of the great Astamī tithi (the eighth day of the bright half) and gave him various boons and delivered the Vaiśya from poverty and bestowed on him wealth and his other desired things. Here ends the 27th Chapter on the virgins fit to be worshipped and the Glory of the Devī in the Mahā Purāṇam Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa in the Third Book.

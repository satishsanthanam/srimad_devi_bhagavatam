[Ch 6 Verse 1]
२.६ षष्ठोऽध्यायः । युधिष्ठिरादीनामुत्पत्तिवर्णनम् ।


सूत उवाच ।
एवं सत्यवती तेन वृता शन्तनुना किल ।
द्वौ पुत्रौ च तया जातौ मृतौ कालवशादपि ॥ १ ॥
English Translation:
Sūta said :—Thus Śantanu married Satyavatī; two sons were born to her and they died in course of time.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 2]
व्यासवीर्यात्तु सञ्जातो धृतराष्ट्रोऽन्ध एव च ।
मुनिं दृष्ट्वाथ कामिन्या नेत्रसम्मीलने कृते ॥ २ ॥
English Translation:
Out of Vyāsa Deva’s semen, Dhritarāstra was born. Ambikā Devī, the mother of Dhritarāstra closed her eyes on seeing Veda Vyāsa; hence Dhṛtaraṣstra was born blind.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 3]
श्वेतरूपा यतो जाता दृष्ट्वा व्यासं नृपात्मजा ।
व्यासकोपात्समुत्पन्नः पाण्डुस्तेन न संशयः ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation:
Seeing Dhritarāstra blind Satyavatī asked Vyāsa to go to Ambālikā (Pānḍu’s mother); the princess Ambālikā, mother of Pānḍu turned pale at the sight of Vyāsa; hence her son became of a pale colour out of Vyāsa’s wrath. Hence the name of the son was Pānḍu.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 4]
सन्तोषितस्तया व्यासो दास्या कामकलाविदा ।
विदुरस्तु समुत्पन्नो धर्मांशः सत्यवाक् शुचिः ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation:
Next the maid servant, expert in the science of amorous pleasures, satisfied Vyāsa; hence her son Vidura was born of Dharma’s part and became truthful and holy.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 5]
राज्ये संस्थापितः पाण्डुः कनीयानपि मन्त्रिभिः ।
अन्धत्वाद्धृतराष्ट्रोऽसौ नाधिकारे नियोजितः ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation:
Though Pānḍu was younger, the ministers installed him on the throne. Dhritarāstra could not become king, as he was blind.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 6]
भीष्मस्यानुमते राज्यं प्राप्तः पाण्डुर्महाबलः ।
विदुरोऽप्यथ मेधावी मन्त्रकार्ये नियोजितः ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation:
By the permission of Bhīsma the powerful Pānḍu obtained the sovereignty; and the intelligent Vidura became his minister.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 7]
धृतराष्ट्रस्य द्वे भार्ये गान्धारी सौबली स्मृता ।
द्वितीया च तथा वैश्या गार्हस्त्येषु प्रतिष्ठिता ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation:
Dhritarāstra had two wives Gāndhāri and Sauvali; this Sauvali was Vaishyā; she was engaged in the household affairs.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 8]
पाण्डोरपि तथा पत्न्यौ द्वे प्रोक्ते वेदवादिभिः ।
शौरसेनी तथा कुन्ती माद्री च मद्रदेशजा ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation:
king Pānḍu had two wives, too; the first was Kunti, the daughter of Sūrasena; and the other was Mādri, the daughter of the Madra king.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 9]
गान्धारी सुषुवे पुत्रशतं परमशोभनम् ।
वैश्याप्येकं सुतं कान्तं युयुत्सुं सुषुवे प्रियम् ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation:
Gāndhāri gave birth to one hundred beautiful sons; Vaiśyā Sauvali gave birth to one beautiful son named Yuyutsu.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 10]
कुन्ती तु प्रथमं कन्या सूर्यात्कर्णं मनोहरम् ।
सुषुवे पितृगेहस्था पश्चात्पाण्डुपरिग्रहः ॥ १० ॥
English Translation:
While Kunti was a virgin, she gave birth, through the medium of the Sun, the lovely Karna; next he became the wife of Pānḍu.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 11-12]
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
किमेतत्सूत चित्रं त्वं भाषसे मुनिसत्तम ।
जनितश्च सुतः पूर्वं पाण्डुना सा विवाहिता ॥ ११ ॥

सूर्यात्कर्णः कथं जातः कन्यायां वद विस्तरात् ।
कन्या कथं पुनर्जाता पाण्डुना सा विवाहिता ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation:
Hearing this, the Ṛṣis said :-- “O Muni Sūta! What are you saying ? First Kunti brought forth a child and afterwards she was married to Pānḍu; this is wonderful, indeed! How was Karna, born of Kunti, unmarried? and how came Kunti to be married afterwards? describe all these in detail.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 13-14]
सूत उवाच ।
शूरसेनसुता कुन्ती बालभावे यदा द्विजाः ।
कुन्तिभोजेन राज्ञा तु प्रार्थिता कन्यका शुभा ॥ १३ ॥

कुन्तिभोजेन सा बाला पुत्री तु परिकल्पिता ।
सेवनार्थं तु दीप्तस्य विहिता चारुहासिनी ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation:
Sūta then said :-- “O Dvija! While Sūrasena’s daughter Kunti was a virgin girl, the king Kuntibhoja asked for Kunti that she might become her girl and Sūrasena gave her to the king Kuntibhoja who brought up this beautifully smiling girl. He put to her the service of Agni of Agnihotra.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 15-16]
दुर्वासास्तु मुनिः प्राप्तश्चातुर्मास्ये स्थितो द्विजः ।
परिचर्या कृता कुन्त्या मुनिस्तोषं जगाम ह ॥ १५ ॥

ददौ मन्त्रं शुभं तस्यै येनाहूतः सुरः स्वयम् ।
समायाति तथा कामं पूरयिष्यति वाञ्छितम् ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation:
Once, on an occassion, Durvāsā Muni, engaged in the vow, lasting for four months, came there; Kunti served him during that period; the Muni became greatly pleased and gave her a very auspicious, mantra, by virtue of which any Deva, when called upon by that mantra will come to Kunti and satisfy her desires.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 17]
गते मुनौ ततः कुन्ती निश्चयार्थं गृहे स्थिता ।
चिन्तयामास मनसा कं सुरं समचिन्तये ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation:
When the Muni went away, Kunti, remaining in her house, wanted to test the accuracy of the mantra and asked within herself “What Devatā to call upon.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 18]
उदितश्च तदा भानुस्तया दृष्टो दिवाकरः ।
मन्त्रोच्चारं तया कृत्वा चाहूतस्तिग्मगुस्तदा ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation:
Seeing the God Sūrya had arisen in the sky, Kunti uttered the Mantra and invoked him. The
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 19]
मण्डलान्मानुषं रूपं कृत्वा सर्वातिपेशलम् ।
अवातरत्तदाकाशात्समीपे तत्र मन्दिरे ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation:
The Sun, then, assuming an excellent human form, came down from the Heavens and appeared before Kunti in the same room.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 20]
दृष्ट्वा देवं समायान्तं कुन्ती भानुं सुविस्मिता ।
वेपमाना रजोदोषं प्राप्ता सद्यस्तु भामिनी ॥ २० ॥
English Translation:
Seeing the Deva Sun, Kunti became greatly surprised and began to shudder and instantly became endowed with the inherent natural quality of passion (had menstruation).
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 21]
कृताञ्जलिः स्थिता सूर्यं बभाषे चारुलोचना ।
सुप्रीता दर्शनेनाद्य गच्छ त्वं निजमण्डलम् ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation:
The beautiful-eyed Kunti, with folded palm; spoke to Sūrya Deva standing before :-- “I am highly pleased to-day seeing Thy form; now go back to Thy sphere.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 22]
सूर्य उवाच ।
आहूतोऽस्मि कथं कुन्ति त्वया मन्त्रबलेन वै ।
न मां भजसि कस्मात्त्वं समाहूय पुरोगतम् ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation:
Sūrya Deva said :-- “O Kunti! What for you called me, by virtue of the Mantra? Calling me, why do you not worship me, standing before you?
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 23]
कामार्तोऽस्म्यसितापाङ्गि भज मां भावसंयुतम् ।
मन्त्रेणाधीनतां प्राप्प्तं क्रीडितुं नय मामिति ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation:
O beautiful blue one! Seeing you, I have become passionate; so come to me. By means of the mantra, you have made me your subservient so take me for intercourse.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 24]
कुन्त्युवाच ॥

कन्यास्म्यहं तु धर्मज्ञ सर्वसाक्षिन्नमाम्यहम् ।
तवाप्यहं न दुर्वाच्या कुलकन्यास्मि सुव्रत ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation:
Hearing this, Kunti said :-- “O Witness of all! O knower of Dharma! You know that I am a virgin girl. O Suvrata! I bow down to you; I am a family daughter; so do not speak ill to me.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 25]
सूर्य उवाच ।
लज्जा मे महती चाद्य यदि गच्छाम्यहं वृथा ।
वाच्यतां सर्वदेवानां यास्याम्यत्र न संशयः ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation:
Sūrya then said :-- “If I go away in vain, I will be an object of great shame, and, no doubt, will be laughed amongst the gods;
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 26]
शप्स्यामि तं द्विजं चाद्य येन मन्त्रः समर्पितः ।
त्वां चापि सुभृशं कुन्ति नोचेन्मां त्वं भजिष्यसि ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation:
So, O Kunti! If you do not satisfy me, I will immediately curse you and the Brāhmin who has given you this mantra.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 27]
कन्याधर्मः स्थिरस्ते स्यान्न ज्ञास्यन्ति जनाः किल ।
मत्समस्तु तथा पुत्रो भविता ते वरानने ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation:
O Beautiful one! If you satisfy me, your virginity will remain; no body will come to know and there will be born a son to you, exactly like me.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 28]
इत्युक्त्वा तरणिः कुन्तीं तन्मनस्कां सुलज्जिताम् ।
भुक्त्वा जगाम देवेशो वरं दत्त्वातिवाञ्छितम् ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation:
Thus saying Sūrya Deva enjoyed the bashful Kunti, with her mind attracted towards him; He granted her the desired boons and went away.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 29]
गर्भं दधार सुश्रोणी सुगुप्ते मन्दिरे स्थिता ।
धात्री वेद प्रिया चैका न माता न जनस्तथा ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation:
The beautiful Kunti became pregnant and began to remain in a house, under great secrecy. Only the dear nurse knew that; her mother or any other person was quite unaware of the fact.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 30]
गुप्तः सद्मनि पुत्रस्तु जातश्चातिमनोहरः ।
कवचेनातिरम्येण कुण्डलाभ्यां समन्वितः ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation:
In time, a very beautiful son like the second Sun and Kārtikeya, decked with a lovely Kavaca coat of mail and two ear-rings, was born there.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 31]
द्वितीय इव सूर्यस्तु कुमार इव चापरः ।
करे कृत्वाथ धात्रेयी तामुवाच सुलज्जिताम् ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation:
Then the nurse caught hold of the hand of the bashful Kunti and said :-- “O Charming one! What care can you possibly have as long as I am living.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 32]
कां चिन्तां करभोरु त्वमाधत्सेऽद्य स्थितास्म्यहम् ।
मञ्जूषायां सुतं कुन्ती मुञ्चन्ती वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation:
Kunti then, placed the son in a box and said :-- “O son! What shall I do? Being afraid of shame, I am leaving you, though you are dear to me as my life itself! I am exceedingly fortunate that I am casting aside this all auspicious son.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 33-34]
किं करोमि सुतार्ताहं त्यजे त्वां प्राणवल्लभम् ।
मन्दभाग्या त्यजामि त्वां सर्वलक्षणसंयुतम् ॥ ३३ ॥

पातुत्वां सगुणागुणा भगवती
सर्वेश्वरी चाम्बिका
स्तन्यं सैव ददातु विश्वजननी
कात्यायनी कामदा ।
द्रक्ष्येऽहम्मुखपङ्कजं सुललितं
प्राणप्रियार्हं कदा
त्यक्त्वा त्वां विजने वने रविसुतं
दुष्टा यथा स्वैरिणी ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation:
May the attributeless Bhāgavatī Ambikā, the World Mother and the Lady of all, endowed with attributes, protect Thee! May Kātyāyani, the giver of all desires, feed you with Her milk! Alas! I am quitting you, born of Sūrya’s semen in this solitary forest like a vitiated wanton woman. I do not know, when shall I see your lotus like beautiful face, dearest to me like myself.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 35]
पूर्वस्मिन्नपि जन्मनि त्रिजगतां
माता न चाराधिता
न ध्यातं पदपङ्कजं सुखकरं
देव्याः शिवायाश्चिरम् ।
तेनाहं सुत दुर्भगास्मि सततं
त्यक्त्वा पुनस्त्वां वने
तप्स्यामि प्रिय पातकं स्मृतवती
बुद्ध्या कृतं यत्स्वयम् ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation:
Alas! I never worshipped in my former birth Śivānī, the mother of the three worlds; I never meditated Her lotus like feet, the Giver of all happiness; hence I am so very unfortunate. O Dear son! I must perform great tapasyā to expiate for this terrible sin, that I knowingly commit in relinquishing you in the forest.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 36]
सूत उवाव
इत्युक्त्या तं सुतं कुन्ती मञ्जूषायां धृतं किल ।
धात्रीहस्ते ददौ भीता जनदर्शनतस्तथा ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation:
Sūta said :-- Thus saying to the son within the casket, Kunti gave over that to the hands of her nurse, terrified, lest some one might see her.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 37]
स्नात्वा त्रस्ता तदा कुन्ती पितृवेश्मन्युवास सा ।
मञ्जूषा वहमाना च प्राप्ता ह्यधिरथेन वै ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation:
Kunti then bathed and remained with a fearful heart in her father’s house. A carpenter (charioteer?) named Adhiratha got accidentally that casket floating in the Ganges.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 38]
राधा सूतस्य भार्या वै तयासौ प्रार्थितः सुतः ।
कर्णोऽभूद्बलवान्वीरः पालितः सूतसद्मनि ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation:
The carpenter’s wife Rādhā, prayed for the son and nourished him under her care. Thus nourished in the carpenter’s house, the famous Kunti’s son Karna became a very powerful warrior.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 39]
कुन्ती विवाहिता कन्या पाण्डुना सा स्वयम्वरे ।
माद्री चैवापरा भार्या मद्रराजसुता शुभा ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation:
king Pānḍu then married Kunti in a Svayamvara, a marriage in which the girl chooses her husband from among a number of suitors, assembled together. And the all auspicious daughter of the king of Madra became also the second wife of Pānḍu.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 40]
मृगयां रममाणस्तु वने पाण्डुर्महाबलः ।
जघान मृगबुद्ध्या तु रममाणं मुनिं वने ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation:
Once, on an occasion, the powerful Pānḍu, while hunting in the forest killed a Muni, in the form of a deer, engaged in the act of co-habitation, thinking it to be a deer.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 41-42]
शप्तस्तेन तदा पाण्डुर्मुनिना कुपितेन च ।
स्त्रीसङ्गं यदि कर्तासि तदा ते मरणं धुवम् ॥ ४१ ॥

इति शप्तस्तु मुनिना पाण्डुः शोकसमन्वितः ।
त्यक्त्वा राज्यं वने वासं चकार भृशदुःखितः ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation:
The dying Muni became inflated with wrath, cursed Pānḍu :-- “If you co-habit, certainly you will die.” Thus cursed by the Muni, Pānḍu became very sorrowful and abandoned his kingdom and began to live in the forest.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 43]
कुन्ती माद्री च भार्ये द्वे जग्मतुः सह सङ्गते ।
सेवनार्थं सतीधर्मं संश्रिते मुनिसत्तमाः ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation:
O Munis! His two wives Kuntī and Mādrī, followed their husband as chaste women do, to serve him in the forest.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 44]
गङ्गातीरे स्थितः पाण्डुर्मुनीनामाश्रमेषु च ।
शृण्वानो धर्मशास्त्राणि चकार दुश्चरं तपः ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation:
Dwelling in the hermitage of the Munis, Pānḍu listened to the Dharma Śāstras and practised severe penance.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 45-46]
कथायां वर्तमानायां कदाचिद्धर्मसंश्रितम् ।
अशृणोद्वचनं राजा सुपृष्टं मुनिभाषितम् ॥ ४५ ॥

अपुत्रस्य गतिर्नास्ति स्वर्गे गन्तुं परन्तप ।
येन केनाप्युपायेन पुत्रस्य जननं चरेत् ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation:
Once while he was listening to the religious discourses of the Munis, he heard unmistakeably the Munis telling that the man who is sonless can never go to the Heavens; so he must get a son somehow or other.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 6 Verse 47-48]
अंशजः पुत्रिकापुत्रः क्षेत्रजो गोलकस्तथा ।
कुण्डः सहोढः कानीनः क्रीतः प्राप्तस्तथा वने ॥ ४७ ॥

दत्तः केनापि चाशक्तौ धनग्राहिसुताः स्मृताः ।
उत्तरोत्तरतः पुत्रा निकृष्टा इति निश्चयः ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation:
The Pundits declare that the sons born of the father’s semen, the sons born of their daughters, the Kṣettraja, the Goloka, the Kunḍa, the Sahoda, the Kānīna, the Krīta, one obtained in the forest, or one offered by another father, unable to nourish his son, all are entitled to inherit the wealth of the father; but the sons, enumerated successively are more and more inferior. N.B.: Ksettraja - of a son, the off spring of the wife by a kinsman appointed to procreate issue to the husband. Goloka - Bastard child of a widow. Kunda - a child born in adultery. Sahoda - the son of a woman pregnant at the time of marriage. Kānīna - the son born of a young and unmarried woman. Krīta - purchased

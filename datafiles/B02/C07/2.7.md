# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 2, CHAPTER 7

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> २.७ सप्तमोऽध्यायः । पाण्डवानां कथानकं मृतानां दर्शनवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> सूत उवाच ।
> पञ्चानां द्रौपदी भार्या सा मान्या सा पतिव्रता ।
> पञ्च पुत्रास्तु तस्याः स्युर्भर्तृभ्योऽतीव सुन्दराः ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Sūta said :-- The chaste Draupadi was the common wife of all the five very beautiful sons of Kunti; and she bore five sons, one to every husband.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अर्जुनस्य तथा भार्या कृष्णस्य भगिनी शुभा ।
> सुभद्रा या हृता पूर्वं जिष्णुना हरिसम्मते ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Arjuna had one wife more; she was Subhadrā, the sister of Śrī Kṛṣṇa. By the order of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, Arjuna stole her away (took her by force).

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्यां जातो महावीरो निहतोऽसौ रणाजिरे ।
> अभिमन्युर्हतास्तत्र द्रौपद्याश्च सुताः किल ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The great hero Abhimanyu was born of Subhadrā. This Abhimanyu and the five sons of Draupadi were killed in battle.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अभिमन्योर्वरा भार्या वैराटी चातिसुन्दरी ।
> कुलान्ते सुषुवे पुत्रं मृतो बाणाग्निना शिशुः ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Abhimanyu’s wife Uttarā was the charming daughter of the king Virāṭ. She gave birth to one dead child, after all the boys, the descendants of the family were extinct.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जीवितः स तु कृष्णेन भागिनेयसुतः स्वयम् ।
> द्रौणिबाणाग्निनिर्दग्धः प्रतापेनाद्भुतेन च ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The above child died out of the arrows of Aśvatthāmā. The extraordinarily powerful Śrī Kṛṣṇa Himself made alive again this his sister’s dead grandson.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> परिक्षीणेषु वंशेषु जातो यस्माद्वरः सुतः ।
> तस्मात्परीक्षितो नाम विख्यातः पृथिवीतले ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

As this son was born after the family had become extinct, he became known in the world by the name of Parīksit.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> निहतेषु च पुत्रेषु धृतराष्ट्रोऽतिदुःखितः ।
> तस्थौ पाण्डवराज्ये च भीमवाग्बाणपीडितः ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When the sons were all destroyed, Dhritarāstra became very sorry, and, tormented by the arrow-like words of Bhīma, remained in the kingdom of the Pāṇḍavas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> गान्धारी च तथातिष्ठत् पुत्रशोकातुरा भृशम् ।
> सेवां तयोर्दिवारात्रं चकारार्तो युधिष्ठिरः ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Gāndharī, too, exceedingly distressed on the bereavement of the sons, remained there also. Yudhisthira, day and night, served Dhritarāstra and Gāndhāri.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विदुरोऽप्यतिधर्मात्मा प्रज्ञानेत्रमबोधयत् ।
> युधिष्ठिरस्यानुमते भ्रातृपार्श्वे व्यतिष्ठत ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The greatly religious Vidura always used to console, by the advice of Yudhisthira, his brother Dhritarāstra, who possessed the eye of wisdom and he remained by his brother’s side.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> धर्मपुत्रोऽपि धर्मात्मा चकार सेवनं पितुः ।
> पुत्रशोकोद्भवं दुःखं तस्य विस्मारयन्निव ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Dharma`s son Yudhisthira used to serve his uncle Dhritarāstra in such a way as he might forget the pain of the death of his sons.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यथा शृणोति वृद्धोऽसौ तथा भीमोऽतिरोषितः ।
> वारबाणेनाहनत्तं तु श्रावयन्संस्थिताञ्जनान् ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

But Bhīma used to pierce his heart by his arrow-like words that he pronounced so loud as to reach the ears of the old king Dhritarāstra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मया पुत्रा हताः सर्वे दुष्टस्यान्धस्य ते रणे ।
> दुःशासनस्य रुधिरं पीतं हृद्यं तथा भृशम् ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Bhīma used to-say :-- “In the battle field I killed all the sons, of the wicked blind king (Dhritarāstra) and it was I that sucked well and drunk, full to the brim, the blood of the heart of Duhśāsana.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भुनक्ति पिण्डमन्धोऽयं मया दत्तं गतत्रपः ।
> ध्वाङ्क्षवद्वा श्ववच्चापि वृथा जीवत्यसौ जनः ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Now this blind king eats shamelessly like a crow and a dog, the mass of food (Piṇḍa) given by me, and thus is bearing uselessly the burden of life.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एवंविधानि रूक्षाणि श्रावयत्यनुवासरम् ।
> आश्वासयति धर्मात्मा मूर्खोऽयमिति च ब्रुवन् ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Daily Bhīma used to tell, thus, harsh words to him; whereas the religious Yudhīsthira used to console him, saying “Bhīma is a quite illiterate brute” and so forth.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 15-16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अष्टादशैव वर्षाणि स्थित्वा तत्रैव दुःखितः ।
> धृतराष्ट्रो वने यानं प्रार्थयामास धर्मजम् ॥ १५ ॥
> 
> अयाचत धर्मपुत्रं धृतराष्ट्रो महीपतिः ।
> पुत्रेभ्योऽहं ददाम्यद्य निर्वापं विधिपूर्वकम् ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

king Dhritarāstra remained there with a grieved heart for eighteen years; the one day he proposed to the Dharma’s son Yudhīsthira about his intention to dwell in forest thus :-- “To-day I wish to perform Tarpaṇas in the names my sons.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वृकोदरेण सर्वेषां कृतमत्रौर्ध्वदैहिकम् ।
> न कृतं मम पुत्राणां पूर्ववैरमनुस्मरन् ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

True it is, that Bhīma performed the funeral obsequies of them all; but, having in view of the former enmity, he did not do anything for my sons.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ददासि चेद्धनं मह्यं कृत्वा चैवोर्ध्वदैहिकम् ।
> गमिष्येऽहं वनं तप्तुं तपः स्वर्गफलप्रदम् ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

If you give me some money, I will, then, perform the funeral obsequies of my sons and then retire to the forest to perform tapasyā that I can go to Heaven.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एकान्ते विदुरेणोक्तो राजा धर्मसुतः शुचिः ।
> धनं दातुं मनश्चक्रे धृतराष्ट्राय चार्थिने ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vidura also asked Yudhīsthira privately pay to Dhritarāshtra the sum that he wanted; Yudhīsthira also intended to pay the required money.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> समाहूय निजान्मर्वानुवाच पृथिवीपतिः ।
> धनं दास्ये महाभागाः पित्रे निर्वापकामिने ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then Yudhīsthira, the lord of the world call his younger brothers and addressed them as follows :-- “O highly fortunate ones! Our revered uncle is desirous to perform the funeral obsequies of his sons; so we will have to give him some money for the purpose.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं भ्रातुर्न्येष्ठस्यामिततेजसः ।
> सङ्ग्रहेऽस्य महाबाहुर्मारुतिः कुपितोऽब्रवीत् ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing these words of his elder brother of indomitable valour, Pavana’s son, the mighty armed Bhīma became very angry and spoke out follows :--

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> धनं देयं महाभाग दुर्योधनहिताय किम् ।
> अन्धोऽपि सुखमाप्नोति मूर्खत्वं किमतः परम् ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

“O highly lucky one! Is it that we will have to give wealth for the spiritual benefit of Duryodhana and others? What a great stupidity can there be than the fact that such a malevolent blind king is deriving so great happiness at your hands?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तव दुर्मन्त्रितेनाथ दुःखं प्राप्ता वने वयम् ।
> द्रौपदी च महाभागा समानीता दुरात्मना ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Ārya! It is by your bad counsel that we suffered endless troubles in the forest and the extremely good Draupadi was brought before the public in the hall by Duhśāsana.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विराटभवने वासः प्रसादात्तव सुव्रत ।
> दासत्वं च कृतं सर्वैर्मत्स्यस्यामितविक्रमैः ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O one of good vows! It is for your satisfaction alone that we, though we were very mighty, had to remain in the house of Matsya Rāj Virāt as servants.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देविता त्वं न चेज्ज्येष्ठः प्रभवेत्सङ्क्षयः कथम् ।
> सूपकारो विराटस्य हत्वाभूवं तु मागधम् ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Had you not been our elder brother and not been addicted to the gambling, would it have been possible I, who killed Jarāsandha, would have been a cook to Virāt Rāj! Never we had been put to so great a trouble!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> बृहन्नला कथं जिष्णुर्भवेद्बालस्य नर्तकः ।
> कृत्वा वेषं महाबाहुर्योषाया वासवात्मजः ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Never would the mighty armed Arjuna, the Vāsava’s son, have acted the part of an actress (a dancer), dressing himself in a female garb, under the name Vrihannalā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 27-28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> गाण्डीवशोभितौ हस्तौ कृतौ कङ्कणशोभितौ ।
> मानुषं च वपुः प्राप्य किं दुःखं स्यादतः परम् ॥ २७ ॥
> 
> दृष्ट्वा वेणीं कृतां मूर्ध्नि कज्जलं लोचने तथा ।
> असिं गृहीत्वा तरसा छेद्म्यहं नान्यथा सुखम् ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Alas! What more painful could there be by assuming a human birth that the hands of Arjuna, that wielded always the Gāndīva bow, would have worn bracelets befitting a woman? I would have been happy then had I, seeing the braid of hair on Arjuna’s head and the collyrium in his eyes, cut off the head of  of Dhṛtaraśstra?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अपृष्ट्वा च महीपालं निक्षिप्तोऽग्निर्मया गृहे ।
> दग्धुकामश्च पापात्मा निर्दग्धोऽसौ पुरोचनः ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Lord of the earth! Without asking you, I set fire on the house, named Jatugriha (a lac-house, as built by Duryodhana in order to burn up the Pāṇḍavas) and therefore the vicious Virocana, who wanted to burn us, was himself burnt up.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कीचका निहताः सर्वे त्वामपृष्ट्वा जनाधिप ।
> न तथा निहताः सर्वे सभार्या धृतराष्ट्रजाः ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Again, O Lord of men! similarly, without asking you, I slew Kichaka; this is now the one thing I regret that I could not have killed in the same way the sons of Dhritarāstra before the public hall.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मूर्खत्वं तव राजेन्द्र गन्धर्वेभ्यश्च मोचिताः ।
> दुर्योधनादयः कामं शत्रवो निगडीकृताः ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O king of kings! It was simply your stupidity that you liberated Duryodhana and other sons, the great enemies of the Gandarbhas, when they had been imprisoned by them.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दुर्योधनहितायाद्य धनं दातुं त्वमिच्छसि ।
> नाहं ददे महीपाल सर्वथा प्रेरितस्त्वया ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Again to-day you are willing to give wealth for the spiritual benefit of those Duryodhana and others! But, O Lord of the earth, I would never give wealth, even if you request me specially to do this.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इत्युक्त्वा निर्गते भीमे त्रिभिः परिवृतो नृपः ।
> ददौ वित्तं सुबहुलं धृतराष्ट्राय धर्मजः ॥ ३३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus saying, Bhīma went away. Dharma’s son Yudhisthira then consulted with the other three brothers and gave abundance of wealth to Dhritarāstra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कारयामास विधिवत्पुत्राणां चौर्ध्वदैहिकम् ।
> ददौ दानानि विप्रेभ्यो धृतराष्ट्रोऽम्बिकासुतः ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

With this sum, the Ambikā’s son Dhritarāstra duly performed the Srādh ceremony of his sons and gave away lots of things to the Brāhmaṇas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 35</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कृत्वौर्ध्वदैहिकं सर्वं गान्धारीसहितो नृपः ।
> प्रविवेश वनं तूर्णं कुन्त्या च विदुरेण च ॥ ३५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

king Dhritarāstra, thus performing all the funeral obsequies, became ready to go early to the forest with Gāndhārī, Kunti and Vidura.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 36-37</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सञ्जयेन परिज्ञातो निर्गतोऽसौ महामतिः ।
> पुत्रैर्निवार्यमाणापि शूरसेनसुता गता ॥ ३६ ॥
> 
> विलपन्भीमसेनोऽपि तथान्ये चापि कौरवाः ।
> गङ्गातीरात्परावृत्य ययुः सर्वे गजाह्वयम् ॥ ३७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

By the help of Sañjaya, the highly intelligent Dhritarāstra became informed of the roads of the forest, and then went out of the house. Sūrasena’s daughter Kunti, though stopped by her sons, followed them. Bhīma and other Kauravas went along with them weeping up to the banks of the Ganges and thence returned to Hastināpura.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ते गत्वा जाह्नवीतीरे शतयूपाश्रमं शुभम् ।
> कृत्वा तृणैः कुटीं तत्र तपस्तेपुः समाहिताः ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The ascetics went to the auspicious Śatayūpa hermitage on the banks of the Ganges and building a hut practised tapasyā with their hearts concentrated.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 39</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> गतान्यब्दानि षट् तेषां यदा याता हि तापसाः ।
> युधिष्ठिरस्तु विरहादनुजानिदमब्रवीत् ॥ ३९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus six years elapsed when Yudhisthira, troubled by their bereavements, said to his younger brothers :--

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 40-44</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्वप्ने दृष्टा मया कुन्ती दुर्बला वनसंस्थिता ।

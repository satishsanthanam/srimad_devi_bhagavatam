# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 2, CHAPTER 1

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 1 Verse 1-2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> २.१ प्रथमोऽध्यायः । मस्त्यगन्धोत्पत्तिवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> ऋषय ऊचुः ।
> आश्चर्यकरमेतत्ते वचनं गर्भहेतुकम् ।
> सन्देहोऽत्र समुत्पन्नः सर्वेषां नस्तपस्विनाम् ॥ १ ॥
> 
> माता व्यासस्य मेधाविन्नाम्ना सत्यवतीति च ।
> विवाहिता पुरा ज्ञाता राज्ञा शन्तनुना यथा ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Ṛṣis said :-- “O Sūta! Your words sound wonderful to us indeed! but you have not as yet definitely spoken to us the original events in detail; so a great doubt has arisen in our minds. We know that the king Śantanu married Vyāsa’s mother, Satyavatī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 1 Verse 3-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्याः पुत्रः कथं व्यासः सती स्वभवने स्थिता ।
> ईदृशी सा कथं राज्ञा पुनः शन्तनुना वृता ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> तस्यां पुत्रावुभौ जातौ तत्त्वं कथय सुव्रत ।
> विस्तरेण महाभाग कथां परमपावनीम् ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Now say, in detail in how Vyāsa became her son? How such a chaste woman Satyavatī, remaining in her own house, came to be married again by Śantanu? and how the two sons came to be born of Śantanu’s sperm and Satyavatī’ś ovum? Now O highly fortunate Suvrata? Kindly describe in detail this highly sanctifying historical fact.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 1 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उत्पत्तिं वद व्यासस्य सत्यवत्यास्तथा पुनः ।
> श्रोतुकामाः पुनः सर्वे ऋषयः संशितव्रताः ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

These Ṛṣis, who are observing vows, are desirous to hear of the birth of Veda Vyāsa and Satyavatī.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 1 Verse 6-7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सूत उवाच ।
> प्रणम्य परमां शक्तिं चतुर्वर्गप्रदायिनीम् ।
> आदिशक्तिं वदिष्यामि कथां पौराणिकीं शुभाम् ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> यस्योच्चारणमात्रेण सिद्धिर्भवति शाश्वती ।
> व्याजेनापि हि बीजस्य वाग्भवस्य विशेषतः ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Sūta said :-- I bow down with devotion to the Highest Primordial Force, the bestower of the four fold aims of existence of human life, who grants to all, their desires when so prayed by the help of the Vāgbhava Vījamantra with their heart and soul, for the success of all their desires.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 1 Verse 8-9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सम्यक् सर्वात्मना सर्वैः सर्वकामार्थसिद्धये ।
> स्मर्तव्या सर्वथा देवी वाञ्छितार्थप्रदायिनी ॥ ८ ॥
> 
> राजोपरिचरो नाम धार्मिकः सत्यसङ्गरः ।
> चेदिदेशपतिः श्रीमान् बभूव द्विजपूजकः ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The above vīja is so potent in its effect that even pronounced very lightly, even under a pretext, it grants all siddhis. So the Devī should be remembered by all means; and now saluting Her, I begin my narration of the auspicious Purāṇic events. In days gone by there reigned a king, named Uparicara; he ruled over the Chedi country and respected the Brāhmins; he was truthful and very religious.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 1 Verse 10</b></summary>

> **Sanskrit:**

>
**English Translation:**

Indra, the lord of the Devas, became very pleased by his asceticism and presented him an auspicious celestial car (going in the air) made of pearls, and crystals, helping him in doing what he liked best.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 1 Verse 11-12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तेनारूढस्तु सर्वत्र याति दिव्येन भूपतिः ।
> न भूमावुपरिस्थोऽसौ तेनोपरिचरो वसुः ॥ ११ ॥
> 
> विख्यातः सर्वलोकेषु धर्मनित्यः स भूपतिः ।
> तस्य भार्या वरारोहा गिरिका नाम सुन्दरी ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Mounting on that divine chariot, that religious king used to go everywhere; he never remained on earth; he used to remain always in the atmosphere and therefore be had his name as “Uparicara Vasu” (moving in the upper regions). He had a very beautiful wife, named Girikā; and five powerful sons, of indomitable vigour, were born to him.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 1 Verse 13-14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पुत्राश्चास्य महावीर्याः पञ्चासन्नमितौजसः ।
> पृथग्देशेषु राजानः स्थापितास्तेन भूभुजा ॥ १३ ॥
> 
> वसोस्तु पत्नी गिरिका कामान् काले न्यवेदयत् ।
> ऋतुकालमनुप्राप्ता स्नाता पुंसवने शुचिः ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The king give separate kingdoms to each of his sons and made them kings. Once on an occasion, Girikā, the wife of the Uparicara Vasu, after her bath after the menstruation and becoming pure came to the king and informed him of her desire to get a son; but that very day his Pitris (ancestors) requested him also to kill deer, etc., for their Srāddha (solemn obsequies performed in honour of the manes of deceased ancestors).

</details>

> 
> तस्य रेतः प्रचस्कन्द स्मरतस्तां च कामिनीम् ।

>
**English Translation:**

Hearing the Pitris, the king of Chedi became somewhat anxious for his menstruous wife; but thinking his Pitris words more powerful and more worthy to be obeyed, went out on an hunting expedition to kill deer and other animals, with the thought of his wife Girikā in his breast.

</details>

He kept this semen on the leaf of a banyan tree and thought “How the above semen be not futile; my semen cannot remain unfruitful; my wife has just now passed her menstruous condition; I will send this semen to my dear wife.”

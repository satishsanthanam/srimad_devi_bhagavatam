Ch 2 Verse 1-2 Verse
Sanskrit Text
२.२ द्वितीयोऽध्यायः । व्यासजन्मवर्णनम् । सूत उवाच । एकदा तीर्थयात्रायां व्रजन् पाराशरो मुनिः । आजगाम महातेजाः कालिन्द्यास्तटमुत्तमम् ॥ १ ॥ निषादमाह धर्मात्मा कुर्वन्तं भोजनं तदा । प्रापयस्व परं पारं कालिन्द्या उडुपेन माम् ॥ २ ॥
English Translation
Once on an occasion, the highly energetic Muni Parāśara went out on pilgrimage and arrived on the banks of the Yamunā, and spoke to the religious fisherman who was taking his food then thus :-- “O fisherman! Take me on your boat and carry me to the other side of the river.”
Ch 2 Verse 3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दाशः श्रुत्वा मुनेर्वाक्यं कुर्वाणो भोजनं तटे । उवाच तां सुतां बालां मत्स्यगन्धां मनोरमाम् ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
Hearing this, the fisherman spoke to the beautiful girl Matsyagandhā :-- “O beautiful smiling one! This religious ascetic intends to cross the river; so take the boat and carry him to the other bank.”
Ch 2 Verse 4 Verse
Sanskrit Text
उडुपेन मुनिं बाले परं पारं नयस्व ह । गन्तुकामोऽस्ति धर्मात्मा तापसोऽयं शुचिस्मिते ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation
Thus ordered by her father, the exceedingly beautiful Vasu girl Matsyagandhā began to steer the boat whereon sat the Muni.
Ch 2 Verse 5-6 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इत्युक्ता सा तदा पित्रा मत्स्यगन्धाथ वासवी । उडुपे मुनिमासीनं संवाहयति भामिनी ॥ ५ ॥ व्रजन् सूर्यसुतातोये भावित्वाद्दैवयोगतः । कामार्तस्तु मुनिर्जातो दृष्ट्वा तां चारुलोचनाम् ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation
Thus while the boat was sliding on the waters of the Yamunā; the Muni Parāśara saw the beautiful-eyed damsel Matsyagandhā and became as if under the command of the great destiny, greatly enamoured of her.

Sanskrit Text
ग्रहीतुकामः स मुनिर्दृष्ट्वा व्यञ्जितयौवनाम् । दक्षिणेन करेणैनामस्पृशद्दक्षिणे करे ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
He desired to enjoy Matsyagandhā, full of youth and beauty and with his right hand caught hold of her left hand; the blue coloured Matsya looking askance spoke out smilingly in the following words :--
Ch 2 Verse 8 Verse

तमुवाचासितापाङ्गी स्मितपूर्वमिदं वचः । कुलस्य सदृशं वः किं श्रुतस्य तपसश्च किम् ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
O knower of Dharma! What are you going to do, pierced by the arrows of Cupid?
Ch 2 Verse 9 Verse
Sanskrit Text
त्वं वै वसिष्ठदायादः कुलशीलसमन्वितः । किं चिकीर्षसि धर्मज्ञ मन्मथेन प्रपीडितः ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation
What you desire now, is it worthy of your family or your study of the Śāstras or worthy of your Tapasyā; see, you are born in the line of Vaśiṣṭha and you are well known as of good character.
Ch 2 Verse 10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दुर्लभं मानुषं जन्म भुवि ब्राह्मणसत्तम । तत्रापि दुर्लभं मन्ये ब्राह्मणत्वं विशेषतः ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
O best of Brāhmins! You are quite aware that the attaining of a man-birth in this world is very rare; and over and above this the attainment of Brāhminhood is, as far as my knowledge goes, particularly difficult.
Ch 2 Verse 11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कुलेन शीलेन तथा श्रुतेन द्विजोत्तमस्त्वं किल धर्मविच्च । अनार्यभावं कथमागतोऽसि विप्रेन्द्र मां वीक्ष्य च मीनगन्धाम् ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
O Prince of Brāhmins! You are the foremost and best as far as your family, goodness, and learning in the Vedas and other Śāstras are concerned; you are well versed in Dharma; how is it, then, that you are going to do this act, not worthy of an Ārya, though you see me possessed of this bad smell of fish throughout my body.
Ch 2 Verse 12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मदीये शरीरे द्विजामोघबुद्धे ॥ शुभं किं समालोक्य पाणिं ग्रहीतुम् । समीपं समायासि कामातुरस्त्वं कथं नाभिजानासि धर्मं स्वकीयम् ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
O one of unbaffled understanding! O best of twice-born! What auspicious sign do you see in my body that you are stricken with passion on my account that you have caught hold of my hand to enjoy me? Why have you gotten your own Dharma?
Ch 2 Verse 13 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अहो मन्दबुद्धिर्द्विजोऽयं ग्रहीष्य- ञ्जले मग्न एवाद्य मां वै गृहीत्वा । मनो व्याकुलं पञ्चबाणातिविद्धं न कोऽपीह शक्तः प्रतीपं हि कर्तुम् ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation
Thus saying, Matsyagandhā thought within herself :-- “Alas! This Brāhmin has certainly lost his brains in order to enjoy me; certainly he will be drowned just now in his attempt to enjoy me in this boat; his mind is so much agitated with the arrows of Cupid that no body, it seems, can act against his will.”
Ch 2 Verse 14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इति सञ्चिन्त्य सा बाला तमुवाच महामुनिम् । धैर्यं कुरु महाभाग परं पारं नयामि वै ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
Thus thinking Matsyagandhā spoke again to the Muni :-- “O highly fortunate one! Hold patience! let me first take you to the other side of the river; then you may do as you please.”
Ch 2 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सूत उवाच । पराशरस्तु तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं हितपूर्वकम् । करं त्यक्त्वा स्थितस्तत्र सिन्धोः पारं गतः पुनः ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
Hearing these reasonable words, the Muni let loose of her hand and took his seat on the boat and gradually got down on the other bank of the river.
Ch 2 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मत्स्यगन्धां प्रजग्राह मुनिः कामातुरस्तदा । वेपमाना तु सा कन्या तमुवाच पुरःस्थितम् ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
But the Muni, becoming again extremely passionate caught hold of Matsyagandhā; when the young woman spoke to Parāśara, in front of her, shuddering :--
Ch 2 Verse 17 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दुर्गन्धाहं मुनिश्रेष्ठ कथं त्वं नोपशङ्कसे । समानरूपयोः कामसंयोगस्तु सुखावहः ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation
“O best of Munis! My body is emitting very bad smell; do you not feel this? You know very well that the sexual intercourse between male and female of similar types brings in happiness and comfort.”
Ch 2 Verse 18-19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इत्युक्तेन तु सा कन्या क्षणमात्रेण भामिनी । कृता योजनगन्धा तु सुरूपा च वरानना ॥ १८ ॥ मृगनाभिसुगन्धां तां कृत्वा कान्तां मनोहराम् । जग्राह दक्षिणे पाणौ मुनिर्मन्मथपीडितः ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
Thus spoken to, Parāśara made Matsyagandhā emit sweet scent like that of musk to a distance of one Yojana (8 miles) and her body exceedingly lovely and beautiful and, becoming extremely passionate, again caught hold of her right hand.
Ch 2 Verse 20-21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ग्रहीतुकामं तं प्राह नाम्ना सत्यवती शुभा । मुने पश्यति लोकोऽयं पिता चैव तटस्थितः ॥ २० ॥ पशुधर्मो न मे प्रीतिं जनयत्यतिदारुणः । प्रतीक्षस्व मुनिश्रेष्ठ यावद्भवति यामिनी ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
Then the auspicious Satyavatī addressed Parāśara Muni, resolved to enjoy her, thus :-- “O Muni! Behold! all are looking at us; my father too, is there on the bank of the Yamunā; so, O Muni! wait till night this beastly act before all is highly unsatisfactory to me.
Ch 2 Verse 22 Verse
Sanskrit Text
रात्रौ व्यवाय उद्दिष्टो दिवा न मनुजस्य हि । दिवासङ्गे महान् दोषः पश्यन्ति किल मानवाः ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation
The wise persons declare it a great sin to commit sexual intercourse during day they have ordained night time as the best time of intercourse for men not the day time; the more so that many person’s eyes are in this direction.
Ch 2 Verse 23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कामं यच्छ महाबुद्धे लोकनिन्दा दुरासदा । तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्या युक्तमुक्तमुदारधीः ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
So, O intelligent one! hold on your passion for a while; for the blame pronounced by the public is horrible.” Hearing these reasonable words, the liberal minded Parāśara created, by his influence of Tapasyā, a dense fog so that both the banks of the Yamunā became covered with darkness.
Ch 2 Verse 24-26 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नीहारं कल्पयामास शीघ्रं पुण्यबलेन वै । नीहारे च समुत्पन्ने तटेऽतितमसा युते ॥ २४ ॥ कामिनी तं मुनिं प्राह मृदुपूर्वमिदं वचः । कन्याहं द्विजशार्दूल भुक्त्वा गन्तासि कामतः ॥ २५ ॥ अमोघवीर्यस्त्वं ब्रह्मन् का गतिर्मे भवेदिति । पितरं किं ब्रवीम्यद्य सगर्भा चेद्भवाम्यहम् ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation
Then Matsyagandhā gently spoke to the Muni :-- “O best of Dvījas! I am not as yet married; I am now a girl; you will go away after enjoying me; your semen virile is not fruitless; so Brāhmaṇ! What will be my fate? If I be pregnant today, what shall I say to my father? and what will be my future state? There is no doubt that, after enjoying me, you will go away; what will I do afterwards; kindly say.”
Ch 2 Verse 27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
त्वं गमिष्यसि भुक्त्वा मां किं करोमि वदस्व तत् । पराशर उवाच । कान्तेऽद्य मत्प्रियं कृत्वा कन्यैव त्वं भविष्यसि ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
Hearing these words of Matsyagandhā, Parāśara said :-- “O beloved! after you have done my pleasant duty, you will remain a girl as you are now; yet, O timid one! ask from me any boon you like; I will grant it to you.”
Ch 2 Verse 28-29 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वृणीष्व च वरं भीरु यं त्वमिच्छसि भामिनि । सत्यवत्युवाच । यथा मे पितरौ लोके न जानीतो हि मानद ॥ २८ ॥ कन्याव्रतं न मे हन्यात्तथा कुरु द्विजोत्तम । पुत्रश्च त्वत्समः कामं भवेदद्भुतवीर्यवान् ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation
Satyavatī then said :-- “O best Brāhmaṇ, O giver of one’s honour! grant me these things :-- That my father and mother do not know anything of this affair and that my virginity be again as ever the same. Also let an extraordinarily powerful energetic son be born to me like you; let this nice smell continue to remain always in my body and let my youth and beauty remain afresh and increase ever more.
Ch 2 Verse 30-31 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गन्धोऽयं सर्वदा मे स्याद्यौवनं च नवं नवम् । पराशर उवाच । शृणु सुन्दरि पुत्रस्ते विष्ण्वंशसम्भवः शुचिः ॥ ३० ॥ भविष्यति च विख्यातस्त्रैलोक्ये वरवर्णिनि । केनचित्कारणेनाहं जातः कामातुरस्त्वयि ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation
Hearing this, Parāśara said :-- “O beautiful one! a son, very pure and holy, will be born to you, from Nārāyana’s part! his name will be famous in the three worlds. O beautiful one! never before my heart was agitated with such passion.
Ch 2 Verse 32-34 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कदापि च न सम्मोहो भूतपूर्वो वरानने । दृष्ट्वा चाप्सरसां रूपं सदाहं धैर्यमावहम् ॥ ३२ ॥ दैवयोगेन वीक्ष्य त्वां कामस्य वशगोऽभवम् । तत्किञ्चित्कारणं विद्धि दैवं हि दुरतिक्रमम् ॥ ३३ ॥ दृष्ट्वाहं चातिदुर्गन्धां त्वां कथं मोहमाप्नुयाम् । पुराणकर्ता पुत्रस्ते भविष्यति वरानने ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation
I do not know why I have become so much passionate for you. I saw the unrivalled beauties of Apsarās but I never lost my patience; but seeing you, I have become attracted to you; it must be under the direction of Providence; know it certain that there must be some mysterious cause in this. However Fate is unavoidable to all; otherwise you are full of so bad smell; why shall I be fascinated by your sight? O beautiful one! your son will be famed in the three worlds; will compose the Purāṇas and will sub-divide the Vedas.
Ch 2 Verse 35 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Thus saying, the Muni Parāśara enjoyed Matsyagandhā, who became quite submissive; and after bathing in the Yamunā, quickly went away.
Ch 2 Verse 36 Verse

English Translation
On the other hand, the chaste Satyavatī, too, became pregnant and immediately gave birth on the island of Yamunā to a son beautiful, as if the Second Kāmadeva, the god of Love, Kāmadeva.
Ch 2 Verse 37 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
No sooner that son, very fiery and highly potent, was born than he devoted his mind to tapasyā and spoke to his own mother Satyavatī thus :--“O Mother! now go wherever you like; I will also go to perform tapasyā. O highly fortunate one; No sooner you remember me, I will come to you.
Ch 2 Verse 38-40 Verse
Sanskrit Text

Ch 2 Verse 41-42 Verse

Muni Dvaipāyana bathed in every Tīrtha and performed the highest asceticism. Thus Dvaipāyan Vyāsa was born of Parāśara in Satyavatī’s womb.

Ch 2 Verse 50-52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अनार्यजुष्टं धर्मज्ञः कृतवान्स कथं मुनिः । सकारणेयमुत्पत्तिः कथिताश्चर्यकारिणी ॥ ५० ॥ श्रुत्वा पापाच्च निर्मुक्तो नरो भवति सर्वथा । य एतच्छुभमाख्यानं शृणोति श्रुतिमान्नरः ॥ ५१ ॥ न दुर्गतिमवाप्नोति सुखी भवति सर्वदा ॥ ५२ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां द्वितीयस्कन्धे व्यासजन्मवर्णनं नाम द्वितीयोऽध्यायः ॥ २.२॥
English Translation
Now has been spoken the wonderful birth story of Vyāsa Deva together with all incidents, and enveloped under the great mystery. If any man hears this holy narrative, he will be freed from sins and will never fall into difficulties and will always be happy. Thus ends the Second Chapter of the Second Skandha on the birth of Vyāsa Deva in the Mahāpurāṇam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses.

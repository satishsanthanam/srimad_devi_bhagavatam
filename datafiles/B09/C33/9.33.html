BOOK 9 • CHAPTER 33
On the description of the destinies of different sinners in different hells

Ch 33 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
९.३३ त्रयस्त्रिंशोऽध्यायः । नानाकर्मविपाकफलकथनम् । धर्मराज उवाच । हरिसेवारतः शुद्धो योगसिद्धो व्रती सति । तपस्वी ब्रह्मचारी च न याति नरकं ध्रुवम् ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Dharmarājan said :-- Those that are in Hari’s service, pure, the Siddhas in Yoga (those that have attained success in Yoga), the performers of Vratas

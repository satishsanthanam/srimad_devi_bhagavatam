Scriptures

Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam

BOOK 9 • CHAPTER 31
On the Yama's giving Śakti Mantra to Sāvitrī
← Previous Chapter ☰ Index Next Chapter →
Ch 31 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
९.३१ एकत्रिंशोऽध्यायः । यमाष्टकवर्णनम् । श्रीनारायण उवाच । शक्तेरुत्कीर्तनं श्रुत्वा सावित्री यमवक्त्रतः । साश्रुनेत्रा सपुलका यमं पुनरुवाच सा ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Nārāyaṇa said :-- O Nārada! Hearing thus the supreme nature of Mūla Prakriti from Dharmarāja Yama, the two eyes of Sāvitrī were filled with tears of joy and her whole body was filled with a thrill of rapture, joy and ecstacy.
Ch 31 Verse 2 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सावित्र्युवाच । शक्तेरुत्कीर्तनं धर्म सकलोद्धारकारणम् । श्रोतॄणां चैव वक्तॄणां जन्ममृत्युजराहरम् ॥ २ ॥
English Translation
She again addressed Yama :-- “O Dharmarāja! To sing the glories of Mūla Prakriti is the only means of saving all. This takes away the old age and death of both the speaker and the hearer.
Ch 31 Verse 3-5 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दानवानां च सिद्धानां तपसां च परं पदम् । योगानां चैव वेदानां कीर्तनं सेवनं विभो ॥ ३ ॥ मुक्तित्वममरत्वं च सर्वसिद्धित्वमेव च । श्रीशक्तिसेवकस्यैव कलां नार्हन्ति षोडशीम् ॥ ४ ॥ भजामि केन विधिना वद वेदविदांवर । शुभकर्मविपाकं च श्रुतं नॄणां मनोहरम् ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation
This is the Supreme Place of the Dānavas, the Siddhas, the ascetics. This is the Yoga of the Yogins and this is studying the Vedas of the Vaidiks. Nothing can compare even to one sixteenth of the sixteenth parts of the (full) merits of those who are in Śakti’s Service; call it Mukti, immortality, or attaining endless Siddhis, nothing can come to it. O Thou, the foremost of the Knowers of the Vedas! I have heard by and by everything from Thee. Now describe to me how to worship Mūla Prakriti and what are the ends of karmas, auspicious and inauspicious.”
Ch 31 Verse 6 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कर्माशुभविपाकं च तन्मे व्याख्यातुमर्हसि । इत्युक्त्वा च सती ब्रह्मन् भक्तिनम्रात्मकन्धरा ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation
Thus saying, the chaste Sāvitrī bowed down her head and began to praise Yama in stotras according to the Vedas.
Ch 31 Verse 7-8 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तुष्टाव धर्मराजं च वेदोक्तेन स्तवेन च । सावित्र्युवाच । तपसा धर्ममाराध्य पुष्करे भास्करः पुरा ॥ ७ ॥ धर्मं सूर्यः सुतं प्राप धर्मराजं नमाम्यहम् । समता सर्वभूतेषु यस्य सर्वस्य साक्षिणः ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
She said :-- “O Dharmarājan! The Sun practised of yore very hard austerities at Puṣkara and worshipped Dharma. On this, Dharma Himself became born of Sūrya as his son. And Thou art that son of Sūrya, the incarnation of Dharma. So I bow down to Thee.
Ch 31 Verse 9-10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अतो यन्नाम शमनमिति तं प्रणमाम्यहम् । येनान्तश्च कृतो विश्वे सर्वेषां जीविनां परम् ॥ ९ ॥ कामानुरूपं कालेन तं कृतान्तं नमाम्यहम् । बिभर्ति दण्डं दण्डाय पापिनां शुद्धिहेतवे ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
Thou art the Witness of all the Jīvas; Thou seest them equally; hence Thy name is Samana. I bow down to Thee. Sometimes Thou by Thy own will takest away the lives of beings. Hence Thy name is Kritānta. Obeisance to Thee! Thou holdest the rod to distribute justice and pronounce sentence on them and to destroy the sins of the Jīvas; hence Thy name is Daṇḍadhara; so I bow down to Thee. (Note :-- Any Jīva, in course of his travelling towards Mukti, can expect to pass through the stage Yamaship; and if he pleases, he can become a Yama.)
Ch 31 Verse 11-12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नमामि तं दण्डधरं यः शास्ता सर्वजीविनाम् । विश्वं च कलयत्येव यः सर्वेषु च सन्ततम् ॥ ११ ॥ अतीव दुर्निवार्यं च तं कालं प्रणमाम्यहम् । तपस्वी ब्रह्मनिष्ठो यः संयमी सञ्जितेन्द्रियः ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
At all times Thou destroyest the universe. None can resist Thee. Hence Thou art named Kāla; so obeisance to Thee! Thou art an ascetic, devoted to Brahmā, self-controlled, and the distributor of the fruits of Karmas to the Jīvas; Thou restrainest Thy senses. Hence Thou art called Yama. Therefore I bow down to Thee.
Ch 31 Verse 13-14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जीवानां कर्मफलदस्तं यमं प्रणमाम्यहम् । स्वात्मारामश्च सर्वज्ञो मित्रं पुण्यकृतां भवेत् ॥ १३ ॥ पापिनां क्लेशदो यस्तं पुण्यमित्रं नमाम्यहम् । यज्जन्म ब्रह्मणोंऽशेन ज्वलन्तं ब्रह्मतेजसा ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
Thou art delighted with Thy Own Self; Thou art omniscient; Thou art the Tormentor of the sinners and the Friend of the Virtuous. Hence Thy name is Puṇya Mitra; so I bow down to Thee. Thou art born as a part of Brahmā; the fire of Brahmā is shining through Thy body.
Ch 31 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यो ध्यायति परं ब्रह्म तमीशं प्रणमाम्यहम् । इत्युक्त्वा सा च सावित्री प्रणनाम यमं मुने ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
Thou dost meditate on Para Brahmā, Thou art the Lord. Obeisance to Thee!” O Muni! Thus praising Yama, She bowed down at the feet of Him. Yama gave her the mantra of Mūla Prakriti. How to worship Her and He began to recite the fruition of good Karmas.
Ch 31 Verse 16-31 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यमस्तां शक्तिभजनं कर्मपाकमुवाच ह । इदं यमाष्टकं नित्यं प्रातरुत्थाय यः पठेत् ॥ १६ ॥ यमात्तस्य भयं नास्ति सर्वपापात्प्रमुच्यते । महापापी यदि पठेन्नित्यं भक्तिसमन्वितः । यमः करोति संशुद्धं कायव्यूहेन निश्चितम् ॥ १७ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां नवमस्कन्धे यमाष्टकवर्णनं नामेकत्रिशोऽध्यायः ॥ ९.३१॥
English Translation
O Nārada! He who recites these eight hymns to Yama early in the morning, getting up from his bed, is freed of the fear of death. Rather he becomes freed of all his sins. So much so, that even if he be a veritable awful sinner and if he recites daily with devotion this Yamāṣṭakam, Yama purifies him thoroughly. Here ends the Thirty-first Chapter of the Ninth Book on the Yama’s giving Śakti Mantra to Sāvitrī in the Mahāpurāṇam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

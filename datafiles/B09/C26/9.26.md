# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 9, CHAPTER 26

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 1-2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ९.२६ षड्विंशोऽध्यायः । सावित्रीपूजाविधिकथनम् ।
> 
> 
> नारद उवाच ।
> तुलस्युपाख्यानमिदं श्रुतं चातिसुधोपमम् ।
> ततः सावित्र्युपाख्यानं तन्मे व्याख्यातुमर्हसि ॥ १ ॥
> 
> पुरा केन समुद्भूता सा श्रुता च श्रुतेः प्रसूः ।
> केन वा पूजिता लोके प्रथमे कैश्च वा परे ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārada said :-- I have heard the anecdote of Tulasī. Now describe in detail the history of Sāvitrī. Sāvitrī is considered as the Mother of the Vedas. Why was She born, in days gone by? By whom was She first worshipped and subsequently also?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 3-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> श्रीनारायण उवाच ।
> ब्रह्मणा वेदजननी प्रथमे पूजिता मुने ।
> द्वितीये च वेदगणैस्तत्पश्चाद्विदुषां गणैः ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> तदा चाश्वपतिर्भूपः पूजयामास भारते ।
> तत्पश्चात्पूजयामासुर्वर्णाश्चत्वार एव च ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārāyaṇa said :-- O Muni! She was first worshipped by Brahmā. Next the Vedas worshipped her. Subsequently the learned men worshipped her. Next the King Aśvapati worshipped Her in India. Next the four Varṇas (castes) worshipped Her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नारद उवाच ।
> को वा सोऽश्वपतिर्ब्रह्मन् केन वा तेन पूजिता ।
> सर्वपूज्या च सा देवी प्रथमे कैश्च वा परे ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārada said :-- O Brāhmaṇ! Who is that Aśvapati? What for did he worship? When the Devī Sāvitrī became adorable by all, by which persons was She first worshipped and by which persons subsequently.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 6-7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> श्रीनारायण उवाच ।
> मद्रदेशे महाराजो बभूवाश्वपतिर्मुने ।
> वैरिणां बलहर्ता च मित्राणां दुःखनाशनः ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> आसीत्तस्य महाराज्ञी महिषी धर्मचारिणी ।
> मालतीति समाख्याता यथा लक्ष्मीर्गदाभृतः ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārāyaṇa said :-- O Muni! The King Aśvapati reigned in Bhadradeśa, rendering his enemies powerless and making his friends painless. He had a queen very religious; her name was Mālatī; She was like a second Lakṣmī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 8-9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सा च राज्ञी च वन्ध्या च वसिष्ठस्योपदेशतः ।
> चकाराराधनं भक्त्या सावित्र्याश्चैव नारद ॥ ८ ॥
> 
> प्रत्यादेशं न सा प्राप्ता महिषी न ददर्श ताम् ।
> गृहं जगाम दुःखार्ता हृदयेन विदूयता ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

She was barren; and desirous of an issue, She under the instruction of Vaśiṣṭha, duly worshipped Sāvitrī with devotion. But She did not receive any vision nor any command; therefore She returned home with a grievous heart.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 10-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> राजा तां दुःखितां दृष्ट्वा बोधयित्वा नयेन वै ।
> सावित्र्यास्तपसे भक्त्या जगाम पुष्करं तदा ॥ १० ॥
> 
> तपश्चकार तत्रैव संयतः शतवत्सरम् ।
> न ददर्श च सावित्र्या प्रत्यादेशो बभूव च ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Seeing her sorry, the king consoled her with good words and himself accompanied her to Puṣkara with a view to perform Tapas to Sāvitrī with devotion and, being self-controlled, practised tapasyā for one hundred years. Still he could not see Sāvitrī, but voice came to him.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 12-13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शुश्रावाकाशवाणीं च नृपेन्द्रश्चाशरीरिणीम् ।
> गायत्र्या दशलक्षं च जपं त्वं कुरु नारद ॥ १२ ॥
> 
> एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र आजगाम पराशरः ।
> प्रणनाम ततस्तं च मुनिर्नृपमुवाच च ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

An incorporeal, celestial voice reached his ears :-- “Perform Japam (repeat) ten lakhs of Gāyatrī Mantram.” At this moment Parāsara came up there. The king bowed down to him.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मुनिरुवाच ।
> सकृज्जपश्च गायत्र्याः पापं दिनभवं हरेत् ।
> दशवारं जपेनैव नश्येत्पापं दिवानिशम् ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Muni said :-- O King! One japa of Gāyatrī, destroys the days sins. Ten Japams of Gāyatrī destroy day and night’s sins.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 15-16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शतवारं जपश्चैव पापं मासार्जितं हरेत् ।
> सहस्रधा जपश्चैव कल्मषं वत्सरार्जितम् ॥ १५ ॥
> 
> लक्षो जन्मकृतं पापं दशलक्षोऽन्यजन्मजम् ।
> सर्वजन्मकृतं पापं शतलक्षाद्विनश्यति ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

One hundred Gāyatrī Japams destroy one month’s sins. One thousand Japams destroy one year’s sins. One lakh Gāyatrī Japams destroy the sins of the present birth and ten lakh Gāyatrī Japams destroy the sins of other births. One hundred lakhs of Japams destroy the sins of all the births. If ten times that (i.e., 1,000 lakhs) be done, then liberation is obtained.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 17-18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> करोति मुक्तिं विप्राणां जपो दशगुणस्ततः ।
> करं सर्पफणाकारं कृत्वा तद्रन्धमुद्रितम् ॥ १७ ॥
> 
> आनम्रमूर्धमचलं प्रजपेत्प्राङ्मुखो द्विजः ।
> अनामिकामध्यदेशादधोऽवामक्रमेण च ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

(Now the method, how to make Japam). Make the palm of the (right) hand like a snake’s hood; see that the fingers are all close, no holes are seen; and make the ends of the fingers bend downwards; then being calm and quiet and with one’s face eastward, practise Japam. Then count from the middle of the ring (name-less) finger and go on counting right-handed (with the hands of the watch) till you come to the bottom of the index finger. This is the rule of counting by the hand.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 19-21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तर्जनीमूलपर्यन्तं जपस्यैवं क्रमः करे ।
> श्वेतपङ्कजबीजानां स्फटिकानां च संस्कृताम् ॥ १९ ॥
> 
> कृत्वा वै मालिकां राजन् जपेत्तीर्थे सुरालये ।
> संस्थाप्य मालामश्वत्थपत्रे पद्मे च संयतः ॥ २० ॥
> 
> कृत्वा गोरोचनाक्तां च गायत्र्या स्नापयेत्सुधीः ।
> गायत्रीशतकं तस्यां जपेच्च विधिपूर्वकम् ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! The rosary is to be of the seed of white lotus or of the crystals; it should be consecrated and purified. Japam is to be done then in a sacred Tīrtha or in a temple. Becoming self-controlled one should place the rosary on a banyan leaf or on a lotus leaf and smear it with cowdung; wash it, uttering Gāyatrī Mantra and over it perform one hundred times Gāyatrī Japam intently in accordance with the rules.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 22-23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अथवा पञ्चगव्येन स्नात्वा मालां सुसंस्कृताम् ।
> अथ गङ्गोदकेनैव स्नात्वा वातिसुसंस्कृताम् ॥ २२ ॥
> 
> एवं क्रमेण राजर्षे दशलक्षं जपं कुरु ।
> साक्षाद्द्रक्ष्यसि सावित्रीं त्रिजन्मपातकक्षयात् ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Or wash it with Pañchagavya, milk, curds, clarified butter, cow urine and cowdung, and then consecrate it well. Then wash it with the Ganges water and perform best the consecrations. O Rājarṣi! Then perform ten lakhs of Japam in due order. Thus the sins of your three births will be destroyed and then you will see the Devī Sāvitrī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 24-25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नित्यं सन्ध्यां च हे राजन् करिष्यसि दिने दिने ।
> मध्याह्ने चापि सायाह्ने प्रातरेव शुचिः सदा ॥ २४ ॥
> 
> सन्ध्याहीनोऽशुचिर्नित्यमनर्हः सर्वकर्मसु ।
> यदह्ना कुरुते कर्म न तस्य फलभाग्भवेत् ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Do this Japam, being pure, everyday in the morning, mid-day, and in the evening. If one be impure and devoid of Sandhyā, one has no right to do any action; and even if one performs an action, one does not get any fruit thereby.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 26-27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नोपतिष्ठति यः पूर्वां नोपास्ते यस्तु पश्चिमाम् ।
> स शूद्रवद्बहिष्कार्यः सर्वस्माद् द्विजकर्मणः ॥ २६ ॥
> 
> यावज्जीवनपर्यन्तं त्रिःसन्ध्यां यः करोति च ।
> स च सूर्यसमो विप्रस्तेजसा तपसा सदा ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He who does not do the morning Sandhyā and the evening Sandhyā, is driven away from all the Brāhminic Karmas and he becomes like Śūdras. He who does Sandhyā three times throughout his life, becomes like the Sun by his lustre and brilliance of tapas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 28-29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तत्पादपद्मरजसा सद्यःपूता वसुन्धरा ।
> जीवन्मुक्तः स तेजस्वी सन्ध्यापूतो हि यो द्विजः ॥ २८ ॥
> 
> तीर्थानि च पवित्राणि तस्य संस्पर्शमात्रतः ।
> ततः पापानि यान्त्येव वैनतेयादिवोरगाः ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

What more than this, the earth is always purified by the dust of his feet. The Dvīja who does his Sandhyā Bandanam and remains pure, becomes energetic and liberated while living. By his contact all the Tīrthas become purified. All sins vanish away from him as snakes fly away at the sight of Garuḍa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 30-31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> न गृह्णन्ति सुराः पूजां पितरः पिण्डतर्पणम् ।
> स्वेच्छया च द्विजातेश्च त्रिसन्ध्यारहितस्य च ॥ ३० ॥
> 
> मूलप्रकृत्यभक्तो यस्तन्मन्त्रस्याप्यनर्चकः ।
> तदुत्सवविहीनश्च विषहीनो यथोरगः ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Dvīja who becomes void of Sandhyā three times a day, the Devas do not accept his worship nor the Pitris accept his Piṇḍas. He who has no Bhakti towards the Mūla Prakriti, who does not worship the specific seed Mantra of Māyā and who does not hold festivities in honour of Mūla Prakriti, know him verily to be an Ajagara snake without poison.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 32-33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विष्णुमन्त्रविहीनश्च त्रिसन्ध्यारहितो द्विजः ।
> एकादशीविहीनश्च विषहीनो यथोरगः ॥ ३२ ॥
> 
> हरेरनैवेद्यभोजी धावको वृषवाहकः ।
> शूद्रान्नभोजी यो विप्रो विषहीनो यथोरगः ॥ ३३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Devoid of the Viṣṇu mantra, devoid of the three Sandhyās and devoid of the fasting on the Ekādaśi Tithi (the eleventh clay of the fortnight), the Brāhmin becomes a snake devoid of poison. The vile Brāhmin who does not like to take the offerings dedicated to Hari and who does the washerman’s work and eats the food of Śūdra and drives the buffaloes, becomes a snake devoid of poison.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शूद्राणां शवदाही यः स विप्रो वृषलीपतिः ।
> शूद्राणां सूपकारश्च विषहीनो यथोरगः ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Brāhmin who burns the dead bodies of the Śūdras, becomes like the man who is the husband of an unmarried girl. The Brāhmin also who becomes a cook of a Śūdra, becomes a snake void of poison.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 35-36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शूद्राणां च प्रतिग्राही शूद्रयाजी च यो द्विजः ।
> मसिजीवी असिजीवी विषहीनो यथोरगः ॥ ३५ ॥
> 
> यः कन्याविक्रयी विप्रो यो हरेर्नामविक्रयी ।
> यो विप्रोऽवीरान्नभोजी ऋतुस्नातान्नभोजकः ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Brāhmin who accepts the gifts of a Śūdra, who performs the sacrifice of a Śūdra, who lives as clerks and warriors becomes like a snake void of poison. The Brāhmin who sells his daughter, who sells the name of Hari or eats the food of a woman who is without husband and son, as well as of one who has just bathed after her menstruation period, becomes like a serpent void of poison.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 37-38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भगजीवी बार्धुषिको विषहीनो यथोरगः ।
> यो विद्याविकयी विप्रो विषहीनो यथोरगः ॥ ३७ ॥
> 
> सूर्योदये स्वपेद्यो हि मत्स्यभोजी च यो द्विजः ।
> शिवापूजादिरहितो विषहीनो यथोरगः ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Brāhmin who takes the profession of pimps and pampers and lives on the interest, is also like a serpent void of poison. The Brāhmin who sleeps even when the Sun has risen, eats fish, and does not worship the Devī is also like a poisonless serpent.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 39-40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इत्युक्त्वा च मुनिश्रेष्ठः सर्वपूजाविधिक्रमम् ।
> तमुवाच च सावित्र्या ध्यानादिकमभीप्सितम् ॥ ३९ ॥
> 
> दत्त्वा सर्वं नृपेन्द्राय ययौ च स्वाश्रमे मुने ।

>
**English Translation:**

Thus stating all the rules of worship in order, the best of the Munis told him the Dhyānam, etc., of the Devī Sāvitrī, what he wanted. Then he informed the King of all the mantras and went to his own Āśrama. The king, then worshipped accordingly and saw the Devī Sāvitrī and got boons.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नारद उवाच ।
> किं वा ध्यानं च सावित्र्याः किं वा पूजाविधानकम् ।
> स्तोत्रं मन्त्रं च किं दत्त्वा प्रययौ स पराशरः ॥ ४१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārada said :-- What is the Sāvitrī’s Dhyān, what are the modes of her worship, what is stotra, mantra, that Parāśara gave to the King before he went away?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 42-43</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

>
**English Translation:**

And how did the King worship and what boon did he get? This great mystery, grand and well renowned in the Śrutis, about Sāvitrī, I am desirous to hear in brief on all the points.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 44</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> श्रीनारायण उवाच ।
> ज्येष्ठकृष्णत्रयोदश्यां शुद्धकाले च यत्नतः ।

>
**English Translation:**

Nārāyaṇa said :-- On the thirteenth day (the trayodaśi tithi) of the black fortnight in the month Jyaistha or on any other holy period, the fourteenth day (the caturdaśi tithi) this vow is to be observed with great care and devotion.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 26 Verse 45-46</b></summary>
  <br>

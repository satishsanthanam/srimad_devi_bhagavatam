# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 9, CHAPTER 47

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 1-2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ९.४७ सप्तचत्वारिंशोऽध्यायः । मङ्गलचण्डीमनसयोरुपाख्यानवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> श्रीनारायण उवाच ।
> कथितं षष्ठ्युपाख्यानं ब्रह्मपुत्र यथाऽऽगमम् ।
> देवी मङ्गलचण्डी च तदाख्यानं निशामय ॥ १ ॥
> 
> तस्याः पूजादिकं सर्वं धर्मवक्त्रेण यच्छ्रुतम् ।
> श्रुतिसम्मतमेवेष्टं सर्वेषां विदुषामपि ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārāyaṇa said :-- O Nārada! I have now narrated the anecdote of Ṣaṣṭhī as stated in the Vedas. Now hear the anecdote of Mangala Caṇḍī, approved of by the Vedas and respected by the literary persons. The Caṇḍī, that is very skilled in all auspicious works and who is the most auspicious of all good things, is Mangal Caṇḍīkā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दक्षा या वर्तते चण्डी कल्याणेषु च मङ्गला ।
> मङ्गलेषु च या दक्षा सा च मङ्गलचण्डिका ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Or the Caṇḍī who is an object of worship of Mangala (Mars), the son of earth and the bestower of desires is Mangala Caṇḍīkā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 4-5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पूज्या या वर्तते चण्डी मङ्गलोऽपि महीसुतः ।
> मङ्गलाभीष्टदेवी या सा वा मङ्गलचण्डिका ॥ ४ ॥
> 
> मङ्गलो मनुवंश्यश्च सप्तद्वीपधरापतिः ।
> तस्य पूज्याभीष्टदेवी तेन मङ्गलचण्डिका ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Or the Caṇḍī who is an object of worship of Mangala of the family of Manu who was the ruler of the whole world composed of seven islands and the bestower of all desires is Mangala Caṇḍī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 6-7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मूर्तिभेदेन सा दुर्गा मूलप्रकृतिरीश्वरी ।
> कृपारूपातिप्रत्यक्षा योषितामिष्टदेवता ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> प्रथमे पूजिता सा च शङ्करेण परात्परा ।
> त्रिपुरस्य वधे घोरे विष्णुना प्रेरितेन च ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Or it may be that the Mūla Prakriti, the Governess, the Ever Gracious Durgā assumed the form of Mangala Caṇḍī and has become the Iṣṭa Devatā of women. When there was the fight with Tripurāsura, this Mangala Caṇḍī, higher than the highest was first worshipped by Mahādeva, stimulated by Viṣṇu, on a critical moment.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 8-9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रह्मन् ब्रह्मोपदेशेन दुर्गतेन च सङ्कटे ।
> आकाशात्पतिते याने दैत्येन पातिते रुषा ॥ ८ ॥
> 
> ब्रह्मविष्णूपदिष्टश्च दुर्गां तुष्टाव शङ्करः ।
> सा च मङ्गलचण्डी या बभूव रूपभेदतः ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Brāhmin! While the fighting was going on, a Daitya threw out of anger one car on Mahādeva and as that car was about to fall on Him, Brahmā and Viṣṇu gave a good advice when Mahādeva began to praise Durgā Devī at once.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 10-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उवाच पुरतः शम्भोर्भयं नास्तीति ते प्रभो ।
> भगवान्वृषरूपश्च सर्वेशस्ते भविष्यति ॥ १० ॥
> 
> युद्धशक्तिस्वरूपाहं भविष्यामि न संशयः ।
> मायात्मना च हरिणा सहायेन वृषध्वज ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Durgā Devī that time assuming the form of Mangala Caṇḍī appeared and said “no fear no fear” Bhagavān Viṣṇu will be Thy Carrier buffalo. I will be also Thy Śakti in the action and Hari, full of Māyā, will also help Thee. Thou better slayest the enemy that dispossessed the Devas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जहि दैत्यं स्वशत्रुं च सुराणां पदघातकम् ।
> इत्युक्त्वान्तर्हिता देवी शम्भोः शक्तिर्बभूव सा ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Child! Thus saying, the Devī Mangala Chandī disappeared and She became the Śakti of Mahā Deva. Then with the help of the weapon given by Viṣṇu, the Lord of Umā killed the Asura.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 13-15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विष्णुदत्तेन शस्त्रेण जघान तमुमापतिः ।
> मुनीन्द्र पतिते दैत्ये सर्वे देवा महर्षयः ॥ १३ ॥
> 
> तुष्टुवुः शङ्करं देवं भक्तिनम्रात्मकन्धराः ।
> सद्यः शिरसि शम्भोश्च पुष्पवृष्टिर्बभूव ह ॥ १४ ॥
> 
> ब्रह्मा विष्णुश्च सन्तुष्टो ददौ तस्मै शुभाशिषम् ।
> ब्रह्मविष्णूपदिष्टश्च सुस्नातः शङ्करस्तथा ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When the Daitya fell, the Devas and Ṛṣis began to chant hymns to Mahādeva with devotion and with their heads bent low. From the sky, a shower of flowers fell instantaneously on Mahā Deva’s head. Brahmā and Viṣṇu became glad and gave their best wishes to Him. Then ordered by Brahmā and Viṣṇu, Śaṅkara bathed joyously.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 16-18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पूजयामास तां भक्त्या देवीं मङ्गलचण्डिकाम् ।
> पाद्यार्घ्याचमनीयैश्च वस्त्रैश्च विविधैरपि ॥ १६ ॥
> 
> पुष्पचन्दननैवेद्यैर्भक्त्या नानाविधैर्मुने ।
> छागैर्मेषैश्च महिषैर्गवयैः पक्षिभिस्तथा ॥ १७ ॥
> 
> वस्त्रालङ्कारमाल्यैश्च पायसैः पिष्टकैरपि ।
> मधुभिश्च सुधाभिश्च फलैर्नानाविधैरपि ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then He began to worship with devotion the Devī Mangala Caṇḍī with pādya, Arghya, Ācamanīya and various clothings. Flowers, sandal paste, various goats, sheep, buffaloes, bisons, birds, garments, ornaments, garlands, Pāyasa (a preparation of rice, ghee, milk and sugar), Piṣṭaka, honey, wine, and various fruits were offered in the worship.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 19-20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सङ्गीतैर्नर्तकैर्वाद्यैरुत्सवैर्नामकीर्तनैः ।
> ध्यात्वा माध्यन्दिनोक्तेन ध्यानेन भक्तिपूर्वकम् ॥ १९ ॥
> 
> ददौ द्रव्याणि मूलेन मन्त्रेणैव च नारद ।
> ॐ ह्रीं श्रीं क्लीं सर्वपूज्ये देवि मङ्गलचण्डिके ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Dancing, music, with instruments and the chanting of Her name and other festivals commenced. Reciting the Dhyān as in Mādhyandina, Mahādeva offered everything, pronouncing the principal Radical Mantra, “Om Hrīm Śrīm Klīm Sarvapujye Devī Mangala Caṇḍīke Hum Phaṭ Svāhā” is the twenty-one lettered Mantra of Mangala Caṇḍī. During worship, the Kalpa Vrikṣa, the tree yielding all desires, must be worshipped.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 21-22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> हूँ हूँ फट् स्वाहाप्येकविंशाक्षरो मनुः ।
> पूज्यः कल्पतरुश्चैव भक्तानां सर्वकामदः ॥ २१ ॥
> 
> दशलक्षजपेनैव मन्त्रसिद्धिर्भवेद् ध्रुवम् ।
> ध्यानं च श्रूयतां ब्रह्मन् वेदोक्तं सर्वसम्मतम् ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Nārada! By repeating the Mantra ten lakhs of times, the Mantra Siddhi (success in realising the Deity inherent in the Mantra) comes. Now I am saying about the Dhyānam of Mangal Caṇḍī as stated in the Vedas and as approved by all. Listen.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 23-24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देवीं षोडशवर्षीयां शश्वत्सुस्थिरयौवनाम् ।
> बिम्बोष्ठीं सुदतीं शुद्धां शरत्पद्मनिभाननाम् ॥ २३ ॥
> 
> श्वेतचम्पकवर्णाभां सुनीलोत्पललोचनाम् ।
> जगद्धात्रीं च दात्रीं च सर्वेभ्यः सर्वसम्पदाम् ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

“O Devī Mangala Caṇḍīke! Thou art sixteen years old; Thou art ever youthful; Thy lips are like Bimba fruits, Thou art of good teeth and pure. Thy face looks like autumnal lotus; Thy colour is like white champakas; Thy eyes resemble blue lilies; Thou art the Preserver of the world and thou bestowest all sorts of prosperity.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> संसारसागरे घोरे ज्योतीरूपां सदा भजे ।
> देव्याश्च ध्यानमित्येवं स्तवनं श्रूयतां मुने ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thou art the Light in this dark ocean of the world. So I meditate on Thee.” This is the Dhyānam. Now hear the stotra, which Mahādeva recited before Her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 26-27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> महादेव उवाच ।
> रक्ष रक्ष जगन्मातर्देवि मङ्गलचण्डिके ।
> हारिके विपदां राशेर्हर्षमङ्गलकारिके ॥ २६ ॥
> 
> हर्षमङ्गलदक्षे च हर्षमङ्गलदायिके ।
> शुभे मङ्गलदक्षे च शुभे मङ्गलचण्डिके ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Mahādeva said :-- Protect me, Protect me, O Mother! O Devī Mangal Caṇḍīke! Thou, the Destroyer of difficulties! Thou givest joy and good. Thou art clever in giving delight and fortune. Thou the bestower of all bliss and prosperity! Thou, the auspicious, Thou art Mangala Caṇḍīkā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 28-29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मङ्गले मङ्गलार्हे च सर्वमङ्गलमङ्गले ।
> सतां मङ्गदे देवि सर्वेषां मङ्गलालये ॥ २८ ॥
> 
> पूज्ये मङ्गलवारे च मङ्गलाभीष्टदेवते ।
> पूज्ये मङ्गलभूपस्य मनुवंशस्य सन्ततम् ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thou art Mangalā, worthy of all good, Thou art the auspicious of all auspicious; Thou bestowest good to the good persons. Thou art worthy to be worshipped on Tuesday (the Mangala day); Thou art the Deity, desired by all. The King Mangala, born of Manu family always worships Thee.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 30-32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मङ्गलाधिष्ठातृदेवि मङ्गलानां च मङ्गले ।
> संसारमङ्गलाधारे मोक्षमङ्गलदायिनि ॥ ३० ॥
> 
> सारे च मङ्गलाधारे पारे च सर्वकर्मणाम् ।
> प्रतिमङ्गलवारे च पूज्ये मङ्गसुखप्रदे ॥ ३१ ॥
> 
> स्तोत्रेणानेन शम्भुश्च स्तुत्वा मङ्गलचण्डिकाम् ।
> प्रतिमङ्गलवारे च पूजां दत्त्वा गतः शिवः ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thou, the presiding Devī of Mangala; Thou art the repository of all the good that are in this world. Thou, the Bestower of the auspicious Mokṣa. Thou, the best of all; Thou, the respository of all good; Thou makest one cross all the Karmas; the people worship Thee on every Tuesday; Thou bestowest abundance of Bliss to all.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 33-34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> प्रथमे पूजिता देवी शिवेन सर्वमङ्गला ।
> द्वितीये पूजिता सा च मङ्गलेन ग्रहेण च ॥ ३३ ॥
> 
> तृतीते पूजिता भद्रा मङ्गलेन नृपेण च ।
> चतुर्थे मङ्गले वारे सुन्दरीभिः प्रपूजिता ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus praising Mangala Caṇḍīkā with this stotra, and worshipping on every Tuesday, Śambhu departed. The Devī Sarva Mangalā was first worshipped by Mahādeva.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 35-36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पञ्चमे मङ्गलाकाङ्क्षिनरैर्मङ्गलचण्डिका ।
> पूजिता प्रतिविश्वेषु विश्वेशपूजिता सदा ॥ ३५ ॥
> 
> ततः सर्वत्र सम्पूज्या बभूव परमेश्वरी ।

>
**English Translation:**

Next she was worsh ipped by the planet Mars; then by the King Mangala; then on every Tuesday by the ladies of every household. Fifthly she was worshipped by all men, desirous of their welfare. So in every universe Mangal Caṇḍīkā, first worshipped by Mahādeva, came to be worshipped by all. Next she came to be worshipped everywhere, by the Devas, Munis, Mānavas, Manus.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 37</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देव्याश्च मङ्गस्तोत्रं यः शृणोति समाहितः ।
> तन्मङ्गलं भवेत्तस्य न भवेत्तदमङ्गलम् ।

>
**English Translation:**

O Muni! He who hears with undivided attention this stotra of the Devī Mangala Caṇḍīkā, finds no evils anywhere. Rather all good comes to him. Day after day he gets sons and grandsons and so his prosperity gets increased, yea, verily increased!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> श्रीनारायण उवाच ।
> उक्तं द्वयोरुपाख्यानं ब्रह्मपुत्र यथागमम् ।

>
**English Translation:**

Nārāyaṇa said :-- O Nārada! Thus I narrated to you the stories of Ṣaṣṭhī and Mangala Caṇḍīkā, according to the Vedas. Now hear the story of Manasā that I heard from the mouth of Dharama Deva.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 39-40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सा च कन्या भगवती कश्यपस्य च मानसी ।
> तेनैव मनसा देवी मनसा या च दीव्यति ॥ ३९ ॥
> 
> मनसा ध्यायते या च परमात्मानमीश्वरम् ।
> तेन सा मनसा देवी तेन योगेन दीव्यति ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Manasā is the mind-born daughter of Maharṣi Kaśyapa; hence she is named Manasā; or it may be She who plays with the mind is Manasā. Or it may be She who meditates on God with her mind and gets rapture in Her meditation of God is named Manasā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 41-42</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> आत्मारामा च सा देवी वैष्णवी सिद्धयोगिनी ।
> त्रियुगं च तपस्तप्त्वा कृष्णस्य परमात्मनः ॥ ४१ ॥
> 
> जरत्कारुशरीरं च दृष्ट्वा यत्क्षीणमीश्वरः ।
> गोपीपतिर्नाम चक्रे जरत्कारुरिति प्रभुः ॥ ४२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

She finds pleasure in Her Own Self, the great devotee of Viṣṇu, a Siddha Yoginī. For three Yugas She worshipped Śrī Kṛṣṇa and then She became a Siddha Yoginī. Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the Lord of the Gopīs, seeing the body of Manasā lean and thin due to austerities, or seeing her worn out like the Muni Jarat Kāru called her by the name of Jarat Kāru.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 43-44</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वाञ्छितं च ददौ तस्यै कृपया च कृपानिधिः ।
> पूजां च कारयामास चकार च स्वयं प्रभुः ॥ ४३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hence Her name has come also to be Jarat Kāru. Kṛṣṇa, the Ocean of Mercy, gave her out of kindness, Her desired boon; She worshipped Him and Śrī Kṛṣṇa also worshipped Her. Devī Manasā is known in the Heavens, in the abode of the Nāgas (serpents), in earth, in Brahmāloka, in all the worlds as of very fair colour, beautiful and charming.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 45</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्वर्गे च नागलोके च पृथिव्यां ब्रह्मलोकतः ।
> भृशं जगत्सु गौरी सा सुन्दरी च मनोहरा । ४४
> जगद्गौरीति विख्याता तेन सा पूजिता सती ।
> शिवशिष्या च सा देवी तेन शैवी प्रकीर्तिता ॥ ४५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

She is named Jagad Gaurī as she is of a very fair colour in the world. Her other name is Śaivī and she is the disciple of Śiva. She is named Vaiṣṇavī as she is greatly devoted to Viṣṇu.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 46-48</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विष्णुभक्तातीव शश्वद्वैष्णवी तेन कीर्तिता ।
> नागानां प्राणरक्षित्री यज्ञे पारीक्षितस्य च ॥ ४६ ॥
> 
> नागेश्वरीति विख्याता सा नागभगिनीति च ।
> विषं संहर्तुमीशा या तेन विषहरी स्मृता ॥ ४७ ॥
> 
> सिद्धयोगं हरात्प्राप तेन सा सिद्धयोगिनी ।
> महाज्ञानं च योगं च मृतसज्जीवनीं पराम् ॥ ४८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

She saved the Nāgas in the Snake Sacrifice performed by Pariksit, she is named Nageśvarī and Nāga Bhaginī and She is capable to destroy the effects of poison. She is called Viṣahari. She got the Siddha yoga from Mahādeva; hence She is named Siddha Yoginī; She got from Him the great knowledge, so she is called Mahā Jñanayutā, and as she got Mritasamjīvanī (making alive the dead) she is known by the name of Mritasañjīvanī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 49-52</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> महाज्ञानयुतां तां च प्रवदन्ति मनीषिणः ।
> आस्तीकस्य मुनीन्द्रस्य माता सापि तपस्विनी ॥ ४९ ॥
> 
> आस्तीकमाता विज्ञाता जगत्यां सुप्रतिष्ठिता ।
> प्रिया मुनेर्जरत्कारोर्मुनीन्द्रस्य महात्मनः ॥ ५० ॥
> 
> योगिनो विश्वपूज्यस्य जरत्कारुप्रिया ततः ।
> जरत्कारुर्जगद्गौरी मनसा सिद्धयोगिनी ॥ ५१ ॥
> 
> वैष्णवी नागभगिनी शैवी नागेश्वरी तथा ।
> जरत्कारुप्रियास्तीकमाता विषहरेति च ॥ ५२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

As the great ascetic is the mother of the great Muni Āstīk, she is known in the world as Āstīka mātā. As She is the dear wife of the great high-souled Yogi Muni Jarat Kāru, worshipped by all, she is called as Jarat Kārupriya, Jaratkāru, Jagadgaurī, Manasā, Siddha Yoginī, Vaiṣṇavī, Nāga Bhaginī, Śaivi, Nageśvarī, Jaratkārupriyā, Āstikamātā, Viṣahari, and Mahā Jñanayutā; these are the twelve names of Manasā, worshipped everywhere in the Universe.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 53-55</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> महाज्ञानयुता चैव सा देवी विश्वपूजिता ।
> द्वादशैतानि नामानि पूजाकाले तु यः पठेत् ॥ ५३ ॥
> 
> तस्य नागभयं नास्ति तस्य वंशोद्भवस्य च ।
> नागभीते च शयने नागग्रस्ते च मन्दिरे ॥ ५४ ॥
> 
> नागशोभे महादुर्गे नागवेष्टितविग्रहे ।
> इदं स्तोत्रं पठित्वा तु मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ५५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He who recites these twelve names while worshipping Manasā Devī, he or any of his family has no fear of snakes. If there be any fear of snakes in one’s bed, if the house be infested with snakes or if one goes to a place difficult for fear of snakes or if one’s body be encircled with snakes, all the fears are dispelled, if one reads this stotra of Manasā. There is no doubt in this. The snakes run away out of fear from the sight of him who daily recites the Manasā stotra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 47 Verse 56-58</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नित्यं पठेद्यस्तं दृष्ट्वा नागवर्गः पलायते ।
> दशलक्षजपेनैव स्तोत्रसिद्धिर्भवेन्नृणाम् ॥ ५६ ॥
> 
> स्तोत्रसिद्धिर्भवेद्यस्य स विषं भोक्तुमीश्वरः ।
> नागैश्च भूषणं कृत्वा स भवेन्नागवाहनः ॥ ५७ ॥
> 
> नागासनो नागतल्पो महासिद्धो भवेन्नरः ।
> अन्ते च विष्णुना सार्धं क्रीडत्येव दिवानिशम् ॥ ५८ ॥
>
> *Colophon:*
> इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां
> संहितायां नवमस्कन्धे नारायणनारदसंवादे
> मङ्गलचण्डीमनसयोरुपाख्यानवर्णनं
> नाम सप्तचत्वारिंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ९.४७॥
>
**English Translation:**

Ten lakhs of times repeating the Manasā mantra give one man success in the stotra. He can easily drink poison who attains success in this stotra. The snakes become his ornaments; they carry him even on their backs. He who is a great Siddha can sit on a seat of snakes and can sleep on a bed of snakes. In the end he sports day and night with Viṣṇu. Here ends the Forty-seventh Chapter of the Ninth Book on Manasā’s story in the Mahā Purāṇam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

</details>

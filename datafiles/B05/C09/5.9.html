BOOK 5 • CHAPTER 9
On the worship by the gods to the Devī

Ch 9 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
५.९ नवमोऽध्यायः । महिषमन्त्रिणा देवीवार्तावर्णनम् । व्यास उवाच । देवा विष्णुवचः श्रुत्वा सर्वे प्रमुदितास्तदा । ददुश्च भूषणान्याशु वस्त्राणि स्वायुधानि च ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- On hearing Viṣṇu’s words, the Devas became very glad and presented immediately their own weapons, ornaments and clothings.
Ch 9 Verse 2 Verse
Sanskrit Text
क्षीरोदश्चाम्बरे दिव्ये रक्ते सूक्ष्मे तथाजरे । निर्मलञ्च तथा हारं प्रीतस्तस्मै सुमण्डितम् ॥ २ ॥
English Translation
The Kṣiroda (Milk) Ocean presented to Her gladly, the well fitted necklace, clear as crystal, and a pair of divine cloths, of a red colour, never becoming old and very fine.
Ch 9 Verse 3-4 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ददौ चूडामणिं दिव्यं सूर्यकोटिसमप्रभम् । कुण्डले च तथा शुभ्रे कटकानि भुजेषु वै ॥ ३ ॥ केयूरान्कङ्कणान्दिव्यान्नानारत्नविराजितान् । ददौ तस्यै विश्वकर्मा प्रसन्नेन्द्रियमानसः ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation
Viśvakarmā was very much gratified in his heart and presented a divine jewel to be worn in Her diadem or crest blazing like hundreds of suns; white earrings; bracelets for Her wrist, bracelets for Her upper arm, and other bracelets decked with various gems and jewels and anklets brilliant like gems, of a clear Sun-like lustre, decked with jewels, and tinkling nicely.
Ch 9 Verse 5-6 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नूपुरौ सुस्वरौ कान्तौ निर्मलौ रत्नभूषितौ । ददौ सूर्यप्रतीकाशौ त्वष्टा तस्यै सुपादयोः ॥ ५ ॥ तथा ग्रैवेयकं रम्यं ददौ तस्यै महार्णवः । अङ्गुलीयकरत्नानि तेजोवन्ति च सर्वशः ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation
The architect of the gods, the ocean of intellect, Viśvakarmā gave Her as offerings beautiful ornaments also for the neck, all very beautiful, as well as for the fingers decked with gems and jewels, all shining splendidly.
Ch 9 Verse 7 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अम्लानपङ्कजां मालां गन्धाढ्यां भ्रमरानुगाम् । तथैव वैजयन्तीञ्च वरुणः सम्प्रयच्छत ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
Varuṇa gave for Her head garland of lotuses, never fading away, of such a sweet fragrance as bees constantly hover round them and the Vaijayantī garland for Her breast.
Ch 9 Verse 8 Verse
Sanskrit Text
हिमवानथ सन्तुष्टो रत्नानि विविधानि च । ददौ च वाहनं सिंहं कनकाभं मनोहरम् ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
The mountain Himālyā gladly offered Her various gems and a beautiful lion, of a golden colour for Her conveyance.
Ch 9 Verse 9 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भूषणैर्भूषिता दिव्यैः सा रराज वरा शुभा । सिंहारूढा वरारोहा सर्वलक्षणसंयुता ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation
Then that beautiful Lady, having all the auspicious signs, wishing welfare to all, and decorated with the divine ornaments began to look grand and splendid, mounted on Her conveyance, the Lion.
Ch 9 Verse 10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विष्णुश्चक्रात्समुत्पाद्य ददावस्यै रथाङ्गकम् । सहस्रारं सुदीप्तञ्च देवारिशिरसां हरम् ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
Viṣṇu then created another thousand spoked discus (Cakram) from His own Cakra, capable to take off the head of any Asura, and offered it to Her.
Ch 9 Verse 11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्वत्रिशूलात्समुत्पाद्य शङ्करः शूलमुत्तमम् । ददौ देव्यै सुरारीणां कृन्तनं भयनाशनम् ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
Śaṅkara created another excellent Triśūla from his own Trident, terrible and demon-killing, and offered it to the Devī.
Ch 9 Verse 12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वरुणश्च प्रसन्नात्मा ददौ शङ्खं समुज्वलम् । घोषवन्तं स्वशङ्खात्तु समुत्पाद्य सुमङ्गलम् ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
Varuṇa created another bright conch from his own conch and offered it gladly to the Devī.
Ch 9 Verse 13 Verse
Sanskrit Text
हुताशनस्तथा शक्तिं शतघ्नीं सुमनोजवाम् । प्रायच्छत्तु प्रसन्नात्मा तस्यै दैत्यविनाशिनीम् ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation
Fire offered Her a weapon named Śataghni which kills violently the demons, as if that is another god of death.
Ch 9 Verse 14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इषुधिं बाणपूर्णञ्च चापं चाद्भुतदर्शनम् । मारुतो दत्तवांस्तस्यै दुराकर्षं खरस्वरम् ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
Maruta (wind), the chief of the gods, offered Her a wonderful bow and arrow case filled with arrows. The bow can be drawn with great difficulty and emits a very harsh sound.
Ch 9 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्ववज्राद्वज्रमुत्पाद्य ददाविन्द्रोऽतिदारुणम् । घण्टामैरावतात्तूर्णं सुशब्दां चातिसुन्दराम् ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
Indra created another dreadful thunderbolt from his own thunderbolt and gave it at once to the Devī; as well the beautiful sonorous bell that used to hang from the elephant Airāvata.
Ch 9 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ददौ दण्डं यमः कामं कालदण्डसमुद्भवम् । येनान्तं सर्वभूतानामकरोत्काल आगते ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
Yama, the God of Death, created another beautiful staff from his own sceptre which takes away when time comes, the life of all beings.
Ch 9 Verse 17 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ब्रह्मा कमण्डलुं दिव्यं गङ्गावारिप्रपूरितम् । ददावस्यै मुदा युक्तो वरुणः पाशमेव च ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation
Brahmā gladly gave Her a divine Kamandalu, filled with the Ganges water; and Varuṇa offered Her a weapon called Pāśa.
Ch 9 Verse 18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कालः खड्गं तथा चर्म प्रायच्छत्तु नराधिप । परशुं विश्वकर्मा च तीक्ष्णमस्यै ददावथ ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
O King! Time gave Her an axe and a shield and Viśvakarmā gave Her a sharp Paraśu.
Ch 9 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
धनदस्तु सुरापूर्णं पानपात्रं सुवर्णजम् । पङ्कजं वरुणश्चादाद्देव्यै दिव्यं मनोहरम् ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
Kuvera, the Lord of wealth, gave her a golden drinking cup, filled with wine; and Vāruṇa offered Her a divine beautiful lotus.
Ch 9 Verse 20-21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गदां कौमोदकीं त्वष्टा घण्टाशतनिनादिनीम् । अदात्तस्यै प्रसन्नात्मा सुरशत्रुविनाशिनीम् ॥ २० ॥ अस्त्राण्यनेकरूपाणि तथाभेद्यञ्च दंशनम् । ददौ त्वष्टा जगन्मात्रे निजरश्मीन्दिवाकरः ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
Viśvakarmā became very glad and gave Her the Kaumodakī gadā, capable to kill the enemy of the gods and whence hundreds of bells are hanging, an impenetrable armour and various other weapons. The Sun gave to the Divine Mother his own rays.
Ch 9 Verse 22 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सायुधां भूषणैर्युक्तां दृष्ट्वा ते विस्मयं गताः । तुष्टुवुस्तां सुरा देवीं त्रैलोक्यमोहिनीं शिवाम् ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation
The Devas, seeing Her adorned with ornaments and weapons, began to praise and chant hymns to that most Auspicious Goddess, the Great Enchantress of the three worlds.
Ch 9 Verse 23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवा ऊचुः । नमः शिवायै कल्याण्यै शान्त्यै पुष्ट्यै नमो नमः । भगवत्यै नमो देव्यै रुद्राण्यै सततं नमः ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
The Deva said :-- “Salutation to Śiva, Salutation to the Most Auspicious; Thou art peace and nourishment; we salute again and again to Thee. Salutation to Thee, the Bhagavatī Devī; Thou art the Goddess Rudrānī (the terrible), we always salute again and again to Thee.
Ch 9 Verse 24 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कालरात्र्यै तथाम्बायै इन्द्राण्यै ते नमो नमः । सिद्ध्यै बुद्ध्यै तथा वृद्ध्यै वैष्णव्यै ते नमो नमः ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation
Thou art the Kālarātri (the night of destruction at the end of the world); Thou art the Indrānī. Thou art the Mother, we salute again and again to Thee; Thou art the success,
Ch 9 Verse 25 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पृथिव्यां या स्थिता पृथ्व्या न ज्ञाता पृथिवीञ्च या । अन्तःस्थिता यमयति वन्दे तामीश्वरीं पराम् ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation
Thou art within the earth; yet the earth does know know Thee. Thou art again the inmost of the earth and controllest the things within this earth; we offer our salutations to that Supreme Cause,
Ch 9 Verse 26 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मायायां या स्थिता ज्ञाता मायया न च तामजाम् । अन्तःस्थिता प्रेरयति प्रेरयित्रीं नुमः शिवाम् ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation
Thou art within this Māyā (the unborn) yet the Māyā does not know Thee. Thou residest again within the innermost of the Māyā and directest that Unborn One, the Māyā, we salute again and again to that Supreme Cause, the Great Directress, the Śivā (the most auspicious).
Ch 9 Verse 27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कल्याणं कुरु भो मातस्त्राहि नः शत्रुतापितान् । जहि पापं हयारिं त्वं तेजसा स्वेन मोहितम् ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! Do what is good to us; we are oppressed by our enemy, dost then protect us; by Thy own power dost Thou overpower and kill that Mahiṣa.
Ch 9 Verse 28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
खलं मायाविनं घोरं स्त्रीवध्यं वरदर्पितम् । दुःखदं सर्वदेवानां नानारूपधरं शतम् ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
That demon is vulnerable by woman only, he is deceitful, cunning, dreadful, and swollen with pride on his having got the blessing; he assumes many forms and torments the Devas.
Ch 9 Verse 29 Verse
Sanskrit Text
त्वमेका सर्वदेवानां शरणं भक्तवत्सले । पीडितान्दानवेनाद्य त्राहि देवि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation
O One, devoted to the Bhaktas! Thou art the only refuge of all the gods; O Thou art the supreme goddess, we are very much harassed and oppressed by the Dānava; therefore dost Thou now protect us; we bow down to Thee.”
Ch 9 Verse 30 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । एवं स्तुता तदा देवी सुरैः सर्वसुखप्रदा । तानुवाच महादेवी स्मितपूर्वं शुभं वचः ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- When the Devas had praised thus, the Highest Goddess, the Giver of all happiness, then smilingly said in the following auspicious terms :--
Ch 9 Verse 31 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देव्युवाच । भयं त्यजन्तु गीर्वाणा महिषान्मन्दचेतसः । हनिष्यामि रणेऽद्यैव वरदृप्तं विमोहितम् ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation
“O Devas! Today in the battle ground I will overpower that wicked Mahiṣa, of cruel disposition and take away his life.”
Ch 9 Verse 32 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । इत्युक्त्वा सा सुरान्देवी जहासातीव सुस्वरम् । चित्रमेतच्च संसारे भ्रममोहयुतं जगत् ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- Speaking thus in a melodious voice, the Supreme One smiled and again said :-- “This world is all full of error and delusion.
Ch 9 Verse 33 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेशाद्याः सेन्द्राश्चान्ये सुरास्तथा । कम्पयुक्ता भयत्रस्ता वर्तन्ते महिषात्किल ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation
Really, it is very wonderful that Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Indra and other gods are all shuddering out of fear from Mahiṣa Dānava.
Ch 9 Verse 34 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अहो दैवबलं घोरं दुर्जयं सुरसत्तमाः । कालः कर्तास्ति दुःखानां सुखानां प्रभुरीश्वरः ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation
The power of Destiny is exceedingly great and terrible; its influence cannot be overcome even by the best of the Devas.
Ch 9 Verse 35 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सृष्टिपालनसंहारे समर्था अपि ते यदा । मुह्यन्ति क्लेशसन्तप्ता महिषेण प्रपीडिताः ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation
O king! The Time is the Lord of happiness and pain; Time is, therefore, the God. The wonder is this that even those who can create, preserve and destroy this world, they are being overpowered and tormented by Mahiṣa.
Ch 9 Verse 36 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इति कृत्वा स्मितं देवी साट्टहासं चकार ह । उच्चैः शब्दं महाघोरं दानवानां भयप्रदम् ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation
The Devī, thinking thus, smiled; then laughed and laughed very hoarsely; it seemed that a roar of laughter then arose.
Ch 9 Verse 37 Verse
Sanskrit Text
चकम्पे वसुधा तत्र श्रुत्वा तच्छब्दमद्भुतम् । चेलुश्च पर्वताः सर्वे चुक्षोभाब्धिश्च वीर्यवान् ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation
And the Dānavas were struck with terror at that very dreadful sound. The earth trembled at that extraordinary sound; the mountains began to move and the vast oceans that remained calm began to be agitated with billows.
Ch 9 Verse 38 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मेरुश्चचाल शब्देन दिशः सर्वाः प्रपूरिताः । भयं जग्मुस्तदा श्रुत्वा दानवास्तत्स्वनं महत् ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation
The uproar filled all the quarters and the mountain Meru trembled. Then the Dānavas, hearing the tumultuous uproar, were all filled with tremendous fear.
Ch 9 Verse 39 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जय पाहीति देवास्तामूचुः परमहर्षिताः । महिषोऽपि स्वनं श्रुत्वा चुकोप मदगर्वितः ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation
The Devas became very glad and said thus :-- “O Devī! Let victory be Yours; save us.” The intoxicated Mahiṣa, too, hearing those words, became very angry.
Ch 9 Verse 40 Verse
Sanskrit Text
किमेतदिति तान्दैत्यान्पप्रच्छ स्वनशङ्कितः । गच्छन्तु त्वरिता दूता ज्ञातुं शब्दसमुद्भवम् ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation
Mahiṣa, struck with terror at those words, asked the Daityas “O Messengers! Go and ascertain how has originated this sound.
Ch 9 Verse 41 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कृतः केनायमत्युग्रः शब्दः कर्णव्यथाकरः । देवो वा दानवो वापि यो भवेत्स्वनकारकः ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation
Who has made this harsh sound? Bring that devil who has made this hoarse noise, be he a Deva, Dānava, or anyone else unto me and I will kill that roaring villain, who, it seems, has been puffed with egoism and vanity.
Ch 9 Verse 42 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गृहीत्वा तं दुरात्मानं मत्समीपं नयन्त्विह । हनिष्यामि दुराचारं गर्जन्तं स्मयदुर्मदम् ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation
The Devas are not making this noise, for they are vanquished and terror-stricken; The Asuras are not doing so, for they are my subjects; then, who is the stupid fellow that has done so?
Ch 9 Verse 43 Verse
Sanskrit Text
क्षीणायुष्यं मन्दमतिं नयामि यमसादनम् । पराजिताः सुराः कामं न गर्जन्ति भयातुराः ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation
Surely he is of very little understanding; his days are numbered; and I will carry him to the home of Death.
Ch 9 Verse 44 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नासुरा मम वश्यास्ते कस्येदं मूढचेष्टितम् । त्वरिता मामुपायान्तु ज्ञात्वा शब्दस्य कारणम् ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation
Go you, ascertain the cause of sound and come back to me; then I will go there and destroy that wretch who made this noise to no purpose.”
Ch 9 Verse 45-46 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अहं गत्वा हनिष्यामि तं पापं वितथश्रमम् । व्यास उवाच । इत्युक्तास्तेन ते दूता देवीं सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरीम् ॥ ४५ ॥ अष्टादशभुजां दिव्यां सर्वाभरणभूषिताम् । सर्वलक्षणसम्पन्नां वरायुधधरां शुभाम् ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- No sooner the messengers heard these words of Mahiṣa, than they at once went to the Devī and saw that Her body and the several parts thereof were all very beautiful; She had eighteen hands, She was decorated completely with various ornaments all over Her body, all the auspicious signs were being seen in Her body and that She was holding excellent divine weapons. That auspicious Goddess beautiful, was holding in Her hands, the cup and drinking wine again and again.
Ch 9 Verse 47 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दधतीं चषकं हस्ते पिबन्तीं च मुहुर्मधु । संवीक्ष्य भयभीतास्ते जग्मुस्त्रस्ताः सुशङ्किताः ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation
Beholding Her this form, they were afraid and fled at once to the Mahiṣa and informed him the cause of that sound.
Ch 9 Verse 48-49 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सकाशे महिषस्याशु तमूचुः स्वनकारणम् । दूता ऊचुः । देवी दैत्येश्वर प्रौढा दृश्यते काचिदङ्गना ॥ ४८ ॥ सर्वाङ्गभूषणा नारी सर्वरत्नोपशोभिता । न मानुषी नासुरी सा दिव्यरूपा मनोहरा ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation
The Daityas said :-- “O Lord! We have seen one grown up woman; whose whereabouts we are quite ignorant. The Devī is decorated with jewels and ornaments all over Her body; She is not human nor Asurī but Her form is extraordinary and beautiful.
Ch 9 Verse 50-51 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सिंहारूढायुधधरा चाष्टादशकरा वरा । सा नादं कुरुते नारी लक्ष्यते मदगर्विता ॥ ५० ॥ सुरापानरता कामं जानीमो न सभर्तृका । अन्तरिक्षस्थिता देवास्तां स्तुवन्ति मुदान्विताः ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation
That noble Lady is mounted on a lion, holding weapons on all Her eighteen hands and is roaring loudly; She is drinking wine; so it seems that She is puffed up with liquor. It is quite certain that She has no husband.
Ch 9 Verse 52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जयेति पाहि नश्चेति जहि शत्रुमिति प्रभो । न जाने का वरारोहा कस्य वा सा परिग्रहः ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation
The Devas are gladly chanting praises from the celestial space that Let Victory be to Her side and that She save the Devas, O Lord! We don’t know at all who is that handsome woman? or whose wife is she; why has she come there? and what is Her motive?
Ch 9 Verse 53 Verse
Sanskrit Text
किमर्थमागता चात्र किं चिकीर्षति सुन्दरी । द्रष्टुं नैव समर्थाः स्मस्तत्तेजःपरिधर्षिताः ॥ ५३ ॥
English Translation
Sentiments of love, heroism, laughter, terror and wonder are all fully shining in Her; therefore we are very much overpowered by the halo emitted from Her; and we could not even see Her well. Note :-- Rasas means sentiments. The rasas are usually eight. Sriṅgāra, Hāsya, Karuṇā, Raudra, Vīra, Bhayānakāh, Bibhatsādbhū tasangau, Cetyaṣṭau, Natyan, Rasāh smritāh but sometimes Sāntarasah, is added thus making the total number nine; sometimes a tenth, Vātsalyarasa is also added.
Ch 9 Verse 54 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शृङ्गारवीरहासाढ्या रौद्राद्भुतरसान्विता । दृष्ट्वैवैवंविधां नारीमसम्भाष्य समागताः ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation
O King! In compliance with your order, we have come back to you no sooner we had seen the Lady, without even addressing Her in any way. Now order us what we are to do.”
Ch 9 Verse 55-57 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वयं त्वदाज्ञया राजन् किं कर्तव्यमतःपरम् । महिष उवाच । गच्छ वीर मयादिष्टो मन्त्रिश्रेष्ठ बलान्वितः ॥ ५५ ॥ सामादिभिरुपायैस्त्वं समानय शुभाननाम् । नायाति यदि सा नारी त्रिभिः सामादिभिस्त्विह ॥ ५६ ॥ अहत्वा तां वरारोहां त्वमानय ममान्तिकम् । करोमि पट्टमहिषीं तां मरालभ्रुवं मुदा ॥ ५७ ॥
English Translation
Mahiṣa said :-- “O Best of ministers! O Hero! Under my command, go there with all the forces and use the means, conciliation, etc., and bring that woman, having a beautiful face (like the Moon), to me. If that Lady do not come even when the three policies, Sāma (conciliation), Dāna (making gifts), and Bheda (sowing dissensions in an enemy’s party and thus winning him over to one’s side, one of the four Upāyas or means of success against an enemy) are adopted by you, then apply the last resort Danḍa, (or war) in such a way that Her life be not destroyed and bring that beautiful woman to me. I will gladly make Her, of black curling hairs, my queen-consort.
Ch 9 Verse 58 Verse
Sanskrit Text
प्रीतियुक्ता समायाति यदि सा मृगलोचना । रसभङ्गो यथा न स्यात्तथा कुरु ममेप्सितम् ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation
In case that deer-eyed one comes gladly, then do my desires without causing any unpleasant feeling; (a cessation of sentiment). I am enchanted on hearing about Her beauties and wealth.”
Ch 9 Verse 59 Verse
Sanskrit Text
श्रवणान्मोहितोऽस्म्यद्य तस्या रूपस्य सम्पदा । व्यास उवाच । महिषस्य वचः श्रुत्वा पेशलं मन्त्रिसत्तमः ॥ ५९ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- The prime minister, on hearing the words of Mahiṣa, took with him elephants, horses, and chariots and hurriedly went to the desired place.
Ch 9 Verse 60 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जगाम तरसा कामं गजाश्वरथसंयुतः । गत्वा दूरतरं स्थित्वा तामुवाच मनस्विनीम् ॥ ६० ॥
English Translation
On coming near to the Devī, the minister began to address Her in sweet words from a sufficient distance in a very humble and courteous way.
Ch 9 Verse 61-62 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विनयावनतः श्लक्ष्णं मन्त्री मधुरया गिरा । प्रधान उवाच । कासि त्वं मधुरालापे किमत्रागमनं कृतम् ॥ ६१ ॥ पृच्छति त्वां महाभागे मन्मुखेन मम प्रभुः । स जेता सर्वदेवानामवध्यस्तु नरैः किल ॥ ६२ ॥
English Translation
O Sweet speaking! Who art Thou? What has caused Thee to come here? O Highly fortunate! My master has asked through me these questions. My master cannot be killed by all the Devas and men; he has conquered all the Lokas (worlds).
Ch 9 Verse 63-64 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ब्रह्मणो वरदानेन गर्वितश्चारुलोचने । दैत्येश्वरोऽसौ बलवान्कामरूपधरः सदा ॥ ६३ ॥ श्रुत्वा त्वां समुपायातां चारुवेषां मनोहराम् । द्रष्टुमिच्छति राजा मे महिषो नाम पार्थिवः ॥ ६४ ॥
English Translation
O Beautiful-eyed! On account of getting his boon from Brahmā, the Lord of the Daityas has become very powerful and consequently being very proud, assumes different forms at will. He, our King-Emperor Mahiṣa, the lord of the earth, hearing about Thy beauty and dress, has expressed a desire to see Thee. O Beautiful one! Whether he will appear before Thee in a human form?
Ch 9 Verse 65 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मानुषं रूपमादाय त्वत्समीपं समेष्यति । यथा रुच्येत चार्वङ्गि तथा मन्यामहे वयम् ॥ ६५ ॥
English Translation
He will do whatever Thou likest. O Deer-eyed One! Be pleased now to go to that intelligent King. In case Thou dost not go, we will bring the King, Thy devotee, to Thee.
Ch 9 Verse 66-68 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तर्ह्येहि मृगशावाक्षि समीपं तस्य धीमतः । नो चेदिहानयाम्येनं राजानं भक्तितत्परम् ॥ ६६ ॥ तथा करोमि देवेशि यथा ते मनसेप्सितम् । वशगोऽसौ तवात्यर्थं रूपसंश्रवणात्तव ॥ ६७ ॥ करभोरु वदाशु त्वं संविधेयं मया तथा ॥ ६८ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां पञ्चमस्कन्धे महिषमन्त्रिणा देवीवार्तावर्णनं नाम नवमोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.९॥
English Translation
O Lord of the Devas! Our King has heard of Thy beauty and grandeur and has become very much submissive to Thee. We will therefore do exactly what Thou desirest. Therefore, Thou having thighs thick and round like those of a young of an elephant! Be pleased to express what Thou likest and we will do quickly as Thou desirest. Here ends the Ninth Chapter of the Fifth Book on the worship offered by the gods to the Devī and the weapons offered by them in the Mahā Purāṇam, Śrī Mad Devī Bhagāvatam, of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

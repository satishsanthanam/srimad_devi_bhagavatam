# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 5, CHAPTER 9

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ५.९ नवमोऽध्यायः । महिषमन्त्रिणा देवीवार्तावर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> व्यास उवाच ।
> देवा विष्णुवचः श्रुत्वा सर्वे प्रमुदितास्तदा ।
> ददुश्च भूषणान्याशु वस्त्राणि स्वायुधानि च ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- On hearing Viṣṇu’s words, the Devas became very glad and presented immediately their own weapons, ornaments and clothings.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> क्षीरोदश्चाम्बरे दिव्ये रक्ते सूक्ष्मे तथाजरे ।
> निर्मलञ्च तथा हारं प्रीतस्तस्मै सुमण्डितम् ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Kṣiroda (Milk) Ocean presented to Her gladly, the well fitted necklace, clear as crystal, and a pair of divine cloths, of a red colour, never becoming old and very fine.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 3-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ददौ चूडामणिं दिव्यं सूर्यकोटिसमप्रभम् ।
> कुण्डले च तथा शुभ्रे कटकानि भुजेषु वै ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> केयूरान्कङ्कणान्दिव्यान्नानारत्नविराजितान् ।
> ददौ तस्यै विश्वकर्मा प्रसन्नेन्द्रियमानसः ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Viśvakarmā was very much gratified in his heart and presented a divine jewel to be worn in Her diadem or crest blazing like hundreds of suns; white earrings; bracelets for Her wrist, bracelets for Her upper arm, and other bracelets decked with various gems and jewels and anklets brilliant like gems, of a clear Sun-like lustre, decked with jewels, and tinkling nicely.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 5-6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नूपुरौ सुस्वरौ कान्तौ निर्मलौ रत्नभूषितौ ।
> ददौ सूर्यप्रतीकाशौ त्वष्टा तस्यै सुपादयोः ॥ ५ ॥
> 
> तथा ग्रैवेयकं रम्यं ददौ तस्यै महार्णवः ।
> अङ्गुलीयकरत्नानि तेजोवन्ति च सर्वशः ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The architect of the gods, the ocean of intellect, Viśvakarmā gave Her as offerings beautiful ornaments also for the neck, all very beautiful, as well as for the fingers decked with gems and jewels, all shining splendidly.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अम्लानपङ्कजां मालां गन्धाढ्यां भ्रमरानुगाम् ।
> तथैव वैजयन्तीञ्च वरुणः सम्प्रयच्छत ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Varuṇa gave for Her head garland of lotuses, never fading away, of such a sweet fragrance as bees constantly hover round them and the Vaijayantī garland for Her breast.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> हिमवानथ सन्तुष्टो रत्नानि विविधानि च ।
> ददौ च वाहनं सिंहं कनकाभं मनोहरम् ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The mountain Himālyā gladly offered Her various gems and a beautiful lion, of a golden colour for Her conveyance.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भूषणैर्भूषिता दिव्यैः सा रराज वरा शुभा ।
> सिंहारूढा वरारोहा सर्वलक्षणसंयुता ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then that beautiful Lady, having all the auspicious signs, wishing welfare to all, and decorated with the divine ornaments began to look grand and splendid, mounted on Her conveyance, the Lion.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विष्णुश्चक्रात्समुत्पाद्य ददावस्यै रथाङ्गकम् ।
> सहस्रारं सुदीप्तञ्च देवारिशिरसां हरम् ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Viṣṇu then created another thousand spoked discus (Cakram) from His own Cakra, capable to take off the head of any Asura, and offered it to Her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्वत्रिशूलात्समुत्पाद्य शङ्करः शूलमुत्तमम् ।
> ददौ देव्यै सुरारीणां कृन्तनं भयनाशनम् ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Śaṅkara created another excellent Triśūla from his own Trident, terrible and demon-killing, and offered it to the Devī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वरुणश्च प्रसन्नात्मा ददौ शङ्खं समुज्वलम् ।
> घोषवन्तं स्वशङ्खात्तु समुत्पाद्य सुमङ्गलम् ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Varuṇa created another bright conch from his own conch and offered it gladly to the Devī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> हुताशनस्तथा शक्तिं शतघ्नीं सुमनोजवाम् ।
> प्रायच्छत्तु प्रसन्नात्मा तस्यै दैत्यविनाशिनीम् ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Fire offered Her a weapon named Śataghni which kills violently the demons, as if that is another god of death.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इषुधिं बाणपूर्णञ्च चापं चाद्भुतदर्शनम् ।
> मारुतो दत्तवांस्तस्यै दुराकर्षं खरस्वरम् ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Maruta (wind), the chief of the gods, offered Her a wonderful bow and arrow case filled with arrows. The bow can be drawn with great difficulty and emits a very harsh sound.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्ववज्राद्वज्रमुत्पाद्य ददाविन्द्रोऽतिदारुणम् ।
> घण्टामैरावतात्तूर्णं सुशब्दां चातिसुन्दराम् ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Indra created another dreadful thunderbolt from his own thunderbolt and gave it at once to the Devī; as well the beautiful sonorous bell that used to hang from the elephant Airāvata.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ददौ दण्डं यमः कामं कालदण्डसमुद्भवम् ।
> येनान्तं सर्वभूतानामकरोत्काल आगते ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Yama, the God of Death, created another beautiful staff from his own sceptre which takes away when time comes, the life of all beings.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रह्मा कमण्डलुं दिव्यं गङ्गावारिप्रपूरितम् ।
> ददावस्यै मुदा युक्तो वरुणः पाशमेव च ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Brahmā gladly gave Her a divine Kamandalu, filled with the Ganges water; and Varuṇa offered Her a weapon called Pāśa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कालः खड्गं तथा चर्म प्रायच्छत्तु नराधिप ।
> परशुं विश्वकर्मा च तीक्ष्णमस्यै ददावथ ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Time gave Her an axe and a shield and Viśvakarmā gave Her a sharp Paraśu.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> धनदस्तु सुरापूर्णं पानपात्रं सुवर्णजम् ।
> पङ्कजं वरुणश्चादाद्देव्यै दिव्यं मनोहरम् ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Kuvera, the Lord of wealth, gave her a golden drinking cup, filled with wine; and Vāruṇa offered Her a divine beautiful lotus.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 20-21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> गदां कौमोदकीं त्वष्टा घण्टाशतनिनादिनीम् ।
> अदात्तस्यै प्रसन्नात्मा सुरशत्रुविनाशिनीम् ॥ २० ॥
> 
> अस्त्राण्यनेकरूपाणि तथाभेद्यञ्च दंशनम् ।

>
**English Translation:**

Viśvakarmā became very glad and gave Her the Kaumodakī gadā, capable to kill the enemy of the gods and whence hundreds of bells are hanging, an impenetrable armour and various other weapons. The Sun gave to the Divine Mother his own rays.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सायुधां भूषणैर्युक्तां दृष्ट्वा ते विस्मयं गताः ।
> तुष्टुवुस्तां सुरा देवीं त्रैलोक्यमोहिनीं शिवाम् ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Devas, seeing Her adorned with ornaments and weapons, began to praise and chant hymns to that most Auspicious Goddess, the Great Enchantress of the three worlds.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देवा ऊचुः ।
> नमः शिवायै कल्याण्यै शान्त्यै पुष्ट्यै नमो नमः ।
> भगवत्यै नमो देव्यै रुद्राण्यै सततं नमः ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Deva said :-- “Salutation to Śiva, Salutation to the Most Auspicious; Thou art peace and nourishment; we salute again and again to Thee. Salutation to Thee, the Bhagavatī Devī; Thou art the Goddess Rudrānī (the terrible), we always salute again and again to Thee.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कालरात्र्यै तथाम्बायै इन्द्राण्यै ते नमो नमः ।
> सिद्ध्यै बुद्ध्यै तथा वृद्ध्यै वैष्णव्यै ते नमो नमः ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thou art the Kālarātri (the night of destruction at the end of the world); Thou art the Indrānī. Thou art the Mother, we salute again and again to Thee; Thou art the success,

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पृथिव्यां या स्थिता पृथ्व्या न ज्ञाता पृथिवीञ्च या ।
> अन्तःस्थिता यमयति वन्दे तामीश्वरीं पराम् ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thou art within the earth; yet the earth does know know Thee. Thou art again the inmost of the earth and controllest the things within this earth; we offer our salutations to that Supreme Cause,

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मायायां या स्थिता ज्ञाता मायया न च तामजाम् ।
> अन्तःस्थिता प्रेरयति प्रेरयित्रीं नुमः शिवाम् ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thou art within this Māyā (the unborn) yet the Māyā does not know Thee. Thou residest again within the innermost of the Māyā and directest that Unborn One, the Māyā, we salute again and again to that Supreme Cause, the Great Directress, the Śivā (the most auspicious).

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कल्याणं कुरु भो मातस्त्राहि नः शत्रुतापितान् ।
> जहि पापं हयारिं त्वं तेजसा स्वेन मोहितम् ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Mother! Do what is good to us; we are oppressed by our enemy, dost then protect us; by Thy own power dost Thou overpower and kill that Mahiṣa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> खलं मायाविनं घोरं स्त्रीवध्यं वरदर्पितम् ।
> दुःखदं सर्वदेवानां नानारूपधरं शतम् ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

That demon is vulnerable by woman only, he is deceitful, cunning, dreadful, and swollen with pride on his having got the blessing; he assumes many forms and torments the Devas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> त्वमेका सर्वदेवानां शरणं भक्तवत्सले ।
> पीडितान्दानवेनाद्य त्राहि देवि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O One, devoted to the Bhaktas! Thou art the only refuge of all the gods; O Thou art the supreme goddess, we are very much harassed and oppressed by the Dānava; therefore dost Thou now protect us; we bow down to Thee.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> एवं स्तुता तदा देवी सुरैः सर्वसुखप्रदा ।
> तानुवाच महादेवी स्मितपूर्वं शुभं वचः ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- When the Devas had praised thus, the Highest Goddess, the Giver of all happiness, then smilingly said in the following auspicious terms :--

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देव्युवाच ।
> भयं त्यजन्तु गीर्वाणा महिषान्मन्दचेतसः ।
> हनिष्यामि रणेऽद्यैव वरदृप्तं विमोहितम् ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

“O Devas! Today in the battle ground I will overpower that wicked Mahiṣa, of cruel disposition and take away his life.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इत्युक्त्वा सा सुरान्देवी जहासातीव सुस्वरम् ।
> चित्रमेतच्च संसारे भ्रममोहयुतं जगत् ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- Speaking thus in a melodious voice, the Supreme One smiled and again said :-- “This world is all full of error and delusion.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेशाद्याः सेन्द्राश्चान्ये सुरास्तथा ।
> कम्पयुक्ता भयत्रस्ता वर्तन्ते महिषात्किल ॥ ३३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Really, it is very wonderful that Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Indra and other gods are all shuddering out of fear from Mahiṣa Dānava.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

> कालः कर्तास्ति दुःखानां सुखानां प्रभुरीश्वरः ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The power of Destiny is exceedingly great and terrible; its influence cannot be overcome even by the best of the Devas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 35</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सृष्टिपालनसंहारे समर्था अपि ते यदा ।
> मुह्यन्ति क्लेशसन्तप्ता महिषेण प्रपीडिताः ॥ ३५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O king! The Time is the Lord of happiness and pain; Time is, therefore, the God. The wonder is this that even those who can create, preserve and destroy this world, they are being overpowered and tormented by Mahiṣa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इति कृत्वा स्मितं देवी साट्टहासं चकार ह ।
> उच्चैः शब्दं महाघोरं दानवानां भयप्रदम् ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Devī, thinking thus, smiled; then laughed and laughed very hoarsely; it seemed that a roar of laughter then arose.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 37</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> चकम्पे वसुधा तत्र श्रुत्वा तच्छब्दमद्भुतम् ।

>
**English Translation:**

And the Dānavas were struck with terror at that very dreadful sound. The earth trembled at that extraordinary sound; the mountains began to move and the vast oceans that remained calm began to be agitated with billows.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मेरुश्चचाल शब्देन दिशः सर्वाः प्रपूरिताः ।
> भयं जग्मुस्तदा श्रुत्वा दानवास्तत्स्वनं महत् ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The uproar filled all the quarters and the mountain Meru trembled. Then the Dānavas, hearing the tumultuous uproar, were all filled with tremendous fear.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 39</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

> महिषोऽपि स्वनं श्रुत्वा चुकोप मदगर्वितः ॥ ३९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Devas became very glad and said thus :-- “O Devī! Let victory be Yours; save us.” The intoxicated Mahiṣa, too, hearing those words, became very angry.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

> गच्छन्तु त्वरिता दूता ज्ञातुं शब्दसमुद्भवम् ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Mahiṣa, struck with terror at those words, asked the Daityas “O Messengers! Go and ascertain how has originated this sound.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 9 Verse 41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

Who has made this harsh sound? Bring that devil who has made this hoarse noise, be he a Deva, Dānava, or anyone else unto me and I will kill that roaring villain, who, it seems, has been puffed with egoism and vanity.

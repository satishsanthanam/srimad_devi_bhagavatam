Ch 8 Verse 1-3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
५.८ अष्टमोऽध्यायः । देव्याः स्वरूपोद्भववर्णनम् । व्यास उवाच । तरसा तेऽथ सम्प्राप्य वैकुण्ठं विष्णुवल्लभम् । ददृशुः सर्वशोभाढ्यं दिव्यसद्मविराजितम् ॥ १ ॥ सरोवापीसरिद्भिश्च संयुतं सुखदं शुभम् । हंससारसचक्राह्वैः कूजद्भिश्च विराजितम् ॥ २ ॥ चम्पकाशोककह्लारमन्दारबकुलावृतैः । मल्लिकातिलकाम्रातयुतैः कुरबकादिभिः ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- Soon the Devas reached Vaikunṭha, protected by Viṣṇu; they at once began to look at the exquisite indescribable beauty of the place. At intervals they saw nice lovely divine houses, shining and appearing very splendid; pools and lakes were seen in front of them beautified with Kalhāra lotus flowers. They began to see, at other places, rivers flowing; swans, cranes, Cakravākas and other aquatic birds were swimming there easily and warbling lovely sounds.

English Translation
At other places again, beautiful gardens came to their sight adorned exquisitely by Champaka, Aśoka Mandāra, Bakula, Āmrātaka, Tilaka, Kuruvaka and Mallikā and various other flower trees, the cuckoos were seen there cooing melodiously, bees humming gently and peacocks dancing beautifully.

English Translation
In the centre was situated the golden palace of Hari, towering to heavens, the rooms and quadrangles were all charming; at places, they were bedecked with gems and jewels and adorned with various paintings. There was the Divine Seat in the centre, composed wholly of gems and jewels; and Viṣṇu was occupying this place.

Ch 8 Verse 15-17 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Hari came up to them and seeing the Devas waiting at the doors very morose and tired, cheered them up by casting a favourable glance full affection and love. The gods bowed down and praised hymns to Jagannātha the Deva of the Devas, the enemy of the Daityas and revealed in the Vedas. O Deva of the Devas! Thou art the Creator, Preserver and the Destroyer of the worlds; Thou art the ocean of mercy and the sole refuge of this Universe; O Lord! We have come to Thee as our Great Refuge; therefore dost Thou save us from the present difficulty. Thus praised by the gods,

Was it that Her face, nose, eyes, etc., and all other parts of Her body were created out of the different fires respectively or whether was it that those limbs were fashioned when the different fires blended into one huge mass?
Ch 8 Verse 50-51 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ब्रूहि तद्विस्तरं व्यास शरीराङ्गसमुद्भवम् । बभूव यस्य देवस्य तेजसोऽङ्गं यदद्भुतम् ॥ ५० ॥ आयुधाभरणादीनि दत्तानि यैर्यथा यथा । तत्सर्वं श्रोतुकामोऽस्मि त्वन्मुखाम्बुजनिर्गतम् ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation
Describe, in detail, the origin of the body and the several limbs thereof; also inform me the limbs that were produced out of the corresponding Deva’s fiery part; as well tell me the several ornaments and several weapons given by the several Devas respectively. I am very desirous to hear all these from your lotus-like mouth.
Ch 8 Verse 52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न हि तृप्याम्यहं ब्रह्मन् सुधामयरसं पिबन् । चरितञ्च महालक्ष्यास्त्वन्मुखाम्भोजनिःसृतम् ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation
O Brahmāṇ! Hearing from your lotus-like mouth the life and doings of Mahā Lakṣmī, the sweet juice as they are, I am as yet not satiated (and am desirous to hear more).
Ch 8 Verse 53 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सूत उवाच । इति तस्य वचः श्रुत्वा राज्ञः सत्यवतीसुतः । उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं प्रीणयन्निव भूपतिम् ॥ ५३ ॥
English Translation
Sūta said :-- Veda Vyāsa, the son of Satyavatī, hearing his words addressed him in the following sweet words :--
Ch 8 Verse 54 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । शृणु राजन्महाभाग विस्तरेण ब्रवीमि ते । यथामति कुरुश्रेष्ठ तस्या देहसमुद्भवम् ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation
“O Best of Kuras! Very fortunate you are. I will describe in detail, to the best of my understanding, the origin of Her body.
Ch 8 Verse 55 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न ब्रह्मा न हरिः साक्षान्न रुद्रो न च वासवः । याथातथ्येन तद्रूपं वक्तुमीशः कदाचन ॥ ५५ ॥
English Translation
Even Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśa and Indra are never competent enough to describe Her form properly.
Ch 8 Verse 56 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कथं जानाम्यहं देव्या यद्रूपं यादृशं यतः । वाचारम्भणमात्रं तदुत्पन्नेति ब्रवीमि यत् ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation
As I already told you that She sprung at the instant the word was spoken, how then can I ascertain the form or likeness of the Devī.
Ch 8 Verse 57 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सा नित्या सर्वदैवास्ते देवकार्यार्थसिद्धये । नानारूपा त्वेकरूपा जायते कार्यगौरवात् ॥ ५७ ॥
English Translation
She is constant, She is always existent; though She is one, yet She assumes different forms for the fulfilment of the Deva’s ends, whenever their positions become serious.
Ch 8 Verse 58-59 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यथा नटो रङ्गगतो नानारूपो भवत्यसौ । एकरूपस्वभावोऽपि लोकरञ्जनहेतवे ॥ ५८ ॥ तथैषा देवकार्यार्थमरूपापि स्वलीलया । करोति बहुरूपाणि निर्गुणा सगुणानि च ॥ ५९ ॥
English Translation
Though the actor is one, yet for the entertainment of the spectators, he assumes different forms in the stage, so the Nirguṇā Devī, though formless, assumes in Her pastime, many different forms of Sāttvic, Rājasic or Tāmasic qualities, to fulfill the Deva’s purposes.
Ch 8 Verse 60 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कार्यकर्मानुसारेण नामानि प्रभवन्ति हि । धात्वर्थगुणयुक्तानि गौणानि सुबहून्यपि ॥ ६० ॥
English Translation
There are various names given to Her, according as the works done by Her vary immensely in their natures, just as the meanings of one root vary, some being principal and some secondary, according to the meanings and objects they convey.
Ch 8 Verse 61 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तद्वै बुद्ध्यनुसारेण प्रब्रवीमि नराधिप । यथा तेजःसमुद्भूतं रूपं तस्या मनोहरम् ॥ ६१ ॥
English Translation
O King! I will now describe to you as far as my knowledge goes, the Excellent Form that came out of that mass of Celestial Light.
Ch 8 Verse 62 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शङ्करस्य च यत्तेजस्तेन तन्मुखपङ्कजम् । श्वेतवर्णं शुभाकारमजायत महत्तरम् ॥ ६२ ॥
English Translation
Her grand beautiful white lotus-like face was created out of the fiery energy of Śaṅkara.
Ch 8 Verse 63 Verse
Sanskrit Text
केशास्तस्यास्तथा स्निग्धा याम्येन तेजसाभवन् । वक्राग्राश्चातिदीर्घा वै मेघवर्णा मनोहराः ॥ ६३ ॥
English Translation
Her glossy black beautiful hairs of the head, overhanging to the knees, were formed out of the light of Yama; these all came to a fine pointed end.
Ch 8 Verse 64 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नयनत्रितयं तस्या जज्ञे पावकतेजसा । कृष्णं रक्तं तथा श्वेतं वर्णत्रयविभूषितम् ॥ ६४ ॥
English Translation
Her three eyes came out of the energy of Fire; the pupils of those eyes were of a black colour; the middle parts were of a white colour and the ends were red.
Ch 8 Verse 65 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वक्रे स्निग्धे कृष्णवर्णे सन्ध्ययोस्तेजसा भ्रुवौ । जाते देव्याः सुतेजस्के कामस्य धनुषीव ते ॥ ६५ ॥
English Translation
The two eyebrows of the Devī were black and came out of the spirit of Sandhyā (twilights); they were nicely curved and were looking spirited, like the bow of the Cupid and they were shedding, as it were, cooling rays.
Ch 8 Verse 66 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वायोश्च तेजसा शस्तौ श्रवणौ सम्बभूवतुः । नातिदीर्घो नातिह्रस्वौ दोलाविव मनोभुवः ॥ ६६ ॥
English Translation
From the light of Vāyu (air), Her two ears were created; they were not very long, nor very short, beautiful like the swinging seat (rocking chair) of the God of Love.
Ch 8 Verse 67 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तिलपुष्पसमाकारा नासिका सुमनोहरा । सञ्जाता स्निग्धवर्णा वै धनदस्य च तेजसा ॥ ६७ ॥
English Translation
Her nose was fashioned out of the fire of Kuvera, the Lord of wealth; it looked like the til flower, glassy and exquisitely charming.
Ch 8 Verse 68 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दन्ताः शिखरिणः श्लक्ष्णाः कुन्दाग्रसदृशाः समाः । सञ्जाताः सुप्रभा राजन् प्राजापत्येन तेजसा ॥ ६८ ॥
English Translation
O King! Her pointed rows of glossy and brilliant teeth, looking like gems, came out of the energy of Dakṣa; they looked like the Kunda flowers.
Ch 8 Verse 69 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अधरश्चातिरक्तोऽस्याः सञ्जातोऽरुणतेजसा । उत्तरोष्ठस्तथा रम्यः कार्तिकेयस्य तेजसा ॥ ६९ ॥
English Translation
Her lower lip was deep red and it came out of the fire of Aruṇa (the charioteer of the Sun); Her beautiful upper lip came out of the energy of Kārtika.
Ch 8 Verse 70 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अष्टादशभुजाकारा बाहवो विष्णुतेजसा । वसूनां तेजसाङ्गुल्यो रक्तवर्णास्तथाभवन् ॥ ७० ॥
English Translation
Her eighteen hands came out of the Tejas of Viṣṇu and Her red fingers came out of the Tejas of the Vasus.
Ch 8 Verse 71 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सौम्येन तेजसा जातं स्तनयोर्युग्ममुत्तमम् । ऐन्द्रेणास्यास्तथा मध्यं जातं त्रिवलिसंयुतम् ॥ ७१ ॥
English Translation
Her breasts came out of the energy of Soma and Her middle (navel) with three folds was created out of the spirit of Indra.
Ch 8 Verse 72 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जङ्घोरू वरुणस्याथ तेजसा सम्बभूवतुः । नितम्बः स तु सञ्जातो विपुलस्तेजसा भुवः ॥ ७२ ॥
English Translation
Her thighs and legs were from Varuṇa and Her spacious loins came out from Earth.
Ch 8 Verse 73-74 Verse
Sanskrit Text
एवं नारी शुभाकारा सुरूपा सुस्वरा भृशम् । समुत्पन्ना तथा राजंस्तेजोराशिसमुद्भवा ॥ ७३ ॥ तां दृष्ट्वा सुष्ठुसर्वाङ्गीं सुदतीं चारुलोचनाम् । मुदं प्रापुः सुराः सर्वे महिषेण प्रपीडिताः ॥ ७४ ॥
English Translation
O King! Thus from the various Tejas, contributed by the Devas, that Heavenly Lady came out. Her body and the several parts thereof were beautiful; Her form was incomparably graceful and the voice was exquisitely sonorous and lovely. The Devas, oppressed by Mahiṣāsura, became overpowered with joy seeing this well decorated Devī, having beautiful eyes and teeth, and charming in all respects.”
Ch 8 Verse 75-77 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विष्णुस्त्वाह सुरान्सर्वान्भूषणान्यायुधानि च । प्रयच्छन्तु शुभान्यस्यै देवाः सर्वाणि साम्प्रतम् ॥ ७५ ॥ स्वायुधेभ्यः समुत्पाद्य तेजोयुक्तानि सत्वराः । समर्पयन्तु सर्वेऽद्य देव्यै नानायुधानि वै ॥ ७६ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां पञ्चमस्कन्धे देव्याः स्वरूपोद्भववर्णनं नामाष्टमोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.८॥
English Translation
Viṣṇu then addressed all the Devas to give all their auspicious ornaments and weapons, He said :-- “O Devas! Better give, all you the various arms and weapons, endowed with strength, created out of your own weapons and give them all today to the Devī.” Here ends the Eighth Chapter of the Fifth Skandha on the description of the origin and the form of the Devī in Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam, the Mahā Purāṇam, of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

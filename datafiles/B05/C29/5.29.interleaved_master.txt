================================================================================
SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 5, CHAPTER 29
================================================================================

[Ch 29 Verse 1]
५.२९ एकोनत्रिंशोऽध्यायः । देव्यासह युद्धकरणाय निशुम्भप्रयाणम् ।


व्यास उवाच ।
वरदानमिदं तस्य दानवस्य शिवार्पितम् ।
अत्यद्भुततरं राजञ्छ्रुणु तत्प्रब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ १ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Please hear attentively about the extraordinary boon that was given by Mahādeva, the God of gods, to the great warrior, Raktabīja.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 2-3]
तस्य देहाद्रक्तबिन्दुर्यदा पतति भूतले ।
समुत्पतन्ति दैतेयास्तद्रूपास्तत्पराक्रमाः ॥ २ ॥

असङ्ख्याता महावीर्या दानवा रक्तसम्भवाः ।
प्रभवन्त्विति रुद्रेण दत्तोऽस्त्यत्यद्भुतो वरः ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation:
Whenever a drop of blood from the body of that great warrior will drop on the surface of the earth, immediately will arise innumerable Dānavas, equal in form and power to him; thus the Deva Rudra granted the Demon the wonderful boon.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 4]
स तेन वरदानेन दर्पितः क्रोधसंयुतः ।
अभ्यगात्तरसा सङ्ख्ये हन्तुं देवीं सकालिकाम् ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation:
Thus elated with the boon, he entered into the battlefield with great force in order to kill Kālikā with Ambikā Devī.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 5]
स दृष्ट्वा वैष्णवीं शक्तिं गरुडोपरिसंस्थिताम् ।
शक्त्या जघान दैत्येन्द्रस्तां वै कमललोचनाम् ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation:
Seeing the Vaiṣṇavī Śakti, lotus-eyed, seated on the bird Garuḍa, the Demon struck Her with a violent weapon (named Śakti).
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 6]
गदया वारयामास शक्तिः सा शक्तिसंयुता ।
अताडयच्च चक्रेण रक्तबीजं महासुरम् ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation:
She then baffled the weapon by Her club and hurled Sudarśana disc on the great Asura Raktabīja.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 7]
रथाङ्गहतदेहात्तु बहु सुस्राव शोणितम् ।
वज्राहतगिरेः शृङ्गान्निर्झरा इव गैरिका ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation:
Thus struck by the disc, blood began to ooze out from his body as the red stream of soft red sandstone comes out of a mountain-top.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 8]
यत्र यत्र यदा भूमौ पतन्ति रक्तबिन्दवः ।
समुत्तस्थुस्तदाकाराः पुरुषाश्च सहस्रशः ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation:
Wherever on the surface of the earth drops of blood fell from his body, then and there sprang out thousands and thousands of demons like him.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 9]
ऐन्द्री तमसुरं घोरं वज्रेणाभिजघान च ।
रक्तबीजं क्रुधाऽऽविष्टा निःससार च शोणितम् ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation:
Indrāṇi, the wife of Indra, became very angry and struck the terrible Raktabīja with his thunderbolt.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 10]
ततस्तत्क्षतजाज्जाता रक्तबीजा ह्यनेकशः ।
तद्वीर्याश्च तदाकारा सायुधा युद्धदुर्मदाः ॥ १० ॥
English Translation:
Streams of blood then began to ooze out from his body. No sooner the drops of blood fell from the Demon’s body, than were instantaneously born from the blood many powerful Asuras, of similar forms, having similar weapons and hard to be conquered in battle.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 11]
ब्रह्माणी ब्रह्मदण्डेन कुपिता ह्यहनद् भृशम् ।
माहेश्वरी त्रिशूलेन दारयामास दानवम् ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation:
Brahmāṇī then becoming enraged struck at him with the staff of Brahmā with greater force. Māheśvarī rent the Dānava asunder by striking him with Her trident.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 12-14]
नारसिंही नखाघातैस्तं विव्याध महासुरम् ।
अहनत्तुण्डघातेन क्रुद्धा तं राक्षसाधमम् ॥ १२ ॥

कौमारी च तथा शक्त्या वक्षस्येनमताडयत् ।
सोऽपि क्रुद्धः शरासारैर्बिभेद निशितैश्च ताः ॥ १३ ॥

गदाशक्तिप्रहारैस्तु मातॄः सर्वाः पृथक्पृथक् ।
शक्तयस्तं शराघातैर्विव्यधुस्तत्प्रकोपिताः ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation:
Nārasinjhi pierced the Asura with Her nails; Vārāhī struck at him with Her teeth. Then the Dānava becoming angry shot at them all with sharpened arrows and pierced them all. Thus when the Mātrikā Devīs were pierced by the club and other various weapons of that great Asura, they got very angry and pierced the Dānavas in return with shots of arrows. Kaumārī, too, struck at his breast with Her weapon, named Śakti. The Dānavas then got angry and hurled on them multitude of arrows and began to pierce them.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 15]
तस्य शस्त्राणि चिच्छेद चण्डिका स्वशरैः शितैः ।
जघानान्यैश्च विशिखैस्तं देवी कुपिता भृशम् ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation:
O King! The Caṇḍikā Devī, getting angry, cut off his weapons into pieces and shot violently at him other arrows.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 16]
तस्य देहाच्च सुस्राव रुधिरं बहुधा तु यत् ।
तस्मात्तत्सदृशाः शूराः प्रादुरासन्सहस्रशः ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation:
O King! Thus struck by severe blows, when blood began to flow in profuse quantities from his body, thousands and thousands of Dānavas resembling Raktabīja sprang out instantly from it.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 17]
रक्तबीजैर्जगद्व्याप्तं रुधिरौघसमुद्भवैः ।
सन्नद्धैः सायुधैः कामं कुर्वद्भिर्युद्धमद्भुतम् ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation:
So much so, that the heavens were all covered over with Raktabījas that sprang up from the blood. They all covered all over their bodies with coats of armour, began to fight terribly with weapons in their hands.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 18]
प्रहरन्तश्च तान्दृष्ट्वा रक्तबीजाननेकशः ।
भयभीताः सुरास्त्रेसुर्विषण्णाः शोककर्षिताः ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation:
Then the Devas, seeing that the innumerable Raktavījas were striking the Devī, became very much frightened and were distressed with sorrow.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 19]
कथमद्य क्षयं दैत्या गमिष्यन्ति सहस्रशः ।
महाकाया महावीर्या दानवा रक्तसम्भवाः ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation:
They began to talk with each other with sorrowful countenances that thousands and thousands of huge bodied warriors were springing from the blood. These were all very powerful; so how could they be destroyed!
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 20]
एकैव चण्डिकात्रास्ति तथा काली च मातरः ।
एताभिर्दानवाः सर्वे जेतव्याः कष्टमेव तत् ॥ २० ॥
English Translation:
In this battlefield there were now left only the Mātrikās, Kālikā, and Caṇḍikā. It would be certainly extremely difficult for them to conquer all these Dānavas.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 21]
निशुम्भो वाथ शुम्भो वा सहसा बलसंवृतः ।
आगमिष्यति सङ्ग्रामे ततोऽनर्थो महान्भवेत् ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation:
And if at that moment, Śumbha and Niśumbha were to join them with his army, certainly a great catastrophe would occur.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 22-23]
व्यास उवाच ।
एवं देवा भयोद्विग्नाश्चिन्तामापुर्महत्तराम् ।
यदा तदाम्बिका प्राह कालीं कमललोचनाम् ॥ २२ ॥

चामुण्डे कुरु विस्तीर्णं वदनं त्वरिता भृशम् ।
मच्छस्त्रपातसम्भूतं रुधिरं पिब सत्वरा ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O King! When the Devas were thus extremely anxious, out of terror, Ambikā Devī said to the lotus-eyed Kālī :-- “O Cāmuṇḍā! Open out your mouth quickly, and no sooner I strike Raktabīja with weapons, you would drink off the blood as fast as it runs out of his body.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 24]
भक्षयन्ती चर रणे दानवानद्य कामतः ।
हनिष्यामि शरैस्तीक्ष्णैर्गदासिमुसलैस्तथा ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation:
Instantly I will kill those Dānavas sprung from the blood with sharpened arrows, clubs, swords and Muṣalas; and you would then be able to devour them all at your will, and, then, roam in this field as you like.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 25]
तथा कुरु विशालाक्षि पानं तद्रुधिरस्य च ।
बिन्दुमात्रं यथा भूम्यां न पतेदपि साम्प्रतम् ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation:
O Large-eyed One! You would drink off all the jets of blood in such a way that not a drop of it escapes and falls on the ground.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 26]
भक्ष्यमाणास्तदा दैत्या न चोत्पत्स्यन्ति चापरे ।
एवमेषां क्षयो नूनं भविष्यति न चान्यथा ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation:
And then when they will all be devoured, no more Dānavas would be able to spring. Thus they will surely be extirpated; otherwise they will never be destroyed.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 27]
घातयिष्याम्यहं दैत्यं त्वं भक्षय च सत्वरा ।
पिबन्ती क्षतजं सर्वं यतमानारिसङ्क्षये ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation:
Let me begin to strike blows after blows on Raktabīja and you better drink off quickly all the blood, being intent on destroying the forces.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 28]
इत्थं दैत्यक्षयं कृत्वा दत्त्वा राज्यं सुरालयम् ।
इन्द्राय सुस्थिरं सर्वं गमिष्यामो यथासुखम् ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation:
O Camuṇḍe! Thus, the Dānavas being all exterminated, we will hand over to the Indra, the lord of the Devas, his Heavens without any enemy; and, thus, we can go peacefully and happily to our own places.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 29]
व्यास उवाच ।
इत्युक्ताम्बिकया देवी चामुण्डा चण्डविक्रमा ।
पपौ च क्षतजं सर्वं रक्तबीजशरीरजम् ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O King! The Cāmuṇḍā Devī, of furious strength, hearing thus the Devī’s words began to drink the jets of blood coming out of the body of Raktabīja.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 30]
अम्बिका तं जघानाशु खड्गेन मुसलेन च ।
चखाद देहशकलांश्चामुण्डा तान्कृशोदरी ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation:
The Devī Ambikā began to cut the Demon’s body into pieces and Cāmuṇḍā, of thin belly, went on devouring them.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 31]
सोऽपि क्रुद्धो गदाघातैश्चामुण्डां समघातयत् ।
तथापि सा पपावाशु क्षतजं तमभक्षयत् ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation:
Then Raktabīja, becoming angry, struck Cāmuṇḍā with his club. But though She was thus hurt severely, She drank off the blood and then devoured all the limbs thereof.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 32]
येऽन्ये रुधिरजाः क्रूरा रक्तबीजा महाबलाः ।
तेऽपि निष्पातिताः सर्वे भक्षिता गतशोणिताः ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation:
O King! Thus Kālikā Devī drank off the blood of all other powerful wicked Dānava Raktabījas that sprang out of the blood. Ambikā thus destroyed them.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 33-34]
कृत्रिमा भक्षिताः सर्वे यस्तु स्वाभाविकोऽसुरः ।
सोऽपि प्रपातितो हत्वा खड्गेनातिविखण्डितः ॥ ३३ ॥

रक्तबीजे हते रौद्रे ये चान्ये दानवा रणे ।
पलायनं ततः कृत्वा गतास्ते भयकम्पिताः ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation:
Thus, all the Dānavas, created out of the blood were devoured; then, there was left, lastly, the real Raktabīja. Ambikā Devī then cut him asunder into pieces by Her axe and thus killed him. Thus, when the dreadful Raktabīja was slain in the battle, the Dānavas fled away trembling with fear.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 35]
हाहेति विब्रुवन्तस्ते शुम्भं प्रोचुः सविह्वलाः ।
रुथिरारक्तदेहाश्च विगतास्त्रा विचेतसः ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation:
Without any weapons, covered all over their bodies with blood, and void of consciousness they uttered, dumb confounded “Alas! Alas! What has happened, what has happened.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 36]
राजन्नम्बिकया रक्तबीजोऽसौ विनिपातितः ।
चामुण्डा तस्य देहात्तु पपौ सर्वं च शोणितम् ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation:
Thus crying, they told their King Śumbha thus :-- “O King of Kings! Ambikā Devī has killed Raktabīja and Cāmuṇḍā has drunk off all their blood.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 37]
ये चान्ये दानवाः शूरा वाहनेनातिरंहसा ।
सिंहेन निहताः सर्वे काल्या च भक्षिताः परे ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation:
The carrier (Vāhana) of Devī, the powerful ferocious Lion killed other powerful warriors and Kali devoured the remaining soldiers.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 38]
वयं त्वां कथितुं राजन्नागता युद्धचेष्टितम् ।
चरितञ्च तथा देव्याः सङ्ग्रामे परमाद्भुतम् ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation:
O Lord of the Dānavas! We have fled and come to you to give the news of the battle and to describe the wonderful doings of that Caṇḍikā Devī in the battlefield.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 39]
अजेयेयं महाराज सर्वथा दैत्यदानवैः ।
गन्धर्वासुरयक्षैश्च पन्नगोरगराक्षसैः ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation:
O King! In our opinion, no one will be able to conquer that Lady, be he a Daitya, Dānava, Gandarbha, Asura, Yakṣa, Pannaga, Cāraṇa, Rākṣasa, or an Uraga.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 40]
अन्यास्तत्रागता देव्य इन्द्राणीप्रमुखा भृशम् ।
युध्यमाना महाराज वाहनैरायुधैर्युताः ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation:
O King of Kings! The other Goddesses, Indrāṇi and others, have come to the battle, on their own carriers respectively and are fighting with various weapons.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 41]
ताभिः सर्वं हतं सैन्यं दानवानां वरायुधैः ।
रक्तबीजोऽपि राजेन्द्र तरसा विनिपातितः ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation:
O Lord of the Dānavas! The Dānava forces are all slain by them with the excellent weapons in their hands. Even Raktabīja has been slain in no time.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 42]
एकापि दुःसहा देवी किं पुनस्ताभिरन्विता ।
सिंहोऽपि हन्ति सङ्ग्रामे राक्षसानमितप्रभः ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation:
That Lion, of indomitable prowess, killed the Rākṣasas in the battle; The Devī alone is hard to conquer; how much more would it be impossible to conquer Her, when She has been joined with other goddesses.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 43]
अतो विचार्य सचिवैर्यद्युक्तं तद्विधीयताम् ।
न वैरमनया युक्तं सन्धिरेव सुखप्रदः ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation:
So consult with the ministers and do what is reasonable. In our opinion it is better to make treaty with Her and quit your enmity.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 44]
आश्चर्यमेतदखिलं यन्नारी हन्ति राक्षसान् ।
रक्तबीजोऽपि निहतः पीतं तस्यापि शोणितम् ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation:
O King! Think over the fact that that Lady destroyed all the Dānavas and at last drank off the blood of Raktabīja and  at last killed him.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 45]
अन्ये निपातिता दैत्याः सङ्ग्रामेऽम्बिकया नृप ।
चामुण्डया च मांसं वै भक्षितं सकलं रणे ॥ ४५ ॥
English Translation:
What on earth can be more wonderful than this? O King! The Devī Ambikā killed all the other Daityas and Cāmuṇḍā devoured their blood, flesh, and all.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 46]
वरं पातालगमनं तस्याः सेवाथवा वरा ।
न तु युद्धं महाराज कार्यमम्बिकया सह ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation:
Considering all this, it is now better for us to serve the Devī Ambikā or fly away to Pātāla. No more fighting is desirable.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 47]
न नारी प्राकृता ह्येषा देवकार्यार्थसाधिनी ।
मायेयं प्रबला देवी क्षपयन्तीयमुत्थिता ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation:
She is not an ordinary woman; She is Mahā Māyā, there is not the least doubt in this. Only to serve the cause of the Gods, She has manifested Herself and is now destroying the Rākṣasaś race.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 48]
व्यास उवाच ।
इति तेषां वचस्तथ्यं श्रुत्वा कालविमोहितः ।
मुमूर्षुः प्रत्युवाचेदं शुम्भः प्रस्फुरिताधरः ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- Hearing thus, Śumbha got confounded by Kāla (Death), as his end was coming nigh, and said the following words, his lips quivering with anger.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 49]
शुम्भ उवाच ।
यूयं गच्छत पातालं शरणं वा भयातुराः ।
हनिष्याम्यहमद्यैव ताञ्च ताश्च समुद्यतः ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation:
You are struck with fear; so you all take the refuge of Caṇḍikā or fly down to Pātāla; but I will kill Her with all my exertion and effort.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 50]
जित्वा सर्वान्सुरानाजौ कृत्वा राज्यं सुपुष्कलम् ।
कथं नारीभयोद्विग्नः पातालं प्रविशाम्यहम् ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation:
I conquered all the hosts of Devas and I have enjoyed their kingdom; shall I now, out of the fear of one Lady, fly and enter into the Pātāla.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 51]
निहत्य पार्षदान्सर्वान् रक्तबीजमुखान् रणे ।
प्राणत्राणाय गच्छामि हित्वा किं विपुलं यशः ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation:
All my attendants, Raktabīja and other heroes, are now slain in the battle and is it possible that I will now fly away out of the sake of preserving my life only.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 52]
मरणं त्वनिवार्यं वै प्राणिनां कालकल्पितम् ।
तद्भयं जन्मनोपात्तं त्यजेत्को दुर्लभं यशः ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation:
See! The death of all the beings is ordained by Kāla and it is unavoidable. No sooner a being is born, he is liable to the fear of death.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 53-54]
निशुम्भाहं गमिष्यामि रथारूढो रणाजिरे ।
हत्वा तामागमिष्यामि नागमिष्यामि चान्यथा ॥ ५३ ॥

त्वं तु सेनायुतो वीर पार्ष्णिग्राहो भवस्व मे ।
तरसा तां शरैस्तीक्ष्णैर्नारीं नय यमालये ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation:
And if I cannot kill Her, I will not then return any more. O Best of warriors! Better stand on my side with all your forces and kill that Lady in no time, with sharp arrows.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 55]
निशुम्भ उवाच ।
अहमद्य हनिष्यामि गत्वा दुष्टाञ्च कालिकाम् ।
आगमिष्याम्यहं शीघ्रं गृहीत्वा तामथाम्बिकाम् ॥ ५५ ॥
English Translation:
Niśumbha said :-- Today I will go to the battle and slaying that Kālikā, will shortly return here with Ambikā.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 56]
मा चिन्तां कुरु राजेन्द्र वराकायास्तु कारणे ।
क्वैषा बाला क्व मे बाहुवीर्यं विश्ववशङ्करम् ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation:
O King! Do not think at all for that Lady; see my world-conquering strength and look at that weak woman; there is a vast difference.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 57]
त्यक्त्वाऽऽर्तिं विपुलां भ्रातर्भुङ्क्ष भोगाननुत्तमान् ।
आनयिष्याम्यहं कामं मानिनीं मानसंयुताम् ॥ ५७ ॥
English Translation:
Cast aside your this great mental anxiety and trouble. Enjoy, O Brother, excellent things. I will bring that dignified Lady with all honours before you.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 58]
मयि तिष्ठति ते राजन्न युक्तं गमनं रणे ।
गत्वाहमानयिष्यामि तवार्थे वै जयश्रियम् ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation:
O King! You ought not to go to the battle when I am alive. I will presently go to the fight and bring for you that Lady as a sign of our victory.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 29 Verse 59-61]
व्यास उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा भ्रातरं ज्येष्ठं कनीयान्बलगर्वितः ।
रथमास्थाय विपुलं सन्नद्धः स्वबलावृतः ॥ ५९ ॥

जगाम तरसा तूर्णं सङ्गरे कृतमङ्गलः ।
संस्तुतो बन्दिसूतैश्च सायुधः सपरिष्करः ॥ ६० ॥

इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां
संहितायां पञ्चमस्कन्धे देव्यासह युद्धकरणाय
निशुम्भप्रयाणं नामकोनत्रिंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.२९॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Thus saying, the younger brother, proud of his own strength, went hurriedly to the battlefield, mounting on his big chariot. He was protected all over his body by his coat of armour and he was well provided with various weapons and all other accoutrements of war. The bards began to sing hymns to him and various other propitious ceremonies were being performed. Here ends the Twenty-ninth Chapter of the Fifth Book on the killing of Raktabīja in Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam, the Mahā Purāṇam, of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

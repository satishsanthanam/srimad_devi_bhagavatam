# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 5, CHAPTER 18

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ५.१८ अष्टादशोऽध्यायः । महिषासुरवधः ।
> 
> 
> महिष उवाच ।
> तस्यास्तु भगिनी कन्या नाम्ना चेन्दुमती शुभा ।
> विवाहयोग्या सञ्जाता सुरूपावरजा यदा ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Mahiṣa said :-- O Devī! Mandodarī had a sister Indumatī; unmarried and endowed with all auspicious signs. She grew up in time to a marriageable age.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्या विवाहः संवृत्तः सञ्जातश्च स्वयंवरः ।
> राजानो बहुदेशीयाः सङ्गतास्तत्र मण्डपे ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Svayambara assembly (a marriage in which the girl chooses her husband from among a number of visitors assembled together) was then called for the marriage of the maiden Indumatī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तया वृतो नृपः कश्चिद्बलवान् रूपसंयुतः ।
> कुलशीलसमायुक्तः सर्वलक्षणसंयुतः ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Kings from various parts came there and the maiden Indumatī selected from among them a beautiful strong king, of noble lineage and endowed with all auspicious signs.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तदा कामातुरा जाता विटं वीक्ष्य नृपं तु सा ।
> चकमे दैवयोगात्तु शठं चातुर्यभूषितम् ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

At that time, by the undescribeable power of Destiny, Mandodarī seeing the deceitful, cunning, and hypocrite King of Madra, became passionate and desired to marry him.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पितरं प्राह तन्वङ्गी विवाहं कुरु मे पितः ।
> इच्छा मेऽद्य समुद्भूता दृष्ट्वा मद्राधिपं त्विह ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

That slender woman Mandodarī then addressed her father thus :-- “O Father! Seeing the King of Madra in this assembly, I am desirous to marry him; so perform also my marriage ceremony now.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> चन्द्रसेनोऽपि तच्छ्रुत्वा पुत्र्या यद्भाषितं रहः ।
> प्रसन्नेन च मनसा तत्कार्ये तत्परोऽभवत् ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When the king heard this request from her own daughter privately, he became very glad and began with promptness, to make preparations for the marriage.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 7-8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तमाहूय नृपं गेहे विवाहविधिना ददौ ।
> कन्यां मन्दोदरीं तस्मै पारिबर्हं तथा बहु ॥ ७ ॥
> 
> चारुदेष्णोऽपि तां प्राप्य सुन्दरीं मुदितोऽभवत् ।
> जगाम स्वगृहं तुष्टो राजापि सहितः स्त्रिया ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He invited the King of Madra to his own palace and gave him in marriage his own daughter Mandodarī, according to due rites and ceremonies with an abundance of dowry and wealth. The King of Madra Cārudeṣṇa became very glad to marry the beautiful Mandodarī and went back with her to his own abode.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 9-10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रेमे नृपतिशार्दूलः कामिन्या बहुवासरान् ।
> कदाचिद्दासपत्न्या स रममाणो रहः किल ॥ ९ ॥
> 
> सैरन्ध्र्या कथितं तस्यै तया दृष्टः पतिस्तथा ।
> उपालम्भं ददौ तस्मै स्मितपूर्वं रुषान्विता ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The King Cārudeṣṇa then enjoyed her for good many days; when one day a maid-servant found the king in sexual intercourse with another maid-servant in a lonely place and divulged this to Mandodarī; she finding the king in that state became angry and rebuked him with a slight smiling countenance.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 11-12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कदाचिदपि सामान्यां रहो रूपवतीं नृपः ।
> क्रीडयँल्लालयन्दृष्टः खेदं प्राप तदैव सा ॥ ११ ॥
> 
> न ज्ञातोऽयं शठः पूर्वं यदा दृष्टः स्वयंवरे ।
> किं कृतं तु मया मोहाद्वञ्चिताहं नृपेण ह ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Again, on another occasion, Mandodarī saw the king willingly engaged in amusements and sports with an ordinary beautiful woman and became very sorry and thought thus :-- When I saw him in the Svayamvara, I could not recognise him as a cheat; I am deceived by this King; Oh! What a wrong act have I done through delusion. This King is a rogue and he is totally shameless and has no dislike for contemptible things; it is now too late to repent for him.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> किं करोम्यद्य सन्तापं निर्लज्जे निर्घृणे शठे ।
> का प्रीतिरीदृशे पत्यौ धिगद्य मम जीवितम् ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

How can I have any affection for this husband; fie on my living now! I forsake from this very day all the pleasures with my husband and all other worldly pleasures, and I take recourse now to contentment alone.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 14-15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अद्यप्रभृति संसारे सुखं त्यक्तं मया खलु ।
> पतिसम्भोगजं सर्वं सन्तोषोऽद्य मया कृतः ॥ १४ ॥
> 
> अकर्तव्यं कृतं कार्यं तज्जातं दुःखदं मम ।
> देहत्यागः क्रियते चेद्धत्यातीव दुरत्यया ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I have committed a very wrong act that I ought never to have done; therefore it causes intense pain to me now. If I now commit suicide, then that sin will never forsake me, and I must have to enjoy the consequences thereof.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 16-17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पितृगेहं व्रजाम्याशु तत्रापि न सुखं भवेत् ।
> हास्ययोग्या सखीनां तु भवेयं नात्र संशयः ॥ १६ ॥
> 
> तस्मादत्रैव संवासो वैराग्ययुतया मया ।
> कर्तव्यः कालयोगेन त्यक्त्वा कामसुखं पुनः ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

And if I return to my father’s house, I will not be happy there, for my companions seeing me thus will, no doubt, ridicule me. Therefore, it is now advisable for me to avoid all the sensuous pleasures, become dispassionate and remain here patiently and abide by the strange combinations of Time.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> महिष उवाच ।
> इति सञ्चिन्त्य सा नारी दुःखशोकपरायणा ।
> स्थिता पतिगृहं त्यक्त्वा सुखं संसारजं ततः ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Mahiṣa said :-- Thus that women lamented and remorsed and began to remain there, very much sorrowful and distressed, renouncing thoroughly all the pleasures of the world.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्मात्त्वमपि कल्याणि मामनादृत्य भूपतिम् ।
> अन्यं कापुरुषं मन्दं कामार्ता संश्रयिष्यसि ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Auspicious One! I am the king, yet you are showing your dislike for me; know, eventually, you, too, will be passionate and entertain afterwards an illiterate coward.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वचनं कुरु मे तथ्यं नारीणां परमं हितम् ।
> अकृत्वा परमं शोकं लप्स्यसे नात्र संशयः ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Keep my word even now, it will be of great benefit and it will serve as a medicinal diet to you as to all women. In case you do not follow my advice, you will have to meet with extreme pain and misery, certainly.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 21-22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देव्युवाच ।
> मन्दात्मन् गच्छ पातालं युद्धं वा कुरु साम्प्रतम् ।
> हत्वा त्वामसुरान्सर्वान्गमिष्यामि यथासुखम् ॥ २१ ॥
> 
> यदा यदा हि साधूनां दुःखं भवति दानव ।
> तदा तेषां च रक्षार्थं देहं सन्धारयाम्यहम् ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing the words of Mahiṣāsura, the Devī said :-- O you fool! Go to the lower worlds or stand up for fight; I will send you and the other Dānavas unto death and then go away at my pleasure. O Demon! I take up form to preserve the righteous, whenever they suffer pain in this earth.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 23-25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अरूपायाश्च मे रूपमजन्मायाश्च जन्म च ।
> सुराणां रक्षणार्थाय विद्धि दैत्य विनिश्चितम् ॥ २३ ॥
> 
> सत्यं ब्रवीमि जानीहि प्रार्थिताहं सुरैः किल ।
> त्वद्वधार्थं हयारे त्वां हत्वा स्थास्यामि निश्चला ॥ २४ ॥
> 
> तस्माद्युध्यस्व वा गच्छ पातालमसुरालयम् ।
> सर्वथा त्वां हनिष्यामि सत्यमेतद् ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Lord of the Daityas! Formless, birthless I am; yet, at times, I take up form and be born to save the Devas. Know this firmly. O wicked Mahiṣa! The Devas prayed to Me for your destruction. Therefore I will not rest until I kill you. I speak all these truly to you. Therefore fight or go to Pātāla, the abode of the Asuras; I speak truly to you again that I will destroy you wholly.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इत्युक्तः स तया देव्या धनुरादाय दानवः ।
> युद्धकामः स्थितस्तत्र सङ्ग्रामाङ्गणभूमिषु ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing thus the Devī’s words, the Dānava took up his bow and came to the battle, fully stretching the string of his bow up to his ears, and began to shoot sharpened arrows with great force

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मुमोच तरसा बाणान्कर्णाकृष्टाञ्छिलाशितान् ।
> देवी चिच्छेद तान्बाणैः क्रोधान्मुक्तैरयोमुखैः ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Devī, too, hurled with anger, arrows tipped with iron and cut off the Asura’s arrows to pieces.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तयोः परस्परं युद्धं सम्बभूव भयप्रदम् ।
> देवानां दानवानाञ्च परस्परजयैषिणाम् ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The fight between them rose to such a terrible pitch that it caused terror to both the Devas and the Dānavas, trying hard to be victorious over each other.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मध्ये दुर्धर आगत्य मुमोच च शिलीमुखान् ।
> देवीं प्रति विषासक्तान्कोपयन्नतिदारुणान् ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

In the midst of the terrible encounter, the demon Durdhara came up to fight and made the Devī angry and shot arrows, all terribly poisonous and sharpened on stones, at Her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 30-31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ततो भगवती क्रुद्धा तं जघान शितैः शरैः ।
> दुर्धरस्तु पपातोर्व्यां गतासुर्गिरिशृङ्गवत् ॥ ३० ॥
> 
> तं तथा निहतं दृष्ट्वा त्रिनेत्रः परमास्त्रवित् ।
> आगत्य सप्तभिर्बाणैर्जघान परमेश्वरीम् ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The demon Trinetra, well skilled in the uses of arrows and weapons, seeing him killed, came up to fight and shot at the Great Goddess with seven arrows.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अनागतांस्तु चिच्छेद देवी तान्विशिखैः शरान् ।
> त्रिशूलेन त्रिनेत्रं तु जघान जगदम्बिका ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Before these arrows came on Her, She cut them to pieces with Her sharp arrows and by Her trident killed Trinetra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अन्धकस्त्वाजगामाशु हतं दृष्ट्वा त्रिलोचनम् ।
> गदया लोहमय्याऽऽशु सिंहं विव्याध मस्तके ॥ ३३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Trinetra thus killed, Andhaka quickly came in the battlefield and struck violently on the head of the lion with his iron club.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सिंहस्तु नखघातेन तं हत्वा बलवत्तरम् ।
> चखाद तरसा मांसमन्धकस्य रुषान्वितः ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The lion killed that powerful Andhaka by striking the demon with his nails and, out of anger, began to eat his flesh.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 35</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तान् रणे निहतान्वीक्ष्य दानवो विस्मयं गतः ।
> चिक्षेप तरसा बाणानतितीक्ष्णाञ्छिलाशितान् ॥ ३५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Mahiṣāsura became greatly astonished at the death of these Asuras and began to shoot pointed arrows, sharpened on stone, at Her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> द्विधा चक्रे शरान्देवी तानप्राप्ताञ्छिलीमुखैः ।
> गदया ताडयामास दैत्यं वक्षसि चाम्बिका ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Devī Ambikā cut his arrows into two before they came on Her and struck the Demon on his breast by Her club.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 37</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स गदाभिहतो मूर्च्छामवापामरबाधकः ।
> विषह्य पीडां पापात्मा पुनरागत्य सत्वरः ॥ ३७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

That vile Mahiṣāsura, the tormentor of the Devas, fell in a swoon under the stoke of the club but patiently bore it and, at the next moment, came again and struck the lion on his head by his club.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 38-39</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जघान गदया सिंहं मूर्ध्नि क्रोधसमन्वितः ।
> सिंहोऽपि नखघातेन तं ददार महासुरम् ॥ ३८ ॥
> 
> विहाय पौरुषं रूपं सोऽपि सिंहो बभूव ह ।
> नखैर्विदारयामास देवीसिंहं मदोत्कटम् ॥ ३९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The lion, too, by his nails rent that great Asura to pieces. Mahiṣāsura, then, quitting the man-form took up the lion-form and by his claws cut the Devī’s lion and wounded him very much by his nails.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तञ्च केसरिणं वीक्ष्य देवी क्रुद्धा ह्ययोमुखैः ।
> शरैरवाकिरत्तीक्ष्णैः क्रूरैराशीविषैरिव ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On Mahiṣāsura taking up this lion-form, the Devī became very angry and began to shoot arrows after arrows at him all very terrible, sharp and like poisonous snakes.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> त्यक्त्वा स हरिरूपं तु गजो भूत्वा मदस्रवः ।
> शैलशृङ्गं करे कृत्वा चिक्षेप चण्डिकां प्रति ॥ ४१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then the Asura quitting the lion form assumed the appearance of a male elephant, oozing out juice from his temples and began to hurl the mountain tops by his trunk.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 42</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> आगच्छन्तं गिरेः शृङ्गं देवी बाणैः शिलाशितैः ।
> चकार तिलशः खण्डाञ्जहास जगदम्बिका ॥ ४२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Seeing the mountain peaks thus hurled on Her, She cut them off to pieces by Her sharp arrows and began to laugh.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 43</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उत्पत्य च तदा सिंहस्तस्य मूर्ध्नि व्यवस्थितः ।
> नखैर्विदारयामास महिषं गजरूपिणम् ॥ ४३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Devī’s lion on the other hand, sprang on the head of the elephant Mahiṣa and by his claws rent him to pieces.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 44</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विहाय गजरूपं च बभूवाष्टापदी तथा ।
> हन्तुकामो हरिं कोपाद्दारुणो बलवत्तरः ॥ ४४ ॥

**English Translation:**

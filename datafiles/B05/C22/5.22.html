BOOK 5 • CHAPTER 22
On the eulogising of the Devī by the Devas

Ch 22 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
५.२२ द्वाविंशोऽध्यायः । देवकृतदेव्याराधनवर्णनम् । व्यास उवाच । पराजिताः सुराः सर्वे राज्यं शुम्भः शशास ह । एवं वर्षसहस्रं तु जगाम नृपसत्तम ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! When the Devas were all defeated, Śumbha began to govern all their kingdoms; thus one thousand years passed away.
Ch 22 Verse 2 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भ्रष्टराज्यास्ततो देवाश्चिन्तामापुः सुदुस्तराम् । गुरुं दुःखातुरास्ते तु पप्रच्छुरिदमादृताः ॥ २ ॥
English Translation
The Devas, on the other hand, deprived of their kingdoms, were all drowned in an ocean of cares and anxieties; at last they began to feel very much and were greatly afflicted.
Ch 22 Verse 3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
किं कर्तव्यं गुरो ब्रूहि सर्वज्ञस्त्वं महामुनिः । उपायोऽस्ति महाभाग दुःखस्य विनिवृत्तये ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
They asked with reverence their own Guru Brihaspati, “O Guru! What are we to do now? O All knowing! You are the Great Muni; kindly say unto us if there be any means by which we can get rid of this our present crisis.
Ch 22 Verse 4 Verse
Sanskrit Text
उपचारपरा नूनं वेदमन्त्राः सहस्रशः । वाच्छितार्थकरा नूनं सूत्रैः संलक्षिताः किल ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation
There are thousands of Vedic Mantrams which yield the desired results, if they are worshipped with due rites and ceremonies and if all the rules be observed thereof.
Ch 22 Verse 5 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इष्टयो विविधाः प्रोक्ताः सर्वकामफलप्रदाः । ताः कुरुष्व मुने नूनं त्वं जानासि च तत्क्रियाः ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation
O best of Munis! Many Yajñās are mentioned in the Vedas that yield all the desired results; you know them all; so kindly perform those Yajñās.
Ch 22 Verse 6-7 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विधिः शत्रुविनाशाय यथोद्दिष्टः सदागमे । तं कुरुष्वाद्य विधिवद्यथा नो दुःखसङ्क्षयः ॥ ६ ॥ भवेदाङ्गिरसाद्यैव तथा त्वं कर्तुमर्हसि । दानवानां विनाशाय अभिचारं यथामति ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
Do all those ceremonies duly that are ordained in the Vedas for the killing of enemies; O Descendant of Āṅgirasa! You ought to perform as early as possible those sacrifices for magical purposes to destroy the Dānavas so that all our miseries come to an end.”
Ch 22 Verse 8-9 Verse
Sanskrit Text
बृहस्पतिरुवाच । सर्वे मन्त्राश्च वेदोक्ता दैवाधीनफलाश्च ते । न स्वतन्त्राः सुराधीश तथैकान्तफलप्रदाः ॥ ८ ॥ मन्त्राणां देवता यूयं ते तु दुःखैकभाजनम् । जाताः स्म कालयोगेन किं करोमि प्रसाधनम् ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation
You all are the presiding Deities of the Vedic Mantras; but, now, by the strange irony of Time, you are put to difficulties and troubles; what can I do now in this case?
Ch 22 Verse 10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इन्द्राग्निवरुणादीनां यजनं यज्ञकर्मसु । ते यूयं विपदं प्राप्ताः करिष्यन्ति किमिष्टयः ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
See! Indra, Agni, Varuṇa, and other gods are invoked in sacrifices; how, then, can sacrificial ceremonies do good when you are put to so great difficulties.
Ch 22 Verse 11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अवश्यम्भाविभावानां प्रतीकारो न विद्यते । उपायस्त्वथ कर्तव्य इति शिष्टानुशासनम् ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
Therefore there is no remedy to those which will take place unavoidably; but those who are wise declare that in such cases means are to be adopted.
Ch 22 Verse 12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दैवं हि बलवत्केचित्प्रवदन्ति मनीषिणः । उपायवादिनो दैवं प्रवदन्ति निरर्थकम् ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
Some sages say that Fate is strong but those who advocate the cause of taking remedial means say that Fate is powerless; remedies or manly exertions lead to all success.
Ch 22 Verse 13 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दैवं चैवाप्युपायश्च द्वावेवाभिमतौ नृणाम् । केवलं दैवमाश्रित्य न स्थातव्यं कदाचन ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation
But, O King of the Devas! The embodied souls ought to resort to both Fate and Remedies; it is never advisable to depend solely on Fate.
Ch 22 Verse 14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
उपायः सर्वथा कार्यो विचार्य स्वधिया पुनः । तस्माद् ब्रवीमि वः सर्वान्संविचार्य पुनः पुनः ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
Therefore, it is advisable to think out again and again as far as one’s own Intellect goes, the best remedies. O Devas! I have thought over again and again on this subject and say to you my opinion. Hear.
Ch 22 Verse 15-16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पुरा भगवती तुष्टा जघान महिषासुरम् । युष्माभिस्तु स्तुता देवी वरदानं ददावथ ॥ १५ ॥ आपदं नाशयिष्यामि संस्मृता वा सदैव हि । यदा यदा वो देवेशा आपदो दैवसम्भवाः ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
In days of yore, the Bhagavatī, being appeased, killed Mahiṣāsura; and when you all praised and chanted hymns to Her, She gave you this boon that She will remove all your sorrows and troubles no sooner you remember Her, and She told that you all must remember Her whenever any difficulty would arise to you out of this Great Destiny.
Ch 22 Verse 17-18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
प्रभवन्ति तदा कामं स्मर्तव्याहं सुरैः सदा । स्मृताहं नाशयिष्यामि युष्माकं परमापदः ॥ १७ ॥ तस्माद्धिमाचले गत्वा पर्वते सुमनोहरे । आराधनं चण्डिकायाः कुरुध्वं प्रेमपूर्वकम् ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
She would, then, free you all of your ocean of great difficulties. Therefore do you all now go to the highly sacred and exquisitely beautiful Himālayān mountains and worship the most worshipful Candikā Devī with your love and devotion.
Ch 22 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मायाबीजविधानज्ञास्तत्पुरश्चरणे रताः । जानाम्यहं योगबलात्प्रसन्ना सा भविष्यति ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
Know all the rules of the Seed mantra of Māyā and be engaged in taking Her name accompanied with burnt offerings. I have come to know, by Yogic power, that She will be pleased with You. I see that today your difficulties will come to an end; there is not the least doubt in this.
Ch 22 Verse 20-21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दुःखस्यान्तोऽद्य युष्माकं दृश्यते नात्र संशयः । तस्मिञ्छैले सदा देवी तिष्ठतीति मया श्रुतम् ॥ २० ॥ स्तुता सम्पूजिता सद्यो वाञ्छितार्थान् प्रदास्यति । निश्चयं परमं कृत्वा गच्छध्वं वै हिमालयम् ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
I have heard that the Devī resides always in the Himācal; if you worship and praise and chant hymns to Her, She will certainly grant you your desired boons. Therefore fully decide on this thing and go to the Himālayās. O Devas! She will fulfil all your desires and carry out all your intentions.”
Ch 22 Verse 22-23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सुराः सर्वाणि कार्याणि सा वः कामं विधास्यति । व्यास उवाच । इति तस्य वचः श्रुत्वा देवास्ते प्रययुर्गिरिम् ॥ २२ ॥ हिमालयं महाराज देवीध्यानपरायणाः । मायाबीजं हृदा नित्यं जपन्तः सर्व एव हि ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing thus his words, the Devas departed to the Himālayās and they became all merged in the devotional worship of the Supreme Goddess and began to meditate constantly in their hearts the Seed mantra of Māyā (Hrīm).
Ch 22 Verse 24-25 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नमश्चक्रुर्महामायां भक्तानामभयप्रदाम् । तुष्टुवुः स्तोत्रमन्त्रैश्च भक्त्या परमया युताः ॥ २४ ॥ नमो देवि विश्वेश्वरि प्राणनाथे सदानन्दरूपे सुरानन्ददे ते । नमो दानवान्तप्रदे मानवाना- मनेकार्थदे भक्तिगम्यस्वरूपे ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation
O Goddess! Salutation to Thee! O Thou, the Lord of the Universe! the Lord of our hearts! Thou art the Everlasting Bliss and the Giver of bliss to the Devas! Salutation to Thee! Thou art the Destroyer of the Dānavas and Thou art the Giver of all desires of human beings.
Ch 22 Verse 26-27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न ते नामसङ्ख्यां न ते रूपमीदृ- क्तथा कोऽपि वेदादिदेवस्वरूपे । त्वमेवासि सर्वेषु शक्तिस्वरूपा प्रजासृष्टिसंहारकाले सदैव ॥ २६ ॥ स्मृतिस्त्वं धृतिस्त्वं त्वमेवासि बुद्धि- र्जरा पुष्टितुष्टी धृतिः कान्तिशान्ती । सुविद्या सुलक्ष्मीर्गतिः कीर्तिमेधे त्वमेवासि विश्वस्य बीजं पुराणम् ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
Thou canst be approached with devotion. Salutation to Thee! O Thou, the Incarnate of all the Devas! Thy names are endless; Thy forms are endless; none can count them. Thou residest always as the Force Incarnate in all the actions, in the Creation, Preservation and Dissolution of Beings. O Goddess! Thou art the Memory, Constancy, Intelligence, Old Age. Thou art the nourishment, contentment; Thou upholdest all; Thou art the beauty, peace, good knowledge, prosperity and happiness, Thou art the Goal, fame, and intellect and Thou art the Eternal Seed unmanifested.
Ch 22 Verse 28-29 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यदा यैः स्वरूपैः करोषीह कार्यं सुराणां च तेभ्यो नमामोऽद्य शान्त्यै । क्षमा योगनिद्रा दया त्वं विवक्षा स्थिता सर्वभूतेषु शस्तैः स्वरूपैः ॥ २८ ॥ कृतं कार्यमादौ त्वया यत्सुराणां हतोऽसौ महारिर्मदान्धो हयारिः । दया ते सदा सर्वदेवेषु देवि प्रसिद्धा पुराणेषु वेदेषु गीता ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation
We now bow down to those forms of Thine through which Thou dost serve the purpose of the Devas in this world as we are now in need of peace. Thou art forgiveness and mercy; Thou art the Yoga Nidrā (a state between sleep and wakefulness); Thou art the kindness and Thou residest in all the beings in so many forms, great and grand, and so very celebrated; O Goddess! Thou hadst already served the cause of the gods in killing our great enemy Mahiṣāsura, puffed up with vanity. Therefore Thy mercy is well known amongst the gods; what more, Thy mercy is known, since very ancient times and it is narrated in the Vedas.
Ch 22 Verse 30-31 Verse
Sanskrit Text
किमत्रास्ति चित्रं यदम्बा सुतं स्वं मुदा पालयेत्पोषयेत्सम्यगेव । यतस्त्वं जनित्री सुराणां सहाया कुरुष्वैकचित्तेन कार्यं समग्रम् ॥ ३० ॥ न वा ते गुणानामियत्तां स्वरूपं वयं देवि जानीमहे विश्ववन्द्ये । कृपापात्रमित्येव मत्वा तथास्मा- न्भयेभ्यः सदा पाहि पातुं समर्थे ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation
What wonder is there that a mother nourishes gladly her own sons and preserves them carefully! For Thou art the Mother of the Devas; Thou art the great source of help to them; therefore dost Thou fulfill all their desires with Thine whole heart. O Devī! We do not know the limit of Thy qualities nor of Thy forms; O Goddess! Thou art worshipped by the whole Universe. Thou art fully competent to save all from dangers; we are objects of Thy pity; dost Thou save us from our present troubles!
Ch 22 Verse 32-33 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विना बाणपातैर्विना मुष्टिघातै- र्विनाशूलखड्गैर्विना शक्तिदण्डैः । रिपून्हन्तुमेवासि शक्ता विनोदा- त्तथापीह लोकोपकाराय लीला ॥ ३२ ॥ इदं शाश्वतं नैव जानन्ति मूढा न कार्यं विना कारणं सम्भवेद्वा । वयं तर्कयामोऽनुमानं प्रमाणं त्वमेवासि कर्तास्य विश्वस्य चेति ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation
Thou art capable to kill enemies without shooting any arrrows, without striking any blows, without hurling any trident, axes, Śaktis, clubs, or any other weapons; merely by Thy mere will Thou canst kill; still for sports and for the good of all beings Thou incarnatest and fightest for the sake of Līlā. The ignorant persons know such things as birth, death, etc., that this world is not eternal; that no actions can be without any cause; we, therefore, ascertain by reasoning and inference that Thou art the Supreme Cause of this whole Universe.
Ch 22 Verse 34 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अजः सृष्टिकर्ता मुकुन्दोऽवितायं हरो नाशकृद्वै पुराणे प्रसिद्धः । न किं त्वत्प्रसूतास्त्रयस्ते युगादौ त्वमेवासि सर्वस्य तेनैव माता ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation
Brahmā is the Creator, Viṣṇu is the Preserver, and Maheśa is the Destroyer; so it is related in the Purāṇas. Thou again hast given birth to these three Gods in the respective cycles; therefore Thou art the Mother of all; there is no doubt in this.
Ch 22 Verse 35 Verse
Sanskrit Text
त्रिभिस्त्वं पुराराधिता देवि दत्ता त्वया शक्तिरुग्रा च तेभ्यः समग्रा । त्वया संयुतास्ते प्रकुर्वन्ति कामं जगत्पालनोत्पत्तिसंहारमेव ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation
O Devī! In days of yore, these three Devas worshipped Thee; Thou Wert pleased and gavest them all the best powers. Being thus endowed with Thy powers, they have been able to create, preserve and destroy this Universe beautifully.
Ch 22 Verse 36 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ते किं न मन्दमतयो यतयो विमूढा- स्त्वां ये न विश्वजननीं समुपाश्रयन्ति । विद्यां परां सकलकामफलप्रदां तां मुक्तिप्रदां विबुधवृन्दसुवन्दिताङ्घ्रिम् ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation
Art they not foolish, though they be Yatis (persons of self-controlled nature), who do not worship the Universal Mother, the Consciousness Incarnate, the Giver of liberation, on Whose feet are worshipped by the Devas, and worshipping Whom, one gets the fruits of all one’s desires?
Ch 22 Verse 37 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ये वैष्णवाः पाशपताश्च सौरा दम्भास्त एव प्रतिभान्ति नूनम् । ध्यायन्ति न त्वां कमलाञ्च लज्जां कान्तिं स्थितिं कीर्तिमथापि पुष्टिम् ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation
Certainly those Vaiṣṇavas, Sauras (worshippers of the Sun) and Paśupatas (worshippers of Śiva) are foolish braggarts who do not meditate Thee as the embodiment of Kamalā (prosperity), modesty, beauty, continuancy, Fame, nourishment.
Ch 22 Verse 38 Verse
Sanskrit Text
हरिहरादिभिरप्यथ सेविता त्वमिह देववरैरसुरैस्तथा । भुवि भजन्ति न येऽल्पधियो नरा जननि ते विधिना खलु वञ्चिताः ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! The Asuras, Hari, Hara and other great Devas worship Thee in this world; therefore those mortals are certainly deceived by their Creator that do not worship Thee on the surface of this earth.
Ch 22 Verse 39 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जलधिजापदपङ्कजरञ्जनं जतुरसेन करोति हरिः स्वयम् । त्रिनयनोऽपि धराधरजाङ्घ्रिपं- कजपरागनिषेवणतत्परः ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation
O Devī! Hari himself serves the lotus feet of Lakṣmī by colouring them (toes and other fingers of the feet) red with lac juice; Hara is very anxious to serve the lotus feet and take the dust thereof of Parvatī; Lakṣmī and Parvatī are but Thy part manifestations; therefore to serve them is, in other words to serve Thee.
Ch 22 Verse 40 Verse
Sanskrit Text
किमपरस्य नरस्य कथानकै- स्तव पदाब्जयुगं न भजन्ति के । विगतरागगृहाश्च दयां क्षमां कृतधियो मुनयोऽपि भजन्ति ते ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation
What to speak of other persons, even those who can discriminate between real and unreal and those who have left their worldly homes and have become dispassionate towards worldly objects, even those Munis worship forgiveness and mercy, that are but Thy parts; therefore who is there in this world that does not serve Thy lotus-feet!
Ch 22 Verse 41 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवि त्वदङ्घ्रिभजने न जना रता ये संसारकूपपतिताः पतिताः किलामी । ते कुष्ठगुल्मशिराधियुता भवन्ति दारिद्र्यदैन्यसहिता रहिताः सुखौघैः ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation
O Devī! Those human beings plunge into the dreadful wells of this Samsāra, the round of birth and death, and are deprived of all pleasures, who do not serve Thy lotus feet. What more can be said than the fact that those fallen beings suffer terribly from poverty, humility, leprosy, headache, and the chronic enlargement of spleen.
Ch 22 Verse 42 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ये काष्ठभारवहने यवसावहारे कार्ये भवन्ति निपुणा धनदारहीनाः । जानीमहेऽल्पमतिभिर्भवदङ्घ्रिसेवा पूर्वे भवे जननि तैर्न कृता कदापि ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! Those persons are void of any wealth and wife; they are the carriers of loads of wood and collect grass and leaves and show their skill in such acts; they are of little understanding and never they served in their previous births Thy lotus-feet. This we have come to know very well within our heart of hearts.
Ch 22 Verse 43 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । एवं स्तुता सुरैः सर्वैरम्बिका करुणान्विता । प्रादुर्बभूव तरसा रूपयौवनसंयुता ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! When all the Devas thus eulogised, instantly the Devī Ambikā, full of youth and beauty appeared there out of mercy.
Ch 22 Verse 44-46 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दिव्याम्बरधरा देवी दिव्यभूषणभूषिता । दिव्यमाल्यसमायुक्ता दिव्यचन्दनचर्चिता ॥ ४४ ॥ जगन्मोहनलावण्या सर्वलक्षणलक्षिता । अद्वितीयस्वरूपा सा देवानां दर्शनं गता ॥ ४५ ॥ जाह्नव्यां स्नातुकामा सा निर्गता गिरिगह्वरात् । दिव्यरूपधरा देवी विश्वमोहनमोहिनी ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation
That extraordinary beautiful Bhagavatī, endowed with all auspicious signs, and adorned with the Divine clothings, ornaments, and garlands and sandal paste, etc., appeared before the Devas. Before Whom, even the world enchanter Cupid bows down; with such beautiful, Divine appearance, the Devī emerged from the mountain cave in order to take Her ablutions in the Ganges.
Ch 22 Verse 47 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवान्स्तुतिपरानाह मेघगम्भीरया गिरा । प्रेमपूर्वं स्मितं कृत्वा कोकिलामञ्जुवादिनी ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation
That Devī, sweet voiced like a cuckoo, gladly smiling began to say to the Devas, singing hymns to Her, in a voice deep like that of a rumbling cloud.
Ch 22 Verse 48 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देव्युवाच । भो भोः सुरवराः कात्र भवद्भिः स्तूयते भृशम् । किमर्थं ब्रूत वः कार्यं चिन्ताविष्टाः कुतः पुनः ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation
The Devī said :-- O Best of Suras! Whom are you praising constantly in this place? What do you want! Why are you so anxious and seem to be so much care-worn? Do please tell all this to Me in detail.
Ch 22 Verse 49 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । तच्छ्रुत्वा भाषितं तस्या मोहिता रूपसम्पदा । प्रेमपूर्वं हृदुत्साहास्तामूचुः सुरसत्तमाः ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! The Devas were first enchanted by Her beauty and softness; then, being encouraged by Her sweet words, began to speak with great joy.
Ch 22 Verse 50-51 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवा ऊचुः । देवि स्तुमस्त्वां विश्वेशि प्रणताः स्म कृपार्णवे । पाहि नः सर्वदुःखेभ्यः संविग्नान्दैत्यतापितान् ॥ ५० ॥ पुरा त्वया महादेवि निहत्यासुरकण्टकम् । महिषं नो वरो दत्तः स्मर्तव्याहं सदापदि ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation
O Devī! We pray toThee, O Lord of his Universe! We bow down to Thee. O Thou, the Ocean of mercy! Protect us from all the troubles; we are very much care-worn and tormented by the Daityas. O Great Goddess! In ancient times Thou didst kill Mahiṣāsura, the source of troubles to all and then told us to remember Thee whenever any difficulty would arise.
Ch 22 Verse 52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्मरणाद्दैत्यजां पीडां नाशयिष्याम्यसंशयम् । तेन त्वं संस्मृता देवि नूनमस्माभिरित्यपि ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation
Then Thou wouldst undoubtedly remove all the troubles arising from the Daityas no sooner we remember Thee. O Devī! We have now remembered Thee for that very reason.
Ch 22 Verse 53 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अद्य शुम्भनिशुम्भौ द्वावसुरौ घोरदर्शनौ । उत्पन्नौ विघ्नकर्तारावहन्यौ पुरुषैः किल ॥ ५३ ॥
English Translation
At present the two dreadful Asuras, Śumbha and Niśumbha have sprang up and are creating great disturbances; and they cannot be killed by any male beings.
Ch 22 Verse 54 Verse
Sanskrit Text
रक्तबीजश्च बलवांश्चण्डमुण्डौ तथासुरौ । एतैरन्यैश्च देवानां हृतं राज्यं महाबलैः ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation
The powerful Raktavīja and Caṇḍa Muṇḍa and other Asuras united have dispossessed the Devas of their Heavens. Thou alone art our goal and refuge; without Thee there is none other to save us.
Ch 22 Verse 55 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गतिरन्या न चास्माकं त्वमेवासि महाबले । कुरु कार्यं सुराणां वै दुःखितानां सुमध्यमे ॥ ५५ ॥
English Translation
Therefore, O Beautiful One! Thou dost do this work for the Devas who are extremely troubled and distressed.
Ch 22 Verse 56 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवास्त्वदङ्घ्रिभजने निरताः सदैव ते दानवैरतिबलैर्विपदं सुनीताः । तान्देवि दुःखरहितान् कुरु भक्तियुक्ता- न्मातस्त्वमेव शरणं भव दुःखितानाम् ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation
O Powerful Devī! The Devas are always at the services of Thy lotus feet; still the very powerful Dānavas are throwing them into dangers; O Mother! Thou art the Preserver of the distressed; therefore dost Thou preserve the Devas, devoted to Thee.
Ch 22 Verse 57-58 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सकलभुवनरक्षा देवि कार्या त्वयाद्य स्वकृतमिति विदित्वा विश्वमेतद्युगादौ । जननि जगति पीडां दानवा दर्पयुक्ताः स्वबलमदसमेतास्ते प्रकुर्वन्ति मातः ॥ ५७ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां पञ्चमस्कन्धे देवकृतदेव्याराधनवर्णनं नाम द्वाविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.२२॥
English Translation
O Mother! The Dānavas, being very much emboldened by their powers, are creating many havocs on the surface of the Earth; now remembering that, in the beginning of the Yugas, Thou didst create all this Universe, Thou dost now ought to protect all this Universe. Here ends the Twenty-second Chapter of the Fifth Book on the eulogising of the Devī by the Devas in Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

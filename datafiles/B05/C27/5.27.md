# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 5, CHAPTER 27

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 1-2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ५.२७ सप्तविंशोऽध्यायः । रक्तबीजद्वारा देवीसमीपे शुम्भनिशुम्भसंवादवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> व्यास उवाच ।
> हतौ तौ दानवौ दृष्ट्वा हतशेषाश्च सैनिकाः ।
> पलायनं ततः कृत्वा जग्मुः सर्वे नृपं प्रति ॥ १ ॥
> 
> भिन्नाङ्गा विशिखैः केचित्केचिच्छिन्नकरास्तथा ।
> रुधिरस्रावदेहाश्च रुदन्तोऽभिययुः पुरे ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! Seeing the two Dānavas killed in the battle, the remnant soldiers all fled away back to Śumbha. Some of them were cut and wounded in many places by arrows, some had their arms severed, some were bleeding; thus they entered crying into the sky.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> गत्वा दैत्यपतिं सर्वे चक्रुर्बुम्बारवं मुहुः ।
> रक्ष रक्ष महाराज भक्षयत्यद्य कालिका ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On reaching the lord of the Daityas, they began to make frequently the noise indicative of danger and exclaimed, “O King! Save us, Save us; Kālikā is devouring everything today.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तया हतौ महावीरौ चण्डमुण्डौ सुरार्दनौ ।
> भक्षिताः सैनिकाः सर्वे वयं भग्ना भयातुराः ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The two great warriors Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa, the tormentors of the Devas, were slain by Her; all the soldiers were devoured by Her; we have fled away panic-stricken.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भीतिदञ्च रणस्थानं कृतं कालिकया प्रभो ।
> पातितैर्गजवीराश्वैर्दासेरकपदातिभिः ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Lord! Kālikā has rendered the battlefield horrible by the dead bodies of elephants, horses, camels, warriors, and foot soldiers.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 6-7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शोणितौघवहा कुल्या कृता मांसातिकर्दमा ।
> केशशैवलिनी भग्नरथचक्रविराजिता ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> छिन्नबाह्वादिमत्स्याढ्या शीर्षतुम्बीफलान्विता ।
> भयदा कातराणां वै सुराणां मोदवर्धिनी ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

A river of blood is flowing there of which the flesh of the soldiers is sufficient mud, their hairs are like aquatic plants, the broken chariot wheels are like whirlpools, the severed arms and feet are like fishes and their heads look like Tumbi fruits (long gourds).

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कुलं रक्ष महाराज पातालं गच्छ सत्वरम् ।
> क्रुद्धा देवी क्षयं सद्यः करिष्यति न संशयः ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Save your line; go quickly to Pātāla. The Devī has become angry and will, no doubt, destroy our race.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सिंहोऽपि भक्षयत्याजौ दानवान्दनुजेश्वर ।
> तथैव कालिका देवी हन्ति बाणैरनेकधा ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Even the lion is eating away the Dānavas; and the Kālikā Devī is killing innumerable Dānavas by Her arrows. Therefore, O King! What intentions are you cherishing in your mind?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 10-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्मात्त्वमपि राजेन्द्र मरणाय मृषा मतिम् ।
> करोषि सहितो भ्रात्रा शुम्भेन कुपिताशयः ॥ १० ॥
> 
> किं करिष्यति नार्येषा क्रूरा कुलविनाशिनी ।
> यस्या हेतोर्महाराज हन्तुमिच्छसि बान्धवान् ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Is it that you have desired to be merely slain with your younger brother Niśumbha! And what good purpose will this cruel woman, destroying your race, serve, for Whose sake, you have desired to kill all your friends?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दैवाधीनौ महाराज लोके जयपराजयौ ।
> अल्पार्थाय महद्दुःखं बुद्धिमान्न प्रकल्पयेत् ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Victory or defeat in this world are under the Daīva. The wise never risk to meet with great difficulties for the gratification of an ordinary whim.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 13-14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> चित्रं पश्य विधेः कर्म यदधीनं जगत् प्रभो ।
> निहता राक्षसाः सर्वे स्त्रिया पश्यैकयानया ॥ १३ ॥
> 
> जेता त्वं लोकपालानां सैन्ययुक्तो हि साम्प्रतम् ।
> एका प्रार्थयते बाला युद्धायेति सुसम्भ्रमः ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! You have conquered by the help of your army all the Lokapālas (guardians of the quarters of the sky); but now that Lady, though alone and unsupported by anybody, is challenging you to fight.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 15-16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पुरा त्वया तपस्तप्तं पुष्करे देवतायने ।
> वरदानाय सम्प्राप्तो ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ॥ १५ ॥
> 
> धात्रोक्तस्त्वं महाराज वरं वरय सुव्रत ।
> तदा त्वयामरत्वं च प्रार्थितं ब्रह्मणः किल ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! In ancient times, in the holy pilgrimage of Puṣkara, the sacred place of the Devas, you performed austerities when Brahmā, the Grandsire of all the worlds, came to you to grant a boon. Then you asked the boon and wanted to become to be immortal.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देवदैत्यमनुष्येभ्यो न भवेन्मरणं मम ।
> सर्पकिन्नरयक्षेभ्यः पुंल्लिङ्गवाचकादपि ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

But when Brahmā refused to grant it you wanted from him and were granted that you would not be killed by any male being, be he a Deva, Dānava, a man, Nāga, Kinnara, Yakṣa, or any other person.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्मात्त्वां हन्तुकामैषा प्राप्ता योषिद्वरा प्रभो ।
> युद्धं मा कुरु राजेन्द्र विचार्यैवं धियाधुना ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Lord! For that very reason we think now that this Lady has timely come to kill you. Think over it seriously and cease fighting.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देवी ह्येषा महामाया प्रकृतिः परमा मता ।
> कल्पान्तकाले राजेन्द्र सर्वसंहारकारिणी ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! This Devī is the great Mahā-Māyā, the Highest Prakriti; It is She that devours everything at the end of a Kalpa .

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उत्पादयित्री लोकानां देवानामीश्वरी शुभा ।
> त्रिगुणा तामसी देवी सर्वशक्तिसमन्विता ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

This auspicious Devī is the Creatrix of all the worlds and the Devas. She is the embodiment of the three qualities, endowed with all the powers.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अजय्या चाक्षया नित्या सर्वज्ञा च सदोदिता ।
> वेदमाता च गायत्री सन्ध्या सर्वसुरालया ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

She is Tāmasi, i.e., is the Destructrix of the whole world. This Devī can never be conquered, Imperishable, Eternal, She is the Sandhyā and the Refuge of the Devas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> निर्गुणा सगुणा सिद्धा सर्वसिद्धिप्रदाव्यया ।
> आनन्दानन्ददा गौरी देवानामभयप्रदा ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

She is Gāyatrī, the Mother of the Vedas. She is All-knowing and always manifested. This Undecaying Lady is void of any Prakritic attributes, though She at times possesses attributes.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एवं ज्ञात्वा महाराज वैरभावं त्यजानया ।
> शरणं व्रज राजेन्द्र देवी त्वां पालयिष्यति ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

She is Success Incarnate and bestows success to all; She is Bliss Herself and gives bliss to all. This Gaurī bids all the Devas discard all their fears.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> आज्ञाकरो भवैतस्याः सञ्जीवय निजं कुलम् ।
> हतशेषाश्च ये दैत्यास्ते भवन्तु चिरायुषः ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

She is Śuddha Sattva. Thus knowing, O King! Quit thy inimical feelings to Her; seek refuge unto Her; the Devī would then certainly protect you. Be obedient to Her and save your race. Then the remnant Dānavas will be able to live for a very long time.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इति तेषां वचः श्रुत्वा शुम्भः सुरबलार्दनः ।
> उवाच वचनं तथ्यं वीरवर्यगुणान्वितम् ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing thus, Śumbha, the conqueror of the Devas, told them truly in words becoming of a hero.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शुम्भ उवाच ।
> मौनं कुर्वन्तु भो मन्दा यूयं भग्ना रणाजिरात् ।
> शीघ्रं गच्छत पातालं जीविताशा बलीयसी ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Śumbha said :-- “O Fools! Hold your tongue.You have fled because your desire to live is very strong. So you better go to Pātāla without any delay.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दैवाधीनं जगत्सर्वं का चिन्तात्र जये मम ।
> देवास्तथैव ब्रह्माद्या दैवाधीना वयं यथा ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

This world is under the control of Fate; so I need not think about Victory. I am under this Fate just as Brahmā and other Devas are under it.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रह्मा विष्णुश्च रुद्रोऽयं यमोऽग्निर्वरुणस्तथा ।
> सूर्यश्चन्द्रस्तथा शक्रः सर्वे दैववशाः किल ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, Yama, Agni, Varuṇa, Sūrya, Candra, and Indra are all under the sway of this Destiny.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> का चिन्ता तर्हि मे मन्दा यद्भावि तद्भविष्यति ।
> उद्यमस्तादृशो भूयाद्यादृशी भवितव्यता ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Fools! Whatever is inevitable will certainly come to pass. What need I think over it then? The effort also comes to be of such a nature as will lead to that ordained by Fate.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सर्वथैव विचार्यैव न शोचन्ति बुधाः क्वचित् ।
> स्वधर्मं न त्यजन्तीह ज्ञानिनो मरणाद्भयात् ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus thinking, the wise never grieve; especially the wise ones never leave their own Dharma for fear of death.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 31-33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सुखं दुःखं तथैवायुर्जीवितं मरणं नृणाम् ।
> काले भवति सम्प्राप्ते सर्वथा दैवनिर्मितम् ॥ ३१ ॥
> 
> ब्रह्मा पतति काले स्वे विष्णुश्च पार्वतीपतिः ।
> नाशं गच्छन्त्यायुषोऽन्ते सर्वे देवाः सवासवाः ॥ ३२ ॥
> 
> तथाहमपि कालस्य वशगः सर्वथाधुना ।
> नाशं जयं वा गन्तास्मि स्वधर्मपरिपालनात् ॥ ३३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The happiness, pain, longevity, birth and death of all the embodied souls are all determined by Fate when their proper time arrives. See! When the time is over, Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Mahādeva, the lord of Pārvatī die away; on the expiration of their terms of lives, Indra and other Devas go to destruction.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 34-35</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> आहूतोऽप्यनया कामं युद्धायाबलया किल ।
> कथं पलायनपरो जीवेयं शरदां शतम् ॥ ३४ ॥
> 
> करिष्याम्यद्य सङ्ग्रामं यद्भावि तद्भवत्विह ।
> जयो वा मरणं वापि स्वीकरोमि यथा तथा ॥ ३५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Similarly I am also completely under the sway of time; so what doubt is there that I, too, will go to destruction when I have observed my own Dharma! This Lady is challenging me to fight of Her own will; how can I fly away and live hundreds of years.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दैवं मिथ्येति विद्वांसो वदन्त्युद्यमवादिनः ।
> युक्तियुक्तं वचस्तेषां ये जानन्त्यभिभाषितम् ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I will fight today. Let the result come whatever it may. I will gladly take the victory or defeat whatever the case may be.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 37-38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उद्यमेन विना कामं न सिध्यन्ति मनोरथाः ।
> कातरा एव जल्पन्ति यद्भाव्यं तद्भविष्यति ॥ ३७ ॥
> 
> अदृष्टं बलवन्मूढाः प्रवदन्ति न पण्डिताः ।
> प्रमाणं तस्य सत्त्वे किमदृश्यं दृश्यते कथम् ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The learned approving of the cause of effort declare Fate as fictitious; those who realise their sayings know that they are full of reason. Without exertion no end can be achieved; weak persons depend on the destiny.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 39</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अदृष्टं क्त्वापि दृष्टं स्यादेषा मूर्खविभीषिका ।
> अवलम्बं विनैवैषा दुःखे चित्तस्य धारणा ॥ ३९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Foolish persons say that Fate is strong; but the wise do not say so. There is no proof whether Fate exists or not; in fact what is called Fate is invisible; how can it then be seen? Has anybody seen Fate?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> चक्रीसमीपे संविष्टा संस्थिता पिष्टकारिणी ।
> उद्यमेन विना पिष्टं न भवत्येव सर्वथा ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

It is simply a scare for the illiterate; remedy only to console one’s mind in times of distress. Simply proximity to a grindmill without any man’s effort cannot grind a material.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उद्यमे च कृते कार्यं सिद्धिं यात्येव सर्वथा ।
> कदाचित्तस्य न्यूनत्वे कार्यं नैव भवेदपि ॥ ४१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Therefore if exertion be made in proportion to the gravity of the work, success is sure to ensue; if exertion be made less in proportion, the work does not come to a successful issue.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 42</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देशं कालञ्च विज्ञाय स्वबलं शत्रुजं बलम् ।
> कृतं कार्यं भवत्येव बृहस्पतिवचो यथा ॥ ४२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

If time, place and one’s enemieś forces be correctly taken into account and then if the proper attempts be made, success follows; thus Bṛhaspati has said.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 43</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इति निश्चित्य दैत्येन्द्रो रक्तबीजं महासुरम् ।
> प्रेषयामास सङ्ग्रामे सैन्येन महताऽऽवृतम् ॥ ४३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! Thus making a firm resolve to send the powerful Raktabīja to the battle with a vast army;

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 44</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शुम्भ उवाच ।
> रक्तबीज महाबाहो गच्छ त्वं समराङ्गणे ।
> कुरु युद्धं महाभाग यथा ते बलमाहितम् ॥ ४४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Śumbha said :-- “O Raktabīja! You are a very powerful hero; therefore do you go to the battle. O Fortunate One! Fight as you are the strength of your forces.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 45-46</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रक्तबीज उवाच ।
> महाराज न ते कार्या चिन्ता स्वल्पतरापि वा ।
> अहमेनां हनिष्यामि करिष्यामि वशे तव ॥ ४५ ॥
> 
> पश्य मे युद्धचातुर्यं क्वेयं बाला सुरप्रिया ।
> दासीं तेऽहं करिष्यामि जित्वेमां समरे बलात् ॥ ४६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Raktabīja said :-- “O King! You need not be a bit anxious for this work.Certainly I will either slay Her or I will bring Her under your control. Please see my skill in this warfare; that Lady, favourite of the gods, is worth nothing; I will just now conquer Her and make Her your slave.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 47-48</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इत्याभाष्य कुरुश्रेष्ठ रक्तबीजो महासुरः ।
> जगाम रथमारुह्य स्वसैन्यपरिवारितः ॥ ४७ ॥
> 
> हस्त्यश्वरथपादातवृन्दैश्च परिवेष्टितः ।
> निर्जगाम रथारूढो देवीं शैलोपरिस्थिताम् ॥ ४८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O Best of Kurus! Thus saying, the powerful Raktabīja mounted on his chariot and went to the battle accompanied by his forces. The battalion consisted of cavalry, infantry, chariots and elephants. Thus surrounded he departed from the city for that Devī, seated on a mountain top.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 49-50</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तमागतं समालोक्य देवी शङ्खमवादयत् ।
> भयदं सर्वदैत्यानां देवानां मोदवर्धनम् ॥ ४९ ॥
> 
> श्रुत्वा शङ्खस्वनं चोग्रं रक्तबीजोऽतिवेगवान् ।
> गत्वा समीपे चामुण्डां बभाषे वचनं मृदु ॥ ५० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then the Devī, seeing him coming, blew Her conchshell; the Dānavas were terrified at that sound and the joy of the Devas increased. Hearing that sound Raktabīja came very hurriedly to Cāmuṇḍā and began to speak to Her sweetly.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 51</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रक्तबीज उवाच ।
> बाले किं मां भीषयसि मत्वा त्वं कातरं किल ।
> शङ्खनादेन तन्वङ्गि वेत्सि किं धूम्रलोचनम् ॥ ५१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Girl! Do you think me weak and thus want to terrify me with the sound of a conchshell? O Lean One! Have you taken me to be a Dhūmralocana?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 52</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रक्तबीजोऽस्मि नाम्नाहं त्वत्सकाशमिहागतः ।
> युद्धेच्छा चेत्पिकालापे सज्जा भव भयं न मे ॥ ५२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Sweet speaking one! My name is Raktabīja; I have come here for Thy sake. If Thou desirest to fight, be prepared; I am not a bit afraid of that.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 53-56</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पश्याद्य मे बलं कान्ते दृष्टा ये कातरास्त्वया ।
> नाहं पङ्क्तिगतस्तेषां कुरु युद्धं यथेच्छसि ॥ ५३ ॥
> 
> वृद्धाश्च सेविताः पूर्वं नीतिशास्त्रं श्रुतं त्वया ।
> पतितं चार्थविज्ञानं विद्वद्गोष्ठी कृताथ वा ॥ ५४ ॥
> 
> साहित्यतन्त्रविज्ञानं चेदस्ति तव सुन्दरि ।
> शृणु मे वचनं पथ्यं तथ्यं प्रमितिबृंहितम् ॥ ५५ ॥
> 
> रसानाञ्च नवानां वै द्वावेव मुख्यतां गतौ ।
> शृङ्गारकः शान्तिरसो विद्वज्जनसभासु च ॥ ५६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Dear! You saw those who were weak; I do not belong to that class. Therefore dost Thou fight as Thou likest and then Thou wilt be able to ascertain my strength. O Beautiful! If Thou didst serve the old persons before, if Thou hadst heard the science of politics and morals, if Thou hadst studied the political economy, joined the assemblage of the Pundits or if Thou hast been well versed in literature and Tantras, then hear this my good counsel which will serve as a medicinal diet to Thee.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 57</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तयोः शृङ्गार एवादौ नृपभावे प्रतिष्ठितः ।
> विष्णुर्लक्ष्म्या सहास्ते वै सावित्र्या चतुराननः ॥ ५७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Out of the nine sentiments, the Śriṅgāra (Amorous love sentiments) and Śānti (Peace) are considered as the chief by the assemblage of the Pundits. Again out of these two, the love sentiment is the king.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 58-59</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शच्येन्द्रः शैलसुतया शङ्करः सह शेरते ।
> वल्ल्या वृक्षो मृगो मृग्या कपोत्या च कपोतकः ॥ ५८ ॥
> 
> एवं सर्वे प्राणभृतः संयोगरसिका भृशम् ।
> अप्राप्तभोगविभवा ये चान्ये कातरा नराः ॥ ५९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Drenched with this sentiment, Viṣṇu lives with Kamalā; Brahmā, the four-faced, lives with Sāvitri; Indra with Śacī and Śaṅkara resides with his wife Umā. The tree stands with creepers surrounding it, the deer lives with his female deer, the pigeon lives with the female pigeon; thus all the beings are very attached to this sentiment of remaining in couples.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 60</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भवन्ति यतयस्ते वै मूढा दैवेन वञ्चिताः ।
> असंसाररसज्ञास्ते वञ्चिता वञ्चनापरैः ॥ ६० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Those who cannot enjoy owing to certain disease or illness, they are deprived by Fate of such enjoyments.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 61</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मधुरालापनिपुणै रताः शान्तिरसे हि ते ।
> क्व ज्ञानं क्व च वैराग्यं वर्तमाने मनोभवे ॥ ६१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Those who are ignorant of this love sentiment in couples, they are deprived of it by the sweet jugglery of words of the cheat and yet attached to the Peace sentiment.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 62</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> लोभे क्रोधे च दुर्धर्षे मोहे मतिविनाशके ।
> तस्मात्त्वमपि कल्याणि कुरु कान्तं मनोहरम् ॥ ६२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When delusion, the destroyer of Buddhi, the common sense, occurs, when the violent indomitable anger, greed, and lust arise, where, then, is the place for knowledge and dispassion? Therefore, O Auspicious One! Dost Thou marry the beautiful Śumbha or the powerful Niśumbha.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 27 Verse 63-64</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शुम्भं सुराणां जेतारं निशुम्भं वा महाबलम् ।
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इत्युक्त्वा रक्तबीजोऽसौ विरराम पुरःस्थितः ।
> श्रुत्वा जहास चामुण्डा कालिका चाम्बिका तथा ॥ ६३ ॥
>
> *Colophon:*
> इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां पञ्चमस्कन्धे
> रक्तबीजद्वारा देवीसमीपे शुम्भनिशुम्भसंवादवर्णनं नाम सस्तविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.२७॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! When Raktabīja spoke all these words, standing before the Devī Kālikā, Ambikā and Cāmuṇḍā began to laugh. Here ends the Twenty-seventh Chapter of the Fifth Book on the description of the war of Raktabīja in Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam, the Mahā Purāṇam, of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

</details>

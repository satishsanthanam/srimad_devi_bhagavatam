# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 5, CHAPTER 23

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ५.२३ त्रयोविंशोऽध्यायः । देव्या सुग्रीवदूताय स्वव्रतकथनम् ।
> 
> 
> व्यास उवाच ।
> एवं स्तुता तदा देवी दैवतैः शत्रुतापितैः ।
> स्वशरीरात्परं रूपं प्रादुर्भूतं चकार ह ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! When the tormented Devas praised thus, the Devī created from Her body another supremely beautiful form.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पार्वत्यास्तु शरीराद्वै निःसृता चाम्बिका यदा ।
> कौशिकीति समस्तेषु ततो लोकेषु पठ्यते ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

This created form, the Ambikā Devī, became known in all the worlds as Kauśikī, as She came out of the physical sheath of the Devī Parvatī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 3-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> निःसृतायां तु तस्यां सा पार्वती तनुव्यत्ययात् ।
> कृष्णरूपाथ सञ्जाता कालिका सा प्रकीर्तिता ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> मषीवर्णा महाघोरा दैत्यानां भयवर्धिनी ।
> कालरात्रीति सा प्रोक्ता सर्वकामफलप्रदा ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When Kauśikī was created out of the body of Parvatī, the Parvatī’s body became transformed and turned out into a black colour and became known as KāIikā. Her terrible black appearance, when beheld, increases the terror even of the Daityas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अम्बिकायाः परं रूपं विरराज मनोहरम् ।
> सर्वभूषणसंयुक्तं लावण्यगुणसंयुतम् ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! This Devī is now become known in this world as Kālarātri, the night of destruction, at the end of the world, identified with Durgā, the Fulfiller of all the desires.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ततोऽम्बिका तदा देवानित्युवाच ह सस्मिता ।
> तिष्ठन्तु निर्भया यूयं हनिष्यामि रिपूनिह ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Ambikā Devī, then, began to look splendid, decked with various ornaments; Her beautiful form began to look very lovely. The Devī Ambikā then smiled a little and said, “Better be fearless; I will slay just now your enemies.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कार्यं वः सर्वथा कार्यं विहरिष्याम्यहं रणे ।
> निशुम्भादीन्वधिष्यामि युष्माकं सुखहेतवे ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

It is My iñcumbent duty to carry out your purposes; I will therefore slay in battle Niśumbha and others for the sake of your happiness.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इत्युक्त्वा सा तदा देवी सिंहारूढा मदोत्कटा ।
> कालिकां पार्श्वतः कृत्वा जगाम नगरे रिपोः ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus saying, the Devī Bhagavatī, elated with pride, mounted on lion and, taking Kālikā with Her, entered into the city of Śumbha, the enemy of the gods.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सा गत्वोपवने तस्थावम्बिका कालिकान्विता ।
> जगावथ कलं तत्र जगन्मोहनमोहनम् ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Ambikā went to a garden adjoining the city accompanied by Kālikā, and began to sing in such a sweet melodious tune that enchants even the God of Love, who fascinates the whole world.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> श्रुत्वा तन्मधुरं गानं मोहमीयुः खगा मृगाः ।
> मुदञ्च परमां प्रापुरमरा गगने स्थिताः ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

What more can be said than the fact that, hearing that sweet melodious song, the birds and beasts became enchanted; the Devas then began to feel much pleasure from the Sky.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 11-12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्मिन्नवसरे तत्र दानवौ शुम्भसेवकौ ।
> चण्डमुण्डाभिधौ घोरौ रममाणौ यदृच्छया ॥ ११ ॥
> 
> आगतौ ददृशाते तु तां तदा दिव्यरूपिणीम् ।
> अम्बिकां गानसंयुक्तां कालिकां पुरतः स्थिताम् ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

In the meanwhile Caṇḍa, Muṇḍa the two dreadful Asuras, and attendants of Śumbha, came out accidentally there on their sportive excursions and saw the beautiful Ambikā Devī singing and Kālikā Devī sitting before Her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दृष्ट्वा तां दिव्यरूपाञ्च दानवौ विस्मयान्वितौ ।
> जग्मतुस्तरसा पार्श्वं शुम्भस्य नृपसत्तम ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O best of Kings! No sooner Caṇḍa, Muṇḍa saw the extraordinary beauty of the Goddess Bhagavatī, than they went at once to Śumbha.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 14-15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तौ गत्वा तं समासीनं दैत्यानामधिपं गृहे ।
> ऊचतुर्मधुरां वाणीं प्रणम्य शिरसा नृपम् ॥ १४ ॥
> 
> राजन् हिमालयात्कामं कामिनी काममोहिनी ।
> सम्प्राप्ता सिंहमारूढा सर्वलक्षणसंयुता ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On approaching towards the lord of the Daityas sitting in his room, they bowed down and told thus in a sweet voice :-- "O King! Here has come from the Himālayās a woman accidentally, mounted on a lion; Her limbs are shining with all good signs so much so that even the God of love would be enchanted by Her sight.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नेदृशी देवलोकेऽस्ति न गन्धर्वपुरे तथा ।
> न दृष्टा न श्रुता क्वापि पृथिव्यां प्रमदोत्तमा ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nowhere, in the Devalokas, the Gandarbha Lokas or in this earth can be found such a beautiful lady; we never saw nor heard about such a lady before.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> गानञ्च तादृशं राजन् करोति जनरञ्जनम् ।
> मृगास्तिष्ठन्ति तत्पार्श्वे मथुरस्वरमोहिताः ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! That lady is singing so beautifully and pleasingly to all that even the deer are standing motionless by Her side enchanted, as it were, by Her melodious voice.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ज्ञायतां कस्य पुत्रीयं किमर्थमिह चागता ।
> गृह्यतां राजशार्दूल तव योग्यास्ति कामिनी ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! That Lady is fit for you; therefore determine first whose daughter is this lady, what for she has come there and then marry Her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ज्ञात्वाऽऽनय गृहे भार्यां कुरु कल्याणलोचनाम् ।
> निश्चितं नास्ति संसारे नारी त्वेवंविधा किल ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Know this as certain that such a beautiful lady is not to be found anywhere in this world. Therefore do you bring Her to your house and marry Her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देवानां सर्वरत्नानि गृहीतानि त्वया नृप ।
> कस्मान्नेमां वरारोहां प्रगह्णासि नृपोत्तम ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Lord of men! You have acquired all the gems and jewels of the Devas; why not, then, accept this Gem in the form of a lady?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इन्द्रस्यैरावतः श्रीमान्पारिजाततरुस्तथा ।
> गृहीतोऽश्वः सप्तमुखस्त्वया नृप बलात्किल ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! You have taken by force the exquisitely beautiful Airāvata elephant of Indra, the Pārijāta Tree, the seven faced horse Uccaiśravā, and many other jewels.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विमानं वैधसं दिव्यं मरालध्वजसंयुतम् ।
> त्वयात्तं रत्नभूतं तद्बलेन नृप चाद्भुतम् ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

You have acquired by your might the Prince of Jewels, the celestial car of the Creator Brahmā, ensigned by the emblematic Swan.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कुबेरस्य निधिः पद्मस्त्वया राजन् समाहृतः ।
> छत्रं जलपतेः शुभ्रं गृहीतं तत्त्वया बलात् ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

You have dispossessed Kuvera of his treasure of the value of a Padma (one thousand billion) and Varuṇa, the God of oceans, of his white umbrella.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पाशश्चापि निशुम्भेन भ्रात्रा तव नृपोत्तम ।
> गृहीतोऽस्ति हठात्कामं वरुणस्य जितस्य च ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! When Varuṇa was defeated, your brother Niśumbha took perforce his Pāśa weapon.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अम्लानपङ्कजां तुभ्यं मालां जलनिधिर्ददौ ।
> भयात्तव महाराज रत्नानि विविधानि च ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! The Great Ocean gave you, out of terror, various jewels and honoured you by presenting a garland of lotuses which never fade away.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मृत्योः शक्तिर्यमस्यापि दण्डः परमदारुणः ।
> त्वया जित्वा हृतः कामं किमन्यद्वर्ण्यते नृप ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

What more can be said than the fact that you have conquered the Death and took away His force and that you have easily conquered Yama, the God of Death and have taken from Him His horrible staff.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 27-28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कामधेनुर्गहीताद्य वर्तते सागरोद्भवा ।
> मेनकाद्या वशे राजंस्तव तिष्ठन्ति चाप्सराः ॥ २७ ॥
> 
> एवं सर्वाणि रत्नानि त्वयात्तानि बलादपि ।
> कस्मान्न गृह्यते कान्तारत्नमेषा वराङ्गना ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus you have got by your strength all the jewels. Why, then, are you not taking this exquisitely beautiful lady, the Prince of Jewels, amongst women.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सर्वाणि ते गृहस्थानि रत्नानि विशदान्यथ ।
> अनया सम्भविष्यन्ति रत्नभूतानि भूपते ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! All the jewels in your house, will serve their real purpose, no doubt, then and then only when they will shine with this queen of jewels, this Lady.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> त्रिषु लोकेषु दैत्येन्द्र नेदृशी वर्तते प्रिया ।
> तस्मात्तामानयाशु त्वं कुरु भार्यां मनोहराम् ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Lord of the Daityas! There cannot be seen in all the Trilokas such a Beautiful Lady as this that I have now described before you. Therefore bring this Beautiful Lady quickly and accept Her as your wife.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 31-32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इति श्रुत्वा तयोर्वाक्यं मधुरं मधुराक्षरम् ।
> प्रसन्नवदनः प्राह सुग्रीवं सनिधौ स्थितम् ॥ ३१ ॥
> 
> गच्छ सुग्रीव दूतत्वं कुरु कार्यं विचक्षण ।
> वक्तव्यञ्च तथा तत्र यथाभ्येति कृशोदरी ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing thus the sweet words of Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa, Śumbha spoke gladly to Sugrīva who was close by :-- “Go, Sugrīva, do my messenger’s work; you are well skilled in these things. Speak so that the Beautiful Lady of thin waist may come over to me.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उपायौ द्वौ प्रयोक्तव्यौ कान्तासु सुविचक्षणैः ।
> सामदाने इति प्राहुः शृङ्गाररसकोविदाः ॥ ३३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Those who are well versed in the science of amorous love declare that only two methods are to be adopted by the clever persons towards the female sex :-- (1) conciliation and gentle words and (2) gifts and presents.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भेदे प्रयुज्यमानेऽपि रसाभासस्तु जायते ।
> निग्रहे रसभङ्गः स्यात्तस्मात्तौ दूषितौ बुधैः ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

For if the policy of division or sowing dissensions be applied, then hypocrisy is shewn and that means the improper manifestation of love sentiment; whereas if chastisement be applied then the love sentiment becomes interrupted.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 35</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सामदानमुखैर्वाक्यैः श्लक्ष्णैर्नर्मयुतैस्तथा ।
> का न याति वशे दूत कामिनी कामपीडिता ॥ ३५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Therefore, the wise have condemned these as corrupt means. O Messenger! Where is that woman who does not come round excited with passion when good and sweet words are spoken to her in accordance with the Śama and Dāna methods?”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 36-37</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> सुग्रीवस्तु वचः श्रुत्वा शुम्भोक्तं सुप्रियं पटु ।
> जगाम तरसा तत्र यत्रास्ते जगदम्बिका ॥ ३६ ॥
> 
> सोऽपश्यत्सुमुखीं कान्तां सिंहस्योपरिसंस्थिताम् ।
> प्रणम्य मधुरं वाक्यमुवाच जगदम्बिकाम् ॥ ३७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- Sugrīva, hearing the nice skilled words of Śumbha went hurriedly to the spot where existed the Mother of the Universe. He saw the Fair Lady mounted on a lion, saluted Her and spoke gently and sweetly as follows :--

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दूत उवाच ।
> वरोरु त्रिदशारातिः शुम्भः सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरः ।
> त्रैलोक्याधिपतिः शूरः सर्वजिद्राजते नृपः ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The messenger said :-- “O Beautiful One! Śumbha, the enemy of the Gods and the King of all, is beautiful in all respects, the ruler of the three Lokas, a great hero and conqueror of all.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 39</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तेनाहं प्रेषितः कामं त्वत्सकाशं महात्मना ।
> त्वद्रूपश्रवणासक्तचित्तेनातिविदूयता ॥ ३९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing your beauty and loveliness, that high-souled monarch is so much attached to you and has become so very passionate that he has sent me to you to express his views.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 40-41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वचनं तस्य तन्वङ्गि शृणु प्रेमपुरःसरम् ।
> प्रणिपत्य यथा प्राह दैत्यानामधिपस्त्वयि ॥ ४० ॥
> 
> देवा मया जिताः सर्वे त्रैलोक्याधिपतिस्त्वहम् ।
> यज्ञभागानहं कान्ते गह्णामीह स्थितः सदा ॥ ४१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O One of delicate limbs! Please hear what that Lord of the Daityas has spoken to Thee, after duly saluting Thee, words full of love and affection towards Thee :-- O Beloved! I have defeated all the Devas and have thus become the Lord of the three worlds; specially I partake of all the offerings made in sacrificial acts and ceremonies, without moving away from my house.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 42</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> हृतसारा कृता नूनं द्यौर्मया रत्नवर्जिता ।
> यानि रत्नानि देवानां तानि चाहृतवानहम् ॥ ४२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I have taken away all the gems, jewels and wealth that belonged to the Devas; consequently the abode of the Gods has become now worthless, on account of all its jewels being carried sway.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 43</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भोक्ताहं सर्वरत्नानां त्रिषु लोकेषु भामिनि ।

O Fair One! I am now enjoying all the jewels that exist in the Trilokas; so much so that all the Devas, Asuras, and human beings are passing away their times, subservient to Me.

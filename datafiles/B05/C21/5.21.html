Scriptures

Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam

BOOK 5 • CHAPTER 21
On the conquest of the Heavens by Śumbha and Niśumbha

Ch 21 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
५.२१ एकविंशोऽध्यायः । शुम्भनिशुम्भद्वारा स्वर्गविजयवर्णनम् । व्यास उवाच । शृणु राजन् प्रवक्ष्यामि देव्याश्चरितमुत्तमम् । सुखदं सर्वजन्तूनां सर्वपापप्रणाशनम् ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! I am describing to you that excellent pure life and doings of the Devī that destroy all the sins of all the beings and make them happy.
Ch 21 Verse 2-3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यथा शुम्भो निशुम्भश्च भ्रातरौ बलवत्तरौ । बभूवतुर्महावीरौ अवध्यौ पुरुषैः किल ॥ २ ॥ बहुसेनावृतौ शूरौ देवानां दुःखदौ सदा दुराचारौ मदोत्सिक्तौ बहुदानवसंयुतौ ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
In days of yore, there were two very powerful demons Śumbha and Niśumbha; they were two brothers, strong heroes and invulnerable by the male persons. Those two wicked Asuras were surrounded with numerable Dānavas; they tormented always the Devas.
Ch 21 Verse 4-5 Verse
Sanskrit Text
हतावम्बिकया तौ तु सङ्ग्रामेऽतीव दारुणे । देवानाञ्ज हितार्थाय सर्वैः परिचरैः सह ॥ ४ ॥ चण्डमुण्डौ महाबाहू रक्तबीजोऽतिदारुणः । धूम्रलोचननामा च निहतास्ते रणाङ्गणे ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation
Then the Goddess Ambikā, for the good of the Devas, killed Śumbha and Niśumbha with all their attendants in a very dreadful battle. In the battlefield the Devī killed their main assistants Caṇḍa Muṇḍa and the exceedingly terrible Rakta Vīja and Dhumralocana.
Ch 21 Verse 6 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तान्निहत्य सुराणां सा जहार भयमुत्तमम् । स्तुता सम्पूजिता देवैर्गिरौ हेमाचले शुभे ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation
When the Devī destroyed those Dānavas, the Devas became fearless; the Devas then went to the beautiful Sumeru mountain and praised Her and chanted hymns to Her.
Ch 21 Verse 7 Verse
Sanskrit Text
राजोवाच । कावेतावसुरावादौ कथं तौ बलिनां वरौ । केन संस्थापितौ चेह स्त्रीवध्यत्वं कुतो गतौ ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
Hearing about the names of Śumbha and Niśumbha, Janamejaya asked :-- O best of Munis! Who were those two Asuras? How came they to be most powerful? Who put them here? Why were they vulnerable to women only?
Ch 21 Verse 8 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तपसा वरदानेन कस्य जातौ महाबलौ । कथञ्च निहतौ सर्वं कथयस्व सविस्तरम् ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation
Under whose tapasyā and under whose boon did they become so strong? And why did that great Devī kill them? Describe all these to me in detail.
Ch 21 Verse 9 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । शृणु राजन्कथां दिव्यां सर्वपापप्रणाशिनीम् । देव्याश्चरितसंयुक्तां सर्वार्थफलदां शुभाम् ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King, I am describing to you that beautiful anecdote where the Devī’s holy deeds are involved. Hear. This incident full of all that is good, destroys the hearer’s all sins and grants them all their desired ends.
Ch 21 Verse 10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पुरा शुम्भनिशुम्भौ द्वावसुरौ भूमिमण्डले । पातालतश्च सम्प्राप्तौ भ्रातरौ शुभदर्शनौ ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
In days of yore, Śumbha and Niśumbha, the two fair and good looking brothers came out of Pātāla to this earth.
Ch 21 Verse 11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तौ प्राप्तयौवनौ चैव चेरतुस्तप उत्तमम् । अन्नोदकं परित्यज्य पुष्करे लोकपावने ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
These two Asuras, when they grew to their manhood, performed severe asceticism in Puṣkara, the holy place of pilgrimage, the most purifying place in this world and they refused to eat rice and water.
Ch 21 Verse 12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वर्षाणामयुतं यावद्योगविद्यापरायणौ । एकत्रैवासनं कृत्वा तेपाते परमं तपः ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
They became so very skilled in their Yoga practices that they passed away in their one posture and seat one Ajuta (10,000) years. Thus they performed very difficult Tapasyā.
Ch 21 Verse 13 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तयोस्तुष्टोऽभवद् ब्रह्मा सर्वलोकपितामहः । तत्रागतश्च भगवानारुह्य वरटापतिम् ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation
Then the God Brahmā, the Grandsire of all, became pleased with their asceticism and appeared before them, riding on His vehicle, the Swan.
Ch 21 Verse 14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तावुभौ च जगत्स्रष्टा दृष्ट्वा ध्यानपरौ स्थितौ । उत्तिष्ठत महाभागौ तुष्टोऽहं तपसा किल ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
The Creator, seeing them thus deeply merged in meditation, asked them to get up from that state and told them thus :-- “I have become pleased with your asceticism.
Ch 21 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वाञ्छितं वां वरं कामं ददामि ब्रुवतामिह । कामदोऽहं समायातो दृष्ट्वा वां तपसो बलम् ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
I fulfil the desires of all the Lokas; I have now come to you, pleased to see you so very strong in your ascetic practices; better ask your desired boons from me; I will grant them to you.”
Ch 21 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य प्रबुद्धौ तौ समाहितौ । प्रदक्षिणक्रियां कृत्वा प्रणामं चक्रतुस्तदा ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing thus the Grandsire’s wards, Śumbha and Niśumbha got up from their meditation; concentrating their attention towards Him, circumambulated Him and bowed down to Him with their hearts full of reverence.
Ch 21 Verse 17-18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
दण्डवत्प्रणिपातञ्च कृत्वा तौ दुर्बलाकृती । ऊचतुर्मधुरां वाचं दीनौ गद्गदया गिरा ॥ १७ ॥ देवदेव दयासिन्धो भक्तानामभयप्रद । अमरत्वञ्च नौ ब्रह्मन्देहि तुष्टोऽसि चेद्विभो ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
The two Asuras were very weak, lean and thin by their hard tapasyā and they looked very humble. They fell down before Him like a piece of wood and began to speak in a sweet voice, choked by intense feelings. O Brāhmaṇ! O Deva of the Devas! O Thou, the Ocean of Mercy! O Destroyer of fear of the devotees! O Lord! If Thou art pleased then dost Thou grant us immortality.
Ch 21 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मरणादपरं किञ्चिद्भयं नास्ति धरातले । तस्माद्भयाच्च सन्त्रस्तौ युष्माकं शरणं गतौ ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
There is nothing in this world more fearful than death; we two have taken refuge unto Thee, being afraid of this death.
Ch 21 Verse 20 Verse
Sanskrit Text
त्राहि त्वं देवदेवेश जगत्कर्तः क्षमानिधे । परिस्फोटय विश्वात्मन् सद्यो मरणजं भयम् ॥ २० ॥
English Translation
O Thou, Ocean of mercy! O Creator of the world! O Lord of the Devas! O Universal Soul! Protect us from this fear due to the terrible Death.
Ch 21 Verse 21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ब्रह्मोवाच । किमिदं प्रार्थनीयं वो विपरीतं तु सर्वथा । अदेयं सर्वथा सर्वैः सर्वेभ्यो भुवनत्रये ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
Brahmā said :-- Is this the boon that you ask? This is in every way, against the Law of Nature; for no one, in these three Lokas, can grant this boon to anybody.
Ch 21 Verse 22 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जातस्य हि ध्रुवो मृत्युर्ध्रुवं जन्म मृतस्य च । मर्यादा विहिता लोके पूर्वं विश्वकृता किल ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation
When one becomes born, one must die; and when one dies, one must be born again. This Law is ordained in this world by the Supreme Creator of this Universe, from time immemorial.
Ch 21 Verse 23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मर्तव्यं सर्वथा सर्वैः प्राणिभिर्नात्र संशयः । अन्यं प्रार्थयतं कामं ददामि यच्च वाञ्छितम् ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
Therefore all the beings must die; there is no doubt in this. Better ask any other boon that you desire; I will grant that to you.
Ch 21 Verse 24-25 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । तदाकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य सुविमृश्य च दानवौ । ऊचतुः प्रणिपत्याथ ब्रह्माणं पुरतः स्थितम् ॥ २४ ॥ पुरुषैरमराद्यैश्च मानवैर्मृगपक्षिभिः । अवध्यत्वं कृपासिन्धो देहि नौ वाञ्छितं वरम् ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing thus the words of Brahmā, the two Dānavas pondered over the matter and bowed down to the Prajāpati, the Lord of the Creation and said :-- O Merciful One! Grant us then so that we shall be invulnerable to any of the male of the Immortal Devas down to human beings and birds and deers; this is the boon that we ask.
Ch 21 Verse 26 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नारी बलवती कास्ति या नौ नाशं करिष्यति । न बिभीवः स्त्रियः कामं त्रैलोक्ये सचराचरे ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation
Where exists the woman so powerful as to kill us? We never fear any woman in all the three Lokas.
Ch 21 Verse 27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अवध्यौ भ्रातरौ स्यातां नरेभ्यः पङ्कजोद्भव । भयं न स्त्रीजनेभ्यश्च स्वभावादबला हि सा ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
O Lotus-born! We, the two brothers, want not to be killed by any male; the females are naturally weak therefore we need not fear them.
Ch 21 Verse 28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । इति श्रुत्वा तयोर्वाक्यं प्रददौ वाञ्छितं वरम् । ब्रह्मा प्रसन्नमनसा जगामाथ स्वमालयम् ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing their words, the Grandsire Brahmā gladly granted them their desired boon and returned to His own abode.
Ch 21 Verse 29-30 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गतेऽथ भवने तस्मिन्दानवौ स्वगृहं गतौ । भृगुं पुरोहितं कृत्वा चक्रतुः पूजनं तदा ॥ २९ ॥ शुभे दिने सुनक्षत्रे जातरूपमयं शुभम् । कृत्वा सिंहासनं दिव्यं राज्यार्थं प्रददौ मुनिः ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation
On Brahmā going away, the two Dānavas, too, returned to their own places. They then appointed the Muni Bhṛgu as their priest and began to worship him. Bhṛgu, the best of the Munis, then, on an auspicious day and when the star was benign, got a beautiful golden throne built and gave it to the king.
Ch 21 Verse 31 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शुम्भाय ज्येष्ठभूताय ददौ राज्यासनं शुभम् । सेवनार्थं तदैवाशु सम्प्राप्ता दानवोत्तमाः ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation
Śumbha, being the eldest, was then installed on the auspicious throne as the king; the other brave and excellent demons began to assemble there quickly for serving him.
Ch 21 Verse 32 Verse
Sanskrit Text
चण्डमुण्डौ महावीरौ भ्रातरौ बलदर्पितौ । सम्प्राप्तौ सैन्यसंयुक्तौ रथवाजिगजान्वितौ ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation
The two great warriors Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa, proud on account of their great strength came there with their large armies, chariots, horses, and elephants.
Ch 21 Verse 33 Verse
Sanskrit Text
धूम्रलोचननामा च तद्रूपश्चण्डविक्रमः । शुम्भञ्च भूपतिं श्रुत्वा तदागाद्बलसंयुतः ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation
Similarly the valiant warriors Dhumralocana, hearing that Śumbha had become their King, came there with his own army.
Ch 21 Verse 34 Verse
Sanskrit Text
रक्तबीजस्तथा शूरो वरदानबलाधिकः । अक्षौहिणीभ्यां संयुक्तस्तत्रैवागत्य सङ्गतः ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation
There came up also at that time the great warrior Rakta Vīja, more powerful on account of his getting a boon, attended by his army of two Akṣauhiṇī soldiers.
Ch 21 Verse 35-36 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तस्यैकं कारणं राजन् सङ्ग्रामे युध्यतः सदा । देहाद्रुधिरसम्पातस्तस्य शस्त्राहतस्य च ॥ ३५ ॥ जायते च यदा भूमावुत्पद्यन्ते ह्यनेकशः । तादृशाः पुरुषाः क्रूरा बहवः शस्त्रपाणयः ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation
O King! Hear why this Rakta Vīja became so very unconquerable; whenever this Asura was wounded by any weapon, if one drop of blood fell on the ground, at once would be created so many innumerable Asuras, resembling his wicked nature and with similar weapons in their hands.
Ch 21 Verse 37 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सम्भवन्ति तदाकारास्तद्रूपास्तत्पराक्रमाः । युद्धं पुनस्ते कुर्वन्ति पुरुषा रक्तसम्भवाः ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation
The Asuras born of this blood would have similar appearances and would be similar in strength and ready to fight at once when they were born.
Ch 21 Verse 38 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अतः सोऽपि महावीर्यः सङ्ग्रामेऽतीव दुर्जयः । अवध्यः सर्वभूतानां रक्तबीजो महासुरः ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation
That great warrior, the great Demon Rakta Vīja was unconquerable in battle for this very reason and no being could now kill him.
Ch 21 Verse 39 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अन्ये च बहवः शूराश्चतुरङ्गसमन्विताः । शुम्भञ्च नृपतिं मत्वा बभूवुस्तस्य सेवकाः ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation
The other Asuras, when they heard that Śumbha had become their king, came up there with their armies consisting of four divisions of elephants, chariots, cavalry and infantry and began to serve him.
Ch 21 Verse 40 Verse
Sanskrit Text
असङ्ख्याता तदा जाता सेना शुम्भनिशुम्भयोः । पृथिव्याः सकलं राज्यं गृहीतं बलवत्तया ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation
The army of Śumbha and Niśumbha thus became countless; and they forcibly conquered and got possession of all the kingdoms that existed then on the surface of the earth.
Ch 21 Verse 41 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सेनायोगं तदा कृत्वा निशुम्भः परवीरहा । जगाम तरसा स्वर्गे शचीपतिजयाय च ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation
Then Niśumbha, the destroyer of enemies, collected his army and marched up to the Heavens without any delay to conquer Indra, the Lord of Śacī.
Ch 21 Verse 42 Verse
Sanskrit Text
चकारासौ महायुद्धं लोकपालैः समन्ततः । वृत्रहा वज्रपातेन ताडयामास वक्षसि ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation
He fought very hard with all the Lokapālas on all sides when Indra struck him on his breast with His thunderbolt.
Ch 21 Verse 43 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स वज्राभिहतो भूमौ पपात दानवानुजः । भग्नं बलं तदा तस्य निशुम्भस्य महात्मनः ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation
Niśumbha fell unconscious on the ground with that blow when his soldiers, defeated in the battle, fled away on all sides.
Ch 21 Verse 44 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भ्रातरं मूर्च्छितं श्रुत्वा शुम्भः परबलार्दनः । तत्रागत्य सुरान्मर्वांस्ताडयामास सायकैः ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation
Śumbha, the destroyer of the enemieś forces, hearing the unconscious state of the younger brother, came up at once on the field and shot at the Devas with multitudes of arrows.
Ch 21 Verse 45 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कृतं युद्धं महत्तेन शुम्भेनाक्लिष्टकर्मणा । निर्जितास्तु सुराः सर्वे सेन्द्राः पालाश्च सर्वशः ॥ ४५ ॥
English Translation
The untiring Śumbha fought so violently that Indra and the other Devas and Lokapalas were defeated.
Ch 21 Verse 46 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ऐन्द्रं पदं तदा तेन गृहीतं बलवत्तया । कल्पपादपसंयुक्तं कामधेनुसमन्वितम् ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation
Śumbha then took away, perforce, the position of Indra and he occupied the Celestial Tree and Heavenly milching cow that yielded all desires and other excellent things over which Indra used to reign.
Ch 21 Verse 47-48 Verse
Sanskrit Text
त्रैलोक्यं यज्ञभागाश्च हृतास्तेन महात्मना । नन्दनं च वनं प्राप्य मुदितोऽभून्महासुरः ॥ ४७ ॥ सुधायाश्चैव पानेन सुखमाप महासुरः । कुबेरं स च निर्जित्य तस्य राज्यं चकार ह ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation
In fact, that high-souled Asura got the dominion of the three Lokas and took away all those that were offered at the sacrifices. He became highly glad on getting the Nandana Garden and was extremely delighted when he drank the celestial nectar. He then defeated in battle Kuvera, the god of wealth and occupied his kingdom.
Ch 21 Verse 49 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अधिकारं तथा भानोः शशिनश्च चकार ह । यमञ्चैव विनिर्जित्य जग्राह तत्पदं तथा ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation
He defeated the Moon, Sun, and Yama, the God of Death and occupied their positions.
Ch 21 Verse 50 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वरुणस्य तथा राज्यं चकार वह्निकर्म च । वायोः कार्यं निशुम्भश्च चकार स्वबलान्वितः ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation
Surrounded by his army, Niśumbha dispossessed Varuṇa, Fire, and Air of their kingdoms and began to reign in their stead.
Ch 21 Verse 51 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ततो देवा विनिर्धूता हृतराज्या हृतश्रियः । सन्त्यज्य नन्दनं सर्वे निर्ययुर्गिरिगह्वरे ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation
Thus deprived of their kingdoms, prosperity and wealth, the Devas left the Nandana Garden and fled, out of terror, to the caves of hills and mountains.
Ch 21 Verse 52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
हृताधिकारास्ते सर्वे बभ्रमुर्विजने वने । निरालम्बा निराधारा निस्तेजस्का निरायुधाः ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation
Thus deprived of all their rights, the Devas without any weapons, without any lustre, without any home, and without anywhere to go, began to wander in lonely forests.
Ch 21 Verse 53-54 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विचेरुरमराः सर्वे पर्वतानां गुहासु च । उद्यानेषु च शून्येषु नदीनां गह्वरेषु च ॥ ५३ ॥ न प्रापुस्ते सुखं वापि स्थानभ्रष्टा विचेतसः । लोकपाला महाराज दैवाधीनं सुखं किल ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation
O King! All the Immortals began to knock about in lonely gardens, mountain caves and rivers; and nowhere they found happiness; for happiness depends entirely unto the hands of Fate.
Ch 21 Verse 55-57 Verse
Sanskrit Text
बलवन्तो महाभागा बहुज्ञा धनसंयुताः । काले दुःखं तथा दैन्यमाप्नुवन्ति नराधिप ॥ ५५ ॥ चित्रमेतन्महाराज कालस्यैव विचेष्टितम् । यः करोति नरं तावद्राजानं भिक्षुकं ततः ॥ ५६ ॥ दातारं याचकं चैव बलवन्तं तथाबलम् । पण्डितं विकलं कामं शूरं चातीव कातरम् ॥ ५७ ॥
English Translation
O Lord of men! Even those fortunate souls, who are powerful, and wealthy and wise, meet at times with distress and poverty. O King! How marvellous are the ways and manners of Time! It makes kings and donors beggars; it renders the powerful, weak; literates, illiterates; and it makes great warriors into terrible cowards.
Ch 21 Verse 58 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मखानाञ्च शतं कृत्वा प्राप्येन्द्रासनमुत्तमम् । पुनर्दुःखं परं प्राप्तं कालस्य गतिरीदृशी ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation
O King! Vāsava performed one hundred horse-sacrifices and got the excellent Indra’s position; but again be fell into extreme difficulties; thus runs the wheel of Time.
Ch 21 Verse 59 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कालः करोति धर्मिष्ठं पुरुषं ज्ञानसंयुतम् । तमेवातीव पापिष्ठं ज्ञानलेशविवर्जितम् ॥ ५९ ॥
English Translation
It is Time that bestows the gem of knowledge to a person and it is Time again that deprives that very same man of his wisdom and makes him a great sinner.
Ch 21 Verse 60-61 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न विस्मयोऽत्र कर्तव्यः सर्वथा कालचेष्टिते । ब्रह्मविष्णुहरादीनामपीदृक्कष्टचेष्टितम् ॥ ६० ॥ विष्णुर्जननमाप्नोति सूकरादिषु योनिषु । हरः कपाली सञ्जातः कालेनैव बलीयसा ॥ ६१ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां पञ्चमस्कन्धे शुम्भनिशुम्भद्वारा स्वर्गविजयवर्णनं नामकविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.२१॥
English Translation
The Bhagavān Viṣṇu takes incarnations, under the control of Time, in several lower wombs as boar, etc., and Mahā Deva carries on His body the human skulls, that are not even fit to be touched. When Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśa and others suffer such painful things, then one need not wonder at the workings of the Great inscrutable Time. Here ends the Twenty-first Chapter of the Fifth Book on the conquest of the Heavens by Śumbha and Niśumbha in Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam, the Mahā Purāṇam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

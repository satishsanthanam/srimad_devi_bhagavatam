# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 5, CHAPTER 33

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ५.३३ त्रयस्त्रिंशोऽध्यायः । देवीमाहात्म्यवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> राजोवाव
> मुने वैश्योऽयमधुना वने मे मित्रतां गतः ।
> पुत्रदारैर्निरस्तोऽयं प्राप्तोऽत्र मम सङ्गमम् ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The king Suratha said :-- “O Muni! This Vaiśya is now become a friend of mine in this forest; he has been expelled from his home by his sons and wife and he has come lately here in this forest.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> (कुटुम्बविरहेणासौ दुःखितोऽतीव दुर्मनाः ।
> न शान्तिमुपयात्येष तथापि मम साम्प्रतम् ।
> गतराज्यस्य दुःखेन शोकार्तोऽस्मि महामते ।)
> निष्कारणञ्च मे चिन्ता हृदयान्न निवर्तते ।
> हया मे दुर्बलाः स्युः किं गजाः शत्रुवशं गताः ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He is now suffering very much from the bereavement of his family and has become very much troubled in his mind. He is not getting any peace whatsoever. I am also become like him and have become very distressed owing to my kingdom being robbed away. This thought, though really devoid of any substantial cause, is not leaving my heart now. Oh! My elephants and horses, now under my enemies, have become weak;

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 3-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भृत्यवर्गस्तथा दुःखी जातः स्यात्तु मया विना ।
> कोशक्षयं करिष्यन्ति रिपवोऽतिबलात्क्षणात् ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> इत्येवं चिन्तयानस्य न मे निद्रा तनौ सुखम् ।
> जानामीदं जगन्मिथ्या स्वप्नवत्सर्वमेव हि ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

My servants are suffering very much owing to my absence! My enemies will, within no time squander away forcibly all my hoarded riches. This thought is not giving me any happiness; nay, I cannot get any sleep owing to this care and anxiety.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 5-6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जानतोऽपि मनो भ्रान्तं न स्थिरं भवति प्रभो ।
> कोऽहं केऽश्वा गजाः केऽमी न ते मे च सहोदराः ॥ ५ ॥
> 
> न पुत्रा न च मित्राणि येषां दुःखं दुनोति माम् ।
> भ्रमोऽयमिति जानामि तथापि मम मानसः ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Lord! I know that this world is false as a dream; yet my mind is so deluded that I cannot make me quiet. Who am I? What are those horses and elephants to me? They are not my brothers, sons, nor friends; yet I feel very much for them and am troubled with their troubles. O Muni! I know these all are delusions; still I am not able to make my mind free from them.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मोहो नैवापसरति किं तत्कारणमद्भुतम् ।
> स्वामिंस्त्वमसि सर्वज्ञः सर्वसंशयनाशकृत् ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

This is very wonderful indeed! What is the cause of all this? O Lord! Nothing is veiled from your sight, you are fully able to solve all these doubts. Therefore, O Ocean of mercy! Kindly explain to me and this Vaiśya the cause of all this delusion.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कारणं ब्रूहि मोहस्य ममास्य च दयानिधे ।
> व्यास उवाच ।
> इति पृष्टस्तदा राज्ञा सुमेधा मुनिसत्तमः ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! When the King Suratha asked thus, the Muni in reply said to him the following words, full of wisdom, so that his delusion and sorrow might vanish.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तमुवाच परं ज्ञानं शोकमोहविनाशनम् ।
> ऋषिरुवाच ।
> शृणु राजन् प्रवक्ष्यामि कारणं बन्धमोक्षयोः ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Muni said :-- “O King! I am telling you the cause of bondage as well the cause of release of all the beings in this Universe. She is known as Mahā Māyā. She is the Mūla Prakriti, the state of equilibrium of the three Guṇas, Sāttva, Rājas and Tāmas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 10-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> महामायेति विख्याता सर्वेषां प्राणिनामिह ।
> ब्रह्मा विष्णुस्तथेशानस्तुराषाड् वरुणोऽनिलः ॥ १० ॥
> 
> सर्वे देवा मनुष्याश्च गन्धर्वोरगराक्षसाः ।
> वृक्षाश्च विविधा वल्ल्यः पशवो मृगपक्षिणः ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Even Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśvara, Indra, Varuṇa, Vāyu, and the other Devas, Gandarbhas, Nagas, Rākṣasas, men, deer, animals, birds, trees and various kinds of creepers all are under Māyā; thus they are all bound; again they all get release when they are released by that Māyā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मायाधीनाश्च ते सर्वे भाजनं बन्धमोक्षयोः ।
> तया सृष्टमिदं सर्वं जगत्स्थावरजङ्गमम् ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

By Her is created all this world, moving and not moving, all the beings are caught in Her net and all are under the control of Her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तद्वशे वर्तते नूनं मोहजालेन यन्त्रितम् ।
> त्वं कियान्मानुषेष्वेकः क्षत्रियो रजसाविलः ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

You are a Kṣattriya; so Rājoguṇa preponderates in you and your heart is thus rendered impure. She, by Her Māyā, deludes even the minds of those who are Jñānins or wise; you are but an ordinary man compared to them.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ज्ञानिनामपि चेतांसि मोहयत्यनिशं हि सा ।
> ब्रह्मेशवासुदेवाद्या ज्ञाने सत्यपि शेषतः ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Even Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśa, though possessed of vast wisdom, still roam, under the sway of Māyā, in the three worlds completely deluded by their attachments to the sensual objects.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तेऽपि रागवशाल्लोके भ्रमन्ति परिमोहिताः ।
> पुरा सत्ययुगे राजन् विष्णुर्नारायणः स्वयम् ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! In the Satya Yuga, in ancient times Viṣṇu Nārāyaṇa himself performed a very hard tapasyā in Śvetadvīpa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> श्वेतद्वीपं समासाद्य चकार विपुलं तपः ।
> वर्षाणामयुतं यावद् ब्रह्मविद्याप्रसक्तये ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He passed away full ten thousand years in meditation, with the object of attaining the unbroken everlasting Bliss and becoming steadfastly attached to Brahmā Vidyā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अनश्वरसुखायासौ चिन्तयानस्ततः परम् ।
> एकस्मिन्निर्जने देशे ब्रह्मापि परमाद्भुते ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Brahmā, too, became engaged in performing a tapasyā, meditating the Primordial Force, Ādyā Śakti, in a very wonderful solitary place for the cessation of delusion.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 18-19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्थितस्तपसि राजेन्द्र मोहस्य विनिवृत्तये ।
> कदाचिद्वासुदेवोऽसौ स्थलान्तरमतिर्हरिः ॥ १८ ॥
> 
> तस्माद्देशात्समुत्थाय जगामान्यद्दिदृक्षया ।
> चतुर्मुखोऽपि राजेन्द्र तथैव निःसृतः स्थलात् ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Once on a time Vāsudeva Hari wanted to go to another place; he got up and started to see other places. Brahmā, also, left his place and started for another destination.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मिलितौ मार्गमध्ये तु चतुर्मुखचतुर्भुजौ ।
> अन्योन्यं पृष्टवन्तौ तौ कस्त्वं कस्त्वमिति स्म ह ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When they met each other in their way, each one asked the other, “Who are you?” The Prajāpati answered :-- “I am the Creator Brahmā.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रह्मा प्रोवाच तं देवं कर्ताहं जगतः किल ।
> विष्णस्तमाह भो मूर्ख जगत्कर्ताहमच्युतः ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing thus the Brahmā’s words, Viṣṇu said :-- “O You Stupid! I am Achyuta Viṣṇu; therefore I am the Creator of this world. You are inferior to Me as there is so much of Rājoguṇa in preponderance in you.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 22-23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> त्वं कियान्बलहीनोऽसि रजोगुणसमाश्रितः ।
> सत्त्वाश्रितं हि मां विद्धि वासुदेवं सनातनम् ॥ २२ ॥
> 
> मया त्वं रक्षितोऽद्यैव कृत्वा युद्धं सुदारुणम् ।
> शरणं मे समायातो दानवाभ्यां प्रपीडितः ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Know Me as the eternal Vāsudeva, preponderating in Sāttva Guṇa. Do you not remember that I fought a dreadful battle for you and thus saved your life a short while ago, I slew the two Dānavas Madhu and Kaiṭava when you were much distressed by them and took My refuge.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मया तौ निहतौ कामं दानवौ मधुकैटभौ ।
> कथं गर्वायसे मन्द मोहोऽयं त्यज साम्प्रतम् ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

How then do you boast now! O Fool! Quit your this vain boasting now. In this wide world, there is none superior to Me.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> न मत्तोऽप्यधिकः कश्चित्संसारेऽस्मिन्प्रसारिते ।
> ऋषिरुवाच ।
> एवं प्रवदमानौ तौ ब्रह्मविष्णू परस्परम् ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Ṛṣi said :-- Thus engaged in disputing with each other, their lips were quivering with anger and their eyes got red.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्फुरदोष्ठौ वेपमानौ लोहिताक्षौ बभूवतुः ।
> प्रादुर्बभूव सहसा तयोर्विवदमानयोः ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When, Behold! there appeared suddenly between those two disputants, a nectar-like white phallic emblem (Liṅgam), wonderfully long and extensive.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मध्ये लिङ्गं सुधाश्वेतं विपुलं दीर्घमद्भुतम् ।
> आकाशे तरसा तत्र वागुवाचाशरीरिणी ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then a voice, from without anybody, broke out in the Heavens and addressed Brahmā and Viṣṇu who were quarrelling thus!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 28-29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तौ सम्बोध्य महाभागौ विवदन्तौ परस्परम् ।
> ब्रह्मन् विष्णो विवादं मा कुरुतां वां परस्परम् ॥ २८ ॥
> 
> लिङ्गस्यास्य परं पारमधस्तादुपरि धुवम् ।
> यो याति युवयोर्मध्ये स श्रेष्ठो वां सदैव हि ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Whoever amongst you will be able to go to the other end of this Liṅgam whether beyond its top or below its bottom, he is certainly the superior of you two; let one of you therefore go down to Pātāla and let the other go up to the Heavens.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एकः प्रयातु पातालमाकाशमपरोऽधुना ।
> प्रमाणं मे वचः कार्यं त्यक्त्वा वादं निरर्थकम् ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Leave off your useless disputations and take my word as proof. It is always advisable to select an umpire to decide such a quarrel as this that has sprung up between you two.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मध्यस्थः सर्वदा कार्यो विवादेऽस्मिन्द्वयोरिह ।
> ऋषिरुवाच ।
> तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं दिव्यं सज्जीभूतौ कृतोद्यमौ ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Ṛṣi said :-- O King! Hearing thus the divine word, both of them became ready and began energetically to measure the length of the wonderful Liṅgam that stood in front of them.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जग्मतुर्मातुमग्रस्थं लिङ्गमद्भुतदर्शनम् ।
> पातालमगमद्विष्णुर्ब्रह्माप्याकाशमेव च ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Viṣṇu went down to Pātāla and Brahmā went up to Ākāśa to measure the Liṅgam and thus to ascertain their superiority.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

> विष्णुर्गत्वा कियद्देशं श्रान्तः सर्वात्मना यतः ॥ ३३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Going down some distance Viṣṇu got tired and doing his best, when he could not find out the end of the Liṅgam, he returned and remained at the desired meeting place.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> न प्रापान्तं स लिङ्गस्य परिवृत्य ययौ स्थलम् ।
> ब्रह्मागच्छत्ततश्चोर्ध्वं पतितं केतकीदलम् ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On the other hand, Brahmā was ascending to the skies when he got one Ketakī flower dropping from the head of the Liṅgam. He became over glad and returned also to the desired meeting place.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 35</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शिवस्य मस्तकात्प्राप्य परावृत्तो मुदावृतः ।
> आगत्य तरसा ब्रह्मा विष्णवे केतकीदलम् ॥ ३५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Brahmā became very much elated with vanity and when he returned, he at once showed that flower to Viṣṇu and spoke thus the false words :--

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दर्शयित्वा च वितथमुवाच मदमोहितः ।
> लिङ्गस्य मस्तकादेतद् गृहीतं केतकीदलम् ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

“O Viṣṇu! This Ketakī flower has been obtained from the head of the Liṅgam. I have brought this to you simply that you would recognise it and be convinced in your heart.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 37-38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अभिज्ञानाय चानीतं तव चित्तप्रशान्तये ।
> श्रुत्वा तद्ब्रह्मणोवाक्यं दृष्ट्वा च केतकीदलम् ॥ ३७ ॥
> 
> हरिस्तं प्रत्युवाचेदं साक्षी कः कथयाधुना ।
> यथार्थवादी मेधावी सदाचारः शुचि समः ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing these words of Brahmā, Viṣṇu saw the Ketakī flower and said :-- “O Brahmā! Who is your witness in this matter? He whose words are true, who is equal to all, who is intelligent, pure, and always of good conduct, he can be the witness in such matters of dispute.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 39-41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> साक्षी भवति सर्वत्र विवादे समुपस्थिते ।
> ब्रह्मोवाच ।
> दूरदेशात्समायाति साक्षी कः समयेऽधुना ॥ ३९ ॥
> 
> यत्सत्यं तद्वचः सेयं केतकी कथयिष्यति ।
> इत्युक्त्वा प्रेरिता तत्र ब्रह्मणा केतकी स्फुटम् ॥ ४० ॥
> 
> वचनं प्राह तरसा शार्ङ्गिणं प्रत्यबोधयत् ।
> शिवमूर्ध्नि स्थितां ब्रह्मा गहीत्वा मां समागतः ॥ ४१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Brahmā said :-- “Who will come now as witness from that far off place? This Ketakī flower is the witness; this will give evidence.” Thus saying, Brahmā requested Ketakī to give evidence; Ketakī soon replied thus to convince Viṣṇu. O Viṣṇu! I was on the head of Mahādeva; Brahmā has brought me from there down to this place; you ought not therefore to have any doubt on this point.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 42</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सन्देहोऽत्र न कर्तव्यस्त्वया विष्णो कदाचन ।
> मम वाक्यं प्रमाणं हि ब्रह्मा पारङ्गतोऽस्य ह ॥ ४२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

My word is the evidence; Brahmā has gone to the other end of the Liṅgam. Some devotee of Śiva put me on His head and Brahmā has got me down from there.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 43</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> गृहीत्वा मां समायातः शिवभक्तैः समर्पिताम् ।
> केतक्या वचनं श्रुत्वा हरिराह स्मयन्निव ॥ ४३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing thus the words of Ketakī, Viṣṇu was very much astonished and said this :-- “I cannot trust your word; if Mahā Deva comes and speaks this Himself, then I can trust and take it as a proof.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 44</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> महादेवः प्रमाणं मे यद्यसौ वचनं वदेत् ।
> ऋषिरुवाच ।
> तदाकर्ण्य हरेर्वाक्यं महादेवः सनातनः ॥ ४४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Ṛṣi said :-- O King! The eternal Mahā Deva, hearing the words of Viṣṇu, spoke thus to Ketakī with great anger, “O Liar! Do not utter such false words; You dropped down from My head and Brahmā while ascending up, picked you up on the way.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 45-46</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कुपितः केतकीं प्राह मिथ्यावादिनि मा वद ।
> गच्छतो मध्यतः प्राप्ता पतिता मस्तकान्मम ॥ ४५ ॥
> 
> मिथ्याभिभाषिणी त्यक्ता मया त्वं सर्वदैव हि ।
> ब्रह्मा लज्जापरो भूत्वा ननाम मधुसूदनम् ॥ ४६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Now as you have told a lie, I will never take you; you are henceforth forsaken by Me.” Brahmā was then very much put to shame; he bowed down to Viṣṇu; Mahā Deva, forsook the Ketakī flower from that date.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 47</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शिवेन केतकी त्यक्ता तद्दिनात्कुसुमेषु वै ।
> एवं मायाबलं विद्धि ज्ञानिनामपि मोहदम् ॥ ४७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Such is the power of Māyā; when need to speak of other ordinary mortals!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 48</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अन्येषां प्राणिनां राजन् का वार्ता विभ्रमं प्रति ।
> देवानां कार्यसिद्ध्यर्थं सर्वदैव रमापतिः ॥ ४८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

See! Viṣṇu, the Lord of Lakṣmī, is self-deluded and is always deceiving the Daityas for the welfare of the Devas, without any fear whatsoever of the sin that he is thereby iñcurring.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 49</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दैत्यान्वञ्चयते चाशु त्यक्त्वा पापभयं हरिः ।
> अवतारकरो देवो नानायोनिषु माधवः ॥ ४९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Though He is the Lord of all yet He has to take several incarnations in several wombs, forsaking the pleasures of the Heavens and fighting with the Daityas. O King! Viṣṇu is omniscient and He is the Lord of this world; specially

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 50</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> त्यक्त्वानन्दसुखं दैत्यैर्युद्धं चैवाकरोद्विभुः ।
> नूनं मायाबलं चैतन्माधवेऽपि जगद्गुरौ ॥ ५० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He is the only One, Supreme in the creation of the Gods. Now when Māyā exercises such a powerful influence on Viṣṇu, what wonder is there that the other ordinary beings would be deluded by Her?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 51</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सर्वज्ञे देवकार्यांशे का वार्तान्यस्य भूपते ।
> ज्ञानिनामपि चेतांसि परमा प्रकृतिः किल ॥ ५१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! That Highest Prakriti draws away violently the hearts of the wise and drags them down into the ocean of world.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 52</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> बलादाकृष्य मोहाय प्रयच्छति महीपते ।
> यया व्याप्तमिदं सर्वं भगवत्या चराचरम् ॥ ५२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

That Omnipresent Bhagavatī is ever the cause of bondage of all when She casts Her net of delusion and She is again ever the cause of liberation when She imparts Her knowledge to them.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 53</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मोहदा ज्ञानदा सैव बन्धमोक्षप्रदा सदा ।
> राजोवाच ।
> भगवन्ब्रूहि मे तस्याः स्वरूपं बलमुत्तमम् ॥ ५३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The King said :-- O Brāhmaṇ! What is the nature of Her? and what is the Supreme Force? What is the Cause of this creation? And where is Her highest place? Kindly narrate all these to me.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 54</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उत्पत्तिकारणं वापि स्थानं परमकं च यत् ।
> ऋषिरुवाच ।
> न चोत्पत्तिरनादित्वान्नृप तस्याः कदाचन ॥ ५४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Ṛṣi said :-- O King! She is beginningless; therefore She had no origin at any time; that Highest Devī is Eternal and She is always the Cause of all Causes. (How then can any other be powerful like Her).

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 55</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नित्यैव सा परा देवी कारणानां च कारणम् ।
> वर्तते सर्वभूतेषु शक्तिः सर्वात्मना नृप ॥ ५५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! She resides in all the beings as the essential vital Force; deprived of that Force, every being is reduced to a dead carcass.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 56</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शववच्छक्तिहीनस्तु प्राणी भवति सर्वथा ।
> चिच्छक्तिः सर्वभूतेषु रूपं तस्यास्तदेव हि ॥ ५६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

She is pervading as the Universal Force of Consciousness in all the beings. The form of this Śakti (Force) is the form made up of consciousness itself, the Brahmā. (For the force of Fire is Fire itself; it is not seen in any other form).

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 57</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> आविर्भावतिरोभावौ देवानां कार्यसिद्धये ।

>
**English Translation:**

Her appearances and disappearances at times are simply for serving the purposes of the Gods. O King! Whenever the Devas and men worship Her, Ambikā makes Her appearance visible to destroy their pains and sufferings.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 58-60</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> प्रादुर्भवति भूतानां दुःखनाशाय चाम्बिका ।
> नानारूपधरा देवी नानाशक्तिसमन्विता ॥ ५८ ॥
> 
> आविर्भवति कार्यार्थं स्वेच्छया परमेश्वरी ।
> दैवाधीना न सा देवी यथा सर्वे सुरा नृप ॥ ५९ ॥
> 
> न कालवशगा नित्यं पुरुषार्थप्रवर्तिनी ।
> अकर्ता पुरुषो द्रष्टा दृश्यं सर्वमिदं जगत् ॥ ६० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

She is not like the Devas, under the control of Daiva or Fate; She is not under the influence of Time (as both Fate and Time are created by Her). She puts always every being to action according to his capacity. Puruṣa is not the Doer; He is simply the Witness.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 61</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दृश्यस्य जननी सैव देवी सदसदात्मिका ।
> पुरुषं रञ्जयत्येका कृत्वा ब्रह्माण्डनाटकम् ॥ ६१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

This whole Universe is the object seen. That Devī is the Mother of all this that is witnessed. She is the Manifested and She is the Unmanifested and She is the Effect also. She alone is the Actress and manifests thus the world and thus gives the colouring to the Puruṣa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 62</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रञ्जिते पुरुषे सर्वं संहरत्यतिरंहसा ।
> तया निमित्तभूतास्ते ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेश्वराः ॥ ६२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When the Puruṣa is coloured thus, She destroys quickly these worlds. It is said that Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśa are respectively the Creator, Preserver and Destroyer of the world; but this is merely a statement; really they are merely instruments in Her hands.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 63</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कल्पिताः स्वस्वकार्येषु प्रेरिता लीलया त्वमी ।
> स्वांशं तेषु समारोप्य कृतास्ते बलवत्तराः ॥ ६३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Bhagavatī has created them in reality for Her Pastime and stationed them in their respective posts. She has bestowed to them Her part manifestations, i.e., Sarasvatī to Brahmā, Lakṣmī to Viṣṇu, and Girijā to Maheśa and has thus rendered them more powerful.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 33 Verse 64-133</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दत्ताश्च शक्तयस्तेभ्यो गीर्लक्ष्मीर्गिरिजा तथा ।
> ते तां ध्यायन्ति देवेशा पूजयन्ति परां मुदा ॥ ६४ ॥
> 
> ज्ञात्वा सर्वेश्वरीं शक्तिं सृष्टिस्थितिविनाशिनीम् ।
> एतत्ते सर्वमाख्यातं देवीमाहात्म्यमुत्तमम् ।
> मम बुद्ध्यनुसारेण नान्तं जानामि भूपते ॥ ६५ ॥
>
> *Colophon:*
> इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां
> संहितायां पञ्चमस्कन्धे
> देवीमाहात्म्यवर्णनं नाम त्रयस्त्रिंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.३३॥
>
**English Translation:**

They, the lords of the Devas, always meditate and worship Her as the Creatrix, Preservrix and Destructrix of this Universe. O King! I have thus described to you, as far as my intelligence and knowledge go, the holy greatness and the excellent glory of Her (in reality, I have not been able to come to the end of it). “Aim Hrīm Klīm Cāmuṇḍāyai bicce” is the (9) nine lettered mantra. Here ends the Thirty-third Chapter of the Fifth Book on the description of the greatness of the Devī in Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam, the Mahā Purāṇam, of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

</details>

# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 11, CHAPTER 7

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 1-2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ११.७ सप्तमोऽध्यायः । रुद्राक्षमाहात्म्यवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> श्रीनारायण उवाच ।
> एवं नारद षड्वक्त्रो गिरिशेन विबोधितः ।
> रुद्राक्षमहिमानं च ज्ञात्वासीत्स कृतार्थकः ॥ १ ॥
> 
> इत्थं भूतानुभावोऽयं रुद्राक्षो वर्णितो मया ।
> सदाचारप्रसङ्गेन शृणु चान्यत्समाहितः ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Śrī Nārāyaṇa said :-- O Nārada! When Girīśa thus explained to Kārtikeya the greatness of Rudrākṣam, he became satisfied. Now I have spoken to you of the glories of the Rudrākṣams as far as I know.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 3-5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यथा रुद्राक्षमहिमा वर्णितोऽनन्तपुण्यदः ।
> लक्षणं मन्त्रविन्यासं तथाहं वर्णयामि ते ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> लक्षं तु दर्शनात्युण्यं कोटिस्तत्स्पर्शनाद्भवेत् ।
> तस्य कोटिगुणं पुण्यं लभते धारणान्नरः ॥ ४ ॥
> 
> लक्षकोटिसहस्राणि लक्षकोटिशतानि च ।
> तज्जपाल्लभते पुण्यं नरो रुद्राक्षधारणात् ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Now, as to our subject of right way of acting, I will now speak on other things that ought to be known. Listen. The seeing of Rudrākṣam brings in a lakh times of Puṇyam and koṭi times the merit arises from touching that; holding it brings in koṭi times merit; again if one makes the japam of a Mantra with that Rudrākṣam, one obtains merit one hundred lakh koṭi times and one thousand lakh koṭi times the merit.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 6-7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रुद्राक्षाणां तु भद्राक्षधारणात्स्यान्महाफलम् ।
> धात्रीफलप्रमाणं यच्छ्रेष्ठमेतदुदाहृतम् ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> बदरीफलमात्रं तु प्रोच्यते मध्यमं बुधैः ।
> अधमं चणमात्रं स्यात्प्रतिज्ञैषा मयोदिता ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The merit in holding the Rudrākṣam is far superior to that in holding Bhadrākṣam. The Rudrākṣam seed that is of the size of an Āmalakī is the best; which is the of the size of a plum, is middling; and which is of the size of a gram is the worst. this is my word and promise.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 8-9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्राह्मणाः क्षत्रिया वैश्याः शूद्राश्चेति शिवाज्ञया ।
> वृक्षा जाताः पृथिव्यां तु तज्जातीयाः शुभाक्षकाः ॥ ८ ॥
> 
> श्वेतास्तु ब्राह्मणा ज्ञेयाः क्षत्रिया रक्तवर्णकाः ।
> पीता वैश्यास्तु विज्ञेयाः कृष्णाः शूद्राः प्रकीर्तिताः ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Rudrākṣam tree is of four kinds :-- Brāhmaṇa, Kṣattriya, Vaiśya, and Śūdra. The white colour is Brāhmaṇa; the red colour is Kṣattriya; the yellow colour is Vaiśya and the black coloured Rudrākṣam seed is Śūdra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 10-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्राह्मणो बिभृयाच्छ्वेतान् रक्तान् राजा तु धारयेत् ।
> पीतान्वैश्यस्तु बिभृयात्कृष्णान् शूद्रस्तु धारयेत् ॥ १० ॥
> 
> समाः स्निग्धा दृढास्तद्वत्कण्टकैः संयुताः शुभाः ।
> कृमिदष्टाञ्छिन्नभिन्नान्कण्टकैरहितांस्तथा ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Brāhmaṇas are to use the white coloured Rudrākṣams; the Kṣattriyas, the red coloured ones, the Vaiśyas, the yellow coloured ones; and the Śūdras, the black ones. Those Rudrākṣa seeds that are nicely circular, smooth, hard, and whose thorns or points are distinctly visible, are the best.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 12-13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्रणयुक्तानावृतांश्च षड्रुद्राक्षांस्तु वर्जयेत् ।
> स्वयमेव कृतद्वारो रुद्राक्षः स्यादिहोत्तमः ॥ १२ ॥
> 
> यत्तु पौरुषयत्नेन कृतं तन्मध्यमं भवेत् ।
> समान्स्निग्धान्दृढान्वृत्तान्क्षौमसूत्रेण धारयेत् ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Those that are pierced by insects, broken in parts, whose thorns are not clearly visible, with swells and holes and those that are coated over, these six varieties of Rudrākṣams are faulty. Those Rudrākṣams that have their holes by nature running through and through are best; and those that base their holes pierced by men are middling.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 14-15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सर्वगात्रेषु साम्येन समानातिविलक्षणा ।
> निर्घर्षे हेमलेखाभा यत्र लेखा प्रदृश्यते ॥ १४ ॥
> 
> तदक्षमुत्तमं विद्यात्स धार्यः शिवपूजकैः ।
> शिखायामेकरुद्राक्षं त्रिंशद्वै शिरसा वहेत् ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Rudrākṣa seeds that are all of uniform shape, bright, hard, and beautifully circular should be strung together by a silken thread. How to test the Rudrākṣa seed? As gold is tested by a touch stone; so the Rudrākṣam is tested by drawing lines on it; those on which the lines are most uniform, bright and beautiful are the best and they should be worn by the Śaivas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 16-17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> षट्त्रिंशच्च गले धार्या बाह्वोः षोडश षोडश ।
> मणिबन्धे द्वादशाक्षान्स्कन्धे पञ्चाशतं भवेत् ॥ १६ ॥
> 
> अष्टोत्तरशतैर्मालोपवीतं च प्रकल्पयेत् ।
> द्विसरं त्रिसरं वापि बिभृयात्कण्ठदेशतः ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

One should hold one Rudrākṣam on the crown hair, thirty on the head, thirty six on the neck; sixteen on each arm, twelve on each wrist, fifty on the shoulders, one hundred and eight Rudrākṣams in the place of the sacrificial thread; and the devotee should have two or three rounds on the neck.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 18-19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कुण्डले मुकुटे चैव कर्णिकाहारकेषु च ।
> केयूरे कटके चैव कुक्षिवंशे तथैव च ॥ १८ ॥
> 
> सुप्ते पीते सर्वकालं रुद्राक्षं धारयेन्नरः ।
> त्रिशतं त्वधमं पञ्चशतं मध्यममुच्यते ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On the earrings, on the crown of the head, the head, on bracelets, on armlets, on necklace, on the ornament worn on the loins one should hold Rudrākṣam always, whether one sleeps or eats. Holding three hundred Rudrākṣams is the lowest; holding five hundred is middling; holding one thousand Rudrākṣams is the best; so one ought to wear  one thousand Rudrāksams.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 20-21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सहस्रमुत्तमं प्रोक्तं चैवं भेदेन धारयेत् ।
> शिरसीशानमन्त्रेण कर्णे तत्पुरुषेण च ॥ २० ॥
> 
> अघोरेण ललाटे तु तेनैव हृदयेऽपि च ।
> अघोरबीजमन्त्रेण करयोर्धारयेत्पुनः ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

At the time of taking Rudrākṣam, on one’s head, one should utter the Mantra of Īsāna; the mantra of Tat Puruṣa while holding on one’s ears; Aghora mantra on one’s forehead and heart; and the vīja of Aghora mantra, i.e., “hasau” while holding on one’s hands.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 22-23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पञ्चाशदक्षग्रथितां वामदेवेन चोदरे ।
> पञ्चब्रह्मभिरङ्गैश्चाप्येवं रुद्राक्षधारणम् ॥ २२ ॥
> 
> ग्रथितान्मूलमन्त्रेण सर्वानक्षांस्तु धारयेत् ।
> एकवक्त्रस्तु रुद्राक्षः परतत्त्वप्रकाशकः ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

One should wear the rosary of fifty Rudrākṣa seeds, suspended up to the belly, uttering the Vāmadeva mantra, i.e., Sadyojātādi, etc., the five Brahmā mantras, and the six-limbed Śiva mantra. One is to string every Rudrākṣa seed, uttering the root mantra and then hold it. One-faced Rudrākṣa reveals Paratattva (the highest Tattva); when worn, the knowledge of the highest Tattva arises; the Brahmā is seen then.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 24-26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> परतत्त्वधारणाच्च जायते तत्प्रकाशनम् ।
> द्विवक्त्रस्तु मुनिश्रेष्ठ अर्धनारीश्वरो भवेत् ॥ २४ ॥
> 
> धारणादर्धनारीशः प्रीयते तस्य नित्यशः ।
> त्रिवक्त्रस्त्वनलः साक्षात्स्त्रीहत्यां दहति क्षणात् ॥ २५ ॥
> 
> त्रिमुखश्चैव रुद्राक्षोऽप्यग्नित्रयस्वरूपकः ।
> तद्धारणाच्च हुतभुक् तस्य तुष्यति नित्यशः ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The two-faced Rudrākṣam is Ardhanārīśvara, the Lord of the other half which represents women (in the same person); if worn, Ardhanārīsvara Śiva is always pleased with that man who holds it. The three-faced Rudrākṣam is Fire made manifest; it destroys in a moment the sin of killing a woman. The three-faced Rudrākṣam is the three Agnis, Dakṣiṇāgni, Gārhapatya, and Āhavaniya; Bhagavān Agni is always pleased with that man who wears the three-faced Rudrākṣam.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 27-28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> चतुर्मुखस्तु रुद्राक्षः पितामहस्वरूपकः ।
> तद्धारणान्महाश्रीमान्महदारोग्यमुत्तमम् ॥ २७ ॥
> 
> महती ज्ञानसम्पत्तिः शुद्धये धारयेन्नरः ।
> पञ्चमुखस्तु रुद्राक्षः पञ्चब्रह्मस्वरूपकः ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The four-faced Rudrākṣam is Brahmā Himself. The wearer gets his prosperity enhanced, his diseases destroyed, the divine knowledge springs in him and his heart is always pleased. The five-faced Rudrākṣam is the five faced Śiva Himself; Mahādeva gets pleased with him who holds it.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 29-30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्य धारणमात्रेण सन्तुष्यति महेश्वरः ।
> षड्वक्त्रश्चैव रुद्राक्षः कार्तिकेयाधिदैवतः ॥ २९ ॥
> 
> विनायकं चापि देवं प्रवदन्ति मनीषिणः ।
> सप्तवक्त्रस्तु रुद्राक्षः सप्तमात्राधिदैवतः ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Presiding Deity of the six faced Rudrākṣam is Kārtikeya. Some Pundits take Gaṇapati to be the Presiding Deity of the six-faced Rudrākṣam. The presiding Deity of the seven-faced Rudrākṣam is the seven Mātrikās, the Sun and the seven Ṛṣis.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 31-32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सप्ताश्वदैवतश्चैव मुनिसप्तकदैवतः ।
> तद्धारणान्महाश्रीः स्यान्महदारोग्यमुत्तमम् ॥ ३१ ॥
> 
> महती ज्ञानसम्पत्तिः शुचिर्वै धारयेन्नरः ।
> अष्टवक्त्रस्तु रुद्राक्षोऽप्यष्टमात्राधिदैवतः ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

By putting on this, the prosperity is increased, health and the pure knowledge are established. It should be put on when one becomes pure. The Presiding Deity of the eight-faced Rudrākṣam is Brāhmī, the eight Mātrikās. By holding this, the eight Vasus are pleased and the river Ganges is also pleased.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 33-34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वस्वष्टकप्रीतिकरो गङ्गाप्रीतिकरः शुभः ।
> तद्धारणादिमे प्रीता भवेयुः सत्यवादिनः ॥ ३३ ॥
> 
> नववक्त्रस्तु रुद्राक्षो यमदेव उदाहृतः ।
> तद्धारणाद्यमभयं न भवत्येव सर्वथा ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The putting on of this makes the Jīvas truthful and pleasant-minded. The Devatā of the nine-faced Rudrākṣam is Yama; holding this puts off the fears of Death.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 35-36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दशवक्त्रस्तु रुद्राक्षो दशाशादैवतः स्मृतः ।
> दशाशाप्रीतिजनको धारणे नात्र संशयः ॥ ३५ ॥
> 
> एकादशमुखस्त्वक्षो रुद्रैकादशदैवतः ।
> तमिन्द्रदैवतं चाहुः सदा सौख्यविवर्धनम् ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Devatā of the eleven-faced Rudrākṣam is ten quarters, the ten quarters are pleased with him who wears the ten-faced Rudrākṣam. The Devata of the eleven mouthed Rudrākṣam is the eleven Rudrās and Indra. Holding this enhances happiness. The twelve-faced Rudrākṣam is Viṣṇu made manifest; its Devatās are the twelve Ādityas; the devotees of Śiva should hold this.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 37-38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रुद्राक्षो द्वादशमुखो महाविष्णुस्वरूपकः ।
> द्वादशादित्यदैवश्च बिभर्त्येव हि तत्परः ॥ ३७ ॥
> 
> त्रयोदशमुखश्चाक्षः कामदः सिद्धिदः शुभः ।
> तस्य धारणमात्रेण कामदेवः प्रसीदति ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The thirteen-faced Rudrākṣam, if worn, enables one to secure one’s desires; he does nowhere experience failures. The Kāma Deva becomes pleased with him who wears this.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 39-40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> चतुर्दशमुखश्चाक्षो रुद्रनेत्रसमुद्भवः ।
> सर्वव्याधिहरश्चैव सर्वारोग्यप्रदायकः ॥ ३९ ॥
> 
> मद्यं मांसं च लशुनं पलाण्डुं शिग्रुमेव च ।
> श्लेष्मातकं विड्वराहं भक्षणे वर्जयेत्ततः ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The fourteen-faced Rudrākṣam destroys all diseases and gives eternal health. While holding this, one ought not to take wine, flesh, onion, garlic, Sajñā fruit, Cāltā fruit and the flesh of the boar which eats excrements, etc.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 7 Verse 41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ग्रहणे विषुवे चैव सङ्क्रमे त्वयने तथा ।
> दर्शे च पौर्णमासे च पुण्येषु दिवसेष्वपि ।
> रुद्राक्षधारणात्सद्यः सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ४१ ॥
>
> *Colophon:*
> इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां
> संहितायामेकादशस्कन्धे
> रुद्राक्षमाहात्म्यवर्णनं नाम सप्तमोऽध्यायः ॥ ११.७॥
>
**English Translation:**

During the Lunar and Solar eclipses, during the Uttarāyaṇa Śamkrānti or the Dakṣināyana Śamkrānti, during the full Moon or the New Moon day, if Rudrākṣam be worn, one becomes instantly freed of all one’s sins. Here ends the Seventh Chapter of the Eleventh Book on the greatness of one faced, etc., Rudrākṣam in the Mahā Purāṇam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Mahārṣi Veda Vyāsa.

</details>

# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 11, CHAPTER 11

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ११.११ एकादशोऽध्यायः । त्रिविधभस्ममाहात्म्यवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> नारद उवाच ।
> त्रिविधत्वं कथं चास्य भस्मनः परिकीर्तितम् ।
> एतत्कथय मे देव महत्कौतूहलं मम ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārada asked :-- O Bhagavān! How is the above Bhaṣma of three kinds? I am eager to hear this. Kindly describe this to me.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 2-3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> श्रीनारायण उवाच ।
> त्रिविधत्वं प्रवक्ष्यामि देवर्षे भस्मनः शृणु ।
> महापापक्षयकरं महाकीर्तिकरं परम् ॥ २ ॥
> 
> गोमयं योनिसम्बद्धं तद्धस्तेनैव गृह्यते ।
> ब्राह्मैर्मन्त्रैस्तु सन्दग्धं तच्छान्तिकृदिहोच्यते ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārāyaṇa said :-- O Nārada! I am now talking of the three kinds of ashes; hearing this even destroys one’s sins and brings in good fame. When a cow evacuates her dung, just as the cow dung leaves her and is far from reaching the ground, one should catch it with one’s hand and this cow-dung burnt with “Sadyojātādi,” i.e., Brahmā Mantra, becomes ashes which are called “Śāntika Bhaṣma,” i.e., ashes producing peace.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 4-5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सावधानस्तु गृह्णीयान्नरो वै गोमयं तु यत् ।
> अन्तरिक्षे गृहीत्वा तत्षडङ्गेन दहेदतः ॥ ४ ॥
> 
> पौष्टिकं तत्समाख्यातं कामदं च ततः शृणु ।
> प्रासादेन दहेदेतत्कामदं भस्म कीर्तितम् ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Before the cowdung is about to reach the ground, the devotee should take it with his hand and uttering the six lettered Mantra, he is to burn the cowdung. The ashes from this are called Pauṣṭik Bhaṣma, i.e., ashes leading to nourishment. If the cowdung be burned with the Mantra “Haum,” the ashes of this are called “Kāmada Bhaṣma,” i.e., ashes leading to the granting of desires.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 6-7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> प्रातरुत्थाय देवर्षे भस्मव्रतपरः शुचिः ।
> गवां गोष्ठेषु गत्वा तु नमस्कृत्य तु गोकुलम् ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> गवां वर्णानुरूपाणां गृह्णीयाद्गोमयं शुभम् ।
> ब्राह्मणस्य च गौः श्वेता रक्ता गौः क्षत्रियस्य च ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Nārada! On the full moon day, new moon day or on the eighth lunar day, a man is to get up from his bed early in the morning and be pure and go the cow enclosure. He is to salute the cows and take the cowdung, uttering the Mantra Haum.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 8-9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पीतवर्णा तु वैश्यस्य कृष्णा शूद्रस्य कथ्यते ।
> पौर्णमास्याममावास्यामष्टम्यां वा विशुद्धधीः ॥ ८ ॥
> 
> प्रासादेन तु मन्त्रेण गृहीत्वा गोमयं शुभम् ।
> हृदयेन तु मन्त्रेण पिण्डीकृत्य तु गोमयम् ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

If he be a Brāhmin, he is to collect the white cow dung; if he be a Kṣattriya, he would take the red cow dung; a Vaiśya, yellow cow dung and if he be a Śūdra, he would take the black cow dung.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 10-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रविरश्मिसुसन्तप्तं शुचौ देशे मनोहरे ।
> तुषेण वा बुसैर्वापि प्रासादेन तु निक्षिपेत् ॥ १० ॥
> 
> अरण्युद्भवमग्निं वा श्रोत्रियागारजं तु वा ।
> तदग्नौ विन्यसेत्तं च शिवबीजेन मन्त्रतः ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then by the mantra “Namaḥ” he is to form that into a ball and cover it with the husk of rice or some other grain and dry it in a sacred place, repeating the mantra “Haum.” Bring fire from a forest or from the house of a Veda-knowing Brāhmin and reduce the cowdung to ashes by this mantra, uttering the mantra Haum.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 12-14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> गृह्णीयादथ तत्राग्निकुण्डाद्भस्म विचक्षणः ।
> नवपात्रं समादाय प्रासादेन तु निक्षिपेत् ॥ १२ ॥
> 
> केतकी पाटली तद्वदुशीरं चन्दनं तथा ।
> नानासुगन्धिद्रव्याणि काश्मीरप्रभृतीनि च ॥ १३ ॥
> 
> निक्षिपेत्तत्र पात्रे तु सद्योमन्त्रेण शुद्धधीः ।
> जलस्नानं पुरा कृत्वा भस्मस्नानमतः परम् ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Next take out the ashes carefully from the fire place (Agni Kuṇḍa) and place it in a new jar or pot, again remembering the mantra “Haum”. Mix with the ashes the Ketakī dust, the Pāṭala flower dust, the root of the fragrant grass called khas khas, saffron and other sweet scented things with the mantra “Satyojātam prapadyāmi,” etc.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 15-16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जलस्नाने त्वशक्तश्च भस्मस्नानं समाचरेत् ।
> प्रक्षाल्य पादौ हस्तौ च शिरश्चेशानमन्त्रतः ॥ १५ ॥
> 
> समुद्धूल्य ततः पश्चादाननं तत्पुरुषेण तु ।
> अघोरेण तु हृदयं नाभिं वामेन तत्परम् ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

First perform the water bath, then the bath of the ashes. In case one cannot have the water bath, one is to have the ash bath. Washing the hands, feet and head with the mantra “Iśānah Sarvavidyānām,” etc., and uttering “Tatpuruṣa” one is to besmear one’s face with ashes and by the mantra “Aghora” apply ashes on one’s chest; with the mantra Vāmadeva.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 17-18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सद्योमन्त्रेण सर्वाङ्गं समूद्धूल्य विचक्षणः ।
> पूर्ववस्त्रं परित्यज्य शुद्धवस्त्रं परिग्रहेत् ॥ १७ ॥
> 
> प्रक्षाल्य पादौ हस्तौ च पश्चादाचमनं चरेत् ।
> भस्मनोद्धूलनाभावे त्रिपुण्ड्रं तु विधीयते ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He is to use ashes on his navel; and with the mantra “Sadyo Jāta,” etc., all over his body; he is to quit his former cloth and put on another fresh cloth. Wash your hands and feet and sip (do Ācaman). It will serve the purpose if one simply uses Tripuṇḍrak and if one does not besmear the whole body with ashes.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 19-20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मध्याह्नात्प्राग्जलैर्युक्तं परतो जलवर्जितम् ।
> तर्जन्यनामिकामध्यैस्त्रिपुण्ड्रं च समाचरेत् ॥ १९ ॥
> 
> मूर्ध्नि चैव ललाटे च कर्णे कण्ठे तथैव च ।
> हृदये चैव बाह्वोश्च न्यासस्थानं हि चोच्यते ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Before the midday one is to use Bhaṣma with water; but after the midday with dry ashes one is to draw the Tripuṇḍra lines of ashes with the forefinger, middlefinger and ringfinger. The head, forehead, ears, neck, heart, and the arms are the places whereon the Tripuṇḍras are used.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 21-24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पञ्चाङ्गुलैर्न्यसेन्मूर्ध्नि प्रासादेन तु मन्त्रतः ।
> त्र्यङ्गुलैर्विन्यसेद्भाले शिरोमन्त्रेण देशिकः ॥ २१ ॥
> 
> सद्येन दक्षिणे कर्णे वामदेवेन वामतः ।
> अघोरेण तु कण्ठे च मध्याङ्गुल्या स्पृशेद्बुधः ॥ २२ ॥
> 
> हृदयं हृदयेनैव त्रिभिरङ्गुलिभिः स्पृशेत् ।
> विन्यसेद्दक्षिणे बाहौ शिखामन्त्रेण देशिकः ॥ २३ ॥
> 
> वामबाहौ न्यसेद्धीमान्कवचेन त्रियङ्गुलैः ।
> मध्येन संस्पृशेन्नाभ्यामीशान इति मन्त्रतः ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

On the head the ashes are applied with five fingers and with the mantra “Haum”; on the forehead, the Tripuṇḍra is applied with mantra Svāhā by the forefinger, mindlefinger and ringfinger; on the right ear, it is applied with “Sadyojāta” mantra; on the left ear, with “Vamadeva” Mantra; on the neck with Aghora mantra by the middlefinger; on the chest with “Namaḥ” mantra by the forefing middlefinger and ringfinger; on the right arm with vaṣaṭ mantra by the three fingers; on the left arm with “Ham” mantra by the three fingers; and on the navel, the ashes are to be applied with the mantra Īśānaḥ sarva devānām by the middlefinger.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 11 Verse 25-28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेशानास्तिस्रो रेखा इति स्मृताः ।
> आद्यो ब्रह्मा ततो विष्णुस्तदूर्ध्वं तु महेश्वरः ॥ २५ ॥
> 
> एकाङ्गुलेन न्यस्तं यदीश्वरस्तत्र देवता ।
> शिरोमध्ये त्वयं ब्रह्मा ईश्वरस्तु ललाटके ॥ २६ ॥
> 
> कर्णयोरश्विनौ देवौ गणेशस्तु गले तथा ।
> क्षत्रियश्च तथा वैश्यः शूद्रश्चोद्धूलनं त्यजेत् ॥ २७ ॥
> 
> सर्वेषामन्त्यजातीनां मन्त्रेण रहितं भवेत् ।
> (अदीक्षितं मनुष्याणामपि मन्त्रं विना भवेत्) ॥ २८ ॥
>
> *Colophon:*
> इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां
> संहितायामेकादशस्कन्धे
> त्रिविधभस्ममाहात्म्यवर्णनं नामकादशोऽध्यायः ॥ ११.११॥
>
**English Translation:**

The first line in every Tripuṇḍra is Brahmā; the second line is Viṣṇu; and the third, the topmost line is Mahā Deva. The line of ashes that is marked by one finger is Īśvara. The head is the place of Brahmā; the forehead is the site of Īśvara; the two ears are the seats of the two Aśvins and the neck is where Gaṇeśa resides. The Kṣattriyas, Vaiśyas, and Śūdras are to use Tripuṇḍras without any mantra; they are also not to use the ashes on the whole of the body. The lowest classes (e.g. the cāṇḍālas, etc.,) and the uninitiated persons are to use the Tripuṇḍraks without any mantra. Here ends the Eleventh Chapter of the Eleventh Book on the description of the greatness of the three kinds of Bhaṣmas in the Mahāpurānam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Mahārṣi Veda Vyāsa.

</details>

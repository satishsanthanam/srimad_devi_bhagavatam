# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 6, CHAPTER 23

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ६.२३ त्रयोविंशोऽध्यायः । एकवीरैकावल्योर्विवाहवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> व्यास उवाच ।
> तस्यास्तु वचनं श्रुत्वा रमापुत्रः प्रतापवान् ।
> प्रफुल्लवदनाम्भोजस्तामुवाच विशाम्पते ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! That powerful son of Lakṣmī, Haihaya, became very glad to hear these words of Yaśovatī and said :--

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> राजोवाच ।
> रम्भोरु यस्त्वया पृष्टो वृत्तान्तो विशदाक्षरः ।
> हेहयोऽहं चैकवीरनाम्ना सिन्धुसुतासुतः ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O One of beautiful thighs! Hear in reply to your query :-- I am Haihaya, the son of Lakṣmī, and I am known in this world by the name of Ekavīra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 3-5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मनो मे यत्त्वया नूनं परतन्त्रं कृतं किल ।
> किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि विरहेणातिपीडितः ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> प्रथमं रूपमाख्यातं सर्वलोकातिगं त्वया ।
> तेन मे विह्वलं जातं कामबाणहतं मनः ॥ ४ ॥
> 
> ततस्तस्या गुणाः प्रोक्तास्तैस्तु चित्तं हृतं पुनः ।
> यत्त्वयोक्तं पुनर्वाक्यं तेन मे विस्मयोऽभवत् ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Now you have made my mind dependent. What am I to do now? where to go? Thus distressed with bereavement from your dear companion, my mind is struck with Cupid’s arrows and is confounded with her extraordinary beauty that you just now described. Next you described her qualifications and my mind is ravished.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एकावल्या वचः प्रोक्तं दानवाग्रे मया वृतः ।
> हैहयस्तं विना नान्यं वृणोमीति विनिश्चयः ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Again when you described before me what she uttered in the presence of the Rākṣasa, I am struck with great wonder. Your dear companion Ekāvalī said before the vicious Dānava Kālaketu, “I have already selected the King Haihaya. I will not select any other than him, this is my firm resolve.” These words have converted me into her slave.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 7-8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तेन वाक्येन तन्वङ्गि भृत्योऽहमधुना कृतः ।
> त्वया तस्याः सुकेशान्ते ब्रूहि किं करवाणि वाम् ॥ ७ ॥
> 
> स्थानं तस्य न जानामि राक्षसस्य दुरात्मनः ।
> गतिर्मे नास्ति गमने पुरे तस्मिन्सुलोचने ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Fair-eyed One! Say how I can go there; for you are the only one that can lead me there. Therefore take me quickly to that place where your beautiful clear companion is staying.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 9-10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वद मां त्वं विशालाक्षि तत्र प्रापयितुं क्षमा ।
> प्रापयाशु सखी ते सा यत्र तिष्ठति सुन्दरी ॥ ९ ॥
> 
> हत्वा तं राक्षसं क्रूरं मोचयिष्यामि साम्प्रतम् ।
> विवशां शोकसन्तप्तां राजपुत्रीं तव प्रियाम् ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Your dear companion, the daughter of the King is very much afflicted with sorrow; soon I will free her, by destroying that cruel Rākṣasa. There is no doubt in this. O Auspicious One! I will rescue your dear companion and bring her to the city of yours and hand her over to her father.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विमुक्तदुःखां कृत्वाऽऽशु प्रापयिष्यामि ते पुरम् ।
> पित्रे चास्याः प्रदास्यामि कन्यामेकावलीमहम् ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then that King, the enemy destroyer, will perform the marriage ceremony of his daugther. I think this is the desire of your heart.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पश्चाद्विवाहं कर्तासौ राजा पुत्र्याः परन्तपः ।
> एवं ते मनसः कामो मम चापि प्रियंवदे ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Sweet-speaking One! Know that that is also my desire. O Beautiful One! Now that desire will be fulfilled by your efforts.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भविष्यति स सम्पूर्णः साधनेन तवाधुना ।
> दर्शयाशु पुरं तस्य पश्य मे त्वं पराक्रमम् ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Show me quickly that place and see my prowess. O One with a face beautiful like the Moon! It seems that you will be able to do my work.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यथा हन्मि दुराचारं परदारापहारकम् ।
> तथा कुरु प्रियं कर्तुं शक्तासि वरवर्णिनि ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Soon do such as I can kill that wicked demon, who steals otherś wives. Now show me the way to the impassable city of that Rākṣasa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मार्गं दर्शय तस्याद्य पुरस्य दुर्गमस्य च ।
> व्यास उवाच ।
> तन्निशम्य प्रियं वाक्यं मुदिता च यशोवती ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :--O King! Hearing the sweet words of the prince, Yaśovatī became very glad and gently began to speak out how he could go to the demon’s city.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तमुवाच रमापुत्रं गमनोपायमादरात् ।
> मन्त्रं गृहाण राजेन्द्र भगवत्यास्तु सिद्धिदम् ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Take the success-giving Mantra of Bhagavatī and I would then be able to show you today the city guarded by the Rākṣasas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 17-18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दर्शयिष्यामि तस्याद्य पुरं राक्षसपालितम् ।
> सज्जो भव महाभाग गमनाय मया सह ॥ १७ ॥
> 
> सैन्येन महता युक्तस्तत्र युद्धं भविष्यति ।
> कालकेतुर्महावीरो राक्षसैर्बलिभिर्वृतः ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Better arrange to take your vast army with you; for you will have to fight no sooner you go there. Kālaketu is personally a great warrior surrounded by Rākṣasas of great power and strength.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्मान्मन्त्रं गृहीत्वा त्वं व्रज तत्र मया सह ।
> दर्शयिष्यामि ते मार्गं पुरस्यास्य दुरात्मनः ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Therefore be initiated in the Mantram of Śrī Bhagavatī and accompany me. So you will surely be successful. I will show you the way to the city of that Demon.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 20-22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> हत्वा तं पापकर्माणं मोचयाशु च मे सखीम् ।
> श्रुत्वा तद्वचनं वीरो मन्त्रं जग्राह सत्वरः ॥ २० ॥
> 
> दत्तात्रेयाद्दैवयोगात्प्राप्ताज्ज्ञानिवराच्छुभात् ।
> योगेश्वरीमहामन्त्रं त्रिलोकीतिलकाभिधम् ॥ २१ ॥
> 
> तेन सर्वज्ञता जाता सर्वान्तश्चारिता तथा ।
> तया सह जगामाशु पुरं तस्य सुदुर्गमम् ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Slay that vicious and vilest of the Rākṣasas and rescue my dear companion. Hearing thus, Haihaya was duly initiated into the great Mantram of Yogeśvarī, named Trilokitilaka Mantra (Hrīm Gaurī Rudradayite Yoge Śvarī Hūm Phaṭ Svāhā is the Yogeśvarī Mantra), by Maharṣi Dattātreya, accidentally come there (as if ordained by Fate), the chief of Jñānins (the Gnostics), that is conducive to the welfare of the beings. Thus by the influence of the Mantram the King got the power of knowing all things and going everywhere with unobstructed speed.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रक्षितं राक्षसैघोरैः पातालमिव पन्नगैः ।
> यशोवत्या च सैन्येन महता संयुतो नृपः ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then the King Haihaya quickly went with Yaśovatī to the impassable city of the Rākṣasas, accompanied by a vast army.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तमायान्तं समालोक्य दूतास्तस्य भयातुराः ।
> क्रोशन्तोऽभिययुः पार्श्वं कालकेतोस्तरस्विनः ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The city was surrounded by snakes and guarded by the terrible Rākṣasas like the city of Pātāla. The messengers of the Rākṣasa, seeing the King coming, were struck with terror and crying aloud quickly went to Kālaketu.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तमूचुः सहसा मत्वा राक्षसं काममोहितम् ।
> एकावलीसमीपस्थं कुर्वन्तं विनयान्बहून् ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Kālaketu, struck with Cupid’s arrows, was sitting beside Ekāvalī and was speaking many modest words when the messenger went there suddenly and said :--

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दूता ऊचुः ।
> राजन् यशोवती नारी कामिन्याः सहचारिणी ।
> आयाति सह सैन्येन राजपुत्रेण संयुता ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

“O King! The attendant of this lady Yaśovatī is coming here with a prince and an army.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जयन्तो वा महाराज कार्तिकेयोऽथवा नु किम् ।
> आगच्छति बलोन्मत्तो वाहिनीसहितः किल ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! We cannot tell exactly whether the prince is the son of Indra, named Jayanta or Kārtikeya. After all, puffed up with the strength of his army, he is coming here.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> संयत्तो भव राजेन्द्र सङ्ग्रामः समुपस्थितः ।
> देवपुत्रेण युध्यस्व त्यज वा कमलेक्षणाम् ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! The battle is imminent; now make your arrangements fully and carefully; fight with the son of a Deva or abandon this lotus-eyed Lady.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इतो दूरेऽस्ति सैन्यं तद्योजनत्रयमात्रतः ।
> सज्जो भव महीपाल दुन्दुभिं घोषयाशु वै ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! At a distance of three Yojanas from this place, be is staying with his army. Now equip yourself and quickly declare the war by blowing the war trumpets.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 30-31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> तेषां तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा राक्षसः क्रोधमूर्च्छितः ।
> राक्षसान्प्रेरयामास सायुधान्सबलान्वहून् ॥ ३० ॥
> 
> गच्छध्वं राक्षसाः सर्वे सम्मुखाः शस्त्रपाणयः ।
> तानाज्ञाप्य कालकेतुः पप्रच्छ प्रणयान्वितः ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing the messenger’s words, Kālaketu, the King of the Demons, became overwhelmed with anger and at once sent many powerful Rākṣasas, holding all sorts of weapons and spoke out to them :-- “O Rākṣasas! With weapons in your hands, go before them quickly.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एकावलीं समीपस्थां विवशां भृशदुःखिताम् ।
> कोऽयमायाति तन्वङ्गि पिता ते वापरः पुमान् ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Ordering them thus, Kālaketu asked in sweet words Ekāvalī who was in front and very distressed. O Thin-bellied One! Who is coming here? Is he your father or any other man coming with his army to release you. Speak this to me truly.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 33-34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> त्वदर्थे सैन्यसंयुक्तो ब्रूहि सत्यं कृशोदरि ।
> पिता ते यदि सम्प्राप्तो नेतुं त्वां विरहातुरः ॥ ३३ ॥
> 
> ज्ञात्वा ते पितरं सम्यक् सङ्ग्रामं न करोम्यहम् ।
> आनयित्वा गृहे पूजां रत्नैर्वस्त्रैर्हयैः शुभैः ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

If your father comes here to take you back, being very much distressed with your bereavement, I will never fight with him, if I come to know this truly; rather I will bring him to my house and worship him with the excellent horses, gems and jewels and clothings. Really I will show my full hospitality duly to him when he comes here.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 35</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> करोमि तस्य चातिथ्यं गृहे प्राप्तस्य सर्वथा ।
> अन्यश्चेद्यदि सम्प्राप्तस्तं हन्मि निशितैः शरैः ॥ ३५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

And if any other person comes, then I will take his life by the sharpened arrows; there is no doubt in this. Know this as certain whoever comes here for your rescue is brought by the hand of Death to me.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> आनीतः किल कालेन मरणाय महात्मना ।
> तस्माद्वद विशालाक्षि कोऽयमायाति मन्दधीः ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Therefore, O Large-eyed One! Say who is this fool that is coming, not knowing me as the powerful and unconquerable Kāla (Death).

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 37-38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अज्ञात्वा मां दुराधर्षं कालरूपं महाबलम् ।
> एकावल्युवाच ।
> न जानेऽहं महाभाग कोऽयमायाति सत्वरः ॥ ३७ ॥
> 
> नमेऽस्ति विदितः कोऽपि स्थितायास्तव बन्धने ।
> नायं पिता मे न भ्राता कोऽप्यन्योऽस्ति महाबलः ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Ekāvalī said :-- “O Highly Fortunate One! I do not know who is this body coming to this side with a violent speed. O King! How can I know that when I am in this state of confinement in your house. This man is not my father nor my brother. Some other powerful man is coming here. I do not know exactly what for he is coming.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 39-40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> किमर्थमिह चायाति नाहं वेद विनिश्चयम् ।
> दैत्य उवाच ।
> एवं वदन्त्यमी दूता वयस्या ते यशोवती ॥ ३९ ॥
> 
> समानीय च तं वीरमागतेति कृतोद्यमा ।
> क्व गता सा सखी कान्ते विदग्धा कार्यनिश्चये ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Demon said :-- My messengers say that your comrade Yaśovatī has taken with her that warrior and is coming to this side with great energy. Where has your clever companion gone now? O Lotus-eyed! There is no enemy in the three worlds strong enough to fight against me.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 41-42</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नान्यः कोऽपि ममारातिर्यो मे प्रतिबलो भवेत् ।
> व्यास उवाच ।
> एतस्मिन्नन्तरे दूतास्तत्रान्ये वै समागताः ॥ ४१ ॥
> 
> ते होचुस्त्वरिता भीताः कालकेतुं गृहे स्थितम् ।
> किं स्वस्थोऽसि महाराज समीपे सैन्यमागतम् ॥ ४२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! Just then other messengers hurriedly came there terrified and spoke to Kālaketu who had been staying in the house, thus :-- “O King! The army has come quite close to the city and how are you staying in the house, calm and quiet?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 43</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> निर्गच्छ नगरात्तूर्णं सैन्येन महताऽऽवृतः ।
> इति तेषां वचः श्रुत्वा कालकेतुर्महाबलः ॥ ४३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Better march out of the city with your vast army as early as possible.” The powerful Kālaketu, then, hearing their words, mounted on the chariot and quickly went out of his city.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 23 Verse 44</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

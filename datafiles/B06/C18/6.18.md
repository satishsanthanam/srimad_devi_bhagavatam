# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 6, CHAPTER 18

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 1-2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ६.१८ अष्टादशोऽध्यायः । शिवप्रसादेन लक्ष्मीद्वारा भगवत्याः समाराधनवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> जनमेजय उवाच ।
> इति शप्ता भगवता सिन्धुजा कोपयोगतः ।
> कथं सा वडवा जाता रेवन्तेन च किं कृतम् ॥ १ ॥
> 
> कस्मिन्देशेऽब्धिजा देवी वडवारूपधारिणी ।
> संस्थितैकाकिनी बाला परोषित्पतिका यथा ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Janamejaya said :-- How did the Goddess Lakṣmī, the daughter of the ocean, come to be born as a mare, when cursed by the Bhagavān in His moment of anger, and what did Revanta do at that time? In what country was the Devī born as a mare and how did She pass Her time alone like one whose husband had gone abroad.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कालं कियन्तमायुष्मन् वियुक्ता पतिना रमा ।
> संस्थिता विजनेऽरण्ये किं कृतं च तया पुनः ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Muni! How long and in what forest unfrequented by persons did she pass her time, thus deprived of the companion of her husband and what did she do at that time?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 4-5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> समागमं कदा प्राप्ता वासुदेवस्य सिन्धुजा ।
> पुत्रः कथं तया प्राप्तो नारायणवियुक्तया ॥ ४ ॥
> 
> एतद्वृत्तान्तमार्येश कथयस्व सविस्तरम् ।
> श्रोतुकामोऽस्मि विप्रेन्द्र कथाख्यानमनुत्तमम् ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When was she reconciled with her husband Vāsudeva? and how did she get a son, when she lived in a state of separation from her husband. O best of Āryas! I am very curious to hear this excellent story. So describe this in full details to me.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सूत उवाच ।
> इति पृष्टस्तदा व्यासः परीक्षित्तनयेन वै ।
> कथयामास भो विप्राः कथामेतां सुविस्तराम् ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Sūta said :-- O Ṛṣis! Thus questioned by Janamejaya, the Dvaipāyan Muni began to recite the story in its full details.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> शृणु राजन् प्रवक्ष्यामि कथां पौराणिकीं शुभाम् ।
> पावनीं सुखदां कर्णे विशदाक्षरसंयुताम् ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :--O King! I will now describe to you the pleasing story of the Purāṇas in a clear distinct language; hear.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रेवन्तस्तु रमां दृष्ट्वा शप्ता देवेन कामिनीम् ।
> भयार्तः प्रययौ दूरात्प्रणम्य जगतां पतिम् ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Revanta, the son of the Sun, became terrified to see Vāsudeva, the Deva of the Devas cursing the Lakṣmī Devī and, after bowing down to Janārdan, the Lord of the world, went off.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

> निवेदयामास कथां भास्कराय स शापजाम् ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Seeing the anger of Viṣṇu, the Lord of the world, he went quickly to his father and informed him of the curse delivered by Nārāyaṇa to the Goddess Lakṣmī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

>
**English Translation:**

And the Lakṣmī Devī, the lotus-eyed, thus cursed, got the permission of Nārāyaṇa and with a grieved heart bowed down to him and came down to the world of mortals.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 11</b></summary>

>
**English Translation:**

She took the form of a mare and went to the spot where the wife of the Sun (named Ccāyā) practised her asceticism in ancient times.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कालिन्दीतमसासङ्गे सुपर्णाक्षस्य चोत्तरे ।

>
**English Translation:**

The place was the confluence of the river Kālindi and the Tamasā, and decorated with lovely forests and trees situated north of the mountain named Suparṇākṣa, yielding all desires.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 13-15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तत्र स्थिता महादेवं शङ्करं वाञ्छितप्रदम् ।
> दध्यौ चैकेन मनसा शूलिनं चन्द्रशेखरम् ॥ १३ ॥
> 
> पञ्चाननं दशभुजं गौरीदेहार्धधारिणम् ।
> कर्पूरगौरदेहाभं नीलकण्ठं त्रिलोचनम् ॥ १४ ॥
> 
> व्याघ्राजिनधरं देवं गजचर्मोत्तरीयकम् ।
> कपालमालाकलितं नागयज्ञोपवीतिनम् ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

There she meditated with her whole heart the auspicious Mahādeva Śaṅkara, the Giver of all desires, thus :-- That Mahādeva is holding the Triśūla (the trident) on his arms; His forehead is adorned with beautiful cooling semi-Moon; He has five faces, each face having three eyes; His throat is coloured blue; He has ten arms; His body is white like camphor; He wears a tiger’s skin; His upper garment is of elephant’s skin; and snakes are his holy thread; He is holding the one-half of the body of Gaurī and his neck is adorned with garlands of human skulls.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

>
**English Translation:**

The Goddess Lakṣmī, the daughter of the ocean, assuming the form of a mare, thus practised severe asceticism in that place of pilgrimage.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

>
**English Translation:**

O King! With a feeling of intense dispassion (Vairāgyam) towards the worldly things, She spent the divine one thousand years in the meditation of Mahādeva, the God of the Gods.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 18</b></summary>
  <br>

**English Translation:**

After that period, the Highest Lord Mahādeva, mounting on His bull, came there with His consort Pārvatī and appeared before the Lakṣmī Devī, perceptible by his eyes.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 19-21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तत्रैत्य सगणः शम्भुस्तामाह हरिवल्लभाम् ।
> तपस्यन्तीं महाभागामश्विनीरूपधारिणीम् ॥ १९ ॥
> 
> किं तपस्यसि कल्याणि जगन्मातर्वदस्व मे ।
> सर्वार्थदः पतिस्तेऽस्ति सर्वलोकविधायकः ॥ २० ॥
> 
> हरिं त्यक्त्वाद्य मां कस्मात्स्तौषि देवि जगत्पतिम् ।
> वासुदेवं जगन्नाथं भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदायकम् ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Appearing thus with His host of His own persons, He then spoke to Lakṣmī, dear to Hari, now practising ascetism in the form of a mare, “O Auspicious One! You are the Mother of this whole Universe and your Husband is the Creator of these worlds and is capable to give all desires. Why are You, then, practising asceticism, when He is present? What is the cause of this? O Devī! Why are You praising hymns to me, instead of to Vāsudeva Śrī Hari, Who is capable to yield enjoyments and final liberation, and Who is the Preserver and the Lord of this world.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वेदोक्तं वचनं कार्यं नारीणां देवता पतिः ।
> नान्यस्मिन्सर्वथा भावः कर्तव्यः कर्हिचित्क्वचित् ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Devī! Work should be done according to the authority of the Vedas; it is stated in the Vedas that the husband is the lord of a woman; therefore it is never advisable to fix one’s mind entirely on another person.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पतिशुश्रूषणं स्त्रीणां धर्म एव सनातनः ।

>
**English Translation:**

The eternal Dharma of women is to serve their husbands; whether the husband be a saint or a sinner, the woman, desirous of her welfare, should serve her husband in every way.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नारायणस्तु सर्वेषां सेव्यो योग्यः सदैव हि ।

>
**English Translation:**

O Daughter of the Ocean! Your husband Nārāyaṇa is fit to be served by all and He is capable to yield all desires. Why are you then worshipping Me, and leaving the Lord of the Goloka, the Deva of the Devas.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> लक्ष्मीरुवाच ।
> आशुतोष महेशान शप्ताहं पतिना शिव ।

>
**English Translation:**

Lakṣmī said :-- O Deva of the Devas! O Seat of Auspiciousness! I know that You are soon pleased with Your servant. My husband has cursed me. O Ocean of mercy! Kindly save me from this curse.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 26-27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तदोक्तं हरिणा शम्भो शापानुग्रहकारणम् ।
> विज्ञप्तेन मया कामं दयायुक्तेन विष्णुना ॥ २६ ॥
> 
> यदा ते भविता पुत्रस्तदा शापस्य मोक्षणम् ।
> भविष्यति च वैकुण्ठवासस्ते कमलालये ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Śambhu! When I informed my husband of my mental agonies, graciously and mercifully He then pointed out how I might be freed of this curse thus :-- “O Kamale! When Your son will be born, then You will be freed of this curse and will, no doubt, come back and live in this Heaven of Vaikuntha.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 28</b></summary>

>
**English Translation:**

Thus spoken, I have come in this hermitage to make tapas and to worship Thee, knowing that Thou art the Bhagavān, the Lord of Bhavanī, the Lord of all and the Giver of all desires.

</details>

<details>

>
**English Translation:**

O Lord of the Devas! How can I get a son without the intercourse of My husband? Though I am guiltless, my husband has forsaken me and is living in Vaikuṇṭha.

</details>

<details>

O Maheśvara! Thou art doing good to all persons; and if Thou art pleased with me, then grant me a boon.

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 31</b></summary>

>
**English Translation:**

O Lord! I know full well that there is no distinction between Him and Thee. O Lord of Girījā! This truth I have come to learn from my husband. O Hara! You are the same thing that He is and what is He is the same as You; there is not the least doubt in this.

</details>

**English Translation:**

O Thou, full of auspiciousness! Recognising the Sameness without any distinction between Him and Thee, I am meditating on Thee. Had it been otherwise, then I would certainly have been guilty when I take Thy refuge and meditate on Thee.

</details>

<details>

>
**English Translation:**

I was very much astonished at this. When His meditation was over and when He was in a pleasant mood, I asked Him with great humility, O Deva of the Devas! I know that You are the Lord of the world and Master of this whole Universe; when Brahmā and the other Devas were united and churned the great ocean, I came out of the waters and looked all around to know who is the superior one whom I can select as my husband and then, thinking You as the superior to all the Devas, I accepted You as my husband. Now whose meditation You are doing again?

</details>

<details>

A great doubt has thus occurred in my mind. O Lord! You are my most Beloved; now disclose to me your innermost desire and thought.”

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 44</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विष्णुरुवाच ।
> शृणु कान्ते प्रवक्ष्यामि यं ध्यायामि सुरोत्तमम् ।
> आशुतोषं महेशानं गिरिजावल्लभं हृदि ॥ ४४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Viṣṇu said :-- “O Beloved! Hear now, whom I am meditating. I am meditating in the lotus of My heart that Mahādeva Maheśvara, the Highest of all the Devas. Mahādeva, the Deva of the Devas, of indomitable prowess, sometimes meditates on Me and sometimes I meditate on the Lord of the Deva, Śaṅkara, the Destroyer of Tripurā.

</details>

<details>

>
**English Translation:**

I am dear to Śiva as his life is dear to him and Śaṅkara is similarly dear to me. The hearts of us both are attached to each other in the most secret way possible; therefore there is not the least difference betwixt us both.

</details>

<details>

**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! Mahādeva, skilled in speech, hearing thus the words of Lakṣmī, consoled Her with sweet words and said :-- “O Beautiful! Be peaceful; I am pleased with Your tapasyā; soon You will come in contact with Your husband.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 18 Verse 60-178</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अन्तर्धानं गतः साक्षादुमया सहितः शिवः ।
> सापि तत्रैव चार्वङ्गी संस्थिता कमलासना ॥ ६० ॥
> 
> ध्यायन्ती चरणाम्भोजं देव्याः परमशोभनम् ।
> देवासुरशिरोरत्ननिघृष्टनखमण्डलम् ॥ ६१ ॥
> 
> प्रेमगद्गदया वाचा तुष्टाव च मुहुर्मुहुः ।
> प्रतीक्षमाणा भर्तारं हयरूपधरं हरिम् ॥ ६२ ॥
>
> *Colophon:*
> इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां षष्ठस्कन्धे
> शिवप्रसादेन लक्ष्मीद्वारा भगवत्याः समाराधनवर्णनं नामाष्टादशोऽध्यायः ॥ ६.१८॥
>
**English Translation:**

Kamalā Devī, lovely in all respects, whose toe nails are always rubbed by the gems on the coronets of the Devas, began to meditate on the lotus-feet of Ambikā and in expectation of his beloved Hari, in the shape of a horse, praised and chanted hymns frequently to the Highest Goddess, in words choked with feelings of intense love. Here ends the Eighteenth Chapter of the Sixth Book on the origin of the Haihaya Dynasty in the Mahāpurāṇam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

</details>

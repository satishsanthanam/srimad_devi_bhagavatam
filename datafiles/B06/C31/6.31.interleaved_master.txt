SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 6, CHAPTER 31

[Ch 31 Verse 1]
६.३१ एकत्रिंशोऽध्यायः । भगवतीमाहात्म्यवर्णनम् ।


व्यास उवाच ।
निशामय महाराज ब्रवीमि विशदाक्षरम् ।
माहात्म्यं खलु मायाया नारदात्तु मया श्रुतम् ॥ १ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said: O King! I am now recounting the detailed glory of Māyā, heard by me from Nārada.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 2]
मया पुनर्मुनिः पृष्टो नारदः सर्ववित्तमः ।
श्रुत्वा कथां मुनेस्तस्य नारीदेहसमुद्भवाम् ॥ २ ॥
English Translation:
Again I asked sage Nārada who is the most omniscient of all, after hearing the story of that sage in the female body.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 3]
ब्रूहि नारद पश्चात्किं कथितं हरिणा तदा ।
क्व गतश्च जगन्नाथो भवता सह माधवः ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation:
O Nārada! Tell me! What did Hari say at that time? Where did Mādhava the lord of the universe go along with you?
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 4]
नारद उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा भगवांस्तस्मिंस्तडागेऽतिमनोहरे ।
आरुह्य गरुडं गन्तुं वैकुण्ठे च मनो दधे ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation:
Nārada said: After saying thus, in that beautiful lake, the lord made up his mind to go to Vaikuntha, mounting on Garuda.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 5]
मामुवाच रमाकान्तो यथेष्टं गच्छ नारद ।
एहि वा मम लोकं त्वं यथारुचि तथा कुरु ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation:
Mahāvisnu, the consort of Lakṣmi, told me. O Nārada! go as you wish. Or come to my world. Do  as you wish.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 6-7]
ब्रह्मलोकं गतश्चाहमापृच्छ्य मधुसूदनम् ।
भगवानपि देवेशस्तत्क्षणाद्गरुडासनः ॥ ६ ॥

वैकुण्ठमगमत्तूर्णं मामादिश्य यथासुखम् ।
ततोऽहं पितृसदनं गतो याते जनार्दने ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation:
After taking leave of Madhusūdana, I went to Brahmaloka. Viṣṇu the lord of Devas, seated on Garuda, went to Vaikuntha quickly, in a happy manner after directing me. When Janārdana was gone, I went to the region of my father (i.e., Brahmā).
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 8]
चिन्तयन्सकलं दुःखं सुखं च परमाद्भुतम् ।
गत्वा प्रणम्य पितरं स्थितो यावत्पुरः पितुः ॥ ८ ॥
English Translation:
Then king about all the wonderful miseries and the joys undergone, I went to my father and after prostrating to him, stood before him.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 9]
तावत्पृष्टो मुने पित्रा वीक्ष्य चिन्तातुरं तु माम् ।
ब्रह्योवाच ।
क्व गतोऽसि महाभाग कस्माच्चिन्तातुरः सुत ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation:
Then seeing me tormented by thoughts, my father Brahmā asked: O great one! Where had you gone? Son! Why are you troubled by thoughts?
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 10]
स्वस्थं नैवाद्य पश्यामि मनस्ते मुनिसत्तम ।
केनापि वञ्चितोऽसि त्वं दृष्टं वा किञ्चिदद्भुतम् ॥ १० ॥
English Translation:
O great sages! I do not see your mind happy today. You have been deceived by some body. Or you have seen some wonder.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 11-12]
विषण्णं गतविज्ञानं पश्यामि त्वां कथं सुत ।
नारद उवाच ।
इति पृष्टस्तदा पित्रा बृस्यां समुपवेश्य च ॥ ११ ॥

तमब्रवं स्ववृत्तान्तं मायाबलसमुद्भवम् ।
वञ्चितोऽहं पितः कामं विष्णुना प्रभविष्णुना ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation:
O Son! How can I see you sad and devoid of discrimination? Nārada said: Thus I was asked by my father. I sat on the seat. I told him my story arising out of the power of Māyā. O father! I have been cheated by the powerful Mahāvisnu.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 13]
स्त्रीभावं गमितः कामं वर्षाणि सुबहून्यपि ।
अनुभूतं महद्दुःखं पुत्रशोकसमुद्भवम् ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation:
I was made to attain the state of a woman for many many years. Extreme misery arising out of death of son was experienced by me.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 14]
प्रबोधितोऽहं तेनैव मृदुवाक्यामृतेन च ।
पुनः सरोवरे स्नात्वा जातोऽहं नारदः पुमान् ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation:
I was instructed by him by nectarine sweet words about my real nature. Again after taking bath in that tank. I became a man (the old) Nārada.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 15]
किमेतत्कारणं ब्रह्मन् यन्मोहमगमं तदा ।
विस्मृतं पूर्वविज्ञानं तन्मयस्तरसा कृतः ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation:
O Brahman! What is the reason that I attained delusion at that time? Earlier knowledge was forgotten. I became identified with that female form, so quickly.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 16]
एतन्मायाबलं ब्रह्मन्न जानेऽहं दुरत्ययम् ।
ज्ञानहानिकरं जातं मूलं मोहस्य विस्फुटम् ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation:
O Brahman! This is the strength of Māyā, which is difficult to cross. It is beyond my understanding. It destroyed all my knowledge. It was a clear cause of delusion.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 17]
अनुभूतं मया सम्यग्ज्ञातं सर्वं शुभाशुभम् ।
कथं त्वं जितवांस्तात तमुपायं वदस्व मे ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation:
I experienced it (power of Māyā) well. All good and bad were known. Of father! How did you attain victory over it? Tell me that clue.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 18]
नारद उवाच ।
विज्ञप्तोऽसौ मया धाता प्रीतिपूर्वमतः परम् ।
मामुवाच स्मितं कृत्वा पिता मे वासवीसुत ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation:
O Muni! When my Father learnt the cause of my cares, he smiled and spoke to me in sweet words :--
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 19-20]
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
दुर्जयैषा सुरैः सर्वैर्मुनिभिश्च महात्मभिः ।
तापसैर्ज्ञानयुक्तैश्च योगिभिः पवनाशनैः ॥ १९ ॥

नाहं तां सर्वथा ज्ञातुं शक्तो मायां महाबलाम् ।
विष्णुर्ज्ञातुं न शक्तश्च तथा शम्भुरुमापतिः ॥ २० ॥
English Translation:
“O Child! The Devas, the high-souled Munis, the wise ascetics and the Yogis subsisting on air only are not able to conquer this Māyā. O Nārada! The power of Māyā is so very great that I, Viṣṇu and Śambhu, the Lord of Umā, none are able to know Her power.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 21]
दुर्ज्ञेया सा महामाया सृष्टिस्थित्यन्तकारिणी ।
कालकर्मस्वभावाद्यैर्निमित्तकारणैर्वृता ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation:
That Mahāmāyā is creating, preserving and dissolving this world by Time, Karma, and Nature and other efficient causes.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 22]
शोकं मा कुरु मेधाविंस्तत्र मायामहाबले ।
न चैव विस्मयः कार्यो वयं सर्वे विमोहिताः ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation:
O Child! Know Her to be inconceivable and unapproachable. O Intelligent One! Do not be sorry nor should you be surprised about Māyā’s great strength, for we all are deluded by Her.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 23]
नारद उवाच ।
पित्रेत्युक्तस्तदा व्यास तमापृच्छ्य गतस्मयः ।
आगतोऽस्म्यत्र पश्यन्वै तीर्थानि च वराणि च ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation:
O Dvaipāyan! Thus advised by my Father, my wonder disappeared. I then asked permission of my Father Padma Yoni (Lotus-born) and went out on tour round the sacred places of pilgrimages and on my way, seeing by and by the chief Tīrthas, I have now come here.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 24-25]
तस्मात्त्वमपि सन्त्यज्य मोहं कौरवनाशजम् ।
कालक्षयं सुखासीनः स्थानेऽस्मिन् कुरु सत्तम ॥ २४ ॥

अवश्यमेव भोक्तव्यं कृतं कर्म शुभाशुभम् ।
निश्चयं हृदये कृत्वा विचरस्व यथासुखम् ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation:
Therefore, O Muni! Dost thou relinquish your sorrows for the extinction of the Kuru’s family and remain here and pass your time in great joy and happiness. One must bear the fruits of one’s Karma, good or bad; knowing this fully roam at your will wherever you like.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 26]
व्यास उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा नारदो राजन् गतो मां प्रतिबोध्य च ।
अहं तच्चिन्तयन्वाक्यं यदुक्तं मुनिना तदा ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Maharṣi Nārada thus kindling knowledge in me, went away; I also thought over his words.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 27]
स्थितः सरस्वतीतीरे कल्पे सारस्वते वरे ।
कालातिवाहनायैतत्कृतं भागवतं मया ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation:
On the banks of the river Sarasvatī, I composed this Devī Bhāgavat to pass away my time during the excellent period of Sārasvata Kalpa.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 28]
पुराणमुत्तमं भूप सर्वसंशयनाशनम् ।
नानाख्यानसमायुक्तं वेदप्रामाण्यसंश्रितम् ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation:
This Purāṇam is excellent; it is composed on the authority of the the Vedas; all doubts are removed by it; many nice events are narrated here.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 29-30]
सन्देहोऽत्र न कर्तव्यः सर्वथा नृपसत्तम ।
यथेन्द्रजालिकः कश्चित्पाञ्चालीं दारवीं करे ॥ २९ ॥

कृत्वा नर्तयते कामं स्वेच्छया वशवर्तिनीम् ।
तथा नर्तयते माया जगत्स्थावरजङ्गमम् ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation:
Therefore, O King! Not the least doubt should be entertained. As a magician makes the wooden dolls dance in his hands at his will, so this world-enchanting Māyā is making this world, moving and non-moving, dance from Brahmā down to the blades of grass and all human beings.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 31]
ब्रह्मादिस्तम्बपर्यन्तं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ।
पञ्चेन्द्रियसमायुक्तं मनश्चित्तानुवर्तनम् ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation:
O King! Know Māyā’s triple Guṇas to be the cause of this mind consisting of five organs of senses, that follows the Chitta (mind, buddhi and Ahamkāra).
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 32-34]
गुणास्तु कारणं राजन् सर्वेषां सर्वथा त्रयः ।
कार्यं कारणसंयुक्तं भवतीति विनिश्चयः ॥ ३२ ॥

भिन्नभिन्नस्वभावास्ते गुणा मायासमुद्भवाः ।
शान्तो घोरस्तथा मूढस्त्रयस्तु विविधा यतः ॥ ३३ ॥

तत्समेतः पुमान्नित्यं तद्विहीनः कथं भवेत् ।
न भवत्येव संसारे रहितस्तन्तुभिः पटः ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation:
Actions arise from the causes thereof; there is no doubt in this; what doubt, then, there can arise that all these creatures of different temperaments will come out of the different Guṇas of Māyā. Peaceful, terrible and stupid become the persons in contact with the Mayic Guṇas.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 35-37]
तथा गुणैस्त्रिभिर्हीनो न देहीति विनिश्चयः ।
देवदेहो मनुष्यो वा तिरश्चो वा नराधिप ॥ ३५ ॥

गुणैर्विरहितो न स्यान्मृद्विहीनो घटो यथा ।
ब्रह्मा विष्णुस्तथा रुद्रस्त्रयश्चामी गुणाश्रयाः ॥ ३६ ॥

कदाचित्प्रीतियुक्तास्ते तथाप्रीतियुताः पुनः ।
तथा विषादयुक्तास्ते भवन्ति गुणयोगतः ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation:
How, then, can they exist, bereft of them? As the cloth cannot exist without threads, so the embodied beings cannot exist in the world without the triple Guṇas of Māyā. There is no doubt in this. As a pot cannot be made without clay, so these bodies, Devas, human or birds, cannot be created without the Guṇas. Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva, too, are possessed of those three Guṇas and therefore they become sometimes happy and satisfied, sometimes unhappy and dissatisfied and sometimes they become sad and remorseful as they are then under the influence of one Guṇa or the other.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 38-40]
ब्रह्मा कदाचित्सत्त्वस्थस्तदा शान्तः समाधिमान् ।
प्रीतियुक्तो भवेत्सर्वभूतेषु ज्ञानसंयुतः ॥ ३८ ॥

पुनः सत्त्वविहीनस्तु रजोगुणसमावृतः ।
तदा भवेद् घोररूपः सर्वत्राप्रीतिसंयुतः ॥ ३९ ॥

यदा तमोगुणाविष्टो बाहुल्येन भवेद्विधिः ।
तदा विषादसम्पन्नो मूढो भवति नान्यथा ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation:
Brahmā happens at times to be full of wisdom and knowledge, his temper peaceful, sweet and pleasant and his soul rapt in Samādhi, when he becomes possessed of Sāttva Guṇa; again when he is void of Sāttva and filled with Rājo Guṇa, His temper becomes unpleasant and his appearance gets dark and awful everywhere; and when he becomes grossly Tāmasic, He becomes sorrowful and bereft entirely of intelligence.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 41-42]
माधवोऽपि सदा सत्त्वसंश्रितः सर्वथा भवेत् ।
यदा शान्तः प्रीतियुक्तो भवेज्ज्ञानसमन्वितः ॥ ४१ ॥

स एव रजाधिक्यादप्रीतिसंयुतो भवेत् ।
घोरश्च सर्वभूतेषु गुणाधीनो रमापतिः ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation:
Viṣṇu, when resting in Sāttva, becomes peaceful, sweet-tempered, and full of knowledge; when Rājo Guṇa preponderates in Him, He becomes void of sweetness and becomes awful to all the beings.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 43-47]
रुद्रोऽपि सत्त्वसंयुक्तः प्रीतिमाञ्छान्तिमान्भवेत् ।
रजोनिमीलितः सोऽपि घोरः प्रीतिविवर्जितः ॥ ४३ ॥

तमोगुणयुतः सोऽपि मूढो विषादयुग्भवेत् ।
एते यदि गुणाधीना ब्रह्मविष्णुहरादयः ॥ ४४ ॥

सूर्यवंशोद्भवास्तद्वत्सोमवंशभवा अपि ।
मन्वादयश्च ये प्रोक्ताश्चतुर्दश युगे युगे ॥ ४५ ॥

अन्येषां चैव का वार्ता संसारेऽस्मिन्नृपोत्तम ।
मायाधीनं जगत्सर्वं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ॥ ४६ ॥

तस्माद्राजन्न कर्तव्यः सन्देहोऽत्र कदाचन ।
देही मायापराधीनश्चेष्टते तद्वशानुगः ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation:
The whole world is under the control of Māyā; the Devas, men and all other beings. None should doubt on this point. All the embodied beings labour under the directions of Māyā; never can they work independently.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 48]
सा च माया परे तत्त्वे संविद्रूपेऽपि सर्वदा ।
तदधीना प्रेरिता च तेन जीवेषु सर्वदा ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation:
This Māyā is again always residing in the Highest Essence, the Samvit or the Universal Pure Consciousness. Thus Māyā is dependent on the Highest Goddess, Who is of the nature of Samvit, and, stimulated by Her, resides in the hearts of all the Jīvas.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 49]
ततो मायाविशिष्टां तां संविदं परमेश्वरीम् ।
मायेश्वरीं भगवतीं सच्चिदानन्दरूपिणीम् ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation:
Therefore one ought to meditate, worship and bow down before the Bhagavatī, the Creatrix of Māyā and Who is of the nature of Samvit, Pure Existence, Intelligence and Bliss.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 50]
ध्यायेत्तथाराधयेच्च प्रणमेच्च जपेदपि ।
तेन सा सदया भूत्वा मोचयत्येव देहिनम् ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation:
Thus She becomes gracious and merciful and liberates the Jīvas, giving them Her realisation and drawing together Her own Māyā away from them.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 51]
स्वमायां संहरत्येव स्वानुभूतिप्रदानतः ।
भुवनं खलु माया स्यादीश्वरी तस्य नायिका ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation:
This whole cosmos is nothing but Māyā and the Consciousness (Samvit) of the nature of Brāhmaṇ is the Lord of Māyā. For this reason that Beautiful One in the triple worlds, the Devī Bhagavatī is known by the name Bhuvaneśvarī, the Great Lady of the worlds.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 52]
भुवनेशी ततः प्रोक्ता देवी त्रैलोक्यसुन्दरी ।
तद्रूपे यदि सक्तं स्याच्चित्तं भूमिपते सदा ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation:
O King! If the Jīvas can fix their hearts on that Samvit, then Māyā, born of the real and unreal, is quite unable to do any harm to them.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 53-54]
मायया किं भवेत्तत्र सदसद्भूतया नृप ।
तस्मान्मायानिरासार्थं नान्यद्वै देवतान्तरम् ॥ ५३ ॥

समर्थं तु विना देवीं सच्चिदानन्दरूपिणीम् ।
तमोराशिं नाशयितुं शक्तं नैव तमो भवेत् ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation:
No other Deva than the Bhuvaneśvarī, of the nature of pure existence, intelligence and bliss is able to remove this Māyā. O King! Darkness cannot destroy darkness; the Sun, Moon, Lightning or Fire can destroy it.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 55-56]
किन्तु भानुप्रभाचन्द्रविद्युद्वह्निप्रभादयः ।
तस्मान्मायेश्वरीमम्बां स्वप्रकाशां तु संविदम् ॥ ५५ ॥

आराधयेदतिप्रीत्या मायागुणनिवृत्तये ।
इति सम्यङ्मयाख्यातं वृत्रासुरवधादिकम् ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation:
Therefore it is highly iñcumbent on us to worship the Lady of Māyā, the Samvit, the Mother with a cheerful heart to remove the Māyā and Her Guṇas. O King! Now I have narrated to you all the events concerning the killing of Vritrāsura that you asked.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 57-58]
यत्पृष्टं राजशार्दूल किमन्यच्छ्रोतुमिच्छसि ।
पूर्वार्धोऽयं पुराणस्य कथितस्तव सुव्रत ॥ ५७ ॥

यत्र देव्यास्तु महिमा विस्तरेणोपपादितः ।
एतद्रहस्यं श्रीमातुर्न देयं यस्य कस्यचित् ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation:
What more do you want to hear now. O One devoted to vows! I have now described the first half of that Purāṇa, which describes in detail the glory of Śrī Devī Bhagavatī. This Purāṇa, the secret of this Mother of the whole Universe, is not to be disclosed indiscriminately to anybody.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 59]
देयं भक्ताय शान्ताय देवीभक्तिरताय च ।
शिष्याय ज्येष्ठपुत्राय गुरुभक्तियुताय च ॥ ५९ ॥
English Translation:
Those that are peaceful, self-restrained, devoted, and possessed of Bhakti to the Devī, the disciples that are devoted to their Gurus and the eldest son, those are the fit recipients thereof.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 31 Verse 60]
इदमखिलकथानां सारभूतं पुराणं
निखिलनिगमतुल्यं सप्रमाणानुविद्धम् ।
पठति परमभावाद्यः शृणोतीह भक्त्या
स भवति धनवान्वै ज्ञानवान्मानवोऽत्र ॥ ६० ॥

इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां
षष्ठस्कन्धे भगवतीमाहात्म्यवर्णनं नामकत्रिंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ६.३१॥
English Translation:
Whoever reads or hears with greatest devotion this Mahāpurāṇam, equivalent to the Vedas, fraught with sound proofs and the essence of all talks, becomes, in this world, possessed of great wealth, becomes wise and passes his time in the greatest happiness. There is no doubt in this. Here ends the Thirty-first Chapter on the Sixth Book on the glory of Māyā in the Mahāpurāṇam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

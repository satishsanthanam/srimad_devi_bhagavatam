# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 6, CHAPTER 14

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 1-2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ६.१४ चतुर्दशोऽध्यायः । वसिष्ठस्य मैत्रावरुणिरितिनामवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> जनमेजय उवाच ।
> मैत्रावरुणिरित्युक्तं नाम तस्य मुनेः कथम् ।
> वसिष्ठस्य महाभाग ब्रह्मणस्तनुजस्य ह ॥ १ ॥
> 
> किमसौ कर्मतो नाम प्राप्तवान् गुणतस्तथा ।
> ब्रूहि मे वदतांश्रेष्ठ कारणं तस्य नामजम् ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Janamejaya said :-- “O Bhagavān! Maharṣi Vaśiṣṭha was the mind-born son of Brahmā; how is it then that you have named him as Maitrā-Vāruṇī. Is it that he got this name by some action or by some Guṇas? Kindly tell me the origin of this name, O Best of speakers!”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 3-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> निबोध नृपतिश्रेष्ठ वसिष्ठो ब्रह्मणः सुतः ।
> निमिशापात्तनुं त्यक्त्वा पुनर्जातो महाद्युतिः ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> मित्रावरुणयोर्यस्मात्तस्मात्तन्नाम विश्रुतम् ।
> मैत्रावरुणिरित्यस्मिँल्लोके सर्वत्र पार्थिव ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! It is quite true that the illustrious Vaśiṣṭha was the noble son of Brahmā but he had to quit that body due to the curse of the King Nimi and he had to take a second body from Mitrā Varuṇa; hence he is named in this world as Maitrā-Vāruṇī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 5-6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> राजोवाच ।
> कस्माच्छप्तः स धर्मात्मा राज्ञा ब्रह्मात्मजो मुनिः ।
> चित्रमेतन्मुनिं लग्नो राज्ञः शापोऽतिदारुणः ॥ ५ ॥
> 
> अनागसं मुनिं राजा किमसौ शप्तवान्मुने ।
> कारणं वद धर्मज्ञ तस्य शापस्य मूलतः ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The King said :-- “O Bhagavān! How was it that the religious Vaśiṣṭha, the best of the Munis, the son of Brahmā was cursed by the King? Oh! The Munis have to suffer the dreadful curse of Kṣattriya kings! This seems very wonderful to me. O Knower of Dharma! Why did that king curse the innocent Muni? I am very curious to hear the cause of this; kindly tell me the cause of the curse.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> कारणं तु मया प्रोक्तं तव पूर्वं विनिश्चितम् ।
> संसारोऽयं त्रिभिर्व्याप्तो राजन्मायागुणैः किल ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! I told you already in particular all the causes of these. This Samsāra is pervaded by the three Guṇas of Māyā, Sāttva, Rāja and Tāma.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> धर्मं करोतु भूपालश्चरन्तु तापसास्तपः ।
> सर्वेषां तु गुणैर्विद्धं नोज्ज्वलं तद्भवेदिह ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Whether the kings practise their Dharma or the ascetics practise their tapas, all their actions are interpenetrated with these Guṇas; therefore they cannot shine so brightly.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कामक्रोधाभिभूताश्च राजानो मुनयस्तथा ।
> लोभाहङ्कारसंयुक्ताश्चरन्ति दुश्चरं तपः ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Kings, Munis performed very severe penances and austerities under the influence of lust, anger, greed and Ahamkāra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यजन्ति क्षत्रिया राजन् रजोगुणसमावृताः ।
> ब्राह्मणास्तु तथा राजन् न कोऽपि सत्त्वसंयुतः ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! All, whether they be the Kṣattriyas or the Brāhmaṇas, who perform their sacrifices overpowered with this Rājo Guṇa, really, none of them performs these actions guided by Sāttva Guṇa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ऋषिणासौ निमिः शप्तस्तेन शप्तो मुनिः पुनः ।
> दुःखाद्दुःखतरं प्राप्तावुभावपि विधेर्बलात् ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The King Nimi was cursed by the Ṛṣi and the Ṛṣi was cursed again by the King Nimi; thus they met with greater calamities and painful sufferings, the fruits from the hands of the powerful Fate.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> द्रव्यशुद्धिः क्रियाशुद्धिर्मनसः शुद्धिरुज्ज्वला ।
> दुर्लभा प्राणिनां भूप संसारे त्रिगुणात्मके ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! In this world of the three Guṇas, it is very difficult for the beings to get the Dravya Śuddhi, Kriyā Śuddhi, and the pure effulgent Chitta Śuddhi,

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पराशक्तिप्रभावोऽयं नोल्लङ्घ्यः केनचित्क्वचित् ।
> यस्यानुगृहमिच्छेत्सा मोचयत्येव तं क्षणात् ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Know this as the influence wielded by the Highest Śakti, the Mother of this Universe. Nobody is able to overstep it; but he, whom She favours, can cross in a moment this world, bounded by the three Guṇas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> महान्तोऽपि न मुच्यन्ते हरिब्रह्महरादयः ।
> पामरा अपि मुच्यन्ते यथा सत्यव्रतादयः ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

What more can be said than the fact that Hari, Hara, and Brahmā and the other Gods cannot free themselves without Her grace.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्यास्तु हृदयं कोऽपि न वेत्ति भुवनत्रये ।
> तथापि भक्तवश्येयं भवत्येव सुनिश्चितम् ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Moreover, the sinners like Satyavrata and others become free when Her Grace comes upon them.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्मात्तद्भक्तिरास्थेया दोषनिर्भूलनाय च ।
> रागदम्भादियुक्ता चेत्सा भक्तिर्नाशिनी भवेत् ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nobody in these three worlds can know what reigns in Her mind; again, this is also a certain fact that She gets Herself bound by Her own will to Her devotees.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इक्ष्याकुकुलसम्भूतो निमिर्नाम नराधिपः ।
> रूपवान् गुणसम्पन्नो धर्मज्ञो लोकरञ्जकः ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Therefore it is extremely desirable that one should have recourse to Sāttvikī devotion for the complete removal of faults and sins.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सत्यवादी दानपरो याजको ज्ञानवाच्छुचिः ।
> द्वादशस्तनयो धीमान्प्रजापालनतत्परः ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

And as the devotion with attachment and vanity is always injurious to men, therefore it is highly beneficial to quit it; there is no doubt in this.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 19-20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पुरं निवेशयामास गौतमाश्रमसन्निधौ ।
> जयन्तपुरसंज्ञं तु ब्राह्मणानां हिताय सः ॥ १९ ॥
> 
> बुद्धिस्तस्य समुत्पन्ना यजेयमिति राजसी ।
> यज्ञेन बहुकालेन दक्षिणासंयुतेन च ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! There was a king named Nimi, born of the family of Ikṣāku. He was beautiful, well qualified, virtuous, truthful, charitable, endearing to his people, a sacrificer, of pure conduct and manners, ready to govern his subjects, intelligent and endowed with knowledge.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इस्वाकुं पितरं पृष्ट्वा यज्ञकार्याय पार्थिवः ।
> कारयामास सम्भारं यथोद्दिष्टं महात्मभिः ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

For the benefit of the Brāhmaṇas, that high-souled king established a city named Jayantupur in close vicinity to the hermitage of Gautama.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> भृगुमङ्गिरसं चैव वामदेवं च गौतमम् ।
> वसिष्ठं च पुलस्त्यं च ऋचीकं पुलहं क्रतुम् ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus some time passed when this Rājasik idea arose in his mind that “I will perform a sacrifice extending for a good many years when I will give exhorbitant Dakṣiṇās (remunerations to the priests and Brāhmins).”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मुनीनामन्त्रयामास सर्वज्ञान्वेदपारगान् ।
> यज्ञविद्याप्रवीणांश्च तापसान्वेदवित्तमान् ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Getting permission from his own father Ikṣāku, he began to collect all the ingredients necessary for the sacrifice, as advised by the high-souled persons.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> राजा सम्भृतसम्भारः सम्पूज्य गुरुमात्मनः ।
> वसिष्ठं प्राह धर्मज्ञो विनयेन समन्वितः ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He invited the all-knowing Munis and ascetics, versed in the Vedas and in conducting sacrifices, e.g., Bhṛgu, Aṅgirā, Vāmadeva, Gautama, Vaśiṣṭha, Pulastya, Richika, Pulaha, Kratu and others, all well-versed in the Vedas.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 25-26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यजेयं मुनिशार्दूल याजयस्व कृपानिधे ।
> गुरुस्त्वं सर्ववेत्तासि कार्यं मे कुरु साम्प्रतम् ॥ २५ ॥
> 
> यज्ञोपकरणं सर्वं समानीतं सुसंस्कृतम् ।
> पञ्चवर्षसहस्रं तु दीक्षां कर्तुं मतिश्च मे ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then that religious King Nimi, collecting all the materials necessary for sacrifice worshipped his own Guru Vaśiṣṭha and then spoke to him (the Guru) with great humility. O Best of Munis! I will perform a sacrifice; kindly perform this my sacrificial act; you are my Guru and therefore you know everything; so do this sacrifice for me. All the articles for this purpose are brought and cleansed.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 27-28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यस्मिन्यज्ञे समाराध्या देवी श्रीजगदम्बिका ।
> तत्प्रीत्यर्थमहं यज्ञं करोमि विधिपूर्वकम् ॥ २७ ॥
> 
> तच्छ्रुत्वासौ निमेर्वाक्यं वसिष्ठः प्राह भूपतिम् ।
> इन्द्रेणाहं वृतः पूर्वं यज्ञार्थं नृपसत्तम ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Guru! Know that for five thousand years I mean to be engaged in this sacrifice, this is my Saṅkalpa (will). I will worship the Goddess Ambikā in this sacrifice and for Her satisfaction I am arranging for it according to the prescribed rules. Hearing the King Nimi’s words, Vaśiṣṭha said :-- “O best of Kings! Indra, the King of the Devas, has already selected me for his sacrificial ceremony.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पराशक्तिमखं कर्तुमुद्युक्तः पाकशासनः ।
> स दीक्षां गमितो देवः पञ्चवर्षशतात्मिकाम् ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Now Indra is ready to do the sacrifice for the propitiation of the Highest Śakti and I have initiated him for five hundred years.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्मात्त्वमन्तरं तावत्प्रतिपालय पार्थिव ।
> इन्द्रयज्ञे समाप्तेऽत्र कृत्वा कार्यं दिवस्पतेः ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Therefore, O King! You will have to wait till I complete the Indra’s Yajñā. After fully completing all his works, I will come here. Therefore, O King! Wait till then.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 31-32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> आगमिष्याम्यहं राजंस्तावत्त्वं प्रतिपालय ।
> राजोवाच ।
> मया निमन्त्रिताश्चान्ये मुनयो यज्ञकारणात् ॥ ३१ ॥
> 
> सम्भाराः सम्भृताः सर्वे पालयामि कथं गुरो ।
> इक्ष्वाकूणां कुले ब्रह्मन्गुरुस्त्वं वेदवित्तमः ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The King said :-- “O Best of Munis! I have already invited other Munis for this sacrifice and have collected all the materials; how, then, can I wait for you? O Brāhmaṇa! You are the foremost of those versed in the Vedas and you are the family Guru of the noble Ikṣāku.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 33-34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कथं त्यक्त्वाद्य मे कार्यमुद्यतो गन्तुमाशु वै ।
> न ते युक्तं द्विजश्रेष्ठ यदुत्सृज्य मखं मम ॥ ३३ ॥
> 
> गन्तासि धनलोभेन लोभाकुलितचेतनः ।
> निवारितोऽपि राज्ञा स जगामेन्द्रमखं गुरुः ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

How is it, then, avoiding my work you are ready to go elsewhere O Best of Brāhmins! Under the uncontrollable greed of wealth you have lost all senses and you are ready to go away without doing my work. This does not behove you.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 35-36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> राजापि विमना भूत्वा गौतमं प्रत्यपूजयत् ।
> इयाज हिमवत्पार्श्वे सागरस्य समीपतः ॥ ३५ ॥
> 
> दक्षिणा बहुला दत्ता विप्रेभ्यो मखकर्मणि ।
> निमिना पञ्चसाहस्री दीक्षा तत्र कृता नृप ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Though thus tempted by the King Nimi, the Ṛṣi Vaśiṣṭha went to the Indra’s sacrifice. The King, too, became absent-minded and selected for the sacrifice the Ṛṣi Gautama.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 37</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ऋत्विजः पूजिताः कामं धनैर्गोभिर्मुदा युताः ।
> शक्रयज्ञसमाप्ते तु पञ्चवर्षशतात्मके ॥ ३७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He then commenced his sacrificial ceremony close to the ocean by the side of the Himālayān range and gave profusely the Dakṣiṇās.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 38-39</b></summary>
  <br>

>
**English Translation:**

The King Nimi was engaged in this sacrificial act for five thousand years. In this the Rittviks (priests) were worshipped with sufficient wealth and cows; they were extremely glad.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वसिष्ठस्य ततो मन्युः प्रादुर्भूतोऽवमानतः ।
> अदर्शनान्निमेस्तत्र चुकोप मुनिसत्तमः ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then, when the five hundred years extending sacrifice of Indra was completed, the Ṛṣi Vaśiṣṭha came to see the King Nimi’s sacrifice and waited there  to see the King.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**

> यस्मात्त्वं मां गुरुं त्यक्त्वा कृत्वान्यं गुरुमात्मनः ॥ ४१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The king was then asleep; so the servants did not awake him from his sleep; and the King did not come to the Ṛṣi. Feeling insult at this, the Maharṣi Vaśiṣṭha became infuriated with rage.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 14 Verse 42</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दीक्षितोऽसि बलान्मन्द मामवज्ञाय पार्थिव ।
> वारितोऽपि मया तस्माद्विदेहस्त्वं भविष्यसि ॥ ४२ ॥

Not seeing the King, he became very angry; and, subject to this, he cursed the King, When I am your lifelong Guru, especially when I prohibited you and you have forsaken me and selected another Guru and by your sheer force you are initiated, then be devoid of your body. Let your body fall off today.

</details>

You are the son of Brahmā; and, being versed in the Vedas and Vedāṅgas, you are yet unaware of the subtle and very difficult nature of the Brāhminic religion.

Now you want to cast your own fault on my shoulders and you are trying in vain to curse me. Anger is more to be blamed than Cāṇḍāla!

The wise men should overcome it by all means. When you, infuriated with rage, have been able to curse me for nothing, then I now curse you,

**English Translation:**

“Let your this body, inflamed with anger, drop off.” O King! Thus the King cursed the Muni and the Muni cursed the King; and both of them were, therefore, very sorry.

Vaśiṣṭha then became troubled with cares and took shelter with Brahmā and informing him about the great curse given by the King Nimi said :-- Father! </details>

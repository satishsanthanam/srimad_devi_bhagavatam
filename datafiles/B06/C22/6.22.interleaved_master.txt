SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 6, CHAPTER 22
================================================================================

[Ch 22 Verse 1]
६.२२ द्वाविंशोऽध्यायः । हैहयैकवीराय यशोवत्यैकावलीमोचनाय देवीस्वप्नवर्णनम् ।


यशोवत्युवाच ।
प्रातरुत्थाय तन्वङ्गी चलिता च सखीयुता ।
चामरैर्वीज्यमाना सा रक्षिता बहुरक्षिभिः ॥ १ ॥
English Translation:
Yaśovatī spoke :-- O King! One day Ekāvalī got up early in the morning and went to the banks of the Ganges, accompanied by her companions; they began to fan her with a chowrie.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 2]
सायुधैश्चातिसन्नद्धैः सहिता वरवर्णिनी ।
क्रीडार्थमत्र राजेन्द्र सम्प्राप्ता नलिनीं शुभाम् ॥ २ ॥
English Translation:
The armed guards accompanied her. Slowly she went where there were the lotuses in order to sport with them.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 3]
अहमप्यनया सार्धं गङ्गातीरे समागता ।
अप्सरोभिः समेता च कमलैः क्रीडमानया ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation:
I, too, went with her playing with the lotuses to the banks of the Ganges and both of us began to play with lotuses with the Apsarās.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 4-5]
एकावली तथा चाहं जाते क्रीडापरे यदा ।
सहसैव तदायातो दानवो बलसंयुतः ॥ ४ ॥

कालकेतुरिति ख्यातो राक्षसैर्बहुभिर्युतः ।
परिघासिगदाचापबाणतोमरपाणिभिः ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation:
When both of us were deeply engaged in the play, then one powerful Dānava, named Kālaketu, came up there suddenly with many Rākṣasas armed with parighas, swords, clubs, bows, arrows and tomaras and many other weapons.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 6]
दृष्टा चैकावली तेन रूपयौवनशालिनी ।
द्वितीया कामपत्नीव क्रीडमाना सुपङ्कजैः ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation:
Ekāvalī was playing with the best lotuses when Kālaketu saw her in that state, blooming with beauty and youth as if like Ratī, the Goddess of Love.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 7]
मयोक्तैकावली राजन् कोऽयं दैत्यः समागतः ।
गच्छावो रक्षपालानां मध्ये पङ्कजलोचने ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation:
O King! I then spoke to Ekāvalī :-- “Look! Who is this Daitya that has come here unexpectedly; O Lotus-eyed One! Let us go into the central part of our armed guards.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 8-10]
विमृश्यैव सखी चाहं त्वरयैव गते भयात् ।
मध्ये वै सैनिकानां तु सायुधानां नृपात्मज ॥ ८ ॥

कालकेतुस्तु तां दृष्ट्वा मोहिनीं मदनातुरः ।
गदां गुर्वी गृहीत्वा तु धावमानः समागतः ॥ ९ ॥

रक्षकान्दूरतः कृत्वा जग्राहाम्बुजलोचनाम् ।
त्रस्तां वेपथुसंयुक्तां क्रन्दमानां कृशोदरीम् ॥ १० ॥
English Translation:
O King! My companion and myself consulting thus, went out of fear immediately into the centre of the armed guards. Kālaketu was seized with the arrows of Cupid, and no sooner he looked at that beautiful young lady than he, with a very big club in his hand, hurriedly came to us, drove away the guards, and took away my lotus eyed companion, of thin waist. Then the young lady, helpless, began to tremble and cried aloud.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 11]
त्यजैनां मां गृहाणेति मया चोक्तोऽपि दानवः ।
न मां जग्राह कामार्तस्तां गृहीत्वा विनिःसृतः ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation:
Seeing this, I spoke to the Dānava :-- Leave her and take me. The passionate Dānava did not take me but he went away, taking my companion.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 12]
तिष्ठ तिष्ठेति भाषन्तो रक्षकास्तं महाबलम् ।
प्रतिषिध्य तु सङ्ग्रामं चक्रुर्विस्मयकारकम् ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation:
The guards exclaimed :-- “Wait, wait; don’t fly away with the girl; we are giving you a good lesson.” Thus saying, they made the powerful Dānavas stop and both the parties engaged in a very terrible conflict, astounding to all.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 13]
तस्यापि राक्षसाः क्रूराः सर्वतः शस्त्रपाणयः ।
युयुधू रक्षकैः सार्धं स्वामिकार्ये कृतोद्यमाः ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation:
The followers of the Dānavas, more cruel and all fully armed began at once to fight with great enthusiasm for their Master’s cause.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 14]
सङ्ग्रामस्तु तदा जातः कालकेतोस्तथा रणे ।
निहत्य रक्षकान्सर्वान्गृहीत्वैनां महाबलः ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation:
Kālaketu himself began to fight afterwards terribly and killed the guards. He, then, with his followers, carried away my companion towards his own city.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 15]
युक्तो राक्षससैन्येन निर्जगाम पुरं प्रति ।
वीक्ष्य तां रुदतीं बालां गृहीता दानवेन तु ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation:
I, too, followed my companion, when I saw her thus carried away by the Dānava and crying out of fear.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 16]
पृष्ठतोऽहं गता तत्र यत्र नीता सखी मम ।
विक्रोशन्ती यथा सा मां पश्येदिति पदानुगा ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation:
I also walked crying aloud by those tracks as would enable my Sakhī to see me. She, too, seeing me, became somewhat consoled.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 17-18]
सापि मामागतां वीक्ष्य किञ्चित्स्वस्थाभवत्तदा ।
गताहं तत्समीपे तु तामाभाष्य पुनः पुनः ॥ १७ ॥

सा मां प्राप्यातिदुःखार्ता स्तम्भस्वेदसमाकुला ।
कण्ठे गृहीत्वा मां भूप रुरोद भृशदुःखिता ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation:
Crying out repeatedly I approached her. She was already very distressed and when she saw me, she clasped me closely around my neck, perspiring and stunned and, becoming more distressed, cried aloud.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 19]
स मामाह कालकेतुः प्रीतिपूर्वमिदं वचः ।
समाश्वासय भीतां त्वं सखीं चञ्चललोचनाम् ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation:
Kālaketu then showed his liking for me and told that my quick-eyed companion was very afraid and that I might comfort her.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 20]
प्राप्तं ममाद्य नगरं देवलोकसमं प्रिये ।
दासोऽस्मि तव रत्या हि कस्मात्क्रन्दसि कातरा ॥ २० ॥
English Translation:
He told me thus :-- “O Dear! My city is like the Deva’s abodes; you will soon be able to go there. From today I become your slave, bound by love. Do not cry thus distressedly; be comforted.” In these words he told me to comfort my dear companion.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 21-22]
कथयैनां सखीं तेऽद्य स्वस्था भव सुलोचने ।
इत्युक्त्वा मां सखीपार्श्वे समारोप्य रथोत्तमे ॥ २१ ॥

जगाम तरसा दुष्टः पुरे स्वस्य मनोहरे ।
सैन्येन महता युक्तः प्रफुल्लवदनाम्बुजः ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation:
Thus speaking, that villain made both of us mount on the beautiful chariot and making us sit by his sides went gladly and quickly to his own beautiful palace, followed by his army.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 23]
एकावली तथा मां च संस्थाप्य धवले गृहे ।
राक्षसान् गृहरक्षार्थं कल्पयामास कोटिशः ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation:
That Demon placed both of us in a beautiful house white washed and mirror-like and kept hundreds and thousands of Rākṣasas to watch and protect us.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 24-25]
द्वितीये दिवसे सोऽथ मामुवाच रहो नृप ।
प्रबोधय सखीं बालां शोचन्तीं विरहातुराम् ॥ २४ ॥

पत्नी मे भव सुश्रोणि सुखं भुङ्क्षव यथेप्सितम् ।
राज्यं त्वदीयं चन्द्रास्ये सेवकोऽहं सदा तव ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation:
He told me to speak the following words to my companion :-- “O One of beautiful hips! Be my wife and enjoy as you like. O One with a face beautiful like the Moon! This kingdom is yours; ever I am your obedient slave.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 26]
पुनरुक्तं मया वाक्यं श्रुत्वा तद्भाषितं खरम् ।
नाहं क्षमाप्रियं वक्तुं त्वमेनां कथय प्रभो ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation:
Hearing his unbearable harsh words I said :-- “O Lord! I will not be able to speak her these words, disagreeable to her. You better speak this yourself.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 27-29]
इत्युक्ते वचने दुष्टो मदनक्षतमानसः ।
उवाच विनयादेनां सखीं क्षामोदरीं प्रियाम् ॥ २७ ॥

कृशोदरि त्वया मन्त्रो निक्षिप्तोऽस्ति ममोपरि ।
तेन मे हृदयं कान्ते हृतं ते वशतां गतम् ॥ २८ ॥

तेनाहं तव दासोऽद्य कृतोऽस्मीति विनिश्चयः ।
भज मां कामबाणेन पीडितं विवशं भृशम् ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation:
When I spoke thus, that wicked Dānava struck by the arrows of Cupid began to speak gently to my dear companion of thin belly, thus :-- “O Dear One! Today you have successfully cast on me the Vasīkaraṇa Mantra (one of the Tāntrik processes by which a lover is made to come under subjection); O Beloved! Therefore it is that my heart is stolen and so much brought under your subjection; this has converted me into a veritable slave of yours; then know this as certain that I am your slave; O Sweet One! I am very much troubled by the Cupid’s arrows and I am semi-unconscious; therefore O Lean-bellied One! Worship me.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 30]
यौवनं याति रम्भोरु चञ्चलं दुर्लभं तदा ।
सफलं कुरु कल्याणि पतिं मां परिरभ्य च ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation:
O One of beautiful thighs! This youth is a very rare and transient thing; O Auspicious One! Now embrace me as your husband and make your youth a veritable success.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 31]
एकावल्युवाच ।
पित्राहं कल्पिता पूर्वं दातुं राजसुताय वै ।
हैहयस्तु महाभाग स मया मनसा वृतः ॥ ३१ ॥
English Translation:
Ekāvalī said :-- “O Fortunate One! My father wanted to hand me over to a prince named Haihaya; I also mentally adopted him as my husband.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 32]
कथमन्यं भजे कान्तं त्यक्त्वा धर्मं सनातनम् ।
कन्याधर्मं विहायाद्य वेत्सि शास्त्रविनिश्चयम् ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation:
You are certainly aware of the Śāstras; how can I now abandon the eternal religion and the virtue of a woman and take up another husband.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 33]
यस्मै दद्यात्पिता कामं कन्या तं पतिमाप्नुयात् ।
परतन्त्रा सदा कन्या न स्वातन्त्र्यं कदाचन ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation:
The girl must accept him to whom the father betrothes. The girl is under every circumstances dependent.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 34]
इत्युक्तोऽपि तया पापी विरराम न मोहितः ।
न मुमोच विशालाक्षीं मां च पार्श्वस्थितां तथा ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation:
Never do they get any independence.” Though Ekāvalī said thus, the vicious Daitya struck by Cupid’s arrows, did not desist and did not leave me and that larged-eyed companion.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 35]
पातालविवरे तस्य पुरं परमसङ्कटे ।
राक्षसै रक्षितं दुर्गं मण्डितं परिखावृतम् ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation:
His city lies in Pātāla and is a very dangerous place; always it is guarded by Rākṣasas and surrounded by a moat; inside is built a beautiful and strong fort.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 36]
तत्र तिष्ठति दुःखार्ता सखी मे प्राणवल्लभा ।
तेनाहं विरहेणात्र रारटीमि सुदुःखिता ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation:
Now my dear companion, the queen of my heart, is staying there with a grievous heart and I am here wandering hither and thither very much troubled on account of her bereavements.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 37]
एकवीर उवाच ।
कथं त्वमत्र सम्प्राप्ता पुरात्तस्य दुरात्मनः ।
विस्मयो मे महानत्र तत्त्वं ब्रूहि वरानने ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation:
Ekavīra said :-- “O Beautiful-faced One! How have you been able to get away from the city of that wicked demon and how have you been able to come here? I am perfectly at my wit’s end.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 38]
त्वया च कथितं वाक्यं सन्दिग्धं भाति भामिनि ।
हैहयार्थे कल्पिता सा पित्रेति मम साम्प्रतम् ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation:
Say quickly all these. O Proud One! I doubt your words; the father of your dear companion resolved to give his daughter to Haihaya in marriage; now I am that Haihaya.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 39]
हैहयो नाम राजाहं नान्योऽस्ति पृथिवीपतिः ।
मदर्थे कथिता सा किं सखी तव सुलोचना ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation:
I am the King by that name, on this earth; there is no other King by the name of Haihaya. Is it that your dear companion is meant for me?
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 40]
एतन्मे संशयं सुभ्रु छेत्तुमर्हसि भामिनि ।
अहं तामानयिष्यामि तं हत्वा राक्षसाधमम् ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation:
O Bhāminī (passionate woman)! Remove my doubts; I will kill that villain Rākṣasa and bring just now your dear companion; there is no doubt in this.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 41]
स्थानं दर्शय मे तस्य यदि जानासि सुव्रते ।
राज्ञे निवेदितं किं वा तत्पित्रे चातिदुःखिता ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation:
O One of good vows! Shew me that place, if it be known to you. Has anybody informed her father that She is suffering from so many troubles?
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 42]
यस्यैषा वल्लभा पुत्री न किं जानाति तां हृताम् ।
नोद्यमः किं कृतस्तेन ततो मोचनहेतवे ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation:
Has her father come to know that her daughter has been stolen and carried away? And has he made any effort to rescue her from the hands of that villain Rākṣasa?
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 43]
बन्दीकृतां सुतां ज्ञात्वा कथं तिष्ठति सुस्थिरः ।
असमर्थो नृपः किं वा कारणं ब्रूहि सत्वरम् ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation:
Is it that the King is calm and quiet, knowing that his daughter has been kept in prison? Or is it that he is unable to free her from bondage? Say quickly all these things before me.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 44]
त्वया मेऽपहृतं चेतो गुणानुक्त्वा ह्यमानुषान् ।
सख्याः पङ्कजपत्राक्षि कृतः कामवशो भृशम् ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation:
O Lotus-eyed One! You have captivated my mind by describing the extraordinary qualities of your dear companion and have made me passionate too.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 45]
कदा पश्यामि तां कान्तां मोचयित्वातिसङ्कटात् ।
इति मे हृदयं चाद्य करोत्यतिमनोरथम् ॥ ४५ ॥
English Translation:
Alas! When will it be that I will free my beautiful beloved from the greatest perilous situation and shall see her face and her eyes, beaming with joy.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 46]
ब्रूहि मे गमनोपायं पुरे तस्यातिदुर्गमे ।
कथं त्वमागता तस्मात्सङ्कटादत्र तद्वद ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation:
O Sweet-speaking One! Say, by what means I can go to that impassable city. How have you been able to come from there?”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 47]
यशोवत्युवाच ।
बालभावान्मया मन्त्रो भगवत्या विशाम्पते ।
प्राप्तोऽस्ति ब्राह्मणात्सिद्धात्सबीजध्यानपूर्वकः ॥ ४७ ॥
English Translation:
Yaśovatī said :-- O King! In my early age I got the Mantram of the Devī Bhagavatī with its seed Mantram (mystic syllable involving in it the power connoted by the Devī) and how to meditate it.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 48]
तत्रावस्थितया राजन् मया चित्ते विचारितम् ।
आराधयामि सततं चण्डिकां चण्डविक्रमाम् ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation:
While I was in the Dānava’s place I thought out that at that juncture I would worship the powerful Candikā who gives instantaneously one’s own desires.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 49]
सा देवी सेविता कामं बन्धमोक्षं करिष्यति ।
भक्तानुकम्पिनी शक्तिः समर्था सर्वसाधने ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation:
If I worship that Śakti, That fructifies all desires, That is all mercy to Her Bhaktas, certainly She will free my dear companion from this her bondage.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 50]
या विश्वं सृजते शक्त्या पालयत्येव सा पुनः ।
कल्पान्ते संहरत्येव निराकारा निराश्रया ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation:
Though She is really without form, yet She, without anybody’s help, by Her own force, She is creating, preserving, and at the end of the Kalpa, destroying this Universe.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 51-52]
इति सञ्चिन्त्य मनसा देवीं विश्वेश्वरीं शिवाम् ।
ध्यात्वा रक्ताम्बरां सौम्यां सुरक्तनयनां हृदि ॥ ५१ ॥

संस्मृत्य मनसा रूपं मन्त्रजाप्यपराभवम् ।
उपासिता मया देवी मासमेकं समाधिना ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation:
Oh! She is very wonderful indeed! Thus thinking I began to meditate that auspicious red-robed and red-eyed Devī, the Lady of the Universe, and recollected mentally Her form and repeated silently Her Vīja Mantram.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 53-54]
स्वप्ने मम समायाता भक्तिभावेन तोषिता ।
मामाहामृतया वाचा किं सुप्तासीति चण्डिका ॥ ५३ ॥

उत्तिष्ठ याहि तरसा गङ्गातीरं मनोहरम् ।
आगमिष्यति तत्रासौ हैहयो नृपपुङ्गवः ॥ ५४ ॥
English Translation:
When I meditated thus for merely one month, Candikā Devī became, through my devotion, manifest to me in my dreams and began to speak in sweet nectar-like words :-- “You are now asleep; go quickly to the beautiful banks of the Ganges.

[Ch 22 Verse 55-56]
एकवीरो महाबाहुः सर्वशत्रुविमर्दनः ।
दत्तात्रेयेण मन्मन्त्रो महाविद्याभिधः परः ॥ ५५ ॥

दत्तोऽस्मै सोऽपि सततं मामुपास्तेऽतिभक्तितः ।
मय्यासक्तमतिर्नित्यं मम पूजापरायणः ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation:
The enemy destroyer, the powerful Ekavīra, the greatest of all the kings, will come there. Dattātreya, the Great Lord of the Munis, has given him my Mantra named Mahāvidyā; the King also constantly worships me devotedly with that.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 57-58]
मामेव सर्वभूतेषु ध्यायन्नास्ते च मत्परः ।
स ते दुःखविनाशं वै करिष्यति महामतिः ॥ ५७ ॥

मासुतो विहरंस्तत्र तव त्राता भविष्यति ।
हत्वा तं राक्षसं घोरं मोचयिष्यति मानिनीम् ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation:
His mind is constantly attached to Me and he constantly worships Me. What more to say than this fact that the king, extremely devoted to Me, meditates on Me as the internal controller of all beings. That intelligent son of Lakṣmī will come for sport to the banks of the Ganges and will remove all your sorrows.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 59-60]
एकावलीमेकवीरः सर्वशास्त्रविशारदः ।
पश्चात्सैव पतिः कार्यस्त्वया राजसुतः शुभः ॥ ५९ ॥

इत्युक्त्वान्तर्दधे देवी प्रबुद्धाहं तदैव हि ।
कथितं स्वप्नवृत्तान्तं देव्याश्चाराधनं तथा ॥ ६० ॥
English Translation:
That king Ekavīra, versed in all the Śāstras will kill the Rākṣasas in a dreadful battle and will rescue Ekāvalī. So now you pay heed to my word.” Lastly, She told me that my companion should marry that beautiful King, endowed with all auspicious qualifications. Thus saying, She disappeared and I instantly woke up.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 61]
प्रसन्नवदना जाता श्रुत्वा सा कमलेक्षणा ।
विशेषेण च सन्तुष्टा मामुवाच शुचिस्मिता ॥ ६१ ॥
English Translation:
Then I informed my lotus-eyed dear Sakhī all the details of my dream as well my worshipping the Devī; hearing this, her lotus-face beamed with joy and gladness.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 62]
गच्छ तत्र त्वरायुक्ता कुरु कार्यं मम प्रिये ।
सत्यवाक्या भगवती सावां मोक्षं विधास्यति ॥ ६२ ॥
English Translation:
That sweet-smiling Ekāvalī very gladly told me, “O dear Companion! Go at once for our success. That truth-speaking Bhagavatī Ambikā Devī will release us from our bondage.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 63]
इत्याज्ञप्ता तया चाहं सख्या वै प्रेमयुक्तया ।
मत्वोपसरणं युक्तं तस्मात्स्थानात्तदा नृप ॥ ६३ ॥
English Translation:
O King! When my dear companion ordered me thus, I thought it proper, as also dictated to me in my dream, to go out and soon I did it.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 64]
चालिताहं ततः शीघ्रं महादेवीप्रसादतः ।
मार्गज्ञानं शीघ्रगतिर्मया प्राप्ता नृपात्मज ॥ ६४ ॥
English Translation:
O King! Due to the grace of the Great Devī, I came to know the way and I also got the quick motion.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 22 Verse 65-66]
इत्येतत्कथितं सर्वं कारणं मम दुःखजम् ।
कस्त्वं कस्य सुतश्चेति वद वीर यथा तथा ॥ ६५ ॥

इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां षष्ठस्कन्धे
हैहयैकवीराय यशोवत्यैकावलीमोचनाय देवीस्वप्नवर्णनं नाम द्वाविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ६.२२॥
English Translation:
Thus I have described to you the cause of my sorrow. O Hero! Who are you, whose son are you? Speak truly to me. Here ends the Twenty-second Chapter in the Sixth Book on the narration to Haihaya the stealing away of Ekāvalī in Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

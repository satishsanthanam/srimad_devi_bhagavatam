BOOK 6 • CHAPTER 17
On the continuance of the family of Bhṛgu

Ch 17 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
६.१७ सप्तदशोऽध्यायः । हैहयानामुत्पत्तिप्रसङ्गे रमाविष्णुसंवादवर्णनम् । जनमेजय उवाच । कथं ताश्च स्त्रियः सर्वा भृगूणां दुःखसागरात् । मुक्ता वंशः पुनस्तेषां ब्राह्मणानां स्थिरोऽभवत् ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Janamejaya said :-- “Munis! How did the Bhārgava wives cross this endless sea of troubles and how was the family of Bhrigu re-established on the surface of this earth?
Ch 17 Verse 2 Verse
Sanskrit Text
हैहयैः किं कृतं कार्यं हत्वा तान्ब्राह्मणानपि । क्षत्रियैर्लोभसंयुक्तैः पापाचारैर्वदस्व तत् ॥ २ ॥
English Translation
And what did the greedy Haihayas, the vilest of the Kṣattriyas, do after they killed the Bhārgavas? Describe all these in detail and satisfy my curiosity.
Ch 17 Verse 3 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न तृप्तिरस्ति मे ब्रह्मन् पिबतस्ते कथामृतम् । पावनं सुखदं नॄणां परलोके फलप्रदम् ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation
O Thou, Ocean of austerities! I am not satisfied with the drink of your nectar like words, very holy and leading to happiness in this world and to good merits in the next.”
Ch 17 Verse 4 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । शृणु राजन्प्रवक्ष्यामि कथां पापप्रणाशिनीम् । यथा स्त्रियस्तु ता मुक्ता दुःखात्तस्माद्दुरत्ययात् ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! I will now narrate to you the sin destroying virtuous story how the Bhārgava wives crossed their great hardships and the ocean of troubles, very difficult to cross.
Ch 17 Verse 5 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भृगुपत्न्यो यदा राजन् हिमवन्तं गिरिं गताः । भयत्रस्ता विभग्नाशा हैहयैः पीडिता भृशम् ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation
The Bhārgava wives, when they were very much harassed by the Haihayas, went to the Himālayās, overwhelmed with terror and disappointment.
Ch 17 Verse 6 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गौरीं तत्र तु संस्थाप्य मृण्मयीं सरितस्तटे । उपोषणपराश्चकुर्निश्चयं मरणं प्रति ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation
There on that mountain they erected an earthen image of Śrī Gaurī Devī by the banks of the Ganges and worshipped Her and, firmly resolved to die, began to fast.
Ch 17 Verse 7 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स्वप्ने गत्वा तदा देवी प्राह ताः प्रमदोत्तमाः । युष्मासु मध्ये कस्याश्चिद्भविता चोरुजः पुमान् ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
The Devī Jagadambikā appeared to those religious women in their dreams and said :-- “A son will be born of My essence to one of you from one of her thighs; that son will redress all your wants.”
Ch 17 Verse 8-10 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मदंशशक्तिसम्भिन्नः स वः कार्यं विधास्यति । इत्यादिश्य पराम्बा सा पश्चादन्तर्हिताभवत् ॥ ८ ॥ जागृतास्तु ततः सर्वा मुदमापुर्वराङ्गनाः । काचित्तासां भयोद्विग्ना कामिनी चतुरा भृशम् ॥ ९ ॥ दधार चोरुणैकेन गर्भं सा कुलवृद्धये । पलायनपरा दृष्टा क्षत्रियैर्ब्राह्मणी यदा ॥ १० ॥
English Translation
Thus speaking, the Devī Bhagavatī disappeared. Those women when they woke up were very glad; one of them that appeared very clever, becoming very much anxious out of the fear of the Kṣattriyas;
Ch 17 Verse 11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विह्वला तेजसा युक्ता तदा ते दुद्रुवुर्भृशम् । गृह्यतां वध्यतां नारी सगर्भा याति सत्वरा ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
The Kṣattriyas, seeing that Brāhmaṇī, came quickly upon her and said :-- “See! This pregnant Bhārgava wife is flying away hastily; seize her and take away her life.”
Ch 17 Verse 12 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इति ब्रुवन्तः सम्प्राप्ताः कामिनीं खड्गपाणयः । सा भयार्ता तु तान्दृष्ट्वा रुरोद समुपागतान् ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation
Thus saying, all of them raised their axes, and pursued her. Then that woman seeing them coming, wept out of fear.
Ch 17 Verse 13-15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गर्भस्य रक्षणार्थं सा चुक्रोशातिभयातुरा । रुदतीं मातरं श्रुत्वा दीनां प्राणविवर्जिताम् ॥ १३ ॥ निराधारां क्रन्दमानां क्षत्रियैर्भृशतापिताम् । गृहीतामिव सिंहेन सगर्भां हरिणीं यथा ॥ १४ ॥ साश्रुनेत्रां वेपमानां सङ्क्रुध्य बालकस्तदा । भित्त्वोरुं निर्जगामाशु गर्भः सूर्य इवापरः ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
She cried, out of terror, for the preservation of the child in her womb; and the child seeing her mother helpless and distressed, trembling with fear and with tears in her eyes having no one to protect her and awfully oppressed by the Kṣattriyas as if a pregnant deer has been attacked by a lion and is crying about, angrily burst out of the thigh of his mother, and quickly came out like a second Sun.
Ch 17 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
मुष्णन्दृष्टीः क्षत्रियाणां तेजसा बालकः शुभः । दर्शनाद्बालकस्याशु सर्वे जाता विलोचनाः ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
That good looking boy took away the power of sight of those Kṣattriyas by his brilliant lustrous light; no sooner the Haihayas saw that boy than they got blind.
Ch 17 Verse 17-18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
बभ्रमुर्गिरिदुर्गेषु जन्मान्धा इव क्षत्रियाः । चिन्तितं मनसा सर्वैः किमेतदिति साम्प्रतम् ॥ १७ ॥ सर्वे चक्षुर्विहीना यज्जाता स्म बालदर्शनात् । ब्राह्मण्यास्तु प्रभावोऽयं सतीव्रतबलं महत् ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
Like those that are born blind; they then began to roam in the caves of mountains and thought within themselves, what an evil turn of Fate had overtaken them! They thought thus :-- “Oh! The mere sight of that boy has turned us blind; what a great wonder is this! Certainly this is due to the influence of the Brāhminī wife; this is, no doubt, the great effect of her virtue of chastity.
Ch 17 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
क्षणाद्वामोघसङ्कल्पाः किं करिष्यन्तिदुःखिताः । इति सञ्चिन्त्य मनसा नेत्रहीना निराश्रयाः ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
We have greatly oppressed the Bhārgava women. They have become very sorry and distressed; now we cannot tell what more evils do these women, of true resolve, inflict on us!”
Ch 17 Verse 20-21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
ब्राह्मणीं शरणं जग्मुर्हैहया गतचेतसः । प्रणेमुस्तां भयत्रस्तां कृताञ्जलिपुटाश्च ते ॥ २० ॥ ऊचुश्चैनां भयोद्विग्नां दृष्ट्यर्थं क्षत्रियर्षभाः । प्रसीद सुभगे मातः सेवकास्ते वयं किल ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
Thus pondering, those Kṣattriyas deprived of their eyes, helpless, and their minds bewildered, took refuge of those Brāhmin ladies. The ladies, seeing them again come, were the more terrified; but those Kṣattriyas bowed down before them with folded hands for the restoration of their sights and said :-- “O Mother! We are your servants. Be gracious unto us. O Auspicious Ones! We are vicious Kṣattriyas; O Mothers! What an amount of offence we have committed to you. O Beautiful Ones! We have become blind, no sooner we have seen you.
Ch 17 Verse 22-23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कृतापराधा रम्भोरु क्षत्रियाः पापबुद्धयः । दर्शनात्तव तन्वङ्गि जाताः सर्वे विलोचनाः ॥ २२ ॥ मुखं ते नैव पश्यामो जन्मान्धा इव भामिनि । अद्भुतं ते तपो वीर्यं किं कुर्मः पापकारिणः ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
O Angry Ones! No more we can see your lotus-like faces, as if we are born blind; O Mother! The spirit of your asceticism is so very wonderful! We are sinners; therefore by no means we can get our sight; therefore we have taken refuge unto you all; better give us back our eyesight and preserve our honour.
Ch 17 Verse 24-26 Verse
Sanskrit Text
शरणं ते प्रपन्नाः स्मो देहि चक्षूंषि मानदे । अन्धत्वं मरणादुग्रं कृपां कर्तुं त्वमर्हसि ॥ २४ ॥ पुनर्दृष्टिप्रदानेन सेवकान्क्षत्रियान्कुरु । उपरम्य च गच्छेम सहिताः पापकर्मणः ॥ २५ ॥ अतः परं न कर्तव्यमीदृशं कर्म कर्हिचित् । भार्गवाणां तु सर्वेषां सेवकाः स्मो वयं किल ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation
O Mother! Blindness is more dreadful than death; therefore do you show your mercy on us. Be pleased unto us and restore our eyesights and make us your slaves; no sooner we get back our sights, we will cease from these vicious acts and go to our homes. In future, we will never commit such heinous acts; from today we all become servants of the Bhārgavas and we will serve them.
Ch 17 Verse 27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
अज्ञानाद्यत्कृतं पापं क्षन्तव्यं तत्त्वयाधुना । वैरं नातः परं क्वापि भृगुभिः क्षत्रियैः सह ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
Forgive all our sins that we committed unconsciously; we promise that, in future, there will no more be any enmity between the Bhārgavas and Kṣattriyas.
Ch 17 Verse 28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कर्तव्यं शपथैः सम्यग्वर्तितव्यं तु हैहयैः । सपुत्रा भव सुश्रोणि प्रणताः स्मो वयं च ते ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
O good-looking Ones! You pass your days happily with your sons; we ever bow down before you. O Auspicious Ones! Be graciously pleased unto us; no more we will cherish any inimical feelings towards you.”
Ch 17 Verse 29-30 Verse
Sanskrit Text
प्रसादं कुरु कल्याणि न द्विष्यामः कदाचन । व्यास उवाच । इति तेषां वचः श्रुत्वा ब्राह्मणी विस्मयान्विता ॥ २९ ॥ तानाह प्रणतान्दुःस्थानाश्वास्य गतलोचनान् । गृहीता न मया दृष्टिर्युष्माकं क्षत्रियाः किल ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! The Bhārgava lady heard their words and was thunderstruck and seeing those Kṣattriyas bowing down before her, blind and distressed, consoled them and said, “O Kṣattriyas! I have not taken away your sights nor am I displeased in any way with you.
Ch 17 Verse 31-33 Verse
Sanskrit Text
नाहं रुषान्विता सत्यं कारणं शृणुताद्य यत् । अयं च भार्गवो नूनमूरुजः कुपितोऽद्य वः ॥ ३१ ॥ चक्षूंषि तेन युष्माकं स्तम्भितानि रुषावता । स्वबन्धून्निहतांज्ञात्वा गर्भस्थानपि क्षत्रियैः ॥ ३२ ॥ अनागसो धर्मपरांस्तापसान्धनकाम्यया । गर्भानपि यदा यूयं भृगूनघ्नंस्तु पुत्रकाः ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation
Now hear what is the real cause. This child of Bhārgava, born of my thigh, is exceedingly angry towards you and has therefore made your eyesight still and to no purpose. For the greed of wealth, you have slain the close relatives of this boy, those that were quite innocent and virtuous ascetics and you have slain their children that were in their motherś wombs; this boy has come to know all those things.
Ch 17 Verse 34 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तदायमूरुणा गर्भो मया वर्षशतं धृतः । षडङ्गश्चाखिलो वेदो गृहीतोऽनेन चाञ्जसा ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation
O children! When you were slaying the children of the Bhārgavas in their motherś wombs, I then bore within my thighs this child for one hundred years.
Ch 17 Verse 35 Verse
Sanskrit Text
गर्भस्थेनापि बालेन भृगुवंशविवृद्धये । सोऽपि पितृवधान्नूनं क्रोथेद्धो हन्तुमिच्छति ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation
This son of mine though as yet in the womb, has mastered all the Vedas within so very short a time for the propagation of the Bhārgava clan.
Ch 17 Verse 36-37 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भगवत्याः प्रसादेन जातोऽयं मम बालकः । तेजसा यस्य दिव्येन चक्षूंषि मुषितानि वः ॥ ३६ ॥ तस्मादौर्वं सुतं मेऽद्य याचध्वं विनयान्विताः । प्रणिपातेन तुष्टोऽसौ दृष्टिं वः प्रतिमोक्ष्यति ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation
Now this Bhārgava son is infuriated with anger for your slaying his father and is now ready to kill you all. My son! Whose divine effulgence has destroyed your eyesights, is born of grace of the Highest Goddess, the Bhagavatī Bhuvaneśvarī; therefore do not consider this boy as an ordinary being. Now bow down with humility before this my son Aurvya (born from the thighs); this son may be pleased by your bowing down and may restore you your eyesight.
Ch 17 Verse 38 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्या हैहयास्तुष्टुवुश्च तम् । प्रणेमुर्विनयोपेता ऊरुजं मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing thus the words of the Brāhmin lady, the Haihayas began to praise the boy with hymns. With great humility, they bowed down to the best of the Munis, born of the thighs.
Ch 17 Verse 39-40 Verse
Sanskrit Text
स सन्तुष्टो बभूवाथ तानुवाच विचक्षुषः । गच्छध्वं स्वगृहान्भूपा ममाख्यानकृतं वचः ॥ ३९ ॥ अवश्यम्भाविभावास्ते भवन्ति देवनिर्मिताः । नात्र शोकस्तु कर्तव्यः पुरुषेण विजानता ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation
The Ṛṣi Aurvya, then, became pleased and spoke thus to the Haihayas who were deprived of their eyesights :-- “Better go back to your own homes. O Kings! And read these following words derived from my this story.
Ch 17 Verse 41 Verse
Sanskrit Text
पूर्ववदृषयः सर्वे प्राप्नुवन्तु यथासुखम् । व्रजन्तु विगतक्रोधा भवनानि यथासुखम् ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation
Whatever is inevitable and created by the hands of gods must come to pass. Knowing this, no one ought to be sorrowful on any such matters.
Ch 17 Verse 42-43 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इति तेन समादिष्टा हैहयाः प्राप्तलोचनाः । और्वमामन्त्र्य जग्मुस्ते सदनानि यथारुचि ॥ ४२ ॥ ब्राह्मणी तं सुतं दिव्यं गृहीत्वा स्वाश्रमं गता । पालयामास भूपाल तेजस्विनमतन्द्रिता ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation
Let you all regain your eyesights as before and forego your anger and go to your own homes respectively at your own will. Let the Ṛṣis, too, get peace and happiness as before.” When the Maharṣi Aurvya ordered thus, the Haihayas got back their eyesights and went at their leisure to their own homes; on the other hand the Brāhmin lady went to her own hermitage, with her Divine-spirited child and began to nourish him.
Ch 17 Verse 44 Verse
Sanskrit Text
एवं ते कथितं राजन् भृगूणां तु विनाशनम् । लोभाविष्टैः क्षत्रियैश्च यत्कृतं पातकं किल ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation
O King! Thus I have described to you the story of the killing of the Bhārgavas and how the Kṣattriyas, actuated by greed, did so very vicious acts.
Ch 17 Verse 45 Verse
Sanskrit Text
जनमेजय उवाच । श्रुतं मया महत्कर्म क्षत्रियाणाञ्च दारुणम् । कारणं लोभ एवात्र दुःखदश्चोभयोस्तु सः ॥ ४५ ॥
English Translation
Janamejaya said :-- “O Ascetic! Hearing this exceedingly heart-rending act of the Kṣattriyas, I come to know, that greed is the sole cause of it and both the parties had suffered so much, simply out of this insatiable greed.
Ch 17 Verse 46 Verse
Sanskrit Text
किञ्चित्प्रष्टुमिहेच्छामि संशयं वासवीसुत । हैहयास्ते कथं नाम्ना ख्याता भुवि नृपात्मजाः ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation
O King of Munis! I want to ask you one more question in regard to this point. How the sons of the Kings came to be known Haihayas in this world?
Ch 17 Verse 47 Verse
Sanskrit Text

English Translation
Amongst the Kṣattriyas, some are called Yādavas for they ware descended from the family of Yadu; some were known as Bhārata, for they were descended from Bhārata.
Ch 17 Verse 48 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तदहं श्रोतुमिच्छामि कारणं करुणानिधे । हैहयास्ते कथं जाताः क्षत्रियाः केन कर्मणा ॥ ४८ ॥
English Translation
But was some king named Haihaya born before in their family or were they known as such on account of other actions? I desire to hear of it. Kindly describe this to me and oblige.”
Ch 17 Verse 49 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । हैहयानां समुत्पत्तिं शृणु भूप सविस्तराम् । पुरातनीं सुपुण्यां च कथां पापप्रणाशिनीम् ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation
Vyāsa said :-- O King! I am describing in detail to you of the origin of the Haihayas. Hear. The sins are destroyed and the merits accrue on hearing this story.
Ch 17 Verse 50-52 Verse
Sanskrit Text
कस्मिंश्चित्समये भूप सूर्यपुत्रः सुशोभनः । रेवन्तेति च विख्यातो रूपवानमितप्रभः ॥ ५० ॥ उच्चैःश्रवसमारुह्य हयरत्नं मनोहरम् । जगाम विष्णुसदनं वैकुण्ठं भास्करात्मजः ॥ ५१ ॥ भगवद्दर्शनाकाङ्क्षी हयारूढो यदागतः । हयस्थस्तु तदा दृष्टो लक्ष्म्यासौ रविनन्दनः ॥ ५२ ॥
English Translation
O King! Once on a time Revanta, the son of the Sun, very beautiful and of boundless lustre, was going to Viṣṇu in Vaikuṇṭha, mounted on the beautiful Uccaiśrava, the jewel of the horses. When he was going on horseback with a desire to see the God Viṣṇu, the Goddess Lakṣmī saw that child of the Sun.
Ch 17 Verse 53 Verse
Sanskrit Text
रमा वीक्ष्य हयं दिव्यं भ्रातरं सागरोद्भवम् । रूपेण विस्मिता तस्य तस्थौ स्तम्भितलोचना ॥ ५३ ॥
English Translation
The Goddess Lakṣmī, born out of the churning of the ocean, on looking at the beautiful appearance of her brother Horse, also born out of the churning of the ocean, became very much astonished and steadily gazed on him.
Ch 17 Verse 54-55 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भगवानपि तं दृष्ट्वा हयारूढं मनोहरम् । आगच्छन्तं रमां विष्णुः पप्रच्छ प्रणयात्प्रभुः ॥ ५४ ॥ कोऽयमायाति चार्वङ्गि हयारूढ इवापरः । स्मरतेजस्तनुः कान्ते मोहयन्भुवनत्रयम् ॥ ५५ ॥
English Translation
The Bhagavān Viṣṇu, capable to show both favour and disfavour, saw the beautiful Revanta, of good figure, coming on horseback; and lovingly asked Lakṣmī :-- “O Beautiful One! Who is coming here on horseback, as it were, enchanting to the three worlds!”
Ch 17 Verse 56 Verse
Sanskrit Text
प्रेक्षमाणा तदा लक्ष्मीस्तच्चित्ता दैवयोगतः । नोवाच वचनं किञ्चित्पृष्टापि च पुनः पुनः ॥ ५६ ॥
English Translation
At that time, the Goddess Lakṣmī was accidentally looking intently on the horse; so she did not reply, though repeatedly asked by the Bhagavān.
Ch 17 Verse 57 Verse
Sanskrit Text
व्यास उवाच । अश्वासक्तमतिं वीक्ष्य कामिनीमतिमोहिताम् । पश्यन्तीं परमप्रेम्णा चञ्चलाक्षीं च चञ्चलाम् ॥ ५७ ॥
English Translation
The Lakṣmī Devī, always restless, was very much intent on the horse and was enchanted and She was looking steadily with great affection on the horse.
Ch 17 Verse 58 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तामाह भगवान्कुद्धः किं पश्यसि सुलोचने । मोहिता च हरिं दृष्ट्वा पृष्टा नैवाभिभाषसे ॥ ५८ ॥
English Translation
Seeing this, the Bhagavān became angry and said :-- “O Beautiful-eyed One! What you are looking at so intently?
Ch 17 Verse 59-60 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सर्वत्र रमसे यस्माद्रमा तस्माद्भविष्यसि । चञ्चलत्वाच्चलेत्येवं सर्वथैव न संशयः ॥ ५९ ॥ प्राकृता च यथा नारी नूनं भवति चञ्चला । तथा त्वमपि कल्याणि स्थिरा नैव कदाचन ॥ ६० ॥
English Translation
Are you so much enchanted with the sight of the horse that you are not speaking to me a single word, though I am repeatedly asking you so often! You lovingly dwell on all the objects; hence your name is Ramā; your mind is also very restless, therefore you would be known as Cañcalā Devī (the restless Devī). O Auspicious One! You are restless like ordinary women; you can never stay steadily for a certain time at any one place.
Ch 17 Verse 61 Verse
Sanskrit Text
त्वं हयं मत्समीपस्था समीक्ष्य यदि मोहिता । वडवा भव वामोरु मर्त्यलोकेऽतिदारुणे ॥ ६१ ॥
English Translation
While sitting before Me, you are enchanted with the sight of a horse; then you be born as a mare in that world of men, full of dreadful troubles, on the surface of the earth.
Ch 17 Verse 62 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इति शप्ता रमा देवी हरिणा दैवयोगतः । रुरोद वेपमाना सा भयभीतातिदुःखिता ॥ ६२ ॥
English Translation
The Goddess Lakṣmī became very much affrightened at the sudden curse given by Hari, a matter as it were ordained by the Devas, and began to cry aloud, shuddering with pain and sorrows.
Ch 17 Verse 63 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तमुवाच रमानाथ शङ्किता चारुहासिनी । प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं स्वपतिं विनयान्विता ॥ ६३ ॥
English Translation
Lakṣmī Devī, then of sweet smiles, frightened, bowed down with great humility to her own lord Nārāyaṇa and said thus :-- O Deva of the Devas! O Govinda! You are the Lord of this world and the Ocean of mercy.
Ch 17 Verse 64-65 Verse
Sanskrit Text
देवदेव जगन्नाथ करुणाकर केशव । स्वल्पेऽपराधे गोविन्द कस्माच्छापं ददासि मे ॥ ६४ ॥ न कदाचिन्मया दृष्टः क्रोधस्ते हीदृशः प्रभो । क्व गतस्ते मयि स्नेहः सहजो न तु नश्वरः ॥ ६५ ॥
English Translation
O Keśava! Why have you inflicted on me so dreadful a curse for such a minor fault of mine! O Lord! I never saw you before so very angry; Alas! Where has now gone that affection, so natural and undying, that you showed towards me!
Ch 17 Verse 66 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वज्रपातस्तु शत्रौ वै कर्तव्यो न सुहृज्जने । सदाहं वरयोग्या ते शापयोग्या कथं कृता ॥ ६६ ॥
English Translation
O Lord! It is not proper to hurl a thunderbolt on one’s own relations; but it is advisable to cast it on the enemies.
Ch 17 Verse 67-68 Verse
Sanskrit Text
प्राणांस्त्यक्ष्यामि गोविन्द पश्यतोऽद्य तवाग्रतः । कथं जीवे त्वया हीना विरहानलतापिता ॥ ६७ ॥ प्रसादं कुरु देवेश शापादस्मात्सुदारुणात् । कदा मुक्ता समीपं ते प्राप्नोमि सुखदं विभौ ॥ ६८ ॥
English Translation
I am always fit for receiving boons from you. Why have you made me now an object, fit for curse. O Govinda! I will quit this life in Your presence. I will never be able to live, separated from You. O Lord! Be graciously pleased and say when shall I be free from this dreadful curse and regain Your happy companion?
Ch 17 Verse 69-70 Verse
Sanskrit Text
हरिरुवाच । यदा ते भविता पुत्रः पृथिव्यां मत्समः प्रिये । तदा मां प्राप्य तन्वङ्गि सुखिता त्वं भविष्यसि ॥ ६९ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां षष्ठस्कन्धे हैहयानामुत्पत्तिप्रसङ्गे रमाविष्णुसंवादवर्णनं नाम सप्तदशोऽध्यायः ॥ ६.१७॥
English Translation
The Bhagavān said :-- “O Devī! When you will get a son in the world like me, you will no doubt come again to be my companion.” Here ends the Seventeenth Chapter on the continuance of the family of Bhrigu in the Sixth Book in the Mahāpurāṇam, Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam of 18,000 verses by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

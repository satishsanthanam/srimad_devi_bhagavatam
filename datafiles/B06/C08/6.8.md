# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 6, CHAPTER 8

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 1-2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ६.८ अष्टमोऽध्यायः । इन्द्राण्या शक्रदर्शनम् ।
> 
> 
> व्यास उवाच ।
> नहुषस्त्वथ तां श्रुत्वा गुरोस्तु शरणं गताम् ।
> चुक्रोध स्मरबाणार्तस्तमाङ्गिरसमाशु वै ॥ १ ॥
> 
> देवानाहाङ्गिरासूनुर्हन्तव्योऽयं मया किल ।
> इतीन्द्राणीं गृहे मूढो रक्षतीति मया श्रुतम् ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing that the wife of Indra had taken refuge under Brihaspati, the King Nahuṣa became very angry towards Brihaspati and spoke to the Devas :-- “ O Devas! I hear that the stupid son of Aṅgirasa has given protection to Indra and has kept her in his house; I will therefore kill him quickly.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इति तं कुपितं दृष्ट्वा देवाः सर्षिपुरोगमाः ।
> अब्रुवन्नहुषं घोरं सामपूर्वं वचस्तदा ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Seeing the terrible Nahuṣa thus angry, the Devas and Ṛṣis consoled him and said :-- O King of kings! Do not be angry; quit this vicious motive yours.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> क्रोधं संहर राजेन्द्र त्यज पापमतिं प्रभो ।
> निन्दन्ति धर्मशास्त्रेषु परदाराभिमर्शनम् ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

See, the Ṛṣis in all the Dharma Śāstras, have declared the holding of illicit connection with other’s wives as a very heinous crime and have blamed it very much. You can consider that the daughter of Pulomā is always chaste, devoted to her husband and very good-natured.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 5-6</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शक्रपत्नी सदा साध्वी जीवमाने पतौ पुनः ।
> कथमन्ये पतिं कुर्यात्सुभगातिपतिव्रता ॥ ५ ॥
> 
> त्रिलोकीशस्त्वमधुना शास्ता धर्मस्य वै विभो ।
> त्वादृशोऽधर्ममातिष्ठेत्तदा नश्येत्प्रजा ध्रुवम् ॥ ६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

How can she, when her husband is alive, take another husband? O Lord! You are now the Lord of the three worlds and hence the Defender of Faith and Religion; and if a person like you act irreligiously, all the subjects will then go to annihilation.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सर्वथा प्रभुणा कार्यं शिष्टाचारस्य रक्षणम् ।
> वारमुख्याश्च शतशो वर्तन्तेऽत्र शचीसमाः ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

One who is a Lord should always observe the rules of good conduct. Besides there are many other celestial women in this Heaven as beautiful as Śacī; you can satisfy your thirst with them.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रतिस्तु कारणं प्रोक्तं शृङ्गारस्य महात्मभिः ।
> रसहानिर्बलात्कारे कृते सति तु जायते ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Mutual love is recognised by the wise as the true originator of amorous dealings; ravishing a woman by force destroys all amorous sentiments.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 9-10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उभयोः सदृशं प्रेम यदि पार्थिवसत्तम ।
> तदा वै सुखसम्पत्तिरुभयोरुपजायते ॥ ९ ॥
> 
> तस्माद्भावमिमं मुञ्च परदाराभिमर्शने ।
> सद्भावं कुरु देवेन्द्र पदं प्राप्तोऽस्यनुत्तमम् ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! And if the mutual love be similar and equal in all respects, then comes the true happiness; you have now got the post of Indra; therefore quit this idea of holding illicit connection with other’s wives and indulge in other good thoughts.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ऋद्धिक्षयस्तु पापेन पुण्येनातिविवर्धनम् ।
> तस्मात्पापं परित्यज्य सन्मतिं कुरु पार्थिव ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Demerits destroy prosperity and merits increase it. Therefore, O King! Leave all these bad thoughts and make your heart take a good turn and be happy.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नहुष उवाच ।
> गौतमस्य यदा भुक्ता दाराः शक्रेण देवताः ।
> वाचस्पतेस्तु सोमेन क्व यूयं संस्थितास्तदा ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nahuṣa said :-- “O Devas! Where were you all when Indra stole away the wife of Gautama and when the Moon stole away the wife of Bhraspati?

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 13-14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> परोपदेशे कुशला प्रभवन्ति नराः किल ।
> कर्ता चैवोपदेष्टा च दुर्लभः पुरुषो भवेत् ॥ १३ ॥
> 
> मामागच्छतु सा देवी हितं स्यादद्भुतं हि वः ।
> एतस्याः परमं देवाः सुखमेव भविष्यति ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

It is easy to give advice to others but to act according to that is very rare in this world. O Devas! Let the qualified Devī come to me you will derive much benefit from it and the Devī, too, will get Her highest happiness; there is no doubt in this.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अन्यथा न हि तुष्येऽहं सत्यमेतद्ब्रवीमि वः ।
> विनयाद्वा बलाद्वापि तामाशु प्रापयन्त्विह ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I tell you truly that in no other way I will be satisfied; bring Indrānī here quickly, whether by good words or by force.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 16-17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इति तस्य वचः श्रुत्वा देवाश्च मुनयस्तथा ।
> तमूचुश्चातिसन्त्रस्ता नहुषं मदनातुरम् ॥ १६ ॥
> 
> इन्द्राणीमानयिष्यामः सामपूर्वं तवान्तिकम् ।
> इत्युक्त्वा ते तदा जग्मुर्बृहस्पतिनिकेतनम् ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Devas and Munis heard the words of the king Nahuṣa, smitten by the Cupid’s arrows, got terrified and said :-- “We will bring Indrānī to you by gentle words.” Saying thus, they went to the house of Brihaspati.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 18-19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यास उवाच ।
> ते गत्वाङ्गिरसः पुत्रं प्रोचुः प्राञ्जलयः सुराः ।
> जानीमः शरणं प्राप्तामिन्द्राणीं तव वेश्मनि ॥ १८ ॥
> 
> सा देया नहुषायाद्य वासवोऽसौ कृतो यतः ।
> वृणोत्वियं वरारोहा पतित्वे वरवर्णिनी ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Vyāsa said :-- O King! The Devas, going to the house of Brihaspati, spoke thus with folded hands :-- O Guru! We know that Indrānī has taken shelter in your house; we will have to hand her over today to the king Nahuṣa for we all united have made over the post of Indra to Nahuṣa. Let this beautiful Lady now choose and worship him.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> बृहस्पतिः सुरानाह तच्छ्रुत्वा दारुणं वचः ।
> नाहं त्यक्ष्ये तु पौलोमीं सतीं च शरणागताम् ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing these awful words of the Devas, Brihaspati said to them :-- “O Devas! This chaste woman, devoted to her husband, has now taken my shelter; therefore I can never part with her.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देवा ऊचुः ।
> उपायोऽन्यः प्रकर्तव्यो येन सोऽद्य प्रसीदति ।
> अन्यथा कोपसंयुक्तो दुराराध्यो भविष्यति ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Devas said :-- “O Guru! Kindly advise then - if you do not part with Śacī Devī - how the king Nahuṣa be pleased; if he becomes angry, it will then be very difficult to please him.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> गुरुरुवाच ।
> तत्र गत्वा शची भूपं प्रलोभ्य वचसा भृशम् ।
> करोतु समयं बाला पतिं ज्ञात्वा मृतं भजे ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Brhapsati  said :-- “O Devas! Let Śacī now go to Nahuṣa, and tempt him with enticing words and make this condition that when her husband’s death will be known to her, she will then accept Nahuṣa as her husband.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इन्द्रे जीवति मे कान्ते कथमन्यं करोम्यहम् ।
> अन्वेषणार्थं गन्तव्यं मया तस्य महात्मनः ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

How could she accept another husband when her husband was alive. Therefore let her now go in quest of her high-souled husband.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इति सा समयं कृत्वा वञ्चयित्वा च भूपतिम् ।
> भर्तुरानयने यत्नं करोतु मम वाक्यतः ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Let Śacī thus make condition with him and, thus deceiving him, let her try her best to bring back her husband.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इति सञ्चिन्त्य मे सर्वे बृहस्पतिपुरोगमाः ।
> नहुषं सहिता जग्मुरिन्द्रपत्न्या दिवौकसः ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Then, after coming to this conclusion, Brihaspati and other Devas went with Indrānī to the king Nahuṣa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तानागतान्ममीक्ष्याह तदा कृत्रिमवासवः ।
> जहर्ष च मुदायुक्तस्तां वीक्ष्य मुदितोऽब्रवीत् ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Seeing them come, especially looking at Indrānī the artificial king Nahuṣa became very glad and said to Indrānī :--

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अद्यास्मि वासवः कान्ते भज मां चारुलोचने ।
> पतित्वे सर्वलोकस्य पूज्योऽहं विहितः सुरैः ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

“O Beloved! Today I am become the real Indra. O beautiful-eyed One! Worship me as your husband; see the Devas now have made me to be worshipped by all the gods.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 28-29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इत्युक्ता सा नृपं प्राह वेपमाना त्रपायुता ।
> वरमिच्छाम्यहं राजंस्त्वत्तः प्राप्तं सुरेश्वर ॥ २८ ॥
> 
> किञ्चित्कालं प्रतीक्षस्व यावत्कुर्वे विनिर्णयम् ।
> इन्द्रोऽस्तीति न वास्तीति सन्देहो मे हृदि स्थितः ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When Nahuṣa spoke thus, the Devī Śacī became filled with great shame; she began to tremble and said to the king :-- “O Lord of the Devas! I desire to ask a boon from you. Better wait till I ascertain whether Indra is dead or alive, there is this doubt in my heart whether he lives or whether he is dead.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ततस्त्वां समुपस्थास्ये कृत्वा निश्चयमात्मनि ।
> तावत्क्षमस्व राजेन्द्र सत्यमेतद्ब्रवीमि ते ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King of kings! Let me, first of all, clear my doubts. Kindly excuse me and wait till then. I tell this truly that after I ascertain the fact, I will worship you.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> न हि विज्ञायते शक्रो नष्टः किं वा क्व वा गतः ।
> एवमुक्तः स चेन्द्राण्या नहुषः प्रीतिमानभूत् ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I do not know else.” When Śacī Devī spoke thus, Nahuṣa became very glad and saying “let it be so” dismissed her.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यसर्जयत्स तां देवीं तथेत्युक्त्वा मुदान्वितः ।
> सा विसृष्टा नृपेणाशु गत्वा प्राह सुरान्सती ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thus having received permission from the King to depart, Śacī hurriedly went to the Devas and spoke to them to try their best to bring Indra back as soon as possible.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इन्द्रस्यागमने यत्नं कुरुताद्य कृतोद्यमाः ।
> श्रुत्वा तद्वचनं देवा इन्द्राण्या रसवच्छुचि ॥ ३३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Hearing these sweet and holy words of Indrānī the Devas intently consulted with each other how they could get back Indra.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मन्त्रयामासुरेकाग्राः शक्रार्थं नृपसत्तम ।
> ते गत्वा वैष्णवं धाम तुष्टुवुः परमेश्वरम् ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

They then went to Vaikunṭha and began to praise with hymns the original Deva, the God Viṣṇu, the Lord of the Universe, kind to those that seek His refuge.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 35</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> आदिदेवं जगन्नाथं शरणागतवत्सलम् ।
> ऊचुश्चैनं समुद्विग्ना वाक्यं वाक्यविशारदाः ॥ ३५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Devas, skilled in speaking, spoke to Viṣṇu with a very troubled heart :-- “O Lord! Indra, the Lord of the Devas, is very much troubled with his sin Brahmahattyā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> देवदेव सुरपतिर्ब्रह्महत्याप्रपीडितः ।
> अदृश्यः सर्वभूतानां क्वापि तिष्ठति वासवः ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Where is he staying now, invisible to all the beings? O Lord! He is now overcome with the sin Brahmahattyā by killing Vṛtra, the best of the Brāhmins.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 37</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> त्वद्धिया निहते विप्रे ब्रह्महत्या कुतः प्रभो ।
> त्वं गतिस्तस्य भगवन्नस्माकं च तथैव हि ॥ ३७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

We ask your skilful and intelligent advice. O Lord! You are the sole refuge of him as well as of us. We are now involved in a great difficulty. Kindly show us the way how we, as well as Indra, can get out of this difficult crisis.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> त्राहि नः परमापन्नान्मोक्षं तस्य विनिर्दिश ।
> देवानां वचनं श्रुत्वा कातरं विष्णुरब्रवीत् ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Hearing the pitiful words of the Devas, Viṣṇu said :-- Let Indra perform the Aśvamedha sacrifice (Horse sacrifice) for the purification of his sins.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 39</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यजतामश्वमेधेन शक्रपापनिवृत्तये ।
> पुण्येन हयमेधेन पावितः पाकशासनः ॥ ३९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

By this Yajña, that can destroy all sins, Indra will be purified and he will regain his Indraship; there is no doubt in this.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पुनरेष्यति देवानामिन्द्रत्वमकुतोभयः ।
> हयमेधेन सन्तुष्टा देवी श्रीजगदम्बिका ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The more so because the Devī, the Universal Mother, will be pleased with his Horse sacrifice and will destroy all his sins, Brahmahattyā and others.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 41</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ब्रह्महत्यादिपापानि नाशयिष्यत्यसंशयम् ।
> यस्याः स्मरणमात्रेण पापजालं विनश्यति ॥ ४१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Lo! Merely remembering Her destroys heaps of sins; and, if by this Horse sacrifice, She be pleased, what wonder is there that sins of a more grave nature would be destroyed!

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 8 Verse 42-44</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> किं पुनर्वाजिमेधेन तत्प्रीत्यर्थं कृतेन च ।
> इन्द्राणी कुरुतान्नित्यं भगवत्याः प्रपूजनम् ॥ ४२ ॥
> 
> आराधनं शिवायास्तु सुखकारि भविष्यति ।
> नहुषोऽपि जगन्मातुर्मायया मोहितः किल ॥ ४३ ॥
> 
> विनाशं स्वकृतेनाशु गमिष्यत्येनसा सुराः ।
> पावितश्चाश्वमेधेन तुराषाडपि वैभवम् ॥ ४४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

And let Indrānī worship Bhagavatī daily; happiness will undoubtedly be gained by worshipping that most Auspicious One! By this the King Nahuṣa will be particularly deluded by the World Mother and will then be quickly destroyed by the sin committed by himself. And Indra, purified by Aśvamedha, will soon regain his position and all his wealth.

</details>

**English Translation:**

O king! Thus hearing the sweet beneficial words of Viṣṇu of indomitable prowess, the Devas went to the spot, where resided Indra. Brihaspati and the other Devas consoled the distressed Indra and made him celebrate duly in right order, the Horse sacrifice the greatest of all sacrifices. Indra then distributed his sin Brahmahattyā amongst the trees, rivers, mountains, women, and the earth.

</details>

<details>

Thus casting aside his sin on all the above things, Indra became again free from his sin, and, getting rid of his fever and uneasiness, abided by the time and remained there invisible in the tubular stem of the lotus. Doing that wonderful act, the Devas started from there and reached their own abodes.

</details>

The daughter of Pulomā, suffering from her bereavements from Indra, spoke then to Brihaspati with great sorrow :-- “O Lord! Why is my husband still invisible to me, when he has performed the Aśvamedha sacrifice? Kindly show me the way how I can get a sight of him.”

</details>

O King! When Brihaspati spoke thus, Śacī Devī got initiated by him in the Devī Mantram, capable to secure success in any undertaking. Thus getting the Mantram from her Guru, She began to worship the Devī Bhuvane’svarī duly with flowers, sacrificial victims and other necessary articles for worship.

</details>

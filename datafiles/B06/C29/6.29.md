# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 6, CHAPTER 29

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 1-3</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ६.२९ एकोनत्रिंशोऽध्यायः । नारदस्य पुनः स्वरूपप्राप्तिवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> नारद उवाच ।
> इत्युक्तोऽहं तदा तेन राज्ञा तालध्वजेन च ।
> विमृश्य मनसात्यर्थं तमुवाच विशाम्पते ॥ १ ॥
> 
> राजन्नाहं विजानामि पुत्री कस्येति निश्चयम् ।
> पितरौ क्व च मे केन स्थापिता च सरोवरे ॥ २ ॥
> 
> किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि कथं मे सुकृतं भवेत् ।
> निराधारास्मि राजेन्द्र चिन्तयामि चिकीर्षितम् ॥ ३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārada said :-- O Dvaipāyana! When the King Tāladhvaja asked me thus, I thought over earnestly and said thus :-- “I do not know whose daughter I am; nor do I know quite certainly where are my father and mother; one man placed me here on this tank and has gone away, whither I do not know.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 4-5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दैवमेव परं राजन्नास्त्यत्र पौरुषं मम ।
> धर्मज्ञोऽसि महीपाल यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ ४ ॥
> 
> तवाधीनास्म्यहं भूप न मे कोऽप्यस्ति पालकः ।
> न पिता न च माता च न स्थानं न च बान्धवाः ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! I am now an helpless orphan; what shall I do now? Where to go? What to do by which I can have my welfare? I am all the while thinking on these. O King! The Destiny is powerful; I have not the least control over it; you know Dharma and you are a King. Do now as you like.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 6-7</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> इत्युक्तोऽसौ मया राजा बभूव मदनातुरः ।
> मां निरीक्ष्य विशालाक्षीं सेवकानित्युवाच ह ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> नरयानमानयध्वं चतुर्वाह्यं मनोहरम् ।
> आरोहणार्थमस्यास्तु कौशेयाम्बरवेष्टितम् ॥ ७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O King! Do nourish me; I have no father, no mother, nor any acquaintances and friends; there is no place for me also to stand on; therefore I am now your dependent.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 8-9</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> मृद्वास्तरणसंयुक्तं मुक्ताजालविभूषितम् ।
> चतुरस्रं विशालं च सुवर्णरचितं शुभम् ॥ ८ ॥
> 
> तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा भृत्याः सत्वरगामिनः ।
> आनिन्युः शिबिकां दिव्यां मदर्थे वस्त्रवेष्टिताम् ॥ ९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

When I spoke thus, the King looked at my face and became love-stricken for me; he then told his attendants to bring an excellent rectangular and spacious palanquin to be carried on four men’s shoulders, gilt and adorned with jewels and pearls, where soft sheets were spread inside and covered all over with silken cloths. Instantly the servants went away and brought for me a beautiful palanquin.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 10-11</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> आरूढाऽहं तदा तस्यां तस्य प्रियचिकीर्षया ।
> मुदितोऽसौ गृहे नीत्वा मां तदा पृथिवीपतिः ॥ १० ॥
> 
> विवाहविधिना राजा शुभे लग्ने शुभे दिने ।
> उपयेमे च मां तत्र हुतभुक्सन्निधौ ततः ॥ ११ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I got on it to serve the best wishes of the King. The King also gladly took me home. In an auspicious day and in an auspicious moment he married me in accordance with due rites and ceremonies in the presence of the Holy Fire.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> तस्याहं वल्लभा जाता प्राणेभ्योऽपि गरीयसी ।
> सौभाग्यसुन्दरीत्येवं नाम तत्र कृतं मम ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I became dearer to him than even his own life and the King, with great fondness, kept my name as Saubhāgya Sundarī.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 13</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> रममाणो मया सार्धं सुखमाप महीपतिः ।
> नानाभोगविलासैश्च कामशास्त्रोदितैस्तथा ॥ १३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The King then began to sport with me amorously according to the rules of the Kāma Śāstra in various ways and with great enjoyments and pleasures.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 14-15</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> राजकार्याणि सन्त्यज्य क्रीडासक्तो दिवानिशम् ।
> नासौ विवेद गच्छन्तं कालं कामकलारतः ॥ १४ ॥
> 
> उद्यानेषु च रम्येषु वापीषु च गृहेषु च ।
> हर्म्येषु वरशैलेषु दीर्घिकासु वरासु च ॥ १५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He then left all his kingly duties and state affairs and he began to remain day and night with me deeply immersed in amorous sports; so much his mind was merged in me in these plays that he could not notice the long time that passed away in the interval.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> वारुणीमदमत्तोऽसौ विहरन्कानने शुभे ।
> विसृज्य सर्वकार्याणि मदधीनो बभूव ह ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He used to drink the Vāruṇī wine and, forsaking all the state affairs, began to enjoy me in nice gardens, beautiful lakes, lovely palaces, beautified houses, excellent mountains and enviable forests and became completely subservient to me.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 17</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> व्यासाहं तेन संसक्ता क्रीडारसवशीकृता ।
> स्मृतवान्पूर्वदेहं न पुम्भावं मुनिजन्म च ॥ १७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Dvaipāyana! Being incessantly engaged with the King in amorous sports and remaining obedient to him, my previous body, male ideas, or the birth of Muni, nothing whatsoever came in my memory.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 18-19</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ममैवायं पतिर्योषाहं पत्नीषु प्रिया सती ।
> पट्टराज्ञी विलासज्ञा सफलं जीवितं मम ॥ १८ ॥
> 
> इति चिन्तयती तस्मिन्प्रेमबद्धा दिवानिशम् ।
> क्रीडासक्ता सुखे लुब्धा तं स्थिता वशवर्तिनी ॥ १९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I remained always attached to him, being obedient to him with a view to be happy and I constantly thought over “that this King is very much attached to me, I am his dearest wife to all others; always he thinks of me, I am his chief consort, capable to give him enjoyment.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 20</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> विस्मृतं ब्रह्मविज्ञानं ब्रह्मज्ञानं च शाश्वतम् ।
> धर्मशास्त्रपरिज्ञानं तदासक्तमना स्थिता ॥ २० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

My mind became entirely his and I completely forgot the eternal Brahmajñān and the knowledge of the Dharma Śāstras.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एवं विहरतस्तत्र वर्षाणि द्वादशैव तु ।
> गतानि क्षणवत्कामक्रीडासक्तस्य मे मुने ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Muni! Thus engaged in various amorous sports, twelve years passed away as if a moment and I could not perceive that.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 22</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> जाता गर्भवती चाहं मुदं प्राप नृपस्तदा ।
> कारयामास विधिवद्गर्भसंस्कारकर्म च ॥ २२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then I became pregnant; and the King became very glad and performed all the ceremonies pertaining to my impregnation and holding of the child in my womb.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 23</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अपृच्छद्दोहदं राजा प्रीणयन्मां पुनः पुनः ।
> नाब्रवं लज्जमानाहं नृपं प्रीतमना भृशम् ॥ २३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

In order to satisfy me, the King used to ask me always what things I liked; I used to be very much abashed; seeing this, the King used to be still more glad.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 24-25</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सम्पूर्णे दशमे मासि पुत्रो जातस्ततो मम ।
> शुभेऽह्नि ग्रहनक्षत्रलग्नताराबलान्विते ॥ २४ ॥
> 
> बभूव नृपतेर्गेहे पुत्रजन्ममहोत्सवः ।
> राजा परमसन्तुष्टो बभूव सुतजन्मतः ॥ २५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Ten months thus passed away and in an auspicious Lagna and when the asterism was favourably strong, I gave birth to a son; the King became very glad and great festivities were held on the birth ceremony of the child.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 26-27</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सूतकान्ते सुतं वीक्ष्य राजा मुदमवाप ह ।
> अहं भूमिपतेश्चासं प्रिया भार्या परन्तप ॥ २६ ॥
> 
> ततो वर्षद्वयान्ते वै पुनर्गर्भो मया धृतः ।
> द्वितीयस्तु सुतो जातः सर्वलक्षणसंयुतः ॥ २७ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Dvaipāyana! When the period of the birth-impurity was over, the King saw the face of the child and was greatly delighted; I then became the dearest wife of the King. Two years after again I became impregnated; the second auspicious son was born.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 28-29</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सुधन्वेति सुतस्याथ नाम चक्रे नृपस्तदा ।
> वीरवर्मेति ज्येष्ठस्य ब्राह्मणैः प्रेरितस्त्वयम् ॥ २८ ॥
> 
> एवं द्वादश पुत्राश्च प्रसूता भूपसम्मताः ।
> मोहितोऽहं तदा तेषां प्रीत्या पालनलालने ॥ २९ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The King gave the name Sudhanvā to the second son and on the authority of the Brāhmins, kept the name of the eldest son as Vīravarmā. Thus I gave birth to twelve sons, in due course of time, to the King’s great liking; and I was engaged in rearing up those children and thus I remained enchanted.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 30-31</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पुनरष्ट सुताः काले काले जाताः स्वरूपिणः ।
> गार्हस्थ्यं मे ततः पूर्णं सम्पन्नं सुखसाधनम् ॥ ३० ॥
> 
> तेषां दारक्रियाः काले कृता राज्ञा यथोचिताः ।
> स्नुषाभिश्च तथा पुत्रैः परिवारो महानभूत् ॥ ३१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Again in due course, I gave birth to eight sons; thus my household was filled with happiness. The King performed the marriage ceremonies of all those children duly and befittingly; and our family became very large with sons and their wives.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 32-33</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ततः पौत्रादिसम्भूतास्तेऽपि क्रीडारसान्विताः ।
> आसन्नानारसोपेता मोहवृद्धिकरा भृशम् ॥ ३२ ॥
> 
> कदाचित्सुखमैश्वर्यं कदाचिद्दुःखमद्भुतम् ।
> पुत्रेषु रोगजनितं देहसन्तापकारकम् ॥ ३३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then I had some grandsons and they increased my attachment and the consequent delusion with their all sorts of playful sports. Sometimes I felt happy and prosperous and sometimes I felt pain and sorrow when my sons fell ill.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> परस्परं कदाचित्तु विरोधोऽभूत्सुदारुणः ।
> पुत्राणां वा वधूनां च तेन सन्तापसम्भवः ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then my body and mind became very much troubled with sorrows. Again the quarrels amongst my sons and my daughters-inlaw , brought terrible pain and remorse in my mind.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 35-36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सुखदुःखात्मके घोरे मिथ्याचारकरे भृशम् ।
> सङ्कल्पजनिते क्षुद्रे मग्नोऽहं मुनिसत्तम ॥ ३५ ॥
> 
> विस्मृतं पूर्वविज्ञानं शास्त्रज्ञानं तथा गतम् ।
> योषाभावे विलीनोऽहं गृहकार्येषु सर्वथा ॥ ३६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Best of Munis! Thus I was greatly immersed in the terrible ocean of these imaginary thoughts, sometimes happy and sometimes painful, and I forgot my previous knowledge and the knowledge of the Śāstras. I was merged in the thought of myself being a woman and lost myself entirely in doing the household affairs.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 37-38</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अहङ्कारस्तु सञ्जातो भृशं मोहविवर्धकः ।
> एते मे बलिनः पुत्राः स्नुषाः सुकुलसम्भवाः ॥ ३७ ॥
> 
> एते पुत्राः सुसन्नद्धाः क्रीडन्ति मम वेश्मसु ।
> धन्याहं खलु नारीणां संसारेऽस्मिन्नहो भृशम् ॥ ३८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

I began to think “that I have so many daughters-in-law; so many powerful sons of mine are playing together in my house; Oh! I am fortunate and full of merits amongst women” and thus my egoistic pride increased.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 39-40</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> नारदोऽहं भगवता वञ्चितो मायया किल ।
> न कदाचिन्मयाप्येवं चिन्तितं मनसा किल ॥ ३९ ॥
> 
> राजपत्नी शुभाचारा बहुपुत्रा पतिव्रता ।
> धन्याहं किल संसारे कृष्णैवं मोहितस्त्वहम् ॥ ४० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Not for a moment even occurred the thought that I had been Nārada; the Bhagavān had deceived me by His Māyā. O Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana! I was deluded by Māyā and passed away my time in the thought “that I am the king’s wife, chaste and of good conduct following good Ācāra; I have so many sons and grandsons; I am blessed in this Samsāra and that I am so happy and prosperous.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 41-43</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अथ कश्चिन्नृपः कामं दूरदेशाधिपो महान् ।
> अरातिभावमापन्नः पतिना सह मानद ॥ ४१ ॥
> 
> कृत्वा सैन्यसमायोगं रथैश्च वारणैर्युतम् ।
> आजगाम कान्यकुब्जे पुरे युद्धमचिन्तयत् ॥ ४२ ॥
> 
> वेष्टितं नगरं तेन राज्ञा सैन्ययुतेन च ।
> मम पुत्राश्च पौत्राश्च निर्गता नगरात्तदा ॥ ४३ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

One powerful king of a distant country turned out an inveterate enemy of my husband and came to the city of Kanauj to fight with my husband, accompanied by chariots, and elephants and the fourfold army.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 44-45</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> सङ्ग्रामस्तुमुलस्तत्र कृतस्तैस्तेन पुत्रकैः ।
> हता रणे सुताः सर्वे वैरिणा कालयोगतः ॥ ४४ ॥
> 
> राजा भग्नस्तु सङ्ग्रामादागतः स्वगृहं पुनः ।
> श्रुतं मया मृताः पुत्राः सङ्ग्रामे भृशदारुणे ॥ ४५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

That enemy besieged the city with his army; my sons and grandsons went out and fought valiantly with him but owing to the great Destiny, the enemies killed all my sons. The King retreated and returned to his palace.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 46</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स हत्वा मे सुतान्पौत्रान्गतो राजा बलान्वितः ।
> क्रन्दमाना ह्यहं तत्र गता समरमण्डले ॥ ४६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Next I heard that powerful King killed all my sons and grandsons and had gone back to his country with his army. I then hurriedly went to the battlefield, crying loudly.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 29 Verse 47-48</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> दृष्ट्वा तान्पतितान्पुत्रान्पौत्रांश्चदुःखपीडिता ।
> विललापाहमायुष्मञ्छोकसागरसम्प्लवे ॥ ४७ ॥
> 
> हा पुत्राः क्व गता मेऽद्य हा हतास्मि दुरात्मना ।
> दैवेनातिबलिष्ठेन दुर्वारेणातिपापिना ॥ ४८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Long-lived One! Seeing my sons and grandsons lying on the ground, in that horrible and distressed state, I became merged in the ocean of sorrows and lamented and wept loudly and wildly, “O my Sons! Where have you gone leaving me thus? Alas! The pernicious Fate is very dominant, and very painsgiving and indomitable.

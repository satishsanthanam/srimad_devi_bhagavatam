SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 6, CHAPTER 13

[Ch 13 Verse 1]
६.१३ त्रयोदशोऽध्यायः । आडीबकयुद्ध वर्णनसहितं देवीमाहात्म्यवर्णनम् ।


इन्द्र उवाच ।
साहसं कृतवान् राजा पूर्वं यत्कथितो मखः ।
वरुणाय प्रतिज्ञातः पुत्रं कृत्वा पशुं प्रियम् ॥ १ ॥
English Translation:
Indra said :-- “O Prince! The King Hariścandra promised before to Varuṇa that he would celebrate for his propitiation the great Naramedha sacrifice when he would offer his own son as a victim to be immolated.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 2]
गते त्वयि पिता पुत्रं बद्ध्वा यूपेऽघृणः पुनः ।
पशुं कृत्वा महाबुद्धे वधिष्यति व्यथातुरः ॥ २ ॥
English Translation:
O Prince! You are very intelligent; can’t you grasp this idea that your father has become merciless due to his suffering in this illness and no sooner you go there than he will make you the victim and tie you to the sacrificial post when you will be slaughtered.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 3]
इत्थं निषिद्धस्तत्पुत्रः शक्रेणामिततेजसा ।
स्थितस्तत्रैव मायेशीमायया मोहितो भृशम् ॥ ३ ॥
English Translation:
The indomitable Indra thus prohibiting the son, he began to stay there deluded by the Māyā of the great Mahā Māyā.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 4]
यदा पुनः पुनः श्रुत्वा पितरं रोगपीडितम् ।
गमनाय मतिं चक्रे तदेन्द्रः प्रत्यषेधयत् ॥ ४ ॥
English Translation:
O King! Thus whenever the prince heard of his father’s severe illness, he wanted to go to his father, Indra repeatedly used to go to him and prevent him from doing so.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 5]
हरिश्चन्द्रोऽतिदुःखार्तः पप्रच्छ गुरुमन्तिके ।
स्थितं वसिष्ठमेकान्ते सर्वज्ञं हिततत्परम् ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation:
On the other hand, the King Hariścandra became very much afflicted, and, seeing his family Guru all-knowing well-wisher Vaśiṣṭha close by, asked him,
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 6]
राजोवाच ।
भगवन् कि करोम्यद्य कातरोऽस्मि व्यथाकुलः ।
त्राहि मां दुःखमनसं महाव्याधिभयातुरम् ॥ ६ ॥
English Translation:
“O Bhagavān! what am I to do now? I am now very impatient with the agonies of this disease and am very weak; besides I am very much afraid of it. Kindly give me a good advice and save me.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 7]
वसिष्ठ उवाच ।
शृणु राजन्नुपायोऽस्ति रोगनाशं प्रति स्तुतः ।
त्रयोदशविधाः पुत्राः कथिता धर्मसङ्ग्रहे ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation:
Vaśiṣṭha said :-- “O King! There is a good remedy for the cure of your disease. It is stated in the Śāstras that the sons are of thirteen kinds; Aurasa, Kṣattraja, Datrima, Krītrima; etc.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 8-9]
तस्मात्क्रीतं सुतं कृत्वा यजस्व मखमुत्तमम् ।
द्रव्यं दत्त्वा यथोद्दिष्टमानयस्व द्विजोत्तमम् ॥ ८ ॥

एवं कृते मखे भूप रोगनाशो भविष्यति ।
वरुणोऽपि प्रसन्नात्मा भविष्यति यथासुखम् ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation:
Therefore pay the reasonable price and buy one good Brāhmin boy and perform your sacrifice with that boy. O King! Thus Varuṇa will be pleased and you will be cured of your disease.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 10]
व्यास उवाच ।
इति तस्य वचः श्रुत्वा राजा प्रोवाच मन्त्रिणम् ।
अन्वेषय महाबुद्धे विषयेष्वतियत्नतः ॥ १० ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O King! Hearing thus the words of Vaśiṣṭha, the King Hariścandra addressed to his minister :-- “O Minister-in-Chief! You are very sharp and intelligent, therefore you better try your best and seek in my kingdom a Brāhmin boy.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 11]
कदाचित्कोऽपि लोभार्थी ददाति स्वसुतं पिता ।
समानय धनं दत्त्वा यावत्प्रार्थयतेऽप्यसौ ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation:
In case a poor Brāhmin be willing, out of his love for money, to give over his son, then give him any amount he wants and bring his son.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 12]
सर्वथैव समानेयो यज्ञार्थे द्विजबालकः ।
न कार्या कृपणा बुद्धिस्त्वया मत्कार्यहेतवे ॥ १२ ॥
English Translation:
O Minister! By all means, bring a Brāhmin boy for this sacrifice; in other words, do not be miserly or act lazily to perform my business.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 13]
प्रार्थनीयस्त्वया पुत्रः कस्यचिद्द्विजवादिनः ।
द्रव्येण देहि यज्ञार्थं कर्तव्योऽसौ पशुः किल ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation:
You should pray to any Brāhmin thus :-- Take this money and give your son, who will be sacrificed in a sacrificial ceremony as a victim.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 14]
इति सञ्चोदितस्तेन सचिवः कार्यहेतवे ।
अन्वेषयामास पुरे ग्रामे ग्रामे गृहे गृहे ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation:
Thus ordered, the minister sought for a Brāhmin boy in towns after towns, villages after villages, and houses after houses.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 15-16]
एवमन्वेषतस्तस्य विषये कश्चिदातुरः ।
निर्धनस्त्रिसुतश्चासीदजीगर्तेति नामतः ॥ १५ ॥

तस्य पुत्रं शुनःशेपं मध्यमं मन्त्रिसत्तमः ।
आनयामास दत्त्वार्थं प्रार्थितं यद्धनं तदा ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation:
Till, at last, he came to know that in his kingdom there was a poor distressed Brāhmin named Ajīgarta, who had three sons. Then the minister gave to the Brāhmin that he wanted and purchased his second son named Śunahśepha and brought him before the King. And handed him over to the King, saying that this Brāhmin boy is fit for the sacrificial victim.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 17]
समानीय शुनःशेपं सचिवः कार्यतत्परः ।
राज्ञे निवेदयामास पशुयोग्यं द्विजात्मजम् ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation:
The King then gladly brought the best Brāhmins versed in the Vedas for the performance of the sacrifice, and collected all the articles requisite for the purpose.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 18-19]
राजातिमुदितस्तेन विप्रानानीय सर्वतः ।
कारयामास सम्भारान्यज्ञार्थं वेदवित्तमान् ॥ १८ ॥

प्रारब्धे तु मखे तत्र विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।
बद्धं दृष्ट्वा शुनःशेपं निषिषेध नृपं तदा ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation:
When the sacrifice was commenced, the great Muni Viśvāmitra, seeing Śunahśepha tied, prohibited the King and said :-- “O King! Do not be so bold as to sacrifice this boy; let this boy be free.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 20]
राजन्मा साहसं कार्षीर्मुञ्चैनं द्विजबालकम् ।
प्रार्थयाम्यहमायुष्मन् सुखं तेऽद्य भविष्यति ॥ २० ॥
English Translation:
O long-lived One! I am asking this thing from you today and if you obey it, certainly it will do good to you.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 21]
क्रन्दत्ययं शुनःशेपः करुणा मां दुनोत्यपि ।
दयावान्भव राजेन्द्र कुरु मे वचनं नृप ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation:
O King! This boy Śunahśepha is crying; his cries are paining my heart and I am feeling pity for him. Hear my word and free this boy out of mercy.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 22]
परदेहस्य रक्षायै स्वदेहं ये दयापराः ।
ददति क्षत्रियाः पूर्वं स्वर्गकामाः शुचिव्रताः ॥ २२ ॥
English Translation:
See! The purehearted Kṣattriyas, in ancient days, used to sacrifice their own bodies and thus preserve otherś bodies, so that they might attain the Heavens.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 23]
त्वं स्वदेहस्य रक्षार्थं हंसि द्विजसुतं बलात् ।
पापं मा कुरु राजेन्द्र दयावान् भव बालके ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation:
And now you are killing this Brāhmin boy forcibly so that you may preserve your own body; judge how vicious is this your act! Be merciful to this boy.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 24]
सर्वेषां सदृशी प्रीतिर्देहे वेत्सि स्वयं नृप ।
मुञ्चैनं बालकं तस्मात्प्रमाणं यदि मे वचः ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation:
O King! Everyone likes his own body to the same extent; you are feeling this yourself; therefore if you take my word, then quit this boy.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 25]
व्यास क्त्वच
अनादृत्य च तद्वाक्यं राजा दुःखातुरो भृशम् ।
न मुमोच मुनिस्तस्मै चूकोपातीव तापसः ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O King! The King Hariścandra was ailing very much; hence be did not pay any heed to the Muni’s words and did not quit the boy.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 26]
उपदेशं ददौ तस्मै शुनःशेपाय कौशिकः ।
मन्त्रं पाशधरस्याथ दयावान्वेदवित्तमः ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation:
Thereupon the very fiery spirited Viśvāmitra became very angry with the King. Then Viśvāmitra, the son of Kuśika, the foremost of the knowers of the Vedas, shewed mercy on Śunahśepha and gave him the “Varuṇa Mantram.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 27]
शुनःशेपोऽपि तं मन्त्रमसकृद्वधकर्शितः ।
स्तुतस्वरेण चुक्रोश संस्मरन्वरुणं भृशम् ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation:
Śunahśepha, very much afraid to lose his life, earnestly repeatedly remembered Varuṇa and uttered that mantram in pluta tone (lengthened or prolonged).
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 28-29]
स्तुवन्तं मुनिपुत्रं तं ज्ञात्वा वै यादसां पतिः ।
तत्रागत्य शुनःशेपं मुमोच करुणार्णवः ॥ २८ ॥

रोगहीनं नृपं कृत्वा वरुणः स्वगृहं ययौ ।
विश्वामित्रस्तु तं पुत्रं कृतवान्मोचितं मृतेः ॥ २९ ॥
English Translation:
Varuṇa, too, the ocean of mercy, knowing that the Brāhmin boy was praising him with hymns came up to that spot and freed Śunahśepha from his bondage and freed the King also from his disease and went back to his own abode. Thus the Maharṣi Viśvāmitra became very glad to rescue the Muni’s son from the jaws of death.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 30]
न कृतं वचनं राज्ञा कौशिकस्य महात्मनः ।
रोषं दधार मनसा राजोपरि स गाधिजः ॥ ३० ॥
English Translation:
The King Hariścandra did not observe the words of Viśvāmitra; hence the son of Gādhi harboured within his heart anger towards the King.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 31-32]
एकस्मिन्समये राजा हयारूढो वनं गतः ।
सूकरं हन्तुकामस्तु मध्याह्ने कौशिकीतटे ॥ ३१ ॥

वृद्धब्राह्मणवेषेण विश्वामित्रेण वञ्चितः ।
सर्वस्वं प्रार्थितं तस्य गृहीतं राज्यमद्भुतम् ॥ ३२ ॥
English Translation:
One day while the King Hariścandra was riding in a forest and there, at midday, on the banks of the river Kauśik, when he desired to kill a boar, Viśvāmitra in the garb of an old Brāhmin asked from him everything that he had, including his dominion and thus cunningly took away everything from the King.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 33]
पीडितोऽसौ हरिश्चन्द्रो यजमानो यतो भृशम् ।
वसिष्ठः कौशिकं प्राह वने प्राप्तं यदृच्छया ॥ ३३ ॥
English Translation:
The Maharṣi Vaśiṣṭha, seeing his Yajamāna Hariścandra suffering much, became wounded and felt pain in his mind.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 34]
क्षत्रियाधम दुर्बुद्धे वृथा ब्राह्मणवेषभृत् ।
बकधर्म वृथा किं त्वं गर्वं वहसि दाम्भिक ॥ ३४ ॥
English Translation:
One day when he met casually with Viśvāmitra in a forest, he said :-- “O wicked Kṣattriya! A disgrace to your family! You have in vain put on the garb of a Brāhmin; your religion is like a crane; you ate full of vanity; you boast for nothing.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 35]
कस्मात्त्वया नृपश्रेष्ठो यजमानो ममाप्यसौ ।
अपराधं विना जाल्म गमितो दुःखमद्भुतम् ॥ ३५ ॥
English Translation:
The best of kings, Hariścandra is my client; be is faultless; still, O Fool! Why are you giving him so much trouble. As you are religious as a crane is religious, so take your birth  as a crane."
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 36]
बकध्यानपरो यस्मात्तस्मात्त्वं वै बको भव ।
इति शप्तो वसिष्ठेन कौशिकः प्राह तं पुनः ॥ ३६ ॥
English Translation:
Viśvāmitra, thus cursed by Vaśiṣṭha, cursed Vaśiṣṭha in return, and said :-- “O Vaśiṣṭha! As long as I will remain a crane, so long you also remain as Śarāli or Āḍi bird.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 37]
त्वमप्याडिर्भवायुष्मन् बकोऽहं यावदेव हि ।
व्यास उवाच ।
एवं परस्परं दत्त्वा शापं तौ क्रोधपीडितौ ॥ ३७ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- O King! The two angry Munis thus cursed each other and the two were born as Crane and Śarāli or Ādi bird.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 38]
अण्डजौ तरसा जातौ सरस्याडीबकौ मुनी ।
एकस्मिन्पादपे नीडं कृत्वासौ बकरूपभाक् ॥ ३८ ॥
English Translation:
The crane Viśvāmitra built its nest on the top of a tree on the Mānasarovara lake and began to live there.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 39]
विश्वामित्रः स्थितस्तत्र दिव्ये सरसि मानसे ।
अन्यस्मित्पादपे कृत्वा वसिष्ठो नीडमुत्तमम् ॥ ३९ ॥
English Translation:
Vaśiṣṭha, too, assumed the form of an Ādi bird, and built his nest on the top of another tree and lived there. Thus the two Ṛṣis spent their days in full enmity towards each other.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 40]
आडीरूपधरस्तस्थावन्योन्यं द्वेषतत्परौ ।
दिने दिने तौ सङ्ग्रामं चक्रतुः क्रोधसंयुतौ ॥ ४० ॥
English Translation:
These two birds used to shriek so terribly loud that they became a nuisance to all; they fought daily with each other.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 41]
दुःखदं सर्वलोकानां क्रन्दमानावुभौ भृशम् ।
चञ्चुपक्षप्रहारैस्तु नखाघातैः परस्परम् ॥ ४१ ॥
English Translation:
They used to strike each other with beaks and wings and nails and thus they were covered all over their bodies with cuts and wounds and they were smeared with blood.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 42]
जघ्नतू रुधिरक्लिनौ पुष्पिताविव किंशुकौ ।
एवं बहूनि वर्षाणि पक्षिरूपधरौ मुनी ॥ ४२ ॥
English Translation:
They began to look like Kiṃśuka trees. Thus the two Ṛṣis, in the shape of birds, in their states of bondage, due to each other’s curse, passed many years there.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 43]
स्थितौ तत्र महाराज शापपाशेन यन्त्रितौ ।
राजोवाच ।
कथं मुक्तौ मुनिश्रेष्ठौ शापाद्वसिष्ठकौशिकौ ॥ ४३ ॥
English Translation:
Janamejaya said :-- “O Brāhmaṇa! Kindly tell me how Vaśiṣṭha and Kauśika, the two Ṛṣis, became free from their curses; I am very curious to hear this.”
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 44]
तन्ममाचक्ष्व विप्रर्षे परं कौतूहलं हि मे ।
व्यास उवाच ।
युध्यमानाब्वुभौ दृष्ट्वा ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ॥ ४४ ॥
English Translation:
Vyāsa said :-- Brahmā, the Grandsire of his subjects, came there with all the Devas, filled with mercy, on seeing those two Ṛṣis at war against each other.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 45]
तत्राजगामानिमिषैर्वृतः सर्वैर्दयापरैः ।
तावाश्वास्य जगत्कर्ता युद्धतो विनिवार्य च ॥ ४५ ॥
English Translation:
Brahmā, the Lotus-seated, made them desist from such a fight, consoled them and freed both of them from each other’s curses.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 46]
शापं सम्मोचयामास तयोः क्षिप्तं परस्परम् ।
ततो जग्मुः सुराः सर्वे स्वानि धिष्ण्यानि पद्मभूः ॥ ४६ ॥
English Translation:
Then the Devas went back to their own abodes and the illustrious lotus-seated Brahmā went to the Satyaloka, seated on his Swan.
----------------------------------------

[Ch 13 Verse 47-49]
सत्यलोकं जगामाशु हंसारूढः प्रतापवान् ।
विश्वामित्रोऽप्यगात्तूर्णं वसिष्ठः स्वाश्रमं गतः ॥ ४७ ॥

मिथः स्नेहं ततः कृत्वा प्रजापत्युपदेशतः ।
मैत्रावरुणिनाप्येवं कृतं युद्धमकारणम् ॥ ४८ ॥

कौशिकेन समं भूप दुःखदं च परस्परम् ।
को नाम मानवो लोके देवो वा दानवोऽपि वा ॥ ४९ ॥
English Translation:
Maharṣi Vaśiṣṭha and Viśvāmitra became then friends and were tied with bonds of affection at the advice of Brahmā; they went back to their own Āśramas. O King! Now see, that the Maharṣi Vaśiṣṭha, the son of Mitrā-Varuṇa, fought for nothing with Viśvāmitra, so painful to both the parties. Who, then, amongst the human beings, the Dānavas or the Devas can conquer his Ahamkāra (egoism) and be always happy?

[Ch 13 Verse 50]
अहङ्कारजयं कृत्वा सर्वदा सुखभाग्भवेत् ।
तस्माद्राजंश्चित्तशुद्धिर्महतामपि दुर्लभा ॥ ५० ॥
English Translation:
Therefore the Chitta-Śuddhi, the purity of the heart (that purity which imparts to man the blessedness of God-vision) is very difficult even for the high-souled persons; with the greatest caution and utmost effort one has to practise for that.

[Ch 13 Verse 51]
यत्नेन साधनीया सा तद्विहीनं निरर्थकम् ।
तीर्थं दानं तपः सत्यं यत्किञ्चिद्धर्मसाधनम् ॥ ५१ ॥
English Translation:
To those persons, that are void of this Chitta Śuddhi, it is all vain to go to places of pilgrimage, to make charities, to practise tapasyā, to be truthful; in fact, anything, which is the means to attain Dharma, becomes useless. O King! Śraddhā (Faith) is of three kinds :-- (1) Sāttvikī, (2) Rājasikī and (3) Tāmasikī to all persons in all their religious matters. The Sāttvik faith is the only one of the three that yields entire results; and it is very rare in this world. The Rājasik faith, done according to due rules, yields half the results thereof and the Tāmasik faith is fruitless and inglorious; the Tāmasik faith arises with those persons that are overwhelmed with lust, anger, greed, etc.

[Ch 13 Verse 52]
(श्रद्धात्र त्रिविधा प्रोक्ता सात्त्विकी राजसी तथा ।

Therefore, O King! Keep to the company of the good and hear the Śāstras, Vedānta, etc., and free the heart of worldly desires and then concentrate it to the worship of the Devī and live in a sacred place of pilgrimage.

[Ch 13 Verse 53-54]
देवीनामानि वचसा गृह्णंस्तस्या गुणान्स्तुवन् ।
ध्यायंस्तस्याः पदाम्भोजं कलिदोषभयार्दितः ॥ ५३ ॥

एवं तु कुर्वतस्तस्य न कदाचित्कलेर्भयम् ।
अनायासेन संसारान्मुच्यते पातकी जनः ॥ ५४ ॥

इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायां षष्ठस्कन्धे
आडीबकयुद्ध वर्णनसहितं देवीमाहात्म्यवर्णनं नाम त्रयोदशोऽध्यायः ॥ ६.१३॥
English Translation:
Men afraid and troubled with the defects of the Kālīyuga, should always take the name of the Devī, sing praises, and meditate on Her lotus feet. Thus the Jīvas will not have any fear of Kālī and the fallen vicious persons will easily be able to cross this ocean of the world and be free. There is no doubt in this.

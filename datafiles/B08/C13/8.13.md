# SRIMAD DEVI BHAGAVATAM — BOOK 8, CHAPTER 13

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 1</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> ८.१३ त्रयोदशोऽध्यायः । भुवनकोशवर्णने क्रौञ्चशाकपुष्करद्वीपवर्णनम् ।
> 
> 
> नारद उवाच ।
> शिष्टद्वीपप्रमाणं च वद सर्वार्थदर्शन ।
> येन विज्ञातमात्रेण परानन्दमयो भवेत् ॥ १ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārada said :-- Thou, the Knower of everything! Describe about the remaining Dvīpas, knowing which we will be highly delighted.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 2</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> श्रीरानारायण उवाच ।
> कुशद्वीपस्य परितो घृतोदावरणं महत् ।
> ततो बहिः क्रौञ्चद्वीपो द्विगुणः स्यात्स्वमानतः ॥ २ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Nārāyaṇa spoke thus :-- The very vast Ghrita Sāgara (the ocean of clarified butter) is encircling the Kuśa Dvīpa. Next to it is the Krauñca Dvīpa. It is twice as large as Kuśa.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 3-4</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> क्षीरोदेनावृतो भाति यस्मिन्क्रौञ्चाद्रिरस्ति च ।
> नामनिर्वर्तकः सोऽयं द्वीपस्य परिवर्तते ॥ ३ ॥
> 
> योऽसौ गुहस्य शक्त्या च भिन्नकुक्षिः पुराभवत् ।
> क्षीरोदेनासिच्यमानो वरुणेन च रक्षितः ॥ ४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The Kṣīra Sāgara (the ocean of milk) is surrounding this Dvīpa. The Krauñca mountain is standing here. The name of this Dvīpa is derived from this mountain. In days gone by, the highly intelligent Kārtikeya burst this mountain by his own prowess. This Dvīpa is washed by the Kṣīra Sāgara; and Varuṇa is its Regent.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 5</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> घृतपृष्ठो नाम यस्य विभाति किल नायकः ।
> प्रियव्रतात्मजः श्रीमान् सर्वलोकनमस्कृतः ॥ ५ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The son of Priyavrata, Ghritapṛṣṭha, respected by all and whose prosperity knows no end, is the Lord of this Dvīpa. He divided this Dvīpa into the seven parts and distributed them to his sons and named the Varṣas after the names of his sons.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 6-8</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्वद्वीपं तु विभज्यैव सप्तधा स्वात्मजान्ददौ ।
> पुत्रनामसु वर्षेषु वर्षपान्सन्निवेशयन् ॥ ६ ॥
> 
> स्वयं भगवतस्तस्य शरणं सञ्जगाम ह ।
> आमो मधुरुहश्चैव मेघपृष्ठः सुधामकः ॥ ७ ॥
> 
> भ्राजिष्ठो लोहितार्णश्च वनस्पतिरितीव च ।
> नगा नद्यश्च सप्तैव विख्याता भुवि सर्वतः ॥ ८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

He made his sons the rulers of those places and he himself took the refuge of the Bhagavān Nārāyaṇa The names of the seven Varṣas are respectively :-- Āma, Madhuruha, Meghapṛṣṭha, Sudhāmaka, Bhrājiṣṭha, Lohitārṇa, and Vanaspati. O Nārada! The seven mountains and the rivers there are very celebrated throughout the worlds.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 9-10</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> शुक्लो वै वर्धमानश्च भोजनश्चोपबर्हणः ।
> नन्दश्च नन्दनः सर्वतोभद्र इति कीर्तिताः ॥ ९ ॥
> 
> अभया अमृतौघा चार्यका तीर्थवतीति च ।
> वृत्तिरूपवती शुक्ला पवित्रवतिका तथा ॥ १० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The names of the mountains are :-- Śūkla, Vardhamāna, Bhojana, Upavarhaṇa, Nanda, Nandana, and Sarvatobhadra. The names of the rivers are :-- Abhayā, Amritaughā, Āryakā, Tīrthavatī, Vrittirūpavatī, Śuklā, and Pavitravatikā.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 11-12</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एतासामुदकं पुण्यं चातुर्वर्ण्येन पीयते ।
> पुरुषऋषभौ तद्वद् द्रविणाख्यश्च देवकः ॥ ११ ॥
> 
> एते चतुर्वर्णजाताः पुरुषा निवसन्ति हि ।
> तत्रत्याः पुरुषा आपोमयं देवमपां पतिम् ॥ १२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The inhabitants there drink the highly pure water of these rivers. The people there are divided into four colours Puruṣa, Ṛṣabha, Draviṇa, and Vedaka and they worship the Bhagavān Varuṇa, of the form of water.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 13-14</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पूर्णेनाञ्जलिना भक्त्या यजन्ते विविधक्रियाः ।
> आपः पुरुषवीर्याः स्थ पुनन्तीर्भूर्भुवः स्वरः ॥ १३ ॥
> 
> ता नः पुनीतामीवघ्नीः स्पृशतामात्मना भुवः ।
> इति मन्त्रजपान्ते च स्तुवन्ति विविधैः स्तवैः ॥ १४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Then they become very discriminative, and, with great devotion, and holding full within their folded palms the water, repeat the following mantra :-- “O Water! Thou art the essence Vīrya of the Person Bhagavāna and Thou sanctifiest the Bhūrloka, Bhuvarloka, and Svarloka.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 15-16</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> एवं परस्तात्क्षीरोदात्परितश्चोपवेशितः ।
> द्वात्रिंशल्लक्षसङ्ख्याकयोजनायाममाश्रितः ॥ १५ ॥
> 
> स्वमानेन च द्वीपोऽयं दधिमण्डोदकेन च ।
> शाकद्वीपो विशिष्टोऽयं यस्मिच्छाको महीरुहः ॥ १६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Thou destroyest the sins of all. We all are touching it; purify our bodies.” After finishing their mantrams, they sing various hymns to Varuṇa. Next to the Kṣiroda Sāgara, is the Śaka Dvīpa, thirty two lakh yoyanas wide, surrounded by the Dadhi Sāgara (the ocean of curds), of similar dimensions. Here the most excellent tree named the Śaka tree exists.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 17-18</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> स्वक्षेत्रव्यपदेशस्य कारणं स हि नारद ।
> प्रैयव्रतोऽधिपस्तस्य मेधातिथिरिति स्मृतः ॥ १७ ॥
> 
> विभज्य सप्त वर्षाणि पुत्रनामानि तेषु च ।
> सप्त पुत्रान्निजान् स्थाप्य स्वयं योगगतिं गतः ॥ १८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Nārada! The Dvīpa is named so after the tree. Medhātithi, the son of Priyavrata is the Lord of this Dvīpa. He divided this land into the seven Varṣas and distributed each to his seven sons respectively; and ultimately he took refuge to the path of Yoga.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 19-21</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पुरोजवो मनःपूर्वजवोऽथ पवमानकः ।
> धूम्रानीकश्चित्ररेफो बहुरूपोऽथ विश्वधृक् ॥ १९ ॥
> 
> मर्यादागिरयः सप्त नद्यः सप्तैव कीर्तिताः ।
> ईशान ऊरुशृङ्गोऽथ बलभद्रः शतकेशरः ॥ २० ॥
> 
> सहस्रस्रोतको देवपालोऽप्यन्ते महाशनः ।
> एतेऽद्रयः सप्त चोक्ताः सरिन्नामानि सप्त च ॥ २१ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The names of the seven Varṣas are Purojava, Manojava, Pavamānaka, Dhūmrānīka, Citrarepha, Bahurūpa, and Viśvadhrik. In these Varṣas there are seven mountains, one in each Varṣa, as forming their boundaries; and there are seven rivers also. The names of the mountains are :-- Īśana, Ūruśriṅga, Valabhadra, Śata Keśara, Sahasra-srotaka, Devapāla, and Mahāsana; the names of rivers are

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 22-24</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> अनघा प्रथमाऽऽयुर्दा उभयस्पृष्टिरेव च ।
> अपराजिता पञ्चपदी सहस्रश्रुतिरेव च ॥ २२ ॥
> 
> ततो निजधृतिश्चोक्ताः सप्त नद्यो महोज्ज्वलाः ।
> तद्वर्षपुरुषाः सर्वे सत्यव्रतक्रतुव्रतौ ॥ २३ ॥
> 
> दानव्रतानुव्रतौ च चतुर्वर्णा उदीरिताः ।
> भगवन्तं प्राणवायुं प्राणायामेन संयुताः ॥ २४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Anaghā, Āyurdā, Ubhayaspṛṣṭi, Aparājitā, Pañchapadī, and Sahasraśruti and Nijadhriti. These seven rivers are all very big and resplendent with lustre. The people are divided into four classes :-- Varṣas, Satyavrata, Kratuvrata, Dānavrata, and Aṇuvrata. They all take the Prāṇayāma exercise and thereby bring the Rājas, and Tamo Guṇa under their subjection and they worship Hari, of the nature of Prāṇa Vayu, Higher than the Highest.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 25-26</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> यजन्ति निर्धूतरजस्तमसः परमं हरिम् ।
> अन्तः प्रविश्य भूतानि यो बिभर्त्यात्मकेतुभिः ॥ २५ ॥
> 
> अन्तर्यामीश्वरः साक्षात्पातु नो यद्वशे इदम् ।
> परस्ताद्दधिमण्डोदात्ततस्तु बहुविस्तरः ॥ २६ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Their mantra is this :-- “He has entered into all the living beings and nourishes them by the Prāṇa and other faculties; He is the Internal Ruler of all and the Supreme Controller; this Universe is under His control; let Him protect and nourish us.”

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 27-28</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> पुष्करद्वीपनामायं शाकद्वीपद्विसङ्गुणः ।
> स्वसमानेन स्वादूदकेनायं परिवेष्टितः ॥ २७ ॥
> 
> यत्रास्ते पुष्करं भ्राजदग्निचूडानिभानि च ।
> पत्राणि विशदानीह स्वर्णपत्रायुतायुतम् ॥ २८ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

O Nārada! Next to this Dadhi Sāgara is Puṣkara Dvīpa; it is twice as large as Śāka Dvīpa. It is surrounded by the Dudha Sāgar (the ocean of milk) all twice as large. The leaves of Puṣkara tree that shines in the Puṣkara Dvīpa, are fiery like golden flames; they are as clean and pure.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 29-30</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> श्रीमद्भगवतश्चेदमासनं परमेष्ठिनः ।
> कल्पितं लोकगुरुणा सर्वलोकसिसृक्षया ॥ २९ ॥
> 
> तद्द्वीप एक एवायं मानसोत्तरनामकः ।
> अर्वाचीनपराचीनवर्षयोरवधिर्गिरिः ॥ ३० ॥
>
**English Translation:**

Crores and crores of leaves, golden in colour ornament this Tree. Vāsudeva, the Guru of all the Lokas, has created this Puṣkara Dvīpa as the seat of Parameṣṭhī Brahmā, possessed of six extraordinary powers, for the purpose of creation. There is one mountain in this Dvīpa; it is divided into two parts, named Arvācīna and Parācīna.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 31-32</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> उच्छ्रायायामयोः सङ्ख्यायुतयोजनसम्मिता ।
> यत्र दिक्षु च चत्वारि चतसृषु पुराणि ह ॥ ३१ ॥
> 
> इन्द्रादिलोकपालानां यदुपर्यर्कनिर्गमः ।
> मेरुं प्रदक्षिणीकुर्वन् भानुः पर्येति यत्र हि ॥ ३२ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

These form the boundaries of the two Varṣas. The mountain is one Ayuta Yoyana high and one Ayuta Yoyana wide. There are four cities on the four sides. Indra and the three other Lokapālas are the lords of these cities. The Sun God comes out from their top and circumambulating Meru, goes there again.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 33-34</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> संवत्सरात्मकं चक्रं देवाहोरात्रतो भ्रमन् ।
> प्रैयव्रतोऽधिपो वीतिहोत्रः स्वात्मजकद्वयम् ॥ ३३ ॥
> 
> वर्षद्वये परिस्थाप्य वर्षनामधरं क्रमात् ।
> रमणो धातकिश्चैव तत्तद्वर्षपती उभौ ॥ ३४ ॥
>
**English Translation:**

The whole year is his Cakram, circle of circuit; His path is Uttrāyaṇam and Dakṣiṇāyaṇam. Vītihotra, the son of Priyavrata is the lord of this island. He distributed the two Varṣas amongst his two sons, Ramaṇa and Dhātakī. They rule over the two Varṣas named also after them.

</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>Ch 13 Verse 35-36</b></summary>
  <br>

> **Sanskrit:**
> कृताः स्वयं पूर्वजवद्भगवद्भक्तितत्पराः ।
> तद्वर्षपुरुषा ब्रह्मरूपिणं परमेश्वरम् ॥ ३५ ॥
> 
> सकर्मकेन योगेन यजन्ति परिशीलिताः ।
> यत्तत्कर्ममयं लिङ्ग ब्रह्मलिङ्गं जनोऽर्चयेत् ।
> एकान्तमद्वयं शान्तं तस्मै भगवते नमः ॥ ३६ ॥
>
> *Colophon:*
> इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायामष्टमस्कन्धे
> भुवनकोशवर्णनं क्रौञ्चशाकपुष्करद्वीपवर्णनं नाम त्रयोदशोऽध्यायः ॥ ८.१३॥
>
**English Translation:**

Like the inhabitants of the above Varṣas, the people also get powers of themselves and worship devotedly the God seated on the lotus and follow such path of the Yoga as leads them to the Brahmā Sālokyā, etc. The mantra runs thus :-- “We bow down to that One God, without a Second, of the nature of Peace, Who is the Fruit of all the Karmas, Who is the seat of illumination of Brahmā, Who is established in Unity, and Who is worshipped by all the Lokas.” Here ends the Thirteenth Chapter of the Eighth Book on the description of the remaining Dvīpas in the Mahāpurāṇam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam, of 18,000 verses, by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.

</details>

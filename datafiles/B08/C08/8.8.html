Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam

BOOK 8 • CHAPTER 8
On the description of Ilāvrita
← Previous Chapter ☰ Index Next Chapter →
Ch 8 Verse 1 Verse
Sanskrit Text
८.८ अष्टमोऽध्यायः । भुवनकोशवर्णने इलावृतभद्राश्ववर्षवर्णनम् । श्रीनारायण उवाच । तेषु वर्षेषु देवेशाः पूर्वोक्तैः स्तवनैः सदा । पूजयन्ति महादेवीं जपध्यानसमाधिभिः ॥ १ ॥
English Translation
Nārāyaṇa said :-- In those Varṣas, Viṣṇu and the other Devas used to worship always the Great Devī with Japam and meditation and to chant hymns to Her.
Ch 8 Verse 2-5 Verse
Sanskrit Text
सर्वर्तुकुसुमश्रेणी शोभिता वनराजयः । फलानां पल्लवानां च यत्र शोभा निरन्तरम् ॥ २ ॥ तेषु काननवर्षेषु वर्षपर्वतसानुषु । गिरिद्रोणीषु सर्वासु निर्मलोदकराशिषु ॥ ३ ॥ विकचोत्पलमालासु हंससारससञ्चयैः । विमिश्रितेषु तेष्वेव पक्षिभिः कूजितेषु च ॥ ४ ॥ जलक्रीडादिभिश्चित्रविनोदैः क्रीडयन्ति च । सुन्दरीललितभ्रूणां विलासायतनेषु च ॥ ५ ॥
English Translation
The forests there are ornamented with all sorts of fruits, flowers and leaves, in all the seasons. In those excellent forests, and on the mountains, in those Varṣas and on the clear waters interspersed with full blewn lotuses and Śārasas (cranes) and in those countries where varieties of mountain trees are standing and where varieties of birds frequent and echo all around, the people play in waters and engage themselves with a great many pleasant occupations; and the beautiful women, as well, roam there with the knitting of their eyebrows.
Ch 8 Verse 6-7 Verse
Sanskrit Text
तत्रत्या विहरन्त्यत्र स्वैरं युवतिभिः सह । नवस्वपि च वर्षेषु भगवानादिपूरुषः ॥ ६ ॥ (नारायणाख्यो लोकानामनुग्रहरसैकदृक् ।) देवीमाराधयन्नास्ते स च सर्वैश्च पूज्यते । आत्मव्यूहेनेज्ययासौ सन्निधत्ते समाहितः ॥ ७ ॥
English Translation
The people there enjoy as they like, surrounded by young women; Nārāyaṇa, the Ādipuruṣa Bhagavān Himself, worships the Devī there, to shew his extreme grace to all the inhabitants of the Navavarṣa. The people also worship the Deity.
Ch 8 Verse 8-9 Verse
Sanskrit Text
इलावृते तु भगवान् पद्मजाक्षिसमुद्भवः । एक एव भवो देवो नित्यं वसति साङ्गनः ॥ ८ ॥ तत्क्षेत्रे नापरः कश्चित्प्रवेशं वितनोति च । भवान्याः शापतस्तत्र पुमान्स्त्री भवति स्फुटम् ॥ ९ ॥
English Translation
By worshipping the Devī only, the Bhagavān remains there in Samādhi, surrounded with Aniruddha and his other Vyūhacatuṣṭaya (the four forms). But in Ilāvrita Varṣa, the Bhagavān Rudra, originated from the eye-brows of Brahmā, resides only with women.
Ch 8 Verse 10-11 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भवानीनाथकैः स्त्रीणामसङ्ख्यैर्गणकोटिभिः । संरुध्यमानो देवेशो देवं सङ्कर्षणं भजन् ॥ १० ॥ आत्मना ध्यानयोगेन सर्वभूतहितेच्छया । तां तामसीं तुरीयां च मूर्तिं प्रकृतिमात्मनः ॥ ११ ॥
English Translation
No other person can enter there; for the Bhāvanī, the Śakti of Rudra has cursed that any male entering there would be transformed into a female. The Lord of Bhavanī, surrounded by innumerable women, remains here engaged in the worship of the unmanifested unborn Bhagavān Samkarṣaṇa. For the good of humanity, with intense meditation, He worships His Own Turīya Form of the nature of Tamas, thus :--
Ch 8 Verse 12-13 Verse
Sanskrit Text
उपधावते चैकाग्रमनसा भगवानजः । श्रीभगवानुवाच । ओन्नमो भगवते महापुरुषाय सर्वगुणसङ्ख्यानायानन्तायाव्यक्ताय नम इति ॥ १२ ॥ भजे भजन्यारणपादपङ्कजं भगस्य कृत्स्नस्य परं परायणम् । भक्तेष्वलं भावितभूतभावनं भवापहं त्वा भव भावमीश्वरम् ॥ १३ ॥
English Translation
Śrī Bhagavāna said :-- “Obeisance to Thee! the Bhagavān, the Great Puruṣa, endowed with all the qualities (the principal six Aiśvaryas or prosperities), the Ananta (the Infinite) and to the Unmanifested! We worship Thee, Whose lotus feet are the refuge of all. Thou art the great storehouse of all the superhuman powers and the divine faculties of omnipotence, etc.
Ch 8 Verse 14 Verse
Sanskrit Text
न यस्य मायागुणकर्मवृत्तिभि- र्निरीक्षितो ह्यण्वपि दृष्टिरज्यते । ईशे यथा नो जितमन्युरंहसा कस्तं न मन्येत जिगीषुरात्मनः ॥ १४ ॥
English Translation
Thou art always present to the Bhaktas. Thou art creating all these beings. Thou givest Mokṣa to the Bhaktas and destroyest their attachment to the world and Thou bindest Thy non-devotees in bondage to this world. Thou art the Lord. We worship Thee.
Ch 8 Verse 15 Verse
Sanskrit Text
असद्दृशो यः प्रतिभाति मायया क्षीबेव मध्वासवतागम्रलोचनः । न नागवध्वोऽर्हण ईशिरे ह्रिया यत्पादयोः स्पर्शनधर्षितेन्द्रियाः ॥ १५ ॥
English Translation
We are entirely under the control of the passions, anger, etc., and our minds are always attached to the senses; but though Thou art always looking at this world for its creation, etc., Thy mind is not a bit attached to it.
Ch 8 Verse 16 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यमाहुरस्य स्थितिजन्मसंयमं त्रिभिर्विहीनं यमनन्तमॄषयः । न वेद सिद्धार्थमिव क्वचित्स्थितं भूमण्डलं मूर्धसहस्रधामसु ॥ १६ ॥
English Translation
So who will not turn to Thee, desirous to conquer his self. Thou art appearing by Thy Māyā as one whose sight is ignorant; Thou lookest dreadful with Thy eyes reddened with the drink of Madhu (wine). By the touch of Thy feet, the mental faculties are very much enchanted; hence the women folk of the Nāgas cannot in any way worship Thee, out of bashfulness.
Ch 8 Verse 17 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यस्याद्य आसीद् गुणविग्रहो महान् विज्ञानधिष्ण्यो भगवानजः किल । यत्संवृतोऽहं त्रिवृता स्वतेजसा वैकारिकं तामसमैन्द्रियं सृजे ॥ १७ ॥
English Translation
The Ṛṣis say that though Thou art the Only One to create, preserve and destroy, yet Thou art quite unconcerned with them. Thou art Infinite and Thou hast innumerable heads. This vast universe is like the mustard seed resting somewhere on one of these heads, which Thou canst not feel even. The Mahat Tattva is Thy body manifested first. It is built of Sattva, Raja and Tamo Guṇas.
Ch 8 Verse 18 Verse
Sanskrit Text
एते वयं यस्य वशे महात्मनः स्थिताः शकुन्ता इव सूत्रयन्त्रिताः । महानहंवैकृततामसेन्द्रियाः सृजाम सर्वे यदनुग्रहादिदम् ॥ १८ ॥
English Translation
Brahmā has come out of this and I again have sprung from this Brahmā and am nurtured by the Sattva and the other Guṇas and with the help of the Teja, created these elements and the senses. These Mahat Tattvas and we all are controlled by Thy Extraordinary Form. Thou hast kept us in our respective places by Thy Kriyā Śakti as birds are kept duly by the strings through them. Mahat Tattva, Ahamkāra, and the Devas, elements and the senses, before mentioned all united create this Universe by Thy Grace.
Ch 8 Verse 19 Verse
Sanskrit Text
यन्निर्मितां कर्ह्यपि कर्मपर्वणीं मायां जनोऽयं गुरुसर्गमोहितः । न वेद निस्तारणयोगमञ्जसा तस्मै नमस्ते विलयोदयात्मने ॥ १९ ॥
English Translation
Thy creation is very big and grand; for this reason the gross thinkers, deluded by Thy power, never understand it. This Māyā is the only means to get the Samsāra Nivritti, Mokṣa, the real Goal of man: and this Māyā, again involves them in the Karma entanglements, very hard to get through. Coming in and going out, both of these are Thy forms; so we bow down to Thee.
Ch 8 Verse 20-21 Verse
Sanskrit Text
श्रीनारायण उवाच । एवं स भगवान् रुद्रो देवं सङ्कर्षणं प्रभुम् । इलावृतमुपासीत देवीगणसमाहितः ॥ २० ॥ तथैव धर्मपुत्रोऽसौ नाम्ना भद्रश्रवा इति । तत्कुलस्यापि पतयः पुरुषा भद्रसेवकाः ॥ २१ ॥
English Translation
Nārāyaṇa said :-- Thus the Bhagavān Rudra, with His Own persons in Ilāvrita Varṣa used to worship the Devī and the Saṅkarṣaṇa, the Controller of all the Lokas. The son of Dharma, well known by the name of Bhadraśrava and all the persons born of his family and his attendants, worship thus the Devī.
Ch 8 Verse 22-23 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भद्राश्ववर्षे तां मूर्तिं वासुदेवस्य विश्रुताम् । हयमूर्तिभिदा तां तु हयग्रीवपदाङ्किताम् ॥ २२ ॥ परमेण समाध्यन्यवारकेण नियन्त्रिताम् । एवमेव च तां मूर्तिं गृणन्त उपयान्ति च ॥ २३ ॥
English Translation
This form is well known to all by the name of Hayagrīva and worshipped thus. All the persons there worship Him with the intense meditation and Samādhi and realise Him thoroughly. Then they praise Him, according to due customs and get the thorough Siddhis (success in getting extraordinary powers).
Ch 8 Verse 24 Verse
Sanskrit Text
भद्रश्रवस ऊचुः । ॐ नमो भगवते धर्मायात्मविशोधनाय नम इति । अहो विचित्रं भगवद्विचेष्टितं घ्नन्तं जनोऽयं हि मिषन्न पश्यति । ध्यायन्न सद्यर्हि विकर्म सेवितुं निर्हृत्य पुत्रं पितरं जिजीविषुः ॥ २४ ॥
English Translation
The Bhadraśravās said :-- Obeisance to Thee, the Bhagavān, the Incarnate of Virtue, and to Him who destroys completely the desires, attachments, etc., to worldly objects! Ho! How wonderful are the feats of the Bhagavān! Death always destroys all, but people, seeing this, seem not to see this. Seeing that the son meets with death, the father desires to live long not for a virtuous purpose but for sense enjoyments, what is called Vikarma. Those who are skilled in Jñāna and Vijñāna say that this Universe that is seen is very transient.
Ch 8 Verse 25 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वदन्ति विश्वं कवयः स्म नश्वरं पश्यन्ति चाध्यात्मविदो विपश्चितः । तथापि मुह्यन्ति तवाज मायया सुविस्मितं कृत्यमजं नतोऽस्मि तम् ॥ २५ ॥
English Translation
Moreover those Pundits who are endowed with much Jñāna, see vividly the transitoriness of this Universe. Still, O Unborn One! When practically they come to deal with this, they all become overpowered with the influence of Māyā.
Ch 8 Verse 26 Verse
Sanskrit Text
विश्वोद्भवस्थाननिरोधकर्म ते ह्यकर्तुरङ्गीकृतमप्यपावृतः । युक्तं न चित्रं त्वयि कार्यकारणे सर्वात्मनि व्यतिरिक्ते च वस्तुतः ॥ २६ ॥
English Translation
So Thy Pastime (Līla) is wonderfully variegated. (Instead of spending our time uselessly in discussing on Śāstras) we bow down to Thee, and Thee alone. Thou art the Self-manifest Caitanya. Thou are not the object to be covered by Māyā. Thou dost not do anything in the sort of creation, etc. Thou remainest simply as the Witness thereof.
Ch 8 Verse 27 Verse
Sanskrit Text
वेदान् युगान्ते तमसा तिरस्कृतान् रसातलाद्यो नृतुरङ्गविग्रहः । प्रत्याददे वै कवयेऽभियाचते तस्मै नमस्ते वितथेहिताय ते ॥ २७ ॥
English Translation
Sill the Vedas declare that Thou createst, preservest and destroyest the Universe. It is quite reasonable and nothing to be wondered at. Thou art the Ātman of all. When the Pralaya (the time of great dissolution) comes, the Vedas were stolen by the Daityas and taken to the nether regions, the Rasātala.
Ch 8 Verse 28 Verse
Sanskrit Text
एवं स्तुवन्ति देवेशं हयशीर्षं हरिं च ते । भद्रश्रवसनामानो वर्णयन्ति च तद्गुणान् ॥ २८ ॥
English Translation
Thou, in the form of Hayagrīva (Horse-faced), rescued the Vedas and gave them to the Grandsire Brahmā who was very eager to get them back and understand their meanings. Thou art the true Saṅkalap (resolve); we bow down to Thee. Thus the Bhadraśravās praise the Haiyagrīva form of Hari and sing the glorious deeds of Him.
Ch 8 Verse 29 Verse
Sanskrit Text
एषां चरितमेतद्धि यः पठेच्छ्रावयेच्च यः । पापकञ्चुकमुत्सृज्य देवीलोकं व्रजेच्च सः ॥ २९ ॥
Sanskrit Colophon
इति श्रीमद्देवीभागवते महापुराणेऽष्टादशसाहस्र्यां संहितायामष्टमस्कन्धे भुवनकोशवर्णने इलावृतभद्राश्ववर्षवर्णनं नामाष्टमोऽध्यायः ॥ ८.८॥
English Translation
He who reads these narratives of the Mahā Puruṣa (the high-souled personage) or he who makes others hear these things, both of them, quitting their sinful bodies, go to the Devī Loka. Here ends the Eighth Chapter of the Eighth Book on the description of Ilāvrīta in the Mahāpurāṇam Śrī Mad Devī Bhāgavatam, of 18,000 verses, by Maharṣi Veda Vyāsa.
